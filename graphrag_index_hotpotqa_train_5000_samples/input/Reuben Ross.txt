Reuben Ross

Reuben Ross (born December 5, 1985) is a Canadian diver.

 He has won gold in the men's 3 m or 3 m synchronised events at the Canadian Nationals every year since 2008.

 He competed in the individual 3 m springboard and 10 m platform events at the 2008 Summer Olympics in Beijing.

 At the 2012 Summer Olympics he competed in the 3 m springboard synchronised event with Alexandre Despatie.

 He currently lives in Edmonton, AB with his wife and 3 kids.
