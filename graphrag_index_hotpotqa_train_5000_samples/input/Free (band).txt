Free (band)

Free were an English rock band formed in London in 1968, best known for their 1970 signature song "All Right Now".

 They disbanded in 1973 and lead singer Paul Rodgers went on to become a frontman of the band Bad Company along with Simon Kirke on drums.

 Lead guitarist Paul Kossoff formed Back Street Crawler in 1975, but died from a pulmonary embolism at the age of 25 in 1976.

 Bassist Andy Fraser formed Sharks.
