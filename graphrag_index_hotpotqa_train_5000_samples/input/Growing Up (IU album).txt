Growing Up (IU album)

Growing Up is the first Korean-language studio album by South Korean singer-songwriter and actress IU.

 It was released on April 23, 2009, as a follow-up to her 2008 debut mini-album "Lost and Found".

 Two of the album's 16 tracks, "Boo" and "You Know (있잖아) (Rock Ver.)"

, were released as singles.
