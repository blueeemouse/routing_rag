No One Knows

"No One Knows" is a song by American rock band Queens of the Stone Age written by band members Josh Homme and Mark Lanegan.

 It was the first single and second track from their third album, "Songs for the Deaf", and was released on November 26, 2002.

 "No One Knows" was a chart success, becoming the band's only single to top the US Modern Rock charts.

 The song was also critically acclaimed, receiving a nomination for Best Hard Rock Performance at the 2003 Grammy Awards.
