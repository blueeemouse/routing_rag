Snow in Brazil

Snow in Brazil occurs virtually every year in some cities located in the high plains of the southern states of Rio Grande do Sul, Santa Catarina, and Paraná.

 They account for approximately 23,000 km2 and are the coldest cities in the country.

 Snow can occur in the following regions: São Joaquim, Urubici, Urupema, and São José dos Ausentes, as well as Bom Jesus, Bom Jardim da Serra, Cambará do Sul, and Palmas.
