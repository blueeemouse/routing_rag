Bryan Caplan

Bryan Douglas Caplan (born April 8, 1971) is an American economist.

 Caplan is a professor of economics at George Mason University, research fellow at the Mercatus Center, adjunct scholar at the Cato Institute, and frequent contributor to Freakonomics as well as publishing his own blog, EconLog.

 He is a self-publicized economic libertarian.

 The bulk of Caplan's academic work is in public economics, especially public choice theory.
