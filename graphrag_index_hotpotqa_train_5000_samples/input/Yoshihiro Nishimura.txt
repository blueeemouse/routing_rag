Yoshihiro Nishimura

Yoshihiro Nishimura (西村喜廣 , Nishimura Yoshihiro ) is a Japanese film director, special effects and makeup effects artist, and a screenwriter who has worked predominantly in the horror genre.

 Nishimura has been described as "a legendary director and effects artist" and "the Tom Savini of Japan" with "talent to burn".
