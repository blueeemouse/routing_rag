Karma (Winger album)

Karma is the fifth studio album by American rock band Winger.

 The album was released on 16 October 2009 in Europe and 27 October 2009 in the United States.

 The inspiration for a new Winger came about at the end of 2008, when Kip Winger had the vision to meld the more commercial, heavier and song-oriented approach of the first two albums with the more "thinking-man" approach of the last two studio records.
