Northwich Manchester Villa F.C.

Northwich Manchester Villa Football Club was an English association football club based in Manchester, Greater Manchester, England.

 The team last competed in the North West Counties Football League Division One in season 2015–16, having been admitted to that league via a successful application.

 The team has had three previous names, Woodley, Northwich Villa, and Northwich Flixton Villa, and played in the Cheshire Football League between 2005 and 2011.

 The club folded in 2016.
