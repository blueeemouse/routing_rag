Kevin Brennan (comedian)

Kevin Brennan is an American stand-up comedian and writer.

 His writing credits include Saturday Night Live and Sports Show with Norm Macdonald.

 He has appeared numerous times on Late Night with Conan O'Brien, Late Show with David Letterman, Last Call with Carson Daly and Red Eye w/Tom Shillue.

 He can also be seen in a half-hour HBO stand-up special for the series One Night Stand.

 He currently has a weekly show called "Burning Bridges" on Compound Media.

 He is the brother of Neal Brennan.
