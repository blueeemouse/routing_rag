Filipendula

Filipendula is a genus of 12 species of perennial herbaceous flowering plants in the family Rosaceae, native to the temperate regions of the Northern Hemisphere.

 Well-known species include meadowsweet ("Filipendula ulmaria") and dropwort ("Filipendula vulgaris"), both native to Europe, and queen-of-the-forest ("Filipendula occidentalis") and queen-of-the-prairie ("Filipendula rubra"), native to North America.
