Wendy Robie

Wendy Robie (born October 6, 1953) is an American actress.

 She is best known for her role as Nadine Hurley in David Lynch's television series "Twin Peaks" (1990-1991) and the prequel film "" (2014).

 She subsequently starred in two of Wes Craven's films: "The People Under the Stairs" (1991) and "Vampire in Brooklyn" (1995).

 In 2017, Robie reprised her role as Nadine in David Lynch's revival series "Twin Peaks: The Return".
