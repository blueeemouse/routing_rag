Rush Hour 3

Rush Hour 3 is a 2007 American martial arts/buddy action comedy film and the third and final film in the "Rush Hour" series, starring Jackie Chan and Chris Tucker.

 The film was officially announced on May 7, 2006, and filming began on July 4, 2006.

 The film is set in Paris and Los Angeles.

 "Rush Hour 3" was released on August 10, 2007, in the United States.
