Bayview Community School

Bayview Community School is located in Mahone Bay, Nova Scotia.

 It was built on a reclaimed marsh in September, 2000.

 When the school first opened, the students came from the Blockhouse Bulldogs and the Mahone Bay Hawks.

 The school created a new mascot and the Bayview Bobcats were born.

 The two feeder schools got to pick a selection of names from a contest and the name chosen was Bayview Community School.
