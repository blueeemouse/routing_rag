Gnome Alone

Gnome Alone is an upcoming Canadian-American computer animated musical comedy film directed by Peter Lepeniotis and written by Michael Schwartz and Zina Zaflow from a story by Robert Moreland, and Jared Micah Herman & Kyle Newman.

 The film, produced by 3QU Media, stars the voices of Becky G, Josh Peck, Olivia Holt, George Lopez, and Patrick Stump.

 On July 14, it was confirmed that the film will be released on October 13, 2017.
