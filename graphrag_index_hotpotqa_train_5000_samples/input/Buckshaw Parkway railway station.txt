Buckshaw Parkway railway station

Buckshaw Parkway is a British railway station which opened on 3 October 2011 on the Manchester to Preston Line, near Euxton Junction with the West Coast Main Line.

 It is one of Euxton's two railway stations being in Buckshaw Village, formerly the Royal Ordnance Factory (ROF Chorley) between Chorley and Leyland.

 It is on the site of the four-platform Chorley ROF Halt, which was closed around 1942; this prior station remained virtually intact until the 1970s, but was finally cleared in the early 2000s.
