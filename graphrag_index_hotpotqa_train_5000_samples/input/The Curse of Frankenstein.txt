The Curse of Frankenstein

The Curse of Frankenstein is a 1957 British horror film by Hammer Film Productions, loosely based on the novel "Frankenstein" (1818) by Mary Shelley.

 It was Hammer's first colour horror film, and the first of their Frankenstein series.

 Its worldwide success led to several sequels, and the studio's new versions of "Dracula" (1958) and "The Mummy" (1959) and established "Hammer Horror" as a distinctive brand of Gothic cinema.

 The film was directed by Terence Fisher and stars Peter Cushing as Victor Frankenstein, Hazel Court as Elizabeth, and Christopher Lee as the creature.
