Brian Sloan (entrepreneur)

Brian Sloan is an American entrepreneur known for creating a number of sex toys and adult-themed beauty contests.

 He is one of the few entrepreneurs in the adult industry to successfully use crowd funding, raising over $375,000 across three Indiegogo campaigns.

 He hosted a vaginal beauty contest, a scrotum beauty contest, and an anus beauty contest and appears as a pitchman in infomercial style videos for most of his products and contests.
