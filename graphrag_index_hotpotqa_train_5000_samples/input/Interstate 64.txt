Interstate 64

Interstate 64 (I-64) is an Interstate Highway in the Eastern United States.

 Its western terminus is at I-70, U.S. Route 40 (US 40), and US 61 in Wentzville, Missouri.

 Its eastern terminus is at an interchange with I-264 and I-664 at Bowers Hill in Chesapeake, Virginia.

 I-64 connects the major metropolitan areas of St. Louis, Louisville and Lexington in Kentucky, Charleston, West Virginia, and Richmond, Virginia.
