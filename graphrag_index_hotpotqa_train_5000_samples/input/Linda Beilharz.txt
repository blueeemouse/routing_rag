Linda Beilharz

Linda Beilharz, OAM, is an Australian adventurer who is the first Australian woman to successfully trek the South and North poles.

 She completed the North Pole expedition with her husband Rob Rigato and Canadian explorer Sarah McNair-Landry in April 2010 while the South Pole feat was completed in December 2004 when she became the first Australian woman to ski 1,100 km from the edge of the Antarctic to the South Pole.
