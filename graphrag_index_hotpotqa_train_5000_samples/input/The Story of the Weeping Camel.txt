The Story of the Weeping Camel

The Story of the Weeping Camel (Mongolian: Ингэн нулимс , "Ingen nulims ", "Tears of the Camel") is a 2003 German docudrama distributed by ThinkFilm.

 It was released internationally in 2004.

 The movie was directed and written by Byambasuren Davaa and Luigi Falorni.

 The plot is about a family of nomadic shepherds in the Gobi Desert trying to save the life of a rare white bactrian camel ("Camelus bactrianus") calf after it was rejected by its mother.
