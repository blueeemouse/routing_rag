Sid G. Hedges

Sidney George Hedges (25 March 1897 – 1974) was a British author of books and articles on swimming, games and hobbies for young people.

 He also wrote young people's fiction.

 He was born in Bicester, the second son of George William Hedges, a draper and outfitter, and Mary Ann Hedges.

 He married Mary Dixon at Bicester in 1929; their son Anthony was born in 1931.
