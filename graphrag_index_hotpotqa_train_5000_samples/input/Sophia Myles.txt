Sophia Myles

Sophia Jane Myles ( ; born 18 March 1980) is an English actress, best known in film for portraying Erika in "Underworld" (2003), Lady Penelope Creighton-Ward in the live-action "Thunderbirds" film, Isolde in "Tristan & Isolde" and Darcy in "", and has received critical acclaim for her television work, particularly as Madame de Pompadour in the "Doctor Who" episode "The Girl in the Fireplace" and Beth Turner in "Moonlight".
