Back in the Saddle

"Back in the Saddle" is a song by American hard rock band Aerosmith.

 It was written by Steven Tyler and Joe Perry.

 It is the first song on Aerosmith's pinnacle hard rock album "Rocks" released in 1976.

 The song was released as the third single from the album in 1977.

 It peaked at #38 on the "Billboard" Hot 100.
