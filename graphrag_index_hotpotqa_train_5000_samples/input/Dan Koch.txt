Dan Koch

Dan Koch (born 1983) is the lead guitarist, vocalist, and principal songwriter for the rock band Pacific Gold and previous lead guitarist and principal songwriter for Sherwood.

 He helped form the band with Nate Henry in 2002 while attending university at Cal Poly.

 Dan was originally Sherwood's drummer, but when drummer Joe Greenetz joined the band he switched from drums to guitar.

 Moreover, Koch transitioned into the rhythm guitar player.

 With the departure of Chris Armstrong, Koch switched into the role of lead guitarist.

 Dan Koch is a native of Los Gatos, California.

 Dan married girlfriend Jaffrey Bagge on November 29, 2009.

 He currently resides in Seattle, Washington with his wife.

 While in high school, he was in a punk band called Ignorant Youth.
