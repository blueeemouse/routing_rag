Hamish Hartlett

Hamish Hartlett (born 14 August 1990) is an AFL footballer currently playing for the Port Adelaide Football Club.

 Hartlett was drafted by Port Adelaide with pick number 4 in the 2008 AFL Draft, making him the Power's earliest draft pick ever.

 He is the younger brother of former Carlton Blues forward Adam Hartlett.

 He is a medium-sized midfielder who reads the game exceptionally well, has clean hands, vision and kicks the ball superbly from anywhere inside 65 meters.
