Vito Spatafore

Vito Spatafore Sr., played by Joseph R. Gannascoli, is a fictional character on the HBO TV series "The Sopranos".

 He was a member of the DiMeo Crime Family and a subordinate of Tony Soprano.

 He was married to Marie Spatafore with two children, Francesca and Vito Jr., and was a closeted homosexual.
