Paramour (Cirque du Soleil)

Paramour was Cirque du Soleil's first resident musical theatre show at the Lyric Theatre on Broadway, New York City.

 "Paramour" was themed to the "Golden age of Hollywood" and followed the life of "a poet who is forced to choose between love and art".

 It had similar elements to Cirque du Soleil's retired Los Angeles resident show in "Iris" (which was also themed on cinema) written and created by Philippe Decouflé, and had a 38-person onstage cast with actress Ruby Lewis in the lead as Indigo.

 "Paramour" began preview shows on April 16, 2016, with an official premiere on May 25, 2016.

 It closed exactly one year after its first preview show, on 16 April 2017.
