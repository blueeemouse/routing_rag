The New Archie and Sabrina Hour

The New Archie and Sabrina Hour (advertised as The Archie-Sabrina Show) is the seventh and final animated series featuring Archie Comics characters under the Filmation banner.

 The series premiered on NBC in September, rebroadcasting segments from "The Archie Show", as well as brand-new segments featuring "Sabrina the Teenage Witch".

 The show's format featured three segments per episode: a 15-minute one, a 30-minute one, and another 15-minute one—with the segments separated by songs (two songs per episode) and the first segment invariably featuring and emphasizing Sabrina.
