Chicken with Vinegar

Chicken with Vinegar (French: Poulet au vinaigre ) is a 1985 French crime film directed by Claude Chabrol.

 It was entered into the 1985 Cannes Film Festival.

 It is distributed in the USA with the title "Cop Au Vin".

 Chabrol made a sequel in 1986 entitled "Inspecteur Lavardin".
