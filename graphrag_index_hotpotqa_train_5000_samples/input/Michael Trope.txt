Michael Trope

Michael Lance "Mike" Trope (born 1951), is a Los Angeles based trial lawyer and former sports agent for over 200 NFL players.

 Trope was a sports agent from the time he was a 21-year-old senior at University of Southern California, USC, in 1972 until he retired from the business in 1985.

 Subsequently, after Trope's graduation from Loyola Law School, he was admitted to practice law in California in 1987.

 As an agent for football stars, Trope was known for negotiating record breaking contracts in the 1970s, including the first million dollar package for an NFL rookie.

 This caused many publications to refer to Trope, still in his 20's, as a Super Agent by 1977, as well as "Whiz Kid" and "LA's Boy Wonder".

 Trope is the son of legendary Los Angeles Trial lawyer and philanthropist, Sorrell Trope.
