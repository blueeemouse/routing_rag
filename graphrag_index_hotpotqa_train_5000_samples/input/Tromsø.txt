Tromsø

Tromsø (] ; Northern Sami: "Romsa" ; Finnish: "Tromssa" ; Kven: "Tromssa" ) is a city and municipality in Troms county, Norway.

 The administrative centre of the municipality is the city of Tromsø.

 Outside of Norway, "Tromso" and "Tromsö" are alternative spellings of the city.

 Tromsø town has a population of 64,376 as of 1 January 2016.

 The most populous town north of it is Alta, Norway, with a population of 14,272 (2013).
