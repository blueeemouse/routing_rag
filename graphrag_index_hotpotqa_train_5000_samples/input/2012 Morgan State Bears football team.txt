2012 Morgan State Bears football team

The 2012 Morgan State Bears football team represented Morgan State University in the 2012 NCAA Division I FCS football season.

 They were led by 12th-year head coach Donald Hill-Eley and played their home games at Hughes Stadium.

 They are a member of the Mid-Eastern Athletic Conference (MEAC).

 Morgan State finished the season 3–8, 2–6 in MEAC play to finish in a tie for ninth place.
