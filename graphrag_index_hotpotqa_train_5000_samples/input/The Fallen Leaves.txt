The Fallen Leaves

The Fallen Leaves are a British garage rock group that formed in Richmond, London in 2004.

 The group was formed by Rob Symmons (dustbin guitar) and Rob Green (vocals) with Paul Myers (bass), and for a very short time, Paul Cook (drums).

 The current line up features drummer,Brett Buddy Ascott ex of Mod/Punk band The Chords, and bassist, Matthew Karas, who plays various instruments for Glassglue.
