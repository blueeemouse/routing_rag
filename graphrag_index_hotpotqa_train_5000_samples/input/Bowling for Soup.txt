Bowling for Soup

Bowling for Soup (often typeset as ¡Bowling for Soup!

 and abbreviated as BFS) is an American rock band originally formed in Wichita Falls, Texas, in 1994.

 The band consists of Jaret Reddick (lead vocals, guitar), Chris Burney (guitar, backing vocals), Erik Chandler (bass, backing vocals, acoustic guitar), and Gary Wiseman (drums, percussion, backing vocals).

 The band is best known for its singles "Girl All the Bad Guys Want", "1985", "Almost", and "High School Never Ends".
