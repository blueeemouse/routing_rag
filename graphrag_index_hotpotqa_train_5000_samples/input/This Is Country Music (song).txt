This Is Country Music (song)

"This Is Country Music" is a song co-written and recorded by American country music artist Brad Paisley.

 After performing it at the Country Music Association awards ceremony, Paisley released the song in December 2010.

 The song is Paisley's twenty-eighth single release overall; it entered the Hot Country Songs charts dated for December 4, 2010.

 It was included on his eighth studio album, of the same name, released on May 23, 2011 release via Arista Nashville.
