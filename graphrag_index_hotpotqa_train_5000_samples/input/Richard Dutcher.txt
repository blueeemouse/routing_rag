Richard Dutcher

Richard Alan Dutcher (born 1964) is an American independent filmmaker who produces, writes, directs, edits, and frequently stars in his films.

 After making "God's Army", a successful 2000 movie about LDS missionaries, Dutcher became well known among members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (LDS Church) and gave himself the nickname "The Father of Mormon Cinema."

 In 2007, Dutcher left the LDS Church.
