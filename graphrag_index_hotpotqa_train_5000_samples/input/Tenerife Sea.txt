Tenerife Sea

"Tenerife Sea" is a song by English singer-songwriter Ed Sheeran.

 It was co-written by Snow Patrol member Johnny McDaid and Foy Vance, and features production from Rick Rubin.

 The song was released on 20 June 2014 as part of his second studio album, "x".

 It entered the UK Singles Chart at number 93 despite lacking an independent release.
