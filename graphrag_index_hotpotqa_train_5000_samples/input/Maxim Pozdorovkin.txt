Maxim Pozdorovkin

Maxim Pozdorovkin is an award-winning Russian filmmaker who is the director and producer of the feature-length documentaries "Capital", "" and "The Notorious Mr. Bout".

 Pozdorovkin holds a PhD from Harvard University and is a junior fellow at the Harvard Society of Fellows.

 He appeared on "The Daily Show with Jon Stewart" on June 3rd, 2013.
