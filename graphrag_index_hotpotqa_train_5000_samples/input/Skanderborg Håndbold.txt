Skanderborg Håndbold

Skanderborg Håndbold is a handball club based in Skanderborg in Jutland, Denmark.

 The club was founded in 1982 and was the result of a merger between Stilling-Skanderborg and Vrold-Skanderborg Handball Club.

 Currently, Skanderborg Håndbold competes in the men's Danish Handball League and the women in the Danish 1st Division.
