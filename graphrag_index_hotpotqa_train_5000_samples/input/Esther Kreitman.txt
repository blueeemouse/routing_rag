Esther Kreitman

Hinde Ester Singer Kreytman (31 March 1891 – 13 June 1954), known in English as Esther Kreitman, was a Yiddish-language novelist and short story writer.

 She was born in Biłgoraj, Congress Poland to a rabbinic Jewish family.

 Her younger brothers Israel Joshua Singer and Isaac Bashevis Singer subsequently became writers.
