Shengjing Bank

Shengjing Bank (in ) is a commercial bank, with its headquarters in Shenyang, Liaoning Province, China.

 It was established in 1996 as Shenyang City Commercial Bank and opened in Shenyang City only.

 It changed its name to Shengjing Bank in 2007 and has since opened a branch in Beijing, Tianjin, Dalian, Yingkou, Huludao, Shanghai, Anshan, Changchun, Benxi, Panjin, Chaoyang, Fushun, Jinzhou, Fuxin, Dandong and Liaoyang.
