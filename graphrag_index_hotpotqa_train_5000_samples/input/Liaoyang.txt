Liaoyang

Liaoyang () is a prefecture-level city of east-central Liaoning province, People's Republic of China, situated on the T'ai-tzu River and, together with Anshan, forms a metro area of 2,057,200 inhabitants in 2010.

 It is approximately one hour south of Shenyang, the provincial capital, by car.

 Liaoyang is home to Liaoning University's College of Foreign Studies and a number of vocational colleges.

 The city hosts a limited number of professional basketball and volleyball games in a modern sports facility.
