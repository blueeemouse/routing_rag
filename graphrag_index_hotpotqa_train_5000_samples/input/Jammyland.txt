Jammyland

Jammyland Records was an independent music retailer in New York City.

 It opened in 1993 at its location on 60 E 3rd Street in Manhattan, by owner Ira Heaps.

 The store specialized in Jamaican music, specifically the ska, rocksteady and reggae genres of the 60s and 70s recorded on vinyl, though it also sold CDs.

 Jammyland also reissued more obscure reggae.

 The store used to have a recording studio in its basement, where artists including Victor Rice, The Slackers and the Dynamos recorded, but later an avant-garde/noise music retailer opened in the basement.
