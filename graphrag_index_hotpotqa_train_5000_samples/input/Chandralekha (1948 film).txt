Chandralekha (1948 film)

Chandralekha (also spelt Chandraleka ) is a 1948 Indian Tamil-language historical adventure film directed and produced by S. S. Vasan.

 Starring T. R. Rajakumari, M. K. Radha and Ranjan in the lead roles, the film follows two brothers (Veerasimhan and Sasankan) who fight over ruling their father's kingdom and marrying the village dancer, Chandralekha.
