Starship Troopers: Traitor of Mars

Starship Troopers: Traitor of Mars is a 2017 Japanese-American computer animated military science fiction film.

 The film is a sequel to the 2012 film "" and the fifth installment of the "Starship Troopers" film series.

 This film also marks the return of two original actors from the first film.

 Casper Van Dien reprises his role as Johnny Rico from the first and film and Dina Meyer reprises her role as Dizzy Flores from the first film.
