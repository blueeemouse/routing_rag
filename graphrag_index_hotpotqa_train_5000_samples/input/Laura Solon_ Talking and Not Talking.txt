Laura Solon: Talking and Not Talking

Laura Solon: Talking and Not Talking is a comedy sketch show created by British Perrier Award winning comedian, Laura Solon.

 The show was first broadcast on BBC Radio 4.

 Following her success with Kopfraper's Syndrome: One Man and His Incredible Mind, Solon went on to create the show with the BBC.

 Like in her previous shows, Talking and Not Talking uses a variety of short sketches, featuring different characters, some recurring, some not.

 The show was voted on the British Comedy Guide website as the "Best British Radio Sketch Show of 2008".
