North Braddock, Pennsylvania

North Braddock is a borough in Allegheny County, Pennsylvania, United States.

 North Braddock was organized from a part of Braddock Township in 1897.

 The borough prides itself in being the "Birth Place of Steel" as the home of Andrew Carnegie's Edgar Thomson Steel Works that opened in 1875.

 North Braddock is a suburb 11 mi east of Pittsburgh with a 15-minute travel time to the city.

 The borough is located in the valley along the Monongahela River, and is made up of three jurisdictional voting wards.
