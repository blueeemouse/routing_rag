Gaspésie National Park

Gaspésie National Park (French: "Parc national de la Gaspésie" ) is a provincial park located south of the town of Sainte-Anne-des-Monts, Quebec, Canada in the inland of the Gaspé peninsula.

 The park contains the highest peak of the Appalachian Mountains in the province of Quebec, Mont Jacques-Cartier, 1,270 meters above sea level.

 In addition, the park contains the only population of Caribou found south of the Saint Lawrence River in Canada.
