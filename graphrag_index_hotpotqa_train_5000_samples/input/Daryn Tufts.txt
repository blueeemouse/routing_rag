Daryn Tufts

Daryn Tufts (born April 1, 1973 in Arcadia, California) is a writer, director, producer, and actor.

 Tufts’s writing, producing, and directing credits include feature films, documentaries, and television commercials and he has acted in several successful independent films.

 Tufts has also performed and taught sketch comedy and improvisational comedy with several professional comedy troupes and is the commercial spokesperson for the Questar Corporation (gas company).

 He currently lives in Holladay, Utah.
