Wedding Wars

Wedding Wars is a 2006 American-Canadian made-for-television LGBT romantic comedy film starring John Stamos, Eric Dane, Bonnie Somerville, James Brolin and Sean Maher.

 Executive produced by Neil Meron and Craig Zadan, the film was directed by Jim Fall and written by Stephen Mazur.

 It premiered in the US on December 11, 2006 on A&E.
