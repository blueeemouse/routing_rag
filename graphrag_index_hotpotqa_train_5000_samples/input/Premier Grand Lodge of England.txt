Premier Grand Lodge of England

The Premier Grand Lodge of England was founded on 24 June 1717 as the Grand Lodge of London and Westminster, later calling itself the Grand Lodge of England.

 Convention calls it the Premier Grand Lodge of England to distinguish it from the "Most Ancient and Honourable Society of Free and Accepted Masons according to the Old Constitutions", more usually referred to as the Ancient Grand Lodge of England, and the "Grand Lodge of All England Meeting at York".

 It existed until 1813 when it united with the Ancient Grand Lodge of England to create the United Grand Lodge of England.

 It was the first Masonic Grand Lodge to be created.
