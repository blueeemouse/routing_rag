RTL Telekids

RTL Telekids is the name of a digital television channel for children.

 The channel is operated by RTL Nederland and can be received through cable.

 In addition, RTL Telekids is broadcasting as a youth block on television channel RTL 8 during daytime.

 Like the rest of the Dutch RTL family RTL Telekids is officially a Luxembourg channel that does not have to adhere to the Dutch media law.

 As Luxembourg has no real regulator, the channel is also not monitored.
