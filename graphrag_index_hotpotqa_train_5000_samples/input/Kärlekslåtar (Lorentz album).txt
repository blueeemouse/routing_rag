Kärlekslåtar (Lorentz album)

Kärlekslåtar is the solodebut studio album by the Swedish recording artist Lorentz.

 After two albums released with his younger brother, Sakarias Berger as the previously Grammis-awarded duo Lorentz & Sakarias, he debuted as a solo artist in 2014.

 The title "Kärlekslåtar" is Swedish for "Love songs", the songs are written in Swedish, including some few terms and expressions in English.

 He got a Grammis award "Best HipHop/Soul of The Year", for his work of the album.
