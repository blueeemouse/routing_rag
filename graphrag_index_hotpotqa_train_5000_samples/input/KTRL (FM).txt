KTRL (FM)

KTRL–FM 90.5 is a noncommercial public radio station licensed to Tarleton State University in Stephenville, Texas.

 KTRL broadcasts to a 10–county area of the Cross Timbers just southwest of Fort Worth, Texas, covering a population of about 200,000 people.

 The station is partnered with Texas A&M's KAMU-FM 90.9 FM, bringing a mix of public radio and student programming to the Cross Timbers region.
