Last Man Standing (E-type album)

Last Man Standing is the third studio album by Swedish singer-songwriter E-Type, which was released in 1998.

 It contains hit songs "Angels Crying", "Here I Go Again" and "Princess of Egypt".

 Nana Hedin provided much of the female lead vocals.

 Album debuted # 1 in Finnish Albums Chart, staying in the top for total 6 weeks going double platinum.

 In Sweden the album peaked # 1 going platinum.

 The US and Japanese editions of the album were released with different cover art.

 This album remains E-Type's best-selling album within sales 3 million copies worldwide.
