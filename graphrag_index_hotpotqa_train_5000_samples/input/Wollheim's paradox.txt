Wollheim's paradox

Wollheim's paradox is a problem in political philosophy that points to an inherent contradiction in the concept of democracy.

 The paradox highlights the fact that a person can simultaneously advocate two conflicting policy options A and B, provided that the person believes that democratic decisions should be followed.

 The paradox was formulated by the British philosopher Richard Wollheim in 1962 in an article entitled "A paradox in the theory of democracy".
