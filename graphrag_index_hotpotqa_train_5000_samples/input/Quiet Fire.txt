Quiet Fire

Quiet Fire is the third studio album by American singer-songwriter Roberta Flack, released in November 1971 by Atlantic Records.

 It was recorded at Atlantic Recording Studios, Regent Studios, and The Hit Factory in New York City.

 The album peaked at number 18 on the "Billboard" Top LPs & Tape, and its single "Will You Still Love Me Tomorrow" charted at number 76 on the Hot 100.
