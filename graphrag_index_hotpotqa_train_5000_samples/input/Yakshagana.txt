Yakshagana

Yakshagana ( Kannada : "ಯಕ್ಷಗಾನ", Tulu : "ಆಟ") is a traditional theatre form that combines dance, music, dialogue, costume, make-up, and stage techniques with a unique style and form.

 This theatre style is mainly found in Tulunadu and some parts of Malenadu region's of Karnataka and Kerala.

Yakshagana is traditionally presented from dusk to dawn.
