John Constantine

John Constantine ( ) is a fictional antihero, appearing in comic books published by DC Comics and its alternative imprint Vertigo.

 The character first appeared in "Swamp Thing" Vol.

 2 No. 25 (June 1984), and was created by Alan Moore, Steve Bissette and John Totleben.

 He serves as the lead character of the comic books "Hellblazer" (1988–2013), "Constantine" (2013–15), "Constantine: The Hellblazer" (2015–16), and "The Hellblazer" (2016–).
