Sviatoslav Mykhailiuk

Sviatoslav "Svi" Mykhailiuk (Ukrainian: Святосла́в Михайлю́к , ] ; born June 10, 1997) is a Ukrainian basketball player who currently plays college basketball at the University of Kansas.

 He played for Cherkaski Mavpy in the Ukrainian Basketball SuperLeague from 2012 to 2014.

 He also plays for the Ukrainian national team in the 2016 FIBA Europe Under-20 Championship.
