St Peter's Church, Duxford

St Peter's Church is a Church of England parish church in the village of Duxford in Cambridgeshire.

 From medieval times until the 19th century it was one of two parish churches in the village (the other being St John's), but their two parishes were merged in 1874 and St Peter's is now the sole parish church for the village.

 It has been Grade I listed since 1967.
