Tim McGraw (album)

Tim McGraw is the first album by American country music artist Tim McGraw, released in 1993.

 It includes the singles "What Room Was the Holiday In", "Welcome to the Club", "Two Steppin' Mind", and "Memory Lane", none of which reached the Top 40 on the country charts.

 This is the only studio album of McGraw's career not to achieve a music recording sales certification or to enter the Top Country Albums charts.
