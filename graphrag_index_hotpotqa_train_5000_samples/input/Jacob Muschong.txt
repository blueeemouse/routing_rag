Jacob Muschong

Jacob Muschong or Iacob Muschong (born 1868, Nagykikinda, Austria-Hungary - died December 13, 1923, Lugoj, Romania) was an ethnic German industrialist who made a fortune producing bricks.

 He was born in Nagykikinda (Serbian: "Velika Kikinda, "now "Kikinda", Serbia), a town in the Banat, in a family with a long tradition in the production of bricks.

 His grandparents and great-grandparents han produced bricks in workshops located at the edge of Lugoj.

 At the age of 20 years he married Margaret Bohn the daughter of a famous German industrialist specialized in the production of tiles and bricks that built the brick factory at Zsombolya (now Jimbolia, Romania) and Gyertyámos (Cărpiniş) and had other several factories in Europe.
