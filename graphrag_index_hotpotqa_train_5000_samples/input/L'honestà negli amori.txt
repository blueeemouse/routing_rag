L'honestà negli amori

L'honestà negli amori is a dramma per musica in 3 acts by composer Alessandro Scarlatti.

 Written in 1679-1680 when Scarlatti was 19 years old, it was his second opera.

 The opera uses an Italian language libretto that was written by either D F Bernini or Domenico Filippo Contini.

 The work premiered at the Teatro di Palazzo Bernini in Rome on 3 February 1680.

 The opera was performed again in 1682 in Acquaviva delle Fonti at the Palazzo De Mari with "Acquaviva laureata" a serenata composed by Giovanni Cesare Netti.
