2013 24 Hours of Le Mans

The 2013 24 Hours of Le Mans (French: "24 Heures du Mans 2013" ) was an automobile endurance race held on 22–23 June 2013 at the Circuit de la Sarthe, Le Mans, France.

 It was the 81st edition of the 24 Hours of Le Mans motor race organised by the Automobile Club de l'Ouest (ACO), as well as the 90th anniversary of the first running of the 24 Hours of Le Mans.

 The race was the third round of the 2013 FIA World Endurance Championship.
