Niharika Acharya

Niharika Acharya is the Editorial Director at National Journal, an Atlantic Media company.

 Prior to joining the National Journal, she was a freelance producer at Fox TV.

 Niharika worked at Bloomberg TV from 2010 to 2014, she was the Producer of Bloomberg Television's flagship political show, "Political Capital with Al Hunt", a political show with an economic edge.

 The show features discussions on national and international news, along with a weekly wrap-up and interviews with key political figures.
