Alexandr Zaichikov

Alexandr Zaichikov (Belarusian: Аляксандр Зайчыкаў ; born (1992--) 17 1992 (age (2017)-(1992)-((11)<(08)or(11)==(08)and(30)<(17)) ) in Minsk) is a Belarusian-born Kazakh weightlifter.

 He competed at the 2012 Summer Olympics in the –105 kg event, and in the same event at the 2016 Summer Olympics.

 He won a Bronze medal at the 2016 Summer Olympics.
