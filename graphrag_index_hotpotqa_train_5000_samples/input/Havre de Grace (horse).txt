Havre de Grace (horse)

Havre de Grace (foaled on May 12, 2007, in Kentucky) is an American Thoroughbred Racehorse who was 2011 American Horse of the Year.

 During her racing career, she was owned by Fox Hill Farms and trained by J. Larry Jones.

 Her sire is the 2005 American Horse of the Year, Saint Liam.

 Her dam is the mare Easter Bunnette, a daughter of Carson City.

 Her name derives from the city and racetrack of Havre de Grace, Maryland.
