Tourism in West Bengal

Tourism in West Bengal refers to West Bengal's tourism.

 West Bengal (পশ্চিম বাংলা) is a state in the eastern region of India and is the nation's fourth-most populous.

 The state capital is Kolkata (কলকাতা) (formerly "Calcutta").

 The state encompasses two broad natural regions: the Gangetic Plain in the south and the sub-Himalayan and Himalayan area in the north.

 The tourism in West Bengal is maintained by WBTDCL, a state government owned enterprise.
