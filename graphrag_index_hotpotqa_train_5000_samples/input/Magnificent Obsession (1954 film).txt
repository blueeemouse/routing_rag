Magnificent Obsession (1954 film)

Magnificent Obsession is a 1954 Technicolor drama romantic film directed by Douglas Sirk starring Jane Wyman and Rock Hudson.

 The screenplay was written by Robert Blees and Wells Root, after the 1929 book "Magnificent Obsession" by Lloyd C. Douglas.

 The film was produced by Ross Hunter.

 Sirk sometimes claimed that the story was based distantly on the Greek legend of Alcestis.
