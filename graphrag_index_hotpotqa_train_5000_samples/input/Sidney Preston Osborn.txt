Sidney Preston Osborn

Sidney Preston Osborn (May 17, 1884 – May 25, 1948) was the first Secretary of State of Arizona, and later the seventh Governor of Arizona and is, as of 2017, the only governor of Arizona to be elected to four consecutive terms (Governors of Arizona served biennial terms with no limits until 1968, when it was changed to serve quadrennial terms, and changed again in 1992 to a limit of two terms at a time).

 Osborn is also the second native-born governor of Arizona, preceded by Thomas Edward Campbell.
