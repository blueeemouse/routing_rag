Tommy Cooper

Thomas Frederick "Tommy" Cooper (19 March 1921 – 15 April 1984) was a British prop comedian and magician.

 Cooper was a member of the Magic Circle, and respected by traditional magicians.

 He was famed for his red tarboosh, and his appearance was large and lumbering, at 6 ft and more than 15 st in weight.

 On 15 April 1984, Cooper collapsed with a heart attack on live national television, and died soon afterwards.
