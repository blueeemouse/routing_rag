KGJN-LP

KGJN-LP (106.7 FM "Traffic Weather Information") is a low-power FM radio station broadcasting a news talk information format.

 As of June 2010, the station was rebroadcasting the local NOAA weather radio station.

 That station is known as WXM-55, which originates on a frequency of 162.550 MHz.

 This broadcast includes local and regional weather forecasts.

 Licensed to Grand Junction, Colorado, United States, the station is currently owned by State of Colorado Telecom Services.
