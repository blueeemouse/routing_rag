Sanam Chandra Palace Railway Halt

Sanam Chandra Palace Railway Halt is a railway halt located in Phra Pathom Chedi Subdistrict, Nakhon Pathom City, Nakhon Pathom.

 It is located 49.798 km from Thon Buri Railway Station.

 It is located near Sanam Chandra Palace, and used to have a royal pavilion, however this was moved to Hua Hin Railway Station.
