2008 Summer Paralympics medal table

The medal table of the 2008 Summer Paralympics ranks the participating National Paralympic Committees (NPCs) by the number of gold medals won by their athletes during the competition.

 The 2008 Paralympics was the thirteenth Games to be held, a quadrennial competition open to athletes with physical and intellectual disabilities.

 The games were held in Beijing, People's Republic of China, from 6 September to 17 September 2008.
