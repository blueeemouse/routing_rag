As I Am

As I Am is the third studio album by American singer and songwriter Alicia Keys.

 It was released on November 9, 2007, by J Records.

 Recording sessions for the album took place at various recording studios during 2005 to 2007.

 Production was handled primarily by Keys, Kerry "Krucial" Brothers, Jack Splash, and Linda Perry, with a guest contribution from musician John Mayer.
