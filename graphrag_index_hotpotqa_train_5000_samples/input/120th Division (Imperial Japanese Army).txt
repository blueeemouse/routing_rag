120th Division (Imperial Japanese Army)

The 120th Division (第120師団 , Dai-hyakunijū Shidan ) was an infantry division of the Imperial Japanese Army.

 Its call sign was the Push Forward Division (邁進兵団 , Maishin Heidan ) .

 It was formed 21 November 1944 in Dongning, Heilongjiang as a triangular division.

 The nucleus for the formation was the leftovers of 12th division .

 The division was initially assigned to the 3rd army.
