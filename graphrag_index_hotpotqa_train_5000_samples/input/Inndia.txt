Inndia

"Inndia" (stylized as "INNdiA") is a song recorded by Romanian singer Inna from her third studio album, "Party Never Ends" (2013).

 It was released on 11 October 2012 through Roton and features Romanian trio Play & Win.

 The track was written by Sebastian Barac, Radu Bolfea, Marcel Botezan and Joddie Connor, while production was handled by the first three under the name of Play & Win.

 "Inndia" is an Arabic and oriental-influenced song encompassing guiar accords and male vocals which complement Inna.
