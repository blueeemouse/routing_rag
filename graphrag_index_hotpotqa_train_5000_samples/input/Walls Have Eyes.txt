Walls Have Eyes

Walls Have Eyes is the fourth solo album released by British singer-songwriter Robin Gibb.

 It was released in November 1985 on EMI America Records in the US and Polydor Records throughout the rest of the world, and produced by Maurice Gibb and Tom Dowd.

 The two singles from the album, "Like a Fool" and "Toys", did not chart in the US and UK.

 Gibb did not release a solo album in eighteen years until 2003 with "Magnet".

 Unlike "Secret Agent" which contains dance numbers, this album contains mostly ballads.
