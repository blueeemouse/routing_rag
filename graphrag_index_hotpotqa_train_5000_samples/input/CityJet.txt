CityJet

CityJet is an Irish regional airline with headquarters in Swords, County Dublin.

 It operates its own scheduled services out of London City Airport and to a lesser extent Dublin Airport, where it has maintenance facilities.

 The airline also operates wet-lease services on behalf of Air France (until October 2017), Scandinavian Airlines and Brussels Airlines.

 Air France sold CityJet to "Intro Aviation" in May 2014.

 In March 2016 the airline was bought by founder Pat Byrne and other investors.
