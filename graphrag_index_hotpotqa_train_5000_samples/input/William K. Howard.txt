William K. Howard

William K. Howard (June 16, 1899 – February 21, 1954) was an American film director, writer, and producer.

 Considered one of Hollywood's leading directors, he directed over 50 films from 1921 to 1946, including "The Thundering Herd" (1925), "The Power and the Glory" (1933), "Fire Over England" (1937), and "Johnny Come Lately" (1943).
