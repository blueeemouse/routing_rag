Bolokada Conde

Moussa "Bolokada" Conde is a master drummer from Kissidougou, Guinea, expert of Malinke rhythms, and one of the world's foremost djembefolas.

 He joined the Les Percussions de Guinée to replace the legendary Noumoudy Keïta as their lead drummer.

 He has traveled and performed in major venues all over the world since 1996 and was featured in the IMAX movie "PULSE: a Stomp Odyssey."

 Since 2004, he has been performing and teaching in the United States.

 He has conducted percussion workshops in many cities in the US and Europe.

 He has released two musical CD's, "Morowaya" and "Sankaran".

 He stars in the DVD "M'bemba Fakoli: A Musical Journey Through Guinea" and has released the djembe instructional DVD "M'bara".

 He is the subject of an upcoming documentary, "Bolokada Conde—Malinke Village Djembefola".

 He was awarded immigrant status as an alien with extraordinary ability in the arts in 2007.
