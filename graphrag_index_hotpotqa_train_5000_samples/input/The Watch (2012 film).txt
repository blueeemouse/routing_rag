The Watch (2012 film)

The Watch (previously known as Neighborhood Watch) is a 2012 American science fiction comedy film directed by Akiva Schaffer and written by Jared Stern, Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg.

 It stars Ben Stiller, Vince Vaughn, Jonah Hill and Richard Ayoade.

 The film follows Evan (Stiller), Bob (Vaughn), Franklin (Hill), and Jamarcus (Ayoade), a group of neighbors who form a suburban neighborhood watch group.

 When they uncover an alien plot threatening the world, they are forced into action.
