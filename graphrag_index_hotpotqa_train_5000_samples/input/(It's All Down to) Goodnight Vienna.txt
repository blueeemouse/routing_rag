(It's All Down to) Goodnight Vienna

"(It's All Down to) Goodnight Vienna", an up-tempo John Lennon composition, is the title track to Ringo Starr's 1974 album "Goodnight Vienna".

 The final song on the album is "Goodnight Vienna (reprise)".

 Also released as a single, the single version is a medley combination of "(It's All Down to) Goodnight Vienna" and "Goodnight Vienna (reprise)".

 The single was released in the US on 2 June 1975.
