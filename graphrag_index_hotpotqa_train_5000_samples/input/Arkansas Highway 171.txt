Arkansas Highway 171

Highway 171 (AR 171, Ark. 171, and Hwy.

 171) is a designation for three state highways in Southwest Arkansas.

 One route of 1.2 mi runs from US Highway 67 (US 67) to Wine Dot Road near an industrial facility.

 A second route of 12.9 mi begins at Highway 84 in Malvern and runs east to Lake Catherine State Park.

 A third route of 4.91 mi begins at US Highway 270 (US 270) and runs north, with state maintenance ending at Tigre Mountain Road.

 All routes are maintained by the Arkansas State Highway and Transportation Department (AHTD).
