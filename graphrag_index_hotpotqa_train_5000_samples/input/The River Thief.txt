The River Thief

The River Thief is a 2016 American teen crime drama written, directed, and co-produced by best-selling novelist N.D. Wilson.

 The film stars Joel Courtney as Diz, a roving burglar who takes a shine to a small town waitress, Selah, played by Raleigh Cain.

 The film follows Diz as he tries to steal the affection of Selah and deal with the fallout from his biggest heist yet.

 The cast includes Paul Johannson, Bas Rutten, and Tommy Cash.
