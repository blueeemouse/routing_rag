Pakistan Navy

The Pakistan Navy (Urdu: ‎ ; "Pɑkistan Bahri'a" ) (reporting name: PN) is the naval warfare branch of the Pakistan Armed Forces, responsible for Pakistan's 1046 km of coastline along the Arabian Sea, and the defence of important civilian harbours and military bases.

 The Pakistan Navy came into the existence after the independence of Pakistan in 1947.

 The President of Pakistan serves as the Supreme Commander of the Navy under Article 243 (2) of the Constitution of Pakistan, and the Chief of Naval Staff heads the Navy.

 Navy Day is celebrated on 8 September in commemoration of the Indo-Pakistani War of 1965.
