Sítio do Picapau Amarelo (comics)

Sítio do Picapau Amarelo was a Brazilian comic book series based on the eponymous novel series Sítio do Picapau Amarelo by Monteiro Lobato.

 It was originally released in 1977 on the success of the TV show produced by Rede Globo, comic books were published by the publisher RGE who would later become Editora Globo.

 It continued to be sold until 1979 when the character Emília now has her own comic replacing the Sítio do Picapau Amarelo, but the comic only lasted a year being sold, and in 1981 the Sítio do Picapau Amarelo returned to be published.

 The comic being published remained until 1984, years before the TV series is no longer in production.
