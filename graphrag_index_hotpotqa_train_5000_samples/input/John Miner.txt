John Miner

Composer John Miner may be best known for his rock opera Heavens Cafe, which was staged at the Flamingo Theater in Las Vegas 1996, the Charleston Performing Arts Center in 1997, and later at Insurgo Theater in Los Angeles in 2004.

 Miner formed the progressive rock group Art Rock Circus to perform the music with a live band on stage alongside the singers and actors.

 Investor Mike Lewis was instrumental in financing and staging Heavens Cafe.

 The Tributary Music Label released a live CD of Heavens Cafe to the progressive rock community in 2000.
