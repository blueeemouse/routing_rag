Here Come the Girls (concert tour)

Here Come the Girls was a co-headlining concert tour by American recording artists Anastacia and Chaka Khan and Scottish recording artist Lulu.

 The tour, primarily reaching Europe, began in November 2009.

 Described as a music extravaganza that mixes high octane, high camp, get up and dance songs, against a backdrop of glamour and fun, the trek became a hit, selling out all 20 dates in 2009.

 Due to demand, the tour continued into 2010, with English singer Heather Small replacing Chaka Khan.
