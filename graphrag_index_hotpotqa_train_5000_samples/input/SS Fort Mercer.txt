SS Fort Mercer

On February 18, 1952, "Fort Mercer" broke in two in a gale, 30 miles east of Chatham, Massachusetts.

 On the same day  , also a T2 tanker, broke up about 20 miles away.

 Daring rescues by the United States Coast Guard were carried out for both the bow and stern sections of "Fort Mercer" as well as "Pendleton"' s stern ("Pendleton"' s bow sank before any rescue could be mounted).

 Only five members of the "Fort Mercer" crew were lost, all from the bow.
