Donal C. Fox

Donal C. Fox (December 16, 1903 – August 15, 1987) was an American Democratic Party official who represented Essex County in the New Jersey State Senate from 1956 to 1964.

 Born in Amsbury, Massachusetts, he graduated from the University of Pennsylvania and Fordham Law School and practiced law in Newark.

 He served as the Deputy Attorney General of New Jersey, the First Assistant Essex County Prosecutor, the Chairman of the South Orange Planning Board, and as President of the Essex County Bar Association.
