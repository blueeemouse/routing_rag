Rogue Anthem

Rogue Anthem is an American Christian punk and Christian rock band, and they primarily play punk rock.

 This band was started, in 2012, with members vocalist, Bill B., lead guitarist, Neill B., guitarist, Carter, bassist and keyboardist, Vulcho, and drummer, Tanner.

 Their former lead vocalist and rhythm guitarist, Myke Augustat died in 2012.

 The band's first release, with Thumper Punk Records, "What to Believe", a studio album, released in 2012.
