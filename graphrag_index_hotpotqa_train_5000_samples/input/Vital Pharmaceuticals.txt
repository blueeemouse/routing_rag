Vital Pharmaceuticals

Vital Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (VPX Sports), which manufactures and distributes sports supplements under the brand name VPX, is a corporation located in United States, Florida founded in 1993 by Jack Owoc.

 Its market consists principally of fitness enthusiasts.

 The company produces sports nutrition supplements and performance beverages.

 Among VPX's better-known products are REDLINE Energy Drink, Power Rush and Bang.
