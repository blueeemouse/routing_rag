Islam: A Short History

Islam: A Short History is a 2000 book by the British writer Karen Armstrong, a former Roman Catholic nun and author of popular books about the history of religion.

 Armstrong condemns the West for being prejudiced about Islam since the Crusades, yet writing about present-day Muslims.

 Armstrong says that when they look at Western society, "they see no light, no heart, no spirituality."
