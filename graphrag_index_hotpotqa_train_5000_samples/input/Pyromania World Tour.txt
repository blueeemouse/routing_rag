Pyromania World Tour

"Pyromania World Tour" was a concert tour by British rock band Def Leppard which was the support tour for their third album, Pyromania.

 This was the first tour without Pete Willis, who left the band in the middle of the recording sessions of the album.

 Phil Collen replace him in the tour and finish the recording of Pyromania.

 This was also the first tour of the band as headliners in North America, they played in mid-size arenas, large theaters and stadiums around the United States and Canada.

 They would also perform at the Rock Pop Festival in Dortmund, Germany on the 17 and 18 December dates with Ozzy Osbourne, Iron Maiden, Scorpions, Judas Priest and Quiet Riot.
