Battlestar Galactica (1978 TV series)

Battlestar Galactica is an American science fiction television series, created by Glen A. Larson, that began the "Battlestar Galactica" franchise.

 Starring Lorne Greene, Richard Hatch and Dirk Benedict, it ran for the 1978–79 season before being canceled.

 Afterward, a write-in campaign revived the show as "Galactica 1980" with 10 episodes in 1980.
