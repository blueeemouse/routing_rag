Lovely (The Primitives album)

Lovely is a 1988 studio album by The Primitives.

 It features the international hit single "Crash", later featured in remix form as 'The 95 mix' on the "Dumb and Dumber" soundtrack, as well as the UK Top 100 hits "Stop Killing Me", "Thru the Flowers", and "Out of Reach". "

Way Behind Me" was issued as a single after the album's initial release, and later included on re-releases as well as on the follow-up album "Pure".
