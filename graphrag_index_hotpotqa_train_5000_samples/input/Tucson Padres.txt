Tucson Padres

The Tucson Padres were a minor league baseball team representing Tucson, Arizona in the Pacific Coast League (PCL).

 They were the Triple-A affiliate for the San Diego Padres.

 The team moved to Tucson from Portland, Oregon for the 2011 season.

 In April 2014, the team moved to El Paso, Texas and changed their name to the El Paso Chihuahuas.
