Doctor Eggman

Dr. Ivo "Eggman" Robotnik (ドクター・エッグマン , Dokutā Egguman , ロボトニック "Robotonikku") is a fictional character and the main antagonist of Sega's "Sonic the Hedgehog" series.

 His original character designer was Naoto Ohshima, who created the character as part of many design choices for the company's new mascot.

 After the creation of Sonic the Hedgehog, Ohshima chose to use his previous egg-shaped character to create the antagonist of the 1991 video game, "Sonic the Hedgehog", as well as making him become the archenemy of the series' titular main character.
