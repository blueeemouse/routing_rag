3-Hydroxyaspartic acid

3-Hydroxyaspartic acid (three letter abbreviation: Hya) also known as "beta"-hydroxyaspartic acid is derivative of aspartic acid which has been hydroxylated at position-3.

 The image to the right shows L-"threo"-3-Hydroxyaspartate.

 The Hya amino acid residue is sometimes contained in EGF-like domains such as Vitamin K-dependent coagulation plasma proteins including protein C.
