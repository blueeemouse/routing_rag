Ruxton Park

Ruxton Park is a park in Manitou Springs, El Paso County, Colorado at 9078 ft in elevation.

 The Manitou and Pike's Peak Railway, which departs from the depot at 6,570 ft in elevation, climbs into Englemann Canyon along Ruxton Creek.

 It passes by the site of the Halfway Hotel and then Ruxton Park at mile marker 3 on the 8.9 mile trip to Pikes Peak summit.
