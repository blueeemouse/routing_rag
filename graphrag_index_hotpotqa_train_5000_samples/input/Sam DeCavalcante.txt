Sam DeCavalcante

Simone Rizzo "Sam" DeCavalcante (March 3, 1912 – February 7, 1997), known as "Sam the Plumber", was a member of the New Jersey Mafia.

 Claiming descent from the Italian royal family, DeCavalcante was nicknamed "The Count".

 The Kefauver hearings later named his crime family the DeCavalcante crime family since he was the boss of the family current to those hearings.
