Powder River Station-Powder River Crossing

The Powder River Crossing, officially known as Powder River Station-Powder River Crossing (48JO134 and 48JO801), is an abandoned settlement located on the east bank of the Powder River in southeast Johnson County about twenty-four miles east of Kaycee, Wyoming.

 It developed after a wooden toll bridge was built across the Powder River in 1877, at a site that was originally used as a ford.

 With crossing secured, a settlement developed here in the late 19th century, incorporating a stage stop on the Bozeman Trail.

 The site is notable for having well-preserved wagon ruts from the pioneer era.
