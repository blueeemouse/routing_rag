Live in Concert (2 Live Crew album)

Live in Concert is the fifth album by rap group, 2 Live Crew.

 This was 2 Live Crew's first and only Live album, and was also the only 2 Live Crew release under the Effect subsidiary label of Luke Records, a move that was deemed necessary for the company to be able to release additional 2 Live Crew material outside of their distribution deal with Atlantic Records, which was signed in 1990 – the same year they released "Banned In The U.S.A..
