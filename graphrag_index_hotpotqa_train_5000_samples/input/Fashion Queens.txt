Fashion Queens

Fashion Queens is an American fashion-based talk show that aired on Bravo.

 The series premiered on March 17, 2013, with a three-week trail run.

 "Fashion Queens" is hosted by Bevy Smith, Derek J, and Lawrence Washington.

 Derek J and Lawrence Washington have both made several appearances on "The Real Housewives of Atlanta".

 Following the series' three-week trial run in March 2013, the first season continued on April 14, 2013.

 The second season premiere on November 3, 2013, which coincided with the sixth season premiere of "The Real Housewives of Atlanta". "

 Fashion Queens" was filmed in New York City.
