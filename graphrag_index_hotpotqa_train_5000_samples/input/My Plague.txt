My Plague

"My Plague" is a song by American heavy metal band Slipknot.

 Produced by Ross Robinson, it was featured on the band's second studio album "Iowa" and released as the second single from the album on July 8, 2002.

 The single version is a remix by Terry Date known as the "New Abuse Mix", which was produced for the soundtrack to the film "Resident Evil".

 The song reached number 43 on the UK Singles Chart and was nominated for the Grammy Award for Best Metal Performance in 2003.
