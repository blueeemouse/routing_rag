Miss World 1991

Miss World 1991, the 41st edition of the Miss World pageant, was held on 28 December 1991 at the Georgia World Congress Center in Atlanta, Georgia, United States.

 The winner was Ninibeth Beatriz Leal Jiménez representing Venezuela.

 She was crowned by Gina Tolleson of the United States.

 Miss World 1991 was scheduled to be held in the Dominican Republic.

 Due to scheduling difficulties, Miss World was first moved to Puerto Rico, then to Atlanta, USA.

 Preliminary swimsuits in Miss World 1991 were held in South Africa.
