Steven Boyer

Steven Boyer is an American stage and television actor, comedian, and musician.

 He is best known for originating the role of Jason/Tyrone in "Hand to God" Off-Broadway and in the Broadway production.

 This role earned him a Tony nomination for Best Leading Actor in a Play.

 He plays the role of Dwayne Reed in the NBC sitcom "Trial & Error", with John Lithgow.

 He played a minor role in Martin Scorsese's "The Wolf of Wall Street" alongside Leonardo DiCaprio.

 He has also played several small parts in television, including "The Good Wife", "Law & Order", and "Orange Is the New Black".
