Scream If You Wanna Go Faster (song)

"Scream If You Wanna Go Faster" is a song recorded by British singer Geri Halliwell for her second album of the same name (2001).

 It was released on 30 July 2001 as the second single from the album by EMI Records.

 Written by Halliwell and Rick Nowels on the day of her first recovery meeting for bulimia, she described the song as "the things I did to avoid staying still and deal with my seemings".

 It is Halliwell's most rock-based song to date, and was inspired by her listening to Led Zeppelin the previous summer as encouraged by close friend, Robbie Williams.

 The song sampled Apollo 440's song "Stop the Rock".
