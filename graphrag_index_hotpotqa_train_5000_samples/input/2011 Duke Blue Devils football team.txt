2011 Duke Blue Devils football team

The 2011 Duke Blue Devils football team represented Duke University in the 2011 NCAA Division I FBS football season.

 The Blue Devils were led by fourth year head coach David Cutcliffe and played their home games at Wallace Wade Stadium.

 They are members of the Coastal Division of the Atlantic Coast Conference.

 The Blue Devils finished the season with a record of 3–9, 1–7 in ACC play to finish in last place of the Coastal Division.
