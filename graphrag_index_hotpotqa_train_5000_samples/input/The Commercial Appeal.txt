The Commercial Appeal

The Commercial Appeal (also known as the Memphis Commercial Appeal) is a daily newspaper of Memphis, Tennessee, and its surrounding metropolitan area.

 It is owned by the Gannett Company; its former owner, The E.W. Scripps Company, also owned the former afternoon paper, the "Memphis Press-Scimitar", which it folded in 1983.

 The 2016 purchase by Gannett of Journal Media Group (Scripps' direct successor) effectively gave it control of the two major papers in western and central Tennessee, uniting the "Commercial Appeal" with Nashville's "The Tennessean".
