Nouchka van Brakel

Nouchka van Brakel (born 18 April 1940 in Amsterdam) is a Dutch film director known for her 1982 movie "Van de koele meren des doods".

 That movie, and a movie about Eve (1979), established her as an important Dutch feminist film director.

 Van Brakel said that her ambition is to make movies about women who want to change their lives and their societies.
