Lanzarote

Lanzarote ( ; ] ) is a Spanish island, the easternmost of the autonomous Canary Islands in the Atlantic Ocean.

 It is located approximately 125 km off the coast of Africa and 1,000 km from the Iberian Peninsula.

 Covering 845.94 km2 , Lanzarote is the fourth-largest of the islands in the archipelago.

 With 141,938 inhabitants, it is the third-most populous Canary Island, after Tenerife and Gran Canaria.

 In the centre-west of the island is Timanfaya National Park, one of its main attractions.

 The capital is Arrecife.
