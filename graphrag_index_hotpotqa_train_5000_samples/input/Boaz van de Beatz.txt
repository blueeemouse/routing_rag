Boaz van de Beatz

Boaz de Jong (born December 15, 1988), better known by his stage name Boaz van de Beatz, is a Dutch music producer and DJ.

 He is the founder of the Nouveau Riche record label, which includes popular Dutch artists Mr. Polska and Jebroer.

 Boaz has helped launch the careers of many artists under his label, as well as his frequent collaborator Ronnie Flex and DJ trio Yellow Claw.

 Since 2013, Boaz has transitioned from mainly producing Dutch hip hop songs to releasing electronic music as a lead artist - he has released 2 EPs under the American dance label Mad Decent.
