Richard Bedford

Richard Bedford (born August 5) is a British singer-songwriter.

 He gained recognition for his vocal work in electronic dance music and trance including his collaborations with Above & Beyond on their 2011 album "Group Therapy", their 2006 album "Tri-state", Armin van Buuren's 2013 album "Intense" and IDMA-nominated singles "Alone Tonight" and "Sun & Moon".

 Richard was named best EDM vocalist in 2013 by theuntz.com and number 3 EDM vocalist by EDM.com in 2014.

 Richard also plays guitar, piano and drums and performed on Armin van Buuren's Armin Only World Tour 2013/2014.

 He is currently working on his artist album forthcoming on his own label Turning Stone Records.
