Signs (Five Man Electrical Band song)

"Signs" is a song by the Canadian rock group Five Man Electrical Band.

 It was written by Les Emmerson and popularized the relatively unknown band, who recorded it for their second album, "Good-byes and Butterflies", in 1970.

 "Signs" was originally released that year as the B-side to the relatively unsuccessful single "Hello Melinda Goodbye" (#55 Canada).
