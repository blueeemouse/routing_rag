Anne Marie Braafheid

Anne Marie Braafheid became the first black woman to attain the position of 1st runner-up in the Miss Universe contest.

 She was the second black woman to reach the top 15 finalists position, after Evelyn Miot of Haiti in 1962.

 She represented Curaçao in the 1968 pageant, and was featured in Ebony Magazine after achieving 1st runner-up status
