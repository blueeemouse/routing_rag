Family Life Network

The Family Life Network is a Christian radio network simulcasting via stations across Western and Central New York, as well as northern Pennsylvania from flagship station WCIK.

 It is owned and operated by the larger Family Life Ministries of Bath, New York.

 Founded in 1957, FLM is an accredited member of the Evangelical Council for Financial Accountability (ECFA).

 Family Life is a listener supported outreach with about 95% of its operating revenue coming directly from listeners, participants, and supporting churches.
