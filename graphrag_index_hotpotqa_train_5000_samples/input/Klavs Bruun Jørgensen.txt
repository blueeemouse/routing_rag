Klavs Bruun Jørgensen

Klavs Hørlykke Bruun Jørgensen (born3 April 1974) is a Danish handball player and coach for the Denmark women's national handball team.

 As a player, he led FCK Håndbold to the Danish championship in May 2008.

 He has previously played for many years by Danish Handball League rivals GOG, with whom he won two Danish titles.

 For a short period he played for German league side SG Wallau-Massenheim
