Trent Aegir

The Trent Aegir, also known as the Eagre, is a tidal bore on the River Trent in England.

 At certain times of the year, the lower tidal reaches of the Trent experience a moderately large bore (up to five feet (1.5m) high).

 It is said to take its name from "Ægir", the Norse god of the ocean, although this is disputed.
