June Brown

June Muriel Brown, MBE (born 16 February 1927) is an English actress, known for her role as Dot Cotton in the BBC soap opera "EastEnders" from 1985 onwards.

 In 2005, she won Best Actress at the Inside Soap Awards, and in the same year, also received the Lifetime Achievement award at the British Soap Awards.

 In 2009, she was nominated for the BAFTA TV Award for Best Actress, but lost out to Anna Maxwell Martin.

 She is only the second performer to receive a BAFTA nomination for their work in a soap opera (the first was Jean Alexander).
