2015–16 Auburn Tigers women's basketball team

The 2015–16 Auburn Tigers women's basketball team will represent Auburn University during the 2015–16 NCAA Division I women's basketball season.

 The Tigers, led by fourth year head coach Terri Williams-Flournoy, play their home games at Auburn Arena and were members of the Southeastern Conference.

 They finished the season 20–13, 8–8 in SEC play to finish in a 3 way tie for seventh place.

 They advanced to the quarterfinals of the SEC Women's Tournament where they lost to South Carolina.

 They received an at-large to the NCAA Women's Tournament where they defeated St. John's in the first before losing to Baylor in the second round.
