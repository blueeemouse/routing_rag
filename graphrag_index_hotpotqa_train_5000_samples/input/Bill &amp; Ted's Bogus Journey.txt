Bill &amp; Ted's Bogus Journey

Bill & Ted's Bogus Journey is a 1991 American science fiction comedy film, and the directing debut of Pete Hewitt.

 It is the second film in the "Bill & Ted" franchise, and a sequel to "Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure" (1989).

 Keanu Reeves, Alex Winter and George Carlin reprise their roles.

 The film's original working title was "Bill & Ted Go to Hell" and the film's soundtrack featured the song "Go to Hell" by Megadeth, which Dave Mustaine wrote for the film.

 Despite mixed reviews from film critics, like its predecessor, the film has since gained a cult following.
