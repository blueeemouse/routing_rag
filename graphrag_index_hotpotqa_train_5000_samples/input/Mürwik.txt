Mürwik

Mürwik (Danish: "Mørvig" ) is a community of Flensburg on the east side of the Flensburg Firth.

 It is on the Angeln peninsula.

 Mürwik is the location of the Naval Academy at Mürwik, which is the main academy that trains German Navy officers.

 Nazi Germany's final government, the Flensburg government, was located in Mürwik.
