Jeff Klinger

Jeff Klinger is an American comedian who lives in Los Angeles.

 Born on February 7 in Brooklyn, NY, he attended school in Salt Lake City.

 After moving to Chicago Jeff wrote, produced and directed several short films including Thanks Tony!

 The 7-minute-long satire about the quest for self-esteem went straight to the film festival circuit.

 Thanks Tony!

 premiered at the Chicago Comedy Shorts Film Festival.
