Joe Cheng

Joe Cheng, () (June 19, 1982) is a Taiwanese model, actor and singer.

 Although he started his career as a model, Cheng is well known for his role as Jiang Zhishu in Taiwanese drama serial version of Japanese manga "Itazura Na Kiss", "It Started with a Kiss".

 Cheng has gained much recognition as an actor in most parts of Asia, particularly Taiwan, China, Hong Kong, Singapore, Philippines and Japan.

 Cheng released his debut EP, "Sing a Song", in October 2009.

 The title of the EP as well as its title track is a play on the third word in his name, Chang (暢) as a homophone of the word “Sing” (唱) in Chinese.
