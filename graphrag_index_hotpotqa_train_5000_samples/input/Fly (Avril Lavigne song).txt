Fly (Avril Lavigne song)

"Fly" is a song by Canadian recording artist Avril Lavigne.

 It was released on April 16, 2015, for digital download by the Special Olympics, Inc.

 Lavigne released the song as a charity single for The Avril Lavigne Foundation to support the 2015 Special Olympics World Summer Games.

 The song was written by Lavigne, Chad Kroeger and David Hodges.

 All the proceeds from the single will be contributed to the ongoing work being done by the Avril Lavigne Foundation.
