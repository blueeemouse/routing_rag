Ute Lemper

Ute Lemper (born 4 July 1963) is a German singer and actress renowned for her interpretation of the work of Kurt Weill.

 Her numerous roles in musicals include playing Sally Bowles in the original Paris production of "Cabaret", for which she won the 1987 Molière Award for Best Newcomer, and Velma Kelly in the revival of "Chicago" in both London and New York, which won her the 1998 Olivier Award for Best Actress in a Musical.
