Dani Calabresa

Daniella Maria Giusti (born November 12, 1981) is a Brazilian comedian and actress.

 She is best known for being a cast member of the APCA award winner show Comédia MTV as well as the host, alongside the actor Bento Ribeiro, of Furo MTV, a show similar to "Saturday Night Live"'s "Weekend Update".

 She was married to the comedian Marcelo Adnet.

 They get divorced at 2017.
