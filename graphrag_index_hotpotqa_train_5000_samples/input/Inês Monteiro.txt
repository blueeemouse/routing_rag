Inês Monteiro

Inês Alexandra das Neves Monteiro (born 18 May 1980) is a Portuguese athlete who competes in middle and long distance track running, as well as road running and cross country.

 She started out as a cross country runner, winning a silver and a gold medal at the 1998 and 1999 European junior race.

 She has represented Portugal at the 2004 and 2008 Summer Olympics, and has competed twice at the World Championships in Athletics.

 She is a regular performer at the IAAF World Cross Country Championships, generally finishing around 30th place.
