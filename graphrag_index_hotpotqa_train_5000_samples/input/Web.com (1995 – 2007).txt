Web.com (1995 – 2007)

Web.com, Inc, formerly Interland, Interland was founded by Waldemar Fernandez and Ken Gavranovic in 1997 out of Atlanta Georgia.

 Mr. Fernandez the Chairman and largest shareholder resigned in 2000 shortly after Interland went public, Interland a provider of websites and web services to small businesses and consumers, based in Atlanta, Georgia.

 Web.com's services included do-it-yourself and professional website design, web hosting, e-commerce, web marketing, and e-mail.

 As of March 2007, there were approximately 166,000 paid hosting subscribers in 2007.
