Curious George: A Very Monkey Christmas

Curious George: A Very Monkey Christmas is a 2009 American animated television movie, distributed by PBS and produced by Imagine Entertainment and Universal Animation Studios.

 It was released on November 25, 2009.

 The movie is a holiday special featuring Curious George, as created in the children's books by Hans Augusto Rey and Margret Rey.

 It is shown every Christmas on PBS Kids.
