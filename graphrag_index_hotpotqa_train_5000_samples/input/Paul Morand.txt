Paul Morand

Paul Morand (March 13, 1888 – July 24, 1976) was a French author whose short stories and novellas were lauded for their style, wit and descriptive power.

 His most productive literary period was the interwar period of the 1920s and 1930s.

 He was much admired by the upper echelons of society and the artistic avant-garde who made him a cult favorite.

 He has been categorized as an early Modernist and Imagist.
