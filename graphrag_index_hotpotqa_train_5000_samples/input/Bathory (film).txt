Bathory (film)

Bathory (also released as "Bathory: Countess of Blood") is a 2008 historical drama horror film written and directed by Juraj Jakubisko.

 Filming began in December 2005, and the film was released in July 2008.

 It was Jakubisko's first English-language film and an international co-production between the cinemas of Slovakia, the Czech Republic, Hungary, and the United Kingdom.
