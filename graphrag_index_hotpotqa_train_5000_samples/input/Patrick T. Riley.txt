Patrick T. Riley

Patrick Thomas Riley (October 27, 1941 – March 10, 2015) was Michael Oakeshott Professor Emeritus of Political Science at the University of Wisconsin-Madison.

 He is notable for his translations of the political writings of Gottfried Leibniz and his research on social contract theory, the general will, and the history of universal jurisprudence.

 His first book, "Will and Political Legitimacy", offered "a critical exposition of social contract theory in Hobbes, Locke, Rousseau, Kant, and Hegel."

 In "Leibniz' Universal Jurisprudence", Riley detailed the social, moral, and political philosophy of Leibniz, arguing for the English-speaking world that Leibniz was the most important German philosopher before Kant.

 He has also written extensively on the general will of Rousseau and Kant's political philosophy.

 He was the editor of the "Cambridge Companion to Rousseau," and Leibniz's political writings for Cambridge University Press.
