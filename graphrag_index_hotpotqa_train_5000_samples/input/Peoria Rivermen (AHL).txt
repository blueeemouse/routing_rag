Peoria Rivermen (AHL)

The Peoria Rivermen was a professional ice hockey team in the American Hockey League.

 They played in Peoria, Illinois, USA at the Carver Arena.

 On June 14, 2013, it was announced that the team would relocate to Utica, New York after the 2012–13 AHL season, and be known as the Utica Comets.
