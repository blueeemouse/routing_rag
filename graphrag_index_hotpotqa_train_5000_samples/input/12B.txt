12B

12B is a 2001 Tamil romance film directed and co-written by Jeeva.

 The film featured Shaam in his debut along with Simran and Jyothika in the leading roles.

 Vivek, Moon Moon Sen and Sunil Shetty play other pivotal role in the film which had music composed by Harris Jayaraj.

 The film released in September 2001 to positive reviews and performed well at the box office.
