Kamuzu Central Hospital

Kamuzu Central Hospital is a teritary referral hospital in the Lilongwe, Malawi.

 It is estimated to have 600-1,000 beds, though the true number of patients always exceeds the number of beds.

 It serves approximately 5 million people.

 It has a partnership with the University of North Carolina in the United States and a partnership project, called MAGNET (Malawi German Networking for Capacity Building in Treatment, Training and Research at Kamuzu Central Hospital), with the Institute of Public Health of the University Hospital Heidelberg in Germany, the University Hospital Cologne and the University Hospital Bonn.
