Sweet Annie

"Sweet Annie" is a song recorded by American country music group Zac Brown Band.

 It was released in August 2013 as the fourth single from the group's third major-label album, "Uncaged".

 The song was written by Zac Brown, Wyatt Durrette, Sonia Leigh, and John Pierce.

 As of March 12, 2014 the single has sold 439,000 copies in the United States and has been certified Gold by the RIAA.
