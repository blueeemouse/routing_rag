Richard Dawson

Richard Dawson (born Colin Lionel Emm; 20 November 1932 – 2 June 2012) was a British-American actor and comedian, and a game show host and panelist in the United States.

 Dawson was well known for playing Corporal Peter Newkirk on "Hogan's Heroes", a regular panelist on the 1970s version of "Match Game" on CBS from 1973–78, and as the original host of the "Family Feud" game show from 1976–85, and again from 1994–95.
