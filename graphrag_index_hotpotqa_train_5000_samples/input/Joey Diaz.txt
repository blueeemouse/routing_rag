Joey Diaz

Jose Antonio "Joey" "CoCo" Diaz (born February 19, 1963) is a Cuban American stand-up comedian, actor and podcast host.

 Born in Cuba and raised in New Jersey, Diaz began his stand-up career in 1991.

 He had roles in the television series "My Name is Earl" and the films "The Longest Yard" and "Taxi".

 Since 2012, Diaz has hosted the podcast, "The Church of What's Happening Now".

 He is also a regular guest on "The Joe Rogan Experience".
