Bellamy (film)

Bellamy — known as Inspector Bellamy in the U.S. — is a French murder mystery film released in 2009.

 It is the last film of celebrated French director Claude Chabrol (who died the following year) and the only time he worked with star Gérard Depardieu.

 Chabrol said in an interview that the film is like a "novel that Simenon never wrote", a kind of "Maigret on vacation".
