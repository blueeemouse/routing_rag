For Your Lungs Only

For Your Lungs Only is the debut EP by the Chicago-based punk rock band Alkaline Trio, released May 26, 1998 through Asian Man Records.

 The song "Southern Rock" was re-recorded for their debut album "Goddamnit" later that year.

 All four tracks from the EP were reissued on the compilation album "Alkaline Trio" in 2000.
