Anna Maria Villani Scicolone

Anna Maria Villani Scicolone (born Anna Maria Villani; 11 May 1938) is the former wife of Romano Mussolini (26 September 1927–3 February 2006), the youngest son of Benito Mussolini.

 The couple had two daughters.

 After her divorce from Romano, she married heart surgeon Magid Tamiz in 1977.
