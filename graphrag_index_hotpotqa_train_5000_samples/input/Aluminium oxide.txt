Aluminium oxide

Aluminium oxide (British English) or aluminum oxide (American English) is a chemical compound of aluminium and oxygen with the chemical formula Al O .

 It is the most commonly occurring of several aluminium oxides, and specifically identified as aluminium(III) oxide.

 It is commonly called alumina, and may also be called aloxide, aloxite, or alundum depending on particular forms or applications.

 It occurs naturally in its crystalline polymorphic phase α-AlO as the mineral corundum, varieties of which form the precious gemstones ruby and sapphire.

 AlO is significant in its use to produce aluminium metal, as an abrasive owing to its hardness, and as a refractory material owing to its high melting point.
