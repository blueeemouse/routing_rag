Once Upon a Time (Tiwa Savage album)

Once Upon a Time is the debut studio album by Nigerian singer Tiwa Savage.

 It was released by Mavin Records and 323 Entertainment on July 3, 2013.

 The album was made available for purchase on iTunes a day before its official release.

 It features guest appearances from Don Jazzy, Flavour N'abania, Leo Wonder, Iceberg Slim, Sarkodie and General Pype.

 Don Jazzy, Tunji "Tee Billz" Balogun and Tiwa Savage executive produced the album, along with contributions from Warren "Oak" Felder, Sauce Wilson, Harmony Samuels, GospelOnDeBeatz, Del B, Raydar Ellis and Spellz.

 The album was supported by seven singles—"Kele Kele Love", "Love Me (3x)", "Without My Heart", "Ife Wa Gbona", "Folarin", "Olorun Mi" and "Eminado".
