Ache Records

Ache Records is an independent record label based in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada.

 It was started by musician Andy Dixon around 1999.

 Ache releases music for both Canadian and international acts.

 The first release on Ache Records was Hot Hot Heat's 1999 debut, "Hot Hot Heat", a four-song EP.

 Other notable artists to work with Ache include Death from Above 1979, Four Tet, Hrvatski, Konono N°1, and Matmos.
