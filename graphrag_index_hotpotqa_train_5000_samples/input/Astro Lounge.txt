Astro Lounge

Astro Lounge, released in 1999, is the second full length album from the Californian rock band Smash Mouth.

 It includes the single "All Star", arguably the group's most well-known song, which reached number four on the U.S. "Billboard" Hot 100.

 Several other singles were released from the album, including "Waste" and "Then the Morning Comes".
