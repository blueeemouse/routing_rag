Belinda O'Hooley

Belinda O'Hooley is a British singer-songwriter and pianist with Irish roots, who was born in Leeds and went to university in Huddersfield, where she is now based.

 Formerly a member of Rachel Unthank and the Winterset (now The Unthanks), she now records and performs with fellow Yorkshire singer-songwriter Heidi Tidow (pronounced "Tee-doe"), who is now also her wife, as O'Hooley & Tidow.
