Deutschland sucht den Superstar (season 6)

Deutschland sucht den Superstar Season 6 was the sixth season of Deutschland sucht den Superstar which is airing on the RTL network.

 This season's winner, Daniel Schuhmacher, was awarded a contract with Sony-BMG.

 The judges in this season are Dieter Bohlen, Nina Eichinger, and Volker Neumüller.

 This season is hosted by Marco Schreyl.

 The viewers chose the contestant's fates as they were able to call for their favorite contestant.
