La somnambule, ou L'arrivée d'un nouveau seigneur

La somnambule is a ballet-pantomime composed by Ferdinand Hérold and premiered on 19 September 1827 at the Académie Royale de Musique.

 The scenario was by Eugène Scribe, and it was choreographed by Jean-Pierre Aumer.

 This ballet was produced in 1827 at the height of a fashion for stage works incorporating somnambulism.

 The work was quite popular in Paris and inspired many more works incorporating somnambulism, including Vincenzo Bellini's well-known Italian opera "La sonnambula".
