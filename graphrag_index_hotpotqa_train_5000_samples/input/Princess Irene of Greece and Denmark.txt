Princess Irene of Greece and Denmark

Princess Irene of Greece and Denmark (Greek: Πριγκίπισσα Ειρήνη της Ελλάδας και Δανίας ; born 11 May 1942) is the youngest child of King Paul of Greece and his wife Frederika of Hanover.

 She is the younger sister of Queen Sofía of Spain and of the deposed King Constantine II of Greece.
