Darmera

Darmera peltata (Indian rhubarb or umbrella plant) is a flowering plant, the only species within the genus Darmera in the family Saxifragaceae.

 It is a slowly spreading rhizomatous perennial native to mountain streamsides in woodland in the western United States (southwestern Oregon to northwestern California), growing to 2 m tall by 1 m wide.

 The name "Darmera" comes after Karl Darmer, a Berlin horticulturist from 19th century.
