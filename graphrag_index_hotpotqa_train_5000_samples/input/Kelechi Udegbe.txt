Kelechi Udegbe

Kelechi Udegbe is a Nigerian film actor and voice over artist.

 He is best known for starring as the lead character in "Officer Titus".

 Since his screen debut in 2009, Kelechi has starred in several films and soaps including "Behind The Smile", "Ojuju", "", "Horn Free Day" and "Kpians: The Feast of Souls".
