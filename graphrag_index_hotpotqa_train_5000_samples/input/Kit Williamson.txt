Kit Williamson

Kit Williamson (born November 13, 1985) is an American actor and filmmaker, best known for his role as Ed Gifford on the final two seasons of AMC's "Mad Men" and for creating and starring in the Daytime Emmy Award-nominated LGBT dark comedy series "EastSiders".

 He was born and raised in Jackson, Mississippi and attended Interlochen Arts Academy, an arts boarding school in northern lower Michigan and Fordham University.

 He is openly gay and has been married to his "EastSiders" co-star John Halbach since February 27, 2016.
