Tell Me (Bobby Valentino song)

"Tell Me" is a song by American hip-hop artist Bobby V.

 It was released in August 2005 as the second single from his debut album "Bobby Valentino".

 The song sampled "For the World" composed by Tan Dun on the Hero soundtrack, starring Jet Li.

 British R&B singer Stevie Hoang's song No Games sampled from the same source.
