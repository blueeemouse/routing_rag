Peter Farrelly

Peter John Farrelly (born December 17, 1956) is an American film director, screenwriter, producer and novelist.

 The Farrelly brothers are mostly famous for directing and producing gross-out humor romantic comedy films such as "Dumb and Dumber", "Shallow Hal", "Me, Myself and Irene", "There's Something About Mary" and the 2007 remake of "The Heartbreak Kid".

 In addition to his extensive film career, Peter is also an acting board member of the online media company DeskSite.
