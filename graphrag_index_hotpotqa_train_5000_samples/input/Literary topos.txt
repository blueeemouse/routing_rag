Literary topos

Topos (from Ancient Greek: τόπος 'place' abbreviated from Ancient Greek: τόπος κοινός "tópos koinós", 'common place'; "pl."

 topoi), in Latin locus (from "locus communis"), referred in the context of classical Greek rhetoric to a standardised method of constructing or treating an argument.

 (See "topoi" in classical rhetoric.)
