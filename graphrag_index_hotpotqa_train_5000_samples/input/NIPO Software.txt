NIPO Software

NIPO Software is a major worldwide Market Research and Enterprise Feedback Management software provider.

 The company provides industrial Windows-based software for all aspects of market research.

 This encompasses questionnaire design, data collection, tabulations, overviews of fieldwork progress and costs along with advanced data analysis.

 The company is headquartered in Amsterdam, Netherlands and has branches in Hong Kong and Argentina.

 NIPO targets Market Research agencies around the world with software products for feedback / data collection, panel management, data processing, analysis, and reporting.
