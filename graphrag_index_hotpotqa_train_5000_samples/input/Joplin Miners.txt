Joplin Miners

The Joplin Miners were a minor league baseball team in Joplin, Missouri that played for 49 seasons between 1902 and 1954.

 Baseball Hall of Fame Inductees Mickey Mantle and Whitey Herzog played for Joplin.

 Professional baseball returned to Joplin and Joe Becker Stadium when the Joplin Blasters began play in 2015.
