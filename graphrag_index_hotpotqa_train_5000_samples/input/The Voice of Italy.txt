The Voice of Italy

The Voice of Italy was a reality singing competition and Italian version of the international syndication "The Voice" based on the reality singing competition launched in the Netherlands, created by Dutch television producer John de Mol.

 The inaugural season was in 2013 with the first episode airing on Rai 2 on March 7, 2013.

 The show is also broadcast via radio on Radio Rai and RTL 102.5.
