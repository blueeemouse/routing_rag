Podgorica

Podgorica ( ; Montenegrin Cyrillic: Подгорица; ] , lit. "

[area] under the hill") is the capital and largest city of Montenegro.

 The city was also called Titograd (Montenegrin Cyrillic: Титоград, ] ) between 1946 and 1992 when Montenegro was part of the Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (SFRY), in honour of Josip Broz Tito.
