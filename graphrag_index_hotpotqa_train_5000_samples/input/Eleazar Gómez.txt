Eleazar Gómez

José Eleazar Gómez Sanchez (] ; better known as Eleazar Gómez born May 29, 1986 in Mexico City, Mexico) is a Mexican actor.

 He is noted for his performance in Mexican telenovelas. Gómez is the brother of actress Zoraida Gómez and actor Jairo Gómez.

 He is a member of the Mexican pop band Eme 15.
