P. J. Hairston

Samuel Peterson "P. J." Hairston Jr. (born December 24, 1992) is an American professional basketball player who last played for the Rio Grande Valley Vipers of the NBA G League.

 He played college basketball for the University of North Carolina.

 He finished his sophomore season in 2013 and was eligible for the 2014 NBA draft.

 He was selected with the 26th overall pick in the 2014 NBA draft by the Miami Heat, and was later traded to the Charlotte Hornets in exchange for Shabazz Napier.
