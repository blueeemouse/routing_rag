Orlando Graham

Orlando Graham (born May 5, 1965 in Montgomery, Alabama), is an American former professional basketball player who was selected by the Miami Heat in the 2nd round (40th overall) of the 1988 NBA Draft.

 A 6'8" forward from Auburn University at Montgomery, Graham played in only one NBA season for the Golden State Warriors during the 1988-89 NBA season.

 He appeared in 7 games and scored a total of 8 points in his brief NBA career.

 Graham was the fifth ever draft pick in Miami Heat history.
