Tarun Bharat

Tarun Bharat is a Marathi newspaper based in Belgaum, India.

 It is the seventh-largest-selling Marathi daily newspaper in the country.

 It has 9 editions from in North Karnataka (Belgaum), East Maharashtra(Nagpur), South Maharashtra (Kolhapur, Sangli, Satara) Kokan (Sindhudurg and Ratnagiri), Mumbai (Started on 26, December, 2013) and Goa.
