Boomerang (Southeast Asia)

Boomerang is a cable and satellite television channel owned by Turner Broadcasting System, a unit of Time Warner and its main flagship channel of Cartoon Network Asia.

 The Southeast Asian version of Boomerang was launched in September 2005 with a lineup very similar to that of the US version.

 It started previously as a programming block on Cartoon Network from 2001 to 2005 during weeknights.

 It uses the same voice overs from the Australian feed.
