Cale Iorg

Garth Cale Iorg (born September 6, 1985) is a former minor league baseball player.

 He is the son of former Major League Baseball (MLB) player Garth Iorg and nephew of former MLB player Dane Iorg.

 Iorg played college baseball for the Alabama Crimson Tide.

 After his freshman season at Alabama, Iorg put his baseball career on hold to go on a Mormon mission to Lisbon, Portugal.

 He was drafted by the Detroit Tigers shortly before he returned from the mission.

 Iorg was ranked as the third best prospect in the Tigers organization by Baseball America following the 2008 season.
