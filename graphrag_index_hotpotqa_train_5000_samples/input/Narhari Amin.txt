Narhari Amin

Narhari Amin (born 5 June 1955) is a politician from Gujarat state of India.

 He is a member of Bharatiya Janta Party.

 Formerly he was affiliated Indian National Congress.

 He left the Indian National Congress on 5 December 2012 after he was denied a ticket to contest the December 2012 Legislative polls.

 On December 6, 2012, he joined the Bharatiya Janata Party in the presence of Narendra Modi.

 On the 29th of October 2013 he was made the vice president of the planning commission of Gujarat which is headed by Narendra Modi himself being its president.
