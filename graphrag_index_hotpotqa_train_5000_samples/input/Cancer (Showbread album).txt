Cancer (Showbread album)

Cancer is a science-fiction rock opera and the tenth studio album by American rock band Showbread.

 The album was released in 2012 through non-profit record label Come&Live!

.

 "Cancer" was produced by Rich Veltrop, who had previously co-produced Showbread's albums "No Sir, Nihilism Is Not Practical", "Age of Reptiles", "The Fear of God" and "Who Can Know It?

".

 This is the band's second album to be funded completely by fans and released as a free download without the assistance of a major record label.
