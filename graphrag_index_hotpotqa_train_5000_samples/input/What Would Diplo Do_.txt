What Would Diplo Do?

What Would Diplo Do?

 is an American comedy television series created by Brandon Dermer and James Van Der Beek.

 The series stars James Van Der Beek, Dillon Francis, Dora Madison Burge, Jamar Malachi Neighbors, Bobby Lee and H. Michael Croner.

 The series premiered on August 3, 2017, on Viceland.
