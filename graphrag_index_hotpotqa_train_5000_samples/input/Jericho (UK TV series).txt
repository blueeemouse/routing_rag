Jericho (UK TV series)

Jericho (also known as Jericho of Scotland Yard) is a British period crime drama series, first broadcast as a series of four episodes on ITV from October 16, 2005.

 The series was written and created by Stewart Harcourt, and starred Robert Lindsay as Detective Inspector Michael Jericho, a Scotland Yard detective who is loved by the public but embarrassed by his status as a hero.

 The series was set in London in 1958.

 The series was seen as an attempt to exploit ITV's success in period crime drama, best exemplified by "Foyle's War", and to rival the BBC's staple dramas such as "Waking the Dead".
