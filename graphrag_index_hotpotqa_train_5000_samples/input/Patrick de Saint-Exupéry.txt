Patrick de Saint-Exupéry

Patrick de Saint-Exupéry (born 1962) is the son of Count Jacques de Saint-Exupéry and the Countess de Saint-Exupéry, born as Martine d'Anglejan.

 Antoine de Saint-Exupéry, the aviator and writer, was the cousin of his grandfather.

 Patrick started his career in journalism at age 19 after winning a young reporters award.
