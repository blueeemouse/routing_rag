Nibris

Nibris was a video game development company located in Krakow, Poland, developing primarily for the Nintendo DS and Wii video game consoles. Nibris was most known for its cancelled Wii project "Sadness", a survival horror game.

 The company no longer exists; its official website closed in February 2010, and was reported later in October to have been transformed into a coordinator for the European Center of Games, ceasing game development permanently.

 Remaining Nibris staff and projects were also reported to have been handed over to Bloober Team, another game developer.
