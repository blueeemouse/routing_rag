Port of Tripoli (Lebanon)

The Port of Tripoli (Arabic: مرفأ طرابلس‎ ‎ ) is the 2nd major port in Lebanon.

 The port covers an approximate area of 3 km2 , with a water area of 2.2 km2 , and the land area composing of 320000 m2 , and a 420000 m2 dump area adjacent to the current port, reserved for the future Container Terminal and Free Market Zone.
