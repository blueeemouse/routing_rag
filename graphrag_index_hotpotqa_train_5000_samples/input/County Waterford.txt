County Waterford

County Waterford (Irish: "Contae Phort Láirge" ; the English name comes from Old Norse "Vedrafjörður") is a county in the South-East Region of Ireland, in the province of Munster.

 It is named after the city of Waterford, which is derived from the Old Norse name "Veðrafjǫrðr" or "Vedrarfjord".

 There is an Irish-speaking area, Gaeltacht na nDéise, in the south-west of the county.

 Waterford City and County Council is the local authority for the county.

 The population of the county at large, including the city, is 113,795 according to the 2011 census.
