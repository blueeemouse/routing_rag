CA module series

The CA module series is a series of two adventures for the "Dungeons & Dragons" role-playing game, designed to be compatible with the "Advanced Dungeons & Dragons" first edition. "

Swords of the Undercity" (CA1) was written for character levels 8–12 by Carl Smith, Bruce Nesmith, Douglas Niles and published in 1985. "

Swords of Deceit" (CA2) was written for character levels 10–15 by Stephen Bourne, Ken Rolston, Steve Mecca, and Michael Dobson and published in 1986.
