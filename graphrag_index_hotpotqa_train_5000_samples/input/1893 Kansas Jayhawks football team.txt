1893 Kansas Jayhawks football team

The 1893 Kansas Jayhawks football team represented the University of Kansas in the Western Interstate University Football Association (WIUFA) during the 1893 college football season.

 In their second and final season under head coach A. W. Shepard, the Jayhawks compiled a 2–5 record (2–1 against conference opponents), tied for the conference championship, and were outscored by all opponents by a combined total of 108 to 85.

 The Jayhawks played their home games at McCook Field in Lawrence, Kansas.

 A. R. Champlin was the team captain.
