Jagannatha Perumal temple

Jagannatha Perumal Temple (also called Tirumazhisai Temple is dedicated to Hindu god Vishnu located in Tirumazhisai, Tiruvallur district, in the South Indian state of Tamil Nadu.

 Constructed in the Dravidian style of architecture, dedicated to Vishnu, who is worshipped as Jagannatha Perumal and his consort Lakshmi as Thirumangaivalli.

 The temple is believed to be the birthplace of Thirumalisai Alvar, one of the twelve Azhwars whose work is compiled as "Divya Prabandha", the early medieval Tamil canon of the Azhwar saints from the 6th–9th centuries AD.
