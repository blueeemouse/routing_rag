Colder Weather

"Colder Weather" is a song performed by the Zac Brown Band, an American country music group.

 Lead singer Zac Brown co-wrote the song with Coy Bowles (the band's keyboardist and guitarist), Wyatt Durrette and Levi Lowrey.

 It is the band's seventh single release overall, and the second single from their 2010 album "You Get What You Give".
