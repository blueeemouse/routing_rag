Lucia Haršányová

Lucie Haršányová (born 27 August 1990) is a Slovak football defender who plays for German Bundesliga (women) side MSV Duisburg.

 She previously represented the Swiss club FC Neunkirch as well as SV Neulengbach in the Austrian ÖFB-Frauenliga and UEFA Women's Champions League.

 In summer 2015 Haršányová transferred from Neulengbach to FC Neunkirch of the Swiss Nationalliga A.
