Mustapha Haida

Mustapha Haida (born 12 October 1988) is a Moroccan Muay Thai kickboxer who competes in the middleweight and welterweight divisions.

 He fought for GLORY, SUPERKOMBAT, Kunlun Fight and Bellator Kickboxing.

 Haida holds notable wins over fighters like Andy Souwer, Enriko Kehl, Dzianis Zuev or Abdallah Mabel.
