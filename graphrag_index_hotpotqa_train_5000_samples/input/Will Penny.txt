Will Penny

Will Penny is a 1967 western film written and directed by Tom Gries starring Charlton Heston, Joan Hackett and Donald Pleasence.

 The picture was based upon an episode of the 1960 Sam Peckinpah television series "The Westerner" called "Line Camp," which was also written and directed by Tom Gries.

 Heston mentioned that this was his favorite film in which he appeared.

 The supporting cast features Ben Johnson, Bruce Dern, and Slim Pickens.
