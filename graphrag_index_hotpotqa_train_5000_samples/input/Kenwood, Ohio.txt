Kenwood, Ohio

Kenwood is a census-designated place (CDP) in Sycamore Township, Hamilton County, Ohio, United States.

 The population was 6,981 at the 2010 census.

 It is a major shopping destination for the Cincinnati area, featuring properties such as Kenwood Towne Centre and The Kenwood Collection.

 The All Saints Catholic Church and St. Vincent Ferrer Catholic Church (Cincinnati, Ohio)  serve Kenwood.
