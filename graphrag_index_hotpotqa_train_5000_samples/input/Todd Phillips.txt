Todd Phillips

Todd Phillips (born Todd Bunzl; December 20, 1970) is an American film director, producer, screenwriter and actor.

 He is best known for writing and directing "Road Trip" (2000), "Old School" (2003), "Starsky & Hutch" (2004), "The Hangover Trilogy" (2009, 2011, and 2013), "Due Date" (2010) and "War Dogs" (2016).
