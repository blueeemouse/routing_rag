Vedarajan temple, Thirunagari

Vedarajan Temple in Thirunagari, a village in Nagapattinam district in the South Indian state of Tamil Nadu, is dedicated to the Hindu god Vishnu.

 Constructed in the Dravidian style of architecture, the temple is glorified in the "Divya Prabandha", the early medieval Tamil canon of the Azhwar saints from the 6th–9th centuries AD.

 It is one of the 108 "Divyadesam" dedicated to Vishnu, who is worshipped as Vedarajan and his consort Lakshmi as Amruthavalli.

 The temple is believed to be the birthplace of Thirumangai Azhwar, one of the twelve Azhwar saints.
