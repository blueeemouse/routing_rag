USS LST-594

USS "LST-594" was an "LST-542"-class tank landing ship , built for the United States Navy during World War II.

 The "LST" stands for Landing Ship, Tank, while the "USS" stands for United States Ship.

 "LST-594" was part of the second wave of LST's, denoted LST Mk.2's, which began production in 1943, replacing the earlier Mk.1 version.

 Within the Mk.2 version, there were three classes: LST-1, LST-491 and LST-542.

 The USS "LST-594" was a member of the 542 class, which had several operational and armament upgrades over the prior classes.
