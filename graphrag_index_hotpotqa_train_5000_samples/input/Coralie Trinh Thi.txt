Coralie Trinh Thi

Coralie Trinh Thi (born April 11, 1976) is a former pornographic actress and French author, also known for writing and directing with Virginie Despentes the film "Baise-moi" (2000).

 During her career as a porn performer, she was generally just credited as Coralie.

 She won a Hot d'Or Award for Best European Actress in 1996, and she received a Hot d'Or Honorary Award in 2009.
