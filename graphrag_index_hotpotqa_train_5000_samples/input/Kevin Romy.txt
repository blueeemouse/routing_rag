Kevin Romy

Kevin Romy (born 31 January 1985) is a Swiss professional ice hockey centre who currently serves as captain of Genève-Servette HC of the National League (NL).

 He previously played for HC La Chaux-de-Fonds and HC Lugano.

 Romy was drafted by the Philadelphia Flyers in the 4th round (108th overall) in the 2003 NHL Entry Draft.

 Romy also represented Switzerland at the 2014 Winter Olympics.
