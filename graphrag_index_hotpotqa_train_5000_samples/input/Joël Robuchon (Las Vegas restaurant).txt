Joël Robuchon (Las Vegas restaurant)

Joël Robuchon is the signature, flagship French gourmet restaurant of French chef and restaurateur Joël Robuchon.

 The restaurant, located at the MGM Grand Las Vegas in Las Vegas, Nevada, has been rated 3 stars by the Michelin Guide, 5-stars by Forbes Travel Guide, and has been ranked by Wine Spectator and Travel and Leisure to be among the finest restaurants in the world.

 It was ranked as one of the Top 5 best restaurants in the United States by Gourmet magazine.
