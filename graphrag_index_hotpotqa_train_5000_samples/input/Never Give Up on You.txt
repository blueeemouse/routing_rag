Never Give Up on You

"Never Give Up on You" is a song performed by Welsh singer Lucie Jones, who previously appeared on series 6 of "The X Factor UK".

 The song was released on 27 January 2017 by ArtPeople, and represented the United Kingdom in the Eurovision Song Contest 2017.

 The song was written by Daniel Salcedo, Lawrie Martin, and Emmelie de Forest, the latter who won the Eurovision Song Contest 2013 with the song "Only Teardrops".

 A new version of the song, along with a music video, was released on 11 March 2017.
