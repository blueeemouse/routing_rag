2016 AFL Goal of the Year

The Australian Football League celebrates the best goal of the season through the annual Goal of the Year competition.

 In 2016, this is officially known as the Coates Hire AFL Goal of the Year.

 Each round three goals are nominated and fans are able to vote online for their favourite at the league's website.

 Eddie Betts of the Adelaide Football Club was the winner with his goal kicked in round ten against the Greater Western Sydney Giants.
