List of Fairy Tail episodes (season 4)

The fourth season of the "Fairy Tail" anime series was directed by Shinji Ishihira and produced by A-1 Pictures and Satelight.

 Like the rest of the series, it follows the adventures of Natsu Dragneel and Lucy Heartfilia of the fictional guild, Fairy Tail.

 It continues the story arc "Tenrōjima-hen" (天狼島編, lit.

 "Sirius Island Arc") from season 3, where Fairy Tail's S-Class examination on Sirius Island is interrupted by the arrival of Grimoire Heart, who attempt to retrieve the dark wizard Zeref living on the island.
