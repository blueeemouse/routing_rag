Gordon Ramsay at Claridge's

Gordon Ramsay at Claridge's was a restaurant owned by Gordon Ramsay and located with Claridge's hotel in Mayfair, London.

 Blackstone Group had enquired about Ramsay's availability to open a restaurant after he was awarded three Michelin Stars at Restaurant Gordon Ramsay.

 It marked the first of a number of restaurants that Ramsay would open at hotels owned by Blackstone.

 It opened in 2001, and by the fourth year was making a profit of £2 million a year.

 Mark Sargeant was the chef de cuisine of the restaurant until 2008, during which time the restaurant was awarded a Michelin star.
