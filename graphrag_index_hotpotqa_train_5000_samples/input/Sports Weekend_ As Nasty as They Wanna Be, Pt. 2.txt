Sports Weekend: As Nasty as They Wanna Be, Pt. 2

Sports Weekend: As Nasty As They Wanna Be, Pt. 2 is the sixth album overall and fifth studio album by the 2 Live Crew (released in 1991) and the sequel of "As Nasty As They Wanna Be".

 A clean version was released later that same year titled Sports Weekend: As Clean As They Wanna Be Part II and was the sequel of "As Clean As They Wanna Be".

 This would be the last studio album by all original members of the 2 Live Crew.
