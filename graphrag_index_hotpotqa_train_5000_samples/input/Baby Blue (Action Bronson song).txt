Baby Blue (Action Bronson song)

"Baby Blue" is a song by American rapper Action Bronson.

 It was released on March 3, 2015 as the fourth and final single off his second album "Mr. Wonderful" (2015).

 It features a guest appearance from fellow rapper Chance the Rapper.

 It was co-written by both artists, along with Zane Lowe (who co-wrote the chorus) and Mark Ronson, who also produced the song.

 A hip hop breakup song, "Baby Blue" is the closer to a three-part suite along with "City Boy Blues" and "A Light in the Addict" that's preluded by the album's interlude, "Thug Love Story 2017 The Musical".
