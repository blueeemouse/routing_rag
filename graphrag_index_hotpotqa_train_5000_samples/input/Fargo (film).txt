Fargo (film)

Fargo is a 1996 American black comedy crime film written, produced, edited, and directed by Joel and Ethan Coen.

 Frances McDormand stars as a pregnant Minnesota police chief investigating roadside homicides that ensue after a desperate car salesman (William H. Macy) hires two criminals (Steve Buscemi and Peter Stormare) to kidnap his wife in order to extort a hefty ransom from his wealthy father-in-law (Harve Presnell).
