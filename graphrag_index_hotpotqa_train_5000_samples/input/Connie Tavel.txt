Connie Tavel

Connie Tavel is an American television and film executive producer and talent manager.

 She is partners with Helen Hunt in Hunt/Tavel Productions, and with Tim Johnson in Tavel/Johnson Television.

 She has served as executive producer on films such as "Bill & Ted's Bogus Journey", "Fever", and "The Wishing Tree", as well as television movies such as "Ride with the Wind" and "Summer's End".

 She also produced an episode of the television series "Judging Amy", which she is credited as a co-creator.
