Necropolis (Horowitz novel)

Necropolis is a fantasy novel by British writer Anthony Horowitz.

 It is the fourth novel in his "The Power of Five" series.

 The book is set in London, Peru, Miami, Ukraine, Macau and Hong Kong.

 The book was released in the United Kingdom and Australia on 30 October 2008.

 It is a rewrite of the corresponding "Day of the Dragon", released 1989 in Horowitz's "Pentagram" series.

 A marked difference between original is evidenced by a lead character, Matt, being replaced with a female counterpart, Scarlett.

 According to Horowitz, this change resulted a complete rewrite of the original.

 Before Christmas 2008, the book had sold 190,000 copies worldwide.
