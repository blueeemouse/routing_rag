Walt Bowyer

Walter Nathaniel Bowyer Jr. (born September 8, 1960) is a former American football defensive end who played four seasons with the Denver Broncos of the National Football League (NFL).

 He was drafted by the Broncos in the tenth round of the 1983 NFL Draft.

 He played college football at Arizona State University and attended Wilkinsburg High School in Wilkinsburg, Pennsylvania.

 Bowyer was also a member of the Edmonton Eskimos of the Canadian Football League.
