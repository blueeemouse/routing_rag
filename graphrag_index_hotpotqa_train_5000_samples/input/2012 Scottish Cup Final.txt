2012 Scottish Cup Final

The 2012 Scottish Cup Final was the 127th final of the Scottish Cup.

 The match took place at Hampden Park on 19 May 2012 and was contested by the Edinburgh derby rivals, Hibernian (Hibs) and Heart of Midlothian (Hearts).

 It was Hibs' 12th Scottish Cup Final and Hearts' 14th.

 It was also the first time the clubs had met in a Scottish Cup Final since 1896.
