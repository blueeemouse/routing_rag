United States Armed Forces

The United States Armed Forces are the military forces of the United States of America.

 It consists of the Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Air Force, and Coast Guard.

 The President of the United States is the U.S. Armed Forces's overall head, and helps form military policy with the U.S. Department of Defense (DoD) and U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS), both federal executive departments, acting as the principal organs by which military policy is carried out.

 All five armed services are among the seven uniformed services of the United States.
