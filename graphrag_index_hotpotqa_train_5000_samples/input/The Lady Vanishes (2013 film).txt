The Lady Vanishes (2013 film)

The Lady Vanishes is a 2013 British television mystery thriller film directed by Diarmuid Lawrence, and a co-production of the BBC and Masterpiece Films.

 It is based on the novel "The Wheel Spins" by Ethel Lina White (1876–1944).

 It stars Selina Cadell in the key role of the disappearing Miss Froy, Tuppence Middleton as the young Iris Carr (who discovers the disappearance, but is not taken seriously), and Tom Hughes and Alex Jennings as Max Hare and the Professor, the two fellow English passengers who come to her aid.

 It was watched by 7.44 million when it was broadcast on 17 March 2013 on BBC One.
