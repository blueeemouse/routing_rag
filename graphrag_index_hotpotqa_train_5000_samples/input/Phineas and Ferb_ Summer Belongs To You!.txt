Phineas and Ferb: Summer Belongs To You!

"Phineas and Ferb: Summer Belongs to You!"

 is a special episode of the animated television series "Phineas and Ferb."

 and is part of the second season.

 It was aired on Disney XD on August 2, 2010 and Disney Channel on August 6, 2010.

 It is the first one-hour special, beating Phineas and Ferb's Christmas Vacation which is 45 minutes long with commercials.

 This episode was first seen at Comic-Con on July 25 from 3pm to 4pm in San Diego, California.
