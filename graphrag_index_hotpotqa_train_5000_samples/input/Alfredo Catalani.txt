Alfredo Catalani

Alfredo Catalani (19 June 1854 – 7 August 1893) was an Italian operatic composer.

 He is best remembered for his operas "Loreley" (1890) and "La Wally" (1892).

 "La Wally" was composed to a libretto by Luigi Illica, and features Catalani's most famous aria "Ebben?

 Ne andrò lontana."

 This aria, sung by American soprano Wilhelmenia Fernandez, was at the heart of Jean-Jacques Beineix’s 1981 cult movie "Diva".

 Catalani's other operas were much less successful.
