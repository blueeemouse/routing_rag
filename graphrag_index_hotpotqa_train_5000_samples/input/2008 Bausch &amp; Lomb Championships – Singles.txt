2008 Bausch &amp; Lomb Championships – Singles

The 2008 Bausch & Lomb Championships – Singles was the singles event of the 2008 Bausch & Lomb Championships, a WTA Tier II tennis tournament held in April.

 Tatiana Golovin was the defending champion, but withdrew from this year's event with injury.

 Maria Sharapova defeated Dominika Cibulková in the final to become the 2008 champion.

 This was Sharapova's 19th career singles title but the first she had won on clay courts.
