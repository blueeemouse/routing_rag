Lindsey Haun

Lindsey Haun (born November 21, 1984) is an American actress and singer.

 She is known for her role as Hadley on the HBO television series "True Blood".

 She was nominated for a Young Artist Award for her role in the 2000 Disney Channel original movie "The Color of Friendship" as Mahree Bok, and has starred in the film "Broken Bridges", for which she also recorded a portion of the soundtrack.
