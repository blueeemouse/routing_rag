Ex Girlfriend (band)

Ex Girlfriend was an American girl group whose line-up comprised Monica Boyd, Julia Robertson, Stacy Francis, and Tisha Hunter.

 Formed in 1989 in Brooklyn, New York by new jack swing group Full Force, the members adopted the surname "X" as part of their dynamic.

 They were signed to Reprise Records in 1990.

 Ex Girlfriend was launched into mainstream recognition following the 1991 release of their debut album, "X Marks the Spot", which contained the singles "Why Can't You Come Home" and "You (You're the One for Me)".

 Following the release of their second album "It's a Woman Thang", the group disbanded in 1994.

 In 2008, the group briefly reformed.
