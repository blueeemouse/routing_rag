Maddilapalem bus station

Maddilapalem Bus Station is a bus station located at the Central part of Visakhapatnam city.

 The bus station is owned by Andhra Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation (APSRTC).

 This is one of the minor bus stations in Andhra Pradesh.

 Many buses from other states like Odissa, Chhattisgarh and Telangana arrive at the station.

 Buses provide their services to all the towns nearby and also within the city as well.

 It is mainly used as city bus terminus.
