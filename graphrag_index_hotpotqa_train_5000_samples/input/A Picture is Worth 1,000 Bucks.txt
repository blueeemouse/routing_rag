A Picture is Worth 1,000 Bucks

"A Picture Is Worth 1,000 Bucks" is the 11th episode from the second season of the Fox animated series "Family Guy".

 It is the 18th episode of "Family Guy" to be aired.

 It guest-starred Candice Bergen as Murphy Brown, Faith Ford as Corky Sherwood and Charles Kimbrough as Jim Dial.

 Mila Kunis permanently takes over the role of Meg from Lacey Chabert beginning with this episode.
