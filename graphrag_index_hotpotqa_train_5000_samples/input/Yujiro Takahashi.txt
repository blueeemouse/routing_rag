Yujiro Takahashi

Yujiro Takahashi (高橋 裕二郎 , Takahashi Yūjirō , born January 13, 1981) , also known by his shortened ring name Yujiro (裕次郎 , Yūjirō ) is a Japanese professional wrestler trained by and currently performing for New Japan Pro Wrestling (NJPW), where he is a former NEVER Openweight Champion, NEVER Openweight 6-Man Tag Team Champion, IWGP Tag Team and Junior Heavyweight Tag Team Champion with former partner Tetsuya Naito, with whom he teamed as No Limit.

 He is also known for being the first non-gaijin member to ever join Bullet Club.
