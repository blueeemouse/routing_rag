Call My Name (Pietro Lombardi song)

"Call My Name" is a song recorded by Pietro Lombardi from his first studio album "Jackpot" (2011).

 The song is the debut single of the winner of the eighth season of Deutschland sucht den Superstar ("DSDS").

 It was written and produced by "DSDS" jury member Dieter Bohlen.

 The song was released on May 7, 2011.
