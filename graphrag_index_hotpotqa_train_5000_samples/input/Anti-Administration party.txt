Anti-Administration party

The "Anti-Administration party" (1789–1792) was an informal faction led by James Madison and Thomas Jefferson that opposed policies of then United States Secretary of the Treasury, Alexander Hamilton in the first term (1789–1792) of President George Washington.

 This was not an organized political party, but an unorganized faction.

 Most had been Anti-Federalists in 1788, meaning they opposed ratification of the Constitution of the United States.

 However, the situation was fluid, with members moving in and out.
