Sun Bowl

The Sun Bowl is an annual American college football bowl game that is usually played at the end of December in El Paso, Texas.

 The Sun Bowl, along with the Sugar Bowl and the Orange Bowl, are the second-oldest bowl games in the country, behind the Rose Bowl.

 Games are currently played at Sun Bowl Stadium on the campus of the University of Texas at El Paso, and since 2014 have featured teams from the Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC) and the Pac-12 Conference (Pac-12).
