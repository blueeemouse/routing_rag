Peter Gallo

Peter Gallo (born 1959 in Rutland, VT) is an artist and writer who lives and works in Hyde Park, VT.

 He received his Ph.D. and MA in Art History from Concordia University, Montreal, and has written about the intersection of biopolitics, medicalization, and artistic experience from the eighteenth to early twenty-first centuries.

 He has a BA from Middlebury College, Middlebury, VT.

 Additionally, the artist has been active in the Grass Roots Art and Community Efforts (GRACE) in Hardwick, VT.

 He has organized numerous exhibitions including "Insider Art," (GRACE traveling exhibition, 1990), and "Our Yard in the Future: The Art of Gayleen Aiken" (Horton Gallery, 2007).

 He has contributed criticism to "Art in America" and "Art New England", among others.

 The artist is represented by Horton Gallery in New York City and Anthony Reynolds Gallery in London, UK.

 Gallo's works have been featured in solo and group exhibitions in the United States and Europe, and are included in notable collections of contemporary art.
