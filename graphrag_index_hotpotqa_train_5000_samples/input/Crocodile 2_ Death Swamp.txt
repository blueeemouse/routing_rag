Crocodile 2: Death Swamp

Crocodile 2: Death Swamp, called Crocodile 2: Death Roll when broadcast on TV, is a 2002 horror film directed by Gary Jones and released directly to DVD on August 1, 2002.

 The film is a loose sequel to the 2000 film "Crocodile".

 It was filmed in Ramoji Film City in Hyderabad, India.

 The film features the two surviving crocodiles from the first film.
