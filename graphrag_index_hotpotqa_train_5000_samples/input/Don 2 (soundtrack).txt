Don 2 (soundtrack)

Don 2 is the soundtrack album composed by the trio Shankar-Ehsaan-Loy to the film of the same name directed by Farhan Akhtar starring Shahrukh Khan, Priyanka Chopra, Boman Irani, Om Puri, Lara Dutta and Kunal Kapoor.

 The album features nine tracks, and was released on 17 November 2011 by T-Series.
