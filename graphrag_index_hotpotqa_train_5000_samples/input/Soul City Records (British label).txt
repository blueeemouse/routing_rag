Soul City Records (British label)

Soul City was a British soul independent record label run by Dave Godin, David Nathan and Robert Blackmore, from a record shop of the same name in London.

 It is not to be confused with the US record label of the same name, run by the singer Johnny Rivers.

 Its first release was Don Gardener & Dee Dee Ford's "Don't You Worry" in March 1969.

 The label released a further 18 singles, the biggest hit being Gene Chandler's "Nothing Can Stop Me" which reached Number 41 in the UK Singles Chart.

 Soul City records were initially distributed by Island Records and then Philips Records.
