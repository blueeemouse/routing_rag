Mohamed Salmeen

Mohamed Ahmed Youssef Salmeen (Arabic: محمد احمد يوسف سالمين‎ ‎ ; born 4 November 1980) is a Bahraini footballer currently playing for Muharraq Club and the Bahrain national football team.

 He was the captain of the Bahrain National team.

 He wears the famous number 10 jersey for his club and country.

 Mohamed Salmeen is the son of legendary Bahraini footballing hero Ahmed Salmeen.

 He appeared in all three matches for Bahrain in 1997 FIFA U-17 World Championship.

 Salmeen has participated in three World Cup qualifying campaigns.
