Mind Your Manners (Pearl Jam song)

"Mind Your Manners" is a song by the American alternative rock band Pearl Jam.

 It was released on July 11, 2013 as a digital download as the lead single from their tenth studio album "Lightning Bolt".

 Writing for "The Globe and Mail", Brad Wheeler said the song was "lean, swift and punishing".

 Pearl Jam guitarist Mike McCready said "It's my attempt to try to make a really hard edge-type Dead Kennedys-sounding song".

 Singer Eddie Vedder's lyrics criticize organized religion, which Vedder considers hypocritical for their intolerance and "so many of the things which have come out of those organizations– like the abuse of children and then its cover-up."
