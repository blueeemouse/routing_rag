Vladimir Pavlovich Titov

Vladimir Pavlovich Titov (Russian: Владимир Павлович Титов ; 12 [O.S. 1807] March , selo Noviki, Spassky Rayon, Ryazan Oblast — 27 [O.S. 1891] September , Kharkov), better known under the pseudonym Tit Kosmokratov (Russian: Тит Космократов ), was a Russian writer, statesman, diplomat.

 As a writer he is best known for the novella "The Remote House on Vasilyevsky Island" (Уединённый домик на Васильевском), which was influenced by the writings of Aleksandr Pushkin.
