Spy Hard

Spy Hard is a 1996 American spy comedy film parody starring Leslie Nielsen and Nicollette Sheridan, parodying James Bond movies and other action films.

 The introduction to the movie is sung by comedy artist "Weird Al" Yankovic.

 It is the first film to be written by Jason Friedberg and Aaron Seltzer.

 The film's title is a pun of "Die Hard".

 The film is directed by Rick Friedberg and produced by Doug Draizin and Jeffery Knovitz.
