Incident management team

Incident management team (IMT) is a term used in the United States of America to refer to a group that responds to an emergency.

 Although the primary purpose of an incident management team is for wildfire response, an IMT can respond to a wide range of emergencies, including fires, floods, earthquakes, hurricanes, tornadoes, tsunami, riots, spilling of hazardous materials, and other natural or human-caused incidents.
