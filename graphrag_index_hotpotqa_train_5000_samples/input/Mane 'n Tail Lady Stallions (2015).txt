Mane 'n Tail Lady Stallions (2015)

The Mane 'n Tail Lady Stallions were a professional volleyball team in the Philippine Super Liga (PSL).

 It was one of two teams owned by Federated Distributors, Inc. and was the second team to play under the name "Mane 'n Tail Lady Stallions".

 The original Mane 'n Tail Lady Stallions was renamed Philips Gold Lady Slammers in 2015.
