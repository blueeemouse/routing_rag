Jack Horkheimer: Star Gazer (2010 season)

The 2010 season of the astronomy TV show starring Jack Horkheimer started on January 4, 2010.

 As a consequence of Jack Horkheimer's death on August 20, 2010, the episodes which were recorded for the last four months of 2010 were hosted by guest hosts.

 The episodes for the month of September of 2010 were the last to be based on scripts written by Jack Horkheimer.

 The show's episode numbering scheme changed several times during its run to coincide with major events in the show's history.

 The official Star Gazer website hosts the complete scripts for each of the shows.
