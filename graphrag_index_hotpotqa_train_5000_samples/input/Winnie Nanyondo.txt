Winnie Nanyondo

Winnie Nanyondo (born 23 August 1993 in Mulago) is a Ugandan middle distance runner.

 She has represented her native country in several important international events, including the 2016 Summer Olympics, the 2014 World University Cross Country Championships, the 2014 Commonwealth Games, the 2013 Summer Universiade, and the 2012 World Junior Championships in Athletics.
