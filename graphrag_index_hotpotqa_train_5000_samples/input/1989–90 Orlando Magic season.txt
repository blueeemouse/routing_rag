1989–90 Orlando Magic season

The 1989–90 NBA season was the Magic's 1st season in the National Basketball Association.

 Several years after local developer and banker Jim Hewitt began promoting the idea of an NBA franchise in Florida, he was awarded the Orlando Magic.

 His first move was to hire Philadelphia 76ers General Manager Pat Williams.

 Together, the two convinced the NBA to give Orlando a franchise after local fans made $100 deposits on season-ticket reservations.

 On November 4, a sold out crowd watched the Magic lose their first ever game at the Orlando Arena.

 The final score was 111–106 to the New Jersey Nets.

 On November 6, the franchise experienced its first victory as the Magic stunned the New York Knicks 118–110.
