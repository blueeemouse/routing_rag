Macon Whoopees (SHL)

The Macon Whoopees are a defunct professional ice hockey team that played in the Southern Hockey League (SHL) during the 1973–74 season.

 Based in Macon, Georgia, the team played its home games at Macon Coliseum.

 The Whoopees were affiliated with both the Houston Aeros and Cleveland Crusaders of the World Hockey Association.
