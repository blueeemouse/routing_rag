The Tom Green Show

The Tom Green Show is a North American television show, created by and starring Canadian comedian Tom Green, that first aired in September 1994.

 The series aired on Rogers Television 22, a community channel in Ottawa, Ontario, until 1996, when it was picked up by The Comedy Network.

 The second season began airing on December 4, 1998.

 (In 1996, Tom Green also produced a pilot episode for CBC Television, although the CBC did not pick up the series.)
