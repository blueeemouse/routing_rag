Kayak.com

Kayak.com, sometimes styled as KAYAK, is a fare aggregator and travel metasearch engine operated by The Priceline Group.

 Its products are available in 18 languages.

 The company also runs travel search engines checkfelix and swoodoo.

 Formerly a separate company, the KAYAK Software Corporation was acquired by The Priceline Group on May 21, 2013.
