Light It Up (soundtrack)

Light It Up (Music From and Inspired by the Movie) is the soundtrack to the 1999 drama film, "Light It Up".

 It was released on November 9, 1999 through Yab Yum/Elektra Records and consisted mainly of hip hop and R&B music.

 It features songs from Master P featuring The No Limit All Stars, featuring, Ja Rule, *NSYNC, 112, DMX, Jon B., OutKast featuring Slimm Cutta-Calhoun and more.

 The soundtrack was a huge success, peaking at #19 on the "Billboard" 200 and #4 on the Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums and featured one charting single, "How Many Wanna".

 On December 6, 1999, the soundtrack was certified gold by the RIAA.
