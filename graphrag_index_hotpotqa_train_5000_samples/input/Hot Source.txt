Hot Source

Hot Source is a Nine Network Australian, Canadian and British television program, aired on Channel Nine at 4pm on weekdays.

 The series targets the demographic of school-aged children to the age of fourteen.

 The show has gone through a number of hosts with Miranda Deakin and Dave Whitehill with it from beginning to end.

 Both have moved on to Saturday Lifestyle show, Saturday Afternoons.

 This showed aired in 2003-2006.
