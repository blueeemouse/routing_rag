Stanbic IBTC Holdings

Stanbic IBTC Holdings, commonly referred to as Stanbic IBTC, is a financial service holding company in Nigeria with subsidiaries in banking, stock brokerage, investment advisory, pension and trustee businesses.

 Stanbic IBTC Holdings is a member of the Standard Bank Group, a financial services giant based in South Africa.

 Standard Bank is Africa's largest banking group ranked by assets and earnings operations in 20 African countries and 13 countries outside Africa.
