Texas Tech Red Raiders basketball

The Texas Tech Red Raiders Basketball team represents Texas Tech University in Lubbock, Texas, United States in NCAA Division I men's basketball competition (the school's women's basketball team is known as the "Lady Raiders".)

 Until April 2016, the team was coached by Tubby Smith, who guided the team to the 2016 NCAA Tournament appearance, its first appearance in the event since 2007.

 With Smith's departure to coach the Memphis Tigers, the school hired former Arkansas-Little Rock coach Chris Beard on April 15, 2016.
