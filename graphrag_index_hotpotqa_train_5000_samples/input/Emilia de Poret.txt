Emilia de Poret

Emilia de Poret, born Emilia Andreen on August 10, 1976, in Luleå, Sweden, is a Swedish singer and songwriter.

 She made her debut under the name Lia Andreen with her first single "Mistreat Me (You’ll be sorry)" from the album "Back where I belong".

 The single was released in 2001, and reached 34th place on the Swedish single chart.

 Her second album, "A lifetime in a moment" reached 20th place on the Swedish album chart.
