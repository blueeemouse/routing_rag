Michael Carrick

Michael Carrick (born 28 July 1981) is an English professional footballer who plays for Manchester United, whom he also captains, and the England national team.

 Carrick primarily plays as a central midfielder, but he has been used as an emergency centre-back under Alex Ferguson, David Moyes and Louis van Gaal.

 Distinctive features of his play include his inventive distribution of the ball along with his passing and crossing abilities.

 He is one of the most decorated English footballers of all time.
