Apache Nugget Casino

The Apache Nugget Casino is located 15 miles north of Cuba, New Mexico, at the junctions of highway 550 and highway 537.

 The casino is operated by the Apache Nugget Corporation (ANC) which oversees all gaming activity for the Jicarilla Apache Nation.

 ANC is a for-profit Federally Chartered Section 17 Corporation owned by the Jicarilla Apache Nation.

 It is located in Dulce, New Mexico, the headquarters for the Nation.

 ANC has been in existence since 2003.
