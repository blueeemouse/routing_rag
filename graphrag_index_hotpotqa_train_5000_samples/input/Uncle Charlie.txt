Uncle Charlie

Uncle Charlie is the fourth studio album by Charlie Wilson and was released on February 17, 2009.

 It features production from Gregg Pagani, The Underdogs, Bigg D, T-Pain and L.O.S. da Maestro.

 It features guest appearances from Snoop Dogg, Jamie Foxx and T-Pain.

 The first two singles from the album are "Supa Sexxy" and "There Goes My Baby", the latter receiving major airplay on the Urban and Adult R&B radio stations as well being named Billboard Magazine's number one Urban Adult Song for 2009.

 It debuted and peaked at number two on the Billboard 200 with sales of 58,000 copies in its first week.

 This was his highest peak on the Billboard 200 to date and so far has sold 362,355 copies.
