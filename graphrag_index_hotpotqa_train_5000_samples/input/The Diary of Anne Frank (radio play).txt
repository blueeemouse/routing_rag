The Diary of Anne Frank (radio play)

Anne Frank: The Diary of a Young Girl is an original radio play by author Meyer Levin (1905–1981).

 It was adapted from Levin’s original stage dramatization of the same name, adapted from "The Diary of a Young Girl", Anne Frank's diary.

 It aired on CBS on September 18, 1952, the eve of Rosh Hashanah, to critical acclaim, and again in November 1952.
