Quest 64

Quest 64 is the game's title in North America.

 It was released as Holy Magic Century in Europe, Australia and New Zealand and as Eltale Monsters (エルテイル モンスターズ , Eruteiru Monsutāzu ) in Japan.

 The game is a single-player role-playing video game developed by Imagineer and published by THQ.

 It was released for the Nintendo 64 in 1998 and was the first role-playing video game released for the system in the United States.
