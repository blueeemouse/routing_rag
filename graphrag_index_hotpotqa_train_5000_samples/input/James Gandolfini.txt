James Gandolfini

James Joseph Gandolfini Jr. (September 18, 1961 – June 19, 2013) was an American actor and producer.

 He was best known for his role as Tony Soprano, an Italian-American crime boss, in the HBO crime drama "The Sopranos".

 He garnered enormous praise for his performance, winning three Emmy Awards, three Screen Actors Guild Awards, and one Golden Globe Award.
