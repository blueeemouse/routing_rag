List of companies of Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe is a landlocked sovereign state located in southern Africa, between the Zambezi and Limpopo Rivers.

 It borders South Africa to the south, Botswana and Namibia to the west, Zambia to the northwest, and Mozambique to the east and northeast.

 The capital and largest city is Harare.

 A country of roughly 13 million people, Zimbabwe has 16 official languages, with English, Shona, and Ndebele the most commonly used.
