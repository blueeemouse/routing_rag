Ted Billings

Ted Billings (April 7, 1880 – July 5, 1947) was an American character actor of the silent and sound film eras.

 Born in London, England on April 7, 1880, Billings made his film debut in the role of the Witch, in 1917's "The Babes in the Woods", which starred Francis Carpenter and Virginia Lee Corbin as Hansel and Gretel.

 Over the course of his career he would appear in over 100 films, mostly in unnamed, un-credited roles.
