Cold Fear

Cold Fear is a 2005 survival horror third-person shooter video game developed by Darkworks and published by Ubisoft for PlayStation 2, Xbox and Microsoft Windows.

 It was Ubisoft's first horror game, and Darkworks' second game, after "" in 2001.

 The game is centered on Tom Hansen, a member of the United States Coast Guard, who comes to the aid of a Russian whaler in the Bering Strait and finds a mysterious virus has turned the crew into zombie-like creatures.

 Discovering the involvement of both the Russian mafia and the CIA, Hansen sets out to ensure the virus doesn't reach land.
