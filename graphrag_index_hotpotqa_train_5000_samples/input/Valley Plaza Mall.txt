Valley Plaza Mall

The Valley Plaza Mall is a major retail shopping mall in Bakersfield, California.

 It is the largest mall in the San Joaquin Valley.

 The mall is owned by General Growth Properties, a real estate development of over 120 regional shopping malls around the United States.

 Valley Plaza Mall has 5 anchor stores and over 140 specialty stores.

 The mall is situated near SR 99 freeway, the city's main north/south freeway.
