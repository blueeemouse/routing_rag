Never Be the Same (Jessica Mauboy song)

"Never Be the Same" is a song recorded by Australian singer Jessica Mauboy.

 It was written by Mauboy, Anthony Egizii and David Musumeci, and produced by DNA.

 The song was released on 7 March 2014 as the fourth single from Mauboy's third studio album, "Beautiful".

 "Never Be the Same" is a mid-tempo pop and R&B power ballad that speaks about "the confusion of growing up and the fact that changes in life are inevitable."

 The song received positive reviews from critics, who noted it as an emotional and powerful track and praised Mauboy's vocal performance.
