Helen Glover (rower)

Helen Glover MBE (born 17 June 1986) is a British professional rower and a member of the Great Britain Rowing Team.

 Ranked the number 1 female rower in the world since 2015, she is a two time Olympic champion, triple World champion, quintuple World Cup champion and triple European champion.

 As of June 2016, she and her partner Heather Stanning are the World, Olympic, World Cup and European record holders, plus the reigning Olympic, World and European champions in the women's coxless pairs.

 She has also been a British champion in both women's fours and quad sculls.
