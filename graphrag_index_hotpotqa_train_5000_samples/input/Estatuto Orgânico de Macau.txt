Estatuto Orgânico de Macau

The Estatuto Orgânico de Macau (abbreviated EOM; Portuguese for Organic Statute of Macau) was an organic statute that provided for government in Portuguese Macau.

 Approved on February 17, 1976, the Portuguese legislation reclassified Macau as a "Chinese territory under Portuguese administration" ("território chinês sob administração portuguesa").

 On December 20, 1999, the "Estatuto Orgânico" was replaced by the Macau Basic Law, as the territory became a Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of China.
