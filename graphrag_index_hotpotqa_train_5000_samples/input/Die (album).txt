Die (album)

Die!

 is the sixth album by the rapper Necro.

 "Die!"

 was initially alluded to on Necro's MySpace profile as a "brand new solo album coming September 2009"; however, on August 13, the page's header was updated with an announcement of the album's title and its release date, which was put back to May 2010.

 "Die!"

 was Necro's first album since "Death Rap" in 2007 and was released by Psycho+Logical-Records.

 The album's release date was finalised when the album cover was released on March 25, 2010.
