WXM87

WXM87 is a NOAA Weather Radio station that serves portions of northeast Colorado, southwest Nebraska, and northwest Kansas.

 It broadcasts on NOAA Weather Radio channel 4, or 162.475 Megahertz.

 It is programmed from the National Weather Service forecast office in Goodland, Kansas with its transmitter located in Wray, Colorado.

 It broadcasts weather and hazard information for the following counties: Cheyenne County, Kansas, Dundy County, Nebraska, and Yuma County, Colorado.
