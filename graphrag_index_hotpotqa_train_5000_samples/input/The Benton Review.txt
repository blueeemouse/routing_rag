The Benton Review

The Benton Review is a weekly newspaper serving Benton, Jasper, Newton, Tippecanoe, Warren and White counties in Indiana.

 It began July 1875 as the "Benton Democrat"; by 1902 it was bought by George Roby as the "Benton Review", and he combined it with the "Fowler Leader" in 1914.

 The paper's masthead describes it as a combination of the "Fowler Leader" (founded 1893 by John P. Carr) and the "Fowler Republican".
