Bust of a Woman (Marie-Thérèse)

Bust of a Woman (Marie-Thérèse) is a 1931 sculpture by the Spanish artist Pablo Picasso that depicts Marie-Thérèse Walter, who was Picasso's mistress at the time of its creation.

 In 2016 the ownership of the sculpture was the subject of a legal dispute between the State of Qatar and the American art dealer Larry Gagosian, who had resold the bust to Leon Black.
