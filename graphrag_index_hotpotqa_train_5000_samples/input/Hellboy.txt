Hellboy

Hellboy is a fictional superhero created by writer-artist Mike Mignola.

 The character first appeared in "San Diego Comic-Con Comics" #2 (August 1993), and has since appeared in various eponymous miniseries, one-shots and intercompany crossovers.

 The character has been adapted into two live-action feature films in 2004 and that starred Ron Perlman in the title role, and two straight-to-DVD animated films, as well as three video games – "", "", and recently as a to-be-released playable character in "Injustice 2".
