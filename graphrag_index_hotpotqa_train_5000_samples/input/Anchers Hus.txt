Anchers Hus

Anchers Hus is the former residence of the painters Michael and Anna Ancher in Skagen, Denmark.

 They were both central figures in the artist colony of Skagen Painters who lived and worked in the town during late 19th and early 20th century.

 They lived in the house for five decades and in 1913 expanded it with a studio annex designed by Ulrik Plesner, another member of the colony.
