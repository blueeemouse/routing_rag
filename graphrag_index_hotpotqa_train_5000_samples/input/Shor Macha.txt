Shor Macha

"Shor Macha" (Urdu: شور مچا, literal English translation: "make noise") is a song by the Pakistani rock band Entity Paradigm.

 It is the overall seventh single release by the band, and also being the first single since their comeback in 2009.

 On August 13, 2010, Entity Paradigm released "Shor Macha" as the first single from their upcoming second studio album, the music video of the song was a dedication to all Pakistanis.

 The music video of the song was sponsored by djuice and directed by Bilal Lashari.
