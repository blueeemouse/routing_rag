Michelle McNulty

Michelle McNulty (born 1972), a former Las Vegas, Nevada review dancer, is today a 67th (2015) and 68th (2016) Emmy Awards winning producer of "The Voice".

 She was formerly the series’ 2013 Emmy Award winning casting director.

 She shared the 2015 and 2016 primetime Emmys for Outstanding Reality Competition Program with other production staff of "The Voice".
