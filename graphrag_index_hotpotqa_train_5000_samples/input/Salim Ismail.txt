Salim Ismail

Salim Ismail (born May 17, 1965 in Hyderabad, India) is a Canadian serial entrepreneur, angel investor, author, sought-after speaker, and technology strategist.

 He is best known as the Founding Executive Director of Singularity University and lead author of ""Exponential Organizations"".

 In March 2017, he was named to the board of the XPRIZE Foundation.
