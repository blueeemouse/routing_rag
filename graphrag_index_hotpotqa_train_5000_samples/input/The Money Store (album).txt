The Money Store (album)

The Money Store is the debut studio album by the group Death Grips.

 It was officially released on April 24, 2012, but was leaked to YouTube on April 14 and made available on vinyl on April 21 to celebrate Record Store Day.

 It was announced along with a second album, which would be titled "No Love Deep Web".

 It is the follow-up to their debut mixtape "Exmilitary".
