Monice

Monice (born Monika Ivkić; 6 June 1989 in Gradačac, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Yugoslavia) is a Bosnian-Austrian pop singer.

 She is best known for achieving fourth place in the fifth season of German casting show "Deutschland sucht den Superstar (DSDS)".

 Monice released her first album "I'm Gonna Make It" in 2009.

 After the end of "Deutschland sucht den Superstar", she had a specially published book released, that was sold in Germany and Austria.
