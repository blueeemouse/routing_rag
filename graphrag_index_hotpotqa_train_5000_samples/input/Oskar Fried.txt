Oskar Fried

Oskar Fried (August 1, 1871July 5, 1941) was a German conductor and composer.

 An admirer of Gustav Mahler, Fried was the first conductor to record a Mahler symphony.

 Fried also held the distinction of being the first foreign conductor to perform in Russia after the Bolshevik Revolution.

 He eventually left his homeland to work in the Soviet Union after the political rise of Adolf Hitler's Nazi Party, and became a Soviet citizen in 1940.
