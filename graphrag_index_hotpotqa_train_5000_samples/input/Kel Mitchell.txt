Kel Mitchell

Kel Johari Rice Mitchell (born August 25, 1978) is an American actor, stand-up comedian, dancer, musician, and rapper.

 He is known for his work as a regular cast member of the Nickelodeon sketch comedy series "All That", as the Invisible Boy in the 1999 Ben Stiller and Geoffrey Rush superhero satire film "Mystery Men", his portrayal of Kel Kimble on the Nickelodeon sitcom "Kenan & Kel", his role as Ed in the film and "All That" sketch "Good Burger", as the voice of Jacob in the Disney XD cartoon "Motorcity", and as Ray in the 2006 sequel to "Like Mike", "".

 He currently stars as Double G on the Nickelodeon series "Game Shakers".
