1978 FIFA World Cup Final

The 1978 FIFA World Cup Final was a football match played to determine the winner of the 1978 FIFA World Cup.

 The match was contested by hosts Argentina and the Netherlands, in the biggest stadium used in the tournament and in Argentina, the Estadio Monumental in the Argentine capital city of Buenos Aires.

 The match was won by the Argentine squad in extra time by a score of 3–1.

 Mario Kempes, who finished as the tournament's top scorer, was named the man of the match.

 The Netherlands lost their second World Cup final in a row, both times to the host nation, after losing to West Germany in 1974.
