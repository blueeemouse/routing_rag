Von Dutch

Von Dutch is an American multinational apparel brand named after Kenny Howard, aka "Von Dutch", an American artist and pinstriper of the Kustom Kulture movement.

 After Howard's death in 1992, his daughters sold the Von Dutch name to Michael Cassel and Robert Vaughn.

 The clothing brand gained popularity in the US and attracted the attention of celebrities such as Whitney Houston, Madonna, Britney Spears, Justin Timberlake, and later Ashton Kutcher and Eric Church.
