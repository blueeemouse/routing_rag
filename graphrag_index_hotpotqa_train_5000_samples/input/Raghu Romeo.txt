Raghu Romeo

Raghu Romeo is a 2003 Bollywood film directed by Rajat Kapoor, starring Vijay Raaz, Sadiya Siddiqui, Maria Goretti, Manu Rishi and Saurabh Shukla.

 The film released on 15 August 2003 to critical acclaim but was a big commercial failure.

 Set in modern-day Mumbai, the film focuses on the life of a waiter working in a dance bar.

 The film won the National Film Award for Best Feature Film in Hindi.
