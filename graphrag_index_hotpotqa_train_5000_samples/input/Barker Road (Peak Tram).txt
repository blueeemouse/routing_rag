Barker Road (Peak Tram)

Barker Road () is an intermediate station on the Peak Tram funicular railway line, and is located on Barker Road in the Peak, Central and Western District, Hong Kong, 363 m above sea level.

 The station opened in 1888 along with the tramline.

 Barker Road itself passes under the tramway at the uphill end of the station on a bridge.
