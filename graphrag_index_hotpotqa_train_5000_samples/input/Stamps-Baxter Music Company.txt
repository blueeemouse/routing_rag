Stamps-Baxter Music Company

The Stamps-Baxter Music Company was an influential southern music publishing company in the shape note gospel field.

 Virgil Oliver Stamps founded the company in 1924 and J. R. Baxter Jr. joined him to form the Stamps-Baxter Music Company, which was based in Dallas, Texas, with offices in Chattanooga, Tennessee and Pangburn, Arkansas.

 Stamps got his start working for the James D. Vaughan Publishing Company from which he got many of his business ideas.
