Yinma River

The Yinma River () is a 386.8-km-long tributary of the Second Songhua River in center Jilin province of China.

 The source of river is located in Yitong Manchu Autonomous County and flows generally from south to north across Panshi、Jiutai、Dehui and joins Second Songhua River at Kaoshan of Nong'an County.
