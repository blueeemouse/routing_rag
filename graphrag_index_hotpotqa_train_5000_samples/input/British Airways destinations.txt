British Airways destinations

British Airways is one of few carriers serving destinations across all six inhabited continents.

 Following is a list of destinations the airline flies to, as of 2017 ; terminated destinations are also listed.

 The list does not include cities served solely by affiliated regional carriers, and some terminated destinations may now be served either via franchise or through codeshare agreements with other carriers.

 Each destination is provided with the name of the country served, the name of the airport served, and both the International Air Transport Association (IATA) three-letter designator (IATA airport code) and the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) four-letter designator (ICAO airport code).
