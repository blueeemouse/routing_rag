The Blue Lagoon (1980 film)

The Blue Lagoon is a 1980 American romantic adventure drama film directed by Randal Kleiser and filmed on Turtle Island in Fiji.

 The screenplay by Douglas Day Stewart was based on the 1908 novel "The Blue Lagoon" by Henry De Vere Stacpoole.

 The film stars Brooke Shields and Christopher Atkins.

 The music score was composed by Basil Poledouris and the cinematography was by Néstor Almendros.
