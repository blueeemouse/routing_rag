Stephen R Lankton

Stephen R. Lankton, (born 29 May 1947) MSW, DAHB is the current editor of the "American Journal of Clinical Hypnosis" (2005–2017).

 He is a recipient of the American Society of Clinical Hypnosis' "Lifetime Achievement Award" and “Irving Sector Award for Advancement of the Field of Hypnosis”.

 as well as the Milton H. Erickson Foundation “Lifetime Achievement Award for Outstanding Contributions to the Field of Psychotherapy.”
