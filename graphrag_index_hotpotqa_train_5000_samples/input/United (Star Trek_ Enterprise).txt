United (Star Trek: Enterprise)

"United" is the 13th episode of the of the American science fiction television series "" and the 89th episode overall.

 It was first broadcast on the UPN network on February 4, 2005.

 It is the second of a three-part story which included the previous episode "Babel One", and the following episode, "The Aenar".

 "United" was written by Judith and Garfield Reeves-Stevens from a story idea by show runner Manny Coto.

 It was directed by David Livingston, his third of the season.
