Talmudical Institute of Upstate New York

The Talmudical Institute of Upstate New York (TIUNY) is an all male educational institution for high school and college age young men located in Rochester, New York.

 It is primarily an American, Lithuanian style Haredi but non-Hasidic yeshiva.

 TIUNY was founded in 1974 as the first external affiliate branch of the education network by Rabbi Menachem Davidowitz and Rabbi Dovid Harris.
