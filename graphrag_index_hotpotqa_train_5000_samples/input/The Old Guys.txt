The Old Guys

The Old Guys is a British sitcom that revolves around two ageing housemates: Tom Finnan (Roger Lloyd-Pack) and Roy Bowden (Clive Swift).

 The pair live across the street from Sally (Jane Asher), whom they both find attractive.

 Tom moved in with Roy after Roy's wife Penny deserted him.

 Baby boomer Tom has little in life but his daughter Amber (Katherine Parkinson), who is dating Sally's son Steve (Justin Edwards).

 Roy is a suburban pensioner who believes that he is one of the country's leading intellectuals.
