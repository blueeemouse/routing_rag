Bonnie Rotten

Bonnie Rotten is the stage name of Alaina Hicks (born May 9, 1993), an American pornographic actress, feature dancer, fetish model, director, and producer.

 She has been nominated for and has won many awards for her pornographic endeavors.

 In 2014, she became the first alt porn star to win the AVN Award for Female Performer of the Year.
