Stephanie Bellars

Stephanie Bellars (born January 25, 1976), known by her ring name Gorgeous George, is an American professional wrestling valet.

 She began her career in wrestling in 1999 as manager to her then-boyfriend "Macho Man" Randy Savage.

 She has worked for World Championship Wrestling (WCW), Total Nonstop Action Wrestling (TNA), and Extreme Championship Wrestling (ECW) under the names Gorgeous George, George Frankenstein, and simply George.
