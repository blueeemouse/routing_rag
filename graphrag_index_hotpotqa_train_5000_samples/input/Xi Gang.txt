Xi Gang

Xi Gang (); 1746-1803 was a renowned seal carver and painter in Qing Dynasty.

 He was born in Qiántáng (钱塘, present day Hangzhou).

 His style name was Chunzhang (纯章) and his pseudonym was Tiesheng (铁生).

 Other names include Hezhusheng (鹤注生) and Mengquan Waishi (蒙泉 外史).

 His specialty was in painting landscapes.

 His seal carving method was similar to that of Ding Jing (丁敬) (with whom he co-founded the Zhe School of carving), though somewhat cleaner and sharper.

 His style is representative of the Southern School.

 He was also talented in prose and calligraphy.
