Russia at the Universiade

Russia participated in 12 editions of the Universiade, debuting at the 1993 Summer Universiade.

 Since then, Russia has been at the top positions in the medal count, only three times being under the top three.

 The 2013 Summer Universiade was the best for Russia, beating the previous total medal and gold record of the 1973 Summer Universiade, of the then USSR.

 Furthermore, Russia produced a number of Universiade records, including the total medals won.
