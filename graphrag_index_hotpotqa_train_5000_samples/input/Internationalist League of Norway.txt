Internationalist League of Norway

The Internationalist League of Norway (Norwegian: "Forbundet Internasjonalen i Noreg" (FIN) is a group of revolutionary socialists, set up in 1999, working in sympathy with the Fourth International.

 Until 2002 FIN worked as a current within the Red Electoral Alliance advocating that the Red Electoral Alliance should be a broad party for revolutionaries on Norway, based on internationalism, anti-stalinism and internal democracy.

 Since 2002 FIN operates both within the Red Electoral Alliance and the Socialist Left Party.
