Epiphany: The Best of Chaka Khan, Vol. 1

Epiphany: The Best of Chaka Khan, Vol.

 1 is a compilation album of recordings by American R&B/funk singer Chaka Khan, first released on the Warner Bros.

 Records label in 1996.

 Although the compilation, which reached #22 on "Billboard"'s R&B chart and #84 on Pop, was given the "Vol.

 1" tag, it remains without a sequel to date.
