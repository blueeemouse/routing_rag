The Inbetweeners (U.S. TV series)

The Inbetweeners is an American comedy television series developed by Brad Copeland for MTV.

 The show stars Joey Pollari, Bubba Lewis, Mark L. Young, Zack Pearlman, Alex Frnka and Brett Gelman.

 The show is a remake of the original UK series of the same name written and created by Damon Beesley and Iain Morris, who serve as executive producers alongside Copeland, Aaron Kaplan and Lauren Corra.

 The show premiered on MTV on August 20, 2012.

 On November 28, 2012, MTV decided to cancel the show due to low ratings.
