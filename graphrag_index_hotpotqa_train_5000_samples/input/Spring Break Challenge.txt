Spring Break Challenge

Spring Break Challenge is a one-off MTV reality game show spun off from MTV's long-running reality game show, "The Challenge".

 The spin-off series was somewhat cast-contestant dependent on "The Challenge" in that the "Spring Break Challenge" used various contestants from the precursor program.

 (To date, "The Challenge" has only used one contestant from "Spring Break Challenge".)

 "Spring Break Challenge" is also cast-contestant dependent on MTV's two flagship reality programs, "The Real World" and the now cancelled series "Road Rules".

 As with "The Challenge", T. J. Lavin was the host of "Spring Break Challenge".
