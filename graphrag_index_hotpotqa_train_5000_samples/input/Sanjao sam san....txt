Sanjao sam san...

Sanjao sam san... (English: I dreamt a dream ) is the ninth studio album of Montenegrin singer Šako Polumenta, which was released on 10 July 2008.

 This album was recorded in Studio Ceca in Belgrade, the first before the studio's official opening on June 14 to coincide with its owner, Ceca Raznatovic's, birthday.

 Sako Polumenta invested more money into this project than in any other album he recorded.
