Rockaway, Queens

The Rockaway Peninsula, commonly referred to as The Rockaways or Rockaway, is the name of a peninsula on Long Island, New York.

 The Rockaway Peninsula is located entirely within the New York City borough of Queens.

 A popular summer resort area since the 1830s, Rockaway has become a mixture of lower, middle, and upper-class neighborhoods.

 Its relative isolation from the urban areas of the city, especially Manhattan, has traditionally made it a popular summer retreat.

 In the 2010s, it became one of the city's most quickly gentrifying areas.
