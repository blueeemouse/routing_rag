Christopher v. SmithKline Beecham Corp.

Christopher v. SmithKline Beecham Corp., 567 US _ (2012) is a US labor law case of the United States Supreme Court.

 It held that pharmaceutical sales representatives were not eligible for overtime pay.

 The court ruled in a majority opinion written by Justice Samuel Alito that sales representatives were classified as "outside salesmen" who are exempt from the Department of Labor's regulations regarding overtime pay.
