La tecnica cubana

"La técnica cubana", often abbreviated as "técnica", is a form of Cuban contemporary dance that was founded by Ramiro Guerra Suarez in Cuba in 1959.

 Unlike other forms of traditional Cuban dance, "técnica" fuses many different dance forms together, such as those from Africa, Europe, and North America.

 It is a highly expressive and robust dance form, incorporating many quick jumps and undulating movements of the torso and pelvis.

 "Técnica" blends a high amount of movement and expression with a degree of synchronization, producing an athletic, theatrical dance form.
