Lewis Wetzel

Lewis Wetzel (February 24, 1763 -1808) was a frontiersman and Native American fighter who roamed the hills of western Virginia and Ohio.

 Wetzel County, West Virginia, is named for him.

 Zane Grey, the great Western novelist, wrote about Wetzel in his books "Spirit of the Border", "Betty Zane", and "The Last Trail".

 Wetzel is regarded as a hero by many Americans, a lesser-known version of Daniel Boone.

 He was accused in 1788 of the murder of peaceful Native Americans, but he escaped and there was no trial.
