David Penn

David Penn is a corporate English magician from Northampton.

 He has been seen in the UK on four major TV series including The Sorcerer's Apprentice on BBC1, The Sorcerer's Apprentice Extra on BBC2, Freaky on Channel 4 and Astounding Celebrities on ITV1 .

 He reached the live semi -finals of Britain's Got Talent on ITV1.

 He has been described in the "Daily Telegraph" as "one of the country's leading illusionists".
