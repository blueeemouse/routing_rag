Minh Le

Minh Le (Vietnamese: Lê Minh, born June 27, 1977), also known by his online nickname Gooseman, is a Canadian video game programmer who co-created the popular "Half-Life" mod "Counter-Strike" with Jess Cliffe in 1999.

 He was later employed by Valve Corporation, the developers of "Half-Life", and worked for 8 years in Korea on the multiplayer first-person shooter "Tactical Intervention".

 He is currently a contractor on the multiplayer survival first-person shooter "Rust".

 In the small-team games that he has worked on, Le has been a programmer, modeler, and designer.
