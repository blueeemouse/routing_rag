It Takes a Family

It Takes a Family is a 2005 book by then Pennsylvania Senator Rick Santorum.

 The title is a response to the 1996 book "It Takes a Village" by then-First Lady Hillary Clinton.

 In the book, Santorum states that the family structure is necessary.

 He argues that liberal social policies have devastated the family structure and that prevailing socially liberal attitudes have systematically devalued families across the board.

 He argues that government should take a proactive role in promoting strong families.
