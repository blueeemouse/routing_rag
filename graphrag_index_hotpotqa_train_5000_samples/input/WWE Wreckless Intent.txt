WWE Wreckless Intent

WWE Wreckless Intent is a compilation album released by WWE on May 23, 2006.

 The album is the sequel to the previously released "WWF Forceable Entry", and like its predecessor it featured rock and metal artists performing versions of the wrestlers' entrance themes.

 A noted change with this album compared to the "Forceable Entry" album is the incorporation of rap and hip-hop artists (similar to the format used on the "WWF Aggression" album) doing versions of entrance themes as well as providing additional original tracks.
