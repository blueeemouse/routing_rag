High Road (album)

High Road is an album by Canadian folk rock band The Grapes of Wrath, released March 19, 2013 on Aporia Records.

 It is the band's first album of new material since "Field Trip" in 2000, and the first to feature all three of the band's original members since 1991's "These Days".

 The tracks "Good To See You" and "Take On The Day" had previously appeared on the band's compilation album "Singles", released in 2012.
