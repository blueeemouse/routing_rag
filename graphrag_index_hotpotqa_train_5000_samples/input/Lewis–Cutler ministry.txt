Lewis–Cutler ministry

The Lewis–Cutler ministry or First Lewis ministry was the 68th ministry of the New South Wales Government, and was led by the 33rd Premier, the Honourable Tom Lewis {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} , of the Liberal Party in coalition with the Country Party, led by the Honourable Sir Charles Cutler {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} .
