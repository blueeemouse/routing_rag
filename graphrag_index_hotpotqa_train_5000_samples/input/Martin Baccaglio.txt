Martin Baccaglio

Martin Hoppe "Marty" Baccaglio (born September 28, 1944) is a former American football defensive end.

 He played college football at San José State University and in high school at Novato High School.

 He played professionally in the American Football League for the San Diego Chargers in 1968 and for the Cincinnati Bengals 1968-1969.

 He also played for the Bengals of the National Football League in 1970.
