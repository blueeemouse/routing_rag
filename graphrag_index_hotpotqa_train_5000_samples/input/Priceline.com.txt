Priceline.com

Priceline.com is an American company and a commercial website that helps users find discount rates for travel-related purchases such as airline tickets and hotel stays.

 The company is not a direct supplier of these services; instead it facilitates the provision of travel services by its suppliers to its customers.

 It is headquartered in Norwalk, Connecticut, United States.

 Priceline.com is wholly owned by The Priceline Group.
