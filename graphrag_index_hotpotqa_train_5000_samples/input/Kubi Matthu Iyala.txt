Kubi Matthu Iyala

Kubi Matthu Iyala is a 1992 Indian Kannada comedy drama film directed and produced by Sadanand Suvarna and written by Poornachandra Tejaswi based on his own novel.

 The film featured Charuhasan , Raghubir Yadav, Lalithanjali and Vaishali Kasaravalli in the lead roles along with a large number of theater artistes in key supporting roles.

 The film's music was composed by L. Vaidyanathan and cinematography is G. S. Bhaskar.
