Kubert Leung

Kubert Leung 梁翹柏 is a musician and Cantopop songwriter in Hong Kong.

 He is one of the pioneers of Cantopop bands who has set up Life Exhibition and Ukiyo-e 「浮世繪」, two important Hong Kong bands in 1980s.

 Later, Kubert Leung became a main songwriter for singer Candy Lo.

 He has also written songs for Faye Wong, Hacken Lee, Miriam Yeung, Anthony Wong, Bibi Zhou among others.
