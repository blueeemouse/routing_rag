Abdul Latif Jameel

Abdul Latif Jameel Company Ltd (عبد اللطيف جميل, abbreviated ALJ) is a Saudi Arabia-based conglomerate active in 30 countries, with a focus on the Middle East, North Africa and Turkey (MENAT) region.

 In addition to a core business of automobile sales, manufacturing, assembly, and engineering, ALJ has operations in the areas of consumer financial services, real estate development, power generation, environmental services and consumer retail.

 It is one of the largest privately held companies in Saudi Arabia.
