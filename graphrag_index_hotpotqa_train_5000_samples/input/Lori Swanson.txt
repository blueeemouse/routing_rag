Lori Swanson

Lori Swanson (born December 16, 1966) from Eagan, Minnesota is the Attorney General of the US state of Minnesota.

 She was elected on November 7, 2006, and took office on January 2, 2007, succeeding Mike Hatch, who declined to run for re-election in order to run for governor.

 She is the first woman elected Minnesota's Attorney General.
