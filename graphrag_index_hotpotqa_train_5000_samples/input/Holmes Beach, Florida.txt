Holmes Beach, Florida

Holmes Beach is a city on Anna Maria Island in Manatee County, Florida, United States.

 The population was 4,966 at the 2000 census.

 As of 2004, the population recorded by the U.S. Census Bureau is 5,100.

 It is part of the Bradenton–Sarasota–Venice Metropolitan Statistical Area.

 The city occupies the central part of Anna Maria Island and is one of three municipalities on the island.

 The others are Bradenton Beach in the south and Anna Maria in the north.
