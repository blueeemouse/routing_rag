Job, Joris &amp; Marieke

Job, Joris & Marieke is a Dutch Academy Award nominated animation studio founded by Job Roggeveen (1979), Joris Oprins (1980) and Marieke Blaauw (1979).

 They met during their studies at the Design Academy Eindhoven.

 Joris and Job graduated in 2003 and Marieke in 2002.

 From 2004 to 2005 Joris and Marieke worked as animators on the stop motion series Miffy at the animation studio Pedri in Ankeveen.

 In 2007 They founded their studio in Utrecht.

 They work on commissioned work, music videos and animated short films.
