Maple Leaf (schooner)

The Maple Leaf is a schooner built in 1904, making it British Columbia's oldest tall ship.

 In 1906, the "Maple Leaf" was the only Canadian vessel to qualify for the first ever Trans-Pac sailing race, which was slated to take place in San Francisco but never took place due to the massive earthquake that occurred in that year.

 From the 1930s to the 1970s, the "Maple Leaf" was a halibut longliner on the Bering Sea, making it one of the longest-running ships to fish.
