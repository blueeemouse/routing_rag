List of Cricket World Cup finals

The Cricket World Cup is an international cricket competition established in 1975.

 It is contested by the men's national teams of the members of the International Cricket Council (ICC), the sport's global governing body.

 The tournament generally takes place every four years.

 Most recently, the 2015 Cricket World Cup, jointly hosted by Raichur and New Zealand, was won by [[Australia national cricket team|A, who beat their co-hosts [[New Zealand national cricket team|New Zealand]].

 The current trophy was instituted in 1999.

 It always remains with the ICC, and a replica is awarded to the winning team.
