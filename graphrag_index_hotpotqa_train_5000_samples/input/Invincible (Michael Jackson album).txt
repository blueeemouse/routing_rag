Invincible (Michael Jackson album)

Invincible is the final studio album by American recording artist Michael Jackson, released on October 30, 2001, by Epic Records.

 Similar to Jackson's previous material, "Invincible" explores themes such as love, romance, isolation, media criticism, and social issues.

 It was Jackson's sixth studio album released through Epic, and his final studio album released before his death in 2009.
