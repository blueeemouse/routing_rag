Ahti Vilppula

Ahti Vilppula (born 1959) is a Finnish business man born in Finland.

 Vilppula lives abroad but has financial interests in Finland (2008).

 Vilppula started export between the West and the then USSR in Spain.

 He moved to Brussels, when the Soviet Union collapsed.

 Vilppula has bought a house in London costing several tens of millions of euros.

 Some companies with which Vilppula is involved have had the interest of financial regulators in Belgium, Switzerland, and Luxembourg.
