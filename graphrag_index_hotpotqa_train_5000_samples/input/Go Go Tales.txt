Go Go Tales

Go Go Tales is an independent 2007 film by Abel Ferrara.

 Ferrara based the film on "The Killing of a Chinese Bookie", directed by John Cassavetes.

 It stars Willem Dafoe as a strip club owner and co-stars Bob Hoskins.

 Ferrara had the cast improvise much of their lines.

 He described the film as his "first intentional comedy".
