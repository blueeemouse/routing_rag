Johnny Frank Garrett's Last Word

Johnny Frank Garrett's Last Word is a 2016 film directed by Simon Rumley.

 It is a work of fiction based on the Jesse Quackenbush documentary "The Last Word", about the trial, conviction, and execution of a Texas man named Johnny Frank Garrett.

 It played at the 2016 South by Southwest Midnighters, Festival Favorites, Shorts Programs and Special Events lineup
