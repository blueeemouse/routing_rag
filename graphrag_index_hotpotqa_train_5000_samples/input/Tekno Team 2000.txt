Tekno Team 2000

Tekno Team 2000 was a professional wrestling tag team that competed in the World Wrestling Federation (WWF) from 1995 to 1996, composed of Travis and Troy (son of WWF writer Bill Watts).

 They started off with strong hype, but did not deliver on it, faltering after winning a few early matches.

 They were sent to the WWF's farm territory, United States Wrestling Association (USWA), for practice.

 After returning, they continued to lose matches and were soon released.
