Nelly (2004 film)

Nelly (French: "À ce soir" ) is a 2004 French drama film directed by Laure Duthilleul and starring Sophie Marceau, Antoine Chappey, and Fabio Zenoni.

 Written by Laure Duthilleul, Jean-Pol Fargeau, and Pierre-Erwan Guillaume, the film is about the four days following the death of a small town doctor, seen through the eyes of his wife, who is a nurse.

 The film was screened in the Un Certain Regard section at the 2004 Cannes Film Festival.
