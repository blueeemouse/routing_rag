Bitch (magazine)

Bitch is an independent, quarterly magazine published in Portland, Oregon.

 Its tagline is "a feminist response to pop culture".

 "Bitch" is published by the non-profit Bitch Media feminist media organization, dedicated to providing and encouraging an engaged, thoughtful feminist response to mainstream media and popular culture.

 The magazine includes analysis of current political events, social and cultural trends, television shows, movies, books, music, advertising, and artwork from a feminist perspective.

 It has about 80,000 readers.
