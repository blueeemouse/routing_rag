Texas–Arlington Mavericks men's basketball

The Texas–Arlington Mavericks men's basketball team is an NCAA Division I college basketball team competing in the Sun Belt Conference.

 Home games are played at College Park Center, located on the University of Texas at Arlington's campus in Arlington.

 The team appeared in the 2008 NCAA tournament, losing against the #1 seed Memphis in the first round, although Memphis was later forced to vacate the win due to infractions committed by the program.
