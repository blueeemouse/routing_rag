Sinners (1920 film)

Sinners is a lost 1920 American silent drama film based on a play of the same name by Owen Davis.

 The play was produced by William A. Brady and starred his daughter Alice Brady who also stars in this film.

 The Realart Company produced and released the film.

 Alice Brady's husband James Crane appears in this picture as well as in her next film, "A Dark Lantern".
