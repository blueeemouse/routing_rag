04 (Six by Seven album)

04 is the fourth album by English indie rock band Six by Seven.

 It was released in 2004, and the first on their own Saturday Night Sunday Morning Records label after being released from Beggars Banquet Records.

 It was also their first after bass player Paul Douglas left the band and found them trying to capture their sound – which had been recorded live in the studio on "The Way I Feel Today" – as a three-piece.
