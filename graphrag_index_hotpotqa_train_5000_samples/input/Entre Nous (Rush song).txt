Entre Nous (Rush song)

"Entre Nous" ("Between Us" in French) is the fourth track on the 1980 album "Permanent Waves" by progressive rock band Rush.

 It was also released as a single.

 The only live performances of the song to date occurred during the 2007 leg of the tour to promote "Snakes & Arrows", an album released on May 1 of that year.

 It was then featured on the live album released on April 15, 2008.
