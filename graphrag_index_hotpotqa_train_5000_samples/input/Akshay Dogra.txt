Akshay Dogra

Akshay Dogra (] ; born 21 September 1981) is an Indian actor and producer working in the television industry.

 He has worked on many shows during his career, both on-screen and off-screen.

 After working backstage at many shows, Akshay finally made his television debut in "12/24 Karol Bagh" on Zee TV.

 He was recently seen playing the role of Akash Singh Raizada in the Hindi daily soap opera "Iss Pyaar Ko Kya Naam Doon?
