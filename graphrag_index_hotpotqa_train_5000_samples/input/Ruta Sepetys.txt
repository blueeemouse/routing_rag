Ruta Sepetys

Ruta Sepetys or Rūta Šepetys (born November 19, 1967) is a Lithuanian-American writer of historical fiction.

 As an author, she is a #1 "New York Times" bestseller, international bestseller, and winner of the Carnegie Medal.Sepetys is a Rockefeller Foundation Bellagio Fellow and the first American writer of young adult literature to speak at European Parliament.

 Her work is published in over fifty countries and thirty languages and is currently in development for two films.
