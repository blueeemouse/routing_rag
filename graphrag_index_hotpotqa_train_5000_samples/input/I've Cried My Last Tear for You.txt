I've Cried My Last Tear for You

"I've Cried My Last Tear for You" is a song written by Chris Waters and Tony King, and recorded by American country music singer Ricky Van Shelton.

 It was released in February 1990 as the second single from his album "RVS III".

 The song became Shelton's seventh number-one single on the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles & Tracks (now Hot Country Songs) chart.
