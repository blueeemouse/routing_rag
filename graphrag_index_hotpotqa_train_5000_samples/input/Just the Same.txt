Just the Same

Just the Same is the second studio album by Canadian country music singer Terri Clark.

 It was released in late 1996 on Mercury Records.

 In the U.S., this album produced the singles "Poor Poor Pitiful Me" (a cover of a Warren Zevon song which was also a Top 40 pop hit for Linda Ronstadt), "Emotional Girl", and the title track, which respectively reached #5, #10 and #49 on the country charts.

 In Canada, the first two singles were both #1 on the country charts, while the title track was a #16.

 The fourth single, "Something in the Water", reached #39 in Canada, but did not chart in the U.S.

 The album was certified platinum by the RIAA.
