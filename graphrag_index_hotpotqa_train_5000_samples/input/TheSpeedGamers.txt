TheSpeedGamers

TheSpeedGamers (also abbreviated as TSG or TSG.TV) is a group of video gamers based out of Arlington, Texas, who raise money for various charities.

 They accomplish this by streaming live marathons of video games on Twitch.tv (formerly Justin.tv).

 The group was founded by Britt LaRiviere in early 2008 and still hosts marathons regularly through the year.

 Viewers can interact with the commentators and other viewers via chat on Twitch.tv.

 The group has raised over $720,000.00 for more than 20 different charities.
