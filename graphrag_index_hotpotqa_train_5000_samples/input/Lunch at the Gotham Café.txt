Lunch at the Gotham Café

"Lunch at the Gotham Café" is a short story by Stephen King.

 It originally appeared in the 1995 anthology "Dark Love" (edited by Nancy A. Collins, Edward E. Kramer and Martin H. Greenberg).

 In 1997, it was published in the limited-edition collection "Six Stories".

 In 2002, it was included in King's collection "Everything's Eventual", with a scene from the story featured on the cover of the collection's first edition dust jacket.
