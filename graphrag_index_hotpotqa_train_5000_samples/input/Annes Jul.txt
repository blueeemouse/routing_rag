Annes Jul

Annes Jul is a 2006 studio album released by Danish singer Anne Gadegaard.

 The title means ""Anne's Christmas"", and the album is a collection of Christmas themed songs.

 The album consists of ten songs, four of which were released as singles: "Peek-A-Boo", "Glædelig Jul Til Dig Fra Mig", "Når Det Bli'r Midnat" and "Tid Til Du Skal Tilgi".

 The album was later rereleased with "Gi Mig Alt", "Kukkelu" and "Min Veninde" added as bonus tracks.

 In 2008, plans to release another album were cancelled, leaving "Annes Jul" the last Anne Gadegaard studio album to date.
