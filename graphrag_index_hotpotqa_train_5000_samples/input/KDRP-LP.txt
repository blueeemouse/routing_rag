KDRP-LP

KDRP-LP (103.1 FM) "Sun Radio" is a radio station licensed to Dripping Springs, Texas, United States.

 The station is currently owned by Principle Broadcasting Foundation and rebroadcasts on 100.1 FM / 1490 AM Austin, 106.9 FM in Fredericksburg, 88.9 FM in Johnson City, 88.1 FM Gonzales, 99.9 FM San Marcos, 104.1 FM Wimberley, and coming soon to Llano and Mason.
