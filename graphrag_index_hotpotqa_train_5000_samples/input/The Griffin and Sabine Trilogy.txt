The Griffin and Sabine Trilogy

The Griffin and Sabine Saga is a series of bestselling epistolary novels written by Nick Bantock.

 The first three novels in the series, "Griffin and Sabine", "Sabine's Notebook" and "The Golden Mean", form the original "Griffin and Sabine Trilogy" and were first published in 1991, 1992 and 1993 respectively.

 Each story is told through a series of letters and postcards between the two main characters, Griffin Moss and Sabine Strohem.

 Every page features a facsimile of a postcard or a letter actually enclosed in an envelope.
