Feels (song)

"Feels" is a song recorded and produced by Scottish DJ Calvin Harris.

 It features vocals from American singer-songwriters Pharrell Williams, Katy Perry and American rapper Big Sean.

 It was released on 16 June 2017 through Sony Music as the fourth single from his fifth studio album "Funk Wav Bounces Vol.

 1" (2017), after "Slide", "Heatstroke" and "Rollin".

 Starrah assisted the artists in writing the song.
