DFB-Pokal (women)

The DFB-Pokal or Frauen DFB-Pokal is the main national women's football cup competition in Germany, thus the female counterpart to the DFB-Pokal.

 It was created in 1980, and since 1991 includes Eastern teams as well.

 The most recent champions are VfL Wolfsburg.

 FFC Frankfurt has won the most titles with nine.

 The final has, with the exception of the 1983 final, always been held on the same day prior to the men's final.

 Since 1985 the final has thus been held in Berlin.

 In 2010 the final will for the second time be held in a different city, Cologne, as a test to move the final permanently to a different place than the men's final.
