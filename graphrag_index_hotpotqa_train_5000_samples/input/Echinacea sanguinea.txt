Echinacea sanguinea

Echinacea sanguinea (sanguine purple coneflower) is a herbaceous perennial native to open sandy fields and open pine woods and prairies in eastern Texas, southeastern Oklahoma, Louisiana, and southwestern Arkansas.

 It is the southernmost "Echinacea" species.

 The specific epithet "sanguinea", which is Latin for "blood", refers to the color of the petals.
