DWFB

DWFB Radyo Pilipinas Laoag is the broadcast outlet of the government-controlled Philippine Broadcasting Service in Laoag City.

 This station serves Laoag City, the province of Ilocos Norte and its neighboring provinces.

 Its studios and offices are located at the Mariano Marcos State University-College of Teacher Education Campus in Laoag City, while its transmitter is located at the main campus of the Mariano Marcos State University in Batac City.

 The station airs from 4:45 AM to 10 PM on weekdays and 6 AM to 10 PM on weekends.
