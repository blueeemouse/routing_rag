Manhunt: Unabomber

Manhunt: Unabomber is an American drama miniseries created by Andrew Sodroski.

 The series stars Paul Bettany, Jane Lynch, Sam Worthington, Katja Herbers, Chris Noth, Michael Nouri and Brían F. O'Byrne.

 The series depicts a fictionalized account of the true story of the FBI's hunt for the man known as the Unabomber.

 The miniseries premiered on August 1, 2017, on Discovery Channel.
