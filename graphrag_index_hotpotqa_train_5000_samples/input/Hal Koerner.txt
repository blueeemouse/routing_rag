Hal Koerner

Hal Koerner (born January 23, 1976 in Morgantown, WV) is an American distance runner specializing in ultramarathon running.

 He is the owner of a specialty running store, Rogue Valley Runners, located in the mountainous Southern Oregon town of Ashland.

 He is one of the subjects of JB Benna's feature-length documentary "Unbreakable: The Western States 100".
