We Got the Power (Gorillaz song)

"We Got the Power" is a song by alternative rock virtual band Gorillaz, featuring Jehnny Beth of British post-punk band Savages and backing vocals from former Oasis guitarist and songwriter Noel Gallagher and American rapper D.R.A.M., who also appears on the group's single "Andromeda".

 The song was released on 23 March 2017.

 It was released as the second single from their fifth studio album "Humanz".
