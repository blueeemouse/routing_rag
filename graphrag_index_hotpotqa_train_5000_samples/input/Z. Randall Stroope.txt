Z. Randall Stroope

Z. Randall Stroope (born Zane Randall Stroope, October 25, 1953) is an American composer, conductor, and lecturer.

 He has published more than 165 works, with: Oxford University Press, Carl Fischer, Alliance Music Publishing, Walton, Colla Voce, and Lorenz.

 Among his most famous works are: Lamentaciones de Jeremias, Amor de mi alma, I Am Not Yours, Homeland, Go Lovely Rose, Song to the Moon, and Hodie!
