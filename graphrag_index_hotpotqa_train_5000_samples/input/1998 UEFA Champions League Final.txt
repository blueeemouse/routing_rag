1998 UEFA Champions League Final

The 1998 UEFA Champions League Final was a football match that took place at the Amsterdam Arena in Amsterdam, Netherlands on 20 May 1998 to determine the winner of the 1997–98 UEFA Champions League.

 It pitted Real Madrid of Spain and Juventus of Italy.

 Juventus appeared in their third consecutive final, while Real Madrid were in their first of the Champions League era.

 Real Madrid won 1–0, the only goal scored by Predrag Mijatović, to clinch their seventh European title, their first for 32 years.

 The repeat of the match was played in the 2017 UEFA Champions League Final in Cardiff, which Real Madrid won 4-1.
