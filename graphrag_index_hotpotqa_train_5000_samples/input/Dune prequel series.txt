Dune prequel series

The "Dune" prequel series is a sequence of novel trilogies written by Brian Herbert and Kevin J. Anderson.

 Set in the "Dune" universe created by Frank Herbert, the novels take place in various time periods before and in between Herbert's original six novels, which began with 1965's "Dune".

 In 1997, Bantam Books made a $3 million deal with the authors for three "Dune" prequel novels, partially based upon notes left behind by Frank Herbert, that would come to be known as the "Prelude to Dune" trilogy.

 Starting with 1999's "", the duo have published ten "Dune" prequel novels to date.

 In 2011 "Publishers Weekly" called the series "a sprawling edifice that Frank Herbert’s son and Anderson have built on the foundation of the original "Dune" novels."
