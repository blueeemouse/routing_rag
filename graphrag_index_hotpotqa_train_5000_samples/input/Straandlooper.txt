Straandlooper

Straandlooper is an animation studio and video game developer based in Donaghadee, Northern Ireland.

 They developed the episodic video game for iPhone, "" which is based on their character "Hector: Fat Arse of the Law".

 Straandlooper is notable for being Northern Ireland's first animation studio to produce a television series for the international market.

 As a video game developer, they are notable for being the first company where Telltale Games took on a publishing role, rather than a directing role.

 Telltale funded the development of the final two episodes of "Hector: Badge of Carnage" and published all three episodes on additional platforms, in addition to iOS.
