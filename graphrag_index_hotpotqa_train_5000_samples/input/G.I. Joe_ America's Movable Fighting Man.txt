G.I. Joe: America's Movable Fighting Man

G.I. Joe: America's Movable Fighting Man is a line of dolls produced by Hasbro.

 The initial product offering represented four of the branches of the U.S. armed forces.

 The term G.I. stands, in popular usage, for Government Issue and became a generic term for U.S. soldiers (predating the action figures), especially ground forces.

 The term originated in WWI, when much of the government-issued equipment was stamped "G.I.", meaning that it was made from galvanized iron.

 The development of G.I. Joe led to the coining of the term "action figure".
