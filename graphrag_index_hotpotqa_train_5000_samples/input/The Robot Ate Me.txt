The Robot Ate Me

The Robot Ate Me is an experimental indie rock band formed by Ryland Bouchard in 2002 which has been through many distinct phases incorporating aspects of folk, jazz, psychedelia and avant-garde rock.

 Their critically acclaimed albums alternated between accessible pop and obscure musical art projects.

 After releasing "Good World" in 2006 Babysue described the band as "one of the most unpredictable and obtuse underground bands around."
