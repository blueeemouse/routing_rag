Juul Haalmeyer

Juul Haalmeyer is a costume designer, best known for doing costume design on several iterations of SCTV.

 Haalmeyer also worked as a costume designer for many movies, television shows, theatre productions, concerts wardrobes for various artists and specials like "Bridge to Silence", "All My Sons", "Long Day's Journey into Night", "Noddy", the "Shining Time Station" Family Specials, "Mr. Conductor's Thomas Tales", "Diamonds", "", and "Ghostwriter".
