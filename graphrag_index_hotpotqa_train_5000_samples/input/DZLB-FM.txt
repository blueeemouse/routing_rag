DZLB-FM

DZLB-FM (97.4 MHz Los Baños) is a radio station owned and operated by the University of the Philippines Los Baños - College of Development Communication.

 Its studio and transmitters are located in the DZLB Broadcast Studio of UP Los Baños College, Los Baños, Laguna.

 It is the premiere college radio station in the Philippines.

 It started operation in the mid-90s.
