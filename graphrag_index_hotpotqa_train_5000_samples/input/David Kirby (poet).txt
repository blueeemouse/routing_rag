David Kirby (poet)

David Kirby (born 1944) is an American poet and the Robert O. Lawton Distinguished Professor of English at Florida State University (FSU).

 His most recent book is "Talking about Movies with Jesus," published in 2011 by LSU Press.

 His new and selected poetry collection, "The House on Boulevard St." ("Louisiana State University Press"), was nominated for the 2007 National Book Award in poetry.
