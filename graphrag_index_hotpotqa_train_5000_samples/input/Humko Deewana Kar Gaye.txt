Humko Deewana Kar Gaye

Humko Deewana Kar Gaye (translation: "You Made Me Crazy") is a 2006 Indian Hindi romantic drama film directed by Raj Kanwar, produced by Raj Kanwar, Bhushan Kumar & Krishan Kumar and starring Akshay Kumar and Katrina Kaif, Bipasha Basu, Anil Kapoor and Manoj Joshi also star in the movie in supporting roles.

 The film is inspired by British romantic movie "Notting Hill".

 The film is produced by the Indian music company, T-Series and Inderjit Films Combine.

 The movie's score and soundtrack is composed by Anu Malik with lyrics by Sameer.

 Himesh Reshammiya rendered a special song for the film which became extremely popular.

 The film released on 14 April 2006.

 The music of the film released on 2 February 2006.
