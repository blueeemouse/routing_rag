Christian Borle

Christian Dominique Borle (born October 1, 1973) is an American actor in theatre, television, and film.

 He is a two-time Tony Award winner for his roles as Black Stache in "Peter and the Starcatcher" and as William Shakespeare in "Something Rotten!

".

 Borle originated the role of Emmett in "Legally Blonde" on Broadway , as Tom Levitt on the NBC musical-drama television series "Smash" and as Marvin in the 2016 Broadway revival of "Falsettos".
