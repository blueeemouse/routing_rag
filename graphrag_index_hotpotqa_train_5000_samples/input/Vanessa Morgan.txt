Vanessa Morgan

Vanessa Morgan (born March 23, 1992) is a Canadian actress.

 She is known for her roles as Beatrix "Bird" Castro in the MTV teen drama series "Finding Carter", as Amanda Pierce in the Family teen sitcom "The Latest Buzz", and as Sarah in the Disney Channel/Teletoon original movie "My Babysitter's a Vampire" and the television series of the same name.
