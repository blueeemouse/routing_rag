Chris Brennan

Chris "The Westside Strangler" Brennan (born October 12, 1971) is an American mixed martial artist who competes in the Lightweight division.

 He has competed for the UFC, PRIDE, King of the Cage and Shooto.

 He is a former King of the Cage Middleweight Champion and former King of the Cage Middleweight Superfight Champion.

 He is also one of 18 fighters to compete in the -77 kg division at the ADCC Submission Wrestling World Championships in 2005.

 Chris was also the founder of Nogi Industries, a Mixed martial arts clothing company.

 In 2014 Chris was inducted in to the Mixed Martial Arts Hall of Fame.
