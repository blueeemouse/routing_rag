NASCAR Kart Racing

NASCAR Kart Racing is a kart racing video game that was released for the Wii console on February 10, 2009.

 The game features fourteen real world NASCAR drivers and ten fictional drivers.

 The cover drivers are, from left to right, Carl Edwards, Kyle Busch, Dale Earnhardt Jr., Jeff Gordon, Tony Stewart, and Jimmie Johnson.

 Other drivers featured in the game are Jeff Burton, Denny Hamlin, Kasey Kahne, Kevin Harvick, Elliott Sadler, and Matt Kenseth.

 Joey Logano and Richard Petty are unlockable drivers in the game.
