Mazda Persona

The Mazda Persona is a mid-sized, front-wheel drive, four-door hardtop sedan produced by Mazda in Japan from November 1988 to 1992, and sold both within its main range and under its upscale Eunos brand, as the Eunos 300.

 It used Mazda's MA platform and was replaced by the ɛ̃fini MS-8 in March 1993.
