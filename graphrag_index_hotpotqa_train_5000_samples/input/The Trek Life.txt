The Trek Life

The Trek Life was a thrice-weekly comic series about three "Star Trek" fans (Carl, Steve and Kate), written by David Reddick and published on StarTrek.com.

 "The Trek Life" first appeared in 2005-07-25.

 It was a regular feature on the Web site with a new episode every Monday.

 "The Trek Life" also appears in every issue of Titan Publishing's "Star Trek Magazine", and also appears as a backup feature in IDW Publishing's Star Trek comic books.

 David Reddick also writes and draws for the "Garfield" comic strip for Jim Davis at the Garfield studio (Paws, Inc.).
