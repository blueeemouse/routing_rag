Blinded (song)

"Blinded" is a song by American alternative rock band Third Eye Blind.

 It was released in April 2003 as the lead single from their 2003 album, "Out of the Vein".

 It was written by Stephan Jenkins, Arion Salazar, and Tony Fredianelli.

 The song received positive reviews from music critics and peaked at number 34 on the "Billboard" Pop Songs chart.
