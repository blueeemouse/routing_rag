Tabom people

The Tabom People or Agudas are the Afro-Brazilian community in South of Ghana who are mostly of Yoruba descent.

 The Tabom People are an Afro-Brazilian community of former slaves returnees.

 When they arrived in South Ghana and Accra and they could speak only Portuguese, so they greeted each other with “Como está?”

 (How are you?)

 to which the reply was “Tá bom”, so the Ewe people, Ga-Adangbe people and Akan people in of South Ghana and Accra started to call them the Tabom People.
