Mary Orr

Mary Caswell Orr (December 21, 1910 – September 22, 2006) was an American actress and author whose short story "The Wisdom of Eve", published in the May 1946 issue of "Cosmopolitan", was the basis of the Academy Award-winning film "All About Eve" (1950).

 In private life, Orr used her married name, Mary Orr Denham.
