Tidal Wave (Sub Focus song)

"Tidal Wave" is the third single by British DJ and record producer Sub Focus to be released from his second studio album "Torus".

 The song features vocals from Alpines.

 The song peaked at number 12 on the UK Singles Chart and number 4 on the UK Dance Chart, making it his highest-charting single until "Endorphins" and "Turn Back Time", which both peaked at number 10 on the UK Singles Chart.

 It is still his highest-charting single on the UK Dance Chart and his biggest-selling single.
