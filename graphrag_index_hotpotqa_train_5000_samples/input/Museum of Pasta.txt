Museum of Pasta

The Museum of Pasta (in Italian Museo della pasta) is an Italian ethnographic museum dedicated to the pasta.

 It is located in Corte di Giarola, between Collecchio and Ozzano Taro, in the Province of Parma, a region historically dedicated to the production and processing of pasta.

 The museum shares space with the Museum of Tomato (in Italian Museo del pomodoro).
