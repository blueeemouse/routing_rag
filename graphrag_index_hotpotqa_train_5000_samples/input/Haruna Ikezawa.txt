Haruna Ikezawa

Haruna Ikezawa (池澤 春菜 , Ikezawa Haruna , born December 15, 1975 in Athens, Greece) is a Japanese actress, voice actress and singer.

 She was formerly affiliated with 81 Produce and Oscar Promotion, but as of 2014, she is affiliated with Across Entertainment.

 Her major roles in anime include: Gō Seiba in "Bakusō Kyōdai Let's & Go!!

", Haruna Hiroko in "Hamtaro", Yoshino Shimazu in "Maria-sama ga Miteru", Momoka Nishizawa in "Sgt. Frog".

 In video games, she has voiced Athena Asamiya in "King of Fighters" since 1998, and voiced Coco Bandicoot in the PlayStation games for the "Crash Bandicoot" series.
