2013 Scottish Cup Final

The 2013 Scottish Cup Final was the 128th final of the Scottish Cup, the most prestigious knockout football competition in Scotland.

 The match took place at Hampden Park on 26 May 2013 and was contested by Hibernian and Celtic.

 It was the first time the two clubs had played each other in the Scottish Cup since the 2001 final, which Celtic won 3–0, but Hibs' second consecutive final following their heavy defeat against Hearts the previous year.

 For the first time in its history, the Scottish Cup Final was played on a Sunday.
