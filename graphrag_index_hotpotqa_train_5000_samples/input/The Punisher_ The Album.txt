The Punisher: The Album

The Punisher: The Album is the official soundtrack for the film "The Punisher" and was released in 2004 by Wind-up Records.

 Its lead singles were "Broken" by Seether and Evanescence frontwoman Amy Lee, "Step Up" by Drowning Pool, and "Eyes Wired Shut" by Edgewater, all of which have accompanying music videos.
