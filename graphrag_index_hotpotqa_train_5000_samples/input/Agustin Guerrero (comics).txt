Agustin Guerrero (comics)

Agustin "Gus" Guerrero, also known as El Gato Negro, is a fictional character and comic book superhero created by Richard Dominguez and published by Azteca Productions.

 The character made his first appearance in "El Gato Negro" #1 (October 1993) as a major supporting character.

 Outside of the comic book continuity he is often referred to as "Silver Age El Gato Negro".
