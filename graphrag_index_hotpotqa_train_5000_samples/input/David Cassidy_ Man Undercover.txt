David Cassidy: Man Undercover

David Cassidy: Man Undercover (the word "undercover" in the series title appeared at various times written as one word or two) was an American police drama starring David Cassidy, four years after his run starring in the "The Partridge Family", marking his first starring role since that series was cancelled.

 The series was spun off after Cassidy starred in a special two-hour episode of "Police Story", titled "A Chance to Live," which aired in May 1978; this episode served as the pilot for "Man Undercover".
