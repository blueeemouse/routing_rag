McLeod, North Dakota

McLeod (also Sandoun) is a census designated place in eastern Ransom County, North Dakota, United States.

 An unincorporated community, it was designated as part of the U.S. Census Bureau's Participant Statistical Areas Program on March 31, 2010.

 It was not counted separately during the 2000 Census, but was included in the 2010 Census, where its population was reported to be 27.
