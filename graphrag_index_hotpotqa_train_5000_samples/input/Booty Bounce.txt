Booty Bounce

"Booty Bounce" is a song by American recording artist Dev.

 It was produced by The Cataracs.

 Although it was released after, "Booty Bounce" was sampled in Far East Movement's hit single "Like a G6" which reached number one on the US "Billboard" Hot 100 chart, and sold over three million downloads in the country.

 It was also featured on Dev's 2011 mixtape "Is Hot: The Mixtape".
