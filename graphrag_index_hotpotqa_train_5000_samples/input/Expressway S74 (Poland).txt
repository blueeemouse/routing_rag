Expressway S74 (Poland)

Expressway S74 or express road S74 is major planned road in Poland connecting express road S12 near Sulejów and S19 near Nisko.

 From Opatów it is going to follow the current national road 74 toward Lipnik (where it's going to cross national road 9) and further is going to follow national road 77 toward express road S19 near Nisko.
