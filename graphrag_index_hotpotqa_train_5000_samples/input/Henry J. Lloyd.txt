Henry J. Lloyd

Henry James Lloyd (2 February 1794 at Marylebone, London – 3 September 1853 at Brighton, Sussex) was an English amateur cricketer who played first-class cricket from 1815 to 1830.

 Mainly associated with Marylebone Cricket Club (MCC), he made 34 known appearances in first-class matches.

 He played for several predominantly amateur teams including the Gentlemen in the Gentlemen v Players series.
