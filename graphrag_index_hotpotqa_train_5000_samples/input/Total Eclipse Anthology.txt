Total Eclipse Anthology

Total Eclipse Anthology is a compilation album released by Bonnie Tyler in 2002.

 The two disc album consisted of material that Tyler had released on all of her previous record labels up until that point: RCA, CBS, Hansa Records and EastWest.

 With tracks included from her first ten albums, the anthology was fairly comprehensive.

 However, no tracks were included from Tyler's albums "Free Spirit" (1995) and "All in One Voice" (1998).
