High Sparrow (Game of Thrones)

"High Sparrow" is the third episode of the fifth season of HBO's fantasy television series "Game of Thrones", and the 43rd overall.

 The episode was written by series co-creators David Benioff and D. B. Weiss, and directed by Mark Mylod, his directorial debut for the series.

 It aired on April 26, 2015.

 Prior to airing, this episode along with the other first four episodes of the season were leaked online.
