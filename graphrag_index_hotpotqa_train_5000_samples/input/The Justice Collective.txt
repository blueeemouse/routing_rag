The Justice Collective

The Justice Collective is a collective of musicians and celebrities.

 The project is spearheaded by Peter Hooton of The Farm.

 It was originally established in 2012 as a fund-raising record raising money for the various charities associated with the Hillsborough disaster.

 It is best known for its charity single "He Ain't Heavy, He's My Brother" in 2012.

 A closely related The Peace Collective released the charity single "All Together Now" in 2014.
