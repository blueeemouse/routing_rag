The Now Show

The Now Show is a British radio comedy broadcast on BBC Radio 4, which satirises the week's news.

 The show is a mixture of stand-up, sketches and songs presented by Steve Punt and Hugh Dennis.

 The show also features skits from Jon Holmes, Laura Shavin (earlier series had Emma Kennedy, or occasionally Sue Perkins, for the female voices), a monologue by Marcus Brigstocke, and music by Mitch Benn, Pippa Evans or Adam Kay.
