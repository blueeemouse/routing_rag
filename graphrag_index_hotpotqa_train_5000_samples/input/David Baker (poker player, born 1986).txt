David Baker (poker player, born 1986)

David "Bakes" Baker (born October 17, 1986) is an American professional poker player who has won a World Series of Poker bracelet and appeared at four World Series of Poker final tables.

 He also has a Spring Championship of Online Poker (SCOOP) championship.

 Baker currently has over US$2 million in tournament winnings.

 In order to avoid confusion with David "ODB" Baker, he is listed in the World Series of Poker (WSOP) database by the name "David Bakes Baker" and listed at "Bluff Magazine" as "David (Bakes) Baker".

 Baker, who is from Bloomfield Hills, Michigan, attended Michigan State University before taking up poker.
