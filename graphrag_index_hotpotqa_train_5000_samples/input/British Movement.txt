British Movement

The British Movement (BM), later called the British National Socialist Movement (BNSM), is a British Neo-Nazi organisation founded by Colin Jordan in 1968.

 It grew out of the National Socialist Movement (NSM), which was founded in 1962.

 Frequently on the margins of the British far-right, the BM has had a long and chequered history for its association with violence and extremism.

 It was founded as a political party but manifested itself more as a pressure and activist group.

 It has had spells of dormancy.
