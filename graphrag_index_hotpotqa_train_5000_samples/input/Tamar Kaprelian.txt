Tamar Kaprelian

Tamar Kaprelian (Armenian: Թամար Գաբրելեան , born October 28, 1986) is an Armenian American musician and singer.

 She is best known for her single "New Day" and for winning the Cover Contest in March 2008 with "Apologize" by OneRepublic.

 She was selected to be part of Genealogy, a collective Armenian international music band to represent Armenia in 2015 Eurovision Song Contest in Vienna with the song "Face the Shadow".
