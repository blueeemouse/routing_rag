Nicholas II, Duke of Opava

Nicholas II of Opava (also: "Nicholas II of Troppau", "Nicholas II of Ratibór"; ; 1288 – 8 December 1365) was Duke of Opava (German: "Troppau" ) from 1318 to 1365 and Duke of Ratibór from 1337 to 1365 and Burgrave of Kladsko (German: "Glatz" ) from 1350 to 1365 and also chamberlain of the Kingdom of Bohemia.
