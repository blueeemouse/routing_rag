Sheffield Cathedral

The Cathedral Church of St Peter and St Paul, Sheffield, usually called simply Sheffield Cathedral, is the cathedral church for the Church of England diocese of Sheffield, England.

 Originally a parish church, it was elevated to cathedral status when the diocese was created in 1914.

 Sheffield Cathedral is one of five Grade I listed buildings in the city, along with Town Hall, Abbeydale Industrial Hamlet, and the parish churches at Ecclesfield and Bradfield.

 It is located in the city centre on Church Street and served by Sheffield Supertram's Cathedral stop.

 It is one of three stops to be served by all tram lines.
