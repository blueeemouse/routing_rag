Donald G. Bloesch

Donald G. Bloesch (May 3, 1928, Bremen, Indiana – August 24, 2010) was a noted American evangelical theologian.

 For more than 40 years, he published scholarly yet accessible works that generally defend traditional Protestant beliefs and practices while seeking to remain in the mainstream of modern Protestant theological thought.

 The ongoing publication of his "Christian Foundation Series", has brought him recognition as an important American theologian.
