Blue Waters F.C.

Blue Waters, also known as Langer Heinrich Mine (LHU) Blue Waters due to sponsorship reasons, are a Namibian football club from Walvis Bay.

 The team is nicknamed Blue Birds, The Birds or Omeva (which means 'water' in the local OshiWambo language by its dying supporters.

 The team trains at its current field, Blue Waters Sport Field in Kuisebmund, a suburb of Walvis Bay.

 The team has a local rivalry with Eleven Arrows F.C., which was formed by former players of Blue Waters in early 1960s.
