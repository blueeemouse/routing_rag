Collecchio

Collecchio is a town in the province of Parma, Emilia-Romagna, northern Italy.

 It is located 12.9 km by road southwest of the centre of Parma.

 A major food producing area, it is home to multinational Italian dairy and food corporation Parmalat and Parma F.C.'s training complex, Centro Sportivo di Collecchio, and is connected by railway.
