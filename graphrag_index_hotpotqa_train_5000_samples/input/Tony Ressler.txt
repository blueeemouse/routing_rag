Tony Ressler

Antony P. Ressler (born July 16, 1959) is an American businessman.

 He co-founded the private equity firm Apollo Global Management in 1990; and co-founded the private equity firm Ares Management in 1997.

 Ressler and Grant Hill are both owners of the Atlanta Hawks.

 They purchased the team on June 24, 2015 for estimated $730 million - $850 million.
