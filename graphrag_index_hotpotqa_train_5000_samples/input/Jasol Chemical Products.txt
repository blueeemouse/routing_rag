Jasol Chemical Products

Jasol Chemical Products was a chemicals manufacturing company founded in Adelaide, South Australia by Charles George Johnson in 1934.

 It was well known in that state and Western Australia for its cleaning products and disinfectants, notably Jasol B.

 The brand is now owned by George Weston Foods.

 The name "Jasol" was an acronym of "Johnson's Antiseptic Soluble Oils Limited".
