Mercury Tracer

The Mercury Tracer is a compact car that was marketed by Mercury from 1987 for the 1988 model year until 1999.

 The replacement for the Ford Escort-derived Lynx, the first generation of the Tracer was a modified version of the Asia-Pacific market Ford Laser, itself a variant of the Mazda 323.

 In 1990 for the 1991 model year, the Tracer became the Mercury counterpart of the Ford Escort in North America.

 All three generations of the Tracer were based on variations of the Mazda 323/Protegé.
