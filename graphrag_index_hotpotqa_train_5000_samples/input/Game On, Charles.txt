Game On, Charles

"Game On, Charles" is the first episode of the sixth season of the American mystery drama series "Pretty Little Liars", and is the 121st episode overall.

 It originally aired on June 2, 2015, ABC Family and serves as the season premiere of the sixth season.

 The episode was directed by Chad Lowe and was written by showrunner I. Marlene King and Lijah J. Barasz.
