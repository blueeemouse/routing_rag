São Luís Island

São Luís Island also known as Maranhão Island is an island in state of Maranhão, Brazil with an area of 1,410 km² (544 sq mi), located between the Baía de São Marcos and the Baía de São José.

 There are 4 cities located in the island: São Luís, after which the island is named, São José de Ribamar, Paço do Lumiar, and Raposa.

 The city of São Luís also covers three more minor islands, Tauá Mirim, Tauá, and Medo.

 Raposa also has an island.

 The population of these 4 cities in July 2013 IGBE estimate is 1,366,266, up from 1,309,330 in the 2010 Census.
