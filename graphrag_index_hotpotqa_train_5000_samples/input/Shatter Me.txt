Shatter Me

Shatter Me is a young adult dystopian thriller written by Tahereh Mafi, published on November 15, 2011.

 The book is narrated by Juliette, a 17-year-old girl with a lethal touch and is unique in that it contains passages and lines that have been crossed out.

 The second book in the series, "Unravel Me", was published on February 5, 2013.

 The third book in the series, "Ignite Me," was published on February 4, 2014.
