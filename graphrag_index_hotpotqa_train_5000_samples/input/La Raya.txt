La Raya

La Raya de Santiago, or simply La Raya, is a village or "pedanía" (suburban district) of the municipality of Murcia, in Región de Murcia (Spain).

 There live a population of 2,289 inhabitants (INE 2004) and it has an approximate area of 2,901 km².

 It is situated in the central area of the orchard of Murcia, on the right bank of river Segura, 4 km from Murcia city centre and 55 metres above sea level.
