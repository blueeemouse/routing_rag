Luke Smith (The Sarah Jane Adventures)

Luke Smith is a fictional regular character played by Tommy Knight in the British children's science fiction television series "The Sarah Jane Adventures", a spin-off of the long-running series "Doctor Who".

 Luke is a regular character in "The Sarah Jane Adventures" both in television and audio adventures.

 He has also appeared in three episodes of "Doctor Who": the two-parter "The Stolen Earth"/"Journey's End" (2008), and the Tenth Doctor's finale episode "The End of Time, Part Two" (2010).
