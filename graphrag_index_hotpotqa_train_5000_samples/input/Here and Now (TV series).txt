Here and Now (TV series)

Here and Now is an American television sitcom that aired on NBC from September 19, 1992 to January 2, 1993.

 The series starred Malcolm-Jamal Warner in the lead role, who prior to this series co-starred in "The Cosby Show" which ended its run in April 1992.

 Bill Cosby served as one on the show's executive producers along with Warner serving as executive consultant credited as M.J. Warner.

 The song "Tennessee" by Arrested Development was used as the show's theme song.
