Horse Creek (McKenzie River)

Horse Creek is a tributary of the McKenzie River in near the unincorporated community of McKenzie Bridge in Lane County in the U.S. state of Oregon.

 It begins in the Cascade Range in the Three Sisters Wilderness of the Willamette National Forest.

 Its headwaters are near the Deschutes County border and the Pacific Crest Trail.

 It flows generally northwest to meet the river about 65 mi upstream of the McKenzie's confluence with the Willamette River.
