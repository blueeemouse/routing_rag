Rise of the Villains: Knock, Knock

"Rise of the Villains: Knock, Knock" is the second episode of the second season and 24th episode overall from the FOX series "Gotham".

 The episode was written by Ken Woodruff and directed by Rob Bailey.

 It was first broadcast in September 28, 2015 in FOX.

 In this episode, Gordon is assigned in investigating the Arkham escapees, now calling themselves "the Maniax".

 Meanwhile, Bruce is getting closer to the truth about his father's office.
