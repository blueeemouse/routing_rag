Westfield Eastgardens

Westfield Eastgardens is an Australian shopping centre in the Sydney suburb of Eastgardens.

 The centre was the largest shopping centre in Australia when it opened in September 1987.

 The centre is owned by the Terrace Tower Group but operated and managed on a long term agreement by the Scentre Group.

 The centre's major tenants include three supermarkets, a department store, three discount department stores and a cinema.
