Chandra Mohan (Telugu actor)

Chandra Mohan (born Mallampalli Chandrasekhara Rao) is an Indian film actor known for his works predominantly in Telugu cinema and few Tamil films.

 He has garnered two Filmfare Awards, and sevenNandi Awards.

 He received critical reception for his performance in box office hits such as "Rangula Ratnam" for which he received the state Nandi Award for Best Actor, "Padaharella Vayasu" (1978) alongside Sridevi for which he won the Filmfare Best Actor Award (Telugu), and "Siri Siri Muvva" (1978) with Jayaprada.

 His first Tamil film is Naalai Namadhe (1976).

 Some of the films in which he was starred as the lead actor are "Seetamalakshmi", "Radha Kalyanam", "Rendu Rella Aaru", "Chandamama Raave" and "Ram Robert Rahim".
