Pesum Dheivam

Pesum Dheivam (Tamil: பேசும் தெய்வம்; English: Talking Deity) is a 1967 Indian Tamil film, directed by K. S. Gopalakrishnan and produced by R. Balasubramaniam.

 The film stars Sivaji Ganesan, Padmini, S. V. Ranga Rao and S. V. Sahasranamam in lead roles.

 The film had musical score by K. V. Mahadevan.

.

The film has many flaws with poor direction.

 The situations are melo dramatic and contrived.

 The actors just go through the motions of acting as the screen play is so bad.
