Marshalltown Community College

Marshalltown Community College (MCC) is a public community college located in Marshalltown, Iowa, United States.

 It is part of the Iowa Valley Community College District.

 The campus is located just to the south of Marshalltown along Highway 30.

 A second campus, Iowa Valley Grinnell, is located in Grinnell.

 MCC offers 55 degree/diploma options, has a student-faculty ratio of 13:1, and has an annual enrollment at about 2,000 students.
