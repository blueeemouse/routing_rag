Eleanor Mlotek

Eleanor Chana Mlotek (née Gordon; April 9, 1922 – November 4, 2013) was a musicologist, specializing in Yiddish folklore.

 Isaac Bashevis Singer, the Yiddish writer and Nobel laureate, once called Mrs. Mlotek and her husband, Joseph, “the Sherlock Holmeses of Yiddish folk songs.”

 WorldCat lists her as author of 70 works in 112 editions, in 6 languages.

 They include both collections of music and exhibition catalogs of Yiddish authors.

 She held the position of Music Archivist at the YIVO Institute for Jewish Research, and was also a columnist at the "Yiddish Forward" for over forty years.

 She was born in Brooklyn and is the mother of Zalmen Mlotek, the artistic director of the Folksbiene.
