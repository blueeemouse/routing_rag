Busby

Busby is the English name for the Hungarian "prémes csákó" ("fur shako") or "kucsma", a military head-dress made of fur, originally worn by Hungarian hussars.

 In its original Hungarian form the busby was a cylindrical fur cap, having a bag of coloured cloth hanging from the top.

 The end of this bag was attached to the right shoulder as a defence against sabre cuts.

 In Great Britain busbies are of two kinds: (a) the hussar busby, cylindrical in shape, with a bag; this is worn by hussars and the Royal Horse Artillery; (b) the rifle busby, a folding cap of astrakhan (curly lambswool) formerly worn by rifle regiments, in shape somewhat resembling a Glengarry but taller.

 Both have straight plumes in the front of the headdress.
