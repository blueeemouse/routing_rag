Blanche Devereaux

Blanche Devereaux (née Hollingsworth) is one of the four main fictional characters on the 1985–1992 NBC sitcom "The Golden Girls", and its CBS spin-off "The Golden Palace".

 In the pilot episode, her last name was given as Hollingsworth, but this was somewhat "corrected" in later episodes by making this her maiden name.

 Blanche was portrayed by Rue McClanahan for 8 years and 206 episodes.
