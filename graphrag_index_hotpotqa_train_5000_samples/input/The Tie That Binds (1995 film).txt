The Tie That Binds (1995 film)

The Tie That Binds is a 1995 thriller film, and the directing debut of screenwriter Wesley Strick.

 The film stars Daryl Hannah, Keith Carradine, Vincent Spano, Moira Kelly and Julia Devin.

 The film follows the struggles of a couple who have just adopted a 6-year-old girl, only to discover that her biological parents, a murderous couple, are trying to reclaim her.
