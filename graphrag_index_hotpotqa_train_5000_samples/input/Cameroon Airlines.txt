Cameroon Airlines

Cameroon Airlines was an airline from Cameroon, serving as flag carrier of the country.

 Based in Douala, it operated scheduled services within Africa, as well as to Europe and the Middle East out of its hub at Douala International Airport, with a second network focus on Yaoundé Nsimalen International Airport.

 The company was 96.43 per cent state owned, with the remaining shares having been held by Air France.

 It ceased operations in March 2008; its role as Cameroon's flag carrier was taken over by Camair-Co.
