Arkansas Highway 101

Highway 101 (AR 101, Ark. 101, and Hwy.

 101) is a designation for two north–south state highways in north central Arkansas.

 A western route of 11.17 mi runs north from US Route 62/US Route 412 (US 62/US 412) to Missouri Route 101 at the Missouri state line.

 A second route of 7.13 mi begins at Hand Valley and runs north to US 62/US 412.
