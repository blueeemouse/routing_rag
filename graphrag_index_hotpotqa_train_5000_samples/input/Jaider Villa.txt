Jaider Villa

Jaider Villa Giraldo (born 30 January 1977), better known as Jaider Villa, is a Colombian actor, host and model.

 He began his acting career in a reality show called "Protagonistas de Novela" in 2002, in which he emerged as the winner.

 He has appeared in many TV soap operas, for example Al ritmo de tu corazón in which he played Santiago Duque (a leading role); Milagros de Amor, Corazón valiente and others.

 In 2014 he won the recognition of Miami Mayor Tomas Regalado for his contribution to the art featured in the work "Mesalina" in the leading role as John.
