Janie Jones (film)

Janie Jones is a 2010 American drama film by writer/director David M. Rosenthal.

 It stars Alessandro Nivola, Elisabeth Shue, Brittany Snow, and Abigail Breslin as the eponymous Janie Jones.

 The film makes extensive use of original music created by Gemma Hayes and Eef Barzelay and sung and played by Abigail Breslin and Alessandro Nivola.

 It premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival on September 17, 2010.
