Norm Thompson Outfitters

Norm Thompson Outfitters is a privately owned catalog and internet retailer based in Hillsboro, Oregon, United States.

 Founded in 1949 by Norman A. (Norm) Thompson as a mail order business, it grew to annual sales of $200 million before it was sold to Golden Gate Capital Partners in 2006.

 The company sells clothing, gadgets, furniture, kitchen items, and gift items from its namesake catalog as well as from its Solutions and Sahalie brands.

 John Difrancesco serves as president and chief executive of the 500 employee company.
