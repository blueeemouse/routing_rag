2005 Hawaii Warriors football team

The 2005 Hawaii Warriors football team represented the University of Hawaii at Manoa in the 2005 NCAA Division I-A college football season.

 Hawaii finished the 2005 season with a 5–7 record, going 4–4 in WAC play.

 The losing record was the first for Hawaii since 2000 and the team did not play in a bowl game for the first time since 2002.
