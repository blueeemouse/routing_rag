PhD: Phantasy Degree

PhD: Phantasy Degree (Korean: 마스터스쿨 올림프스 "Maseuteo Seukur Ollimpeuseu", lit.

 "Master School Olympus") , is a manhwa series created by Son Hee-joon.

 The series is published by in English by Tokyopop.

 There are currently 10 volumes published, and the series' original publisher, Daiwon C.I. lists it as ongoing, but no new volumes have been published since 2005.

 A one-volume prequel to the series entitled "Magic Academy Zeus" (매직 아카데미 제우스, "Maejig Akademi Jeusu") was published in South Korea in 1998.
