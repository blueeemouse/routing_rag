The Paul McCartney Collection

The Paul McCartney Collection is a series of 16 remastered CDs by Paul McCartney of his solo and Wings albums, with most adding bonus tracks.

 The albums in the collection were released separately, with the first eight released on 7 June 1993, and the remainder on 9 August of the same year.

 The first half comprised albums from "McCartney" (1970) to "London Town" (1978), and the second half "Wings Greatest" (1978) to "Flowers in the Dirt" (1989).
