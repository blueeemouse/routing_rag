The Tracker

The Tracker is an Australian drama film produced in 2002.

 It was directed and written by Rolf de Heer.

 It is set in 1922 in outback Australia where a racist white colonial policeman (Gary Sweet) used the tracking ability of an Indigenous Australian tracker (David Gulpilil) to find the murderer of a white woman.

 The tagline is 'All men choose the path they walk.'
