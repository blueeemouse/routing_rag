Iwan Lewis

Iwan Lewis (born 28 December 1988) is a Welsh theatre and film actor.

 He is known for the roles of Emmett in the UK tour of the stage version of "Legally Blonde" and Bahorel in the film version of "Les Miserables".

 Lewis graduated from the Guildford School of Acting in 2010.

 In 2007, during his time at the GSA, he was a recipient of an award from the Elizabeth Evans Trust.

 In addition to his roles in "Les Misérables" and "Legally Blonde", Lewis also appeared in the 2010 London Revival of Stephen Sondheim's "Passion".
