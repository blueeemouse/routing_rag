The Sin Eater’s Daughter

The Sin Eater's Daughter is a young adult fantasy series written by Melinda Salisbury and published by Scholastic Press.

 The first book in the series, "The Sin Eater’s Daughter", was published on 24 February 2015 and marked Salisbury's first book in print.

 The second book in the series, "The Sleeping Prince", was published the following year along with a short story prequel, "The King of Rats".

 The third book in the series, "The Scarecrow Queen", was released in 2017.

 A fourth book, "The Heart Collector", was also published in 2017, it is a collection of 3 short stories from the same fictional universe, including the previously released "The King of Rats", as well as 2 new stories, "The Heart Collector" and "Mully No-Hands".
