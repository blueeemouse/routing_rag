Rondebosch Common

Rondebosch Common is an open common of about 40 ha in Rondebosch, Cape Town in South Africa.

 A common is defined as "A piece of open land for public use, esp.

 in a village or town."

 It contains one of the only surviving pockets of the critically endangered “Cape Flats Sand Fynbos” vegetation type, which exists nowhere else in the world.
