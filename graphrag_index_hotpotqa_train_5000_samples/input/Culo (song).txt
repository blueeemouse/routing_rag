Culo (song)

"Culo" (English: "Ass" ) is the debut single by Cuban-American rapper Pitbull.

 It features rapper Lil Jon and serves as the lead single from Pitbull's debut album "M.I.A.M.I." The song uses the Coolie Dance riddim, which gained prominence from Nina Sky's hit "Move Ya Body".

 "Culo" also sampled Mr. Vegas' song "Pull Up," for which Pitbull and Lil Jon got sued by Mr. Vegas.
