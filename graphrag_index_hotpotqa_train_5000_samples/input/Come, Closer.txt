Come, Closer

Come, Closer () is a 2010 South Korean indie romance melodrama film.

 A directorial debut by Kim Jong-kwan and starring Yoon Kye-sang, Jung Yu-mi, Yoon Hee-seok and Yozoh, the full-length omnibus tells the love story of five couples.

 It made its debut at the 15th Busan International Film Festival in 2010.
