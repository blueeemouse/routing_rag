Ringo Mendoza

Genaro Jacobo Contreras is a semi-retired Mexican professional wrestler, or "Luchador" in Spanish, and is a professional wrestling trainer for Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre (CMLL).

 Contreras is best known under the ring name Ringo Mendoza, which he has used since his debut in 1968.

 Contreras has two brothers who were also professional wrestlers, Pedro Jacobo Contreras who worked as "Cachorro Mendoza" ("Cub Mendoza") and another brother who wrestled as "Indio Mendoza".

 Over the course of his career Ringo Mendoza held several Middleweight championships, including five NWA World Middleweight Championships, two Mexican National Middleweight Championships, one CMLL World Middleweight Championship and one Occidente Middleweight Championship.

 Mendoza wrestled his last match in 2011.
