Half-Life: Opposing Force

Half-Life: Opposing Force is an expansion pack for Valve Software's science fiction first-person shooter video game "Half-Life".

 The game was developed by Gearbox Software and Valve Corporation and published by Sierra Entertainment on November 19, 1999.

 "Opposing Force" is the first expansion for "Half-Life" and was first announced in April 1999.

 Randy Pitchford, the lead designer on the game, later noted that he believed Gearbox was selected to develop "Opposing Force" because Valve wanted to concentrate on their future projects.

 Over the course of development, Gearbox brought in a variety of outside talent from other areas of the video games industry to help bolster various aspects of design.
