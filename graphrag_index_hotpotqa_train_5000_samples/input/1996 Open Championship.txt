1996 Open Championship

The 1996 Open Championship was a men's major golf championship and the 125th Open Championship, held from 18–21 July at the Royal Lytham & St Annes Golf Club in Lytham St Annes, England.

 Tom Lehman won his only major championship by two strokes over runners-up Mark McCumber and Ernie Els.

 Lehman built a six-stroke lead after 54 holes and became the first American to win at Lytham since Bobby Jones seventy years earlier.
