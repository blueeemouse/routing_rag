Bao (musician)

Bao Vo (born 19 February 1982), also known as BAO, is a Vietnamese-American musician, singer-songwriter and record producer based in Los Angeles, California.

 He has done extensive work with Mariqueen Maandig and the band Ming & Ping, where he was responsible for the production, concept, branding, marketing, and visual design, in addition to being a co-writer to the music itself.
