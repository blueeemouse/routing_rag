Benjamin Wills Newton

Benjamin Wills Newton, (12 December 1807 – 26 June 1899) was an English evangelist and author of Christian books.

 He became the most notable leader of the church in Plymouth after which the Plymouth Brethren were named.

 Although he had been a close friend of John Nelson Darby, they began to clash on matters of church doctrine and practice which led to the 1848 split of the brethren movement into the Open Brethren and Exclusive Brethren and Newton's withdrawal.
