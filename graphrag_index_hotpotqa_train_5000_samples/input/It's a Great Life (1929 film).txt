It's a Great Life (1929 film)

It's a Great Life is a 1929 American comedy film directed by Sam Wood and written by Al Boasberg and Willard Mack.

 The film stars Rosetta Duncan, Vivian Duncan, Lawrence Gray, Jed Prouty and Benny Rubin.

 The film was released on December 6, 1929, by Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer.
