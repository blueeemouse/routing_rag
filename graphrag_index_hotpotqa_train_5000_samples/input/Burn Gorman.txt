Burn Gorman

Burn Hugh Gorman (born 1 September 1974) is an English-American actor and musician.

 He is known for his roles as Dr. Owen Harper in the BBC series "Torchwood" (2006–08), Karl Tanner in the HBO series "Game of Thrones" (2013–14), Phillip Stryver in "The Dark Knight Rises" (2012), Dr. Hermann Gottlieb in "Pacific Rim" (2013), Major Edmund Hewlett in the AMC series "" (2014), and Mr. Holly in "Crimson Peak" (2015).

 Since 2015, Gorman has portrayed The Marshal in the Amazon drama "The Man in the High Castle".
