Indian vice-presidential election, 2007

The 2007 Indian vice-presidential election was held on 10 August 2007 to elect Vice-President of India to serve from 2007 until 2012.

 Mohammad Hamid Ansari from Indian National Congress was elected for the post.

 The incumbent, Bhairon Singh Shekhawat did not seek reelection and instead ran for President in 2007 election, where he lost to Pratibha Patil.

 He subsequently resigned from VP post days before Patil's inauguration.
