Buena Familia

Buena Familia (lit.

 Good Family) is a 2015 Philippine family drama series directed by Gil C. Tejada, starring Kylie Padilla, Martin del Rosario, Julie Anne San Jose, Jake Vargas and Julian Trono.

 The drama series premiered on GMA Network and worldwide via GMA Pinoy TV on July 28, 2015, replacing the top-rating drama series "Yagit" on the network's Afternoon Prime block.

 It was replaced by Hanggang Makita Kang Muli.

 The drama series also features the return of the love team of Bobby Andrews and Angelu de Leon in Philippine television.
