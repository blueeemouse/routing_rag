Rasa Komuter station

The Rasa Komuter station is a Malaysian commuter train station stationed at the northern side of and named after the town of Rasa, Hulu Selangor, Selangor.

 The station was opened on April 21, 2007 alongside the Batang Kali and Serendah stations.

 It was the third stop in the Rawang-Tanjung Malim shuttle service (formerly known as the Rawang-Kuala Kubu Bharu shuttle service) until the service was merged with the Port Klang Line in 2016.
