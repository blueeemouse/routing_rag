Rush Hour (dance album)

Rush Hour is an album of club dance music for the purpose of playing while driving.

 It was released in the summer of 2004.

 It is a double CD claiming "exhilarating club anthems for the drive of your life".

 There are 42 tracks, by artists including Basement Jaxx, X-Press 2, William Orbit and Fatboy Slim.

 The same company has released Rush Hour 2 and Rush Hour 3 with similar styles of music on each.
