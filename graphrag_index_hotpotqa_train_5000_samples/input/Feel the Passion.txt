Feel the Passion

"Feel the Passion" is a song by Aurela Gaçe, composed by Shpëtim Saraçi and with lyrics by Sokol Marsi.

 The song was the winner of the 49th edition of the Albanian musical festival Festivali i Këngës, performed as "Kënga ime" (My song) on 25 December 2010, and represented Albania at the Eurovision Song Contest 2011, held in Düsseldorf, Germany.
