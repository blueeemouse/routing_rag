Death and Life

Death and Life (Italian: Morte e Vita) is an oil on canvas painting by Austrian symbolist painter, Gustav Klimt whose primary subject was the female body.

 His paintings, murals, and sketches are marked by a sensual eroticism, which is especially apparent in his pencil drawings.

 The painting was started in 1908 and completed in 1915.

 It is created in an Art Nouveau (Modern) style by use of allegorical painting genre during Golden phase.

 The painting measures 178 by 198 centimeters and is now housed at the Leopold Museum in Vienna.
