Kinky Business

Kinky Business is an American pornographic movie takeoff of the mainstream film "Risky Business".

 It stars Tom Byron in the Tom Cruise role, and Tanya Lawson in the Rebecca De Mornay part.

 Others stars include Ginger Lynn, Misty Mallory, Laurie Smith, Lois Ayres, Jerry Butler, Raven, Crystal Breeze, Cynthia Brooks and Gina Carrera.

 Ron Jeremy and (uncredited) Kristara Barrington are featured in non-sex roles.

 Traci Lords had one scene in the film.

 Controversy would come after the news that Traci Lords was underage at the time of production, the film was later re-released with her sex scene removed.

 Even so, the original, uncut version is still very widely available.

 A scene with Lynn and Byron won the 1985 AVN Award for Best Couples Sex Scene.
