Limitless (TV series)

Limitless is an American comedy-drama television series based on the 2011 film of the same name, which originally aired on CBS from September 22, 2015 to April 26, 2016.

 It stars Jake McDorman as Brian Finch, who discovers the power of a mysterious nootropic drug by the name of NZT-48.

 This drug unlocks the full potential of the human brain and gives its user enhanced mental faculties.
