ACE Bakery

ACE Bakery is a maker of European-style, artisan breads and baked goods.

 The company, based in Toronto, has some 35 varieties of bread, made preservative free and from natural starters, in addition to other specialty products, including a line of granola and artisan crisps, marketed across Canada and parts of the United States.

 Along with fresh baked breads, ACE Bakery also makes par-baked or partially baked, flash-frozen dough.

 Founded by a Caledon, Ontario husband and wife, the company is today owned by George Weston Limited.
