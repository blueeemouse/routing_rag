Springville Carnegie Library

The Springville Carnegie Library at 175 S. Main St. in Springville, Utah, United States is a Prairie School style Carnegie library building completed in 1922.

 It is one of the 23 Carnegie Libraries built in Utah.

 It functioned as the city public library until 1965, when the library was moved to a new larger.

 The 1922 building was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1991.

 It now houses a pioneer relic museum.
