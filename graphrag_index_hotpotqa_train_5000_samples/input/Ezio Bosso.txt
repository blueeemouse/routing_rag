Ezio Bosso

Ezio Bosso (13 September 1971) is an Italian composer, classical musician and conductor.

 Born in Turin, Italy, Bosso learned to read and play music before the age of four.

 At the age of 14, he became the bass player for the band Statuto.

 Bosso later abandoned popular music, in order to become an orchestral conductor and classical composer.

 He conducted such prominent orchestras as the London Symphony Orchestra.
