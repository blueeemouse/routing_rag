Jiehkkevárri

Jiehkkevárri (sometimes spelled as Jiekkevarri or Jiekkevarre or Norwegianized Jæggevarre) is a mountain on the border of the municipalities of Lyngen and Tromsø in Troms county, Norway.

 It is the highest mountain in Troms county and has the second highest prominence (primary factor) among Norwegian mountains.

 The 1834 m tall mountain sits about 10 km northeast of the village of Lakselvbukt and about 17 km southwest of Lyngseidet.
