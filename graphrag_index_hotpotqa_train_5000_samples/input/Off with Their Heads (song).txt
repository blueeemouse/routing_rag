Off with Their Heads (song)

"Off With Their Heads" is the sixth single by British rapper Devlin, and the second single from Devlin's second studio album "A Moving Picture" (2013), the song was released in the United Kingdom on 10 October 2012 and features British rapper Wretch 32.

 The song was written by James Devlin and Jermaine Scott and produced by Drop Lamond.
