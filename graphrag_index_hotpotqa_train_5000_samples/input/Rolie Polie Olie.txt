Rolie Polie Olie

Rolie Polie Olie is a French-Canadian-American children's television series produced by Nelvana and Sparx*, distributed by Disney, and created by William Joyce, Maggie Swanson, and Anne Wood.

 The show focuses on a little robot who is composed of several spheres and other three-dimensional geometric shapes.

 The show was one of the earliest series that was fully animated in CG, and the first CG-animated preschool series.

 "Rolie Polie Olie" aired reruns on Disney Junior from March 23, 2012–September 28, 2014.
