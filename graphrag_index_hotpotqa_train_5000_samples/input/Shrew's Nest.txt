Shrew's Nest

Shrew's Nest (Spanish: "Musarañas" , "Shrews", renamed "Sangre de mi sangre" ("Blood of my blood") in Mexico, and "La habitación del mal" ("The evil room") in Colombia, is a Spanish 2014 thriller/horror film directed by Juan Fernando Andrés and Esteban Roel, and starring Macarena Gómez, Nadia de Santiago, Hugo Silva and Luis Tosar.

 It was nominated for three awards at the 29th Goya Awards ceremony.
