I Feel Lucky

"I Feel Lucky" is a song co-written and recorded by American country artist Mary Chapin Carpenter.

 It was released in May 1992 as the first single from the album "Come On Come On".

 The song reached number 4 on the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles & Tracks chart.

 The Chipettes recorded a cover of this song for the 1992 album "Chipmunks in Low Places".

 It was written by Carpenter and Don Schlitz.
