College of Southern Nevada

The College of Southern Nevada (CSN) is primarily a two-year college in Clark County, Nevada, with four four-year degrees in Dental Hygiene, Medical Lab Scientist, Respiratory Sciences, and Fire & Emergency Services Admin.

 It constitutes of 2900 Teaching and Non Teaching staff.

 The school is the largest public higher education institution in Nevada.

 It is part of the Nevada System of Higher Education.
