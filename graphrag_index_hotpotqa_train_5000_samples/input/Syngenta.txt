Syngenta

Syngenta AG is a global Swiss agribusiness that produces agrochemicals and seeds.

 As a biotechnology company, it conducts genomic research.

 It was formed in 2000 by the merger of Novartis Agribusiness and Zeneca Agrochemicals.

 s of 2014 Syngenta was the world’s largest crop chemical producer, strongest in Europe.

 s of 2009 it ranked third in seeds and biotechnology sales.
