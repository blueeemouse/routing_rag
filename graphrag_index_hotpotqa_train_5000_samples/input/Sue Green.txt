Sue Green

Susan Tex "Sue" Green (born August 13, 1953) is an American professional wrestler.

 She began wrestling at the age of 15 and was trained by The Fabulous Moolah.

 She was the frequent tag team partner of Sandy Parker, with whom she won the National Wrestling Alliance (NWA)'s NWA Women's World Tag Team Championship.

 The title reign, however, is unrecognized.

 In 1975, Green defeated Moolah for the NWA World Women's Championship, which is unrecognized by the NWA.

 The following year, Green was recognized by "Pro Wrestling Illustrated" as the Girl Wrestler of the Year.

 In 1992, she joined the Professional Girl Wrestling Association, holding the promotion's championship and later acting as commissioner.
