South Tipperary

South Tipperary (Irish: "Tiobraid Árann Theas" ) is a former county in Ireland.

 It is part of the South-East Region and is also located in the province of Munster.

 It was named after the town of Tipperary and consisted of 52% of the land area of the traditional county of Tipperary.

 South Tipperary County Council was the local authority for the county.

 The population of the county was 88,433 according to the 2011 census.

 It was abolished on 3 June 2014, merged with North Tipperary under a new Tipperary County Council.
