Battle of Davao

The Battle of Davao was fought by Allied forces during World War II.

 It contained American and Philippine Commonwealth troops including locally organized guerrillas against the Japanese from May 3 to 18, 1945 at the city of Davao and its vicinity in the island of Mindanao in the Philippine Archipelago.

 It is part of Operation VICTOR V, an offensive operation against Japanese forces in Mindanao, and part of the campaign for the liberation of the Philippines during World War II.

 The battle was the decisive engagement of the Mindanao Campaign.
