Rush 2: Extreme Racing USA

Rush 2: Extreme Racing USA is a racing video game developed by Atari Games and published by Midway Games exclusively for the Nintendo 64 video game console.

 It was released on November 10, 1998 in North America, and February 4, 1999 in Europe.

 "Rush 2: Extreme Racing USA" is a sequel to "" and the second game in the "Rush" series.
