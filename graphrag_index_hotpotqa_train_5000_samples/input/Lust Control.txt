Lust Control

Lust Control is a Christian thrash punk band, originally formed in 1988.

 They are known for their explicit lyrical content, which is devoted to matters of sexual purity and sin, including abstinence, masturbation, pornography, sex ed, and related topics.

 For their unwavering views on sexual purity, "CCM" magazine has called Lust Control as "the Josh McDowell of the Christian rock world."

 Musically they have been likened to The Ramones or The Dead Milkmen.

 The band formed as a joke and was not meant for long term exposure, which has led some to refer to it as a Christian version of Spinal Tap.

 Lust Control received the title of "The Worst Christian Band of the Decade" for the 1990s from "HM".
