Spyderco

Spyderco is a Golden, Colorado, U.S.A. based cutlery company that produces knives and knife sharpeners.

 Spyderco pioneered many features that are now common in folding knives, including the pocket clip, serrations, and the opening hole.

 Spyderco has collaborated with 30 custom knife makers, athletes, and self-defense instructors for designs and innovated the usage of 20 different blade materials.
