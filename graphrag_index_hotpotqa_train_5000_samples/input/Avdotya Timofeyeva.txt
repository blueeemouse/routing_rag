Avdotya Timofeyeva

Avdotia Timofeyeva (1739-?)

, was a Russian ballerina.

 She belonged to the first group of ballet dancers in the history of Russian ballet.

 Timofejeva was a part of the first group of ballet students trained by the founder of the Russian ballet, Jean-Baptiste Landé, and was given a position in the ballet of the Imperial theatres in 1748.

 She performed many parts in the ballets by Giovanni Battista Locatelli and Antonio Sacco.
