Wil van der Aalst

Wil M.P. van der Aalst (born 29 January 1966) is a Dutch computer scientist, and professor at the Department of Mathematics & Computer Science of the Eindhoven University of Technology, where he chairs the Architecture of Information Systems group.

 His research and teaching interests include information systems, workflow management, Petri nets, process mining, specification languages, and simulation.

 He is also known for his work on workflow patterns.
