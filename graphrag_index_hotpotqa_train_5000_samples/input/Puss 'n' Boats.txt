Puss 'n' Boats

Puss 'n' Boats is a 1966 Tom and Jerry cartoon directed by Abe Levitow and produced by Chuck Jones.

 It is the first Tom and Jerry cartoon (both regarding Tom and Jerry cartoons produced by Chuck Jones, and Tom and Jerry cartoons produced altogether) with Carl Brandt as the music composer.

 The title Puss 'n' Boats is a play-on-words of the phrase "Puss in Boots".

 The Cartoon is reminiscent of the 1952 shorts Cruise Cat, produced by Hanna-Barbera.
