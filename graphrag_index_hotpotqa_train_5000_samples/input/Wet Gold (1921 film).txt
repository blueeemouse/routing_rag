Wet Gold (1921 film)

Wet Gold is a 1921 American silent drama film directed by and starring Ralph Ince.

 It features pirates, a race for buried treasure, and a submarine.

 The protagonist learns about the treasure from the pirates, who are promptly and conveniently killed in an accident.

 The second part of the film starts in Havana, Cuba where the protagonist reveals the location of the treasure to others, who split up and race to get it.

 When they arrive at the undersea location where the treasure is, the separate groups fight, with the protagonist eventually being victorious.
