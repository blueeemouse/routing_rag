The Invisible Band

The Invisible Band is the third studio album from Scottish indie pop band Travis.

 The album was released on 11 June 2001.

 The title of the album makes reference to the band's feelings regarding 'music being more important than the band making it'.

 Band frontman Fran Healy stated in an interview that the album's title referred to the band's status in 'having famous songs, but not being famous themselves'.

 The album spent four weeks at the top of the UK Albums Chart, selling more copies in that time than "The Man Who" managed in half a year.
