Chris Bacon (boxer)

Christopher P. "Chris" Bacon (born 8 October 1969) is a retired professional British cruiserweight boxer and judoka born in Tasmania, Australia and residing in Manchester.

 During his professional boxing career he was trained by Bob Shannon a long-time Manchester boxing coach.

 He is a former WBF European Super Cruiserweight Champion, former National Judo Champion, and Bronze medal winner at the 1990 Commonwealth Games.

 Bacon has professionally wrestled in Japan, is former MMA fighter and also participated in the 1992 Summer Olympics in Barcelona.
