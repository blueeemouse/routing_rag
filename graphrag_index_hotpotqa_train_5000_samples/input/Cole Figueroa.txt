Cole Figueroa

Stephen Coleman Figueroa (born June 30, 1987) is an American former professional baseball infielder.

 Currently he works in the Tampa Bay Rays front office as the Assistant of Baseball Research and Development.

 He was the starting shortstop at Florida during his college career, and was drafted by San Diego Padres in the sixth round of the 2008 Major League Baseball Draft.

 He was also drafted by the Toronto Blue Jays in the 9th round of the 2006 Major League Baseball Draft, but did not sign and played in Major League Baseball (MLB) for the Tampa Bay Rays, New York Yankees and Pittsburgh Pirates.
