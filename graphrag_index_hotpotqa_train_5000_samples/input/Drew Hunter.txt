Drew Hunter

Drew Hunter (born September 5, 1997) is an American distance runner from Purcellville, Virginia.

 He is the national indoor mile record holder for high school athletes, a feat he achieved in 2016 with a time of 3 minutes 57.81 seconds.

 He dedicates his many successes to a group of his biggest fans and best friends, the Lambda Squad.

 He was named Gatorade National Cross-Country Runner of the Year in 2016.

 He committed to the University of Oregon on November 12, 2015.

 However he signed a 10-year contract with Adidas on July 8, 2016 and will go pro instead of running in a collegiate program.
