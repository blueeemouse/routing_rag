K Theory

K Theory is an electronic hip-hop act by Dylan Lewman, which formerly included Dustin Musser and Malcolm Anthony.

 The group was founded by Dylan Lewman and Dustin Musser in 2011.

 They have done official remixes for Flo Rida's "GDFR", Rich Homie Quan's "Flex", Fetty Wap's "Trap Queen", and many more songs.

 Their remixes and originals have over to 100 millions plays across all platforms.
