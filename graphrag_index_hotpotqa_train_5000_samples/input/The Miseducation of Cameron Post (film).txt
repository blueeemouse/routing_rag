The Miseducation of Cameron Post (film)

The Miseducation of Cameron Post is an American-British drama film under production, which is directed by Desiree Akhavan, from a screenplay by Akhavan and Cecilia Frugiuele.

 It is based upon the novel of the same name by Emily M. Danforth.

 It stars Chloë Grace Moretz, Sasha Lane, John Gallagher, Jr., Forrest Goodluck, Jennifer Ehle and Quinn Shephard.
