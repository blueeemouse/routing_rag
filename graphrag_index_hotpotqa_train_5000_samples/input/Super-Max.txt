Super-Max

Super-Max is a brand of disposable shaving blades, disposable razors and system razors amongst other personal care products for both men and women.

 The brand commenced operations in 1940 and has gained an experience of over 50-years in making shaving blades.

 Super-Max also offers toiletries which ranges from shaving creams, shaving foams, shaving gels, after-shave splash and balm and deodorants.
