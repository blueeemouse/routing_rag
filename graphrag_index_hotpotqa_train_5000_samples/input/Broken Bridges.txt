Broken Bridges

Broken Bridges is a 2006 film starring Toby Keith, Lindsey Haun, Burt Reynolds and Kelly Preston.

 The film, a music-drama, is centered on a fading country singer's return to his hometown near a military base in Tennessee where several young men who were killed in a training exercise on the base were from.

 He is reunited with his former sweetheart and estranged daughter, who returns to the town as well.
