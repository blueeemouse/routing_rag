The Fight (Parks and Recreation)

"The Fight" is the thirteenth episode of the third season of the American comedy television series "Parks and Recreation", and the 43rd overall episode of the series.

 It originally aired on NBC in the United States on May 12, 2011.

 In the episode, the parks department employees become very drunk during a bar outing, where Leslie and Ann have their first major fight.

 Meanwhile, Chris tells Tom he must sell his share in the bar due to a conflict with his government job.

 According to Nielsen Media Research, "The Fight" was seen by an estimated 4.55 million household viewers, a drop from the previous original episode, "Eagleton".
