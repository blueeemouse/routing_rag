Harbin–Manzhouli Railway

Harbin–Manzhouli Railway or Binzhou Railway (), is a double-track electrified arterial railroad in Northeast China between Harbin and Manzhouli on the Russian border.

 The line was originally built by Russia as the western branch of the Chinese Eastern Railway.

 Today, the 934.8 km Binzhou Railway remains a major rail transport corridor across Northeast China, traversing Inner Mongolia and Heilongjiang Province.

 Major cities and towns along route include Manzhouli, Jalainur, Hailar, Dayan, Yakeshi and Zhalantun in Inner Mongolia, and Qiqihar, Daqing, Anda, Zhaodong and Harbin in Heilongjiang.
