The Great Escape (U.S. TV series)

The Great Escape is a reality television series on TNT that premiered on June 24, 2012, at 10:00 pm EDT.

 The competition series, hosted by Rich Eisen, features three two-person teams each week who are dropped "into the middle of their own epic action/adventure movie" and competing for a cash prize.

 The show is produced by Imagine Television directors Ron Howard and Brian Grazer, and "The Amazing Race" producers, Bertram Van Munster and Elise Doganieri.

 The series finished airing on August 26, 2012 and was cancelled in October 2012.
