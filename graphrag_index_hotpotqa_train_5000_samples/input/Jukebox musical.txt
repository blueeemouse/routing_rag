Jukebox musical

A jukebox musical is a stage or film musical that uses previously released popular songs as its musical score.

 Usually the songs have a connection with a particular popular musician or group—because they were either written by, or for, the artists in question, or at least covered by them.

 The songs are contextualized into a dramatic plot: often the biographical story of the performer(s) whose music is featured, although in some cases the plot does not revolve around the particular group at all.
