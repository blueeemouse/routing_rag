Stephanie Mitelman

Stephanie Mitelman Bercovitch (February 25, 1976-) is a Sex Education and Family Life Education professor, public speaker, entrepreneur, sex educator, and author specializing in special education, particularly for youth with autism spectrum disorders.

 She is the owner and creator of Senseez Pillows, a company making vibrating pillows for people with sensory needs, and Sex Ed Mart, a sex education publishing and distribution company specializing in sex education for youth with special needs.

 Stephanie also runs a private sex education practice for youth on the autism spectrum, their parents, and couples who are neurologically diverse.
