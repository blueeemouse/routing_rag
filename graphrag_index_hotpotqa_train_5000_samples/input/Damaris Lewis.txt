Damaris Lewis

Damaris Lewis (born October 10, 1990) is an American model born to West Indian parents from the island of St. Kitts that appeared in the 2009, 2010, and 2011 "Sports Illustrated Swimsuit Issues".

 Lewis was born and raised in Brooklyn, New York.

 She studied with a conservatory-style arts concentration in dance at Fiorello H. LaGuardia High School of Music & Art and Performing Arts.
