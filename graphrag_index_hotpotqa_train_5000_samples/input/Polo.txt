Polo

Polo (Persian: چوگان "chogān") is a team sport played on horseback.

 The objective is to score goals against an opposing team.

 Players score by driving a small white plastic or wooden ball into the opposing team's goal using a long-handled mallet.

 The traditional sport of polo is played on a grass field up to 300 by .

 Each polo team consists of four riders and their mounts.
