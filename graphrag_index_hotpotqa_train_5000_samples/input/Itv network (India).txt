Itv network (India)

"iTV Network" (Information TV Pvt Ltd) is one of the fastest growing media conglomerates in India.

 It is founded by Kartikeya Sharma.

 Presently, this group owns seven news channels NewsX, India News,India News Haryana, India News Punjab, India News UP/UK, India News Rajasthan, India News MP/CG a Hindi daily paper, "Aaj Samaj", and a weekly newspaper, "Sunday Guardian".

 ITV School Of Media And Management is also a part of this group.
