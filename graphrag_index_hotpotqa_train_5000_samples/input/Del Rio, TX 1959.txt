Del Rio, TX 1959

Del Rio, TX 1959 is the debut album of American country music artist Radney Foster.

 It was released in 1992 (see 1992 in country music) on the Arista Nashville label, and it produced five singles for Foster on the "Billboard" country charts: "Just Call Me Lonesome", "Nobody Wins", "Easier Said Than Done", "Hammer and Nails", and "Closing Time".

 All of these except "Closing Time" were Top 40 hits on the country charts; "Nobody Wins" was the highest-charting, reaching #2.
