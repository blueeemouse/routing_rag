Thiel Fellowship

The Thiel Fellowship (originally named 20 under 20) is a fellowship created by Peter Thiel through the Thiel Foundation.

 The fellowship is intended for students under the age of 23 and offers them a total of $100,000 over two years, as well as guidance and other resources, to drop out of school and pursue other work, which could involve scientific research, creating a startup, or working on a social movement.

 Selection for the fellowship is through a competitive annual process, with about 20–25 fellows selected annually.

 It has a selection rate below 1%.
