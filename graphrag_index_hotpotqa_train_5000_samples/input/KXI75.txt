KXI75

KXI75 is an older NOAA Weather Radio station that serves parts of the north Georgia mountains with 300 watts on 162.475 MHz from a location near Blue Ridge, Georgia.

 It is programmed from NWSFO Peachtree City, the National Weather Service forecast office at Falcon Field airport in Peachtree City, originally at Hartsfield International Airport (now Hartsfield-Jackson) in Atlanta.

 It is now simulcast on newer station KXI22 located atop Brasstown Bald near Young Harris, Georgia, and the two cover much of the Blue Ridge Mountains in the far north-central part of the state.
