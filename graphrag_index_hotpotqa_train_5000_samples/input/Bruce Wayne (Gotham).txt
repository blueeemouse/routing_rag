Bruce Wayne (Gotham)

Bruce Wayne is a fictional character appearing in the Fox series "Gotham".

 The Bruce Wayne character is the alter-ego of the superhero Batman, created by Bill Finger and Bob Kane, as "Gotham" portrays Bruce's teenage years and transition into adapting the Batman persona in later years.

 "Gotham" marks the second time Batman was adapted for television after the 1960s Batman series, though the lead character is James Gordon.

 Bruce is portrayed by David Mazouz.
