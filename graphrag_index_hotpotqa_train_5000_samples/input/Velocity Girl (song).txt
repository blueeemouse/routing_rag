Velocity Girl (song)

"Velocity Girl" is a song by British alternative rock band Primal Scream, originally released as the B-side to their second single, "Crystal Crescent", in 1986.

 Shortly preceding its release, Primal Scream’s frontman, Bobby Gillespie, left his post as the drummer of The Jesus and Mary Chain, recording only one album with them, the influential noise pop release "Psychocandy" (1985).

 Disentangled from their major label "whirlwind", he resumed activity with the lesser known Primal Scream, proceeding to, as "Pitchfork Media" later described it, "reduce the pop song to its subatomic essence: quick, breezy, quirky, and above all, exquisitely small" with "Velocity Girl".
