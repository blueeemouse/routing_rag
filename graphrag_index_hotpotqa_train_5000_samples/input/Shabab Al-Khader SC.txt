Shabab Al-Khader SC

Shabab Al-Khader Sports Club Arabic: نادي شباب الخضر الرياضي‎ ‎ ) is a football club based in the town of Al-Khader, within the Bethlehem Governorate in the Palestinian West Bank.

 The club currently compete in the West Bank Premier League, one of the top two divisions of the Palestinian Football Association which runs parallel to the Gaza Strip League.

 Shabab Al-Khadr plays out of Al-Khader Stadium.
