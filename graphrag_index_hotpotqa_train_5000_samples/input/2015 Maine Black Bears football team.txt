2015 Maine Black Bears football team

The 2015 Maine Black Bears football team represented the University of Maine in the 2015 NCAA Division I FCS football season.

 They were led by 23rd-year head coach Jack Cosgrove and played their home games at Alfond Stadium.

 They were a member of the Colonial Athletic Association.

 They finished the season 3–8, 3–5 in CAA play to finish in a four-way tie for seventh place.
