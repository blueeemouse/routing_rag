St. Mary's Church, Swillington

St. Mary's Church is located on Church Lane next to Swillington Primary School, on Wakefield Road, Swillington, West Yorkshire, England.

 There has been a church at this location for at least 900 years.

 The "Domesday Book" of 1086 notes that 'a church is there' but no other records of that building remain.

 The church on the site today is a Grade II* listed building, over 600 years old, built around 1360.

 It is in need of repairs, especially the nave roof, estimated to cost around £250,000.

 The tower was renovated in 1883–1884 with Harehills Stone, which has now weathered almost black, in stark contrast to the creamy yellow of the rest of the exterior.
