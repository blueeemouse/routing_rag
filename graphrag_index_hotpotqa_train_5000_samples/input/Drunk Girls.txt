Drunk Girls

"Drunk Girls" is a song by American rock band LCD Soundsystem.

 It was released as the second single from their third studio album, "This Is Happening" (2010), on May 3, 2010.

 Band frontman James Murphy has described the song as "dumb" but added "I like dumb, short stuff."

 The 7" single features a cover of the song by San Francisco psychedelic rock band Wooden Shjips.
