Amietia wittei

Amietia wittei (common name: Molo frog) is a species of frog in the Pyxicephalidae family.

 It is found in Kenya, Tanzania, and eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo.

 Its type locality is in Molo, Kenya, located near the top of the Mau Escarpment.

 It is a common species associated with montane grasslands, perhaps forests, and also found in a town.

 It is found in the Kenyan central highlands and northern Tanzania in the East African montane moorlands and the East African montane forests ecoregions.
