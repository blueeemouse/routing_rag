Hitman 2: Silent Assassin

Hitman 2: Silent Assassin is a stealth video game developed by IO Interactive and published by Eidos Interactive for Microsoft Windows, PlayStation 2, Xbox and GameCube.

 It is the second installment in the "Hitman" video game series and the sequel to "".

 The game was re-released for Windows through the Steam online distribution service and later a DRM-free version was available through GOG.com.

 A commercial success, the game has sold more than 3.7 million copies as of 23 April 2009 and is the best selling "Hitman" game to date.
