A Simple Favor

A Simple Favor is the debut thriller novel by Darcey Bell.

 It was first published on March 21, 2017 by Harper.

 The novel centers around Stephanie, a mom blogger who seeks to uncover the truth behind her best friend Emily's sudden disappearance from their small town in Connecticut.

 Employing the unreliable narrator technique, the story is told through Stephanie, Emily and Emily's husband Sean.
