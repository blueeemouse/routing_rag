Charee Pineda

Charee Pineda, (born Crissha Charee Morrison Pineda on 27 September 1990) is an American - Filipina actress, politician and one of ABS-CBN's Star Magic artists.

 Her career began when she was cast as the "sweetilicious" girl on ABS-CBN's defunct teen sitcom Let's Go.

 She is best known for playing the roles of Marissa Ocampo in Katorse, Jeri Cenarosa in and Rosalie Dimaano in and where she was paired up with JM De Guzman.
