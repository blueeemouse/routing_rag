Golden Nugget Laughlin

Golden Nugget Laughlin (formerly Bobcat Club and Nevada Club) is a hotel and casino located on the banks of the Colorado River in Laughlin, Nevada.

 It is owned and operated by Landry's, Inc.

 It offers a number of restaurants, 300 guest rooms and suites, an expansive casino floor and dedicated meeting space.

 Golden Nugget offers water taxi service from Bullhead City, Arizona, on the opposite side of the river.
