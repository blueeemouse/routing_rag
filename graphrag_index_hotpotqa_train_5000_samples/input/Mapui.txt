Mapui

Mapui Kawlim (alias Wingstar) is a superhero appearing in the Indian comic book "Tinkle".

 It was created by Sean D′mello and Artist Vineet Nair.

 It made a debut on the 35th anniversary issue of the comic book, issue number 639 released in November 2015.

 It is the first superhero from Mizoram.

 The name "Mapui" is a popular pet name among the Mizo people.

 The character originates from Aizawl, the capital city of Mizoram.

 She is the first female superhero from northeast India in the series of "Tinkle".

 The editor Rajani Thindiath described it as a "reluctant superhero" but "has the potential to be the most iconic female superhero characters in Indian comic history."

 She acquires super flight and super strength from gadgets developed by her inventor-father.
