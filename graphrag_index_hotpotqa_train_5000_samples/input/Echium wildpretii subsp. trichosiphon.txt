Echium wildpretii subsp. trichosiphon

Echium wildpretii" subsp.

 "trichosiphon is an "Echium" that is found high up in the area of La Caldera de Taburiente on the Canary Island of La Palma.

 Like its sister plant on Tenerife, this plant forms a large rosette of silvery leaves which eventually give rise to tall spikes of bright pink flowers.

 The plant is biennial and dies after flowering.

 This plant can be grown in gardens, but requires good drainage and fairly mild conditions.

 The plant hates water falling in the centre of the rosette and will often rot in such conditions.
