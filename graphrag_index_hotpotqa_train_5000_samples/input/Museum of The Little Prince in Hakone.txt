Museum of The Little Prince in Hakone

The Museum of The Little Prince in Hakone (星の王子さまミュージアム , Hoshi no Ōjisama Myūjiamu ) (French: "Musée du Petit Prince de Saint-Exupéry à Hakone" ) is a museum in Sengokuhara, Hakone, Kanagawa Prefecture, Japan.

 The museums is dedicated to the character in the story "The Little Prince" by Antoine de Saint-Exupéry.
