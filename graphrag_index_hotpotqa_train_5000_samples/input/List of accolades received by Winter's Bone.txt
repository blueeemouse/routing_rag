List of accolades received by Winter's Bone

"Winter's Bone" is a 2010 independent American drama film directed by Debra Granik.

 Adopted by Granik and Anne Rosellini from the 2006 novel of the same name by author Daniel Woodrell, the movie was released by Roadside Attractions in the United States and Canada on June 11, 2010.

 It grossed over USD $84,000 in its opening weekend on limited release.

 Since then it has grossed over USD $6,500,000 domestically and USD $12,460,000 worldwide.

 "Winter's Bone" was well received by movie critics, with an approval rating of 94 percent on the review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes.

 The film appeared in more than two dozen movie reviewers' Top Ten lists for the best movies of the year.
