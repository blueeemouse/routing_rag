South Park (season 8)

Season eight of "South Park", an American animated television series created by Trey Parker and Matt Stone, began airing on March 17, 2004.

 The eighth season concluded after 14 episodes on December 15, 2004, and was written and directed by Trey Parker.

 The season deals with various topics that were relevant at the time of release.

 The episodes portray a spectrum of topics, from effect of large scale retails corporations to immigration.
