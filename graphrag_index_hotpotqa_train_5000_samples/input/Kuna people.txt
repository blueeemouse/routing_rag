Kuna people

The Kuna live in three politically autonomous "comarcas" or reservations in Panama, and in a few small villages in Colombia.

 There are also communities of Kuna people in Panama City, Colón, and other cities.

 The most Kunas live on small islands off the coast of the "comarca" of Kuna Yala known as the San Blas Islands.

 The other two Kuna "comarcas" in Panama are Kuna de Madugandí and Kuna de Wargandí.

 They are Kuna speaking people who once occupied the central region of what is now Panama and the neighboring San Blas Islands and still survive in marginal areas.
