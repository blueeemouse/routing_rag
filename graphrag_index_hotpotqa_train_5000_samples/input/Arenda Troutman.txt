Arenda Troutman

Arenda Troutman (born 1957) was the Democratic alderman of the 20th Ward in Chicago.

 She was appointed to her position by Mayor Richard M. Daley in 1990, to fill a vacancy after the death of Alderman Ernest Jones.

 Troutman was the 16th woman to serve as a Chicago alderman.

 Despite her arrest and indictment on bribery charges, Troutman ran for alderman in 2007; she lost.
