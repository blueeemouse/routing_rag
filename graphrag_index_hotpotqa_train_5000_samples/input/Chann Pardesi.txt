Chann Pardesi

Chan Pardesi is a Punjabi language film released in 1981.

 It was directed by Chitrarth Singh and starred Raj Babbar, Rama Vij, Amrish Puri, Kulbhushan Kharbanda and Om Puri.

 Chan Pardesi was a seminal film that debuted many future Bollywood legends both in front of and behind the camera, and was Raj Babbar's first ever film.

 "Chan Pardesi" won India's National Film Award in 1980 and went on to become a big commercial success when it eventually found distribution in 1981.
