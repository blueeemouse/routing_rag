K-10 (Kansas highway)

K-10 is a 38-mile (61 km) state highway in the U.S. state of Kansas.

 It was originally designated in 1929.

 It is mostly a controlled-access freeway, linking Lawrence to Lenexa.

 It provides an important toll-free alternate route to Interstate 70 (the Kansas Turnpike).

 Several scenes for the TV-movie "The Day After" were filmed on the highway in 1982 portraying a mass exodus evacuating the Kansas City area on I-70.
