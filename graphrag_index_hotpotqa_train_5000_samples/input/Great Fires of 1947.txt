Great Fires of 1947

The Great Fires of 1947 were a series of forest fires in the State of Maine in the United States that destroyed a total area of 17188 acre of wooded land on Mount Desert Island and 200000 acre statewide.

 Collectively, the fires killed a total of 16 people.

 This disaster is an important part of the local history of the York County and Mount Desert Island areas.
