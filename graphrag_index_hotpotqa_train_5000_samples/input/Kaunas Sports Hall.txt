Kaunas Sports Hall

Kaunas Sports Hall (Lithuanian: "Kauno sporto halė" ), also known as the S. Darius and S. Girėnas Hall is the second largest arena of Kaunas, Lithuania.

 It is the first arena built in the residential Žaliakalnis neighbourhood of Kaunas specially for basketball in Europe and is referred to as a ""Mecca" of the Lithuanian basketball".

 The arena's seating capacity is 5,000.

 Initial seating capacity was 3,500 and the other spectators (up to 11,000) used to have standing room.

 Its length is 62.8 meters and its width is 61 meters.

 It is a part of the S. Darius and S. Girėnas sport center, which also includes the nearby national stadium.
