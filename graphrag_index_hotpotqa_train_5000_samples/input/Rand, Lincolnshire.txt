Rand, Lincolnshire

Rand is a small village and civil parish in the West Lindsey district of Lincolnshire, England.

 It is situated approximately 9 mi north-east from the city of Lincoln and approximately 2 mi west from Wragby, and near the A158 road from Lincoln to Skegness.

 The nearest large town is Market Rasen, about 5 mi north-east.

 The village is 87 ft above sea level.

 The population is listed under Goltho.
