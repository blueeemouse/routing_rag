Listen (Beyoncé song)

"Listen" is a song recorded by American singer Beyoncé.

 The song was written by Henry Krieger, Scott Cutler, Anne Preven, and Beyoncé, and produced by The Underdogs, Matt Sullivan and Randy Spendlove for the 2006 musical film "Dreamgirls", in which Beyoncé's character Deena Jones sings the song in an expression of independence from her controlling husband.

 Columbia Records released "Listen" as the lead single from the "" soundtrack album on December 5, 2006.

 It additionally appeared as a hidden track on international editions, and on the deluxe edition of Beyoncé's second solo studio album, "B'Day".

 The Spanish version of the song, "Oye", was released on the EP, "Irreemplazable", and the Spanish deluxe edition of "B'Day".
