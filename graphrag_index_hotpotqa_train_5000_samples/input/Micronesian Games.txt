Micronesian Games

The Micronesian Games (or "Micro Games", "MicroGames") are a quadrennial international multi-sport event within the Micronesian region.

 The Games were first held in 1969 in Saipan (Northern Mariana Islands).

 The 2010 Micronesian Games were initially due to be held in Majuro (Marshall Islands), until the hosts withdrew.

 The 2010 Games were hosted by Palau.

 The Federated States of Micronesia won the bidding to host the 2014 Micronesian Games in Pohnpei State, and later won again against CNMI for the 2018 Micronesian Games to be held in Yap State.
