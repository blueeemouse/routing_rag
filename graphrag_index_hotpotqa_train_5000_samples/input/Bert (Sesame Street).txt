Bert (Sesame Street)

Bert is a yellow Muppet character on the long-running children's television show, "Sesame Street".

 Bert was originally performed by Frank Oz.

 Since 1997, Muppeteer Eric Jacobson has been phased in as Bert's primary performer, like Grover, although Frank Oz still performs Bert occasionally.

 Bert has also made cameo appearances within "The Muppets" franchise, including "The Muppet Show", "The Muppet Movie", and "The Muppets Take Manhattan".
