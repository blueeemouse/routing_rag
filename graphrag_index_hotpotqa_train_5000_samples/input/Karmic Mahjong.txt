Karmic Mahjong

Karmic Mahjong () is a 2006 Chinese comedy film directed by Wang Guangli.

 It stars Francis Ng as a mechanic from Chengdu plagued by bad luck and Cherrie Ying as a young woman who appears to share the same affliction.

 The film also features cameos by the prominent Chinese directors Wang Xiaoshuai and Jia Zhangke.
