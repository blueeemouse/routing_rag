Off the Wall

Off the Wall is the fifth studio album by American singer Michael Jackson, released on August 10, 1979 in the United States by Epic Records and internationally by CBS Records.

 It was his nineteenth album in total since "Diana Ross Presents The Jackson 5" (1969), as well as his first album released through Epic Records, the label he would record on until his death roughly 30 years later.

 It was released following Jackson's critically well-received film performance in "The Wiz".

 While working on that project, Jackson and Quincy Jones had become friends, and Jones agreed to work with Jackson on his next studio album.
