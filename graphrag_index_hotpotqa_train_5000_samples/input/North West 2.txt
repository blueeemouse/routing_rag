North West 2

North West 2 was an English Rugby Union league which was at the eighth tier of the domestic competition and was available to teams in North West England.

 Promoted teams moved up to North West 1 while relegated teams dropped to North West 3.

 The division was abolished at the end of the 1999-00 season due to RFU restructuring with teams being transferred to their relevant regional leagues such as South Lancs/Cheshire 1 or North Lancs/Cumbria.
