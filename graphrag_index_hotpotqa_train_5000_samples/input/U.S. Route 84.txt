U.S. Route 84

U.S. Route 84 (US 84) is an east–west U.S. Highway.

 It started as a short Georgia–Alabama route in the original 1926 scheme, but by 1941 it had been extended all the way to Colorado.

 The highway's eastern terminus is a short distance east of Midway, Georgia, at an interchange with Interstate 95.

 The road continues toward the nearby Atlantic Ocean as a county road.

 Its western terminus is in Pagosa Springs, Colorado, at an intersection with U.S. 160.
