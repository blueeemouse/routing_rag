Mykal Cushnie

Michael Edward Cushnie (born 28 December 1980) also known as Mykal Cushnie, is a Jamaican film director, film producer and editor.

 He is most recognized for his work on "Magnum Kings and Queens of Dancehall" - the Jamaican dancehall version of "American Idol", "The Wray & Nephew Contender" - The Jamaican version of Mark Burnett's original boxing reality series "The Contender", and "Mission Catwalk"- The Jamaican version of "Project Runway".

 He is the CEO of DSE Media and founder of Edward Cushnie Films.
