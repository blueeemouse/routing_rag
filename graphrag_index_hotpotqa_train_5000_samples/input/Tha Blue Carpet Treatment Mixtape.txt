Tha Blue Carpet Treatment Mixtape

Tha Blue Carpet Treatment Mixtape is a mixtape by American West Coast hip hop artist Snoop Dogg; it was released on November 18, 2006.

 The mixtape contains appearances from Nate Dogg, Ice Cube, Young Jeezy, Game, Xzibit, Flavor Flav, B. Real, Warzone, Western Union, Daz Dillinger, E-40, Goldie Loc, Kurupt, MC Eiht, Swizz Beats, Lil Wayne, Big Daddy Kane and Katt Williams.

 "Tha Blue Carpet Treatment Mixtape" was released as a prelude to his eighth studio album, "Tha Blue Carpet Treatment".
