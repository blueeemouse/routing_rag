The King of Pigs

The King of Pigs () is a 2011 South Korean animated drama film based on a true story, directed by Yeon Sang-ho.

 It won three awards at the 2011 Busan International Film Festival.

 The film was selected to be screened in the Directors' Fortnight section at the 2012 Cannes Film Festival.

 One primary trait of the film is that its events are based on actual accounts, accurately depicted down to every last detail.
