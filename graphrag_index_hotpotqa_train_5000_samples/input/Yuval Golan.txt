Yuval Golan

Yuval Golan is an Israeli materials scientist who works at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev (BGU).

 Golan, a professor of materials engineering, studies materials at the nanoscale level and focuses on their synthesis, characterization and applications.

 Golan is the Director of the Ilse Katz Institute for Nanoscale Science and Technology, and chairman of the synchrotron committee of the Israeli Academy of Sciences and Humanities.
