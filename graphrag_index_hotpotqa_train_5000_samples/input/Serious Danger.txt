Serious Danger

Serious Danger (born Richard Phillips) was a speed garage artist and record producer.

 First becoming prominent in 1997, he was quite popular in the London area.

 Signed to Fresh Records, his best-known releases are "Deeper" (1997) and "High Noon" (1998).

 He also remixed "God Is a DJ" (1998) by Faithless.

 "Deeper" and "High Noon" both reached the UK Singles Chart peaking at #40 and #54 respectively.
