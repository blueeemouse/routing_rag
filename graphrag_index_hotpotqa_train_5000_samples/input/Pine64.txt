Pine64

Pine64 is a family of single-board computers and the name of the company that produces them.

 Their first single-board computer, the Pine A64, was designed to compete with the Raspberry Pi in both power and price.

 Its name descends from the constant variables pi and Euler's constant.

 The A64 was first funded through a Kickstarter crowdfunding drive in December 2015, where the company raised over $1.4 million.
