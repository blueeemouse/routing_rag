Lakshyam (2007 film)

Lakshyam (English:Target) is a 2007 Telugu Action film, produced by Nallamalupu Srinivas (Bujji) on Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Productions banner and directed by Srivass.

 Starring Gopichand, Jagapati Babu, Anushka in lead roles and music composed by Mani Sharma.

 The film recorded as "Super Hit" at box office.

 It was later remade in Tamil as "Maanja Velu", in Bengali as "Fighter" and in Kannada as "Varadhanayaka".

 The movie was dubbed in Hindi as "Bhai - The Lion" and in Malayalam as "Chandu - The Hot Her".
