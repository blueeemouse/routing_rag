Ravi Thornton

Ravi Thornton is a British author, graphic novelist, playwright and founder of creative technology company Ziggy’s Wish Ltd.

 Her notable works including graphic novel "The Tale of Brin & Bent and Minno Marlybone" and multi-part project "HOAX", consisting of musical stage play "HOAX My Lonely Heart", graphic novel "HOAX Psychosis Blues", and narrative app "HOAX Our Right to Hope"; all based on the life and poetry of her late brother and now used as tools to reduce mental health stigma.
