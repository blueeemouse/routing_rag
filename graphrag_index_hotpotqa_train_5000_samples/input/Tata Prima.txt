Tata Prima

Tata Prima is a range of heavy trucks produced by Tata Daewoo, a wholly owned subsidiary of Tata Motors of India.

 It was first introduced in 2008 as the company's 'global' truck.Tata Prima was the winner of the 'Commercial Vehicle of the Year' at the Apollo Commercial Vehicles Awards, 2010 and 2012.

The 'HCV (Cargo) Truck of the Year,2010' and the 'HCV (Rigid) Truck of the Year, 2012'.
