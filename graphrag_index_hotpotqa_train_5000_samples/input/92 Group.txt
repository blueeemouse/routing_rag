92 Group

The 92 Group is a right-wing grouping within the British Conservative Party.

 They are so named because they would meet at Conservative MP Sir Patrick Wall's home, 92 Cheyne Walk in Chelsea, London.

 It was founded in 1964 in order to "keep the Conservative Party conservative" and membership is by invitation only.

 During the period of Margaret Thatcher's leadership of the Conservative Party it was a prominent supporter of her policies.

 During John Major's premiership it became a focus for Thatcherite MPs dissatisfied with his leadership and was prominent in supporting John Redwood's unsuccessful candidacy against John Major for the leadership of the Conservative Party in 1995.
