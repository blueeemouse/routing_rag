Nick Nanton

Nick Nanton is an award winning director, agent, and author.

 He is a 5 time Emmy Award winner for directing and producing the short films Jacob's Turn, Esperanza, Mi Casa Hogar, and Visioneer.

 Nanton is a 13 time Telly Award winner and has co-authored several best-selling books.

 He is the founder and CEO of The Dicks and Nanton Celebrity Branding Agency.
