Legally Blondes

Legally Blondes (also known as Legally Blonde 3) is a 2009 film of the "Legally Blonde" series from MGM.

 The character Elle Woods, previously played by Reese Witherspoon, does not appear in the film, but is mentioned several times.

 Witherspoon produced the film.

 The film aired on ABC Family and Disney Channel, and is directed towards a younger audience than the first two films.

 The film stars Camilla and Rebecca Rosso as Elle Woods' British twin cousins.

 The film was universally panned by film critics, mostly for the film's little to no connection with the first two films, and is considered to be one of the worst direct-to-DVD sequels of all time.
