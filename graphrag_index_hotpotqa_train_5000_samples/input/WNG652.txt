WNG652

WNG652 is a NOAA Weather Radio ("Radio del Tiempo de NOAA" in Spanish) station that serves the El Paso metropolitan area and surrounding cities.

 It is programmed from the National Weather Service forecast office in El Paso, Texas with its transmitter located in El Paso.

 It broadcasts weather and hazard information in Spanish for El Paso & Hudspeth counties in Texas and Doña Ana & Otero Counties in New Mexico.

 It is one of few NOAA Weather Radio stations across the United States to broadcast in a Spanish language.
