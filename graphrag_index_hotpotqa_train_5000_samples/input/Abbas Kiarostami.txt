Abbas Kiarostami

Abbas Kiarostami (Persian: عباس کیارستمی‎ ‎ ; 22 June 1940 – 4 July 2016) was an Iranian film director, screenwriter, photographer and film producer.

 An active film-maker from 1970, Kiarostami had been involved in over forty films, including shorts and documentaries.

 Kiarostami attained critical acclaim for directing the "Koker trilogy" (1987–94), "Close-Up" (1990), "Taste of Cherry" (1997) – which was awarded the Palme d'Or at the Cannes Film Festival that year – and "The Wind Will Carry Us" (1999).

 In his later works, "Certified Copy" (2010) and "Like Someone in Love" (2012), he filmed for the first time outside Iran: in Italy and Japan, respectively.
