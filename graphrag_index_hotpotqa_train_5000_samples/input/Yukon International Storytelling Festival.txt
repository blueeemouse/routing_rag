Yukon International Storytelling Festival

The Yukon International Storytelling Festival was held every Summer in Whitehorse, Yukon, generally in an outdoor setting.

 Cofounders of the storytelling festival were storytellers Louise Profeit-Leblanc and Anne Taylor.

 Profeit-Leblanc, from the Northern Tutchone Nation, was the niece of Angela Sidney {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (1902 – 1991), one the Yukon's last Tagish.

 Sidney had devoted her life to preserving the stories of the Tagish of Southern Yukon, Profeit-Leblanc and Taylor were motivated to found a more local venue for sharing Yukon stories when they realized that Sidney had had to travel in 1984 Toronto Festival of Storytelling to disseminate her peoples' stories to a world audience.

 In 1987 interested parties came together to plan the first Yukon Storytelling Festival in 1988.

 It later grew beyond the scope of Yukon and Canada to attract storytellers from all over the world with an emphasis on native peoples storytelling and circumpolar countries.
