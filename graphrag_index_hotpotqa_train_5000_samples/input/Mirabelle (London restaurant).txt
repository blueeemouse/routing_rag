Mirabelle (London restaurant)

Mirabelle was a restaurant in the Mayfair area of London.

 It opened in 1936, and became popular during the 1950s and 1960s, with some celebrities being regulars.

 Chef Marco Pierre White bought it and owned it from 1998 to 2007, when there wetter again reports of celebrities dining at the restaurant.

 It was sold in 2007.

 It earned a Michelin star in 2008 under head chef Charlie Rushton, and the star was kept until its closure for refurbishment later in 2008.

 It remained closed until the site was demolished in 2016/17.
