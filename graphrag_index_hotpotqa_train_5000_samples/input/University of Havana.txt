University of Havana

The University of Havana or UH (in Spanish, "Universidad de La Habana") is a university located in the Vedado district of Havana, the capital of the Republic of Cuba.

 Founded on January 5, 1728, the university is the oldest in Cuba, and one of the first to be founded in the Americas.

 Originally a religious institution, today the University of Havana has 15 faculties (colleges) at its Havana campus and distance learning centers throughout Cuba.
