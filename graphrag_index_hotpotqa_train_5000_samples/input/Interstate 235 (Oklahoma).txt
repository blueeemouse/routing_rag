Interstate 235 (Oklahoma)

Interstate 235 (abbreviated I-235) in Oklahoma is also known as the Centennial Expressway or the I-235 Central Expressway.

 The spur route of Interstate 35 is a 5.4-mile-long north–south alignment in central and north-central Oklahoma City.

 It connects northbound to U.S. Highway 77 to suburban Edmond and southbound at Interstate 44 on to Interstate 35 and the I-40 Crosstown Expressway near downtown Oklahoma City.

 U.S. Highway 77 is concurrent with I-235 for the entire route.

 South of its junction with I-40, I-235 becomes Interstate 35.
