WisconsinEye

WisconsinEye is a non-profit, private public affairs cable network in the state of Wisconsin, United States.

 The network airs gavel-to-gavel coverage of the Wisconsin Legislature, including floor sessions of the Wisconsin State Assembly and Wisconsin State Senate, plus committee meetings and other programs of state interest such as panels, town halls, and programs about state history.

 The coverage is available live both on the cable network as well as through the WisconsinEye website.
