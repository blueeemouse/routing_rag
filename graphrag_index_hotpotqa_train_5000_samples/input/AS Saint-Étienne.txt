AS Saint-Étienne

Association Sportive de Saint-Étienne Loire (] ; commonly known as AS Saint-Étienne, ASSE, or simply Saint-Étienne) is a French association football club based in Saint-Étienne.

 The club was founded in 1933 and currently plays in Ligue 1, the top division of French football.

 Saint-Étienne plays its home matches at the Stade Geoffroy-Guichard.

 The team is managed by Óscar García and captained by Loïc Perrin, who started his career at the club in 1996.

 Saint-Étienne is known as "Les Verts" meaning "the Greens" due to its home colours.
