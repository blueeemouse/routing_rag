1980 Argentine Grand Prix

The 1980 Argentine Grand Prix was a Formula One motor race held on 13 January 1980 at the Autodromo Municipal Ciudad de Buenos Aires in Argentina.

 It was the opening round of the 1980 Formula One season.

 The race was the 16th Argentine Grand Prix.

 It was the sixth to be held on the #15 variation of this racing facility in Buenos Aires.

 The race was held over 53 laps of the 5.81-kilometre circuit for a total race distance of 308 kilometres.
