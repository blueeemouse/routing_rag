Jeremy Hoad

Jeremy Hoad is best known as co-author of the BBC Doctor Who novel, "The Blue Angel" (with partner, Paul Magrs).

 The Blue Angel has been adapted to script form by Piers Britton and was used as the basis of a course at the University of Redlands in Southern California in 2007.

 On 29th April 2016, Hoad admitted in an interview on Manchester's All FM to having very limited involvement in the writing of the novel, and stated that his role was largely limited to proof reading.
