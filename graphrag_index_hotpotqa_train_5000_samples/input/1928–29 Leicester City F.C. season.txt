1928–29 Leicester City F.C. season

The 1928–29 season was Leicester City's 31st season in the Football League and their 5th in the First Division.

 Led by their formidable strike force of Ernie Hine, Arthur Chandler and Arthur Lochhead (all three of whom are still placed today among Leicester's top five goalscorers of all-time) and inspirational captain and creative lynch-pin Johnny Duncan, Leicester finished runners-up in the First Division, just one point behind winners The Wednesday.

 It was the club's highest ever league finish until the 2015-16 Season where the team won their first ever top division championship.
