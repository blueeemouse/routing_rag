Finnish hip hop

Finnish hip hop or rap (Finnish: "suomiräp, räp" ) or ("suomirap, rap") is a variant of hip hop music from Finland.

 The rapping (räppäys) is mainly done in Finnish.

 Many rappers use Helsinki slang words; general slang and Finnish dialects are used too by some artists.

 Many early rappers (Finnish: "räppäri" ) used the English language.

 Some Finnish rap was already recorded in the early 1980s and some popular artists emerged in the early 1990s but it was not until the 2000s that it finally became a long-lasting phenomenon in the form of a wider scene and numerous popular performers.
