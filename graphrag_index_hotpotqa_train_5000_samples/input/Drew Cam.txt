Drew Cam

"Drew Cam" is the ninth episode of the fifth season of the American sitcom "The Drew Carey Show", and the 110th overall.

 The episode sees Drew (Drew Carey) becoming a 24-hour salesman for the Winfred-Louder department store.

 Webcams are installed in his house and he has to promote the store's range of appliances.

 While Drew is out, the webcams continue to stream events that occur in his house.

 When the viewers become bored of Drew's life, his friend Kate O'Brien (Christa Miller) is hired to play his girlfriend for the show.

 Isabel (Rosa Blasi) replaces Kate, who becomes jealous.

 She eventually tells Drew she loves him during the webcast.
