U.D. Oliveirense

União Desportiva Oliveirense, commonly known as simply as Oliveirense, is a Portuguese sports club from the city of Oliveira de Azeméis, Aveiro.

 The club was founded on 25 October 1922.

 The club currently plays at the Estádio Carlos Osório which holds a seating capacity of 4,000.

 As a sports club it fields very successful teams in rink hockey and basketball.

 Its rink hockey team has won the Taça de Portugal on three occasions whilst its basketball team has won the Portuguese Basketball SuperCup and the Portuguese Basketball Cup.

 The club currently plays in the LigaPro.
