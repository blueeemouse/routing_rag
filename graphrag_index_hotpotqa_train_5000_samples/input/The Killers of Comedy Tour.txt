The Killers of Comedy Tour

The Killers of Comedy Tour features cast and regulars from "The Howard Stern Show" such as Reverend Bob Levy, Sal Governale, Richard Christy, Jim Florentine, Yucko the Clown and Shuli performing stand up comedy.

 On occasion, Beetlejuice, Gary the Conqueror, Bigfoot and The Iron Sheik participate.

 A recurring joke is Bob Levy eating blue cheese or whipped cream out of a woman's rear end.

 It also featured up and coming stand up comedians like John Tole & Brad Thacker who toured & hosted the shows.
