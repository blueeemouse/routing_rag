Illinois Route 92

Illinois Route 92 (IL 92 or Route 92) is an east–west state highway in northwest Illinois.

 It runs from the Norbert F. Beckey Bridge across the Mississippi River where it meets the eastern end of Iowa Highway 92, east to U.S. Route 34 in La Moille.

 This is a distance of 106.41 mi .

 Illinois Route 92 is part of a continuous 886 mi four-state "Highway 92" which begins in Torrington, Wyoming, goes through Nebraska and Iowa and ends in La Moille, Illinois.
