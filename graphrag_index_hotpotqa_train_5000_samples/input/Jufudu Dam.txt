Jufudu Dam

The Jufudu Dam (居甫渡) is a gravity dam on the Lixian River, bordering the counties of Yuanjiang and Mojiang in Yunnan Province, China.

 It is located 56 km from Pu'er City.

 The primary purpose of the dam is hydroelectric power generation and it supports a 285 MW power station.

 Construction began in September 2004, and the three 95 MW generators were commissioned in December 2008.

 It is the fifth dam in the Lixian cascade.
