Cerro de los Ángeles

The Cerro de los Ángeles ("Hill of the Angels") is a famous hill located in Getafe, Spain, about 10 km south of Madrid.

 The site is famous for being considered the geographic centre of the Iberian Peninsula.

 On top of the hill there is a 14th-century monastery named Our Lady of the Angels ("Nuestra Señora de los Ángeles"), as well as the Monument to the Sacred Heart of Jesus ("Monumento al Sagrado Corazón"), built in 1919 to dedicate the country and inaugurated by king Alfonso XIII.
