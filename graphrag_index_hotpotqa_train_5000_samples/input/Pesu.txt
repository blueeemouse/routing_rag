Pesu

Pesu (Tamil: பேசு ; English: Speak! )

 is an unreleased Indian Tamil romance film written and directed by debutant Ra.

 Cyril.

 It stars newcomers J. Vignesh and Vibha in the lead, and Jayaprakash, comedian M. S. Baskar, Mahadevan and Ajay Ratnam in supporting roles, whilst featuring film score and soundtrack by noted composer Yuvan Shankar Raja.

 The film was in its post-production stage before it failed to hit theatres for unknown reasons.
