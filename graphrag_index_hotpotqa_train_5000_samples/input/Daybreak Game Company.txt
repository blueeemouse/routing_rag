Daybreak Game Company

Daybreak Game Company LLC (formerly Sony Online Entertainment Inc.) is an American video game developer and video game publisher.

 It is a subsidiary of Columbus Nova.

 They are known for owning the franchise "EverQuest", "EverQuest II", "The Matrix Online", "PlanetSide", "Star Wars Galaxies", "Star Wars Clone Wars: Adventures", "Free Realms", "", "DC Universe Online", "PlanetSide 2", "", and "", along with more recent acquisitions "Dungeons and Dragons Online" and "Lord of the Rings Online".

 In February 2015, Sony sold Sony Online Entertainment to Columbus Nova, who renamed it Daybreak Game Company.
