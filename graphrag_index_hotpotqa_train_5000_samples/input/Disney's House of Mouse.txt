Disney's House of Mouse

Disney's House of Mouse is an American animated television series, produced by Walt Disney Television Animation (now Disney Television Animation), that originally aired from 2001 to 2003.

 On September 2, 2002, an all night marathon of this show titled "Night of 1000 Toons" aired on Toon Disney.

 "Disney's House of Mouse" is a spin-off of the popular short-lived Disney series "Mickey Mouse Works", and featured many Mouse Works shorts as well as new shorts created specifically for the spin-off series.
