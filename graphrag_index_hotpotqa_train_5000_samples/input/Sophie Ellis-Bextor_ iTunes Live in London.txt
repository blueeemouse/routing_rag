Sophie Ellis-Bextor: iTunes Live in London

Sophie Ellis-Bextor: iTunes Live in London is the debut extended play (EP) by English singer and songwriter Sophie Ellis-Bextor.

 It was released on 3 August 2009 by Fascination Records and Polydor Records, exclusively to the iTunes Store.

 It features five singles taken from her career, including her top 20 collaboration "Heartbreak (Make Me a Dancer)".
