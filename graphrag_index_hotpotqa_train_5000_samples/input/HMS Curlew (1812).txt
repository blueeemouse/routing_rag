HMS Curlew (1812)

HMS "Curlew" (1812) was a Royal Navy "Cruizer" class brig-sloop built by (William) Good & Co., at Bridport and launched in 1812.

 She served with the Navy for only 10 years.

 During the War of 1812 she sailed from Halifax and captured several American privateers.

 Her greatest moment was her role in the 1819 British occupation of Ras al-Khaimah.

 "Curlew" was sold in 1822 in Bombay.

 She then had a 13 or so year career as an opium runner for James Matheson, one of the founders of the firm Jardine Matheson.
