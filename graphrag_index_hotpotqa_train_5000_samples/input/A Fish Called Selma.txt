A Fish Called Selma

"A Fish Called Selma" is the nineteenth episode of "The Simpsons"<nowiki>'</nowiki> seventh season.

 It originally aired on the Fox network in the United States on March 24, 1996.

 The episode features Troy McClure, who attempts to resurrect his acting career by marrying Selma Bouvier.

 Show runners Bill Oakley and Josh Weinstein were fans of Phil Hartman and wished to produce an episode that focused on his character McClure.

 Freelance writer Jack Barth wrote the episode, and Mark Kirkland directed it.
