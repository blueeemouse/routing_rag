Vio (flavoured milk)

Vio marks Coca-Cola India’s entry into the value added dairy category.

 It is the first ready-to-drink flavoured milk product launched by the Coca-Cola Company in India.

 The new offering further expands Coca-Cola’s existing portfolio of beverages, providing consumers more choice.

 There are two variants - VIO Almond Delight and VIO Kesar Treat.

 The milk is sourced from dairy farmers and blended with flavours.
