Love Channel

Love Channel is a 2001 Tamil romance film directed by R. N. Kumaresan.

 The film features newcomer Eashwar and Monica in lead roles, with V. S. Raghavan, Rajeev, Rajesh, R. Sundarrajan, Anand and Dhamu playing supporting roles.

 The film, produced by R. Guru Moorthy and K. Sabari Giri, had musical score by Deva and was released on 22 June 2001 to negative reviews.
