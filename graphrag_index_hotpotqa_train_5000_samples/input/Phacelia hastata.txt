Phacelia hastata

Phacelia hastata is a species of flowering plant in the borage family, Boraginaceae.

 Its common names include silverleaf scorpionweed, silverleaf phacelia, and white-leaf phacelia.

 It is native to western North America from British Columbia and Alberta south to California and east to Nebraska.
