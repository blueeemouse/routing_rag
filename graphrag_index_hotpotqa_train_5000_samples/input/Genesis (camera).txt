Genesis (camera)

The Genesis is Panavision's high-end digital movie camera, which uses a proprietary, full frame 35 mm-width, 1.78:1 (16:9) aspect ratio, 12.4-megapixel, RGB filtered CCD.

 It was first used by a feature crew to shoot Bryan Singer's "Superman Returns" and was shortly followed up thereafter by the World War I film "Flyboys".

 However, the CGI-heavy nature of these two movies meant that ultimately the comedy "Scary Movie 4" was the first theatrically released feature sourced primarily from the Genesis camera.
