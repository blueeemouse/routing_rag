Steve Young (writer)

Steve Young is a television writer for the "Late Show with David Letterman" and "Late Night with David Letterman".

 He is a Harvard University graduate and former writer for the Harvard Lampoon.

 He also wrote "The Simpsons" season eight episode "Hurricane Neddy".

 Steve Young adapted the holiday book "Olive, the Other Reindeer" for the animated holiday special.

 He won an Annie Award in 2000 for his screenplay.

 Young's other television writing credits include "Not Necessarily the News".
