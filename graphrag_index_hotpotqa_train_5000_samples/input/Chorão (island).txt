Chorão (island)

Chorão, also known as Choddnnem or Chodna, is an island along the Mandovi River near Ilhas, Goa, India.

 It is the largest among other 17 islands of Goa.

 It is located 5 km away from the state capital, the city of Panaji and 10 km away from the city of Mapusa.

 Even today, one can visit this area and see the distinct churches, graveyards and buildings all with a Portuguese air.
