Newport (Isle of Wight) bus station

Newport bus station, on the Isle of Wight is located at Orchard Street in Newport town centre.

 Recent redevelopment has seen the former 1960s-built South Street bus station demolished, to make way for a new row of shops, and the current facility built behind that site.

 Newport is used as the hub of Southern Vectis's network of bus routes, so the majority of routes use Newport bus station, currently 11 out of 14.

 On 1 July 2007 the bus station became a no smoking area in its entirety.
