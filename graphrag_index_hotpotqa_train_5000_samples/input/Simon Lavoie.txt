Simon Lavoie

Simon Lavoie (born May 15, 1979) is a Canadian film director and screenwriter from Quebec.

 He is best known as codirector with Mathieu Denis of "Those Who Make Revolution Halfway Only Dig Their Own Graves (Ceux qui font les révolutions à moitié n'ont fait que se creuser un tombeau)", which won the award for Best Canadian Film at the 2016 Toronto International Film Festival and garnered several Canadian Screen Award nominations at the 5th Canadian Screen Awards in 2017, including for Best Picture and Best Director.
