Florida gubernatorial election, 2010

The 2010 Florida gubernatorial election took place on November 2, 2010 to determine the Governor for the State of Florida.

 Republican-turned-Independent incumbent Governor Charlie Crist chose not to run for a second term.

 He instead ran (unsuccessfully) for the Senate seat vacated by Mel Martínez.

 This resulted in an open race for Governor of Florida in which Republican Rick Scott narrowly defeated Democrat Alex Sink.
