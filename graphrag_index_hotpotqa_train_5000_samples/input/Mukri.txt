Mukri

Mukri (5 January 1922 – 4 September 2000) was an Indian film actor, who worked as a comedian in Hindi films.

 Born as Muhammad Umar Mukri in Alibagh, he started his film career from film "Pratima" along with noted film actor Dilip Kumar in 1945.

 Subsequently in career spanning 50 years, he acted in over 600 films.
