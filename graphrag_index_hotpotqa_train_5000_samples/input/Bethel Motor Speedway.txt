Bethel Motor Speedway

Bethel Motor Speedway is a quarter-mile asphalt oval race track, located near the site of the original Woodstock Music Festival in White Lake, New York.

 The track was constructed in 1959, and opened for operations in 1960.

 It has existed under several names, including White Lake Speedway, Catskill Mountain Speedway, Sullivan County Speedway, and Kauneonga Speedway.

 The track closed in 2006 and remained idle through 2007.

 Fred Graf Racing, LLC, purchased the speedway, and it reopened under new ownership bearing the Bethel Motor Speedway name on August 9, 2008.

 Dave and Joanne Rocket, who promoted races at the speedway from 1998 until 2004, returned as promoters under the new owner.

 The speedway was resurfaced in November 2010, providing a new racing surface for the 2011 season.

 For the 2011 season, management of the speedway was assumed by Tracy Chirico, Business Operations Manager; and John Condit, Race Director.
