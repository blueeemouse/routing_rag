Wet Wet Wet

Wet Wet Wet are a Scottish band formed in 1982.

 They scored a number of hits in the UK charts and around the world in the 1980s and 1990s.

 The band is composed of Graeme Clark (bass, vocals), Tommy Cunningham (drums, vocals), and Neil Mitchell (keyboards, vocals).

 Lead vocalist and founding member Marti Pellow quit the band in 2017.

 A fifth, unofficial member, Graeme Duffin (lead guitar, vocals), has been with them since 1983.

 The band were named Best British Newcomer at the 1988 Brit Awards.
