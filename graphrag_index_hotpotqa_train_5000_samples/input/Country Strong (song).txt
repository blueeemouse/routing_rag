Country Strong (song)

"Country Strong" is a song written by Jennifer Hanson, Tony Martin and Mark Nesler.

 It was recorded by singer Britni Hoover in 2007 for her debut album under the same name.

 Then it was recorded again by actress Gwyneth Paltrow for the movie "Country Strong" in which Paltrow stars.

 Vince Gill and Patty Griffin also provide background vocals for the song.

 The song is included on the movie's soundtrack that was released on October 26, 2010.
