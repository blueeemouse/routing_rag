Prit Buttar

Prit Buttar is a British general practitioner and writer.

 He has written five books: "Battleground Prussia: The Assault on Germany's Eastern Front 1944-45" (2010), "Between Giants: The Battle of the Baltics in World War II" (2013), "Collision of Empires: The War on the Eastern Front in 1914" (2014), "Germany Ascendant: The Eastern Front 1915" (2015), and "Russia's Last Gasp: The Eastern Front 1916-17" (2016).

 Dr. Buttar was Senior Partner at Abingdon Surgery until he moved to Scotland in late 2017.
