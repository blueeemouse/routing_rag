United States presidential election, 1988

The United States presidential election of 1988 was the 51st quadrennial United States presidential election.

 It was held on Tuesday, November 8, 1988.

 Incumbent Vice President George H. W. Bush won the Republican nomination, and chose Senator Dan Quayle of Indiana as his running mate.

 The Democrats nominated Governor Michael Dukakis of Massachusetts, with Senator Lloyd Bentsen of Texas – who had coincidentally defeated Bush in a U.S. Senate race 18 years earlier – as his running mate.
