All for Love (song)

"All for Love" is a song written by Bryan Adams, Robert John "Mutt" Lange and Michael Kamen for the soundtrack " The Three Musketeers: Original Motion Picture Soundtrack".

 It was performed by Bryan Adams, Rod Stewart and Sting.

 The song was released as a CD single in the United States on November 16, 1993.

 It was a worldwide hit, reaching number one across Europe and North America.
