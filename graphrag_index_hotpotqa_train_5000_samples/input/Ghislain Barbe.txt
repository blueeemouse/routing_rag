Ghislain Barbe

Ghislain Barbe is a Canadian illustrator and artist.

 He is best known for designing the visual aspects of the Heavy Gear science fiction franchise in the 1990s and early 2000s.

 He was also responsible for the overall graphics of publisher Dream Pod 9's role-playing game lines "Jovian Chronicles" and "Tribe 8", along with some other works, for which he illustrated nearly a hundred books.
