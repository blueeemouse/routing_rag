Kahani Raima Aur Manahil Ki

Kahani Raima Aur Manahil Ki (Story of Raima and Manahil) is a Pakistani drama that started broadcasting on Hum TV.

 It revolves around the lives of two couples, the first couple hate each other while the second couple love each other.

 The series is a comedy tale focusing on how the two sort out their lives, and try their best to get what they want.

 It firstly aired on 25 February 2014 and cast Sajjal Ali, Mehreen Syed, Vasay Chaudhry and Shehroz Sabzwari as its main characters.

 The show is Aired during Tuesday at 9:10pm.

 After Ramadan the timings of the show changed and it aired airing during Wednesday and Thursday at 9:10.

 On 20 August 2014 the series ended after completing 20 episodes.
