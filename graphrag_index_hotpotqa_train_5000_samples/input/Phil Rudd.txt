Phil Rudd

Phillip Hugh Norman "Phil" Rudd (born Phillip Hugh Norman Witschke Rudzevecuis, 19 May 1954) is an Australian drummer, best known for his membership in Australian hard rock band AC/DC from 1975 through 1983, and again from 1994 to 2015.

 Upon the 1977 departure of bass guitarist Mark Evans from AC/DC, Rudd became the only Australian-born member of the band.

 In 2003, he was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame along with the other members of AC/DC.

 Due to ongoing legal problems in New Zealand, Rudd was unable to join the band for the 2015 "Rock or Bust" tour and was replaced by Chris Slade.
