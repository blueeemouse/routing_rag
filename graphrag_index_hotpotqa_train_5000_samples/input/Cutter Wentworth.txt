Cutter Wentworth

Cutter Wentworth is a fictional character on the ABC daytime soap opera "One Life to Live".

 He was portrayed by Josh Kelly from December 29, 2010 to December 29, 2011.

 Kelly reprised the role when new daily episodes of "OLTL" began airing on Hulu via The Online Network from April 29, 2013 to August 19, 2013, until the show (along with "All My Children") were both put on indefinite hiatus due to Prospect Park's lawsuit against ABC.
