Sabre (Eclipse Comics)

Sabre (subtitled "Slow Fade of an Endangered Species"), published in August 1978, is the title of an American graphic novel.

 Created by writer Don McGregor and artist Paul Gulacy, it was published by Eclipse Enterprises, whose eventual division Eclipse Comics would publish a spin-off comic-book series.

 It was one of the first modern graphic novels and the first to be distributed in comic book shops.
