Jason Bourne

Jason Bourne ( ) is a fictional character created by novelist Robert Ludlum.

 Bourne is the antihero in a series of fourteen novels (to 2016) and subsequent film adaptations.

 He first appeared in the novel "The Bourne Identity" (1980), which was adapted for television in 1988.

 The novel was adapted in 2002 into a feature film under the same name and starred Matt Damon in the lead role.
