The White Masai

The White Masai (German: "Die weiße Massai" ), directed by Hermine Huntgeburth, is a 2005 film about a woman named Carola (Nina Hoss) falling in love in Kenya with Maasai Lemalian (Jacky Ido).

 The film is based on an autobiographical novel of the same name by the German born writer Corinne Hofmann.

 In the film version, names have been changed from those in the novel.
