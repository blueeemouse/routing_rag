School for Deaf and Mute (Dushanbe)

The school has 13 classrooms, a library, the director's office, offices for the director's assistants, and a computer lab.

 There are 15 teachers and a student body of 135.

 The school's currect director is Rafik S. H. Odinev.

 Lessons are four days per week for grades 1 through 9 and 2 days per week for grades 10-12.
