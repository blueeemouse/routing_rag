Sergei Gurchenkov

Sergei Sergeyevich Gurchenkov (Russian: Серге́й Серге́евич Гурченков ; born May 1, 1983 in Moscow) is a retired Russian professional footballer.

 As of May 2009, he coaches youth teams of FC Spartak Moscow.

 He made his professional debut in the Russian Second Division in 1999 for FC Spartak-2 Moscow.
