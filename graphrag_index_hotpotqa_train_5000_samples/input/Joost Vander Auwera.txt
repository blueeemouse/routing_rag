Joost Vander Auwera

Dr. Joost Vander Auwera (born 24 January 1957) is a Belgian art historian and museum curator.

 He is Senior Curator at the Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium in Brussels.

 He is the Co-Founder and Project Leader of the international multidisciplinary Jordaens Van Dyck Panel Paintings Project and the Project Leader for the establishment of the visitors centre at the Brueghel House, Brussels.
