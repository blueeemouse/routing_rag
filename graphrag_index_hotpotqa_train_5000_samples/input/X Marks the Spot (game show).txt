X Marks the Spot (game show)

X Marks the Spot was a British radio quiz and panel game, broadcast on BBC Radio 4 between 1998 and 2006.

 It could be likened to be a more light-hearted version of "Round Britain Quiz".

 It was presented by the comedian and author Pete McCarthy until his death in October 2004.

 The writer and broadcaster David Stafford took over the reins from series 7.
