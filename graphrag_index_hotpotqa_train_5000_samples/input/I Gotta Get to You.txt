I Gotta Get to You

"Gotta Get to You" is a song written Blaine Larsen, Jim Lauderdale and Jimmy Ritchey, and recorded by American country music artist George Strait.

 It was released in February 2010 as the 90th single of his career, as well as the third single from his album "Twang".

 The single debuted at #57 on the "Billboard" Hot Country Songs charts for the week of January 16, 2010, at the time his previous single, "Twang," was still on the charts.
