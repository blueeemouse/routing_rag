Karl May (film)

Karl May is a 1974 West German biographical drama film directed by Hans-Jürgen Syberberg, starring Helmut Käutner as the writer Karl May.

 It is considered the second part in Syberberg's "German trilogy", preceded by "" from 1972 and succeeded by "" from 1977.

 Filming took place in Vienna from 4 April to 17 May 1974.

 The budget was 1.1 million Deutsche Mark, of which the broadcaster ZDF provided 700,000 Mark.
