Software copyright in China

Software copyright in China in Chinese Law means that creator or other oblige enjoys exclusive rights of the software under related copyright law.

 It is a civil right and has the common features of all the civil rights.

 Copyright is an exception in intellectual property right because it is owned without individual confirmation.

 This is usually called as principle of “automatic protection”.

 The copyright owner enjoys right of publication, right of authorship, right of consent to use, as well as right of being paid.
