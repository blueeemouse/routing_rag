Sítio do Picapau Amarelo (video game)

Sítio do Picapau Amarelo, is a 1997 platforming video game developed by Tectoy for the Sega Master System.

 It was based on the eponymous series broadcast in the 1970s, which in turn was based on the book series by Monteiro Lobato.

 As Férias Frustradas do Pica-Pau it was released in Brazil only.
