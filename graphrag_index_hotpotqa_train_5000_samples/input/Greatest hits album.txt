Greatest hits album

A greatest hits album, sometimes called a "best of" album or a catalog album, is a compilation of songs by a particular artist or band.

 Most often the track list contains previously released recordings with a high degree of notability.

 However, to increase the appeal, especially to people who already own the original release, it is common to include remixes or alternate takes of popular songs; sometimes even new material (previously unreleased) will function as bonus tracks.

 At times, a greatest hits compilation is the original album release for songs that have themselves been released as a single and charted successfully.
