American Dance Machine

The American Dance Machine was a theatrical dance company created by Lee Theodore, which played on Broadway at the Century Theatre, opening Jun 14, 1978 and in total running 199 performances.

 It was duplicated with a second cast for the American Dance Festival at Duke University in 1978.

 The show was a "Living Archive" of Broadway theatre dance; great theatre dances saved from oblivion.

 Films were made of the performances to preserve original Broadway choreography and can be found at the Lincoln Center Library of the Performing Arts in New York City.

 Broadway legend Gwen Verdon appeared in film versions of the show.

 Choreographers included: Agnes De Mille, Jack Cole, Joe Layton, Michael Kidd, Ron Field, Bob Fosse, Onna White and Peter Gennaro.

 Featured dancers and guest artists included Janet Elber, Carol Estey, Harold Cromer, Liza Gennaro, Patti Mariano, Nancy Chismar, Lawrence Leritz and Donald Young.
