Hans Loewald

Loewald's father, who died shortly after his birth, was a Jewish physician with an interest in dermatology and psychiatry; his mother a gifted musician, who played the piano.

 Loewald did medical training in Germany - where he also studied philosophy with Martin Heidegger, who would influence him with his theory of language - before taking a medical degree from Rome University in 1935.

 He practised psychiatry in Italy until 1939, before moving with his family to the United States.

 There he became fascinated by Freud’s theory, in which he rooted all the features of his own thinking.

 He did not want to create a new psychoanalytic terminology, but, although he used Freud’s terms, he gave them radically new meanings.
