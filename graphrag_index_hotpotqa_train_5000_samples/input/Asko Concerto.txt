Asko Concerto

The Asko Concerto is a concerto for chamber orchestra by the American composer Elliott Carter.

 The work was commissioned by the Dutch chamber group Asko Ensemble, for which the piece is titled.

 It was composed in January 2000 and was first performed in Concertgebouw, Amsterdam on April 26, 2000, by the Asko ensemble under the conductor Oliver Knussen.

 The piece is dedicated to Asko/Schönberg.
