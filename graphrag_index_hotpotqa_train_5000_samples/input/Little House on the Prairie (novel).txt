Little House on the Prairie (novel)

Little House on the Prairie is an autobiographical children's novel by Laura Ingalls Wilder, published in 1935.

 It was the third-published one in the "Little House" series but it continues that of the first, "Little House in the Big Woods" (1932), and is not related to the second.

 Thus it is sometimes called the second one in the series, or the second volume of "the Laura Years".
