Audrey Assad

Audrey Nicole Assad (born July 1, 1983) is an American singer-songwriter and contemporary Christian music artist.

 Her debut album, "The House You're Building", was released through Sparrow Records in July 2010 and went to be named Christian Album of 2010 on Amazon.com and the Christian Breakthrough Album of the Year on iTunes.

 She has worked and toured with other CCM artists such as Chris Tomlin, Tenth Avenue North, Matt Maher and Jars of Clay.

 Her most recent album, "Inheritance", was released in 2016.
