Sheheryar Munawar

Sheheryar Munawar (born 1988,) is a Pakistani actor and film producer.

 He is notable for his role in Hum TV serial "Meray Dard Ko Jo Zuban Miley", for which he won Hum Award for Best New Sensation Television.

 He followed it with roles in male-centric serials such as "Tanhaiyan Naye Silsilay", "Kahi Un Kahi", "Zindagi Gulzar Hai" and "Aasmanon Pay Likha".
