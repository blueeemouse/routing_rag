Commack School District

Commack Union Free School District is a public school district located in Suffolk County, New York and is based throughout Dix Hills and Commack.

 It serves residents of Commack as well as parts of Dix Hills, Smithtown and East Northport.

 It serves approximately 7,800 students.

 There are four primary schools (grades K–2), two intermediate schools (grades 3–5) one middle school (grades 6–8) and one high school (grades 9–12).
