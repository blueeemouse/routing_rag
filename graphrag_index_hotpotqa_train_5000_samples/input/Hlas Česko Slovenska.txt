Hlas Česko Slovenska

Hlas Česko Slovenska (Czech and Slovak for "The Czech / Slovak Voice", literally "The Voice of Czecho Slovakia") is a reality singing competition and version of The Voice of Holland for Czech Republic and Slovakia.

 It is part of the international syndication "The Voice" based on the reality singing competition launched in the Netherlands, created by Dutch television producer John de Mol.

 It kicked off on February 12, 2012 on CME channels TV Nova and Markíza, and is currently airing.
