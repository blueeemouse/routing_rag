St Peter's Church, Edensor

St Peter’s Church, Edensor is a Grade I listed church in Edensor, Derbyshire.

 St Peter's is the closest parish church in the Church of England to Chatsworth House, home of the Dukes of Devonshire, most of whom are buried in the churchyard.

 St Peter’s Church, Edensor is in a joint parish with St Anne’s Church, Beeley.
