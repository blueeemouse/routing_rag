2012 World Indoor Archery Championships

The 2012 World Indoor Target Archery Championships was the 11th edition of the World Indoor Archery Championships.

 The event was held in Las Vegas, United States from February 5 to February 9, 2012 and was organized by International Archery Federation (FITA).

 The event was contested at the South Point Hotel, and the event was followed by the World Archery Festival and the finals of the second Indoor Archery World Cup.
