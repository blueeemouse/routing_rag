Mount Moriah Cemetery (Philadelphia)

Mount Moriah Cemetery is a historic cemetery in southwest Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, along Cobbs Creek.

 It was incorporated on March 27, 1855 and established by an act of the Pennsylvania Legislature.

 The cemetery, which originally occupied 54 acre , was among a number of cemeteries established along the "rural ideal" popular at that time.

 An ornate Romanesque entrance and gatehouse were built of brownstone on Islington Lane, today known as Kingsessing Avenue.
