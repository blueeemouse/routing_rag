Mount Baker (Waputik Mountains)

Mount Baker is a mountain on the Continental Divide, in Alberta and British Columbia, in the Waputik Mountains of the Canadian Rockies.

 It was named in 1898 by J. Norman Collie after his friend and climbing partner George Percival Baker (1855–1951), textile manufacturer, plantsman and gardener, and keen mountaineer.

 Baker described his visit to this area which took place in 1897.

 In this small volume Baker noted that Collie also proposed to name a pass after him.
