Stachyurus praecox var. matsuzakii

Stachyurus praecox" var.

 "matsuzakii (Hachijō-kibushi, ハチジョウキブシ), is a variety of "Stachyurus praecox", and is only found in Japan.

 Compared to the other "Stachyurus praecox" varieties, this variety has thicker branches, longer inflorescences, and larger fruits.
