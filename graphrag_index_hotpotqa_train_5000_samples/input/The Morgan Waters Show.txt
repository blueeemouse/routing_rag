The Morgan Waters Show

The Morgan Waters Show is a six-minute sketch comedy show starring Morgan Waters, made by CBC Television for its afternoon youth programming lineup, next to the 3 minute "Mr. Meaty" and "Yam Roll".

 Morgan goes through a variety of situations with most characters being played by himself, including the badminton-loving Goth Trent, Phillip the Nerd, and the lovable CBC Executive, Boss Boss.

 Guests appearing on the show include "Canadian Idol" hosts Ben Mulroney and Jon Dore, Rick Mercer, the cast of "", Ashlee Simpson, Simple Plan, OK GO, Alexz Johnson, Ed the Sock, and Tyler Kyte among others.
