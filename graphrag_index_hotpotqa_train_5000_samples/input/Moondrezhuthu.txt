Moondrezhuthu

Moondrezhuthu (English: "Three Letters") is a 1968 Tamil-language crime drama film was directed by T. R. Ramanna and produced by T. K. Ramarajan.

 The film's script were written by T. N. Balu.

 Music by T. K. Ramamoorthy assets to the film.

 The film starring Ravichandran Jayalalitha Nagesh Sheela S. A. Ashokan and R. S. Manohar played lead role to protagonist.
