Nicholas Pal Dukagjini

Nicholas Dukagjini (Albanian: "Nikollë Dukagjini" ) was an Albanian nobleman of the Dukagjini family in the 15th century.

 He was the son of Pal Dukagjini, one of the founding members of the League of Lezhë.

 Nicholas Dukagjini fled to Italy after the second Siege of Shkodra in 1479, but is well known for the return to his homeland two years later, together with Skanderbeg’s son Gjon Kastrioti and other noblemen to lead the armed movement against the Ottomans.
