Human (Christina Perri song)

"Human" is a song recorded by American singer-songwriter Christina Perri for her second studio album, "Head or Heart" (2014).

 The song was written by Perri and Martin Johnson, who also produced the song.

 It was released on November 18, 2013 as the lead single for the album.

 Released to generally positive reviews, "Human" has since become a Top 10 Adult Pop hit in the United States, as well as her third top 40 entry on the Billboard Hot 100.

 It was certified Platinum by RIAA in June 2014.
