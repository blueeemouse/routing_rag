Hymn for the Weekend

"Hymn for the Weekend" is a song by British rock band Coldplay with uncredited guest vocals from American singer Beyoncé.

 It was released on 25 January 2016 as the second single from their seventh studio album, "A Head Full of Dreams" (2015).

 The song was written by the members of Coldplay and produced by Rik Simpson, Tim Bergling, Digital Divide, and Stargate.
