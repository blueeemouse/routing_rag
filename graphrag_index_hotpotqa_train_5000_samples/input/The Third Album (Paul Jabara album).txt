The Third Album (Paul Jabara album)

The Third Album is the third studio album by American actor, singer and songwriter Paul Jabara.

 The album includes the single releases "Disco Wedding" and "Never Lose Your Sense of Humor".

 "The Third Album", whose title and cover picture paraphrases the Barbra Streisand album of the same name, was released in 1979 on the Casablanca Records label, and again features guest vocalist Donna Summer.

 In 1979 Jabara also composed the Streisand & Summer duet "No More Tears (Enough Is Enough)".
