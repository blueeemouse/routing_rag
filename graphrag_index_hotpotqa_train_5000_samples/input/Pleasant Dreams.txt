Pleasant Dreams

Pleasant Dreams is the sixth studio album by American punk rock band the Ramones released on July 20, 1981, through Sire Records.

 While the band members wanted Steve Lillywhite to produce, Sire chose Graham Gouldman in an attempt to gain popularity through a well-known recording manager.

 The recording process brought about many conflicts between band members, most notably the strife between Joey Ramone and Johnny Ramone, where Johnny began dating one of Joey's ex-girlfriends.

 There were also disputes about the overall genre of the album, with Johnny leaning towards hard rock and Joey towards pop music.

 Ultimately, the album incorporated a high production value and a variation of tone throughout the album.

 "Pleasant Dreams" featured songs such as "We Want the Airwaves", "She's a Sensation", and "Come On Now", strayed from traditional punk rock and took on different styles.
