Chuck Wendig

Chuck Wendig is an American author, comic book writer, screenwriter, and blogger.

 He is best known for his popular online blog Terribleminds, and for his 2015 "Star Wars" novel "", which debuted at #4 on "The New York Times" Best Seller list and #4 on "USA Today"'s best seller list.

 As of early 2016, Wendig writes "Hyperion" for Marvel and "The Shield" for Dark Circle Comics.
