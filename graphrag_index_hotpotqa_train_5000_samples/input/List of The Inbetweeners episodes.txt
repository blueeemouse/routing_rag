List of The Inbetweeners episodes

"The Inbetweeners" is a BAFTA Award-winning British sitcom created by Damon Beesley and Iain Morris, and broadcast on E4.

 The series follows the lives of four sixth form students – Will McKenzie (Simon Bird), Simon Cooper (Joe Thomas), Jay Cartwright (James Buckley) and Neil Sutherland (Blake Harrison).

 The series is narrated by Will, who acts as the programme's lead character.
