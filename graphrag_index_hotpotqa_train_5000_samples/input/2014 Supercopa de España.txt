2014 Supercopa de España

The 2014 Supercopa de España was a two-legged football match-up played in August 2014 between the champions of 2013–14 La Liga, Atlético Madrid, and the winner of the 2013–14 Copa del Rey, Real Madrid.

 The first leg ended 1–1.

 Atlético won the trophy after beating Real Madrid 1–0 in second leg.

 This was the first time that the Supercopa de España was a Madrid derby, and also the first time since 2008 that the competition did not feature Barcelona team.
