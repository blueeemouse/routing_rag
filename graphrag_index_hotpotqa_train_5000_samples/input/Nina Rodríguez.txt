Nina Rodríguez

Carolina Rodríguez Ferrero (born 15 July 1985), better known as Nina Rodríguez, is a Colombian pop singer-songwriter and model.

 She participated in the National Beauty Contest of Colombia, where she held the position of 1st princess (2nd finalist).

 In 2010 she won the contest for the international Top Model of The World, where she was appointed by the organization of the National Beauty Contest of Colombia.1
