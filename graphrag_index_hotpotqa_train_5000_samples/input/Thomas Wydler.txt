Thomas Wydler

Thomas Wydler (born 9 October 1959, Zurich, Switzerland), is a Swiss musician best known for being a core member of Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds, of which he has been a drummer since 1985.

 Prior to joining them, he was a member of the experimental German band Die Haut.

 Wydler has been present on almost every Bad Seeds album, making his debut appearance on the group's third album "Kicking Against The Pricks" (1986).

 After the departure of founding member Mick Harvey in January 2009, Wydler became the longest-serving member of the Bad Seeds apart from singer Nick Cave.
