Moruya, New South Wales

Moruya is a town in New South Wales, Australia, situated on the Moruya River, on the far south coast situated on the Princes Highway 305 km south of Sydney and 175 km from Canberra.

 At the 2011 census, Moruya had a population of 2,531 people.

 The town relies predominantly on agriculture, aquaculture, and tourism.

 Moruya is administered by the Eurobodalla Shire council and the shire chambers are located in the town.
