Anna M. Peterson

Anna M. Peterson (née Chadwick ; born May 30, 1947) is an American politician who served as Mayor of Salem, the state capital of Oregon, from 2011 until 2016.

 She is married to Edwin J. Peterson, who was Chief Justice of the Oregon Supreme Court from 1983 until 1991.

 A Republican, and the third woman to hold the non-partisan office, Peterson has served as mayor since January 11, 2011, when she succeeded Janet Taylor.
