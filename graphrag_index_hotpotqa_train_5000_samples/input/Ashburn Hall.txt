Ashburn Hall

Ashburn Hall, also known as the Capehart House, is a historic plantation house located near Kittrell, Vance County, North Carolina.

 It was built in the 1840s or early 1850s, and is a two‑story, three‑bay, T‑shaped frame dwelling in a restrained Greek Revival style.

 It features a broad, one‑story pedimented entrance portico, with four spaced, paired fluted Tuscan order columns.
