If You Can Do Anything Else

"If You Can Do Anything Else" is a song written by Billy Livsey and Don Schlitz, and recorded by American country music artist George Strait.

 It was released in February 2001 as the third and final single from his album "George Strait".

 The song reached number 5 on the U.S. "Billboard" Hot Country Singles & Tracks chart in July 2001.

 It also peaked at number 51 on the U.S. "Billboard" Hot 100.
