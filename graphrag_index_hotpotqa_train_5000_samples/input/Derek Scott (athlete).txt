Derek Scott (athlete)

Derek Scott (born November 7, 1985) is an American distance runner and coach.

 Previously ran for Team Indiana Elite based out of Bloomington, Indiana and coached by Robert Chapman.

 2007 NAIA Champion in the 1500 meters.

 Ran at Cornerstone University.

 While at Cornerstone he was a 10x All-American and 2x national runner up.

 One in the indoor mile and the other in the steeplechase.

 As a freshman, he was US JR. runner-up in the steeplechase and qualified for the Track and Field World Junior Championships in Grosseto, Italy.
