2016 Pakistan Cup

The 2016 Pakistan Cup was a five-team one-day cricket tournament based on a draft system.

 It started on 19 April in Faisalabad and included teams from the country's four provinces and a Federal Capital side.

 Sarfraz Ahmed, Shoaib Malik, Younis Khan, Azhar Ali and Misbah-Ul-Haq led the sides from Sindh, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan and Islamabad.

 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa won the tournament after defeating Punjab in the final by 151 runs.
