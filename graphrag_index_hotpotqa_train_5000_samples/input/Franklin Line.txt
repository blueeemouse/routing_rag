Franklin Line

The Franklin Line, part of the MBTA Commuter Rail system, runs from Boston's South Station in a southwesterly direction toward Franklin, Massachusetts, utilizing the Northeast Corridor before splitting off onto the namesake Franklin Branch.

 Most Franklin Line trains connect to the Providence/Stoughton Line at Readville, though some weekday trains use the Dorchester Branch (Fairmount Line) to access South Station.

 Most weekday trains, and all weekend trains, bypass Hyde Park and Plimptonville.

 Several weekday trains originate at Norwood Central or Walpole.

 Trains only serve Foxboro from Boston during special events at Gillette Stadium, but regular service is proposed.
