Dilip Sinha (actor)

Dilip Sinha (Hindi: दिलीप सिन्हा "Dilīpa Sinhā") (born 1951) is an Indian actor and a voice-dubbing artist who principally speaks Hindi and Bhojpuri.

 Dilip Sinha was staying in Marina Guest House Bandra at West Mumbai for 20 years and at the present time, he is currently staying in Goregaon West, Mumbai, India.

 He is married and has 2 children.
