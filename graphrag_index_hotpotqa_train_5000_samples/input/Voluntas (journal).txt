Voluntas (journal)

"VOLUNTAS: International Journal of Voluntary and Nonprofit Organizations" is a peer-reviewed academic journal covering research on the third sector.

 It is the official journal of the International Society for Third-Sector Research.

 It was established in 1990 with Helmut Anheier and Martin Knapp as its founding editors-in-chief.

 Its current editors are Ruth Simsa (University of Vienna, Vienna, Austria) & Taco Brandsen (Radboud University, Nijmegen, the Netherlands).
