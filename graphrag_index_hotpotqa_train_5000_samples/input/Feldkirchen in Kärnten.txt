Feldkirchen in Kärnten

Feldkirchen in Kärnten (Slovene: "Trg" ) is a town in the Austrian state of Carinthia and the capital of the district of the same name.

 It consists of the Katastralgemeinden "Fasching", "Feldkirchen", "Glanhofen", "Gradisch", "Hoefling", "Klein Sankt Veit", "Pernegg", "Rabensdorf", "Sankt Ulrich", "Sittich", "Tschwarzen" and "Waiern".
