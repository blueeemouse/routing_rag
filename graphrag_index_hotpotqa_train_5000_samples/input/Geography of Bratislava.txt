Geography of Bratislava

Bratislava, the capital city of Slovakia, is situated in Central Europe and it is located in the extreme south-west within Slovakia.

 The city borders Austria in the west and Hungary in the south making it the only national capital in the world to border two foreign countries.

 State border with the Czech Republic is only 62 kilometres (38.5 mi) distant.

 Bratislava lies on the foothills of the Little Carpathians mountains and the city straddles both banks of the Danube River.

 The city has a total area of 367.58 square kilometres (141.9 mi²), making it the second largest city in Slovakia by area (after the township of Vysoké Tatry).

 Geomorphologically the city covers the southern tip of the Záhorie Lowland, the entire range of the Devín Carpathians, small westernmost part of the Pezinok Carpahians and the northern tip of the Danubian Lowland.
