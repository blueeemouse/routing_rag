The Huntsman: Winter's War

The Huntsman: Winter's War is a 2016 American fantasy adventure film based on characters from the German fairy tale "Snow White" compiled by the Brothers Grimm, as well as "The Snow Queen" by Hans Christian Andersen.

 Both a prequel and sequel to "Snow White and the Huntsman", the film takes place before and after the events of the first film.

 The film is written by Craig Mazin and Evan Spiliotopoulos and is the directorial debut of Cedric Nicolas-Troyan, who acted as visual effects supervisor on the first film.
