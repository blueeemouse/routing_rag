The Desert Forges

The Desert Forges was a game show set in the Wadi Rum desert region in Jordan.

 It was first aired on Channel 5 from 23 June to 25 August 2001.

 It is based on a French format called "Les Forges du Désert", created by Pierre Sportolaro in 1999 and produced by Adventure Line Productions, also producers of "Fort Boyard".
