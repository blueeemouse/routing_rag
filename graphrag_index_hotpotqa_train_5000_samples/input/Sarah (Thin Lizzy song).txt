Sarah (Thin Lizzy song)

"Sarah" is a pop song released in 1979 by Irish rock group Thin Lizzy, included on their album, "".

 The song was written by the band's frontman Phil Lynott and guitarist Gary Moore about Lynott's newborn daughter.

 The song was also issued as a single, and appeared on several compilation albums including "".

 The song was never performed live by Thin Lizzy, but it was adopted as a live favourite by Lynott's post-Thin Lizzy project, Grand Slam, and featured on "Live in Sweden 1983", a recording of Lynott's solo band.
