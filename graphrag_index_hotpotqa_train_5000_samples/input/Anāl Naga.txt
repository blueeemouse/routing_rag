Anāl Naga

The Anāls are a Naga tribe native to Manipur state in North-East India and part of Myanmar.

 They are listed as a Scheduled Tribe, in accordance with The Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Orders (Amendment) Act, 1976 Indian Constitution.

 The Anāl tribe is one of the 'sixty six Naga tribes' of the Naga ancestral homeland.

 The members of this tribe are found both in India and Myanmar.

 In India, they are situated in the States of Manipur and Nagaland but mostly concentrated in the former.

 In the State of Manipur, the Anāl Naga population concentrated in Chandel and a few Anāl villages are located in its neighbouring districts, Churachandpur district has about three villages and Thoubal district has one or two.
