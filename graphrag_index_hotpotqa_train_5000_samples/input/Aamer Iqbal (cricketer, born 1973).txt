Aamer Iqbal (cricketer, born 1973)

Iqbal Ahmed Aamer (born 2 June 1973 in Karachi; generally known as Aamer Iqbal) is a Pakistani former first-class cricketer active 1991–2007 who played mainly for Karachi Blues.

 He represented his country at under-19 level.

 Aamer Iqbal was a right-handed batsman and a wicket-keeper.

 He scored 2,869 career runs with a highest score of 129, one of two centuries.

 He held 155 catches in his 71 first-class appearances and completed ten stumpings.
