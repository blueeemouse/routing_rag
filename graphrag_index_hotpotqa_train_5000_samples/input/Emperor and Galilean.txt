Emperor and Galilean

Emperor and Galilean (in Norwegian: "Kejser og Galilæer" ) is a play written by Henrik Ibsen.

 Although it is one of the writer's lesser known plays, on several occasions Henrik Ibsen called "Emperor and Galilean" his major work.

 "Emperor and Galilean" is written in two complementary parts with five acts in each part and is Ibsen's longest play.
