Miss Perú 1992

The Miss Perú 1992 pageant was held on April 12, 1992.

 That year, 20 candidates were competing for the national crown.

 The chosen winner represented Peru at the Miss World 1992.

 The Miss Universo Perú was chosen by representatives of GENTE Magazine, and would enter in Miss Universe 1992.

 The rest of the finalists would enter in different pageants.
