Helen Beardsley

Helen Eileen Beardsley ("née" Brandmeir, formerly North, April 5, 1930 – April 26, 2000) was the mother of the famous blended family of twenty children — eight by her first marriage to Richard North, ten stepchildren from her second husband Frank Beardsley, and two that she and Frank had during their marriage.

 She wrote a book, "Who Gets the Drumstick?"

, about her blended family's experiences.

 The book was the basis for two motion pictures: the 1968 "Yours, Mine and Ours", starring Lucille Ball and Henry Fonda; and a 2005 remake of the same film with Rene Russo and Dennis Quaid.
