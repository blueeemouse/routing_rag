Luke (album)

Luke is the third solo album by Steve Lukather released in 1997.

 It was a much different and more introspective album than Lukather's previous two solo efforts.

 The album is a concentrated collection of many of Lukather's musical influences, and he deliberately let those influences come out on the album.

 "Luke" is an experimental album, and like "Candyman" it was recorded mostly in live sessions with minimal overdubbing and processing afterward.

 "Luke" also features instrumentation not heard on previous Lukather albums such as pedal steel, harmonicas, Mellotrons, and experimental guitar, bass, and drum sounds.
