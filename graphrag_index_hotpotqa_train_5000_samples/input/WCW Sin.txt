WCW Sin

Sin was a professional wrestling pay-per-view (PPV) event produced by World Championship Wrestling (WCW) that replaced WCW's January PPV event, Souled Out which was held from 1997 to 2000.

 Sin was the antepenultimate WCW PPV before the company was absorbed by the World Wrestling Federation (now World Wrestling Entertainment) in March 2001.

 It took place on January 14, 2001 from the Conseco Fieldhouse in Indianapolis, Indiana.
