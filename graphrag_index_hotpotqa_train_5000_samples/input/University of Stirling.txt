University of Stirling

The University of Stirling is a public university founded by Royal charter in 1967.

 It is a plate glass university located in the Central Belt of Scotland, built within the walled Airthrey Castle estate close to Stirling.

 Since its foundation, it has expanded to four faculties, a Management School, a Graduate School, and a number of institutes and centres covering a broad range of subjects in the academic areas of arts and humanities, natural sciences, social sciences, and health sciences and sport.
