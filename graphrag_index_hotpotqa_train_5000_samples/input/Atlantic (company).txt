Atlantic (company)

Atlantic was an Italian toy manufacturing company based in Treviglio from 1966 to 1984.

 In the 1970s it became widely popular both in Italy and in Europe as a producer of 1:32- and HO-scale toy soldiers.

 The original Atlantic company ceased to exist in 1984; in the late 1990s, the "Atlantic" brand and logo were acquired by Nexus Editrice.
