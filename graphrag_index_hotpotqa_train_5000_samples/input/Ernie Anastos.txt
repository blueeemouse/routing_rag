Ernie Anastos

Ernie Anastos (born July 12, 1943) is an Emmy Award-winning news anchor for New York City's Fox 5 News at 6:00pm.

 He was formerly the Anchor of the 5 and 10 o'clock news on Fox 5.

 As of 2017, every March 21st, it is Ernie Anastos day in New York City, Ernie was awarded, honored, and commended by Mayor Bill DeBlasio.
