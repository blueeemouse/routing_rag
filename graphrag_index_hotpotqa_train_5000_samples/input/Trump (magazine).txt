Trump (magazine)

Trump was a glossy magazine of satire and humor, mostly in the forms of comics features and short stories.

 It was edited by Harvey Kurtzman and published by Hugh Hefner, with only two issues produced in 1957.

 The first issue appeared in January 1957.

 The magazine's mascot was a trumpeter herald in the style of John Tenniel's "Alice in Wonderland" illustrations.
