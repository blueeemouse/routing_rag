1984–85 Iraq FA Cup

The 1984–85 Iraq FA Cup was the ninth edition of the Iraq FA Cup.

 The tournament was abandoned at the semi-final stage with Al-Rasheed, Al-Tayaran, Al-Talaba and Al-Tijara being the four teams still remaining in the competition.

 It was abandoned along with the 1984–85 Iraqi National League due to the national team's World Cup qualifying campaign, and no champions were announced for either tournament.
