Dogfish Head Brewery

Dogfish Head Brewery is a brewing company based in Milton, Delaware founded by Sam Calagione.

 It opened in 1995 and produces 262,000 barrels of beer annually.

 Dogfish Head has been a rapidly growing brewery – it grew nearly 400% between 2003 and 2006.

 The brewery was featured prominently in the documentary "Beer Wars" and was the subject of the Discovery Channel series "Brew Masters", which premiered Sunday, November 21, 2010.

 The brewery takes its name from Dogfish Head, Maine where Calagione spent summers as a child.
