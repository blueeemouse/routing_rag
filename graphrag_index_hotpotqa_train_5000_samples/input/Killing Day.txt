Killing Day

Killing Day is a cancelled video game that was being developed for the PlayStation 3.

 The game was first revealed during Sony's E3 press conference in 2005.

 However, since then the game was subsequently cancelled in the middle of development.

 Although, in summer of 2009, developer Ubisoft filed for a trademark on "Killing Day", sparking rumors that the game is still in development.

 As of September 2012, no news of the game's development has been revealed.

 On January 4, 2013, a new trademark application was filed but there have been no new comments by Ubisoft.
