2013 SEC Championship Game

The 2013 SEC Championship Game was a college football game that was played on Saturday, December 7, 2013 in the Georgia Dome in Atlanta, Georgia, with the kickoff at 4:12pm ET.

 The 22nd annual SEC Championship Game, determined the 2013 champion of the Southeastern Conference (SEC).

 The game was played between the Auburn Tigers, champions of the SEC's Western division, and the Missouri Tigers, champions of SEC's Eastern division.

 In the highest-scoring contest in the game's 21-year history, Auburn won 59–42.
