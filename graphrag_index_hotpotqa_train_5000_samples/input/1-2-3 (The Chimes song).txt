1-2-3 (The Chimes song)

"1-2-3" is the 1989 debut single by Scottish dance trio, The Chimes.

 Along with the track, "Underestimate", their debut single reached number one for two weeks on the "Billboard" dance chart and was also their biggest single.

 "1-2-3" was The Chimes only Hot 100 hit, peaking at number eighty-six and was also their first of four entry on the soul singles chart, peaking at number forty-one.
