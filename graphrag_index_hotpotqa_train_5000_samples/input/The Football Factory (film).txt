The Football Factory (film)

The Football Factory is a 2004 British sports drama film written and directed by Nick Love.

 The film stars Danny Dyer, Tamer Hassan, Frank Harper, Roland Manookian, Neil Maskell and Dudley Sutton.

 The film is loosely based on the novel of the same name by John King and the first foray into film making by video game producers Rockstar Games, credited as executive producers.

 The film was released in the United Kingdom on 14 May 2004.
