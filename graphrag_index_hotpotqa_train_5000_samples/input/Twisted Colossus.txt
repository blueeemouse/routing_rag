Twisted Colossus

Twisted Colossus (formerly Colossus) is a steel roller coaster at Six Flags Magic Mountain amusement park.

 Originally designed and built by International Amusement Devices, the roller coaster opened as Colossus on June 29, 1978.

 It was the tallest and fastest wooden roller coaster in the world and the first with two drops greater than 100 ft .

 Colossus became well known after appearances in film and television, including the box-office hit "National Lampoon's Vacation".
