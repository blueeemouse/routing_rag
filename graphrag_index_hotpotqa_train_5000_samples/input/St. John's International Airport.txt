St. John's International Airport

St. John's International Airport (IATA: YYT, ICAO: CYYT) is in Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada.

 It is an international airport located 3 NM northwest of St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador that serves the St. John's Metro Area and the Avalon Peninsula.

 The airport is part of the National Airports System, and is operated by St. John's International Airport Authority Inc.
