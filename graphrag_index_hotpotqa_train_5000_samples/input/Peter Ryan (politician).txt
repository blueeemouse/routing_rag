Peter Ryan (politician)

Peter Julian Ryan (born 30 October 1950) is a former Australian politician who was leader of The Nationals in Victoria.

 He represented the electoral district of Gippsland South from 1992 to 2015, and from 2010 to 2014 was the Deputy Premier of Victoria as well as the Minister for Rural and Regional Development.

 In addition, Ryan was the Minister for Police from 2010 to 2013.
