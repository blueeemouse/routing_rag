The Way I Am (MC Mong album)

The Way I Am is a 2006 album by MC Mong.

 This is MC Mong's third album.

 This album comes in a digital disc format.

 For MC Mong's third album, he invited M-Flo's former member Lisa.

 His third album also includes duets with Park Hyo Shin, Ivy, and MayBee.
