Maurice White

Maurice "Moe" White (December 19, 1941 – February 4, 2016) was an American singer-songwriter, musician, record producer, arranger, and bandleader.

 He was the founder of the band Earth, Wind & Fire.

 He was also the older brother of current Earth, Wind & Fire member Verdine White, and former member Fred White.

 He served as the band's main songwriter and record producer, and was co-lead singer along with Philip Bailey.
