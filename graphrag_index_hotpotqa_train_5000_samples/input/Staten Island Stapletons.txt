Staten Island Stapletons

The Staten Island Stapletons also known as the Staten Island Stapes were a professional American football team founded in 1915 that played in the National Football League from 1929 to 1932.

 The team was based in the Stapleton section of Staten Island.

 They played under the shortened nickname the "Stapes" the final two seasons.

 Jack Shapiro, who was a blocking back for the Stapletons, was the shortest player in NFL history.

 The team was based in Staten Island, New York.
