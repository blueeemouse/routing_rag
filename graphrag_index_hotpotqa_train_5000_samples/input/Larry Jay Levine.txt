Larry Jay Levine

Larry Jay Levine (born July 1, 1961) is an American federal prison consultant.

 In April 2007, Larry Levine founded American Prison Consultants, a legal services firm that provides information and federal criminal litigation assistance to lawyers, and offenders going through criminal proceedings in U.S. District Court, who are about to enter the federal prison system.

 American Prison Consultants also operates under the synonymous name of Wall Street Prison Consultants.
