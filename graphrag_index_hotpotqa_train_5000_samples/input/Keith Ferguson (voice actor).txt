Keith Ferguson (voice actor)

Keith James Ferguson (born February 26, 1972) is an American voice actor best known for the voice over roles of Bloo Kazoo from the Cartoon Network animated series "Foster's Home for Imaginary Friends", Basch fon Ronsenburg from "Final Fantasy XII", Marluxia from the Kingdom Hearts franchise, Lord Hater from "Wander Over Yonder", and Reaper from "Overwatch".

 He also provides a number of "sound-alike" portrayals in the VO industry, including Harrison Ford's Han Solo and Indiana Jones and Steve Martin in "Robot Chicken", and the voice of Lightning McQueen in "Cars Toons" and "Cars" video games, replacing Owen Wilson in that role.
