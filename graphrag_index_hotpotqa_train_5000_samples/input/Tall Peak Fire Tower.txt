Tall Peak Fire Tower

The Tall Peak Fire Tower is a historic fire tower in Ouachita National Forest.

 It is located at the top of Tall Peak in the southwestern part of the national forest in Polk County, Arkansas.

 It is a fieldstone structure, built about 1938 by a crew of the Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC), and features the distinctive flared corners that typify CCC architecture.

 It is accessible via a forest service road off Polk County 64 (also known as Forest Service Road 38).
