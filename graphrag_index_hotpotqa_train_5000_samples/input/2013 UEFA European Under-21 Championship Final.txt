2013 UEFA European Under-21 Championship Final

The 2013 UEFA European Under-21 Championship Final was a football match that took place on 18 June 2013 at the Teddy Stadium in Jerusalem, Israel, and determined the winner of the 2013 UEFA European Under-21 Championship.

 Spain won their fourth title defeating Italy 4–2.

 Thiago scored a first half hat-trick for Spain in the final.
