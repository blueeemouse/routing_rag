Eric Murray (bridge)

Eric R. Murray (born 1928) is a Canadian contract bridge player and co-founder of the Canadian Bridge Federation (CBF).

 He and his long-time regular partner Sami Kehela are considered two of the best Canadian players in the history of the game.

 The Eric R. Murray Trophy, named in his honour, is awarded to the open team representing Canada in the quadrennial World Team Olympiad (continued after 2004 as part of the World Mind Sports Games).
