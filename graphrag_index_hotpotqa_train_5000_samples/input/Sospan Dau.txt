Sospan Dau

The TSHD Sospan Dau is a Dutch trailing suction hopper dredger owned by Sosban BV.

 The vessel has worked on dredging projects, including offshore aggregates, port maintenance, land reclamation, coastal defense and beach replenishment.

 The name "Sospan Dau" is Welsh and originates from Sosban Fach and Llanelli's tin plating industry, Sospan being the Welsh for Saucepan and "Dau" being Welsh for Two as the ship is a successor to the original Sospan.
