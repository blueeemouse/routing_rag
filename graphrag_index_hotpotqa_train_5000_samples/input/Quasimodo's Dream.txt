Quasimodo's Dream

Quasimodo's Dream is the second album by Australian group The Reels, released in May 1981 by Polygram Records.

 It is considered to be the band's best work and contains the iconic title track, which was voted as number 10 on APRA's list of Top 30 Australian songs.

 Other singles from the album include "After the News" and "Shout and Deliver".

 The song "According to My Heart", a cover of the 1961 Jim Reeves song had been included on the album against the wishes of the band, as a result of this, there was a sticker on the inner sleeve that said "Due to Record Company Pressure This Album Contains The Hit Single According To My Heart".
