&quot;Heroes&quot; (David Bowie song)

"Heroes" is a song recorded by the English musician David Bowie, written by Brian Eno and Bowie.

 Produced by Bowie and Tony Visconti, it was recorded in July and August 1977, and released on 23 September 1977.

 A product of Bowie's "Berlin" period, the track was not a huge hit in the UK or US at the time, but has gone on to become one of Bowie's signature songs.

 In January 2016, following Bowie's death, the song reached a new peak of number 12 in the UK Singles Chart.

 "Heroes" has been cited as Bowie's second-most covered song after "Rebel Rebel".
