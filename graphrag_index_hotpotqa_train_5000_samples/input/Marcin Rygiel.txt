Marcin Rygiel

Marcin "Martin" Rygiel, (born 30 April 1983 in Krosno, Poland), is a Polish musician, best known as the former bass guitarist of the technical death metal band Decapitated from 1997 to 2007, leaving several months before their hiatus.

 He was also a member of the avant-garde metal/black metal band Lux Occulta from 1998 until their hiatus in 2002.

 In June 2008, Rygiel toured with the death metal band Vader as a session musician.

 In 2010, Rygiel joined California-based death metal band Annihilated as their bassist until 2013.

 He was the bassist for the Egyptian-American death metal band Nader Sadek from 2014 to 2015.
