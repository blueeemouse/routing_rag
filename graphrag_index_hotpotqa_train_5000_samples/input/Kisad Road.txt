Kisad Road

Kisad Road is a major road in Baguio in the Philippines.

 It connects from Aspiras-Palispis Highway (formerly Marcos Highway) to Shuntug Road in the city's downtown core.

 Burnham Park, which is named after Daniel Burnham, is located along the road.

 The Mama Well Restaurant, Nationwide Health Systems Baguio, Ina Mansion Condominium, Baguio Midland Courier, Shakey's Pizza Parlor, Professional Regulation Commission, Cafe by the Ruins Restaurant, The Hotels Starwood Hotel, Baguio Crown Legacy, Kisad Hotel, Venus Parkview Hotel, Baguio Burnham Suites, Holiday Park Hotel, and Eurotel are also located along the road.
