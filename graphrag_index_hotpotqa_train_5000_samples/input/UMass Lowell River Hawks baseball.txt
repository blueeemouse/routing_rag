UMass Lowell River Hawks baseball

UMass Lowell River Hawks baseball is the varsity intercollegiate team representing University of Massachusetts Lowell in the sport of college baseball at the Division I level of the National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA).

 The team is led by Ken Harring, and plays its home games at Edward A. LeLacheur Park off campus in Lowell, Massachusetts.

 The River Hawks are members of the America East Conference, which they joined in 2014 when they begin the transition from Division II.
