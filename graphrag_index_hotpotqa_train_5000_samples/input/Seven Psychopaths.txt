Seven Psychopaths

Seven Psychopaths is a 2012 British-American crime black comedy film written and directed by Martin McDonagh.

 It stars Colin Farrell, Sam Rockwell, Woody Harrelson, and Christopher Walken, with Tom Waits, Abbie Cornish, Olga Kurylenko, and Željko Ivanek in supporting roles.

 The film marks the second collaboration between McDonagh, Farrell, and Ivanek, following 2008's "In Bruges".

 The film was a co-production of the United States and the United Kingdom.
