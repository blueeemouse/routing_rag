One Flight Down (Grey's Anatomy)

"One Flight Down" is the twentieth episode of the eleventh season of the American television medical drama "Grey's Anatomy", and is the 240th episode overall.

 It aired on April 16, 2015 on ABC in the United States.

 The episode was written Austin Guzman and directed by David Greenspan.

 The episode features a plane crash in Seattle bringing patients to Grey Sloan Memorial and old memories of the season 8's tragic plane crash that claimed the lives of Mark Sloan (Eric Dane) and Lexie Grey (Chyler Leigh) back to Meredith Grey, Arizona Robbins and Owen Hunt.
