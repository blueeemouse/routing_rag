Picasso (restaurant)

Picasso is a two Michelin star restaurant run by chef Julian Serrano in Las Vegas, Nevada, United States.

 The name is derived from the artist, Pablo Picasso and features the artist's paintings throughout the restaurant.

 The restaurant is known for its reinvention and interpretation of French and Spanish Cuisine.

 Today, the restaurant holds two Michelin stars, the AAA Five-Diamond Award, a 28/30 rating from the Zagat guide, the "Wine Spectator" Grand Award since 2001, and is considered to be one of the finest restaurants in the United States.
