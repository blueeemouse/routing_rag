Satyarup Siddhanta

Satyarup Siddhanta (Bengali: সত্যরূপ সিদ্ধান্ত) is a Bengaluru based Bengali mountaineer.

 He has climbed the highest peaks of six continents.

 If he climbs the final peak, Vinson Massif in Antarctica, he will be the first person from West Bengal to climb the seven summits.

 Satyarup is a certified mountaineer from Himalayan Mountaineering Institute, Darjeeling.

 He is the winner of the first season of Grab Your Dream contest, an online hunt for travel ambassadors, sponsored by travel company Cox and Kings.

 He is the President and founder of ABETO – "A better tomorrow" foundation, and works as a consultant at Brady Corporation, India.
