Bnei Brak

Bnei Brak (Hebrew: בְּנֵי בְרַק‎ , "bənê ḇəraq") is a city located on the central Mediterranean coastal plain in Israel, just east of Tel Aviv.

 A center of Ultra Orthodox Judaism, Bnei Brak covers an area of 709 hectares (1752 acres, or 2.74 square miles), and had a population of 188,964 in 2016 .

 It is one of the poorest and most densely populated cities in Israel , and sixth most densely populated city in the world.
