Agnes Buen Garnås

Agnes Buen Garnås (born 23 October 1946) is a Norwegian folk singer from the county of Telemark.

 She comes from a famous musical family from the town of Jondal, and is known particularly for her singing of ancient unaccompanied Norwegian ballads, as well as her updated arrangements of these songs in collaboration with the Norwegian saxophonist Jan Garbarek on the ECM album "Rosensfole".
