Tod Scott Brody

Tod Scott Brody (October 23, 1956 – December 23, 2016) was an American film producer, editor, and still photographer who, during the 1980s, was an executive at both Cannon Films and MGM.

 He was associated with Miramax and the producer Scott Rudin during the 1990s, and was an executive producer on the film "Marvin's Room", which starred Meryl Streep, Diane Keaton (Oscar-nomination for her role), Leonardo DiCaprio, and Robert De Niro.
