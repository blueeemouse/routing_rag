2010 Duke Blue Devils football team

The 2010 Duke Blue Devils football team represented Duke University in the 2010 NCAA Division I FBS football season.

 The Blue Devils were led by 3rd year head coach David Cutcliffe and played their home games at Wallace Wade Stadium.

 They are members of the Atlantic Coast Conference in the Coastal Division.

 They finished the season 3–9, 1–7 in ACC play.
