Cary Elwes

 He is best known for his roles in films such as "The Princess Bride", "Glory", "Kiss the Girls", "", "Days of Thunder", "Bram Stoker's Dracula", "Hot Shots!

", "Twister", "The Jungle Book", "Liar, Liar", and "Saw".
