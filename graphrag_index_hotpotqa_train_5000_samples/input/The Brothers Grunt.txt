The Brothers Grunt

The Brothers Grunt is a Canadian animated comedy television series by "Ed, Edd n Eddy" creator Danny Antonucci that originally aired from August 15, 1994 to April 9, 1995 on MTV.

 The series centered on Frank, Tony, Bing, Dean and Sammy, an ensemble cast of pale, rubbery humanoids distantly related to human beings, all of them ostensibly male, wandering around in their underpants, in search of their lost brother Perry.

 The series had a short run and was met with a generally negative reception.
