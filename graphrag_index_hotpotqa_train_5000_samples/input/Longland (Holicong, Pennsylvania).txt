Longland (Holicong, Pennsylvania)

Longland, also known as the Margaret Mead Farmstead, is a historic home located near Holicong, in Buckingham Township, Bucks County, Pennsylvania.

 The house was built in 1845, and is a large 2 3/4-story, five bay by two bay, stuccoed stone dwelling.

 It has Greek Revival design details and features an ornate entranceway with rectangular transom and sidelights.

 Also on the property is a three-story, stone bank barn dated to 1844.

 It has an attached one-story stone ell.

 Other contributing buildings are a two-story, wood frame wagon house and woodshed / garage that abuts the house.

 It was a childhood home of American anthropologist Margaret Mead (1901-1978).

 Her family purchased the property in 1912 and sold it in 1926.
