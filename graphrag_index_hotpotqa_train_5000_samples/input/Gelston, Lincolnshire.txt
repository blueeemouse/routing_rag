Gelston, Lincolnshire

Gelston is a village in the South Kesteven district of Lincolnshire, England.

 It is situated 2 mi west from the A607 road, 5 mi north from Grantham, and in the civil parish of Hough-on-the-Hill, a village 1 mi to the north-east.

 It is in the civil parish of Brant Broughton and Stragglethorpe.
