FC Thun

FC Thun ("Fussballclub Thun 1898") is a Swiss football team from the Bernese Oberland town of Thun.

 The club currently plays in the Swiss Super League after being promoted in the 2009/10 season.

 The club plays at the Stockhorn Arena which accommodates a total of 10,000 supporters, both seated and standing.

 The club's colours are red and white.
