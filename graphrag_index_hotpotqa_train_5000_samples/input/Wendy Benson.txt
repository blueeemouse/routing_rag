Wendy Benson

Wendy Benson-Landes (born July 8, 1971) is an American actress seen in many television shows.

 She played the role of Julie Harris in the 2001 biographical television movie "James Dean".

 She guest starred in many television series, including "Murder, She Wrote", "The X-Files", "Charmed", "JAG", "Ugly Betty", "According to Jim", "Ghost Whisperer", "", "Desperate Housewives" and "Mad Men".
