Loggers Leap

Loggers Leap (also referred to as "Logger's Leap") is a log flume ride in Thorpe Park, UK.

 It it is the tallest log flume ride in the UK when it opened, and although the park does not advertise the fact, it regained that record in 2005 with the removal of ""Nightmare Niagara"" from the now-defunct American Adventure theme park near Derby.

 The ride has been standing but not operating since the end of the 2015 season.
