Camp Grohn

Camp Grohn was a military base of the U.S. Army on the outskirts of Bremen, Germany after the end of World War II from 1945 to 1954.

 Camp Grohn was originally built in 1936 as "Flak Kaserne" and housed the first battalion of the Luftwaffe's 26th Anti-Aircraft Artillery Regiment prior to the outbreak of World War II.

 Following the defeat of Germany in the war, the U.S. Army took over the military base and renamed it Camp Grohn after the part of Bremen in which the camp was located.

 Camp Grohn was in a small, joint American/British sector in northern Germany called the "Bremen Enclave", which included the ports of Bremen and Bremerhaven.
