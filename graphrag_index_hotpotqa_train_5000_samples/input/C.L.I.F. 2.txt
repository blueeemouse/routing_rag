C.L.I.F. 2

C.L.I.F. 2 ("Courage, Loyalty, Integrity, Fairness", or 警徽天职2) was a police procedural series produced by MediaCorp Singapore in 2012 in collaboration with the Singapore Police Force.

 It was broadcast from 18 February to 15 March 2013 on free-to-air MediaCorp Channel 8 and consists of 20 episodes.

 The series stars from the first season, with new additions Li Nanxing, Rui En, Thomas Ong, Cavin Soh and Pierre Png as the main characters of the series.

 The series is co-sponsored by Gain City, Sheng Siong Supermarkets and Gold Roast Coffee.
