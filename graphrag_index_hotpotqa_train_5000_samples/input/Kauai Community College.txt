Kauai Community College

Kauaʻ i Community College is a public, co-educational commuter college in Līhuʻ e, Hawaiʻ i on the island of Kauaʻ i.

 It is one of ten branches of the University of Hawaiʻ i system anchored by the University of Hawaiʻ i at Mānoa in Honolulu and is accredited by the Western Association of Schools and Colleges.
