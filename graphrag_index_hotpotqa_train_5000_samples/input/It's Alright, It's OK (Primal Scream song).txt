It's Alright, It's OK (Primal Scream song)

"It's Alright, It's OK" is a song by the band Primal Scream.

 It was released as a single on 7 May 2013, as the second single off of the band's tenth album, "More Light".

 The song bears resemblance to "Movin' On Up", the first track off of Primal Scream's critically acclaimed 1991 album, "Screamadelica".
