The Otto Show

"The Otto Show" is the twenty-second episode of "The Simpsons"<nowiki>'</nowiki> third season.

 It first aired on the Fox network in the United States on April 23, 1992.

 In the episode, Bart decides that he wants to become a rock star after attending a Spinal Tap concert, so Homer and Marge buy him a guitar.

 He shows the guitar to Otto the bus driver, who plays it and consequently makes the children late for school.

 Racing to Springfield Elementary, Otto crashes the school bus and is suspended until he can get his license back.

 Bart, who respects Otto, invites him to move in with the Simpson family.
