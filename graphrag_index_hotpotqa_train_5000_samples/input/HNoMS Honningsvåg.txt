HNoMS Honningsvåg

HNoMS "Honningsvåg was a naval trawler that served throughout the Second World War as a patrol boat in the Royal Norwegian Navy.

 She was launched at the North Sea harbour of Wesermünde in Hanover, Germany in February 1940 as the fishing trawler Malangen" and was captured by Norwegian militiamen at the North Norwegian port of Honningsvåg during her maiden fishing journey to the Barents Sea.

 Having taken part in the defence of Norway in 1940 she spent the rest of the war years patrolling the ocean off Iceland.

 She was decommissioned in 1946, sold to a civilian fishing company in 1947 and scrapped in 1973.
