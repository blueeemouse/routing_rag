Mykhailo Horyn

Mykhailo Mykolayovych Horyn (Ukrainian: Михайло Миколайович Горинь ) (born (1930--)17 1930 in Kniselo, Lviv Oblast – died (2013--)13 2013 in Lviv, Lviv Oblast) was a Ukrainian human rights activist and dissident.

 He was a People's Deputy of the first convocation of the Verkhovna Rada from May 15, 1990 to May 10, 1994.

 He played an important role in the country's struggle for independence.

 Regarding Russian-Ukrainian relations he said: "Our historical mission is to be the doctor who will cure Russia of its imperial ambition.

 Ukraine is a European power.

 Russia is not.

 It is a Eurasian power.

 It is sitting on top of the Ural mountains looking East and trying to decide how to tackle its problems".
