The Bakery Girl of Monceau

The Bakery Girl of Monceau or The Girl at the Monceau Bakery is a 1963 film by Éric Rohmer.

 The original French title is "La Boulangère de Monceau".

 The film was the first of Rohmer's Six Moral Tales (Contes moraux), which consisted of two shorts and four feature films.
