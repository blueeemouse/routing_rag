Thoroton

Thoroton is a village located in the parish of Thoroton in Rushcliffe, Nottingham, England.

 It lies along the banks of the River Smite, about 15 mi east of Nottingham, 4 mi north-west of Bingham, and adjacent to Scarrington, Hawksworth, Sibthorpe, Orston and Aslockton.

 Thoroton has a population of 112.

 It is bounded by the A46 3 mi to the west, the A1 3 mi to the east and the A52 road 2 mi to the south.
