Evgenia Tur

Evgenia Tur (Russian: Евге́ния Тур ) (August 24, 1815 – March 27, 1892) was a Russian writer, critic, journalist and publisher.

 Her birth name was Elizaveta Vasilyevna Sukhovo-Kobylina.

 Her full married name was Countess Elizaveta Vasilyevna Salias De Tournemire.

 The novelist Evgeny Salias De Tournemire was her son.

 The playwright Aleksandr Sukhovo-Kobylin was her brother.

 Her sister, Sofia, was a painter of some note
