Crawlspace (2013 film)

Crawlspace (also known as The Attic and Hideaway) is a 2013 horror film directed by Josh Stolberg.

 It was released on January 4, 2013, and was optioned by Hulu for exclusive VOD distribution starting June 5, 2014.

 "Crawlspace" focuses on a family that is terrorized by the former occupant of their new home.
