Patient Zero (film)

Patient Zero, previously known as Patient Z, is an upcoming British-American fantasy-horror thriller film directed by Stefan Ruzowitzky and written by Mike Le.

 The film stars Matt Smith, Natalie Dormer, Stanley Tucci, Agyness Deyn and Clive Standen.

 Filming began on March 3, 2015 in London.

 Screen Gems will release the film.
