Vikas Sethi

Vikas Sethi (born 12 May 1976) is an Indian actor.

 He played the lead role in the 2003 adult drama film "Oops!

", he went on to play supporting roles in numerous hit TV series and Bollywood films.

 His most notable television appearances include in ‘Kahiin To Hoga’ as Swayam Shergill and in the Indian soap opera "Kasautii Zindagii Kay" where he plays the role of Prem Bajaj.

 He appeared in the fourth season of "Nach Baliye" performing along with his then wife, Amita.
