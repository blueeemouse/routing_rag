Bossgoo

'Bossgoo.com' (Chinese: "'全球贸易通") is one of the world's largest B2B ( Business-to-business ) marketplace for global trade based in China.

 It facilitates trade from Greater China to the world, using a wide range of English-language media and facilitates trade from the world to Greater China, using Chinese-language media.

 The company provides sourcing information to volume buyers and integrated marketing services to suppliers.

 In addition, Bossgoo verifies the quality of the manufacturer.
