BMW i3

The BMW i3 is a five-door urban electric car developed by the German car manufacturer BMW.

 The i3 is part of BMW's "Project i" and was launched as a new brand, BMW i .

 The i3 is BMW's first zero emissions mass-produced vehicle due to its electric powertrain, and BMW is the first company to launch a volume production vehicle on the market featuring carbon-fiber-reinforced polymer to improve the vehicle's energy consumption.
