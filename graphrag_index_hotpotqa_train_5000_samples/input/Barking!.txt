Barking!

Barking!

 is a British children's TV series that produced two series between 2004 & 2005.

 The show was originally broadcast on ITV1's children's slot CITV.

 It stars Katy McGowan as Jezza, a teenage girl with a talking dog named Georgie, voiced by Will Mellor.

 Other major characters in the show included Jezza's mother, Pippa (Robin Weaver), her stepbrothers Dan (Adam Scourfield) and Ollie (Tom Millner), and her stepfather, Greg (Charles Dale).
