2010 Seattle Seahawks season

The 2010 Seattle Seahawks season was the 35th season for the team in the National Football League.

 Jim Mora was fired on January 8, 2010, leading the Seahawks to come to an agreement with Pete Carroll soon after.

 This marked the ninth season that the Seahawks played their home games at Qwest Field.

 The team exceeded their win total from 2009 and won the NFC West with a 7–9 record.

 They became the first team in a full season to finish with a sub-.500 record and make the playoffs, a berth which was by virtue of winning the division.

 The 2010 Seahawks also became the first sub-.500 team to win a playoff game with their home win against the 11–5 New Orleans Saints, but had their season ended by the second-seeded Chicago Bears in the divisional round.

 The 2010 Seahawks currently have the lowest win percentage of any team to ever make the playoffs.
