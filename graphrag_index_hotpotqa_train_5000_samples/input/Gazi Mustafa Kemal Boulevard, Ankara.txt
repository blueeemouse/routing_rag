Gazi Mustafa Kemal Boulevard, Ankara

Gazi Mustafa Kemal Boulevard (Turkish: "Gazi Mustafa Kemal Bulvarı" ), abbreviated as GMK Boulevard, is a boulevard connecting Anadolu Square (formerly: Tandoğan Square) in Yenimahalle with Kızılay Square in Çankaya of Ankara, Turkey.

 The 2.2 km long boulevard runs in northwest-southeast direction.

 It is named after Mustafa Kemal Atatürk (1881–1938), the founder of Turkish Republic.
