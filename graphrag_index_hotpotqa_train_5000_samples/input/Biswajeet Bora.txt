Biswajeet Bora

Biswajeet Bora (born 8 August 1979) is an Indian film director, actor, screenwriter and script writer from Assam.

 He made his directorial debut in Bollywood with the first Indian carbon neutral film "Aisa Yeh Jahaan".

 He directed the Superhit Assamese film "Bahniman" which was released in 2016.
