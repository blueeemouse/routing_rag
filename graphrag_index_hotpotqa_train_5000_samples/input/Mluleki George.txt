Mluleki George

Mluleki Editor George (born 2 February 1948) is the former deputy minister of defence of South Africa.

 He served as treasurer-general of the Congress of the People, a South African political party formed by former Defence Minister Mosiuoa Lekota in 2008.

 He has founded a new political party, the United Congress, which he says will restore the moral fibre of society which has disintegrated.
