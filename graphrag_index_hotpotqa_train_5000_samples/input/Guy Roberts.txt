Guy Roberts

Guy Thomas "Zeke" Roberts (May 10, 1900 - 1993) was a professional American football player in the early National Football League and the first American Football League.

 He played for three pro teams over the course of two years.

 In 1926 he played with the Canton Bulldogs in the NFL and the Cleveland Panthers in the AFL.

 He played the following season with the NFL's Pottsville Maroons.

 Prior to his professional career, Roberts played college football for the Iowa State Cyclones.
