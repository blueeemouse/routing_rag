Dibba Al-Fujairah

Dibba Al-Fujairah (دبا الفجيرة) is a major city in the emirate of Fujairah located on the North east part of the United Arab Emirates.

 It is geographically part of the Dibba region.

 Dibba Al-Fujairah is considered to be the 2nd largest city in the emirate of Fujairah.

 With an area of 600 square kilometres, Dibba Al-Fujairah has a population of 30,000.
