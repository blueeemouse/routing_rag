Mini sprint

A mini sprint is an American type of open-wheel racing vehicle.

 Mini sprint cars resemble the shape of a full-size sprint car and the size of a midget car.

 Although often used liberally to describe several different types of motorcycle-powered open-wheel racing cars, the term "mini sprint" actually applies to cars that have an upright-style chassis (the driver sits upright in the seat as in a sprint or midget), a center-mounted 4-cylinder motorcycle engine and dimensions and appearance that are similar to today’s midget.

 Mini sprints are chain driven and use 13 in wheels and tires.
