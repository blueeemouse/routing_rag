Winkelman v. Parma City School District

Winkelman v. Parma City School District, 550 U.S. 516 (2007) , is a civil suit under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act decided by the Supreme Court of the United States.

 Justice Kennedy held for the seven-justice majority that parents may file suit under IDEA pro se.

 Justice Kennedy declined to reach the question whether parents may represent the interests of their children pro se, instead concluding that IDEA created a set of independently enforceable rights in parents.
