Thandie Newton

Melanie Thandiwe "Thandie" Newton ( ; born 6 November 1972) is an English actress.

 She has appeared in several British and American films, and is known for roles such as Linda in "The Pursuit of Happyness", Nyah Nordoff-Hall in "" and Christine in "Crash", for which she received a number of awards, including a BAFTA Award for Best Actress in a Supporting Role.
