Timothy Carhart

Timothy Carhart (born December 24, 1953) is an American actor.

 He starred in the CBS drama "Island Son" (1989–90) and has had recurring roles in "" (2000–03) and "24" (2002).

 He also starred in the 1992 Broadway revival of "A Streetcar Named Desire".

 His film appearances include "Pink Cadillac" (1989), "Thelma & Louise" (1991), and "Beverly Hills Cop III" (1994).
