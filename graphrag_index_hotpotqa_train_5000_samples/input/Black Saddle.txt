Black Saddle

Black Saddle is an American Western television series starring Peter Breck that aired 44 episodes on ABC from January 10, 1959, to May 6, 1960.

 The half-hour program was produced by Dick Powell's Four Star Television, and the original pilot was an episode of CBS's "Dick Powell's Zane Grey Theater", with Chris Alcaide portraying the principal character, Clay Culhane.
