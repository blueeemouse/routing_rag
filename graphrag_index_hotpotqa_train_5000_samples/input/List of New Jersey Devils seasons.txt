List of New Jersey Devils seasons

The New Jersey Devils are a professional ice hockey team based in Newark, New Jersey.

 The team is a member of the Metropolitan Division of the Eastern Conference of the National Hockey League (NHL).

 The Devils arrived in New Jersey in 1982 after transferring from Denver, Colorado, where they had been known as the Colorado Rockies since 1976.

 Before that, the franchise entered the league as the Kansas City Scouts in 1974.

 The 2010–11 season is the 29th season of play in New Jersey.

 It is the 37th year for the Devils franchise, and including the team's time in Kansas City and Denver, the Devils have won over 1100 regular season games, 17th overall in NHL history.
