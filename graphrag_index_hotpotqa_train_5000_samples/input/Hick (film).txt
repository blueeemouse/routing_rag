Hick (film)

Hick is a 2011 comedy-drama film directed by Derick Martini, based on the novel of the same name by Andrea Portes that draws on non-fictional elements.

 The film stars Chloë Grace Moretz, Eddie Redmayne, Ray McKinnon, Rory Culkin, Juliette Lewis, Blake Lively, and Alec Baldwin.

 It premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival on September 10, 2011.

 It had a limited theatrical release on May 11 and is distributed by Phase 4 Films.
