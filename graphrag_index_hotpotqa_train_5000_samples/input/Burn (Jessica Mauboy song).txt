Burn (Jessica Mauboy song)

"Burn" is a song by Australian recording artist Jessica Mauboy.

 It was written by Taj Jackson, Jonas Jeberg and Mich Hansen, and was produced by Jeberg and Cutfather.

 "Burn" was released digitally on 17 November 2008, as the second single from Mauboy's debut studio album, "Been Waiting".

 Musically, "Burn" is an R&B-dance track that incorporates electronica influences.

 The song became Mauboy's first number-one on the ARIA Singles Chart and was certified platinum by the Australian Recording Industry Association for selling over 70,000 copies.

 "Burn" also became Mauboy's first charting single internationally, reaching number 92 on the Japan Hot 100.
