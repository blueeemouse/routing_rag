Nigel Godrich

Nigel Timothy Godrich (born 28 February 1971) is an English record producer, recording engineer and musician.

 He is best known for his work with the English rock band Radiohead, having produced all of their studio albums since "OK Computer" (1997); he has been dubbed the "sixth member" of the band, in an allusion to George Martin being called the "Fifth Beatle".

 Godrich has also worked extensively with Radiohead singer Thom Yorke on his solo material, and is a member of the bands Atoms for Peace (with Yorke) and Ultraísta.

 Other acts Godrich has worked with include Beck, Paul McCartney, U2, R.E.M. and Roger Waters.

 He is the creator of the music webseries "From the Basement".
