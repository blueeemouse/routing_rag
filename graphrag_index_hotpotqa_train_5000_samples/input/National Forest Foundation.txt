National Forest Foundation

The National Forest Foundation, an American non-profit organization, was created by Congress in 1992 to be the official non-profit partner of the United States Forest Service.

 Its mission is to help the Forest Service care for the nation's forests for the benefit of future generations.

 The foundation receives funding from Congress, soliciting additional funds from the private sector.

 The Forest Service is prohibited by law from soliciting outside funding, but the foundation has been expressly designated to fulfill that function.
