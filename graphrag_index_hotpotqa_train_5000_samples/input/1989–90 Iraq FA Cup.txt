1989–90 Iraq FA Cup

The 1989–90 Iraq FA Cup was the 13th edition of the Iraq FA Cup.

 The tournament was won by Al-Zawraa for the sixth time, beating Al-Shabab 2–1 on penalties in the final after a 0–0 draw.

 The first three rounds were between teams from the lower division, before the top-flight clubs began to enter at the round of 32.
