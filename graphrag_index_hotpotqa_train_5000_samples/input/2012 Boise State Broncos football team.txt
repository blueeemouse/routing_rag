2012 Boise State Broncos football team

The 2012 Boise State Broncos football team represented Boise State University in the 2012 NCAA Division I FBS football season.

 The Broncos were led by head coach Chris Petersen and played their home games at Bronco Stadium.

 This season was Boise State's second in the Mountain West Conference.

 They finished the season 11–2, 7–1 in Mountain West play to share the conference championship with Fresno State and San Diego State.

 They were invited to and were champions of the Maaco Bowl Las Vegas for the third consecutive year, this year defeating Washington 28–26.
