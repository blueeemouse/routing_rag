Yoo Se-yoon

Yoo Se-yoon (born September 12, 1980) is a South Korean comedian and television comedy show host.

 He hosts and co-hosts a variety of shows, in addition to being a part of musical/comedic duo, UV.

 Yoo has been a part of shows such as South Korean talk show Non-Summit, Witch Hunt (South Korean TV series), SNL Korea, South Korean talk show Golden Fishery as well as its segments, Knee Drop Guru and Radio Star.
