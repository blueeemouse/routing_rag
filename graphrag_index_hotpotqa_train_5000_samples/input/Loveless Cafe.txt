Loveless Cafe

The Loveless Cafe is in southwest Nashville, Tennessee on Highway 100, just east of the northern terminus of the Natchez Trace Parkway.

 It is known for its Southern cooking, especially for its biscuits, fruit preserves, country ham, and red-eye gravy.

 The establishment has received acclaim from "USA Today", "Southern Living", Frommer's, and a number of other prominent national publications.

 It was purchased by Charles A. Elcan and his wife Trisha Frist, the daughter of Thomas F. Frist, Jr., in December 2014.
