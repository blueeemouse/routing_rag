Second weekend in box office performance

In the United States, a film's box office gross in its second weekend of theatrical release is one of several factors used to predict overall box office performance.

 Most films experience a decline in box office gross in its second weekend.

 If the percentage of the drop is below the average, it indicates a subpar box office performance for the rest of its theatrical run.

 Some films are exceptions that they perform better in their second weekend of release than on opening weekend.
