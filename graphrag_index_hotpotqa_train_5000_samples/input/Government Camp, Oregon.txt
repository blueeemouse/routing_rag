Government Camp, Oregon

Government Camp is an unincorporated community and census-designated place located in Clackamas County, Oregon, United States, south of Mount Hood and north of Tom Dick and Harry Mountain.

 It is the only town within 5 mi of Mount Hood and therefore is the "de facto" "mountain town".

 It is a gateway to several ski resorts, the most popular being Timberline Lodge and Mount Hood Skibowl.

 Government Camp also has its own, smaller ski resort, Summit Ski Area.

 Somewhat farther east there is another very popular resort, Mount Hood Meadows.
