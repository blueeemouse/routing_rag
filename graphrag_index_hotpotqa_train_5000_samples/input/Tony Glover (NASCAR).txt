Tony Glover (NASCAR)

Anthony Glover (born April 17, 1957 in Kingsport, Tennessee) is an American race mechanic, crew chief, and manager.

 He has won the Daytona 500 three times as a crew chief in the NASCAR Sprint Cup Series.

 He is the son of former NASCAR Late Model Sportsman champion Gene Glover and most recently served as the crew chief of the #33 Circle Sport Chevrolet in the NASCAR Sprint Cup Series.

 He is currently the director of NASCAR's Touring Series, a position he has held since July 30, 2013.
