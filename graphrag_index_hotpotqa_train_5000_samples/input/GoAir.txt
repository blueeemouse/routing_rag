GoAir

GoAir is a low-cost carrier based in Mumbai, India.

 It is owned by the Indian business conglomerate Wadia Group.

 In July 2017 it was the fifth largest airline in India with an 7.8% passenger market share.

 It commenced operations in November 2005 and operates a fleet of Airbus A320 aircraft in all economy configuration.

 As of 2017 , the airline operates over 140 daily flights to 23 cities from its hubs at Mumbai, Delhi and Kolkata.
