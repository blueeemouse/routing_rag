Slavyanka Russian Chorus

The Slavyanka Men's Russian Chorus is a chorus based in San Francisco with a repertoire of folk song, art songs, and Orthodox liturgical music in the languages of the Orthodox Christian countries.

 Slavyanka has released a number of CDs, and provided music for several movies, most notably What Dreams May Come, starring Robin Williams, and Little Odessa.

 The chorus has toured Russia, Georgia, Croatia and performed at the Russian Embassy in Washington, DC.
