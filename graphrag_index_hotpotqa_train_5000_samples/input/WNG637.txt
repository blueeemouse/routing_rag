WNG637

WNG637 (sometimes referred to as San Diego Marine All Hazards) is a NOAA Weather Radio station that serves the coastal area of the San Diego metropolitan area and can be heard 40 miles in the Pacific Ocean.

 It is programmed from the National Weather Service forecast office in San Diego, California with its transmitter located in Mt. Soledad.

 Unlike its general weather radio counterpart KEC62, it broadcasts marine forecasts and hazard information only for San Diego County.
