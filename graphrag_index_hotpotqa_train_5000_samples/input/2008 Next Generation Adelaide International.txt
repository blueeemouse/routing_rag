2008 Next Generation Adelaide International

The 2008 Next Generation Adelaide International was a men's tennis tournament played on outdoor hard courts.

 It was the 31st edition of the event known that year as the Next Generation Adelaide International, and was part of the International Series of the 2008 ATP Tour.

 It took place at the Memorial Drive Park in Adelaide, Australia, from 31 December 2007 through 6 January 2008.
