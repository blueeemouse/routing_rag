Simon Turner (album)

"Simon Turner" is the debut album of the singer and later film soundtrack composer Simon Fisher Turner.

 It was released in 1973 on UK Records.

 At the time Turner was being widely promoted in the UK pop press as a homegrown alternative to David Cassidy.

 The album featured a combination of covers, including David Bowie's "The Prettiest Star", and original songs mainly written by the producer Jonathan King.
