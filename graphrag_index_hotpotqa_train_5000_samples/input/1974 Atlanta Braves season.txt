1974 Atlanta Braves season

The 1974 Atlanta Braves season was the ninth season in Atlanta along with the 104th season as a franchise overall.

 The team finished third in the National League West with a record of 88–74, 14 games behind the Los Angeles Dodgers.

 During the season, Braves outfielder Hank Aaron became the all-time career leader in home runs, surpassing Babe Ruth.
