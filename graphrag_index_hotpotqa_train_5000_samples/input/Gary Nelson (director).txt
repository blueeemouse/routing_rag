Gary Nelson (director)

Gary Nelson (born January 1934) is an American television and film director.

 He has directed many well-known television series, including "Gunsmoke", "The Patty Duke Show", "Gilligan's Island" and "Happy Days".

 In addition, Nelson has directed five feature films, including two for Walt Disney Pictures, and many television movies, including "Murder in Coweta County" starring Johnny Cash and Andy Griffith.
