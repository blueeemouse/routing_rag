Toulouse Rodéo FC

Rodéo Football Club is a football club based in Toulouse, France.

 Though the club's proper name is Rodéo Football Club, the club is often referred to as Toulouse Rodéo Football Club to show the club's location.

 The club currently plays in Championnat National 3 after winning promotion from Division d'Honneur Midi-Pyrénées in 2015–16
