Frank Rijkaard

Franklin Edmundo "Frank" Rijkaard (] ; born 30 September 1962) is a Dutch former footballer and former manager who played as a midfielder or defender.

 Rijkaard has played for Ajax, Real Zaragoza and Milan and represented the Netherlands national team side 73 times, scoring 10 goals.

 In his managerial career, he has been at the helm of the Netherlands national team, Sparta Rotterdam, Barcelona, Galatasaray and the Saudi Arabia national team.
