Go Fish (TV series)

Go Fish is an American television sitcom that aired on NBC from June 19 to July 3, 2001.

 The series was created by Adam Herz (a screenwriter for the "American" Pie film series), developed by Pam Brady (a consultant writer for "South Park") and starred Kieran Culkin in his only regular role in a television series.

 A total of five episodes were produced, leaving one of those five unaired.
