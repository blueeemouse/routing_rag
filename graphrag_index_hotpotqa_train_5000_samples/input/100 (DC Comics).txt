100 (DC Comics)

The 100, the 10 and the 1000 are fictional organized crime groups appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics.

 The 10 debuted in "Superman" #665 (September 2007), and were created by Kurt Busiek and Rick Leonardi.

 The 100 debuted in "Superman's Girl Friend Lois Lane" #105 (October 1970), and were created by Bob Kanigher.

 The 1,000 debuted in "Booster Gold" #2 (March 1986), and were created by Dan Jurgens.
