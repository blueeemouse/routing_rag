Rock n' Roll Nights

Rock n' Roll Nights is the eighth studio album by Canadian rock band BTO, released in 1979.

 This album was one of three BTO albums that did not feature co-founder Randy Bachman.

 "Rock n' Roll Nights" is also one of the two albums from this band to feature Jim Clench, formerly of April Wine.

 This album generated sales of only about 350,000 copies, though a single from the album called "Heartaches" managed to reach #60 on the U.S. charts and cracked the Top 40 in Canada.

 "Rock n' Roll Nights" is a rare find on CD, as it was released only for a short time on that format in 1990.
