Life Goes On (Fergie song)

"Life Goes On" is a song recorded by American singer Fergie for her second studio album, "Double Dutchess" (2017).

 It was released as single on November 11, 2016, by Interscope and will.i.am Music Group.

 The song serves as the third single from Fergie's second studio album, following "M.I.L.F. $".

 "Life Goes On" was written by Fergie, Tristan Prettyman, Keith Harris and Toby Gad.
