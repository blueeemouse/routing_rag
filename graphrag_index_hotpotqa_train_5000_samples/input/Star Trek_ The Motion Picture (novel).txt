Star Trek: The Motion Picture (novel)

Star Trek: The Motion Picture.

 A Novel is a 1979 novelization of the film "", which was released in the same year.

 It is especially notable for being the only "Star Trek" novel to be written by Gene Roddenberry, who created the franchise.

 It was also the first "Star Trek" novel published by Pocket Books, beginning a prolific relationship with the franchise that continues as of 2012.

 At the time the book was published, however, Bantam Books held the rights to publish original Star Trek-based fiction; Pocket Books wouldn't publish its first original Trek novel until 1981.
