Diriyah Governorate

Al-Dir'iyyah Governorate is a governorate ("muhafazah") within Riyadh Region in Saudi Arabia.

 The governorate is bounded by the Saudi capital city of Riyadh to the south and east, by Dhruma Governorate to the south and west, and by Huraymla Governorate to the north.

 The governorate is seated in the city of al-Dir'iyyah, the original hometown of the House of Saud, which served as the capital of the Saudi dynasty from 1744 to 1818.

 The population of the governorate was 60,777 at the time of the 2004 census, including 18,040 non-Saudis.

 34,117 live in the city of al-Dir'iyyah itself.
