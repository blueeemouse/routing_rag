Michelle Anches

Michelle Anches (?

 - 1926) was a woman who claimed to be the Grand Duchess Tatiana Nikolaevna of Russia.

 In 1925, Anches headed to Denmark to meet with Grand Duchess Tatiana's grandmother, Maria Feodorovna.

 But Michelle was killed in her house, so she never met her alleged grandmother.

 There are still unknown details about her death.

 Michelle Anches was considered very similar to the Grand Duchess Tatiana Nikolaevna of Russia.
