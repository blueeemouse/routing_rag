Space Ducks

Space Ducks: Soundtrack is a compilation album by American recording artist Daniel Johnston.

 It was released on Daniel Johnston's Eternal Yip Eye Label in 2012, with a later, longer version released on Feraltone in 2013.

 It was inspired by Johnston's comic Space Ducks: An Infinite Comic Book of Musical Greatness.

 Its also features songs from Unknown Mortal Orchestra, Eleanor Friedberger, Deer Tick, Fruit Bats, and Lavender Diamond.
