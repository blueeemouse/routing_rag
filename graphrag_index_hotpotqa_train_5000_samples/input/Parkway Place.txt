Parkway Place

Parkway Place is an upscale shopping mall in Huntsville, Alabama that opened on October 16, 2002.

 Parkway Place is located at the site of the older Parkway City Mall, which was torn down to allow for the construction of the newer facility.

 The mall is located at the intersection of Memorial Parkway (U.S. 231) and Drake Avenue.

 With a total of 643135 sqft and 70 in-line stores, Parkway Place is anchored by Dillard's and Belk.

 The mall is now the only indoor shopping mall in Huntsville after Madison Square Mall closed in early 2017.
