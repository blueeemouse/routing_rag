Taratata

Taratata was a French TV show showcasing live and pre-recorded footage of current rock acts.

 Presented by Nagui since its début in 1993, the show was initially shown on France 2.

 The show often involved surprise and unlikely duets, as well as brief interviews with the artists.

 "Taratata" was cancelled in 2000, but returned in April 2005 on the public French network France 4 once a week on prime time, France 3, and late night once a month France 2 and Virgin Radio.

 Artists including Devendra Banhart, Katie Melua, and Ayo made their French TV debut on the show.

 The show's chief lighting technician, Jean-Philippe Bourdon, was awarded two Sept d'or in 1994 and 1995.
