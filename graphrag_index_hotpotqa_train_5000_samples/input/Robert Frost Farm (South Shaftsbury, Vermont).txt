Robert Frost Farm (South Shaftsbury, Vermont)

The Robert Frost Farm, also known as "The Gully", is a historic farm property on Buck Hill Road in South Shaftsbury, Vermont.

 The 1790 farmstead was purchased in 1929 by poet Robert Frost, and served as his primary residence until 1938.

 During this period of residency, Frost was awarded two Pulitzer Prizes for his poetry.

 The property was designated a National Historic Landmark and listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1968; its landmark designation was withdrawn in 1986 after its private owners made alterations that destroyed important historic elements of the property.
