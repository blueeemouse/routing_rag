Rufus Gifford

John Rufus Gifford (born August 5, 1974) is an American diplomat.

 He served as the United States Ambassador to Denmark from 2013 to 2017, and was the Finance Director for Barack Obama's presidential re-election campaign in 2012.

 On August 1, 2013, Gifford's nomination from President Obama to be the next United States Ambassador to the Kingdom of Denmark was confirmed by the United States Senate.

 He was sworn into the role on August 15, 2013, and presented his credentials to the Queen of Denmark on September 13, 2013.
