Betty Blue

Betty Blue is a 1986 French film.

 Its original French title is 37° 2 le matin, meaning "37.2°C in the morning" (the normal temperature of a pregnant woman in the morning).

 The film was directed by Jean-Jacques Beineix and stars Béatrice Dalle and Jean-Hugues Anglade.

 It is based on the 1985 novel of the same name by Philippe Djian.

 The film had 3,632,326 admissions and was the eighth highest-grossing film of the year in France.
