Fairfield, Maine

Fairfield is a town in Somerset County, Maine, United States.

 The population was 6,735 at the 2010 census.

 The town includes Fairfield Center, Fairfield village and Hinckley, and borders the city of Waterville to the south.

 It is home to the Good Will-Hinckley School, Lawrence High School and Kennebec Valley Community College.
