2007 UEFA Champions League Final

The 2007 UEFA Champions League Final was an association football match between A.C. Milan of Italy and Liverpool F.C. of England on 23 May 2007 at the Olympic Stadium, Athens, Greece.

 The showpiece event was the final match of the 2006–07 season of Europe's premier cup competition, the UEFA Champions League.

 The teams were appearing in the final, two years after facing each other in the 2005 final which Liverpool won 3–2 in a penalty shootout after the match finished 3–3.
