Stele Forest

The Stele Forest, or Beilin Museum (碑林; pinyin: Bēilín), is a museum for steles and stone sculptures in Xi'an, China.

 The museum, which is housed in a former Confucian Temple, has housed a growing collection of Steles since 1087.

 By 1944 it was the principal museum for Shaanxi province.

 Due to the large number of steles, it was officially renamed the Forest of Stone Steles in 1992.

 Altogether, there are 3,000 steles in the museum, which is divided into seven exhibitions halls, which mainly display works of calligraphy, painting and historical records.
