The Vanishing Act

The Vanishing Act: The Untold Story of the Missing Malaysian Plane is an upcoming English language feature film from India inspired by the events surrounding the disappearance of Malaysia Airlines Flight 370.

 To be directed by Kamasutra 3D writer-director and Academy Awar Long List Nominee Rupesh Paul, The Vanishing Act is under production currently.

 The new storyline of the movie makes it an Action-Psychological-Thriller, revolving around the unintended actions of a young software developer.
