Priyanga Burford

Priyanga Burford is an actress and writer, who has appeared in roles in a number of British films and television series.

 Her credits include roles in "A Long Way Down", "A Rather English Marriage", "The Thick of It", "Silent Witness" and "Casualty".

 In 2015 she starred in the Channel 4 mockumentary "" in which she played the fictional Deepa Kaur, the first Asian woman to be elected as a UK Independence Party Member of Parliament.

 In 2017 she appeared in the premiere production of Consent at the Royal National Theatre, London.
