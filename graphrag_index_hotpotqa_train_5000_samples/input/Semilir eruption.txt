Semilir eruption

The Semilir eruption was a major volcanic event which took place in Indonesia during the Early Miocene.

 This eruption formed Semilir Formation and Nglanngran Formation.

 These two geological formations are in Southern Mountains of East Java.

 The eruption created two formations consists mostly pyroclastic rock (minor lava flows).

 It has incredibly thickness (at least 1200 m).

 In contrast, Toba eruption only formed 600 m ignimbrite.

 Semilir eruption age date by isotope method is 21 million years ago.
