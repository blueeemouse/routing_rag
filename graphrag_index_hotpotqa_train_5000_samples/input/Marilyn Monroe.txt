Marilyn Monroe

Marilyn Monroe (born Norma Jeane Mortenson; June 1, 1926 – August 5, 1962) was an American actress and model.

 Famous for playing comic "dumb blonde" characters, she became one of the most popular sex symbols of the 1950s and was emblematic of the era's attitudes towards sexuality.

 Although she was a top-billed actress for only a decade, her films grossed $200 million by the time of her unexpected death in 1962.

 More than half a century after her death, she continues to be considered a major popular culture icon.
