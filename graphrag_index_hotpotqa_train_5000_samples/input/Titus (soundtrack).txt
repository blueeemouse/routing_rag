Titus (soundtrack)

Titus is the original soundtrack to the 1999 motion picture "Titus".

 Elliot Goldenthal wrote the score for the film, an adaptation of Shakespeare's first, and bloodiest, tragedy "Titus Andronicus"; written and directed by Julie Taymor, Goldenthal's long-time friend and partner.

 The only non-Goldenthal piece is an old Italian song called ""Vivere"" performed by Italian singer Carlo Buti.
