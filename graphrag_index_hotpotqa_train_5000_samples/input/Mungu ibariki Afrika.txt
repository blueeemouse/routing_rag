Mungu ibariki Afrika

"Mungu ibariki Afrika " (English: God bless Africa) is the national anthem of Tanzania.

 The anthem is the Swahili language version of Enoch Sontonga's popular hymn "Nkosi Sikelel' iAfrika ".

 The word "Mungu " in Swahili means God and the title of the anthem therefore translates as "God bless Africa".
