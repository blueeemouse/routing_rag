Udhayan (film)

Udhayan is an Indian Tamil action-romance film directed by debutant Chaplin.

 Starring Arulnithi, in his second film, alongside newcomer Pranitha, the film features musical score by saxophone player Kadri Gopalnath's son Manikanth Kadri.

 A Muthamizh Padaippagam production, it was started during Pongal 2011 and released on 24 June 2011 with mixed reviews.
