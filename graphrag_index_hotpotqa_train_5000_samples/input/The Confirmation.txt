The Confirmation

The Confirmation is a 2016 Canadian drama film starring Clive Owen, Jaeden Lieberher, and Maria Bello.

 Written and directed by Bob Nelson, who also produced it with Todd Hoffman, the film concerns a young boy (Lieberher), who tries to reconnect with his divorced father (Owen).

 The film also co-stars Robert Forster, Tim Blake Nelson, Patton Oswalt and Matthew Modine.

 It marked the first time Nelson had ever directed a film.

 "The Confirmation" received a limited release in selected theaters in the United States and on iTunes on March 18, 2016.
