Old Town, Staten Island

Old Town is a neighborhood in the New York City borough of Staten Island, located on its East Shore.

 Old Town was established in August 1661 as part of New Netherland, and was the first permanent European settlement on Staten Island.

 Originally described as "Oude Dorpe" (old village in Dutch), much of its original territory makes up what is present-day South Beach, with parts of Midland Beach and Dongan Hills.

 The area was settled by a group of Dutch, Walloon (from what is now southern Belgium and its borders with France) and French Protestants (Huguenots) led by Walloon Pierre Billiou.
