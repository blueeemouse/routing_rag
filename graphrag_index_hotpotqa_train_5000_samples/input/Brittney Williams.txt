Brittney Williams

Brittney L. Williams (October 10, 1989) is a Burbank, California-based character designer and comic artist.

 She has a bold, colorful style that is unusual in superhero comics.

 She has worked on comics such as Patsy Walker, A.K.A. Hellcat!

 with Kate Leth and Goldie Vance with Hope Larson.

 She has also done character design for DC Superhero Girls.
