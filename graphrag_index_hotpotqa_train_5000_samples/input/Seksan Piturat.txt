Seksan Piturat

Seksan Piturat (also written Sakesan Pituratana) (Thai: เศกสรรค์ ปิตุรัตน์ ) or the nickname "James" is a Thai former football player, who has been described as the "Ronaldo" of Thai football.

 He played striker for Thailand national team in various tournaments such as World Cup 2002 (Qualifying), Asian Cup 2000 and scored 19 goals for the national team.

 Currently, he plays in Thailand Division 1 League with Royal Thai Police FC.
