2004 LPGA Tour

The 2004 LPGA Tour was a series of weekly golf tournaments for elite female golfers from around the world which took place from March through December 2004.

 The tournaments were sanctioned by the United States-based Ladies Professional Golf Association (LPGA).

 This was the 55th season since the LPGA Tour officially began in 1950.

 The season consisted of 32 official money events.

 Total prize money for all tournaments was $42,875,000.
