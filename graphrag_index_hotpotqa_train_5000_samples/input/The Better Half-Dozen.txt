The Better Half-Dozen

The Better Half-Dozen was an American garage rock band formed in New Orleans, Louisiana, in 1966.

 The band played at local colleges, high schools and clubs in New Orleans and surrounding cities and states.

 They released one 45 single with 2 originals called "I'm Gonna Leave You" and "I Could Have Loved Her", which, despite insufficient promotion, has remained a classic garage rock recording.
