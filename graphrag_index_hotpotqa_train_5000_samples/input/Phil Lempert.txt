Phil Lempert

Phil Lempert (born April 1953) has been the Food Trends Editor for NBC's "Today" show since 1991.

 Known as the "SupermarketGuru", Lempert appears weekly with "New Product Hit's & Misses" on "ABC Now" and hosts a weekly radio show called "Good Day with SupermarketGuru."

 Lempert is a contributing editor of "Supermarket News", and a content provider for "SN."

 He has written for "Newsday", "Family Circle", and "Meat & Seafood Merchandising", among other publications.
