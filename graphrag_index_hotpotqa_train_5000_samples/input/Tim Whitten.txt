Tim Whitten

Tim Whitten is an Australian record producer, audio engineer, and mixer.

 He has worked with numerous successful Australian musicians, in a career spanning 1990—present.

 Whitten's first recording was Boxcar's "Vertigo", where he joined Adrian Bolland as engineer.

 Whitten has produced records for prominent Australian artists, including Powderfinger, The Go-Betweens, Hoodoo Gurus, and Augie March.
