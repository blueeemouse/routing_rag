Anmol Ratan

Anmol Rattan (Rare Jewel) is 1950 Bollywood drama film directed by M. Sadiq.

 The film was produced by Jaimani Dewan for his Dewan Productions banner, with music composed by Vinod.

 The film starred Karan Dewan, who was known as a "jubilee star" with most of his films becoming box-office successes.

 He was the younger brother of producer Jaimani Dewan.

 Karan's co-stars were Meena Shorey (credited, as she was in all her films, as just Meena), Nirmala, Gope, Madan Puri and Gulab.
