Anil V. Kumar

Anil V Kumarr (born 4 June 1975) is a television and film director and producer in India.

 He started his production house "Flying Turtle Films" with his partners Shabbir Ahluwalia and Sakett Sawhnney in 2010.

 The shows he has directed include "Kahaani Ghar Ghar Kii" and "Kahin To Hoga", both on Star Plus.

 In 2013, Flying Turtle Films co-produced the movie "Zanjeer" starring Priyanka Chopra and Ram Charan.
