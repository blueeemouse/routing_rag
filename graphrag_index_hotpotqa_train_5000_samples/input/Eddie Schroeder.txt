Eddie Schroeder

Edward "Eddie" Julius Schroeder (January 20, 1911 – December 1, 2005) was an American speed skater who competed at the 1932 and 1936 Winter Olympics.

 In 1932 he finished eighth in the 10,000 m event.

 Four years later he again finished eighth in the 10000 m; he also placed 12th over 1500 m and 15th over 5000 m.

 He was selected for the 1940 Olympic team, but the games were canceled due to World War II.
