The Flat (2011 film)

The Flat (Hebrew: הדירה) is a 2011 feature documentary film, an Israeli–German co-production written and directed by Arnon Goldfinger.

 It was theatrically released in Israel in September 2011.

 It played continuously for thirteen months and has received rave reviews. "

Time Out Tel Aviv" chose to place the film at the top of its recommended films for 49 weeks under the headline: "not to be missed" and chose it as one of the 25 most important art works from around the world for 2011.

 "The Flat" was theatrically released in Germany in June 2012.

 The German version of the film features the voice of renowned German actor Axel Milberg taking on the role of narrator Arnon Goldfinger.

 "The Flat" was theatrically released in USA in October 2012 and was theatrically released in Hungary in April 2015.
