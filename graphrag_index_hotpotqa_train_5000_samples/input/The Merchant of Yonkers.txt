The Merchant of Yonkers

The Merchant of Yonkers is a play by Thornton Wilder.

 The play had its origins in an 1835 one-act farce by the English dramatist John Oxenford.

 Oxenford's work, "A Day Well Spent", was later extended into a full-length play entitled "Einen Jux will er sich machen" ("He Will Go on a Spree" or "He'll Have Himself a Good Time") by Austrian playwright Johann Nestroy in 1842.

 Wilder adapted Nestroy's version into an Americanized comedy entitled "The Merchant of Yonkers", which revolves around Horace Vandergelder, a wealthy Yonkers, New York businessman in the market for a wife.
