Asheville, North Carolina

Asheville is a city in and the county seat of Buncombe County, North Carolina, United States.

 It is the largest city in Western North Carolina, and the 12th largest city in North Carolina.

 The city's population was 87,236 according to the 2013 estimates.

 It is the principal city in the four-county Asheville metropolitan area, with a population of 424,858 in 2010.

 Asheville is home to the United States National Climatic Data Center (NCDC), the world's largest active archive of weather data.
