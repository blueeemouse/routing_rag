Black on Broadway

Black on Broadway is a 2004 HBO stand up comedy special by Lewis Black.

 In it Black discusses topics such as: George W. Bush, Bottled water, and the 2004 winter.

 He apparently said the word "fuck" 78 times, but was informed that the number was 42: this arose when the Kennedy Center wanted him to do "Red White & Screwed" there, but required him to tone down his language.
