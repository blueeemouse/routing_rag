Çetmi

Çetmi is a small town in Taşkent district of Konya Province, Turkey.

 According to mayor's page the original name was "Çepni" (referring to a branch of Oghuz Turks); but eventually the word "Çepni" was corrupted to "Çetmi".

 It is a secluded town situated in Toros Mountains at .

 The distance to Taşkent is 20 km and to Konya is 155 km .

The sole road to the town passes through a narrow pass.

 The population of the town was 1281 as of 2011.
