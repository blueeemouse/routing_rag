Port of Lake Charles

The Port of Lake Charles is an industrial port based in the city of Lake Charles, Louisiana, U.S.A.

 It is a major employer in Lake Charles.

 It is the twelfth-busiest port in the United States according to the American Association of Port Authorities "U.S. Port Ranking by Cargo Tonnage, 2013" report and the 83rd-busiest in the world in terms of tonnage according to the American Association of Port Authorities "World Port Rankings 2013" report.

 The Calcasieu Ship Channel provides direct access to the Gulf of Mexico, 34 miles downstream from the city docks.

 The ship channel intersects the Gulf Intracoastal Waterway just north of Calcasieu Lake.

 The Ship Channel has a project depth of 40 feet and a bottom width of 400 feet.
