Alkaline Trio / Hot Water Music

Alkaline Trio / Hot Water Music is a split EP by the Chicago-based punk rock band Alkaline Trio and the Gainesville, Florida-based punk rock band Hot Water Music, released January 22, 2002 through Jade Tree.

 In addition to new material, the EP features each band performing cover versions of songs from the other band's catalog, with Alkaline Trio covering Hot Water Music's "Rooftops" (from "No Division") and Hot Water Music covering Alkaline Trio's "Radio" (from "Maybe I'll Catch Fire") and "Bleeder" (from "I Lied My Face Off").

 It was Alkaline Trio's first release with drummer Derek Grant, who replaced previous drummer Mike Felumlee in 2001.
