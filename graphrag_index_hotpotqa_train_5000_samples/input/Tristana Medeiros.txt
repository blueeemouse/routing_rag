Tristana Medeiros

Tristana Medeiros Da Souza, known in Spanish as La Niña Medeiros is a fictional character and main antagonist of the "REC film series".

 Created by Jaume Balagueró and Paco Plaza, Medeiros Girl was introduced in the first film and had an expanded role in "REC 2".

 In "", she has a cameo that reveals she is the root cause of the demonic outbreak.

 Her origins are told by the character itself in the comic book "REC: Historias Ineditas", released in 2012 alongside "Genesis".
