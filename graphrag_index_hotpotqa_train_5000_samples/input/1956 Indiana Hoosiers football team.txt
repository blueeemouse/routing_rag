1956 Indiana Hoosiers football team

The 1956 Indiana Hoosiers football team was an American football team that represented the Indiana Hoosiers in the 1956 Big Ten Conference football season.

 The Hoosiers played their home games at Memorial Stadium in Bloomington, Indiana.

 The team was coached by Bernie Crimmins, in his fifth and final year as head coach of the Hoosiers.

 On November 28, 1956, Crimmins, at age 37, resigned as Indiana's head football coach.

 He had compiled a 13–32 record and was unable to produce a winning team in five years in the position.
