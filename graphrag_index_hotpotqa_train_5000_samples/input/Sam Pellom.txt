Sam Pellom

Samuel Troy "Sam" Pellom (born October 2, 1951) is a retired American basketball player born in Wilmington, North Carolina.

 The 6'9" forward-center played professionally for the Atlanta Hawks and the Milwaukee Bucks of the NBA and played college basketball at the University at Buffalo, and to date the only player that ever played in the NBA for that university's basketball team.

 Pellom also played for the Washington Generals, the team that plays the Harlem Globetrotters.
