Nauset Regional High School

Nauset Regional High School an NEASC accredited high school located in North Eastham, Massachusetts.

 Nauset is inside the Cape Cod National Seashore, making it the only high school on the East Coast located within a National Park.

 The open campus is situated about a half-mile from Nauset Light.

 Nauset's colors are Black and Gold and the school's mascot is the Warrior.
