Chuck Yeager's Air Combat

Chuck Yeager's Air Combat, often shortened as "CYAC", is a 1991 combat flight simulator video game by Electronic Arts.

 Famed pilot Chuck Yeager was a technical consultant in the game and his digitized voice is featured in the game, giving encouragement and praise before and after missions.

 The game is characterized for its good balance of an action laden gameplay which focuses on classical dog fights and a simple but still realistic flight model.
