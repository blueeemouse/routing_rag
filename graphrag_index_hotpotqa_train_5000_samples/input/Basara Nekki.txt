Basara Nekki

Basara Nekki (熱気バサラ , Nekki Basara ) is a fictional character in the Macross universe, the lead singer and guitarist of the band Fire Bomber.

 He first appears in "Macross 7", and also appears in the movie "Macross 7: The Galaxy is Calling Me" and the OVAs "Macross 7 Encore" and "Macross Dynamite 7".

 He is voiced by Nobutoshi Canna (under the name Nobutoshi Hayashi).

 His singing voice and guitar work are provided by Yoshiki Fukuyama.

 His family name "Nekki", as written in Japanese kanji, means "hot air" or "enthusiasm".
