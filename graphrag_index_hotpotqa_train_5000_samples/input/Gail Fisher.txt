Gail Fisher

Gail Fisher (August 18, 1935 – December 2, 2000) was an American actress who was one of the first black women to play substantive roles in American television.

 She was best known for playing the role of secretary Peggy Fair on the television detective series "Mannix" from 1968 through 1975, a role for which she won two Golden Globe Awards and an Emmy Award; she was the first black woman to win either award.

 She also won an NAACP Image Award in 1969.
