Walthamstow Studios

Walthamstow Studios was a British film studio located in Walthamstow, London which operated between 1914 and 1930.

 Two earlier studios had existed in Walthamstow.

 It was the base of Broadwest films for a number of years, which also used Catford Studios at an overflow facility.

 Later it was owned by British Filmcraft.

 After the bankrupt the studio's were sold off for non-film use.
