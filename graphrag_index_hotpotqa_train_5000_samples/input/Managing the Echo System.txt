Managing the Echo System

"Managing the Echo System" is a 10-page funny animal comic book short story written, drawn, and lettered by Carl Barks.

 Characters in the story include Donald Duck, his nephews Huey, Dewey and Louie, a zookeeper, and members of the Nature Boys club.

 The story was first published in "Walt Disney's Comics & Stories" #105 (June 1949).

 The story has been reprinted several times since.
