Flaran Abbey

Flaran Abbey is a former Cistercian abbey located in Valence-sur-Baïse, in the "département" of Gers, France.

 The abbey was founded in 1151, as a daughter house of Escaladieu Abbey, at the confluence of the Auloue and Baïse rivers, between the towns of Condom and Auch.

 The abbey was founded by Burgundian monks and today represents one of the best preserved abbeys in the south-west of France.
