Lancaster station (Pennsylvania)

Lancaster is an Amtrak railroad station and a former Pennsylvania Railroad station in Lancaster, Lancaster County in the U.S. state of Pennsylvania.

 Located on the Keystone Corridor, the station is served by the "Keystone Service" between New York City and Harrisburg, and by the "Pennsylvanian" between New York and Pittsburgh.

 Lancaster is the second busiest Amtrak station in Pennsylvania, and the twenty-first busiest in the United States.
