Gawsworth

Gawsworth is a civil parish and village in the unitary authority of Cheshire East and the ceremonial county of Cheshire, England.

 The population of the civil parish as of the 2011 census was 1,705.

 It is one of the eight ancient parishes of Macclesfield Hundred.

 Twenty acres of the civil parish were transferred to Macclesfield civil parish in 1936
