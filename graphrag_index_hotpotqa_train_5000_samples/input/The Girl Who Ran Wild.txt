The Girl Who Ran Wild

The Girl Who Ran Wild is a 1922 Universal film directed by Rupert Julian.

 It is a black and white silent film, which is based on the 1863 novella "" by Bret Harte.

 It is a remake of the 1918 film "M’Liss".

 The film was written by George C. Hull and features cast such as Gladys Walton (as M’liss), Marc Robbins (“Bummer” Smith) and Vernon Steele (The Schoolmaster).

 It is a western melodrama which was released on October 9, 1922.

 It was filmed in Seven Oaks, California.
