How Rastus Gets His Turkey

How Rastus Gets His Turkey is a 1910 slapstick comedy silent film directed by Theodore Wharton.

 The film stars Billy Quirk (in blackface), Edward José and Octavia Handworth.

 The movie was written by José and produced and released by Pathé Frères.

 The film was part of a series of comedies that featured the title character, Rastus.
