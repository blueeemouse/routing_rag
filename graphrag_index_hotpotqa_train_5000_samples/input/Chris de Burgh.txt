Chris de Burgh

Christopher John Davison (born 15 October 1948), known professionally as Chris de Burgh, is a British-Irish singer-songwriter and instrumentalist.

 He is an art rock performer who also writes pop-oriented material.

 He has had several top 40 hits in the UK and two in the US, but he is more popular in other countries, particularly Norway and Brazil.

 He is most famous for his 1986 love song "The Lady in Red", which reached number one in several countries.

 De Burgh has sold over 45 million albums worldwide.
