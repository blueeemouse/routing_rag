Sing Your Life

"Sing Your Life" is a single by Morrissey released in April 1991.

 It was the second single taken from the "Kill Uncle" album.

 On release this was Morrissey's lowest charting single in the UK charts, reaching only number 33.

 One of the single's B-sides was a cover version of "That's Entertainment" by The Jam which had backing vocals by "Chas Smash", a.k.a. Carl Smyth the second singer of the band Madness.

 Vic Reeves also recorded backing vocals for the song, but they weren't included in the final version.

 He is thanked in the sleeve notes.
