We Can't Live Without Cosmos

We Can't Live Without Cosmos (Russian: My ne mozhem jit bez cosmosa) is a 2015 Russian animated short film directed and written by Konstantin Bronzit.

 It is produced by Alexander Boyarsky.

 The film received critical praise and wide recognition.

 It received many awards including a nomination for the Academy Award for Best Animated Short Film at the 88th Academy Awards.

 The film is mainly a traditionally animated short.
