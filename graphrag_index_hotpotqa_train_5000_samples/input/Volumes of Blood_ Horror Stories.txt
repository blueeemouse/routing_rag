Volumes of Blood: Horror Stories

Volumes of Blood: Horror Stories is a 2016 horror anthology film directed by Sean Blevins, John William Holt, Jon Maynard, Nathan Thomas Milliner, Justin Seaman and James Treakle.

 The screenplay was a collaboration by Sean Blevins, Nathan Thomas Milliner, P.J. Starks, and Jason Turner.

 This installment of the "Volumes of Blood" franchise was produced by Eric Huskisson, David Justice, P.J. Starks, and Devin Taylor.

 Special effects were designed and created by Special Effects Supervisor, Cassandra Baker.

 The film is a sequel to the 2015 horror anthology "Volumes of Blood", both of which were filmed in Owensboro, Kentucky.
