Sentimental Me (album)

Sentimental Me is the third studio album by Wet Wet Wet frontman Marti Pellow.

 This album brought Marti together with multi-award winning jazz pianist Jim Watson.

 All instrumentation was arranged by Marti himself, with a stronger focus on the tempo, enabling a move away from the big band feel usually associated with some of these tracks.

 The single "Take a Letter Miss Jones" was released from the album.
