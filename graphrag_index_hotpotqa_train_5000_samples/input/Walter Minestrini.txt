Walter Minestrini

Walter Minestrini ( born in Cellere, Viterbo, Italy 1925, died Terni, Umbria, Italy 1977) was an Italian writer for the young.

 In between his works he wrote "Ogni uomo è mio fratello", "Tra gli indios delle lagune" (in collaboration with G. Mazzoleni), "Un popolo che scompare" ( in collaboration with S. Zavatti).

 "Pastori di renne" ( in collaboration with Mario Pucci), and "Fiamme sull'Algeria"
