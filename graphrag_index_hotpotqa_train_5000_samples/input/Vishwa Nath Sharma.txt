Vishwa Nath Sharma

General Vishwa Nath Sharma, PVSM, AVSM, ADC was the 15th Chief of the Army Staff of the Indian Army.

 He is the younger brother of the late Major Som Nath Sharma, recipient of Independent India's first posthumous Param Vir Chakra, and also Lieutenant General Surendra Nath Sharma, formerly Engineer in Chief of the Indian Army.

 Both brothers were educated at the Prince of Wales' Royal Indian Military College, Dehradun.
