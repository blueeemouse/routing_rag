Warmness on the Soul

"Warmness on the Soul" is the first EP/single by Avenged Sevenfold.

 It was released on August 8, 2001 by Good Life Recordings.

 The release features the video for the title track, "Warmness on the Soul" as an Enhanced CD bonus.

 The EP was the first release to feature guitarist, Synyster Gates.

 All of the songs are included on Avenged Sevenfold's debut album, "Sounding the Seventh Trumpet", except for the heavy metal version of "To End the Rapture", which was later featured on the album's re-release by Hopeless Records.
