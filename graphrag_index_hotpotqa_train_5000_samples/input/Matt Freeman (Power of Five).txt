Matt Freeman (Power of Five)

Matthew J. Freeman is the protagonist of Anthony Horowitz's "The Power of Five" novels, "Raven's Gate", "Evil Star" and one of the main characters in "Necropolis".

 He also briefly appeared in the third book of the series, "Nightrise".

 He was born in London, England, to an English mother and a father from New Zealand.

 However, after the death of his parents he went to live in Ipswich, then York.
