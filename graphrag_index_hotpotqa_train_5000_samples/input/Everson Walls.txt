Everson Walls

Everson Collins Walls (born December 28, 1959) is a former American football defensive back who played professionally in the National Football League (NFL) for the Dallas Cowboys, the New York Giants, and the Cleveland Browns.

 During his 14 seasons, he was a four-time Pro Bowl selection.

 He was also a 3-time All-Pro selection.

 Walls won a Super Bowl with the Giants after the 1990 season.

 He played college football at Grambling State University.
