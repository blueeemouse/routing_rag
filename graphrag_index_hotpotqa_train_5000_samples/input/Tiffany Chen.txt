Tiffany Chen

Tiffany Chen Ming-Yin is a Hong Kong administrative producer for films made by China Star Entertainment Group.

 Chen has been named one of The Hollywood Reporter's international Women in Entertainment.

 Women in Entertainment is an annual list of 100 women power players in Hollywood as well as selected influential women film executives overseas.

 Chen is the wife of actor-turned-film producer/presenter Charles Heung.

.

They have 2 sons together.
