Sunset Blvd (song)

"Sunset Blvd" is a song by Scott Grimes, which appears on his album, "Livin' on the Run".

 The single was written by Scott Grimes and Dave Harris and produced by Tom Fletcher.

 The song climbed into the Top 20 of Billboard’s Adult Contemporary chart in April 2005.

 The song remained in the Top 20 for 10 weeks without climbing higher than #18.
