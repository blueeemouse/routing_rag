Can't Stop (EP)

Can't Stop is the fifth Korean mini-album (ninth overall) by the South Korean rock band CNBLUE.

 It was released on February 24, 2014 with the track "Can't Stop" as the promotional single.

 This mini album marks a whole turn on CNBLUE's music.

 Its lead singer, Jung Yong-hwa ditches the guitar and plays piano on most of the songs.

 The style of this album is, as its leader singer stated on an interview, a bit of brit rock meets melodic pop rock and even Latin melodies that can be heard in Diamond Girl.
