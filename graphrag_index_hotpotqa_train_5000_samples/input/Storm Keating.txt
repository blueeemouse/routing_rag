Storm Keating

Storm Keating (born 27 October 1981) is an Australian-born fashion designer, brand ambassador, producer-director and blogger, now based in London.

 Keating has worked on a number of Australian and British television programmes such as "The Apprentice Australia", "Masterchef Australia", "The X Factor", "The Voice Australia", and "The Voice UK".

 She is the wife of Ronan Keating.
