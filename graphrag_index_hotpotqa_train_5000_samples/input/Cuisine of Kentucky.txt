Cuisine of Kentucky

In northwestern parts of Kentucky burgoo is a favorite, while in southwestern parts of the state regular chili con carne is typical staple.

 In northern Kentucky plus a smaller amount in Louisville and Lexington Cincinnati chili is a popular fast food.

 That region and the Louisville area also are both home to a pronounced German-American population, translating into northern-like preferences for beer and European sausages.

 However, the remainder of the state's cuisine tends to be thoroughly Southern, preferring breakfast meats like country ham, ground pork sausage and as their beverage of choice, the state's renowned bourbon whiskey.

 Some common desserts would be chess pie, pecan pie, blackberry cobbler and bread pudding.
