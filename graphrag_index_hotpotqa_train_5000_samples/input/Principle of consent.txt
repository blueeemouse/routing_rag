Principle of consent

Principle of consent is a term used in the context of the Northern Ireland peace process and is one of the key points of the Good Friday Agreement.

 The principle states both the legitimacy of the aspiration to a United Ireland and the legitimacy of the wish of the majority of the people of Northern Ireland to remain part of the United Kingdom.

 The doctrine also underlines the right of self-determination for the people of both jurisdictions in Ireland, Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland, without external interference, and only with the consensus of a majority of people in both polities.
