Scadoxus nutans

Scadoxus nutans is a herbaceous plant endemic to southwest Ethiopia.

 Its red to pink flowers face downwards as the top of the flowering stem bends over, unlike any of the other species of "Scadoxus".

 It grows mainly as an epiphyte in tropical mountain forests, which are disappearing, making the species vulnerable to extinction.

 It is sometimes cultivated as an ornamental plant.
