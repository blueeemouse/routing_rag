The Circus Is Coming

The Circus Is Coming is a children's novel by Noel Streatfeild, about the working life of a travelling circus.

 It was first published in 1938 with illustrations by Steven Spurrier.

 For this novel, Streatfeild was awarded the annual Carnegie Medal from the Library Association, recognising the year's outstanding children's book by a British subject.

 American editions and some later British editions are titled Circus Shoes.
