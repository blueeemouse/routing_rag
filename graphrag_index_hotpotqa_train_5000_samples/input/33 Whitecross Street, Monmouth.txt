33 Whitecross Street, Monmouth

33 Whitecross Street is a grade II listed building in Monmouth, Monmouthshire, Wales.

 It is in the historic St James Square neighbourhood.

 The property was the site of archaeological excavation in 2009, which demonstrated evidence of Neolithic (prehistoric), Roman, and Medieval activity.

 The following year, archaeological excavation in the square discovered the first evidence of Mesolithic human settlement in Monmouth.
