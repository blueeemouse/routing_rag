WBZW

WBZW (1520 AM) is a radio station in Altamonte Springs, Florida.

 Owned by Pennsylvania Media Associates, Inc., the station operates at 1520 kHz with a daytime power of 5 kW & a nighttime power of 350 watts.

 Its transmitter is located in Apopka, Florida.

 The station currently programs a Business News/Talk format.
