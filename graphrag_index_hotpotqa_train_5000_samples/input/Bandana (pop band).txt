Bandana (pop band)

Bandana is an Argentine pop girl group formed in 2001.

 Following the international trend of teen pop girl groups established by the Spice Girls, the five members of Bandana — Ivonne Guzmán, Lourdes Cecilia Fernández, Valeria Gastaldi, Virginia Da Cunha and María Elizabeth Vera — were selected on the TV show "Popstars" and were a carefully marketed product that became a phenomenon in the country, particularly among young girls.

 Their self-titled debut album was released in 2001 on Sony BMG Records, led by the hit single "Guapas", and was certified four-times platinum by the CAPIF.

 Despite being initially snubbed by many critics and artists, Bandana emerged as Argentina's best-selling act since 2001.
