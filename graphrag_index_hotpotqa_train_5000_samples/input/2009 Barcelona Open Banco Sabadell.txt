2009 Barcelona Open Banco Sabadell

The 2009 Barcelona Open Banco Sabadell (also known as the "Trofeo Torneo de Godó") was a men's tennis tournament played on outdoor clay courts.

 It was the 57th edition of the event known that year as the Barcelona Open Banco Sabadell, and was part of the ATP World Tour 500 series of the 2009 ATP World Tour.

 It took place at the Real Club de Tenis Barcelona in Barcelona, Spain, from 20 April through 26 April 2009.
