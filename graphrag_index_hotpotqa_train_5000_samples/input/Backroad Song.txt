Backroad Song

"Backroad Song" is a song recorded by American country music artist Granger Smith.

 It was initially released to radio independently on March 24, 2015, but was officially re-released to radio on October 5, 2015 via Wheelhouse Records.

 It is included on his major label debut album, "Remington", released March 4, 2016.

 The song was written by Smith and Frank Rogers.

 It garnered positive reviews from music critics who praised Smith's vocal performance and his detail-oriented lyrics with Rogers.
