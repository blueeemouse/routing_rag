Hum TV

Hum TV is a 24-hour entertainment TV channel based in Karachi, Pakistan.

 It was founded by Sultana Siddiqui and Duraid Qureshi.

 It is owned by Hum Network Limited (KSE:HUMNL).

 Hum Network Limited was known as "Eye Television Network Limited" prior to 21 January 2011.

 Hum TV began its transmission on 17 January 2005.

 In March 2013, Hum Network held its first Hum Awards ceremony.
