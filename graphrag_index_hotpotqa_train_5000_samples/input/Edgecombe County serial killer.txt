Edgecombe County serial killer

The Edgecombe County serial killer, also known as the Seven Bridges Killer, is an as yet unidentified serial killer in the surroundings of Edgecombe and Halifax counties in North Carolina, United States.

 There are ten suspected victims, all African-American women, and the remains of eight have been recovered.

 It is suspected that Antwan Maurice, a registered sex offender who was charged with a murder fitting a similar profile, was responsible for the Edgecombe deaths.

 All of the victims had a history of drugs and/or prostitution.
