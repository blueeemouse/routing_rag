Miss Perú Universo 2008

The Miss Perú 2008 was held on May 1, 2005.

 That year, only 16 candidates were competing for the national crown.

 The chosen winner represented Peru at the Miss Universe 2008 pageant which was held in Vietnam.

 The Miss Earth Perú would enter in Miss Earth 2008.

 The first runner up would enter in Miss Continente Americano 2008.

 The second runner up would in Miss Tourism World 2008.
