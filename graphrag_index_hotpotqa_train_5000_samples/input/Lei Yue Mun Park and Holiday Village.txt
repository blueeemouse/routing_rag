Lei Yue Mun Park and Holiday Village

Lei Yue Mun Park and Holiday Village used to be Lyemun Barracks, barracks for the British Soldiers stationed in Hong Kong.

 It is located in the east of Shau Kei Wan, facing Lei Yue Mun, with an area of 22.97 Hectares.

 This is the only Holiday Village in the urban districts of Hong Kong (Hong Kong Island and Kowloon).
