Saint Monica (film)

Saint Monica is a 2002 Canadian film written and directed by Terrance Odette.

 It was nominated for Best Lead Performance by a Female actress (Genevieve Buechner) in a Feature Length Drama at the 2002 Leo Awards and won Best Achievement in Music for an Original Song (""Com Estas Asas"" by Carlos Lopes) at the 2003 Genie Awards.
