Samm Levine

Samuel Franklin "Samm" Levine (born March 12, 1982) is an American actor and comedian.

 He is known for his portrayal of Neal Schweiber on NBC's short-lived "Freaks and Geeks" and PFC Hirschberg in the 2009 film "Inglourious Basterds".

 Levine is also the sidekick and fill-in host on the internet talk show "Kevin Pollak's Chat Show".
