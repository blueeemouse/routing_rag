Hansel and Gretel (1982 film)

Hansel and Gretel is a TV special that was made in 1983 for Disney directed by Tim Burton.

 It only aired once on October 31, 1983 at 10:30pm.

 The only other times it was shown was as part of the Tim Burton retrospective at the Museum of Modern Art in New York City, and the Tim Burton L'Exposition at the Cinémathèque Française in Paris as part of a traveling exhibit.
