Lenoir–Rhyne University

Lenoir–Rhyne University is a co-educational, private liberal arts university founded in 1891 and located in Hickory, North Carolina, USA.

 The university is affiliated with the North Carolina Synod of the Evangelical Lutheran Church in America (ELCA).

 In 2013, additional campuses were added.

 A graduate school in downtown Asheville, NC and the Lutheran Theological Southern Seminary in Columbia, SC bring the total student population in all 3 campuses to over 2,300, faculty to over 190 and staff to 246 in the 2016-17 school year.
