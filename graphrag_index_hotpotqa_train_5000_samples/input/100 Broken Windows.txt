100 Broken Windows

100 Broken Windows is the second full-length studio album by Scottish band Idlewild, released on 9 May 2000.

 The album showcased a significantly mellower, less distorted sound for Idlewild, with the songs being of a slower tempo than those on the punk influenced "Hope Is Important".

 In 2008, vocalist Roddy Woomble stated that the album "seems to be the record many people make a connection with."
