Hans Zollner

Hans Zollner is a German theologian and psychologist.

 He has been a member of the Jesuits since 1990.

 He studied philosophy and theology in Regensburg and Innsbruck.

 Since 2003, he has taught at the Institute of Psychology at the Pontifical Gregorian University in Rome.

 Zollner has been the academic Vice Rector of the Gregorian since 2010.
