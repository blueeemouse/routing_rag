The Best of Minnie Riperton

The Best of Minnie Riperton is a posthumous greatest hits album by American singer Minnie Riperton, released in 1981 and issued by Capitol Records.

 The album consists of the hits like "Perfect Angel", "Lovin' You", "Inside My Love", and "Adventures in Paradise".

 Also included, her last two released singles "Here We Go" from the album "Love Lives Forever" and "You Take My Breath Away" (both from the album "Love Lives Forever").
