Olimpiu G. Urcan

Olimpiu G. Urcan (born 1977, Romania) is a Singapore-based chess player, historian, author and journalist.

 A medievalist scholar, he contributes history-related articles to world's top chess magazines ("New In Chess", "CHESS", and "Chess Life") and has authored several biographies of forgotten chess masters. His research is largely focused on American chess history.

 In particular, he focused on little-remembered chess experts with a special focus on immigrants.
