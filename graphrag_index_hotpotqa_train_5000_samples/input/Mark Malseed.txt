Mark Malseed

Mark Malseed is an author, information industry consultant and investigative reporter.

 Malseed graduated from Devon Preparatory School in suburban Philadelphia in 1993 and from Lehigh University in 1997 where he majored in architecture and urban studies.

 After graduation, Malseed continued to volunteer at Lehigh, via the Young Alumni Council, promoting alumni education and networking.

 After Lehigh University, he also studied at the DIS – Danish Institute for Study Abroad in Copenhagen.
