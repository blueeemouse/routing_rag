Nákladové nádraží Žižkov

Praha-Žižkov freight railway station (Czech: "nákladové nádraží Žižkov" ) is a railway station formerly used for rail freight transport, located in the Žižkov district of Prague, the capital of the Czech Republic.

 It was originally opened in 1936 and ceased operations in 2002.

 The station building was confirmed as a cultural monument in March 2013.
