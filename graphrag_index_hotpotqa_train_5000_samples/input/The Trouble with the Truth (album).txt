The Trouble with the Truth (album)

The Trouble with the Truth is the eighth album by country music artist Patty Loveless.

 It peaked at number 10 on the Billboard Top Country albums charts, and number 86 on the Pop charts.

 It was certified Platinum for shipments of over 1,000,000 copies in the U.S.

 The singles "Lonely Too Long" and "You Can Feel Bad" both made number 1 on the Hot Country Songs charts; "She Drew a Broken Heart" hit number 4.

 "A Thousand Times a Day" and the Title Track both made Top 20 hitting number 13 and 15 respectively.
