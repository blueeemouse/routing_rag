Candyman (album)

Candyman is the second solo album from Steve Lukather.

 It was a collaboration of musicians who were for the most part also in Lukather's band Los Lobotomys.

 Toto familiars Simon Phillips and David Paich participated as well as David Garfield, John Pêna, Chris Trujillo, Lenny Castro, Larry Klimas, Fee Waybill, Richard Page and Paul Rodgers.

 Lukather recorded the album in mostly live takes with little overdubbing.
