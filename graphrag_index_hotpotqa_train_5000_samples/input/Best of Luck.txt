Best of Luck

Best of Luck is a 2010 Malayalam comedy film written and directed by M.A. Nishad.

 It stars Prabhu, Urvashi, Asif Ali, Kailash, Archana Kavi and Rima Kallingal, and features Mammootty in a cameo role.

 The soundtrack was composed by the Indian rock band Euphoria.

 The song "NaNa", which was usred in the trailers, was composed by A. R. Rahman for an American film, "Couples Retreat".

 The film was a remake of the Bollywood comedy film "".

 Unlike the original, the film was extremely negatively received and was a commercial failure.

 The movie was remade in Kannada in 2015 as "Ond Chance Kodi".
