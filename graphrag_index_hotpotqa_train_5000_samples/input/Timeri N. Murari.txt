Timeri N. Murari

Timeri Murari is an Indian novelist, journalist, playwright and screenwriter.

 He is the author of fourteen published novels, including best-sellers "The Taliban Cricket Club" (2012) and "Taj" (2007), and has written extensively for Indian and international newspapers including "The Guardian".

 He has also written the screenplay of the award-winning Hindi movie "Daayraa" (1997), which was voted one of the ten best films of 1997 by "Time" magazine.
