Jack Hermansson

Jack Hermansson (born June 10, 1988) is a Swedish mixed martial artist currently living in and fighting out of Norway.

 He competes in the Middleweight division of the Ultimate Fighting Championship.

 He is the former champion in Cage Warriors and Warrior Fight Series.

 In 2012-2013 he fought in the United States in the Bellator MMA promotion.

 On the European circuit he held notable victories over UFC veteran Karlos Vemola and Bellator veteran Norman Paraisy among others.
