Arkan Sonney

An arkan sonney (literally "lucky urchin" or "plentiful pig") is the Manx term for hedgehog.

 In Manx folklore it is a type of fairy creature which looks like a pig with long hairs.

 They are said to bring good luck to one who catches them.

 For this reason they're also called "lucky piggies".

 They flee human beings.

 They live in the wild, are faster than lighting on land and have feathered wings that can be up to one and a half times their body size.
