Yuh-Line Niou

Yuh-Line Niou () is a Taiwanese-American politician.

 She is a Democratic Party member of the New York State Assembly for the 65th district, the seat previously held by Sheldon Silver.

 This district which, is heavily Democratic and 40% Asian, includes Chinatown, the Financial District, Battery Park City, and the Lower East Side.

 Niou is the first Asian-American elected to the State Assembly for this district.

 Her name is pronounced "you-lean nee-oh" or "you lean new", according to various sources.
