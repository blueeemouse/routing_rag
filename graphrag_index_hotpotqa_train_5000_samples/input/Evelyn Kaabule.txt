Evelyn Kaabule

Kaabule Evelyn Naome Mpagi (born 1 July 1966) is a Ugandan human resource management consultant and politician.

 She is a former Member of Parliament, having served as the first Women's Representative for Luuka District after it was created by an act of parliament and became functional on 1 July 2010.

 She served in this role until 2016 when she was defeated by Esther Mbulakubuza Mbayo in the 2016 general election.

 Then a member of the National Resistance Movement (NRM), she served on three parliamentary committees during her time in Parliament: the Public Accounts Committee, the Committee on Social Services, and the Presidential Affairs Committee.

 Having been defeated by Mbayo in the NRM primary, she ran on an independent ticket in the 2016 election cycle.
