Chatelaine (magazine)

Chatelaine is an English-language Canadian magazine of women's lifestyles and the number one magazine in Canada in paid circulation.

 Both "Chatelaine" and its French-language version, "Châtelaine", are published monthly by Rogers Media, a division of Rogers Communications.

 It was first published in March 1928 by Maclean Publishing.

 Due to falling print ad revenues, "Chatelaine" reduced its publication frequency to 6 times a year beginning in 2017.

 Other Rogers Media publications are also to either reduce their publication frequency or become digital only publications.

 Rogers Media also announced that it intends to divest itself of its French-language publications, including "Châtelaine".
