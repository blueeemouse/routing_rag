Bittersweet (Sophie Ellis-Bextor song)

"Bittersweet" is a song by British singer-songwriter Sophie Ellis-Bextor, written by Sophie Ellis-Bextor, James Wiltshire, Russell Small, Richard Stannard and Hannah Robinson for Ellis-Bextor's fourth studio album "Make a Scene".

 The song was released as the album's third single on 3 May 2010, following two singles on which Ellis-Bextor collaborated, that also appear on "Make a Scene".
