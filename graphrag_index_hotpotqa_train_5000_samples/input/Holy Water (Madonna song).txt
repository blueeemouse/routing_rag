Holy Water (Madonna song)

"Holy Water" is a song recorded by American singer and songwriter Madonna for her thirteenth studio album, "Rebel Heart" (2015).

 The song was written by Madonna, Martin Kierszenbaum, Natalia Keery-Fisher, Mike Dean, Kanye West and Tommy Brown.

 It was produced by Madonna, Dean and West, with Charlie Heat serving as co-producer.

 The demo of "Holy Water" leaked on the internet in late December 2014 and later its full version leaked in February 2015, a month prior to the album's release.

 "Holy Water" is an electronica and technopop song, and it features minimalist bass stutter, ricocheting synths and "martial beats" in its instrumentation.
