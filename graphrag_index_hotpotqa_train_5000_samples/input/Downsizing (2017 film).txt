Downsizing (2017 film)

Downsizing is a 2017 American science fiction comedy-drama film directed by Alexander Payne and written by Payne and Jim Taylor.

 The film stars Matt Damon, Christoph Waltz, Hong Chau, Jason Sudeikis, and Kristen Wiig.

 Principal photography on the film began in Ontario, Canada on April 1, 2016.
