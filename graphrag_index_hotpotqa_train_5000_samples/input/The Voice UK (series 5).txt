The Voice UK (series 5)

The Voice UK is a British television music competition to find new singing talent.

 The fifth series began airing on 9 January 2016 on BBC One.

 The series is hosted by Emma Willis and Marvin Humes, and are joined by coaches will.i.am and Ricky Wilson.

 Rita Ora did not appear in this series of the show after joining "The X Factor" and was replaced by Boy George.

 In addition, Sir Tom Jones did not return and was replaced by Paloma Faith.
