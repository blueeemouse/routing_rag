Federation of Trade Unions of Ukraine

The Federation of Trade Unions of Ukraine (known by its Ukrainian acronym, FPU) is an all-Ukrainian voluntary association of trade unions that represents national-level industrial sector in the Crimea and the city of Kiev and it surrounding region.

 It is the largest trade union confederation in Ukraine, with more than 8.5 million members.

 As of 1 March 2011, 44 national trade unions and 26 regional trade unions were affiliated to the FPU.
