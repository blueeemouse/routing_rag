A Virus Knows No Morals

A Virus Knows No Morals (German: " Ein Virus kennt keine Moral") is a 1986 German film directed, written and produced by Rosa von Praunheim.

 The film is a black comedy about the AIDS epidemic.

 Made with a low budget, campy style and no professional actors, it covers many aspects of AIDS and its effects as well as attacking the rumors surrounding it in a satirical way.
