Shouma Yamamoto

Shouma Yamamoto (山本 匠馬 , Yamamoto Shōma ) is a Japanese actor from Osaka Prefecture.

 His first major role was as Tsubasa Yamagatana/Dan the Knight of the White Night in "".

 Yamamoto later portrayed Seiji Hayami in "".

 He also portrayed Takato Shiramine/Kamen Rider Rey in the "Kamen Rider Kiva" movie "", and in the TV series as Taiga Nobori/Kamen Rider Saga.

 He is one of two actors to portray two different characters in a single Kamen Rider Series film and television series; the other is Kenji Matsuda in "Kamen Rider Hibiki" and "Kamen Rider Hibiki & The Seven War Oni".
