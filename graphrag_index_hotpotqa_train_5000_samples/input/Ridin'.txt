Ridin'

"Ridin'" is a song by American rapper Chamillionaire, released as the lead single from his debut studio album "The Sound of Revenge" (2005).

 The song, produced by Play-N-Skillz, features a guest appearance from fellow American rapper Krayzie Bone, of Bone Thugs n Harmony.

 Its title is often incorrectly assumed to be "Ridin' Dirty", due to the chorus.

 The lyrics concern racial profiling and police brutality, as well as the stereotyping of African-Americans driving a vehicle with drugs or other contraband on the inside ("Riding dirty").
