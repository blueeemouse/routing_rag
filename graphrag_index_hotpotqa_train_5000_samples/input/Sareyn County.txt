Sareyn County

Sareyn County (Persian: شهرستان سرعین‎ ‎ ) is a county in Ardabil Province in Iran.

 The capital of the county is Sareyn.

 The county was split off from Ardabil County after the 2006 census.

 At the 2006 census, the county's population was 17,197, in 3,937 families.

 The county consists of two districts: the Central District and Sabalan District.

 The county has one city: Sareyn.
