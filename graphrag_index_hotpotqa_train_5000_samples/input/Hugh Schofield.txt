Hugh Schofield

Hugh Robert Armstrong Schofield (born 19 August 1961), is the Paris Correspondent for BBC News, the main newsgathering department of the BBC, and its 24-hour television news channels BBC World News and BBC News Channel, as well as the BBC's domestic television and radio channels and the BBC World Service.

 He was formerly a BBC correspondent across Europe, the Middle East and United States, with over 25 years' experience in reporting for BBC radio and television.

 He became BBC Paris Correspondent in 1996.
