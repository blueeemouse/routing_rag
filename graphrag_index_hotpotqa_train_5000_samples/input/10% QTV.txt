10% QTV

10% QTV is a Canadian television newsmagazine series, which aired on Rogers Television stations in Ontario from 1995 to 2001.

 It was the first multiseason television series in Canada targeted specifically to the lesbian, gay, bisexual and transgender community, being preceded only by short run series such as "Coming Out" in 1972, and "Out of the Closets" and "Gay News and Views" in 1977-78.
