Irsee Abbey

Irsee Abbey, also the Imperial Abbey of Irsee (German: "Reichsabtei Irsee" ), was a Benedictine abbey located at Irsee near Kaufbeuren in Bavaria.

 The self-ruling imperial abbey was secularized in the course of the German mediatization of 1802–1803 and its territory annexed to Bavaria.

 The buildings of the former abbey now house a conference and training centre for Bavarian Swabia.
