The Chimes (album)

The Chimes is the debut and only album by Scottish dance music act The Chimes, released in 1990.

 Featuring singer Pauline Henry on vocals, the album includes their cover of U2's "I Still Haven't Found What I'm Looking For", which reached number 6 in the UK Singles Chart, along with their other Top 40 hit "Heaven", which reached number 24.

 The album was certified silver in the UK, and gold in Australia.
