John Wall Dance

The John Wall Dance is a dance performed by flexing the arms and twisting the wrist.

 John Wall first performed the eponymous dance during his introduction at Big Blue Madness at the University of Kentucky in October 2009.

 Wall says that his idea for the dance came from the music video of the song "Do the Shizz" made by the Louisville rapper Kenzo.

 LaShawn "Sugar Shizz" Talbert, who inspired the dance, performed "The Shizz" in the music video.

 The John Wall Dance has subsequently integrated itself into popular culture.
