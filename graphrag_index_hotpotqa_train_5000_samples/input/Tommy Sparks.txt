Tommy Sparks

Thomas "Tommy" Sparks (born 7 February 1986) is a Swedish-born English singer-songwriter from London.

 He is known for his songs "She's Got Me Dancing" and "I'm a Rope".

 His self-titled album was released on 11 May 2009 in the United Kingdom.

 He co-wrote The Prodigy song "Wild West" featured on the bonus disc for "Invaders Must Die".

 He also features on the Filthy Dukes album "Nonsense in the Dark" on the song "Messages".
