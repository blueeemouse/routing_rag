List of Evil Con Carne characters

The American animated television series "Evil Con Carne" features a cast of fictional characters created by Maxwell Atoms.

 The series, which originally aired on Cartoon Network from July 11, 2003 to October 22, 2004, follows the brain of a playboy who wants to rule the world with his general Skarr and his lover, Major Doctor Ghastly.

 Since Hector Con Carne is attached to a bear named Boskov, Hector wants to find the rest of his body parts and take over the world.

 Unlike Maxwell Atoms' The Grim Adventures of Billy & Mandy, Evil Con Carne lived a short life with only 13 episodes.
