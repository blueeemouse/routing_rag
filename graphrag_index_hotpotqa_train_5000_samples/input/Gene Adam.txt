Gene Adam

Gene Adam was the original singer of Iced Earth/Purgatory.

 Adam joined Purgatory in 1985 and performed many shows with them, as well as recording several demo tapes.

 In 1988, the band changed their name to Iced Earth and added Randall Shawver as a second guitarist.

 Adam wrote the lyrics to "Written On The Walls", the only Iced Earth song he is noted as co-writing.

 The band began working on demos in 1988 and released their first official demo tape "Enter The Realm" in 1989, followed by the self-titled Iced Earth album in 1990.

 Gene continued to perform many shows with the band, notably touring Germany in early 1991 as support for Blind Guardian.
