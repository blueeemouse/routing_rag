American Bank of Investments (Albania)

American Bank of Investments operates in the Albanian market since 1999.

 Original known as Emporiki Bank, a local branch of Greece's Emporiki Bank Group in March 2010 it became part of France's Crédit Agricole Group when it acquired 100% stake at the Greek parent company.

 In December 2015 Crédit Agricole Albania Bank SHA changed its name to American Bank of Investments and became a subsidiary of NCH Capital.

 The bank provides products and services to individuals and small enterprises in Albania.
