Russell Lake West, Nova Scotia

Russell Lake West is a planned residential subdivision and commercial development in the eastern part of the community of Dartmouth in the Halifax Regional Municipality, Nova Scotia.

 The area began construction in 2006 with the creation of Exit 8 on Highway 111, providing access from the southwest to the area via Mount Hope Avenue, which as of October 2011, Exit 8 also provides direct access into the Woodside Industrial Park.

 Russell Lake West is located next to the community of Woodside and a park and walking trails provide direct access to and from Woodside.
