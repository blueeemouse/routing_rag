Kelly Fu

This is a Chinese name; the family name is Fu.

 Kelly Fu Ka Lei (Chinese: 傅嘉莉) (born 6 January 1985) is a Hong Kong-German actress and model born in Hong Kong.

 She is currently active in Hong Kong, managed under the Television Broadcasts Limited (TVB) having debuted in 2003, in the drama series Hearts of Fencing.

 She has starred in popular award-winning TV series, "Captain of Destiny", opposite Ruco Chan in 2015.

 She can speak Cantonese, English and Mandarin.

 She is currently one of the Next-Gen Fadans at TVB.
