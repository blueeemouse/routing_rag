Congregation of the Blessed Sacrament

The Congregation of the Blessed Sacrament (S.S.S. – "Societas Sanctissimi Sacramenti") is a Catholic religious congregation of priests, deacons, and brothers founded by St. Peter Julian Eymard.

 By their life and activities, they assist the Church in her efforts to form Christian communities whose center of life is the Eucharist.

 They commit themselves to the implementation of this ideal in collaboration with lay men and women engaged in various ministries.
