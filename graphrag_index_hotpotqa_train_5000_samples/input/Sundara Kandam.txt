Sundara Kandam

Sundara Kandam (English: The Beautiful chapter) is 1992 Tamil comedy movie directed by K. Bhagyaraj.

 The movie's name is adaption from the title of fifth major division or 'Kaandam' of Indian Epic Ramayanam.

 The film was remade in Hindi as Andaz with Anil Kapoor.

 It was also remade in Telugu and Kannada as Sundarakanda with Venkatesh and Shivarajkumar respectively.

 Mammootty starrer Malayalam film Mazhayethum Munpe was partially inspired from this film.
