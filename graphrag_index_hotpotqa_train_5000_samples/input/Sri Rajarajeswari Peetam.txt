Sri Rajarajeswari Peetam

The Rajarajeswari Peetam in Rush, New York is a Hindu temple that practices the teachings of SriVidya.

 Sri Chaitanyananda Natha Saraswathi (or Aiya, as they call him) is the peetathipathy of the temple along with Gnanamba (or Amma,) his wife.

 Aiya is a disciple of Sri Amritananda Natha Saraswathi who is the Peetathipathy of the Devipuram temple.

 The temple was founded in Zambia, then moved to the Aiya's home in Rush in New York state before the current, purpose-built temple was constructed.
