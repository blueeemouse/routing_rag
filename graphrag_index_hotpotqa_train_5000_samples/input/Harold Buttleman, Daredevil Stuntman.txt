Harold Buttleman, Daredevil Stuntman

Harold Buttleman, Daredevil Stuntman (also known as Buttleman) is a 2003 film written and directed by Francis Stokes; the only movie he has directed.

 It won the Jury Prize at the Deep Ellum Film Festival in 2003.

 It was awarded the audience award in the Had to Be Made Film Festival in 2005.
