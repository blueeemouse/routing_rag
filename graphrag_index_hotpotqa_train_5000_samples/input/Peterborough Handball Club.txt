Peterborough Handball Club

Peterborough Handball Club is a handball club based in Peterborough, England.

 It was founded in August 2012, building on interest in handball generated by the London 2012 Olympics.

 In the 2012-13 season Peterborough HC's men's team played in the England Handball East Regional League.

 Peterborough's first win came on 12 January 2013 against Norwich Terriers, Peterborough recording an 18-17 win thanks to a last minute goal by Dan Gammons.
