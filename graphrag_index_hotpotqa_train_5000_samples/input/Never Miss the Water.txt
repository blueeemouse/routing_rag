Never Miss the Water

"Never Miss the Water" is a 1995 recording written by Gerry DeVeaux and Charlie Mole and performed by Chaka Khan, featuring vocals by Meshell Ndegeocello "Never Miss the Water" had been previously unreleased and was included in Chaka Khan's 1997, ".

 The track went to #36 on the soul singles chart.
