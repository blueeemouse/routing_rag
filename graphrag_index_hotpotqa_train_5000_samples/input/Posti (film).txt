Posti (film)

Posti (Punjabi: ਪੋਸਤੀ ) is a 1950 Punjabi film and the first production of Kwatra Production (of Sardul Singh Kwatra and Harcharan Singh Kwatra), directed by K.D. Mehra (Krishan Dev Mehra), starring Majnu and Shyama in lead roles.

 Shyama was the principal female actress of this movie
