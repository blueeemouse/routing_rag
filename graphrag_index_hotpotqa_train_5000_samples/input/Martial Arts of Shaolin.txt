Martial Arts of Shaolin

Martial Arts of Shaolin (, lit.

 "Southern and Northern Shaolin") aka Shaolin Temple 3: Martial Arts of Shaolin, is a 1986 Hong Kong martial arts film.

 It is notable as the only collaboration between film director Lau Kar-leung and actor Jet Li.

 The film was later released on Region 1 DVD by The Weinstein Company under the Dragon Dynasty imprint.
