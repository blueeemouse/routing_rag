Lula, Georgia

Lula is a city in Hall and Banks counties in the U.S. state of Georgia.

 Most of the city is in Hall County, with a small eastern portion of the city located in Banks County.

 The population was 2,758 at the 2010 census.

 The Hall County portion of Lula is part of the Gainesville, GA Metropolitan Statistical Area.

 Lula is home of RailRoad Days, a yearly festival usually every May.

 Major roads near Lula are Georgia Highway 51, Georgia Highway 52, Georgia Highway 365, U.S. Route 23, and old US 23.

 Lula is the railroad junction between the Atlanta northeast line and the Athens north line.
