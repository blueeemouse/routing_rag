Celeborn

Celeborn (pronounced with a hard "c" as in "cat"; ) is a fictional character in J.R.R. Tolkien's Middle-earth legendarium.

 Celeborn is an Elf, and the husband of Galadriel.

 He appears in "The Lord of the Rings" as the Lord of the Galadhrim, and co-ruler along with Galadriel of Lothlórien.

 He was the father of Celebrían (the wife of Elrond), and thus the grandfather of Arwen Evenstar and her older brothers Elladan and Elrohir.

 He was also a kinsman of the First Age Elven King Thingol.

 "Celeborn" means "(tall) silver tree" in Tolkien's invented language of Sindarin.
