David Christopher Davies

David Christopher Davies (1827 - 1885) was a geologist.

 He grew up in the Oswestry area, and began his career as the apprentice of a local ironmonger.

 As a member of the Oswestry Naturalists' Field Club he developed an interest in the geology of the area, and the age of 30 set himself up as a mining engineer, working mainly in north Wales, but also across the channel, in France and Germany.
