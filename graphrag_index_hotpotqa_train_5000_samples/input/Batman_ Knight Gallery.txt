Batman: Knight Gallery

Batman: Knight Gallery is an American comic book published in 1995 by the DC Comics imprint "Elseworlds".

 Written by Doug Moench, and features the art of Thomas Grummett, Michael Manley, Vincent Giarrano, James Balent, Bret Blevins, Graham Nolan, Norm Breyfogle, Neal Adams, George Pérez, Stephen De Stefano, Tom Lyle, Gary Frank, David Taylor, Anton Furst.
