Slicked-up Pup

Slicked-up Pup is a 1951 American one-reel animated cartoon and is the 60th "Tom and Jerry" cartoon directed by William Hanna and Joseph Barbera and produced by Fred Quimby.

 The cartoon was scored by Scott Bradley and animated by Ed Barge, Kenneth Muse, Irven Spence and Ray Patterson.

 It features the second appearance of both Spike and Tyke together (although Tyke is referred to as 'Chip').
