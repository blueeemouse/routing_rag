Truant Officer Donald (comics)

"Truant Officer Donald" is a 10-page funny animal comic book short story written, drawn, and lettered by Carl Barks.

 Characters in the story include Donald Duck, his nephews Huey, Dewey and Louie, and the boys' schoolmates Butch and Finnegan.

 The story was first published in "Walt Disney's Comics & Stories" #100 (January 1949).

 The story has been reprinted several times since.
