Whitefield's Tabernacle, Moorfields

Whitefield's Tabernacle, Moorfields (also known as Moorfields Tabernacle) is a former church at the corner of Tabernacle Street and Leonard Street, Moorfields, London, England.

 The first church on the site was a wooden building erected by followers of the evangelical preacher George Whitefield in 1741.

 This was replaced by a brick building in 1753.

 John Wesley preached a sermon "On the death of the Rev Mr George Whitefield" both here and at Whitefield's Tabernacle, Tottenham Court Road in 1770.
