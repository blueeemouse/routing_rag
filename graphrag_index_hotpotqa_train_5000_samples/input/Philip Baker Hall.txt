Philip Baker Hall

Philip Baker Hall (born September 10, 1931) is an American actor.

 In film, Hall has portrayed the lead roles of President Richard Nixon in Robert Altman's "Secret Honor" (1984) and Arthur Pratt in "Duck" (2005).

 He also starred in several of Paul Thomas Anderson's films, "Hard Eight" (1996), "Boogie Nights" (1997) and "Magnolia" (1999).

 Hall is known for his portrayal of Lieutenant Bookman, Library Cop on "Seinfeld".
