Mihai Gavrilă

Mihai Gavrilă (] ; b. October 16, 1929, Cluj) is a Romanian quantum physicist, member of the Romanian Academy since 1974.

 He made fundamental contributions to quantum theories of electromagnetic interactions with atoms.

 His parents were Ion and Florica Gavrilă (née Vișoiu).

 His father taught medicine and his mother taught English at the University of Cluj.
