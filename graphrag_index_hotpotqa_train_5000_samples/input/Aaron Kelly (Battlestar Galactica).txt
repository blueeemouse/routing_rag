Aaron Kelly (Battlestar Galactica)

Captain Aaron Kelly is a fictional character, played by Ty Olsson, in the reimagined "Battlestar Galactica" TV program.

 The character has been part of the show since the 2003 Miniseries.

 Captain Kelly is the landing signal officer (LSO) aboard the Battlestar "Galactica".

 At the beginning of the second season, Captain Kelly was third in line of command, after then-Commander Adama and then-Colonel Tigh.

 The character of Aaron Kelly has no direct analog in the original "Battlestar Galactica".
