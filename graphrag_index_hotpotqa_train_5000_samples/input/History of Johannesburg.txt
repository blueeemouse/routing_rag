History of Johannesburg

Johannesburg is a large city in Gauteng Province of South Africa.

 It was established as a small village controlled by a Health Committee in 1886 with the discovery of an outcrop of a gold reef on the farm Langlaagte.

 The population of the city grew rapidly,becoming a municipality in 1897.

 In 1928 it became a city making Johannesburg the largest city in South Africa.

 In 2002 it joined ten other municipalities to form the City of Johannesburg Metropolitan Municipality.

 Today, it is a centre for learning and entertainment for all of Africa.

 It is also the capital of Gauteng.
