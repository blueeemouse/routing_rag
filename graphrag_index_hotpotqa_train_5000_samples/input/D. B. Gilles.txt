D. B. Gilles

D.B. Gilles is an American screenwriter, playwright, academic, Script Consultant and Writing Coach specializing in screenplays, TV pilots, plays and novels.

 In 2010 he entered the world of blogging with the introduction of his blog Writers Rehab: For Writers Who Can't Get Their "Acts" Together.

 In 2013 he turned his blog into a book on writer's block: Writers Rehab: A 12-Step Program For Writers Who Can't Get Their Acts Together.
