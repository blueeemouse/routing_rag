The Vengeance of She

The Vengeance of She is a 1968 British fantasy film directed by Cliff Owen and starring John Richardson, Olga Schoberová (credited as Olinka Berova), Edward Judd and Colin Blakely.

 It bears little in common with the novel "Ayesha: The Return of She" by H. Rider Haggard.

 It was made by Hammer Films as a loose sequel to the 1965 hit film "She".
