Cheeseman Island

Cheeseman Island is a 7.6 ha rocky volcanic island in the southwest Pacific Ocean (located at ).

 It is named after Thomas Frederick Cheeseman of the Auckland Museum - who was on board the New Zealand Government steamer 'Stella' when it visited the island in 1887.

 Partly named after Matthew Cheeseman who was first to map the island with his brother.

 It neighbours Curtis Island to the east and lies about 20 km south of Macauley Island.

 They are part of the Kermadec Islands, a straggling outlying island group of New Zealand.

 The Kermadec Islands lie halfway between the North Island of New Zealand and Tonga at approximately the latitude of the Gold Coast, Queensland.
