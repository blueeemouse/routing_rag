U.S. Route 1 in New York

U.S. Route 1 (US 1) is a part of the U.S. Highway System that runs from Key West, Florida, to the Canada–United States border at Fort Kent, Maine.

 In the U.S. state of New York, US 1 extends 21.54 mi from the George Washington Bridge in Manhattan to the Connecticut state line at Port Chester.

 It closely parallels Interstate 95 (I-95) for much of its course, and does not serve as a major trunk route within the state.

 It does not overlap with any other roads besides I-95 and (briefly) US 9, and few other state highways intersect it.
