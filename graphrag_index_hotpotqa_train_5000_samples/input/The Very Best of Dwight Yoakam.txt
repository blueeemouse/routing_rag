The Very Best of Dwight Yoakam

The Very Best of Dwight Yoakam is the third greatest hits compilation album of songs recorded by country music artist Dwight Yoakam.

 The album comprises twenty hit singles from his career.

 The album was certified Gold by the RIAA on July 23, 2008, and Platinum on October 28, 2016.

 It has sold 1,043,000 copies in the United States as of April 2017.
