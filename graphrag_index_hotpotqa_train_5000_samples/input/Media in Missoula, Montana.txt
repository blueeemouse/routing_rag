Media in Missoula, Montana

Since its incorporation in 1885, Missoula, Montana has been one of the primary media markets in the state of Montana, beginning with the weekly newspaper the "Missoula and Cedar Creek Pioneer".

 The Missoula single-broadcast over-air television media market has been the largest in Montana (#166 nationally) since 2002.

 Though Billings is the largest city in Montana, Missoula's single-broadcast over-air television media market includes Missoula, Ravalli, Granite, Mineral, Lake, Flathead, and Sanders and serves over 113,000 television homes (2011).

 Missoula is also home to the state's third largest daily newspaper, the "Missoulian", and the state's largest alternative weekly, the "Missoula Independent".
