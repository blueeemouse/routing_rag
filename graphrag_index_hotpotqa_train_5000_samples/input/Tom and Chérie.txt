Tom and Chérie

Tom and Chérie is a 1955 one reel animated "Tom and Jerry" short directed by William Hanna and Joseph Barbera and produced by Fred Quimby with music by Scott Bradley.

 It was the third (of four) cartoons in the "Mouseketeer" series of cartoons to take place in France, the first of which, "The Two Mouseketeers" (1952) won an Academy Award, and the second, "Touché, Pussy Cat!

" (1954) received an Academy Award nomination.
