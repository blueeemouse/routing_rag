The New Breed (album)

The New Breed is the third album by MC Breed.

 It was released on April 27, 1993 for Wrap Records.

 "The New Breed" found decent success, making it to #156 on the "Billboard" 200 and #17 on the Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums, but the album is best known for the hit single "Gotta Get Mine" which featured Tupac Shakur.

 "Gotta Get Mine" made it to #6 on the Hot Rap Singles, #96 on the "Billboard" Hot 100 and #61 on the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Singles & Tracks.

 Another single, "Tight", made it to #19 on the Hot Rap Singles.

 The song "Gotta Get Mine" appeared in the 2002 Academy Award winning hip-hop film "8 Mile", starring Academy Award winning rapper Eminem.
