Antisemitism in 21st-century France

Anti-Semitism in France has become heightened From the late 20th century into the 21st century.

 In the early 21st century, most Jews in France, like most Muslims in France, are of North African origin.

 France has the largest population of Jews in the diaspora after United States—an estimated 500,000–600,000 persons.

 Paris has the highest population, followed by Marseilles, which has 70,000 Jews, most of North African origin.
