Anjum Fakih

Anjum Fakih is an Indian television actress and model, who has appeared in Hindi television series like "MTV's Chat House", "Time Quake", and "Tere Sheher Mein".

 Currently, she is seen in Kundali Bhagya.

 She has also appeared in the very Popular Show Ek Tha Raja Ek Thi Rani as Rani Rageshwari Singh.

 She played an evil character in the show.

 Anjum Fakih has also won the "Ford Super Model" title.
