Tom's Photo Finish

Tom's Photo Finish is a 1957 one reel animated "Tom and Jerry" short directed and produced by William Hanna and Joseph Barbera with music by Scott Bradley.

 The cartoon was animated by Kenneth Muse, Bill Schipek, Lewis Marshall, Jack Carr, Herman Cohen and Ken Southworth, with backgrounds by Robert Gentle and layouts by Richard Bickenbach.

 It was released on November 1, 1957 by Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer.
