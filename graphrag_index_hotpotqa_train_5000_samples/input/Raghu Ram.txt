Raghu Ram

Raghu Ram Ambadapudi (born 15 April 1973) is an Indian television producer and actor.

 He was earlier a senior supervising producer at MTV India and the executive producer of reality television shows "MTV Roadies" and "MTV Splitsvilla".

 He was married to actress Sugandha Garg.

 His brother is Rajiv Laxman.

 The brothers launched their own content studio called Monozygotics in 2014.
