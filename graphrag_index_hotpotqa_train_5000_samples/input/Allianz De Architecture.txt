Allianz De Architecture

Allianz De Architecture is an architecture, design and planning firm that was founded in 2001 in Mumbai, India.

 It currently operates offices in Mumbai and Bangalore in India and provides urban design and architectural consulting and planning services.

 The firm's designs can be seen at various parts of Mumbai and nearby metropolitan regions and it is known for its work inspired by sustainable modern architectural practices as well as a sensitivity for society and the built-natural environment.
