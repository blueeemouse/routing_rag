Doll &amp; Em

Doll & Em is a British episodic television comedy created by and starring Emily Mortimer and Dolly Wells.

 A six-episode order was commissioned by Sky Living in 2013.

 The series was directed and co-written by Azazel Jacobs.

 A 124-minute theatrical cut of the series was shown at the London Film Festival, premiering 10 October 2013.

 Sky Living premiered "Doll & Em" on 18 February 2014.

 The American cable network HBO acquired the series in September 2013, and premiered it on 19 March 2014.
