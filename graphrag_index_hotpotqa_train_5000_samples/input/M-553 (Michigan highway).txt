M-553 (Michigan highway)

M-553 is a north–south state trunkline highway in the Upper Peninsula (UP) of the US state of Michigan.

 It connects M-35 near Gwinn with the Marquette Bypass, an expressway carrying US Highway 41 (US 41) and M-28 in Marquette.

 M-553 connects Marquette with Sawyer International Airport at the unincorporated community of K.I. Sawyer AFB, the former site of a US Air Force base, in the Sands Plains area of Marquette County.

 The intersection with CR 480 in Sands Township, known locally as the Crossroads, is the site of several businesses.

 North of this location, M-553 runs through some hilly terrain around a local ski hill.
