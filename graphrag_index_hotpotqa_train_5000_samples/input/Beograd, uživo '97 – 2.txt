Beograd, uživo '97 – 2

Beograd, uživo '97 – 2 (trans. "

Belgrade, Live '97 – 2") is the second disc of the fourth live album by Serbian and former Yugoslav rock band Riblja Čorba, released in 1997.

 "Beograd, uživo '97 - 2" was preceded by "Beograd, uživo '97 - 1", as the band, instead of releasing a double live album, opted for two separate releases.

 Album was recorded on Riblja Čorba concert held on June 1, 1997 on Belgrade's Tašmajdan Stadium.
