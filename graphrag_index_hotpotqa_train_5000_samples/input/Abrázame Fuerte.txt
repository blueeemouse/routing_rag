Abrázame Fuerte

"Abrázame Fuerte" (English: "Hold Me Tight" ) is a ballad written by Jorge Luis Piloto, produced by Rudy Pérez and performed by Puerto-Rican American singer Lourdes Robles.

 The song was released as the second single from Robles' second studio album "Imágenes" (1990) and became her first number-one song in the "Billboard" Top Latin Songs chart in the United States and her biggest hit.
