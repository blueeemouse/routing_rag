Ken Pease

Kenneth George "Ken" Pease {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (born August 5, 1943) is a British forensic psychologist and criminologist.

 He is a visiting professor at the Jill Dando Institute of Crime Science at University College London, and is also a visiting professor at the University of Loughborough.

 Previous positions he has held include professor of criminology at the University of Huddersfield (where he founded the Applied Criminology Group in 1995), the University of Manchester (where he was appointed to a professorial chair in 1995), and the head of the Home Office's Policing and Reducing Crime Unit from 1999 to 2000.

 He has been described as a "leading British criminologist" by Zoe McKnight of "Maclean's".
