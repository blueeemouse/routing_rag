Krantiveer

Krantiveer(English:"Brave Revolutionary") is a 1994 Indian Hindi-language action crime film directed by Mehul Kumar.

 The film stars Nana Patekar, Dimple Kapadia, Atul Agnihotri, Mamta Kulkarni, Danny Denzongpa and Paresh Rawal in lead roles.

 It became the third highest-grossing film of the year, additionally winning three Star Screen Awards, four Filmfare Awards and one National Film Award.
