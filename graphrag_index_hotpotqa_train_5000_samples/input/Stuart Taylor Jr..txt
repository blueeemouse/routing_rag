Stuart Taylor Jr.

Stuart Taylor Jr. is an American journalist and author.

 He also served as a Nonresident Senior Fellow in Governance Studies at the Brookings Institution and practices law occasionally.

 He was a reporter for the Baltimore "Sun" from 1971-1974; "The New York Times" from 1980-1988, covering legal affairs and then the Supreme Court; wrote commentaries and long features for "The American Lawyer", "Legal Times" and their affiliates from 1989-1997, and for "National Journal" and "Newsweek" from 1998 through 2010; and has written on a freelance basis for numerous publications both before and since 2010.

 He has also coauthored two books and he and a coauthor will soon publish a third.
