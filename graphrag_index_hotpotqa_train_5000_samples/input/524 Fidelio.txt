524 Fidelio

524 Fidelio is a large minor planet with a diameter of 71 km, orbiting the Sun near the center of the main asteroid belt.

 Fidelio contains both metals and carbon (Spectral class XC).

 Concerning its name, the "Catalogue of Minor Planet Names and Discovery Circumstances" notes, "This is the name of Leonora when disguised as a man in the opera "Fidelio" (composed 1805) by the German composer Ludwig van Beethoven.

 The name dates from a period when Max Wolf assigned the names of female operatic characters to asteroids he had newly discovered.
