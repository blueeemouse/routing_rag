Los Angeles mayoral election, 2009

The 2009 election for Mayor of Los Angeles took place on March 3, 2009.

 Incumbent mayor Antonio R. Villaraigosa was re-elected overwhelming and faced no serious opponent.

 Since Los Angeles holds nonpartisan elections, there was no Democratic or Republican primary.

 Villaraigosa would have faced a run-off against second place-finisher Walter Moore had he failed to win a majority of the vote.
