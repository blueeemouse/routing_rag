Moustafa Elgammal

"Dr. Moustafa Elgammal" (born September 4, 1943), is an Egyptian scientist in the field of marine corrosion and oil spills. Elgammal is a former head of the Marine Engineering Department at Alexandria University.

 Dr. Elgammal received his bachelor's degree in marine engineering with distinction in 1967 from Alexandria University.

 He pursued his academic career by completing his master's degree in Welding Technology in 1969 and was offered a Ph.D.scholarship at the University of Newcastle upon Tyne’s Shipbuilding Department.

 He successfully completed his Ph.D. in Fatigue Life Estimation of Welded ship structural details by 1976.
