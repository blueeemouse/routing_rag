Beautiful Maria of My Soul

"Beautiful Maria of my Soul" (Spanish: "Bella María de mi alma" ) is a song prominently featured in the 1992 motion picture "The Mambo Kings".

 In the film, it is performed in Spanish by Antonio Banderas and in English by Los Lobos.

 The song was written and composed by Arne Glimcher and Robert Kraft (composer).

 The film is based on the book "The Mambo Kings Play Songs of Love".
