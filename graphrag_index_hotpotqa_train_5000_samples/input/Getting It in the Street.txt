Getting It in the Street

Getting It in the Street is the third and final release by David Cassidy on RCA Records and would also be the last album David Cassidy released in the United States until 1990 (with the release of "David Cassidy").

 "Gettin' it in the Street" was released in Germany and Japan in November 1976, but did not reach the album charts.

 The few copies that were pressed for the U.S. were released in July 1979.
