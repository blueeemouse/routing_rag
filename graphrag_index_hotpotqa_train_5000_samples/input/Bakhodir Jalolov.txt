Bakhodir Jalolov

Bakhodir Jalolov (born 8 July 1994) is a Uzbekistani boxer.

 He competed in the men's super heavyweight event at the 2016 Summer Olympics.

 He defeated Edgar Muñoz of Venezuela by TKO in the round of 16.

 He was defeated by eventual silver medalist, Joe Joyce of Great Britain in the quarterfinals.

 Jalolov was the flagbearer for Uzbekistan during the opening ceremony.
