Sunday Life (TV series)

Sunday Life was a British magazine/discussion television programme broadcast on Sundays on BBC One beginning 20 April 2008.

 It was presented by Louise Minchin and Colin Jackson.

 The show, which replaced the "Heaven and Earth Show" was intended to focus on "inspiring stories and thought-provoking discussion", with the slogan "Real stories.

 Real people.

 Real life."

 The show was partly intended to fill the public service remit of the BBC's broadcasting licence, as well as its Sunday morning religious quota.

 It was dropped from the schedule after one series and its slot in the schedule replaced by The Big Questions.
