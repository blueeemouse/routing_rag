Joe Dirt 2: Beautiful Loser

Joe Dirt 2: Beautiful Loser is a 2015 American comedy film directed by Fred Wolf and written by David Spade and Fred Wolf.

 It is the sequel to the 2001 film "Joe Dirt".

 The film stars David Spade reprising his role as the title character, Brittany Daniel, Dennis Miller, Adam Beach, Christopher Walken, Mark McGrath and Patrick Warburton.

 The film premiered on Crackle on July 16, 2015.
