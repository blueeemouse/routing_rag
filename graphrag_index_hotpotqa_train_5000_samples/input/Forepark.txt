Forepark

Forepark is a business park in The Hague, Netherlands, east of the Prins Clausplein highway interchange (A4 and A12).

 It was developed in the 1990s, together with the nearby residential neighbourhood Leidschenveen.

 Originally part of the municipality of Leidschendam, it was annexed by The Hague in 2002 and now falls under the district of Leidschenveen-Ypenburg.

 Since 2007, Forepark is home to Kyocera Stadion, the home stadium of ADO Den Haag.

 Since the construction of the RandstadRail network, the business park is connected to the metro line between Rotterdam and the city centre of The Hague via Forepark Station.
