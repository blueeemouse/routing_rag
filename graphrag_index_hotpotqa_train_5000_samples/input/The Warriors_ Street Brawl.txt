The Warriors: Street Brawl

The Warriors: Street Brawl is a beat 'em up scroller video game created by CXTM.

 It was released on the Mac OS X, Microsoft Windows, and Xbox Live Arcade in Summer 2009.

 It is based on the 1979 movie "The Warriors", which in turn is based on the 1965 novel of the same name written by Sol Yurick.

 The plot of the game is exactly like the film itself, and the 2005 video game of the same name, about a street gang named the Warriors from start to finish with you leading the Warriors street gang back to its home turf on Coney Island, after being unfairly blamed for the assassination of wannabe messiah Cyrus in Van Cortlandt Park.
