Natalya Sergeyeva

Natalya Sergeyeva (born May 3, 1976) is a Kazakhstani sprint canoe racer who competed from the mid-2000s.

 At the 2004 Summer Olympics in Athens, she was eliminated in the semi-finals of the K-2 500 m event while being disqualified in the heats of the K-1 500 m event.

 At the 2012 Summer Olympics she was eliminated in the semi-finals of the K-1 200 m.

 In the K-1 500 m she reached the B Final.
