Break on Me

"Break on Me" (stylized as "Break on Me.")

 is a song written by Ross Copperman and Jon Nite and recorded by New Zealand-born Australian country music singer Keith Urban.

 It was released on 23 October 2015 as the second single from his ninth studio album, "Ripcord", through Hit Red and Capitol Nashville.

 The bass-driven ballad was co-produced by Urban with Nathan Chapman and has been deemed a stylistic counterpoint to its funky predecessor "".

 The song is also the second of his career not to have an accompanying music video, after 2010's "I'm In".
