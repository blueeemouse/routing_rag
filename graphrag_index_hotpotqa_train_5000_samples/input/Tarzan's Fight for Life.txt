Tarzan's Fight for Life

Tarzan's Fight for Life (1958) is an action adventure film featuring Edgar Rice Burroughs' famous jungle hero Tarzan and starring Gordon Scott, Eve Brent, Rickie Sorensen, Jil Jarmyn, and Cheeta the chimpanzee.

 The movie was directed by H. Bruce Humberstone.

 The picture was the second Tarzan movie released in color, and the last to portray the ape man speaking broken English until "Tarzan, the Ape Man" (1981).

 The filming locations were in Africa and Hollywood, California.
