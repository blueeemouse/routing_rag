Frank L. Hayes

Frank Louis "Duke" Hayes (November 21, 1893July 1, 1967) was an American football and basketball player, coach, and athletic director.

 He was a football and basketball player at Marietta College from 1915 to 1918.

 From 1919 to 1924, he was a physical education teacher and coach at McClain High School in Greenfield, Ohio.

 In the four years from 1920 to 1923, Hayes coached the McClain basketball team to an 89–8 record, outscoring opponents 3,319 to 1,251.

 In 1924, he was hired by the University of Michigan as an assistant basketball and football coach.

 He next served as the athletic director at Grinnell College from 1926 to 1927.

 From 1927 to 1941, he served as athletic director and head coach of the basketball and football teams at Marietta College.

 He was posthumously inducted into the Marietta College Hall of Fame in 1984.
