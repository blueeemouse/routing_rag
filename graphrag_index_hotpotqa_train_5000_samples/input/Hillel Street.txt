Hillel Street

Hillel Street (Hebrew: רחוב הלל‎ ‎ , "Rehov Hillel") is one of the central streets of Jerusalem.

 It connects King George Street to the small Ben Sira Street and the Mamilla neighbourhood and is parallel to Ben Yehuda Street.

 The lower part of the road is between Independence Park and the Nahalat Shiva neighborhood.
