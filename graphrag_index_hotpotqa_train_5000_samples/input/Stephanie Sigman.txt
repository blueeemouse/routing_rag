Stephanie Sigman

Stephanie Sigman is a Mexican-American actress.

 Her breakthrough role was in the 2011 crime drama film "Miss Bala".

 She has gone on to appear in "Pioneer" (2013), "Spectre" (2015), "Going Under" (2016), and "" (2017).

 On television, Sigman starred as Valeria Vélez in the first and second seasons of Netflix crime thriller, "Narcos" (2015).
