Decode (song)

"Decode" is a song by American rock band Paramore for the soundtrack of the 2008 romantic fantasy film "Twilight".

 It was written by group members Hayley Williams, Josh Farro, and Taylor York.

 The song was first made available October 1, 2008 through "Twilight" author Stephanie Meyer's website.

 "Decode" was promoted to American modern rock radio by Fueled by Ramen and impacted on October 21, 2008, serving as the soundtrack's lead single.

 It is also included as a bonus track on the international version of Paramore's third studio album, "Brand New Eyes" (2009).
