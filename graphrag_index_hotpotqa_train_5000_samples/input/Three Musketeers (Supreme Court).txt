Three Musketeers (Supreme Court)

The "Three Musketeers" was the nickname given to three liberal members during the 1932–37 terms of the United States Supreme Court, who generally supported the New Deal agenda of President Franklin Delano Roosevelt.

 They were Justices Louis Brandeis, Benjamin N. Cardozo, and Harlan Fiske Stone.

 They were opposed by the Four Horsemen, consisting of Justices James Clark McReynolds, George Sutherland, Willis Van Devanter, and Pierce Butler.

 Chief Justice Charles Evans Hughes and Justice Owen J. Roberts controlled the balance.
