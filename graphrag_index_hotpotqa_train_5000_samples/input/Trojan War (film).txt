Trojan War (film)

Trojan War is a 1997 American romantic comedy film directed by George Huang.

 It stars Will Friedle, Jennifer Love Hewitt, and Marley Shelton.

 The film was a critical and box office disaster.

 Produced for $15 million, it made only $309 in ticket sales because it was played in a single movie theater and was pulled after only a week.
