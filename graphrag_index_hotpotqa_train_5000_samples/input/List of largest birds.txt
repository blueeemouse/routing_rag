List of largest birds

The largest living bird, a member of the Struthioniformes, is the ostrich ("Struthio camelus"), from the plains of Africa and Arabia.

 A large male ostrich can reach a height of 2.8 m and weigh over 156 kg .

 A mass of 200 kg has been cited for the ostrich but no wild ostriches of this massive weight have been verified.

 Eggs laid by the ostrich can weigh 1.4 kg and are the largest eggs in the world today.
