The Bellmores, New York

Bellmore is a hamlet and census-designated place (CDP) in Nassau County, New York, United States.

 The population according to the 2010 census was 16,218.

 Bellmore is located on the south shore of Long Island 5 miles from Jones Beach State Park, approximately 27 mi east of Manhattan, and 10 mi east of the Nassau-Queens (New York City) Line.
