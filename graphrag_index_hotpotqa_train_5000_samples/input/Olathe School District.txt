Olathe School District

The Olathe Unified School District (Kansas Unified School District 233) is one of the major school districts in the Kansas City Metropolitan Area and is one of the larger school districts in the state of Kansas.

 There are nearly 30,000 students enrolled in the district, which currently operates 5 high schools, 9 middle schools, and 34 elementary schools, as well as a number of additional educational and support facilities.
