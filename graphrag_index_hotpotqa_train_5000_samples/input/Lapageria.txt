Lapageria

Lapageria is a genus of flowering plants with only one known species, Lapageria rosea, commonly known as Chilean bellflower or copihue ("co-pee-way" < Mapudungun "kopiwe").

 "Lapageria rosea" is the national flower of Chile.

 It grows in forests in the southern part of Chile, being part of the Valdivian temperate rain forests flora.
