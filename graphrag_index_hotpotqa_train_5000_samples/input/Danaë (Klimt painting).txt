Danaë (Klimt painting)

Danaë is an oil painting by Gustav Klimt, created in 1907.

 An example of Symbolism, the canvas measures 77 x 83 cm, and is in the Galerie Würthle in Vienna.

 Danaë was a popular subject in the early 1900s for many artists; she was used as the quintessential symbol of divine love, and transcendence.
