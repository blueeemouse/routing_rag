Sincil Bank

Sincil Bank is a football stadium in Lincoln, England which has been the home of Lincoln City since 1895.

 Previously, Lincoln City had played at the nearby John O'Gaunts ground since the club's 1884 inception.

 Sincil Bank has an overall capacity of 10,120 and is colloquially known to fans as "Sinny Bank".

 It is overlooked by Lincoln Cathedral.

 Former Lincoln City chairman John Reames re-purchased the ground from the local council in 2000 at a cost of £175,000.

 The club had sold it in 1982 for £225,000 in order to fend off the threat of eviction, arranging a 125-year lease.
