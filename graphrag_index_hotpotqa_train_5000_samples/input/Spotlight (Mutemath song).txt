Spotlight (Mutemath song)

"Spotlight" is the first single from New Orleans rock group Mutemath's second album "Armistice".

 The song was first released on the soundtrack to the 2008 film adaptation of "Twilight", and it was the second single to be released from the album.

 The song was released digitally on "Spotlight EP" on February 10, 2009.

 The EP was also released on limited edition vinyl on March 24, 2009.
