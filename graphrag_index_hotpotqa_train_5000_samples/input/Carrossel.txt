Carrossel

Carrossel (Portuguese word for "Carousel") is a children's telenovela created by and written by Abel Santa Cruz.

 The TV show originally transmitted on SBT from May 21, 2012 to July 26, 2013.

 It involves a Brazilian remake of the Mexican telenovela Carrusel (which in turn had been inspired by the Argentinean telenovela "Jacinta Pichimahuida, la Maestra que no se Olvida").

 The Telenovela became successful particularly among the children, and led to several spin-offs including a cartoon series and television sitcom.
