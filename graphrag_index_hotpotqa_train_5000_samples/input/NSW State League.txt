NSW State League

The NSW State League Division 1 is a regional Australian semi-professional association football league comprising teams from New South Wales.

 The league sits at Level 4 on the New South Wales league system (Level 5 of the overall Australian league system).

 The competition is administered by Football NSW, the governing body of the sport in the southern region of the state (the northern region governed by Northern NSW Football).

 For the 2016 season it was announced that the State League 2 would become the NSW State League as State League 1 became NPL NSW 3
