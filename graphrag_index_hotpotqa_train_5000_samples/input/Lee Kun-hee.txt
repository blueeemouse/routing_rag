Lee Kun-hee

Lee Kun-hee (Hangul:  ; Hanja:  ; ] ; born January 9, 1942) is a South Korean business magnate and the chairman of Samsung Group.

 He resigned in April 2008, owing to a Samsung slush funds scandal, but returned on March 24, 2010.

 In 1996, Lee became a member of the International Olympic Committee.

 With an estimated net worth of $7.2 billion, he and his family rank among the Forbes richest people in the world.

 He is the third son of Samsung founder Lee Byung-chul.
