Tom Scott (businessman)

Tom Scott (born 1966) is an American entrepreneur best known as the CEO and co-founder of Nantucket Nectars, a beverage company Scott founded with Tom First in 1989.

 The company reached national prominence, appearing on the “Inc. 500” list of fastest growing U.S. companies five years in a row.

 In 2002, Scott and his partner sold Nantucket Nectars to Cadbury Schweppes.
