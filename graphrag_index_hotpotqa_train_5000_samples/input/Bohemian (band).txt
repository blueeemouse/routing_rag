Bohemian (band)

Bohemian (Korean: 보헤미안 , Japanese: ボヘミアン; stylized as BOHEMIAN), is a three-member South Korean pop/ballad group, that along with group endeavors, has recorded many soundtracks, abbreviated as OSTs, for South Korean films and Korean drama or k-drama for television in South Korea, as individual artists.

 The group is composed of (Korean: 박상우 ), (Korean: 김용진 ), and Yoo Kyu Sang (Korean: 유규상 ).

 Bohemian debuted in 2010 with members Park Sang Woo and Yoo Kyu Sang.

 In 2012, they were joined by Kim Yong Jin.

 The group is managed by HMG Entertainment, with recordings released by their distribution company, LOEN Entertainment.

 Through a LOEN Entertainment agreement with Viki, a video streaming site, both Kim Yong Jin and Bohemian are listed as artists, and their music videos can be viewed.
