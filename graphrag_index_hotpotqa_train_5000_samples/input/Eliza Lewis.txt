Eliza Lewis

Eliza Lewis (Hindi: एलिजा लुईस) is an Indian people voice actress, voice-dubbing artist and a director of Indian dubbed content and foreign dubbed content.

 She speaks English and Hindi as her mother tongue languages.

 She did her schooling from Bombay Scottish School and was a classmate and very good friend of the youngest Ashok Chokra awardee Neerja Bhanot
