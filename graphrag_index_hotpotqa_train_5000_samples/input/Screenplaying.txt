Screenplaying

Screenplaying is a compilation album by British singer-songwriter and guitarist Mark Knopfler, released on 9 November 1993 by Vertigo Records internationally and Warner Bros.

 Records in the United States.

 The album contains previously released tracks from Knopfler's soundtrack albums "Cal" (1984), "Last Exit to Brooklyn" (1989), "The Princess Bride" (1987), and "Local Hero" (1983).
