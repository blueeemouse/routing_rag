List of Morgan State University alumni

This is a list of notable alumni which includes graduates, non-graduate former students, and current students of Centenary Biblical Institute (1867–1890), Morgan College (1890–1938), Morgan State College (1938–1975), and Morgan State University (1975–present).

 Located in residential Baltimore, Maryland, Morgan State is a historically black university and Maryland's designated public urban university.

 The Morgan State University National Alumni Association is the official alumni organization of the university.
