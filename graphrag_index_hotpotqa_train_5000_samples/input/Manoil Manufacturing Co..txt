Manoil Manufacturing Co.

Manoil Manufacturing Company was an American metal and plastic toy company that began production in 1935 or 1936, and left the business in 1959.

 From June 1940 they were located on Providence Street, in Waverly, NY (Tioga County).

 Its prominence was from 1937-1941 when it produced hollowcast toy soldiers (sometimes called dimestore soldiers) along with toy airplanes and cars.
