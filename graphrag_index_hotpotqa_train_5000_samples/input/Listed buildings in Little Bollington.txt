Listed buildings in Little Bollington

Little Bollington is a civil parish in Cheshire East, England.

 It contains four buildings that are recorded in the National Heritage List for England as designated listed buildings, all of which are at Grade II.

 This grade is the lowest of the three gradings given to listed buildings and is applied to "buildings of national importance and special interest".

 Apart from the village of Little Bollington the parish is rural, and all the listed buildings are houses.
