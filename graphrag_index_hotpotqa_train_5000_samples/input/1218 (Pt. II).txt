1218 (Pt. II)

1218 (Part II) is the seventh studio album by Chicano rapper Lil Rob.

 The album was set to be released in 2006, advertisement posters were created stating April 2007, but it ended up just getting pushed back because songs kept being leaked.

 Lil Rob decided he needed to remake some songs so they would differ from leaked tracks.

 Release date by Upstairs Records advertisements state October 21, 2008.

 The album leaked onto the internet October 18, 2008.
