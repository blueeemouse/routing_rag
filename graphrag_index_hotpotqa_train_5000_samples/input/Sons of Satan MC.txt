Sons of Satan MC

Sons of Satan Motorcycle Club, known locally as The Sons, is an outlaw motorcycle club and support club for the Pagan's Motorcycle Club.

 It was formed in 1949 and incorpohrated in 1954 in Lancaster, Pennsylvania by returning World War II veterans.

 The club was eventually taken over by its namesake, John 'Satan' Marron, who later became the National President of the Pagan's Motorcycle Club.
