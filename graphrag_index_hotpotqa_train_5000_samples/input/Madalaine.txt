Madalaine

"Madalaine" the debut single by American rock band Winger, from their self-titled debut album "Winger".

 Released in 1988, the song reached #27 on the Mainstream rock "Billboard" charts.

 According to Kip Winger, it was one of the first four songs he and band mate Reb Beach wrote when they first got together, based on a handful of riffs Reb had from when he was younger.

 Kip figured out how best to arrange the riffs, enabling the pair to complete the song.
