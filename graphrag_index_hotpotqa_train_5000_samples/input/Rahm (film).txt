Rahm (film)

Rahm (also spelled 'Rehm') is a 2016 Pakistani drama-thriller Film, based on William Shakespeare's play "Measure for Measure".

 It is written and produced by Mahmood Jamal and its directed by Ahmed Jamal.

 Sanam Saeed, Sunil Shanker, Sajid Hasan and Nayyar Ejaz play lead roles in the film.

 The film's plot will be based on honesty, justice, love and unity.
