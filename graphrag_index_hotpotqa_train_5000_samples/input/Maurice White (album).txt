Maurice White (album)

Maurice White is a 1985 album by American singer and musician Maurice White released on Columbia Records.

 Released on April 18, 1985, The album went to number 12 on the Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums Charts.

 The single "Stand by Me" a cover of the Ben E. King classic reached number 6 on the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Singles & Tracks and number 11 on the Adult Contemporary charts.

 Filipino singer Gary Valenciano covered Maurice White's "Stand By Me" from his 2008 album "Rebirth".
