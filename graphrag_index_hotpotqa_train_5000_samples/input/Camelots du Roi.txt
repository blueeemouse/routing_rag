Camelots du Roi

The King's Camelots, officially the National Federation of the King's Camelots (French: "Fédération nationale des Camelots du Roi" ) was a far-right youth organization of the French militant royalist and integralist movement Action Française active from 1908 to 1936.

 It is best known for taking part in many right-wing demonstrations in France in the 1920s and 1930s.
