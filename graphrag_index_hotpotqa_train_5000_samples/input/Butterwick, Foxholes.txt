Butterwick, Foxholes

Butterwick is a village in the Ryedale district of North Yorkshire, England in the parish of Foxholes (1.75 miles), near the village of Weaverthorpe (2 miles).

 Butterwick is a small village and only contains about 13 to 15 houses.

 It has a church dedicated to St Nicholas, and several farming families.

 The closest school is Weaverthorpe Primary about 1 mile away.

 It is approximately 9 miles from Driffield, 10 miles from Bridlington, 15 miles from Scarborough and 35 miles from York.

 There is a small farm shop that sells almost everything.
