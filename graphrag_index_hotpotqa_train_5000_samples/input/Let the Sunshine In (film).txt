Let the Sunshine In (film)

Let the Sunshine In (French: Un beau soleil intérieur ), or Bright Sunshine In, is a 2017 French film directed by Claire Denis.

 The film is an adaptation of Roland Barthes's 1977 text "" (French: "Fragments d’un discours amoureux")"."

 Novelist Christine Angot and frequent Denis collaborator Jean-Pol Fargeau have both been reported as Denis's co-writers on the project.

 It opened the Directors' Fortnight section of the 2017 Cannes Film Festival.

 At Cannes in won the SACD Award.
