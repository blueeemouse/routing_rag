Maréchal, nous voilà !

"Maréchal, nous voilà !"

 ("Marshal, here we are!")

 is a French song dedicated to Marshal Philippe Pétain.

 Lyrics were composed by André Montagard (18881963), and music was attributed to André Montagnard and Charles Courtioux.

 The song was performed by André Dassary and on many official occasions in France and Algeria during Vichy France.
