Hansi Kürsch

Hans Jürgen Kürsch, better known as Hansi Kürsch (born 10 August 1966 in Lank-Latum/Meerbusch, Germany), is a German musician best known for being the co-founder, lead vocalist, co-composer, and lyricist of power metal band Blind Guardian.

 He was also the bassist in Blind Guardian until the 1998 album "Nightfall in Middle-Earth".

 He also provides lead vocals for the band Demons and Wizards.
