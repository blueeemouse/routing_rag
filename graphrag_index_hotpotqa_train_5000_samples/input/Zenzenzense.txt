Zenzenzense

"Zenzenzense" (Japanese: 前前前世 , "Past Past Past Life") is a song by Japanese rock band Radwimps.

 It was used as one of the four theme songs to the animated film "Your Name", and was one of 26 songs the band composed for the film's soundtrack.

 It reached number-one on the "Billboard" Japan Hot 100.

 It also received a digital download song certification of Triple Platinum from the Recording Industry Association of Japan for sales of 750,000.
