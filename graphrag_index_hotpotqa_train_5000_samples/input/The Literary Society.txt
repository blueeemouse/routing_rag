The Literary Society

The Literary Society is a London dining club, founded by William Wordsworth and others in 1807.

 Its members are generally either prominent figures in English literature or eminent people in other fields with a strong interest in literature.

 No papers are delivered at its meetings.

 It meets monthly at the Garrick Club. "

The Daily Telegraph's " online site called the club "Britain's most distinguished and discreet literary dining club".
