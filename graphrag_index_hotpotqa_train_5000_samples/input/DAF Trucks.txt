DAF Trucks

DAF Trucks NV is a Dutch truck manufacturing company and a division of Paccar.

 Its headquarters and main plant are in Eindhoven.

 Cabs and axle assemblies are produced at its Westerlo plant in Belgium.

 Some of the truck models sold with the DAF brand are designed and built by Leyland Trucks at their Farington plant in Leyland near Preston, England.
