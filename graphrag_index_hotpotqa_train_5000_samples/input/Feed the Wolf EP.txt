Feed the Wolf EP

Feed the Wolf is the seventh release and sixth EP by the Brisbane band The Red Paintings.

 It was released in Australia on 11 June 2007, and was funded entirely by fan donations and created without management or label.

 The album spawned the single "We Belong in the Sea".

 The album included two cover songs, "Sing" by The Dresden Dolls and "Mercy Seat" by Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds.
