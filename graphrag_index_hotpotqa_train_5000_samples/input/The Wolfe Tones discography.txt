The Wolfe Tones discography

The discography of the Wolfe Tones, an Irish folk and rebel group from the suburbs of Dublin, consists of sixteen studio albums, three extended plays, three live albums and ten compilation albums.

 The Wolfe Tones released their first album with Fontana Records in 1965 and released their most recent studio album with Shanachie Records in 2004.

 In the interim, the band has also released albums with Dolphin Records, Triskel Records and MCA.
