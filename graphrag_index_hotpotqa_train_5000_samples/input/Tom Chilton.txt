Tom Chilton

Thomas James "Tom" Chilton (born 15 March 1985 in Reigate) is a British racing driver.

 He has spent most of his career competing in touring car racing; he was a former factory Vauxhall and Honda driver in the British Touring Car Championship, and currently drives for Sébastien Loeb Racing in the World Touring Car Championship.

 His younger brother Max Chilton, who is also a racing driver, made his Formula One debut in and as of 2016, races in the American IndyCar Series for Chip Ganassi Racing.
