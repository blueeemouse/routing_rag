Julie Kedzie

Julie Kedzie (born March 18, 1981) is a retired American mixed martial artist.

 She is a third degree black belt in Tae Kwon Do and specializes in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.

 Kedzie was Greg Jackson's personal assistant at Jackson's Mixed Martial Arts.

 Kedzie has been working for Invicta Fighting Championships as a fight commentator and interviewer.

 In December 2013, Kedzie became the new matchmaker for Invicta FC.
