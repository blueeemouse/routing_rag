Rhythm, Country and Blues

Rhythm, Country and Blues is an album featuring duets between R&B and country music artists on classic songs.

 It was released by MCA Records on March 1, 1994.

 The album debuted at #1 on Top Country Albums and #15 on Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums charts.

 Marty Stuart's rendition of "The Weight" later appeared on his 1995 compilation "The Marty Party Hit Pack".
