NME Awards 2012

The NME Awards 2012 were held in London, England, on 29 February 2012, at the Brixton Academy and was hosted by English comedian Jack Whitehall.

 The nominations were announced exclusively on Zane Lowe's BBC Radio 1 show on 30 January 2012.

 Arctic Monkeys had seven nominations, followed by Kasabian, who went on to win best British Band, Lana Del Rey and Muse with three apiece.
