Eva May

Eva May (29 May 1902 – 10 September 1924) was an Austrian actress.

 She was the daughter of the film director Joe May and his wife Mia May.

 She was married three times on each occasion to a film director: Manfred Liebenau, Lothar Mendes and the final time to Manfred Noa.

 In 1924 she committed suicide by gunshot.
