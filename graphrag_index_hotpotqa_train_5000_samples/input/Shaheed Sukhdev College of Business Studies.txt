Shaheed Sukhdev College of Business Studies

Shaheed Sukhdev college of business studies is a constituent college of the University of Delhi, imparting education in the fields of management,finance and information technology.

 The college was established in 1987 by the Ministry of Human Resource Development as the first collegiate business college in India.The college was earlier an off campus college of Delhi University located in Vivek Vihar, but now has joined the list of the prestigious colleges of North Campus of Delhi University after shifting to its new campus at Rohini, North Delhi.

 Recently, the "Hindustan Times" survey placed the college in the top 5 colleges of North Campus( North Campus colleges are considered more prestigious in terms of •Academics •Faculty •Student knowledge & talent •Placements, than the South Campus colleges) of Delhi University.

 It is considered as the only college that gives a tough competition to SRCC and St Stephen's college of Delhi University.
