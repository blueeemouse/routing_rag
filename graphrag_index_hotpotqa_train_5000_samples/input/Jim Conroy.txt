Jim Conroy

James Conroy (born February 6, 1977) is an American voice actor, television writer and actor.

 He is known for appearing on television shows, such as "Celebrity Deathmatch", "Kenny the Shark" and "Fetch!

 with Ruff Ruffman", radio commercials and video games.

 He worked for companies such as WGBH, The Walt Disney Company and Discovery Channel.
