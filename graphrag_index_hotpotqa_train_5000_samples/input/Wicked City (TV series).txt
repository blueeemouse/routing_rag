Wicked City (TV series)

Wicked City is an American procedural drama television series created by Steven Baigelman for the broadcast network ABC.

 The series aired from October 27, 2015 to December 30, 2015, and focused on two LAPD detectives (Jeremy Sisto and Gabriel Luna) as they search for a pair of romantically-linked serial killers (Ed Westwick and Erika Christensen) terrorizing the Sunset Strip.

 The main cast also includes Taissa Farmiga, Karolina Wydra, Evan Ross, Anne Winters, and Jaime Ray Newman.
