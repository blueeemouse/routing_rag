List of Kenner Star Wars action figures

Kenner released more than 100 action figures in their "Star Wars" range between 1977 and 1985.

 The table below takes information as given on the cards with which each action figure debuted.

 After the debut of an action figure, details in the packaging, in the shape of the action figure and the name of the action figure were subject to change.

 No one characteristic (name, shape or product number) completely defines the list, rather all the characteristics taken together form a clear list.
