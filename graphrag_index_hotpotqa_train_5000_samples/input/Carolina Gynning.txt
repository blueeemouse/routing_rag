Carolina Gynning

Carolina Gynning Nilsson (born 6 October 1978) is a Swedish glamour model, actress, author, jewelry designer and television presenter.

 She started her career as a model at age sixteen.

 She entered the "Big Brother" house in 2004 and emerged as the winner of that season.

 Gynning has since made a career as a television presenter on TV4 and Kanal5, and also as an artist and author of several books.

 Gynning has participated in several television shows after "Big Brother" such as "I huvudet på Gynning", and as a celebrity dancer in season one of "Let's Dance".
