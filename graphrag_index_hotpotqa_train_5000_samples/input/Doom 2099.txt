Doom 2099

Doom 2099 (Victor Von Doom) is a fictional anti-hero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics.

 The character was primarily featured in the Marvel 2099 series "Doom 2099".

 The character is based on Doctor Doom, created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby.

 The series was written by John Francis Moore for its first two years, and by Warren Ellis for its third.
