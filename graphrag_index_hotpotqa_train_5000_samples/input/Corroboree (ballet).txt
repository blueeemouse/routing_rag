Corroboree (ballet)

Corroboree is a ballet written by Australian composer John Antill in the early 1940s.

 The first full version of the score was completed in 1944 and it was first performed as a concert suite in 1946.

 On 3 July 1950 it was performed as a ballet, at the Empire Theatre in Sydney, choreographed by Rex Reid, with dancers of the Melbourne-based National Theatre Ballet.
