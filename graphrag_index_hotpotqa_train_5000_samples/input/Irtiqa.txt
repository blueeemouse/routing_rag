Irtiqa

Irtiqa (Urdu: ارتقاء, literal English translation: "evolution") is the debut album by the Pakistani rock band, Entity Paradigm.

 It was originally slated for a 25 September 2003 release date, but was delayed to 1 October.

 Entity Paradigm released singles from "Irtiqa" which were "Hamesha," "Hamein Aazma", "Kahan Hai Tu", "Aghosh", "Waqt" and "Irtiqa III"; the videos for "Waqt" and "Irtiqa III" were directed by Xulfi himself.
