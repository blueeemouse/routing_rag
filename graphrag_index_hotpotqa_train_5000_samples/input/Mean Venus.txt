Mean Venus

Mean Venus is an American hard rock band from New Jersey.

 The quintet consists of 'J' (lead singer and songwriter), Adrian Freyre & Marcelo Cardosa (guitar), Russ Ippoliti (drums), and Chigger (bass).

 Formed in early 2006, Mean Venus has shared the stage with numerous national acts including 7Mary3, Skid Row, Extreme, Ratt, Dokken, L.A.Guns, Stryper, King's X, Rainbow, UFO, Sebastian Bach and others.

 In April 2008, Mean Venus headlined the Hawaiian Chopper Magazine's Motorhead Classic in Honolulu, Hawaii.

 They also performed at 2008's Clearwater Festival.

 Mean Venus songs have received radio airplay all over the country (WDHA, WZZO, WYSP, etc.)
