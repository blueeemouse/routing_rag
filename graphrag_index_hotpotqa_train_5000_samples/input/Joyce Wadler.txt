Joyce Wadler

Joyce Judith Wadler (born January 2, 1948) is a journalist and reporter for "The New York Times", as well as a writer and humorist.

 Prior to working at the "New York Times", she was a reporter and feature writer for the "New York Post", New York correspondent for "The Washington Post" and a contributing editor for "New York Magazine" and "Rolling Stone".

 She authored "Liaison: The True Story of the M. Butterfly Affair" (ISBN  ) after interviewing Bernard Boursicot, who granted her wide access to information and insight into his affair with Shi Pei Pu.
