Mi Mi Mi

"Mi Mi Mi" is a song by Russian girl group Serebro from their upcoming third studio album.

 It was released on 14 June 2013 in Russia as a digital download, while an accompanying music video premiered a few days earlier on 10 June 2013.

 Although not a commercial success worldwide, only peaking at number 11 in Italy, the song slowly gained popularity for its catchiness and summer atmosphere.

 The song is played in many clubs worldwide, as well as in movies and television shows, especially in South Korea.
