Maine Black Bears football

The Maine Black Bears football program is the intercollegiate American football team for the University of Maine located in the U.S. state of Maine.

 The team competes in the NCAA Division I Football Championship Subdivision (FCS) and are members of the Colonial Athletic Association.

 Maine's first football team was fielded in 1892.

 The team plays its home games at the 10,000 seat Alfond Stadium in Orono, Maine.
