Tabas-e Masina

Tabas-e Masina (Persian: طبس مسينا‎ ‎ , also Romanized as Ţabas-e Masīnā and Ţabas Masīnā; also known as Ţabas and Masīnā) is a city in Gazik District, Darmian County, in South Khorasan Province, Iran.

 At the 2006 census, where it had village status, its population was 3,776, in 779 families.

 It was promoted to city status following the 2006 census.
