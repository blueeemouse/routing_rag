Brooklyn–Battery Tunnel

The Brooklyn–Battery Tunnel – officially known as the Hugh L. Carey Tunnel, although this name is seldom used by locals – is a toll tunnel in New York City that connects Red Hook in Brooklyn with Battery Park City in Manhattan.

 The tunnel consists of twin tubes that each carry two traffic lanes under the mouth of the East River.

 Although it nearly passes underneath Governors Island, the tunnel does not provide vehicular access to the island.

 With a length of 9,117 feet (2,779 m), the Brooklyn–Battery Tunnel is the longest continuous underwater vehicular tunnel in North America.
