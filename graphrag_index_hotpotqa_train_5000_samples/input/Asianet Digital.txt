Asianet Digital

Asianet Satellite Communications Limited is the largest cable network services company in the Indian state of Kerala.

 Started in 1993, Asianet Satellite Communications Limited has grown in size and reach.

 Its cable network services operate from over 55 centers spread throughout Kerala and touch over half a million homes and establishments.

 Asianet Digital is a cable network service along with a set-top box which carries more than 270 Video channels.

 It includes seven exclusively-owned channels which are only cable-cast over its network.
