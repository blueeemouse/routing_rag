Myriad Pictures

Based in Santa Monica, California, Myriad Pictures is a leading independent entertainment company specializing in production, financing and worldwide distribution of feature films and television programming.

 President and CEO Kirk D’Amico opened the company in 1999 and has built a diverse library of art house and mainstream programming.

 Mr. D’Amico was named one of the Top 50 independent producers in the annual list published by The Hollywood Reporter, and is a member of the Board of Directors of IFTA.
