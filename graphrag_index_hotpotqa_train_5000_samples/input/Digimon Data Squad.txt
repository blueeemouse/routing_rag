Digimon Data Squad

Digimon Data Squad, known in Japan as Digimon Savers (デジモンセイバーズ , Dejimon Seibāzu ) , is a Japanese anime television series produced by Toei Animation, serving as the fifth series in the "Digimon" franchise, the first to be produced in four years following 2002's "Digimon Frontier".

 The series aired in Japan on Fuji TV between April 2, 2006 and March 25, 2007.

 An English-language version was produced by Studiopolis in conjunction with Toei Animation USA and Disney Enterprises and aired in North America on Jetix (and subsequently Disney XD) between October 1, 2007 and November 1, 2008.

 It aired in the United Kingdom on Kix!

.

 A movie based on the series was released in Japan on December 9, 2006.

 The series was later succeeded by "Digimon Fusion".
