DBridge

Darren White, better known as dBridge, is an English record producer whose career has stretched from the early days of the genre's conception back in the early 1990s.

 dBridge was originally part of the duo Future Forces Inc., a project made with fellow DJ Maldini, with releases on the first Renegade Hardware records.

 Later, they would join Fresh and Vegas to form the drum and bass super-group Bad Company, which dBridge was a member of during the late 1990s and early 2000s.

 During his time with Bad Company, they released several popular and successful tracks such as Planet Dust, Nitrous, and The Nine.
