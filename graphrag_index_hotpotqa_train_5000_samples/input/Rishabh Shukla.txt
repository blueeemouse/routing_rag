Rishabh Shukla

Rishabh Shukla (Hindi: ऋषभ शुक्ला "R̥ṣabha Śuklā") (born 20 August 1966) is an Indian film and television actor and a voice-dubbing artist, who speaks Hindi as his mother tongue language.

 On television he is most known for his role of King Shantanu in "Mahabharat" (TV series) (1988–1990) and as a Hindi dubbing actor he is most known as the voice of Lord Voldemort in Harry Potter series.
