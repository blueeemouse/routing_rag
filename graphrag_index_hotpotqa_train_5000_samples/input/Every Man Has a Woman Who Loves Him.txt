Every Man Has a Woman Who Loves Him

"Every Man Has a Woman Who Loves Him" is a Yoko Ono song from her "Double Fantasy" album with John Lennon.

 The version released as a single, and on the compilation album "Every Man Has a Woman", is credited to Lennon, and has stripped away Ono's lead vocal, while bringing Lennon's backing vocal up in the mix (this version was also released as a bonus track on the CD reissue of "Milk and Honey").

 The B-side featured "It's Alright" by Ono and Sean Lennon.

 The last Lennon song to be released as a single, it failed to chart in either the United Kingdom or the United States.
