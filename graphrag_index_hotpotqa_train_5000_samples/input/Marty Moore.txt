Marty Moore

Marty Moore (born March 19, 1971) is a former professional American football player who played linebacker for eight seasons for the New England Patriots and Cleveland Browns.

 Moore was the first Mr. Irrelevant ever to play in a Super Bowl (Super Bowl XXXI).

 Moore played high school football for Highlands High in Ft. Thomas and college football at Kentucky, playing in the 1993 Peach Bowl and becoming the final pick in the 1994 NFL Draft after his senior season.
