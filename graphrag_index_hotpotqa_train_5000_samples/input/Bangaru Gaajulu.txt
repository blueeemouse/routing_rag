Bangaru Gaajulu

Bangaru Gaajulu (Telugu: బంగారు గాజులు ; English: Golden Bangles) is a 1968 Telugu drama film produced by Tammareddy Krishna Murthy on Ravindra Art Pictures banner and directed by C. S. Rao.

 Starring Akkineni Nageswara Rao, Bharathi, Vijaya Nirmala in the lead roles and music composed by T. Chalapathi Rao.
