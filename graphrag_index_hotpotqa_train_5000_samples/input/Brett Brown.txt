Brett Brown

Brett William Brown (born February 16, 1961) is an American professional basketball coach who is currently the head coach of the Philadelphia 76ers of the NBA.

 Brown is a former college basketball player who previously served as an assistant coach for the San Antonio Spurs.

 Brown also has extensive experience coaching in Australia previously serving as the head coach of the North Melbourne Giants and Sydney Kings of the NBL and the Australia men's national basketball team.
