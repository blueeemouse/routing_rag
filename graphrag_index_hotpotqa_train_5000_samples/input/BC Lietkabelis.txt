BC Lietkabelis

BC Lietkabelis (Lithuanian: "Krepšinio klubas Lietkabelis" ) is a Lithuanian professional basketball team based in Panevėžys, Lithuania, participating in the Lithuanian Basketball League and the Baltic Basketball League.

 BC Techasas had many financial problems at the 2011–2012 season start, they even boycotted a LKF Cup game.

 Because of that Darius Gaudiešius sold the basketball club to Algirdas Kriščiūnas, Antanas Kazys Liorentas and Kazimieras Antanynas.

 Due to the club owner changes, club got back his legendary name Lietkabelis.
