Two Steps Behind

"Two Steps Behind" is a 1993 song by British hard rock band Def Leppard from their album "Retro Active" and Last Action Hero Soundtrack.

 It reached #5 on the Billboard Hot Mainstream Rock Tracks chart, and #12 on the "Billboard" Hot 100.

 In the 1993 Metal Edge Readers' Choice Awards, the song was voted "Song of the Year" and "Best Song From a Movie Soundtrack."
