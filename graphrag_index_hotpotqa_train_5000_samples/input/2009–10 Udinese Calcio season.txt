2009–10 Udinese Calcio season

The 2009–10 Udinese Calcio season was the club's 15th consecutive and 30th overall season in Serie A.

 The team competed in Serie A, finishing 15th, and in the Coppa Italia, reaching the semi-finals.

 The highlight of Udinese's season was captain Antonio Di Natale's excellent campaign, as he finished top scorer in Serie A, or "capocannoniere", with 29 goals.
