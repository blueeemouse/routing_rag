2014 Kansas Jayhawks football team

The 2014 Kansas Jayhawks football team represented the University of Kansas in the 2014 NCAA Division I FBS football season.

 The Jayhawks were led by head coach Charlie Weis for the first four games of the season and interim head coach Clint Bowen for the remainder of the season.

 The team played their home games at Memorial Stadium.

 They were a member of the Big 12 Conference.

 They finished the season 3–9, 1–8 in Big 12 play to finish in ninth place.
