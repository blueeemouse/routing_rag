Brown's Hotel (Catskills)

Brown’s Hotel was a nationally known resort complex located in the Borscht Belt area of upstate New York, in the Catskill Mountains.

 It was one of the largest and most elaborate establishments of its kind during an era when the entire region prospered as a tourist destination.

 From the 1940s to the 1980s, the hotel was a popular vacation destination for many upper-middle-class families living in the New York City metropolitan area.

 Jewish-American families were welcomed and even catered to specifically by the hotels in the Borscht Belt during a time period when anti-semitism was prevalent in the hospitality industry.

 Filling a niche, the area quickly became a mecca for Jewish-American families.

 Brown's Hotel was located in the hamlet of Loch Sheldrake in the Town of Fallsburg, Sullivan County, New York.
