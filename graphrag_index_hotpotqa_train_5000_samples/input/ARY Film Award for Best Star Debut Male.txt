ARY Film Award for Best Star Debut Male

ARY Film Award for Best Star Debut Male is one of the ARY Film Awards of Merit presented annually by the ARY Digital Network and Entertainment Channel to recognize the male actor who has delivered an outstanding debut performance while working in the film industry.

 Since its inception, however, the award has commonly been referred to as the AFA for Best Star Debut Male.

 While actors are nominated for this award by AFA members who are actors and actresses themselves, winners are selected by the AFA membership as a whole.
