Heaven (Inna song)

"Heaven" is a song recorded by Romanian recording artist Inna.

 It was released for digital download on 10 June 2016 through Roton.

 The recording was written by Andreas Schuller, Leroy Clampitt, Sebastian Barac, Marcel Botezan, Laila Samulesen, Trey Campbell and Theea Eliza Miculescu, while production was handled by Barac and Botezan.

 A dance-pop and tropical house recording, the single was generally acclaimed by music critics, who praised it as an earworm with Middle Eastern influences.

 While reviews noted lyrics in English, French, and Spanish, the singer explained that the refrain was written in a language she and her label invented.
