2017 U.S. Men's Clay Court Championships

The 2017 U.S. Men's Clay Court Championships (also known as the Fayez Sarofim & Co.

 U.S. Men's Clay Court Championships for sponsorship purposes) was a tennis tournament played on outdoor clay courts.

 It was 49th edition of the U.S. Men's Clay Court Championships, and an ATP World Tour 250 event on the 2017 ATP World Tour.

 It took place at River Oaks Country Club in Houston, Texas, United States, from April 10 through April 16, 2017.
