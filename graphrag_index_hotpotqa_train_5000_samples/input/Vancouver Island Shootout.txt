Vancouver Island Shootout

The Vancouver Island Shootout was an annual curling bonspiel started in 2011 as part of the World Curling Tour.

 For the 2011–12 curling season, the event took place at the Juan de Fuca Curling Club in Colwood, British Columbia.

 The men's event was held in a triple knockout format, and the women's event was held in a round robin format.

 For the 2012–13 curling season and 2014–15 curling season, the event took place at the Victoria Curling Centre in Victoria, British Columbia, and both the men's and women's events were held in a triple knockout format.
