Rehab (Quiet Riot album)

Rehab is the eleventh studio album released from the heavy metal band Quiet Riot in 2006.

 It is their first studio release since 2001's "Guilty Pleasures".

 This is the last Quiet Riot studio album to feature lead singer Kevin DuBrow before his death in November 2007, which caused the band to split, though they would eventually reform in 2010 and continue to record and tour.
