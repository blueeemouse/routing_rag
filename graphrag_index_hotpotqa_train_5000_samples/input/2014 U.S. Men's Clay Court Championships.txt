2014 U.S. Men's Clay Court Championships

The 2014 U.S. Men's Clay Court Championships (also known as the Fayez Sarofim & Co.

 U.S. Men's Clay Court Championships for sponsorship purposes) is a tennis tournament played on outdoor clay courts.

 It is part of the 2014 ATP World Tour.

 It is the 46th edition of the U.S. Men's Clay Court Championships, and an ATP World Tour 250 event.

 It will take place at River Oaks Country Club in Houston, Texas, United States, from April 7 through April 13, 2014.
