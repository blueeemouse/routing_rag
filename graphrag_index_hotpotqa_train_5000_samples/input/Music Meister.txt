Music Meister

The Music Meister is a fictional character created by Mike Jelenic, appearing in "Mayhem of the Music Meister!

", an episode of the "" animated series, voiced by Neil Patrick Harris.

 The character later appeared in the comic book sequel to the series, of the same name, published by DC Comics.

 A separate character also known as the Music Meister was later developed for the live-action Arrowverse family of shows on The CW, portrayed by Darren Criss in "Supergirl" and "The Flash", adapted from the original version.
