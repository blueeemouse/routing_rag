64th Berlin International Film Festival

The 64th annual Berlin International Film Festival was held from 6 to 16 February 2014.

 Wes Anderson's film "The Grand Budapest Hotel" opened the festival.

 British film director Ken Loach was presented with the Honorary Golden Bear.

 The Golden Bear was awarded to the Chinese film "Black Coal, Thin Ice" directed by Diao Yinan, which also served as closing film.
