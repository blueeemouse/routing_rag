Weekend Edition

Weekend Edition is the name of a set of American radio news magazine programs produced and distributed by National Public Radio (NPR).

 It's the weekend counterpart to the NPR radio program "Morning Edition".

 It consists of "Weekend Edition Saturday" and "Weekend Edition Sunday", each of which airs for two hours, from 8:00 AM to 10:00 AM Eastern time, with refeeds until 2:00 PM.

 "Weekend Edition Saturday" is hosted by Scott Simon, while "Weekend Edition Sunday" has been hosted by Lulu Garcia-Navarro since January 8, 2017.
