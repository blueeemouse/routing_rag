Want Ads

"Want Ads" was a million-selling Number 1 pop and R&B hit recorded by female group, Honey Cone for their third album "Sweet Replies" and also appears on their fourth album "Soulful Tapestry".

 The song on the Detroit-based Hot Wax label was written by Greg Perry, General Norman Johnson and Barney Perkins.

 It was produced by staff producer, Greg Perry, and features a young Ray Parker, Jr. ("Ghostbusters") on rhythm guitar.
