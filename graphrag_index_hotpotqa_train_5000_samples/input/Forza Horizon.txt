Forza Horizon

Forza Horizon is a racing video game for Microsoft's Xbox 360 video game console.

 Developed principally by British games developer Playground Games in association with American developer Turn 10 Studios, the game is the fifth instalment in the "Forza" series, though it was considered more of a spin-off instead of the next true member of the series at the time.

 A sequel, "Forza Horizon 2", was released on 30 September 2014.

 Another instalment, "Forza Horizon 3", was released on 27 September 2016.
