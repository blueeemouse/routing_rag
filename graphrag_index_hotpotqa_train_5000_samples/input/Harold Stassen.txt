Harold Stassen

Harold Edward Stassen (April 13, 1907 – March 4, 2001) was the 25th Governor of Minnesota.

 He was a leading candidate for the Republican nomination for President of the United States in 1948, considered for a time to be the front-runner.

 He thereafter regularly continued to run for that and other offices, such that his name became most identified with his status as a perennial candidate.
