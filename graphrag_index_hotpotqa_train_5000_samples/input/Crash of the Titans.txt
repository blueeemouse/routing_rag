Crash of the Titans

Crash of the Titans is a platform game published by Sierra Entertainment and developed by Vancouver-based Radical Entertainment for the PlayStation 2, PlayStation Portable (ported by SuperVillain Studios), Wii and Xbox 360.

 The Game Boy Advance and Nintendo DS versions of the game were developed by Amaze Entertainment.

 The game was first released in North America on October 3, 2007, in Europe on October 12, 2007 and in Australia on October 25, 2007.

 It is the first game in the "Crash Bandicoot" series not to have a Japanese release.
