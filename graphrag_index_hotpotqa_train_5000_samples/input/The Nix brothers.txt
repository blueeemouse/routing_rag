The Nix brothers

Evan Nix (born December 10, 1983) and Adam Nix (born October 11, 1986), known together professionally as the Nix Bros., are American film directors, producers, and musicians.

 They are most well known for filming the series "The Grawlix" on Funny Or Die, a weekly comedy show featuring regular appearances by comedians Adam Cayton-Holland, Ben Roy, and Andrew Orvedahl, and for directing an episode of Paul Feig's 2015 comedy series "Other Space".

 The two are also known as the founding members of the synthpop comedy band Total Ghost, a group for which they won best music video at the Festivus film festival in Denver, Colorado.

 The Nix Bros. later directed the Festivus spinoff Laugh Track Comedy Festival in 2011 and 2012.
