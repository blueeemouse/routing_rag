In Your Wildest Dreams (song)

"In Your Wildest Dreams" is a duet released by Tina Turner and Barry White in 1996, from Turner's album "Wildest Dreams".

 The single's largest success was in Austria where it peaked at #2.

 The original European album version featured spoken vocals by actor Antonio Banderas, while for the single version and U.S. edition of the "Wildest Dreams" album, Turner re-recorded the track with White.

 "In Your Wildest Dreams", released in December 1996, was the seventh and final single release from the album and was issued in a wide range of formats and versions; the promo 12" singles featured remixes by, among others, Deep Dish and Pink Noise.

 Certain European CD singles also included two live recordings from the "Wildest Dreams Tour", "Missing You" and "GoldenEye".
