Big Time Christmas

Big Time Christmas is a 2010 teen made-for-television film based on the Nickelodeon TV series "Big Time Rush".

 It has also been broadcast divided in two parts that serve as the third film of the series film franchise of "Big Time Rush" and the season two eighth and ninth episodes of the series.

 It stars Kendall Schmidt, James Maslow, Carlos Pena Jr. and Logan Henderson as Big Time Rush and also Miranda Cosgrove, Snoop Dogg, Tanya Chisholm and Stephen Kramer Glickman.

 It aired December 4, 2010.
