Ashbourne railway station

Ashbourne railway station formerly served the town of Ashbourne in Derbyshire.

 There have been two stations in the town.

 The first, opened in 1852, was operated by the North Staffordshire Railway (NSR); it was replaced in 1899 by a station at a new location, jointly operated by the NSR and the London and North Western Railway (LNWR).

 In 1923 the station passed into the ownership of the London, Midland and Scottish Railway and in 1948 that of the London Midland Region of British Railways.

 It was finally closed to all traffic in 1963 (regular passenger services having been withdrawn in 1954).
