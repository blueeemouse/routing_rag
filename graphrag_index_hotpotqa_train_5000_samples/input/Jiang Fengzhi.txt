Jiang Fengzhi

Jiang Fengzhi (蒋风之, 1908—1986) was an erhu artist from Yixing, Jiangsu Province, China.

 His career of playing the erhu can be divided into four periods.

 In the first period, he was educated by Wang Laosi (simplified Chinese: 王老四) from 1920 to 1921.

 The second is learning from Chu Shizhu (simplified Chinese: 储师竹, also a Chinese erhu artist).

 The third, he asked Wu Bochao for help later in 1927 after school.

 The fourth, he became a student of Liu Tianhua (simplified Chinese: 刘天华, a national musical instrument composer) officially from 1929 to 1932.

 And his concept is that: for study, the first is working hard, the second is being creative.
