Fabian Cowdrey

Fabian Kruuse Cowdrey (born 30 January 1993) is former English professional cricketer who played for Kent County Cricket Club.

 He made history by becoming the first third generation player to play for the county, following his father, Chris Cowdrey, and grandfather Colin Cowdrey.

 Cowdrey was often employed as an all-rounder, batting right-handed and bowling slow left arm orthodox deliveries.
