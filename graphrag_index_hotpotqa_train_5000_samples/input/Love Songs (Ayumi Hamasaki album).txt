Love Songs (Ayumi Hamasaki album)

Love Songs (stylized as Love songs) is the twelfth studio album by Japanese singer-songwriter Ayumi Hamasaki.

 The album was released on December 22, 2010 through Avex Trax, eight months after her eleventh studio album "Rock 'n' Roll Circus".

 A stylistic return to the aesthetic of her albums before "Secret", "Love Songs" contains pop songs with lyrics primarily revolving around love, many of them ballads.
