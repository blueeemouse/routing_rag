Hustler's Ambition

"Hustler's Ambition" is a song by American rapper 50 Cent.

 Written by 50 Cent and produced by B-Money "B$", the song was released as the first single from the soundtrack to the film "Get Rich or Die Tryin' " (2005).

 Built around a soul–influenced production sampling the Frankie Beverly and Maze song "I Need You", "Hustler's Ambition" features lyrics regarding 50 Cent's rise to fortune and fame, intended to mirror the experience faced by 50 Cent's character in the film: it marks a shift from influence of hardcore hip hop present in 50 Cent's earlier work.

 "Hustler's Ambition" was released to digital retailers in the United States in October 2005 via Interscope Records, with a CD release following in February 2006.
