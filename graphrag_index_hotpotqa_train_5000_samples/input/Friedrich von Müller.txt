Friedrich von Müller

Friedrich von Müller (17 September 1858, Augsburg – 18 November 1941, Munich) was a German physician remembered for describing Müller's sign.

 He was the son of the head of the medical department in the hospital in Augsburg.

 He studied natural sciences in Munich and medicine in Munich, under Carl von Voit, and Würzburg.

 He was awarded his doctorate in Munich in 1882, and became assistant to Carl Jakob Adolf Christian Gerhardt in Würzburg and later Berlin.

 He was habilitated in internal medicine in 1888 and became professor of clinical propaedeutics and laryngology in Bonn.

 He moved to Breslau in 1890, Marburg in 1892, and Basel in 1899, before returning to Munich in 1902.
