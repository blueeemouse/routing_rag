Avolites

Avolites Ltd is a British manufacturing company based in Park Royal, London.

 Avolites manufactures lighting control consoles and stage dimming equipment for use in the entertainments lighting industry.

 In 2011 the company expanded into the media server sector of the entertainments technology market by acquiring software developed by Immersive Ltd, supplying control systems used in the 2012 Olympics opening ceremony, and the 2014 Winter Sochi Olympics ceremonies.
