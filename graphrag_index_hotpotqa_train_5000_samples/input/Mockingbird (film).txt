Mockingbird (film)

Mockingbird is a 2014 American found footage horror film that was written and directed by Bryan Bertino.

 The film was released to video on demand on October 7, 2014 and was given a DVD and Blu-ray release on October 21 of the same year.

 It stars Todd Stashwick, Alexandra Lydon, and Barak Hardley as three people that have been given video cameras with the instructions to film their daily activities for a strange contest.
