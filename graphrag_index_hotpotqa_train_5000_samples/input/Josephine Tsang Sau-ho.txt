Josephine Tsang Sau-ho

Josephine Tsang Sau-ho ( is an elected member of the Hong Kong District Council (Islands District), representing the Peng Chau & Hei Ling Chau constituency.

 Tsang first ran for the District Council after the then incumbent, Mr On Hing-Ying, died on 18 April 2014, while in office.

 During the subsequent by-election, Ms Tsang secured 55.7% of the vote and was duly elected on 7 September, 2014.

 Regular District Council elections were held the following year during which Ms Tsang ran uncontested.
