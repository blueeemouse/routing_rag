UFC Fight Night: Thomas vs. Florian

UFC Fight Night: Thomas vs. Florian (also known as UFC Fight Night 11) was a mixed martial arts (MMA) event held by the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) on Wednesday, September 19, 2007 at the Palms Casino Resort in Las Vegas, Nevada.

 It was broadcast live in the United States and Canada on Spike TV as a lead-in to the debut of "The Ultimate Fighter 6".
