WJAR

WJAR (more commonly known as NBC 10) is the NBC-affiliated television station for the state of Rhode Island and Bristol County, Massachusetts licensed to Providence.

 It broadcasts a high definition digital signal on UHF channel 50 from a transmitter in Rehoboth, Massachusetts.

 Owned by Sinclair Broadcast Group, the station shares studios on Kenney Drive in Cranston with ZGS Communications-owned Telemundo affiliate WRIW-CD.
