Mark Hittner

Mark Hittner is an American football official in the National Football League (NFL) since the beginning of the 1997 NFL season.

 He works as a head linesman and wears the uniform number 28.

 He is most notable for officiating in the last three of five Super Bowls, most recently in Super Bowl XL on February 5, 2006 between the Seattle Seahawks and Pittsburgh Steelers.
