Iqaluit Airport

Iqaluit Airport (IATA: YFB, ICAO: CYFB) serves Iqaluit, Nunavut, Canada and is located adjacent to the town.

 It is operated by the government of Nunavut.

 It hosts scheduled passenger service from Ottawa, Montreal, Rankin Inlet and Kuujjuaq on carriers such as First Air and Canadian North, and from smaller communities throughout eastern Nunavut.

 It is also used as a forward operating base by the CF-18 Hornet.

 In 2011, the terminal handled more than 120,000 passengers.
