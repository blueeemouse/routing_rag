Leben… I Feel You

"Leben… I Feel You" is the second single from the 2003 Schiller album "Leben" with vocals by Peter Heppner.

 The song was officially released on 5 January 2004 and peaked at number 15 in the German singles chart and at number 1 in the Romanian singles chart in 2004.

 It is the second co-operation between Schiller and the German singer Heppner after the song "Dream of You".

 The single and music video version differs slightly from the album version; the video version has a length of 3:49 minutes and the album version has a length of 5:35 minutes.

 The single includes the song ″Vielklang″, which was no released on any album.
