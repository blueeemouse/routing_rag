Beromünster

Beromünster is a municipality in the district of Sursee in the canton of Lucerne in Switzerland.

 On 1 January 2004 the former municipality of Schwarzenbach merged into the municipality of Beromünster.

 On 1 January 2009 the former municipality of Gunzwil merged into the municipality of Beromünster followed by Neudorf on 1 January 2013.
