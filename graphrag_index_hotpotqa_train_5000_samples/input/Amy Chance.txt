Amy Chance

Amy Chance (born July 21, 1975) is a former American child actor born in Hollywood, California.

 Amy played Aphrodesia, one of the first characters introduced in the cult television show "Buffy the Vampire Slayer", a minor role which she reprised from the original unaired Buffy pilot.

 She had numerous guest starring roles on popular shows such as "Punky Brewster", "ER" and "Step By Step" and appeared in several television commercials.
