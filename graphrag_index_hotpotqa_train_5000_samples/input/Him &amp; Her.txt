Him &amp; Her

Him & Her is a British television sitcom about a lazy twenty-something couple: Steve and Becky, who live in Walthamstow, London.

 It was first broadcast in the United Kingdom on BBC Three on 6 September 2010.

 It is written by Stefan Golaszewski and stars Russell Tovey and Sarah Solemani.

 The theme tune is the song "Boom Bang-a-Bang" by Lulu.
