The SeeYa

The SeeYa (Korean: 더 씨야 ; stylized as "The SEEYA") was a South Korean R&B girl group formed by MBK Entertainment (formerly known as Core Contents Media) in 2012.

 The group was a reboot of original girl group SeeYa.

 The group consisted of Minkyung, Youngjoo, Yoojin and Yeonkyung.

 They debuted on November 12, 2012 with their first single album "Good To Seeya" featuring the title track "Be With You".

 They had their debut stage on November 15, 2012 at M!

 Countdown.

 In late 2015, MBK Entertainment removed their profile page from the official website and it was reported that the group was disbanded.
