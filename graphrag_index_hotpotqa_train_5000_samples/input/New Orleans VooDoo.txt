New Orleans VooDoo

The New Orleans VooDoo were a professional arena football team based in New Orleans, Louisiana.

 The VooDoo was a member of the East Division of the American Conference of the Arena Football League (AFL).

 They played their home games in Smoothie King Center.

 The VooDoo were unrelated to an earlier AFL team, the New Orleans Night, who had competed in the 1991 and 1992 AFL seasons in the Louisiana Superdome.
