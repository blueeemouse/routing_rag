Live at Planet Rock Xmas Party

"Live at Planet Rock Xmas Party" is the first of a planned series of 'official bootlegs' by Northern Irish rock band The Answer.

 It was recorded live at the Islington Academy in London on 11 December 2007 for the radio station Planet Rock.

 During the concert Paul Rodgers joined the band on stage to sing two songs, "I'm a Mover" and "The Hunter", from his former band Free's debut album "Tons of Sobs" with the group.
