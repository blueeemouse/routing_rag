Jagged Alliance 2

Jagged Alliance 2 is a tactical role-playing game for PC, released in 1999 for Windows, and later ported to Linux by Tribsoft.

 It is the third game in the "Jagged Alliance" series, and was followed by the expansion "Unfinished Business" in 2000.

 Two commercial releases of the mod "Wildfire" (2004) are also available as expansion packs.

 "Jagged Alliance 2" and the "Unfinished Business" expansion were combined in the "Jagged Alliance 2 Gold Pack", released on August 6, 2002.
