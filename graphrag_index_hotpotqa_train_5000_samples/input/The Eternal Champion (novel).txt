The Eternal Champion (novel)

The Eternal Champion is a fantasy novel by Michael Moorcock.

 First published in 1970, it is based on stories Moorcock published in "Avillion" and "Science Fantasy".

 It is the first in a trilogy of books about the Eternal Champion in his incarnation as Erekosë.

 The sequels are "Phoenix in Obsidian" (1970), also published as "The Silver Warriors", and "The Dragon in the Sword" (1987).
