Steven Lowy

Steven Lowy {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (born 24 November 1962) is co-Chief Executive Officer of Westfield Corporation, a leading global shopping centre company with 35 shopping centres under management valued at US$29.3 billion.

 He also serves on the board of Scentre Group, the premier Australian shopping centre company, which has 40 shopping centres under management valued at A$43.3 billion.

 In November 2015 he was elected Chairman of Football Federation Australia.
