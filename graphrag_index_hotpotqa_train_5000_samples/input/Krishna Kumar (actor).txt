Krishna Kumar (actor)

Krishna Kumar is an Indian film actor who has appeared in Malayalam and Tamil in supporting roles as well in many TV serials.

 He is from Kerala.

 He was a TV news reader and later he started his acting with the 1994 Malayalam film "Kashmeeram".

 After that he had acted several Malayalam films and Malayalam TV serials.

 When he faced less opportunities in Malayalam, he tried his luck in Tamil by acting in some TV serials.

 It helped him to get some good roles in Tamil films such as Billa II, Deivathirumagal, Mugamoodi etc. as well as in some Malayalam movies.
