Eddy Silvestre Pascual Israfilov

Eddy Silvestre Pascual Israfilov (born 2 August 1992), known as Eddy Silvestre, Eddi İsrafilov or simply Eddy, is an Azerbaijani footballer who plays for Spanish club Gimnàstic de Tarragona as an attacking midfielder.

 Born in Spain, he made his debut for Azerbaijan in 2015.
