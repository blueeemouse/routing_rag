Hemel Hempstead Swimming Club

Hemel Hempstead Swimming Club is a swimming club based in Hemel Hempstead, Hertfordshire, United Kingdom and was established in 1913.

 Hemel Hempstead Swimming Club (HHSC) ranks as one of Hertfordshire's premier clubs.

 The Club has in excess of 220 members from age 5 to Masters Level and their swimmers compete regularly in a wide range of competitions.
