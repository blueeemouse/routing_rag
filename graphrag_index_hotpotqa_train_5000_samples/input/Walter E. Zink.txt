Walter E. Zink

Walter E. Zink II is a major general in the United States Army.

 He is the current commander of Operational Command Post (OCP) 1, United States Army North.

 He commands the OCP as a task force in response to consequence management operations.

 He also commands a Joint Task Force, leading and coordinating air, ground, and naval forces in homeland defense and civil support operations.

 He previously served as the deputy commanding general of the Fifth US Army.
