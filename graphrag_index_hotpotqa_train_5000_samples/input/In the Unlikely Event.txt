In the Unlikely Event

In the Unlikely Event is the fourth full-length album by American progressive rock band The Fall of Troy, that was released on October 6, 2009.

 The album was recorded with producer Terry Date and is the only full-length album to feature bassist Frank Ene (first featured on "Phantom on the Horizon").

 It was recorded at Studio Lithio.

 The album featured a slightly less aggressive style compared to the band's earlier work, in turn facing mixed critical reception.
