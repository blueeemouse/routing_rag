Mage Knight

Mage Knight is a miniatures wargame using collectible figures, created by WizKids, Inc, and is the earliest example of what is now known as a Collectible Miniatures Game.

 The game was designed by founder Jordan Weisman along with Kevin Barrett.

 The game is the first to use WizKids' Clix system, combining roleplaying and wargaming elements with aspects of collectible card games.

 "Mage Knight" achieved success after it was introduced in 2000.

 WizKids announced in October 2010 that "Mage Knight" was being relaunched with a board game, card game, and role-playing game.
