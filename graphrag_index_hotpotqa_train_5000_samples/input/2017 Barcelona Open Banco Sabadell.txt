2017 Barcelona Open Banco Sabadell

The 2017 Barcelona Open Banco Sabadell (also known as the "Torneo Godó") was a men's tennis tournament played on outdoor clay courts.

 It was the 65th edition of the event and part of the ATP World Tour 500 series of the 2017 ATP World Tour.

 It took place at the Real Club de Tenis Barcelona in Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain, from April 24 through April 30, 2017.
