Caroline Bynum

Caroline Walker Bynum, FBA (born in Atlanta, Georgia, in 1941) is an American Medieval scholar.

 She is a University Professor emerita at Columbia University and Professor emerita of Western Medieval History at the Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton, New Jersey.

 She was the first woman to be appointed University Professor at Columbia.

 She is a former Dean of Columbia's School of General Studies, and served as President of the American Historical Association in 1996.
