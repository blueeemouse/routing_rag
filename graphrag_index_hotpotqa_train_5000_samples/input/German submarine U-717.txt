German submarine U-717

German submarine "U-717" was a Type VIIC U-boat of Nazi Germany's "Kriegsmarine" during World War II.

 The submarine was laid down on 24 April 1942 at the H. C. Stülcken Sohn yard at Hamburg, launched on 20 February 1943, and commissioned on 19 May 1943 under the command of "Oberleutnant zur See" Siegfried von Rothkirch und Panthen.

 Attached to "5th U-boat Flotilla" based at Kiel, "U-717" completed her training period on 31 July 1944 and was assigned to front-line service.
