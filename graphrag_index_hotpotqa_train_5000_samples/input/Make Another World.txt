Make Another World

Make Another World is the fifth full-length studio album by the Scottish rock band Idlewild, released on 5 March 2007 through Sequel Records.

 The album is the first with bass guitarist Gareth Russell, following Gavin Fox's departure at the end of 2005, and is their first since leaving Parlophone.

 Vocalist Roddy Woomble said that the band "made a conscious decision to make a strong, loud, rock record."
