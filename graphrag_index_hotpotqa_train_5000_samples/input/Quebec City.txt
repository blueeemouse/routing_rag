Quebec City

Quebec City (pronounced or ; French: "Québec" ] ); French: "Ville de Québec" ), officially Québec, is the capital city of the Canadian province of Quebec.

 The city had a population estimate of 531,902 on July 2016, (an increase of 3.0% from 2011) and the metropolitan area had a population of 800,296 on July 2016, (an increase of 4.3% from 2011) making it Canada's seventh-largest metropolitan area and Quebec's second-largest city after Montreal.
