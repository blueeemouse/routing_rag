Aslak Sira Myhre

Aslak Sira Myhre (born 28 May 1973 in Stavanger) is a Norwegian culture administrator.

 Since 2014 he is director of the National Library of Norway.

 Myhre has been a leftwing politician, and was for a period leader of the former party Red Electoral Alliance (RV).

 In the local election in Oslo in 2015 he was listed for Red, the party succeeding Red Electoral Alliance since 2007, however not on top, and he did not get a seat.
