Dwaraka

Dwaraka is a 2017 Indian Telugu drama film directed by Srinivas Ravindra (MSR), produced by Pradyumna Chandrapati and Ganesh Penubotu, under Legend Cinema , and is presented by R. B. Choudary under the banner Super Good Films.

 It features Vijay Deverakonda and Pooja Jhaveri in the lead roles while Prakash Raj and Prudhviraj appear in crucial roles.

 The movie was set to release on November 2016 but due to unknown reasons released on March 3 2017, premiers in USA on March 2.
