She Stoops to Conquer

She Stoops to Conquer is a comedy by Anglo-Irish author Oliver Goldsmith that was first performed in London in 1773.

 The play is a favourite for study by English literature and theatre classes in the English-speaking world.

 It is one of the few plays from the 18th century to have an enduring appeal, and is still regularly performed today.

 It has been adapted into a film several times, including in 1914 and 1923.
