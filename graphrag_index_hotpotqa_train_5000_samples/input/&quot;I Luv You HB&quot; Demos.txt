&quot;I Luv You HB&quot; Demos

"I Luv You HB" Demos are a collection of demos from I Love You, Honeybear.

 The cassette-only release features different track names than the final release and also a cover of a Cass McCombs track exclusively to "I Luv You HB" Demos.

 The EP was given away free with the pre-orders of the Sub Pop CD and LP edition while supplies lasted as well as with some of the Bella Union CD and LP releases for the UK and Ireland.
