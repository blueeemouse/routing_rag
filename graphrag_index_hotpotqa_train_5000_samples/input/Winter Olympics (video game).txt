Winter Olympics (video game)

Winter Olympics, released in the United States as Winter Olympic Games, is the official video game of the XVII Olympic Winter Games in Lillehammer, Norway.

 All versions were published by U.S. Gold, the rightsholder; home computer versions (Amiga and PC) were developed by ID Software (not to be confused with id Software) and console versions (GG, MD/Gen, Master System, Super NES) were developed by Tiertex.

 The game featured 10 winter sporting events.

 There are also major differences between platforms.

 Players can represent countries from all over the world.
