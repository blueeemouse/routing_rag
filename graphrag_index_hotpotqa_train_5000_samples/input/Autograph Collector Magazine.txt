Autograph Collector Magazine

Autograph Collector Magazine is a U.S. magazine for autograph collectors.

 The magazine was started in 1986.

 They offer news, celebrity interviews and addresses, in-person signing event listings, counterfeit-detection articles, and in-depth articles on collecting autographs from people in various professions, from sports to Hollywood to business and more.

 The magazine is now a web-only publication.

 The print version was published on a monthly basis.
