Make (magazine)

Make: (or "MAKE:") is an American bimonthly magazine published by Maker Media which focuses on do it yourself (DIY) and/or DIWO (Do It With Others) projects involving computers, electronics, robotics, metalworking, woodworking and other disciplines.

 The magazine is marketed to people who enjoy making things and features complex projects which can often be completed with cheap materials, including household items.

 "Make" magazine is considered "a central organ of the maker movement."
