There's No Home for You Here

"There's No Home for You Here" is a song by American alternative rock band The White Stripes, featured on their 2003 fourth studio album "Elephant".

 It was released as the fourth single from the album on March 15, 2004 in the United Kingdom.

 The B-side of the 7" vinyl record is a medley of "I Fought Piranhas" and "Let's Build a Home" – from "The White Stripes" (1999) and "De Stijl" (2000), respectively – recorded at Electric Lady Studios on November 16, 2003.

 The single failed to chart.

 Of the track, Jack said, "Our idea was to see how far we could go with an eight track recorder, and I think how far we went is too far."
