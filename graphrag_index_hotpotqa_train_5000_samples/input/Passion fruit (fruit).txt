Passion fruit (fruit)

The passion fruit is the fruit of a number of plants in the "Passiflora" family.

 Edible passion fruits can be divided into three main types: purple ones (fruits of "Passiflora edulis" Sims), yellow ones ("Passiflora edulis f. flavicarpa" Deg.)

, and giant granadilla ("Passiflora quadrangularis" L.).
