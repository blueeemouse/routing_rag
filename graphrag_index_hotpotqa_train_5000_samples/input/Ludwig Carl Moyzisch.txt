Ludwig Carl Moyzisch

Ludwig Carl Moyzisch (born 1905) was a diplomatic attaché of the Nazi Germany Embassy in Ankara, Turkey in 1943.

 Under this cover, he led in Turkey the work of the German secret services, including Elyesa Bazna, codename 'Cicero'.

 Moyzisch wrote the book "Operation Cicero" in part to explain his role and activities working for the Sicherheitsdienst (SD).

 The book was subsequently adapted into the film "5 Fingers", starring James Mason as the spy.
