Final Cut Studio

Final Cut Studio is a discontinued professional video and audio production suite for Mac OS X from Apple Inc., and a direct competitor to Avid Media Composer in the high-end movie production industry.

 It was developed from 2005 to 2011.

 Three of its primary applications - Final Cut Pro X, Motion, and Compressor - continue to be developed and are published as separate applications on the Mac App Store.

 The legacy boxed version of Final Cut Studio was last made available only through Apple's phone sales, and therefore not online or in Apple Stores.

 As of 2017, Fianl Cut Pro 7 is no longer supported and will not run on macOS High Sierra.
