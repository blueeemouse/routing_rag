Darpan Inani

Darpan Inani (born 1994) is a prolific blind Indian chess player from Vadodara.

 He is the second highest rated blind chess player in India with an ELO rating of 2041 as of Oct 2013.

 He won the bronze medal at the World Junior chess Championship for the blind at Belgrade in 2013.

 He is also the youngest to win the National blind chess championships.
