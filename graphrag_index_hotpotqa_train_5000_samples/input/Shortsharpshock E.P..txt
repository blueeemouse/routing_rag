Shortsharpshock E.P.

Shortsharpshock was the first EP released by the band Therapy?

.

 It was released on 8 March 1993 through A&M Records.

 Its lead track is "Screamager", which reached number 9 in the UK Top 40, leading to an appearance on Top of the Pops.

 It also reached number 2 in the Irish Singles Chart.

 In Australia, the EP peaked at number 228 on the ARIA singles chart, and it peaked at number 22 on the Swedish singles chart.

 In September 2000 "Screamager" placed #86 in "Kerrang!

" magazine's "100 Greatest Riffs Ever" feature.
