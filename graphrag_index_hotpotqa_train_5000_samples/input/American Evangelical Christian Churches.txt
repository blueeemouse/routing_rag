American Evangelical Christian Churches

The American Evangelical Christian Churches is a denomination founded in 1944, with the aim of 'Security with Liberty', enabling orthodox Christian evangelical ministers the opportunity to minister without particular decrees about non-essential Christian doctrines.

 The denomination is unique in that it recognizes dual ordination status with most other mainline Christian denominations.

 The denomination is attracting members of 'Generation X' and 'Generation Y' Christians who seek professional ministerial status after having grown up in an 'nondenominational' or 'interdenominational' church culture.

 The AECC ordains both female and male ministers called by God for service in the world.
