Reece Shearsmith

Reeson Wayne "Reece" Shearsmith (born 27 August 1969) is an English actor, writer and comedian.

 He created, wrote and starred in sketch show "The League of Gentlemen", along with Steve Pemberton, Mark Gatiss, and Jeremy Dyson.

 He and Pemberton also created, wrote and starred in another dark comedy series "Psychoville".

 He and Pemberton currently write and star in their anthology series "Inside No. 9".

 In "The League of Gentlemen", "Psychoville" and "Inside No. 9", Shearsmith plays multiple characters.
