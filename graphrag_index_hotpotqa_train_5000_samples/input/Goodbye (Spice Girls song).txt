Goodbye (Spice Girls song)

"Goodbye" is a song recorded by British girl group Spice Girls.

 It was written by the Spice Girls, Richard Stannard and Matt Rowe, while it was produced by the latter two.

 The song became the group's first song without the vocals of Geri Halliwell.

 It was released by Virgin Records on 14 December 1998 as a Christmas single, along with the B-side, "Christmas Wrapping".

 The song was composed by Richard Stannard and Matt Rowe along with the remaining Spice Girls.

 It was later included as the eleventh and final track on their third studio album "Forever".
