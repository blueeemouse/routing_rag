Forest Avenue School

The Forest Avenue School is a historic one-room schoolhouse in Brockton, Massachusetts.

 The school, originally located on Forest Avenue, was built in 1875 and operated until 1963.

 It is a two-story wood frame structure, with a single classroom on the first floor, and an open play area on the second.

 The building was moved to the (then) new Brockton High School grounds, on Concord Avenue (now Memorial Drive), in 1969.

 It currently serves as the Little Red Schoolhouse Museum with exhibits of local history.
