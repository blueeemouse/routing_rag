Blackmail (2005 film)

Blackmail is a 2005 Hindi action thriller film directed by Anil Devgan, which stars Ajay Devgan, Sunil Shetty and Priyanka Chopra in lead roles.

 The film is inspired from the 1996 Hollywood film "Ransom".

 This movie was second movie directed by Ajay Devgan's brother Anil Devgan.

 He directed Raju Chacha earlier in the year 2000.
