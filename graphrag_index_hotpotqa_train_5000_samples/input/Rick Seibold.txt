Rick Seibold

Rick Seibold ( ; born April 16, 1983) is an American singer-songwriter from Wilmington, North Carolina, now based in Los Angeles.

 Seibold has received considerable recognition as an independent recording artist from his debut record "That's the Day".

 He has worked with artists such as Lee DeWyze, David Archuleta, Katelyn Tarver, Kat DeLuna, and Alex Lambert.
