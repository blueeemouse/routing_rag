Halehsam

Halehsam (Persian: هله سم‎ ‎ , also Romanized as Haleh Sam and Halesam) is a village in Shabab Rural District, in the Shabab District of Chardaval County, Ilam Province, Iran.

 At the 2006 census, its population was 663, in 137 families.

 The village was chosen to be the new capital city of Shabab Rural District after the village of Shabab, The previouse capital was upgraded to a village and was chosen to become the capital city of Shabab District, established on June 30, 2013.
