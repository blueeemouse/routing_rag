Restaurant Nathan Outlaw

Restaurant Nathan Outlaw is a two Michelin star restaurant owned and operated by Nathan Outlaw.

 It has previously been located in Marina Villa Hotel in Fowey, Cornwall, and in the St Enodoc Hotel in Rock, Cornwall.

 It has received mostly positive reviews from food critics.

 In March 2015 the restaurant relocated to Port Isaac, Cornwall and retained its two Michelin stars upon review.
