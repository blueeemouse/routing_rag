The Silver Chair

The Silver Chair is a high fantasy novel for children by C. S. Lewis, published by Geoffrey Bles in 1953.

 It was the fourth published of seven novels in "The Chronicles of Narnia" (1950–1956); it is volume six in recent editions, which are sequenced according to Narnian history.

 Like the others, it was illustrated by Pauline Baynes and her work has been retained in many later editions.
