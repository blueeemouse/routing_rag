Grandmaster Flash &amp; the Furious Five (album)

Grandmaster Flash & The Furious Five is a compilation album release by Grandmaster Flash & the Furious Five ("On The Strength" is their official second album).

 It was released in July 1983, and it is a compilation of their Sugar Hill Records hit singles including "The Message", "New York New York and "White Lines".

 Over half of the tracks were single-only releases prior to this compilation.
