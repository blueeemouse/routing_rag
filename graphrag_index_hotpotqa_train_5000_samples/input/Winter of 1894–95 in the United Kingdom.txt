Winter of 1894–95 in the United Kingdom

The winter of 1894–95 was severe for the British Isles with a CET of 1.27 C .

 Many climatologists have come to view this winter as the end of the Little Ice Age and the culmination of a decade of harsh winters in Britain.

 Whereas the average CET for the ten winters from 1885–86 to 1894–95 was 2.87 C , no winter with a CET under 3.0 C followed for twenty-two years and no month as cold as February or January 1895 until 1940.

 In contrast, between 1659 and 1894 no spell with every winter CET above 3.0 C had lasted longer than twelve winters.
