Ro-Tel

Ro-Tel (stylized as Ro★Tel) is the brand name of a line of canned tomatoes and green chili.

 There are different varieties of Ro-Tel in varying degrees of hotness and spiciness.

 The brand was acquired by ConAgra Foods in 2000 from International Home Foods.

 Ro-Tel gets its name from its inventor, Carl Roettele, who started a family canning company in Elsa, Texas, in the 1940s.

 It is commonly used in making chili con queso, particularly with Velveeta.
