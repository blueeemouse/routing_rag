USS Mervine (DD-489)

USS "Mervine" (DD-489/DMS-31), a "Gleaves"-class destroyer , was the second ship of the United States Navy to be named for Rear Admiral William Mervine, who served during the War of 1812, the Mexican-American War and the American Civil War.

 "Mervine" was laid down on 3 November 1941 by the Federal Shipbuilding & Dry Dock Company of Kearny, New Jersey and launched on 3 May 1942, sponsored by Miss Mildred Mervine great-granddaughter of the admiral.

 The ship was commissioned on 17 June 1942, with Lieutenant Commander S. D. Willingham in command.
