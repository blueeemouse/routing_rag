2003 Auto Club 500

The 2003 Auto Club 500 was a NASCAR Winston Cup Series stock car race held on April 27, 2003 at California Speedway in Fontana, California.

 Contested over 250 laps on the 2-mile (3.23 km) asphalt D-shaped oval, it was the tenth race of the 2003 NASCAR Winston Cup Series season.

 Steve Park of DEI won the pole.

 Kurt Busch of Roush Racing won the race.

 This was also the last race for Jerry Nadeau.

 His career ended with an injury during practice at Richmond
