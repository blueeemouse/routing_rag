I Loved Her First (song)

"I Loved Her First" is a debut song recorded by American country music band Heartland.

 It was released in June 2006 as the first single title track from their debut studio album "I Loved Her First" (2006).

 The song reached Number One on the U.S. "Billboard" Hot Country Songs charts in late 2006.

 The song was written by Walt Aldridge and Elliott Park.
