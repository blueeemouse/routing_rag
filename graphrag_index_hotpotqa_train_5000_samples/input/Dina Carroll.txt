Dina Carroll

Dina Carroll (born Geraldine Carroll, 21 August 1968) is an English singer of Scottish and African American heritage.

 She had a string of hits during the 1990s, including the UK top ten hits, "It's Too Late" (1991), "Don't Be a Stranger" (1993), "The Perfect Year", (1993), and "Escaping" (1996).

 Her first two albums, "So Close" (1993), and "Only Human" (1996), both reached number two on the UK Albums Chart and went platinum.

 She won Best British Female Solo Artist at the 1994 Brit Awards.
