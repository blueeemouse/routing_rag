Loop (band)

Loop are an English rock band, formed in 1986 by Robert Hampson in Croydon.

 The group topped the UK independent charts with their albums "Fade Out" (1989) and "A Gilded Eternity" (1990).

 Their droning, dissonant "trance-rock" sound drew on the work of artists like Can and the Stooges, and helped to resurrect the concept of space rock in the late 1980s.
