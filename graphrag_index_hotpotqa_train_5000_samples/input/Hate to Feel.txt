Hate to Feel

"Hate to Feel" is the tenth track on Alice in Chains' album "Dirt" (1992).

 The song was included on the compilation albums "Music Bank" (1999) and "The Essential Alice in Chains".

 It has also been the ninth track on later prints of the album and eleventh on others.

 Some editions of "Dirt" may merge this song with the then-unlisted 43-second track "Iron Gland".
