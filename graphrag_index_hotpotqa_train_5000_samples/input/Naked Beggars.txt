Naked Beggars

Naked Beggars are a glam metal band featuring Cinderella veteran Eric Brittingham.

 Founded by Eric's wife Inga along with Kris Casamento, Naked Beggars offer up a choice blend of classic, modern and southern rock with a subtle shot of blues.

 The remaining member of the band is drummer Lisa Rav who joined the group in September, 2006.

 Dustin Carpenter has been the drummer since 2008.

 Naked Beggars have released 2 albums thus far—a self-titled debut in 2003 and the follow-up "Spit It Out" in 2005.

 April 2007, Jeff LaBar and his wife parted ways with Naked Beggars.

 They are currently in pre-production for their third album "XXX".
