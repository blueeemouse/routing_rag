Isabel Strauss

Isabel Strauss [Strauß] (1928-1973) was a German operatic soprano.

 She appeared in Munich, Bern, Amsterdam, and Brussels, as well as singing Gutrune in Wieland Wagner's 1963 production of "Götterdämmerung", in Cologne.

 Also in 1963, she portrayed Marta in a film of "Tiefland".
