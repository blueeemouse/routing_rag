Emerson Etheridge

Henry Emerson Etheridge (September 28, 1819 – October 21, 1902) was an American politician and a member of the United States House of Representatives for Tennessee's 9th congressional district from 1853 to 1857, and again from 1859 to 1861.

 He also served one term in the Tennessee House of Representatives (1845–1847) and one term in the Tennessee Senate (1869–1871).

 After Tennessee seceded in 1861, he was elected Clerk of the United States House of Representatives, serving until 1863.
