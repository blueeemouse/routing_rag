Conny van Bentum

Cornelia "Conny" van Bentum (born August 12, 1965 in Barneveld) is a former butterfly and freestyle swimmer from the Netherlands, who competed in three consecutive Summer Olympics for her native country, starting in 1980.

 At each of those swimming tournaments she won a medal with the 4×100 m freestyle relay team: one bronze (1980), and two silvers (1984 and 1988).
