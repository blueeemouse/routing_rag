The Torkelsons

The Torkelsons is an American sitcom which aired on the NBC television network from September 21, 1991 to June 6, 1993.

 Produced by Walt Disney Television in season 1 and Touchstone Television in season 2, the series starred Connie Ray, Olivia Burnette, and William Schallert.

 For the second and final season, the series was retooled and renamed Almost Home.

 The series lasted a total of two seasons, consisting of 33 episodes.
