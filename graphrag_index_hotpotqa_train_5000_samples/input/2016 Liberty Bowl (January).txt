2016 Liberty Bowl (January)

The 2016 Liberty Bowl was a post-season American college football bowl game played on January 2, 2016 at Liberty Bowl Memorial Stadium in Memphis, Tennessee.

 The 57th edition of the Liberty Bowl featured the Kansas State Wildcats of the Big 12 Conference against the Arkansas Razorbacks of the Southeastern Conference.

 It began at 2:20 p.m. CST and aired on ESPN.

 It was one of the 2015–16 bowl games that concluded the 2015 FBS football season.

 Sponsored by automobile parts and accessories store AutoZone, it is officially known as the AutoZone Liberty Bowl.
