CKBE-FM

CKBE-FM (9-2-5 the Beat) is an English language Canadian radio station located in Montreal, Quebec.

 It is owned and operated by Cogeco, and broadcasts on 92.5 MHz with an effective radiated power (ERP) of 100,000 watts (class C1) using an omnidirectional antenna from a transmitter atop Mount Royal, while its studios are located at Place Bonaventure in downtown Montreal.

 The station broadcasts a Rhythmic Adult Contemporary format.
