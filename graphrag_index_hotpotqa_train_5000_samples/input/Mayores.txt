Mayores

"Mayores" is a song by Mexican-American singer Becky G featuring vocals from Puerto Rican rapper Bad Bunny.

 The song was released on July 14, 2017, as the fourth single from her unnamed debut album.

￼ "Mayores" topped the chart in Spain, Peru, Costa Rica, and El Salvador, while peaking at number 10 on the "Billboard" Hot Latin Songs chart.

 The music video for the song was released on YouTube on July 13, 2017.
