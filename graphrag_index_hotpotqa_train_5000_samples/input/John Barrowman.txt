John Barrowman

John Scot Barrowman, {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (born 11 March 1967), is a Scottish-American actor, singer, dancer, presenter and writer, who holds both British and American citizenship.

 Born in Glasgow, Scotland, he moved to the United States with his family in 1975.

 Encouraged by his high school teachers, Barrowman studied performing arts at the United States International University in San Diego before landing the role of Billy Crocker in Cole Porter's "Anything Goes" at London's West End.
