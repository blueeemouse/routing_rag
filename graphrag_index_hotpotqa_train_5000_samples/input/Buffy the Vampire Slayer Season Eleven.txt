Buffy the Vampire Slayer Season Eleven

Buffy the Vampire Slayer Season Eleven is the sequel to the "Season Ten" comic book series, a canonical continuation of the television series "Buffy the Vampire Slayer".

 The entire season consists of only 12 issues, a much shorter run than the previous seasons.

 The series was published by Dark Horse Comics and began on November 23, 2016.
