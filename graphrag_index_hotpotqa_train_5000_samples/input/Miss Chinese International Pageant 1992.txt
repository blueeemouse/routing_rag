Miss Chinese International Pageant 1992

The 4th Miss Chinese International Pageant, Miss Chinese International Pageant 1992 was held on January 26, 1992 in Hong Kong.

 The pageant was organized and broadcast by TVB in Hong Kong.

 Miss Chinese International 1991 Yen-Thean Leng crowned Rosemary Chan of Toronto, Canada as the winner.

 It was also the first victory for Canada and North America.

 As of 2010, Toronto would win the crown for two more times.
