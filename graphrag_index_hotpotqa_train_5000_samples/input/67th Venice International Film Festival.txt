67th Venice International Film Festival

The 67th annual Venice International Film Festival held in Venice, Italy, took place from 1 to 11 September 2010.

 American film director and screenwriter Quentin Tarantino was head of the Jury.

 John Woo was awarded the Golden Lion for Lifetime Achievement prior to the start of the Festival.

 The opening film was Darren Aronofsky's "Black Swan".
