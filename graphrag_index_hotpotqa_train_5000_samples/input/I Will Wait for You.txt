I Will Wait for You

"I Will Wait for You" is the English version of "Je ne pourrai jamais vivre sans toi", a song from the French musical "The Umbrellas of Cherbourg" ("Les Parapluies de Cherbourg", 1964).

 Its music was composed by Michel Legrand and the original lyrics written by Jacques Demy.

 It was performed in the film by Catherine Deneuve, whose voice was dubbed by Danielle Licari.

 The English lyrics of the song were written by Norman Gimbel.

 This version was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Song at the 38th Academy Awards held in 1966.
