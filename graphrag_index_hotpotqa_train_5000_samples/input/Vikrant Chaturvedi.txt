Vikrant Chaturvedi

Vikrant a/p Chaturvedi (Hindi: विक्रांत चतुर्वेदी "Vikrānta Caturvēdī", born 1 August 1974 in Allahabad) is an Indian actor and voice-dubbing artist who speaks Hindi as his mother tongue.

 He has acted in Bollywood and has also voiced Indian animation and for many Hindi dubs of Hollywood and other foreign films and media.

 He played Mir Khorason in "Chakravartin Ashoka Samrat".
