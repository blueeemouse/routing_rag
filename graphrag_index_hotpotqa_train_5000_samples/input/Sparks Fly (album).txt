Sparks Fly (album)

Sparks Fly is the debut studio album by American actress and pop singer Miranda Cosgrove.

 The plans for the album were first announced in July 2008, and the album was released by Columbia Records on April 27, 2010 as a standard edition and a deluxe edition and Epic Records in India and Italy.

 The album has been preceded by its lead single, "Kissin U" which was co-written by Cosgrove, Claude Kelly and Dr. Luke, who produced it with HitmanKTI, and was released on March 22, 2010.

 The album sold 36,000 copies in its first week, making its peak No. 8 on the "Billboard" 200.
