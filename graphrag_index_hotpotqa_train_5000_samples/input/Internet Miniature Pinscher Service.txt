Internet Miniature Pinscher Service

Internet Miniature Pinscher Service (IMPS) is a non-profit animal rescue organization based in United States of America.

 The organization was established in 1998 by Petie Hoving Durand and is dedicated to adoption and rescue of Miniature Pinscher, a small breed of dogs originating from Germany.

 Internet Miniature Pinscher Service (IMPS) is the largest single breed rescue service in America.

 Currently, organization operates in United States of America and Canada.

 The organization has to date rescued over 20,000 dogs and currently has 500 volunteers throughout the United States and Canada.
