Lookin' After No. 1

"Lookin' After No. 1" is the first single by The Boomtown Rats.

 It appears on their first album "The Boomtown Rats".

 The single was released in August 1977 after the band had performed a five date tour supporting Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers.

 "Lookin' After No. 1" was the first so-called new wave single to be play listed by the BBC and the Boomtown Rats subsequently became the first new wave band to be offered an appearance on "Top Of The Pops" performing the song.

 The song reached number 2 on the Irish Singles Chart and spent nine weeks in the UK Singles Chart reaching a peak of number 11.

 Different covers were produced for releases in the Netherlands and Japan.

 Reviewer David Clancy described the song as having a "breakneck sneering selfishness".
