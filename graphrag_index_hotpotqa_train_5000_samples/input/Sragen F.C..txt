Sragen F.C.

Sragen Football Club (also known as Sragen United) is an Indonesian football team based in Sragen, Central Java.

 Sragen FC played at Liga 2 Indonesia.

 Sragen FC previously named Laga FC, after a businessman Sragen, Indika Wijaya Kusuma buy Laga FC of PT Laga Nusantara Mandiri worth IDR 5 billion, Laga FC switch homebase to Sragen, and changed its name to Sragen United at February 27, 2017.
