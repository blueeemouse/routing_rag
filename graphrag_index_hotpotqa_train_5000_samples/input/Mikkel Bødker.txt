Mikkel Bødker

Mikkel Boedker; ] ; born 16 December 1989) is a Danish professional ice hockey right winger currently playing for the San Jose Sharks of the National Hockey League (NHL).

 He was selected eighth overall by the Phoenix Coyotes in the 2008 NHL Entry Draft, making him the highest-ever drafted Danish player.

 He also features on the cover of the video game "NHL 10" in Denmark and Norway.
