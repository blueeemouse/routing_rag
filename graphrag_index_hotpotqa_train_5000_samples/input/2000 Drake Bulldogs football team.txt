2000 Drake Bulldogs football team

The 2000 Drake Bulldogs football team represented Drake University during the 2000 NCAA Division I-AA football season.

 It was the 107th year for the football program.

 The season ended with a 7–4 record and a Pioneer Football League Championship.

 The Bulldogs were chosen as a 2000 Sports Network Cup Finalist, finishing second to Duquesne in both overall votes and first place votes.
