2014–15 Florida Gators men's basketball team

The 2014–15 Florida Gators men's basketball team represented the University of Florida in the sport of basketball during the 2014–15 NCAA Division I men's basketball season.

 The Gators competed in the Southeastern Conference (SEC).

 They were led by nineteen-year head coach Billy Donovan, and played their home games in the O'Connell Center on the university's Gainesville, Florida campus.

 They finished the season 16–17, 8–10 in SEC play to finish in a tie for eighth place.

 They advanced to the quarterfinals of the SEC Tournament where they lost to Kentucky.
