Renato Berta

Renato Berta is a Swiss cinematographer and film director, best known for his collaborations with directors Alain Tanner and Jean-Marie Straub.

 Trained at the Centro Sperimentale di Cinematografia in Rome, Berta has worked as cinematographer in more than 100 films since 1969.

 He won a César Award for Best Cinematography for "Au revoir les enfants" in 1988 and a David di Donatello for Best Cinematography for "Noi credevamo" in 2011.
