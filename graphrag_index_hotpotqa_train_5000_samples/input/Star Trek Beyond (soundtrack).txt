Star Trek Beyond (soundtrack)

Star Trek Beyond: Music from the Motion Picture is a soundtrack album for the 2016 film, "Star Trek Beyond", composed by Michael Giacchino.

 The soundtrack album was released in physical form on July 29, 2016, through Varèse Sarabande, as the follow-up to the critically successful 2009 soundtrack album "Star Trek" and the 2013 soundtrack album "Star Trek Into Darkness".
