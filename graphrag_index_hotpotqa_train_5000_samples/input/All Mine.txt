All Mine

"All Mine" is the second track and first single from English band Portishead's self-titled second album.

 It was released as a single, along with "Cowboys", "Only You" and "Over".

 The music video—based on an 1968 Italian music show and "The Outer Limits" —featured a little girl singing in front of an orchestra.

 The single reached #8 in the United Kingdom and is so far the band's only Top 10 hit there.
