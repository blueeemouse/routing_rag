Greg Hawthorne

Greg Hawthorne (born September 5, 1956) is a former American football player with the National Football League.

 Drafted out of Baylor University, Hawthorne played 9 seasons with the Pittsburgh Steelers, as a rookie, he won a Super Bowl ring with the Steelers in Super Bowl XIV over the Los Angeles Rams.

 He also played for the New England Patriots (including playing in Super Bowl XX), and also played for the Indianapolis Colts.

 As a running back, tight end, and wide receiver, he accumulated 527 rushing yards and 92 receptions between 1979 and 1987.
