Supernoobs

Supernoobs is an American-Canadian flash animated series produced by DHX Media for Teletoon.

 The series is about four kids who navigate through middle school and fight viruses as well.

 It first premiered on Cartoon Network in the United Kingdom and Ireland on November 2, 2015.

 "Supernoobs" was created by Scott Fellows, who also created "Johnny Test", "Ned's Declassified School Survival Guide", "Big Time Rush", and "100 Things to Do Before High School".
