Jim Carrey filmography

The filmography of Canadian American actor and comedian Jim Carrey comprises both film and television roles.

 He appeared in over forty-two feature films, and ten television films and series.

 He is one of the top-50 highest-grossing actors of all time at the North American box office, with over $2.5 billion total gross and an average of $94.3 million per film.

 He was involved with thirteen films that grossed over $200 million at the worldwide box office, the highest grossing one was "Bruce Almighty".
