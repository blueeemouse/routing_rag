Mfon Udoka

Mfon Sunday Udoka (born June 16, 1976) is a Nigerian American former professional basketball player and the new Assistant Coach of the Nigeria women's national basketball team the Lady Tigers.

 Born in Portland, Oregon, Udoka graduated from Benson Polytechnic High School (1994) in Portland before attending DePaul University in Chicago, Illinois from 1994–1998.

 She is the sister of NBA player Ime Udoka.
