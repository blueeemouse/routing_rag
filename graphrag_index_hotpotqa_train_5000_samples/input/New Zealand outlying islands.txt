New Zealand outlying islands

The New Zealand outlying islands comprise nine island groups, located in the subtropics and subantarctic, which are part of New Zealand but lie outside of the New Zealand continental shelf.

 Although considered as integral parts of New Zealand, seven of the nine island groups are not part of any region or district, but are instead designated as "Area Outside Territorial Authority".

 The two exceptions are the Chatham Islands, which form a special territorial authority (island council) themselves, and the Solander Islands, which are part of the Southland Region and Southland District.
