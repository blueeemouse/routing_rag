Eugene Chantrelle

Eugene Marie Chantrelle (born Nantes 1834, died Edinburgh 31 May 1878) was a French teacher who lived in Edinburgh and who was convicted for the murder of his wife, Elizabeth Dyer.

 He is claimed to be the inspiration for Robert Louis Stevenson's character Dr Jekyll featured in "The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde".

 Stevenson met Chantrelle at the home of Victor Richon, Stevenson's old French master.
