List of awards and nominations received by Noah

Noah is an Indonesian alternative pop/rock band.

 The band originally consisted of four members, known as Ariel, Uki, Lukman, and David.

 Before renaming as Noah, the band was originally named Peterpan from 2000 to 2009, and included members Ariel, Uki, Lukman, Reza, and David.

 This is list awards received by Noah.
