Fred Miller (defensive lineman)

Fred David Miller (born August 8, 1940) was an American football defensive tackle in the National Football League from 1963 through 1972.

 During that span, he appeared in 3 world championship games for the Baltimore Colts: the 1964 NFL championship game against the Browns, Super Bowl III against the Jets, and Super Bowl V against the Cowboys.

 He played college football at Louisiana State University.
