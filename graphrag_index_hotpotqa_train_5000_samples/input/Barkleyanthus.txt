Barkleyanthus

Barkleyanthus is a monotypic genus.

 of flowering plants in the aster family, Asteraceae, containing the single species Barklyanthus salicifolius, a plant formerly classified in the genus "Senecio" It is native to North and Central America, where its distribution extends from the southwestern United States to El Salvador.

 Its common names include willow ragwort, willow groundsel, Barkley's-ragwort, and jarilla.
