Landhi Town

Landhi Town ( Sindhi: لانڍي ٽائون ‎ Urdu: لانڈھی ٹاؤن ‎ ) is a large industrial town in the eastern part of Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan.

 It is bordered by the Faisal Cantonment and Shah Faisal Town to the north across the Malir River, Bin Qasim Town to the south and east, and Korangi Town to the west.

 The population of Landhi Town was estimated to be over 660,000 at the 1998 census, of which 99% are Muslim.

 Sindhis and Pashtuns constitute an overwhelming majority of the population, followed by Muhajirs and Baloch.
