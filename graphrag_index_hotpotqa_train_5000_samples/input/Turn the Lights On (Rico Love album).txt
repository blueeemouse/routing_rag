Turn the Lights On (Rico Love album)

Turn the Lights On (also known under the acronym TTLO) is the debut studio album by American recording singer-songwriter Rico Love.

 It was released on May 18, 2015, by Interscope.

 The production on the album was handled primarily by Love, Danja, Jim Jonson, Jack Splash, DJ Dahi and his own orchestra, TTLO.
