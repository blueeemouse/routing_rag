First Satanic Church

The First Satanic Church is an organization founded by Karla LaVey on October 31, 1999, in San Francisco, California, that is dedicated to LaVeyan Satanism as codified by Anton LaVey in "The Satanic Bible".

 The church's stated mission is to carry on the legacy of Anton LaVey through "the study of Satanism and the occult sciences".

 The church operates The 600 Club, an internet forum dedicated to discussions of Satanism.
