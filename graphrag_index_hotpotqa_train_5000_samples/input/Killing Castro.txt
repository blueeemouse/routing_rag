Killing Castro

Killing Castro is a 2006 play by British playwright Brian Stewart.

 The Haymarket Productions's National Tour of the play was also performed at the Festival Theatre in Malvern in June 2006, and starred Robin Hood's Michael Praed and Clive Mantle.

 It was described by the "Birmingham Mail" as an "acclaimed comedy" which "chronicles the more bizarre of America's attempts to kill the Cuban leader Fidel Castro - including filling his shoes with poison and inventing an exploding cigar."
