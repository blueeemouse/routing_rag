Deutsche Schule Kobe/European School

Deutsche Schule Kobe/European School (DESK; 神戸ドイツ学院) is a German international school on Rokko Island, in Higashinada-ku, Kobe, Japan.

 The Deutsche Schule Kobe / European School provides a holistic, multilingual education to learners ages 2 to 14.

 The school provides curriculum in two languages: German and English.
