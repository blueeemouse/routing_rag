Two Worlds II

Two Worlds II is an action role-playing game developed by Polish video game developer Reality Pump and published by TopWare Interactive as a sequel to 2007's "Two Worlds".

 It was released on November 9, 2010 in Europe for Microsoft Windows, Mac OS X, Xbox 360 and PlayStation 3.

 The game was released on January 25, 2011 in North America for the same platforms.

 "Two Worlds II" is a real-time role-playing game that takes place in an open fantasy world where players take the role of a single character with whom they can explore and undertake quests.
