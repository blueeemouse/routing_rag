Jersey Shore

The Jersey Shore is the coastal region of the U.S. state of New Jersey.

 Geographically, the term encompasses about 141 mi of oceanfront from Perth Amboy in the north to Cape May Point in the south, bordering the Atlantic Ocean.

 The Jersey Shore area includes Middlesex, Monmouth, Ocean, Atlantic, and Cape May counties.

 While there is no defined border between North Jersey and South Jersey, the Raritan River, Manasquan River or I-195 are often mentioned as the border, with most of the shore region being located in South Jersey.

 Many New Jersey residents refer to it simply as "The Shore", as in to go "down the shore", or to have done something "down the shore".
