Beyond International Group

The Beyond International Group is an international television and film production company with operations in Ireland, the United Kingdom, Australia, and the United States.

 Listed on the Australian Stock Exchange as Beyond International Limited (), the company is involved in the creation and international exploitation of television, feature films and ancillary products.

 The company has an extensive television program catalogue of over 5,000 hours and covers all genres including adult drama, feature films, comedy, children’s, reality, and documentaries.
