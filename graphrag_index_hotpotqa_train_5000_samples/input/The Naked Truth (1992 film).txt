The Naked Truth (1992 film)

The Naked Truth is a 1992 comedy film starring Robert Caso and Kevin Schon.

 Also featured in the film are Zsa Zsa Gabor, Lou Ferrigno, Erik Estrada, Ted Lange, Billy Barty, Yvonne De Carlo, Norman Fell, Little Richard, David Birney, M. Emmet Walsh, Dick Gautier, John Vernon and Camilla Sparv among others.

 It is directed by Nico Mastorakis.
