CHLK-FM

CHLK-FM, branded as Lake 88.1 FM, is a Canadian radio station offering a soft adult contemporary music format with a focus on local/regional news programming broadcasting at 88.1 FM in Perth, Ontario.

 The station is located at 43 Wilson Street West in the town of Perth, Ontario, while the antenna is at the top of a Bell Mobility cell tower located at the edge of town off Lanark Road 511, approximately 1 km north of Dufferin Street (Highway 7).
