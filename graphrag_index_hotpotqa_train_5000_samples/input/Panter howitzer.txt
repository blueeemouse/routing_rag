Panter howitzer

The Panter howitzer is an artillery weapon developed by MKEK for the Turkish Land Forces Command.

 It has a 155 mm/52-calibre towed howitzer gun and is able to fire projectiles to a maximum range of 40 kilometers using extended-range ammunition.

 Mounted on the forward part of the carriage is an Auxiliary Power Unit (APU) that enables the Panter to propel itself at a maximum speed of 20 km/h on asphalt road.
