Leslie Averill

Leslie Cecil Lloyd Averill {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (25 March 1897 – 4 June 1981) was a New Zealand soldier who served during the First World War on the Western Front.

 After the war, he became a doctor and established a private practice in his hometown of Christchurch.
