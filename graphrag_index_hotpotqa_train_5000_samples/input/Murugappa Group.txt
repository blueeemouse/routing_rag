Murugappa Group

Founded in 1900, the Murugappa Group is an Indian business conglomerate, founded, managed, and largely owned by the Murugappa family.

 The group has 28 businesses including eleven listed companies traded on the National Stock Exchange of India and the Bombay Stock Exchange.

 Headquartered in Chennai, the major companies of the group include Carborundum Universal Ltd, Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Ltd., Cholamandalam MS General Insurance Company Ltd., Coromandel International Ltd., Coromandel Engineering Company Ltd., EID Parry (India) Ltd., ParryAgro Industries Ltd., Tube Investments of India Limited, and Wendt (India) Ltd., and Shanthi Gears Limited (Coimbatore).
