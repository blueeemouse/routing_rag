Audi A4 DTM

The Audi A4 DTM is a 4-door touring car (DTM) constructed by the German car manufacturer Audi.

 It was first developed for use in the 2004 DTM season, replacing the Audi TT DTM at the end of the 2003 DTM season.

 Based on the Audi A4, it was continually improved over the course of six facelifts between 2004 and 2011.

 The Audi A4 DTM was succeeded by the Audi A5 DTM in 2012.
