Far Away Love

Far Away Love (), also translated as Love of Far Away and Remote Love, is a 1947 Chinese film directed by Chen Liting.

 Made during the Republican era, it was produced by the state-owned China Film No. 2 Studio, and stars prominent actors and actresses Zhao Dan, Qin Yi, and Wu Yin.

 The film was well received, and its premiere in Shanghai is considered a landmark event in postwar Chinese cinema.
