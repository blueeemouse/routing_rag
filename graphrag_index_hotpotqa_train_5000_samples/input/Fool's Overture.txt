Fool's Overture

"Fool's Overture" is the closing track from Supertramp's 1977 album "Even in the Quietest Moments...".

 Written and sung by guitarist/keyboardist Roger Hodgson, the song is a collage of progressive instrumentation and sound samples.

 First there are excerpts of Winston Churchill's famous 4 June 1940 House of Commons speech regarding Britain's involvement in World War II ("Never Surrender"), and later sounds of police cars and bells from the London's Big Ben clock tower are heard.

 The flageolet-sounding instrument plays an excerpt from Gustav Holst's "Venus", from his orchestral suite "The Planets".

 There is also a reading of the first verse of William Blake's poem "And did those feet in ancient time" (more commonly known as "Jerusalem"), ended by a short sample of the band's song "Dreamer".
