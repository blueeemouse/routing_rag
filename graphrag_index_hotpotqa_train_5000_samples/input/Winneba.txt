Winneba

Winneba, is a town and the capital of Effutu Municipal District in Central Region of South Ghana.

 Winneba has a population of 55,331.

 Winneba, traditionally known as "Simpa", is a historic fishing port in south Ghana, lying on the south coast, 90 mi east of Cape Coast.

 The current member of parliament is Alexander Kwamina Afenyo-Markin.
