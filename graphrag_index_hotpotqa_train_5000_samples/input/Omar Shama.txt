Omar Shama

Omar Azmy Shama (born 1976) (Arabic: عمر عزمي شامة‎ ‎ ) (Various spellings include "Omar Schama", "Omar Chama") is an Egyptian screenwriter and film producer born in Cairo, Egypt.

 Following university, he worked as a reporter at the Associated Press news agency.

 He later quit his job to focus on a career in the film industry.
