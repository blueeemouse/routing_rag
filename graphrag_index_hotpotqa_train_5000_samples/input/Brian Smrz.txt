Brian Smrz

Brian Smrz (born c. 1960), also Brian Delaney Smrz, is a Hollywood stunt coordinator and second unit director of projects such as "Live Free or Die Hard", "", "Eagle Eye", "Night at the Museum", "Windtalkers" and "Superman Returns", among others.

 He is also the director of "Hero Wanted", starring Cuba Gooding, Jr. and Ray Liotta.

 He won the Taurus Award twice and was nominated a third time.
