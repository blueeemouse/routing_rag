Kiss &amp; Love (album)

Kiss & Love is a 2014 two-CD charity album by various French-language artists in favour of Sidaction, a major French public event that started in 1994 in France for raising awareness and collecting charitable funds for AIDS.

 It donates important sums to various AIDS charities, HIV/AIDS research, institutions specializing in medical care and social aid for those suffering of HIV/AIDS in France and internationally.
