Happy? (Mudvayne song)

"Happy?"

 is a single from Mudvayne's third album, "Lost and Found".

 It was released in 2005 and is a highly played song on the radio.

 The song was the theme song of "WWE Vengeance 2005" and has been played as a commercial bumper track for "The Jim Rome Show".

 "Happy?"

 was named both the No. 1 Headbangers Ball Video of 2005 and "Billboard" Monitor's Active Rock Song of the Year.

 The song held the No. 1 spot on the "Billboard" Hot Mainstream Rock Tracks chart for one week, and reached No. 8 on the Hot Modern Rock Tracks chart.
