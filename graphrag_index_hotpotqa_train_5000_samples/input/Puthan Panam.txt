Puthan Panam

Puthan Panam(English:Black Money) is a 2017 Indian Malayalam black comedy film based on the issue of black money, written and directed by Ranjith, starring Mammootty in lead role.

 Mammooty speaks Kasargod dialect in the movie.

 The movie released on 12 April 2017 and is based on the black money and organized crime's.

.

 Mammootty's character appreciated by the critics, but the movie get mixed review.
