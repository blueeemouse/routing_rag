City of Playford

The City of Playford is a local government area of South Australia in Adelaide's northern suburbs.

 The name 'Playford' comes from the recognition of Sir Thomas Playford, who played a part in the development of the area, and was South Australia's premier from 1938-1965.

 The City covers an area of 346 km, and is home to approximately 88,000 residents.

 Playford is the fastest growing local government area in South Australia.

 s of 2016 the city motto is: "a great place to live, work and play".
