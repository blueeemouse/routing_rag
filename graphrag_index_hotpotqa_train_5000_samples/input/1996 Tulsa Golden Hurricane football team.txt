1996 Tulsa Golden Hurricane football team

The 1996 Tulsa Golden Hurricane football team represented the University of Tulsa during the 1996 NCAA Division I-A football season.

 In their ninth year under head coach David Rader, the Golden Hurricane compiled a 4–7 record.

 In the teams first season as members of the Western Athletic Conference the team finished seventh in the Mountain Divsision with a 2–6 conference record.

 The team's statistical leaders included quarterback Troy DeGar with 1,336 passing yards, Reggie Williams with 759 rushing yards, and Wes Caswell with 817 receiving yards.
