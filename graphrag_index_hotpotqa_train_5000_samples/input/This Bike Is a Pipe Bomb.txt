This Bike Is a Pipe Bomb

This Bike Is a Pipe Bomb was a folk punk band from Pensacola, Florida, United States.

 Their first recording was released in 1997 on Ghostmeat Records.

 Their later releases have been on Plan It X Records and No Idea Records, but now appear on their own label Plan-It X South.

 This Bike Is a Pipe Bomb initially started as a new wave band, with folk singer David Dondero on drums.

 They quickly switched to playing country music, but their punk roots were evident enough in their music that they became one of the premiere bands at the forefront of the folk-punk genre.

 They did several US tours, played at Geekfest, and toured to Europe.
