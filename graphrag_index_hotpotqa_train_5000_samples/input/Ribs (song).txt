Ribs (song)

"Ribs" is a song by New Zealand singer Lorde, taken from her debut studio album "Pure Heroine" (2013).

 The song was released as a promotional single on 30 September 2013 by Universal Music Group (UMG).

 Written by Lorde and Joel Little and produced by Little, with additional production from Lorde, "Ribs" is an electronica and electropop song discussing Lorde's stress of ageing.
