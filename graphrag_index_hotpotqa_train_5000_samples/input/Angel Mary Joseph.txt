Angel Mary Joseph

Angel Mary Joseph (born 24 September 1953) is a retired Indian track and field athlete.

 She specialized in 100 metres hurdles, Long Jump, Pentathlon, and once held national records in all the three and high jump and heptathlon.

 At the 1978 Asian Games in Tehran, she won silver medals in long jump and pentathlon.

 She also represented Karnataka and Railways playing basketball in the National Championships.
