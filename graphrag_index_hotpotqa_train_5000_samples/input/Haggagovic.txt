Haggagovic

Ahmed Haggagovic (Russian: Ахмед Хаггагович , ), Ahmed Haggag nicknamed Haggagovic, is an Egyptian globe trotter, adventurer, TV presenter and expert international tourism specialist.

 He is the host of the most influential travel TV show in the Arab world Safari Haggagovic aired weekly on ONTV (Egypt), Haggagovic is also known as "Ibn Batuta of the 21st century" and the "extraordinary ambassador" of Egypt, and the "ambassador of world peace", his mission is to be the first human in history to travel to all the countries of the world in order to spread the values of peace and love, and to break stereotypes and correct the wrong thoughts about the world's countries.

 He raises his Egyptian flag in all these countries; this flag is famous due to bearing the signatures of many of the world's leaders, presidents and ambassadors.
