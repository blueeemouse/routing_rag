13 Sins

13 Sins is a 2014 American horror comedy film directed by Daniel Stamm.

 The film is a remake of the 2006 Thai horror comedy film "13 Beloved".

 Mark Webber stars as Elliot, a meek salesman who accepts a series of increasingly disturbing and criminal challenges.

 It premiered at the 2014 SXSW film festival and was released theatrically in the United States on April 18, 2014.
