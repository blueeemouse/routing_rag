Bennie and Stella Dickson

Bennie (Born Topeka, Kansas, died April 6, 1939) and (Estelle?)

 Stella Dickson (née Redenbaugh) (August 25 Topeka, Kansas, 1922 – 1995 in Missouri) were Depression-era outlaws and bank robbers in the United States.

 They successfully stole over US $50,000 in an eight-month period from August 1938 to April 1939.
