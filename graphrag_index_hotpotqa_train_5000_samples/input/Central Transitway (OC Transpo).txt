Central Transitway (OC Transpo)

The Central Transitway is the downtown section of Ottawa, Ontario, Canada's Transitway.

 The section of the Transitway uses dedicated lanes on existing streets, namely Albert Street for westbound routes and Slater Street for eastbound routes.

 The lanes are dedicated to buses only on weekdays from the start of morning rush hour (6:00 am) to the end of evening rush hour (6:00 pm); they are otherwise open to regular traffic.

 Even during rush hour, regular traffic needs to cross the dedicated lanes to access right turning lanes.
