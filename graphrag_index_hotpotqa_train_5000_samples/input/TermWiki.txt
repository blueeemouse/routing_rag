TermWiki

TermWiki.com (pronounced ) is a major social learning network that allows users to learn, discover, share, and store personal terms and glossaries in 1487 domains in 97 languages.

 The site emphasizes collaboration, with a forum, a question/answer module, messaging features that encourage user interaction, and discussion pages on each term.

 The personal profile page allows users to become fans of other users, add photos, and add links and post comments on other users recent activity.

 TermWiki also allows companies to conduct international ad campaigns on keyword terms, for improved SEO performance.
