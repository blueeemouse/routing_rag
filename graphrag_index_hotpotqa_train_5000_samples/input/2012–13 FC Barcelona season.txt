2012–13 FC Barcelona season

The 2012–13 season was Futbol Club Barcelona's 113th in existence and the club's 82nd consecutive season in the top flight of Spanish football.

 The season marked the managerial debut of Tito Vilanova, who served as Pep Guardiola's longtime assistant.

 Vilanova assumed management of the club after Guardiola, who had managed Barcelona for the prior four seasons, declined to renew his contract.
