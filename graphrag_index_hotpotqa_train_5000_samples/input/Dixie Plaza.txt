Dixie Plaza

Dixie Plaza is a shopping plaza located in Mississauga, Ontario, Canada, at 3100 Dixie Rd (intersection of Dundas Street West and Dixie Rd).

 Dixie Plaza has 15 stores and one anchor store which is LA Fitness (formerly Premier Fitness).

 The plaza has three restaurants, one grocery store, two thrift stores, and one department store.

 Dixie Plaza's manager, developer, and owner is RioCan.
