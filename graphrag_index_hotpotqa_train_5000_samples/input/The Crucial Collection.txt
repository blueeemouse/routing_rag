The Crucial Collection

The Crucial Collection is a compilation album by Look Mexico, released on February 12, 2008 on Lujo Records.

 The disc re-releases the songs found on "The Crucial EP" (where the album's name is taken from) and the "So Byzantine EP", and a selection of b-sides and remixes, as well as revised versions of songs taken from their debut, "This Is Animal Music", with a more polished-sounding mix.
