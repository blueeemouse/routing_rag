Atsuko Nakajima

Atsuko Nakajima (中嶋敦子 , Nakajima Atsuko , born December 21, 1961 in Kanagawa, Japan) is a Japanese animator, character designer, and illustrator.

 She is married to anime director and animator Tomohiro Hirata, with whom she also collaborated in the "Trinity Blood" anime (in which Nakajima was character designer and Hirata was director).
