Federal Reserve Bank of Richmond Baltimore Branch

The Federal Reserve Bank of Richmond Baltimore Branch Office is one of the two Federal Reserve Bank of Richmond branch offices.

 The Federal Reserve Bank of Richmond's Baltimore Branch is an operational and regional center for Maryland, the metropolitan Washington D.C. area, Northern Virginia, and northeastern West Virginia.

 The Baltimore branch is part of the Fifth District and has the code E5.

 It supports Check 21 operations, supplies coin and currency to financial institutions and works to maintain stability in the financial sector throughout the Fifth District and also works with local elected officials and non-profit organizations to support fair housing initiatives throughout the Fifth District.

 The Baltimore branch was founded in March 1918 and is currently headed by William R. Roberts.
