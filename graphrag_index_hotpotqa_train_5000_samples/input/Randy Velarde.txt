Randy Velarde

Randy Lee Velarde (born November 24, 1962) is a former American baseball infielder and utility player who played 16 seasons in Major League Baseball (MLB).

 He played the majority of his career for the New York Yankees, and also played for the California/Anaheim Angels, Oakland Athletics and Texas Rangers from 1987 to 2002.

 Although he primarily played as a utility infielder, Velarde was utilized in the outfield as well.
