T-Mo

T-Mo (born Robert Barnett on February 2, 1972 in Fairburn, Georgia) is an American rapper best known for being a member of Southern hip-hop group Goodie Mob and being the more educated member of the group.

 He acquired his Bachelor of Science Degree from Morris Brown College in Atlanta, Georgia.

 He is also one-half of the hip-hop duo The Lumberjacks (with fellow former Goodie Mob member Khujo).

 He released his first solo album "Freedom" on October 7, 2008.
