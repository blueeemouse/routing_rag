Barbra Streisand discography

Barbra Streisand is an American actress, singer-songwriter, and author.

 Her discography consists of 117 singles, 35 studio albums, and 9 compilations, 7 live albums and 15 soundtracks.

 She is one of the best-selling female music artists of all time, with more than 68.5 million albums in the United States and with a total of 145 million records sold worldwide, making her the best-selling female artist among the top-selling artists recognized by the Recording Industry Association of America.

 (The only female in the top ten, and the only artist outside of the rock 'n' roll genre.)
