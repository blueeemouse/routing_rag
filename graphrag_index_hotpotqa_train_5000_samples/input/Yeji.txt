Yeji

Yeji is a town in south Ghana and is the capital of Pru District of the Brong-Ahafo Region in Ghana.

 Yeji is in the north-east of Brong-Ahafo Region, and is adjacent to Lake Volta.

 Yeji is connected by highway to Ejura and Kwadjokrom.

 It had a population of 29,515 at the census of 2010.
