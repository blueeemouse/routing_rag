Bade Achhe Lagte Hain

Bade Achhe Lagte Hain (English: "It Seems So Beautiful" ; Hindi: बड़े अच्छे लगते हैं ; ] ) is a Hindi-language Indian television soap opera which was broadcast by Sony Entertainment Television India from 30 May 2011 to 10 July 2014, when it ended its run after telecasting 644 episodes.

 Based on the Gujarati play "Patrani" by Imtiaz Patel, the soap opera was created by Ekta Kapoor and was produced by her production company Balaji Telefilms.

 The name of the soap opera, along with its title track, has been inspired by a song of the same name, composed by R.D. Burman, from the soundtrack of the 1976 Bollywood film "Balika Badhu".

 Ekta Kapoor had got the name, "Bade Achhe Lagte Hain", registered about six years before the soap opera premiered.

 "Bade Achhe Lagte Hain" is the seventh most watched television show of 2011, in India.
