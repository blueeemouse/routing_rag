Superman in film

The fictional character Superman, an American comic book superhero in DC Comics publications, has appeared in movies almost since his inception.

 He debuted in cinemas in a series of animated shorts beginning in 1941, and then starred in two movie serials in 1948 and 1950.

 An independent studio, Lippert Pictures, released the first Superman feature film, "Superman and the Mole Men", starring George Reeves, in 1951.
