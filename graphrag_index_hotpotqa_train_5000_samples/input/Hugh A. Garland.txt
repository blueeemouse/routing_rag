Hugh A. Garland

Hugh A. Garland (June 1, 1805 – October 14, 1854) was an American slaveholder, lawyer and politician.

 He served in the Virginia House of Delegates.

 He was a staunch supporter of slavery in the United States, and he led the defense for Dred Scott's owner, John F. A. Sanford, in the notorious case of Dred Scott v. Sandford, but died two years before the case was argued in court.
