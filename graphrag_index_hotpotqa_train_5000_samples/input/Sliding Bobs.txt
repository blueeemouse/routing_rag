Sliding Bobs

"Sliding Bobs" is the first episode and season premiere of the sixth season of the animated comedy series "Bob's Burgers" and the overall 89th episode; it is written by Greg Thompson and directed by Don MacKinnon.

 It aired on Fox in the United States on September 27, 2015.

 In the episode, Bob begins to notice that he is losing hair from his moustache, and Linda tells how important his moustache was in their meeting.

 The kids then tell their own versions of how Bob and Linda would have met, if Bob didn't have a moustache at that time.
