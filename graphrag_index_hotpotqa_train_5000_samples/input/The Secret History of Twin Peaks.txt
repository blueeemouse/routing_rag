The Secret History of Twin Peaks

The Secret History of Twin Peaks is an epistolary (dossier-style) novel by Mark Frost, which provides background information on the history of the fictional town and characters from the television series "Twin Peaks".

 The book was published in October 2016, several months before the debut of a new season of the series in May 2017.

 A follow-up book, "Twin Peaks: The Final Dossier", also written by Frost, will be released after the new series has aired.
