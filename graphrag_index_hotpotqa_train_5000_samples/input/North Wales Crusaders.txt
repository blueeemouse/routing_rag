North Wales Crusaders

North Wales Crusaders R.L.F.C. (Welsh: "Croesgadwyr Gogledd Cymru Rygbi'r Gynghrair" ) is a professional rugby league club based in Wrexham, Wales.

 They are the successors to the former Super League club Crusaders Rugby League.

 Crusaders compete in Kingstone Press League 1, the third tier of European rugby league (behind the Super League and Kingstone Press Championship).

 Until the end of 2016 they played their home games at the Racecourse Ground in Wrexham.

 From 2017 they are based at the Queensway Stadium (also in Wrexham) but will also play several games at Hare Lane in Chester.
