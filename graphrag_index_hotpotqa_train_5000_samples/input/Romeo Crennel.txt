Romeo Crennel

Romeo Crennel (born June 18, 1947) is an American football coach who is the assistant head coach  for the Houston Texans of the National Football League (NFL).

 Though he has spent much of his career as a defensive assistant, he has also been the head coach of the Cleveland Browns and the Kansas City Chiefs.

 On January 20, 2014, he became the defensive coordinator for the Houston Texans.

 He was promoted to assistant head coach prior to the 2017 season.
