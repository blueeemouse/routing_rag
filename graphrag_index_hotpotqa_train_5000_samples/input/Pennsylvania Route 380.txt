Pennsylvania Route 380

Pennsylvania Route 380 (officially, SR 400 because of I-380 elsewhere in Pennsylvania), also known as J.F. Bonetto Memorial Highway and within the city of Pittsburgh Bigelow Boulevard, Baum Boulevard and Frankstown Road, is a 32.80 mi long state highway in western portions of the U.S. state of Pennsylvania.

 The western terminus of the route is at Interstate 579 in downtown Pittsburgh near PPG Paints Arena.

 The eastern terminus is at Pennsylvania Route 286 in Bell Township, near the hamlet of Wakena.
