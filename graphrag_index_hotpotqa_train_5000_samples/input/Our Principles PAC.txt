Our Principles PAC

Our Principles PAC was a super PAC established in January 2016.

 Its goal was to oppose Donald Trump's campaign for president.

 Our Principles PAC was led by Katie Packer, who was deputy campaign manager of Mitt Romney's 2012 campaign.

 The month of the Iowa caucuses, the group sent out mailers to voters in the state, attacking Trump.

 In March, it released a video attacking Trump, featuring women reading some of his sexist statements about women.
