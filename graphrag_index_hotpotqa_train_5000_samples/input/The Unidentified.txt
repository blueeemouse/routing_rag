The Unidentified

The Unidentified is a 2008 drama film by writer-director Kevan Tucker in his directorial debut for Floodgate Features.

 It won "Best New Director" at the 2008 Brooklyn International Film Festival and "Best First Feature" at the 2008 FirstGlance Film Festival in Philadelphia.

 It also screened at the Asheville Film Festival and Methodfest Film Festival in 2008, and is available on DVD through Vanguard Cinema.

 It is considered part of the mumblecore movement.
