Fulletby

Fulletby is a village and a civil parish in the East Lindsey district of Lincolnshire, England.

 It is situated in the Lincolnshire Wolds, and 3 mi north-east from Horncastle, 9 mi south from Louth, and 8 mi north-west from Spilsby.

 The parish covers approximately 1950 acre .

 At the time of the 2011 census the population remained less than 100 and is included in the civil parish of Low Toynton.
