Passing Glory

Passing Glory is a 1999 basketball-drama film produced for TNT, written by Harold Sylvester, and directed by Steve James.

 This movie stars Andre Braugher, Rip Torn, and Sean Squire.

 The film also features a speaking role by Arthur Agee, subject of the documentary "Hoop Dreams", also directed by Steve James.

 Music composed by Stephen James Taylor.
