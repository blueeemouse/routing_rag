Antonio Nicaso

Antonio Nicaso (born 1964, in Calabria, Italy) is an Italian author, university professor, researcher, speaker and consultant to governments and law-enforcement agencies now based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

 He is an expert on the Calabrian mafia (known as 'Ndrangheta).

 Nicaso lives and works in North America.

 He teaches courses on "Social History of Organized Crime in Canada" and "Mafia Culture and the Power of Symbols, Rituals and Myth" at Queen's University, in Kingston, Ontario in Canada.

 He also teaches at St. Jerome's University in Waterloo, Ontario and the Italian School of Middlebury College in Oakland, California in the United States and is the co-director of the Research in Forensic Semiotics Unit at Victoria College (University of Toronto).
