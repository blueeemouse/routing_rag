Shinobu Otake

Shinobu Otake (大竹 しのぶ , Ōtake Shinobu , born 17 July 1957) is a Japanese actress.

 She has won three Japanese Academy Awards: the 2000 Best Actress award for "Poppoya", and the 1979 awards for both Best Actress ("The Incident") and Best Supporting Actress ("Seishoku no ishibumi").

 She also won the award for best actress at the 12th Hochi Film Award for "Eien no 1/2".

 At the 25th Moscow International Film Festival she won the award for Best Actress for her role in "Owl".

 She has received a total of 12 nominations.
