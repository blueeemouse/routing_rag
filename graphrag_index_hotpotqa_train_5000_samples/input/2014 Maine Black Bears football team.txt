2014 Maine Black Bears football team

The 2014 Maine Black Bears football team represented the University of Maine in the 2014 NCAA Division I FCS football season.

 They were led by 22nd-year head coach Jack Cosgrove and played their home games at Alfond Stadium.

 They were a member of the Colonial Athletic Association.

 They finished the season 5–6, 4–4 in CAA play to finish in a four-way tie for fifth place.
