Henry Street (Manhattan)

Henry Street is a street in the Lower East Side of the New York City borough of Manhattan that runs one-way eastbound, except for a small two-way segment west of Pike Street.

 It spans from Oliver Street in the west (locally called "south" because it is towards downtown), passing underneath the Manhattan Bridge and on to Grand Street in the east ("north").

 The street is named for Henry Rutgers, a hero of the American Revolutionary War and prominent philanthropist.

 Rutgers Street, which intersects with Henry Street, is also named for him.
