Prime Minister of Albania

The Prime Minister of Albania (Albanian: "Kryeministri i Shqipërisë" ), officially styled the Prime Minister of the Republic of Albania (Albanian: "Kryeministri i Republikës së Shqipërisë" ), is the head of government of Albania and as well the most powerful and influential person in Albanian politics.

 The Prime Minister holds the executive power of the nation and represents the Council of Ministers and chairs its meetings.
