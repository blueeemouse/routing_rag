Van Zant

Van Zant is an American musical duo composed of brothers Donnie Van Zant and Johnny Van Zant.

 Both are brothers of the late Ronnie Van Zant, former lead singer for the Southern rock band Lynyrd Skynyrd.

 Johnny became the lead vocalist for the reunited Lynyrd Skynyrd in 1987, and Donnie was also the leader and vocalist of .38 Special.
