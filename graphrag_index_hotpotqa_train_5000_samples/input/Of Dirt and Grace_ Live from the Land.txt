Of Dirt and Grace: Live from the Land

Of Dirt and Grace: Live from the Land is the thirteenth live album from Australian contemporary worship music band Hillsong United, a worship band from Hillsong Church.

 The album was released on 15 July 2016, under Hillsong Music, Sparrow Records and Capitol Christian Music Group.

 Touted as a "visual album", the record features new arrangements of their fourth studio album, "Empires" (2015), which were performed live and filmed around various locations in Israel.

 The album was also released in DVD on 29 July 2016.

 "Prince of Peace" was released as the album's lead single in July 2016.
