McCartney II

McCartney II is the second completely solo studio album by Paul McCartney, and the first since the formation of Wings in 1971.

 It was released in 1980, a year before the band's dissolution and while their future lay in limbo.

 The album is a significant departure for McCartney, as much of it relies heavily on synthesizers and studio experimentation.

 It was reissued on 13 June 2011 as part of the "Paul McCartney Archive Collection".

 The album remains a cult favourite.
