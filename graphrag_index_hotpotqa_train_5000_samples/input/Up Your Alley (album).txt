Up Your Alley (album)

Up Your Alley is the sixth studio album by the American rock band Joan Jett and the Blackhearts.

 It was originally released in May 1988, on the labels Blackheart and CBS-Sony in the U.S., and Polydor in (Europe and Japan), a year and a half after their previous album "Good Music".

 This album contains the single "I Hate Myself for Loving You", which peaked at No. 8 on the "Billboard" Hot 100, and had been used as the theme song for "Sunday Night Football" NFL games in America (with altered lyrics, by two singers) during the 2006 and 2007 seasons.

 The follow-up single "Little Liar" continued Jett's chart success, peaking at No. 19 on the Hot 100 in late 1988/early 1989.
