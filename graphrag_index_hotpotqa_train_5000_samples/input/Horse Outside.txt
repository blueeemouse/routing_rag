Horse Outside

"Horse Outside" is a song by Irish comedy group The Rubberbandits.

 It was released on 8 December 2010, after its accompanying music video was aired on RTÉ Television programme "Republic of Telly".

 The video gained almost 2.5 million views in ten days on video sharing site YouTube and, as of January 2017, has over 15 million views.

 It was targeted to become the Irish Christmas number one single of 2010, ultimately finishing second.
