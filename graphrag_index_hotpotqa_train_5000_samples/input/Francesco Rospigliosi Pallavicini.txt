Francesco Rospigliosi Pallavicini

Francesco Rospigliosi Pallavicini (March 2, 1828 – January 14, 1887) was an Italian politician.

 He was born in Rome, in what was then the Papal States.

 He was the 2nd mayor of Rome from 1871 to 1873.

 He served in the Senate of the Kingdom of Italy.

 He died in Rome, Italy.
