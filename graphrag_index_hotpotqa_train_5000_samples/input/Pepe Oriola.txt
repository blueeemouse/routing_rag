Pepe Oriola

Josep "Pepe" Oriola Vila (born 9 July 1994) is a Spanish racing driver.

 He became the youngest driver to race in the World Touring Car Championship when he competed in the 2011 season.

 As of 2014, he will act as a driver coach for Campos Racing's drivers competing in the European Touring Car Cup and the World Touring Car Championship.

 He will also compete in the last three rounds of the 2014 World Touring Car Championship season, that will be held in China, Japan and Macau.
