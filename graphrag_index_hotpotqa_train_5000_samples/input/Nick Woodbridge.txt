Nick Woodbridge

Nicholas Lloyd "Nick" Woodbridge (born 1 July 1986) is a British modern pentathlete.

 The modern pentathlon includes the disciplines of shooting, swimming, fencing, equestrian, and cross country running.

 Woodbridge competed for Great Britain at the 2008 Summer Olympics, in Beijing, China, and finished 25th in the men's modern pentathlon.

 He also competed at the 2012 Summer Olympics.

 where he finished in 10th place.

 In 2013 he achieved his best career result when he took the silver medal in the World Championships.
