On a Storyteller's Night

On a Storyteller's Night is the fifth studio album by the English rock band Magnum, released in 1985 on FM Records in the UK and on Polydor Records in Germany.

 The album is widely considered as Magnum's breakthrough album.

 This album features one of Tony Clarkin's most thoughtful songs, "Les Morts Dansants" — a simple song in that paints a picture of a man being executed by a firing squad.

 It is the way in which the song accomplishes this, with some very colorful and imaginative metaphors.

 The song was covered in 1987 by the American singer Patty Smyth of the band Scandal on her first solo album "Never Enough", but because of its French title the song was retitled "Call To Heaven".
