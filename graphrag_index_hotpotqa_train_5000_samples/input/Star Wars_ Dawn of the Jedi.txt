Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi

Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi is an American comic book series set in the "Star Wars" universe.

 The series, published by Dark Horse Comics, is written by John Ostrander and illustrated by Jan Duursema.

 It is set in the earliest days of the Jedi Order, beginning in 36,453 BBY (Before the Battle of Yavin).

 A preview issue, #0, was released on February 1, 2012, and followed by the first issue on February 15, 2012.

 Dawn of the Jedi ended after the third arc, since Marvel Comics acquired the Star Wars comic license in 2015.

 The miniseries concluded with the last issue Force Wars #5 having been released March 19, 2014.
