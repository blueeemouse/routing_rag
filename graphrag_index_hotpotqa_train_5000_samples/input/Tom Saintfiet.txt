Tom Saintfiet

Tom Saintfiet (born 29 March 1973 in Mol, Belgium) is a Belgian UEFA PRO licensed association football manager and former player.

 He played football between 1983 and 1997 (FC Boom) before becoming a football manager at the age of 24, becoming the youngest manager in Belgian football.

 He was appointed as the coach of the Trinidad and Tobago national team on 7 December 2016, before resigning after only 4 games on 10 January 2017.
