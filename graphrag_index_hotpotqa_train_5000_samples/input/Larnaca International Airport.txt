Larnaca International Airport

Larnaca International Airport (Greek: Διεθνής Aερολιμένας Λάρνακας "Diethnís Aeroliménas Lárnakas" Turkish: "Larnaka Uluslararası Havaalanı" ) (IATA: LCA, ICAO: LCLK) is an international airport located 4 km southwest of Larnaca, Cyprus.

 Larnaca International Airport is Cyprus' main international gateway and the largest of the country's two commercial airports, the other being Paphos International Airport on the island's southwestern coast.

 In July 2016 Larnaca airport was renamed "Larnaca International Airport - Glafcos Clerides” in honour of former President of the Republic Glafcos Clerides.
