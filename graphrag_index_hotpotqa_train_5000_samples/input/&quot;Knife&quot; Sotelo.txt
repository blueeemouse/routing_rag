&quot;Knife&quot; Sotelo

Marvin Sotelo, (born March 3, 1987), known professionally as "Knife" Sotelo or Knifer, is an American rapper, martial artist and author from Los Angeles California.

 Sotelo is a devout LaVeyan Satanist and politically liberal.

 He has written several books on different aspects of Satanism and donated to a number of Satanic candidates and causes.

 In 2015 he made an unsuccessful run for a seat on the United States House of Representatives.
