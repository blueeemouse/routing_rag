Texas Longhorns football under Mack Brown

William Mack Brown (born August 27, 1951) is the former head coach of the University of Texas Longhorn football team.

 Through 2008 the Texas Longhorns football team under Mack Brown have had a winning season for all eleven seasons since Brown took over the program for the 1998 season.

 As of 2008, they have won at least ten games in each of the past eight seasons; that is the longest active streak in the nation.
