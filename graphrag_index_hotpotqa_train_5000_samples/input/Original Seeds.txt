Original Seeds

Original Seeds: Songs that inspired Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds is a compilation album by Various Artists, which was initially released in June 1998.

 It was re-titled as "Original Seeds Vol 1: Songs that inspired Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds" when the follow-up "Original Seeds Vol 2: Songs that inspired Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds" appeared in 2004.

 Both appeared on the Rubber Records label in Australia and the United Kingdom.
