Ahmadullah Alizai

Ahmadullah Alizai (born 1972) is a politician in Afghanistan, currently serving as the Advisor Minister of President in Local Governnance and have worked as the governor of Badghis & the Governor of Kabul Province in Afghanistan.

 He previously served as the deputy governor of Kabul Province, deputy governor of Nangarhar Province, and as director of Counter-Narcotic directorate for the south-west zone.
