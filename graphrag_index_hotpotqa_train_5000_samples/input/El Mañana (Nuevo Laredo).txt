El Mañana (Nuevo Laredo)

El Mañana ("Early Morning") is a Spanish language newspaper published in Nuevo Laredo, Tamaulipas, Mexico.

 The newspaper was founded in 1924 by Heriberto Deandar Amador, it is Nuevo Laredo's oldest newspaper currently still published.

 El Mañana uses the slogan "La verdad sin fronteras" ("The truth without boundaries").

 El Mañana is also circulated in Laredo, Texas, United States.
