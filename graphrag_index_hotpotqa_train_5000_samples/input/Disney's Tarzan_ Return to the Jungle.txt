Disney's Tarzan: Return to the Jungle

Disney's Tarzan: Return to the Jungle is an action platformer video game developed by Digital Eclipse Software and released by Activision for Game Boy Advance in 2002.

 The game is based on the 2001-2003 animated series The Legend of Tarzan.

 In this game, Tarzan and Terk are lost, and they have to go back to protect the jungle from Queen La.
