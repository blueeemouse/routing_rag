Anne E. Pusey

Anne Elizabeth Pusey is director of the Jane Goodall Institute Research Center and a professor of evolutionary anthropology at Duke University.

 Since the early 1990s, Pusey has been archiving the data collected from the Gombe chimpanzee project.

 The collection housed at Duke University consists of a computerized database that Pusey oversees.

 In addition to archiving Jane Goodall’s research from Gombe, she is involved in field study and advising students at Gombe .
