Mike Bell (third baseman)

Michael John Bell (born December 7, 1974) is a Class-A minor league baseball coach for the Visalia Rawhide, former coach of the Yakima Bears, and former third baseman in Major League Baseball who played for the Cincinnati Reds in the 2000 season.

 He is the brother of David Bell, son of Buddy Bell and grandson of Gus Bell.

 Bell is a graduate of Moeller High School.
