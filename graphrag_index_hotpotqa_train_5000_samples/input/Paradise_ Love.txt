Paradise: Love

Paradise: Love (German: Paradies: Liebe ) is a 2012 drama film directed by Ulrich Seidl.

 It tells the story of a 50-year-old white woman who travels to Kenya as a sex tourist.

 The project is an Austrian production with co-producers in Germany and France.

 It is the first installment in Seidl's Paradise trilogy, a project first conceived as one film with three parallel stories.
