Prince Victor of Thurn and Taxis

Prince "Victor" of Thurn and Taxis (German: "Viktor Theodor Maximilian Egon Maria Lamoral Prinz von Thurn und Taxis" ; 18 January 1876, Ecska – 28 January 1928, Vienna, Austria ) was a member of the Princely House of Thurn and Taxis and a Prince of Thurn and Taxis.
