Le Beau Mariage

Le Beau Mariage ("The Good Marriage") is a 1982 French film directed by Éric Rohmer, starring Béatrice Romand, André Dussollier, Féodor Atkine.

 It is one of Rohmer's "comedies and proverbs" ("comédies et proverbes").

 Its theme is a proverb from La Fontaine: "Quel esprit ne bat la campagne ?

 qui ne fait château en Espagne ?"

 (that is, "Who doesn't daydream?

 Who doesn't build castles in Spain").
