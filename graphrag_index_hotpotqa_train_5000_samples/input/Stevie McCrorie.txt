Stevie McCrorie

Stevie McCrorie (born 23 March 1985) is a Scottish singer-songwriter.

 He is best known as a firefighter who won the fourth series of the BBC television singing competition "The Voice UK" in 2015.

 He auditioned with Kodaline's "All I Want", with all four coaches turning their chairs for him.

 His debut single, "Lost Stars", was released the day following his win and reached number six on the UK Singles Charts, number one in Scotland and eighty-seven in Ireland.

 Since winning 'The Voice', he has performed at T in The Park.
