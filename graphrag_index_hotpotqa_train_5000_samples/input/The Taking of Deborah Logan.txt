The Taking of Deborah Logan

The Taking of Deborah Logan is a 2014 American found footage supernatural horror film and the feature film directorial debut of Adam Robitel, written by Robitel and co-writer Gavin Heffernan.

 The film stars Jill Larson, Anne Ramsay, and Michelle Ang.

 Set in Virginia, it tells the story of a documentary crew making a film about Alzheimer's patients who uncover something sinister while documenting a woman who suffers from the disease.

 The film was produced by Jeff Rice and Bryan Singer and was released on October 21, 2014.
