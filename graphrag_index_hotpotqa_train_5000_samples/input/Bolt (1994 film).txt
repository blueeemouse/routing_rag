Bolt (1994 film)

Bolt is a 1994 drama film starring Richard Grieco in the title role as a New Jersey biker.

 After fleeing west to escape a gang war, Bolt becomes romantically involved with Native American Patty Deerheart (Sean Young), and is compelled to battle to protect her family from a land runner.

 The appearance of former gang rival Billy Niles (Michael Ironside) on the reservation causes the conflict to escalate into a violent climax.

 It was released on DVD as Rebel Run in 1999.
