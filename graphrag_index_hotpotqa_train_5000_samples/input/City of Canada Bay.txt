City of Canada Bay

The City of Canada Bay is a local government area in the Inner West of Sydney, New South Wales, Australia.

 The city was formed on 1 December 2000, following the merger of Concord and Drummoyne councils.

 The city covers an area of 19.82 km2 and as at the 2016 census had a resident population of .

 The city is ultimately named after Canada Bay, a bay on the Parramatta River.
