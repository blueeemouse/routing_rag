South African cheetah

The South African cheetah ("Acinonyx jubatus jubatus"), also known as the Namibian cheetah, is the most numerous and the nominate cheetah subspecies native to Southern Africa.

 Since 1986, it has been classified as Vulnerable by the IUCN.

 The South African cheetah live mainly in the lowland areas and deserts of the Kalahari, the savannahs of Okavango Delta and the grasslands of the Transvaal region in South Africa.

 In Namibia, cheetahs are mostly found in farmlands.

 The South African cheetah was first described by the German zoologist Johann Christian Daniel von Schreber under the trinomen "Felis jubatus jubatus" in the Dutch Cape Colony in 1775.
