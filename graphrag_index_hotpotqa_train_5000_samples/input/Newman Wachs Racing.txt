Newman Wachs Racing

Newman Wachs Racing (also referred to as NWR) is an auto racing team owned by Eddie Wachs and, until his death, Paul Newman.

 NWR was based outside of Chicago in Mundelein, Illinois.

 Newman and Wachs met when they both competed in Can-Am series in the mid-1970s and over the years talked about joining forces to create a driver development race team.

 They formed a Champ Car Atlantic team for the 2006 season to meet their objectives.
