Never Surrender (song)

"Never Surrender" is a song by Canadian singer Corey Hart.

 It was released in June 1985 as the first single from his second album, "Boy in the Box".

 The song was number-one for four weeks in Canada and was Hart's highest charting single in the United States, reaching number three on the "Billboard" Hot 100 (topping the sales only chart for one week) in August 1985.
