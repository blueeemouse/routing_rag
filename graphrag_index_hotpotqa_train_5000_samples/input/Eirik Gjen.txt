Eirik Gjen

Eirik Bodding Gjendemsjø (born 27 June 1995, Kristiansund, Norway), better known by his stage name Eirik Gjen, is a Norwegian singer.

 He has been a soloist in the public celebration of King Harald's 70-year day, he has sung for Michelle Obama, he has participated in the choir for Andrea Bocelli and he has participated in the choir for Morten Harket and Diana Ross in the Nobel Peace Prize concert in 2007 and 2008.

 All of those achievements were gained before the age of fifteen.
