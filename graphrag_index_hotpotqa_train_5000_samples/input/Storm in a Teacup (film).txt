Storm in a Teacup (film)

Storm in a Teacup is a 1937 British romantic comedy film starring Vivien Leigh, Rex Harrison in his first starring role, Cecil Parker, and Sara Allgood.

 It is based on the German play "Sturm im Wasserglas" by Bruno Frank, as well as the English-language adaptations: London's "Storm in a Teacup" and Broadway's "Storm Over Patsy", both written by James Bridie.

 A reporter writes an article that embarrasses a politician.

 Meanwhile, the newspaperman is also attracted to his target's daughter.
