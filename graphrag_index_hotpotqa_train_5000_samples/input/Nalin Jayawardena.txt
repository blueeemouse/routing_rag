Nalin Jayawardena

Nalin Jayawardena (Sinhala : නලින් ජයවර්ධන) (born 18 April 1957) is a popular Sri Lankan singer and vocalist.

 He was the first Sri Lankan singer to release an internet based audio album in Sinhala allowing his fans from around the world to download the album free of charge.

 This album titled Kanda Paamule was released in 2004.

 Nalin Jayawardena lives in Perth, Australia.
