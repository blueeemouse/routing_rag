Daybreak (1931 film)

Daybreak is a 1931 American Pre-Code drama film directed by Jacques Feyder and written by Cyril Hume and Ruth Cummings.

 The film stars Ramon Novarro, Helen Chandler, Jean Hersholt, C. Aubrey Smith, William Bakewell and Karen Morley.

 The film was released on May 2, 1931, by Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer.
