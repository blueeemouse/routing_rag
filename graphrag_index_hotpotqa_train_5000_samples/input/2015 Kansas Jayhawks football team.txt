2015 Kansas Jayhawks football team

The 2015 Kansas Jayhawks football team represented the University of Kansas in the 2015 NCAA Division I FBS football season.

 The Jayhawks were led by first year head coach David Beaty.

 The team played their home games at Memorial Stadium.

 The Jayhawks finished the season 0–12, 0–9 in conference play to finish in last place in the Big 12 Conference, and winless for the second time in school history.
