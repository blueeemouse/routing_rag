Macquarie Dictionary

The Macquarie Dictionary is a dictionary of Australian English.

 It is generally held by universities and the legal profession to be the authoritative source on Australian English.

 It also pays considerable attention to New Zealand English.

 Originally it was a publishing project of Jacaranda Press, a Brisbane educational publisher, for which an editorial committee was formed, largely from the Linguistics department of Macquarie University in Sydney, Australia.

 It is now published by Macquarie Dictionary Publishers an imprint of Pan Macmillan Australia Pty Ltd.

 In October 2007 it moved its editorial office away from Macquarie University to the University of Sydney.
