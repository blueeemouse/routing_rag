Outpost in Morocco

Outpost in Morocco is a 1949 action adventure film directed by Robert Florey, starring George Raft and Marie Windsor.

 Paul Gerard (George Raft), a Moroccan Spahi officer and his French Foreign Legion garrison, holds off attacks from the native tribes of the Emir of Bel-Rashad (Eduard Franz), the father of Cara (Marie Windsor), the woman he loves.

 As a rarity amongst American films of the Foreign Legion genre, the Legion cooperated with the producers.

 A second unit led by Robert Rossen filmed scenes in Morocco.

 Some of the large scale action scenes of the film were reused in "Fort Algiers" and "Legion of the Doomed".
