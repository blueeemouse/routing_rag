All the Queen's Men

All the Queen's Men is a 2001 English-language German-Austrian action comedy war film.

 It was directed by Stefan Ruzowitzky and stars Matt LeBlanc and Eddie Izzard.

 Made on a budget of $15mn, the film was widely panned by critics, and it earned only $23,662 in the United States, yielding an approximate -99.92% return.
