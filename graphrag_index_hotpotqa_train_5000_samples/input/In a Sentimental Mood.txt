In a Sentimental Mood

"In a Sentimental Mood" is a jazz composition by Duke Ellington that is also performed as a song.

 Ellington composed the piece in 1935 and recorded it with his orchestra the same year.

 Lyrics were later written for the tune by Manny Kurtz; as was the custom of the day, Ellington's manager Irving Mills cut himself in for a percentage of the publishing, the song credited to all three.
