Harrods Creek, Louisville

Harrods Creek is a neighborhood of Louisville, Kentucky centered near Harrods Creek at the intersection of River Road and Wolf Pen Branch Road.

 It is roughly bordered by the Ohio River to the west and US 42 to the east.

 Its ZIP code is 40027.

 Formerly an unincorporated community, it was designated a neighborhood of Louisville when the city merged with Jefferson County in 2003.
