Dementium II

Dementium II is a first person, survival horror video game developed for the Nintendo DS.

 It is the sequel to 2007's "", also for the Nintendo DS.

 The game was developed by Renegade Kid and published by SouthPeak Games.

 It was released in 2010 and features many improvements over its predecessor "", including different weapons, a larger variety of enemies, the ability to jump and crouch, save points, more environments, an improved map system and the removal of respawning enemies.

 Weapons include shank, revolver, sledge hammer, shotgun, assault rifle, dynamite, flame thrower, nail gun, and relic.

 Items include flashlight, ammo for any of the weapons, adrenaline shots, pills, and medkits.

 A remastered version of the game was released for Microsoft Windows and Mac OS X on December 17, 2013.
