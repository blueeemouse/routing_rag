We Married Margo

We Married Margo is a 2000 American independent comedy film directed by J. David Shapiro and co-written by Shapiro and William Dozier.

 The film tells a story of two friends who were married to the same woman and is loosely based on real events in that Shapiro and Dozier actually met after dating the same woman named Margaux.

 The film was awarded the "Audience Award for Comedy Film of the Year" at the 2000 The Comedy Festival (formerly known as the "US Comedy Arts Festival") and was nominated for the "Grand Jury Prize" at the Slamdance Film Festival the same year.
