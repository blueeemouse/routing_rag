Admiral of the fleet (Russia)

Admiral of the fleet or fleet admiral (Russian: aдмирал флoта , "admiral flota") is the highest naval (deck) rank of the Russian Federation.

 It is the equivalent of the Soviet naval rank of admiral of the fleet and the present Russian rank of general of the army.

 The rank is roughly equivalent to 4-star admiral ranks of other nations.

 Marshal of the Russian Federation is the only superior rank in the Russian armed forces.
