German Hound

The German Hound ("Deutsche Bracke") (FCI No. 299) is a breed of dog originating in Westphalia, a region of Germany.

 The German Hound is of the scenthound type, used for hunting both large and small game.

 The breed is normally referred to as the "Deutsche Bracke" in English, rather than by the translation of the name, German Hound.
