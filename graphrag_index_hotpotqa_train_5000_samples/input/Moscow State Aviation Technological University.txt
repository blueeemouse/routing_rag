Moscow State Aviation Technological University

Moscow State Aviation Technological University is a university in Moscow, Russia.

 The modern name of this university is «MATI» - Russian State University of Aviation Technology (Russian: Российский государственный технологический университет им.

 К.Э.Циолковсокого ).

 It is named after the Russian aeronautics and rocketry pioneer Konstantin Eduardovich Tsiolkovskii.
