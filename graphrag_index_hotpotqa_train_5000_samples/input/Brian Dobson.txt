Brian Dobson

Brian Dobson (born September 9, 1973) is an English-Canadian voice actor, who works for Ocean Studios and various other studios in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada.

 He is best known for voicing Ben "The Thing" Grimm in "", Skeletor in "He-Man and the Masters of the Universe", Majin Buu and Dr. Gero in the Ocean dub of "Dragon Ball Z", Red Alert in "" and "", Muso in "InuYasha", and Martin DaCosta in "Mobile Suit Gundam SEED" and "SEED Destiny".

 He is the brother of Michael Dobson and Paul Dobson.
