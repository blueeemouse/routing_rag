Robin Reid (cyclist)

Robin Neil Reid (born 16 December 1975 in Invercargill) is a former road racing cyclist from Blenheim, New Zealand who was a New Zealand time trial champion and Olympian.

 He won the Tour de Pakistan race in 2007.

 There he met the German-born former professional female cyclist Britta Martin, who won the female sections of the event and has been together with her ever since.

 End of 2007 they moved in together in Nelson, New Zealand.

 Britta Martin competes since 2008 as a professional triathlete mainly at the Ironman distance.
