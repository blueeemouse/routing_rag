679 (song)

"679" is the second single by American rapper Fetty Wap from his self-titled debut album.

 The song features Remy Boyz member Monty and former Remy Boyz member P-Dice.

 "679" peaked at number 4 on the US "Billboard" Hot 100, becoming his second highest-charting single after "Trap Queen".

 The album version of the song omits P-Dice's verse, only featuring Monty.
