Al Shearer

Al Shearer (born August 14, 1977) is an American actor.

 He portrayed Nevil Shed in the 2006 Disney blockbuster "Glory Road (film)", produced by Jerry Bruckheimer.

 Shearer might be best known for his role on the MTV series Punk'd, or as "Hits" - host of the once-popular BET series "Hits from the Street".
