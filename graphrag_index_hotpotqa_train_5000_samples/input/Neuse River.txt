Neuse River

The Neuse River is a river rising in the Piedmont of North Carolina and emptying into Pamlico Sound below New Bern.

 Its total length is approximately 275 mi , making it the longest river entirely contained in North Carolina.

 The Trent River joins the Neuse at New Bern.

 Its drainage basin, measuring 5630 sqmi in area, also lies entirely inside North Carolina.

 It is formed by the confluence of the Flat and Eno rivers prior to entering the manmade, artificial Falls Lake reservoir in northern Wake County.

 Its fall line shoals, known as the "Falls of the Neuse", lie submerged under the waters of Falls Lake.
