Sully Díaz

Sully Diaz (July 12, 1960; New York City) is a Spanish actress and singer born to Sephardic parents from, Puerto Rico.

 Sully's career started in Puerto Rican television with her first starring role as Coralito in the "novela" called "Coralito".

 "Coralito" was her first starring role.

 Sully was invited to star in various soap operas in Puerto Rico, Venezuela and Argentina.
