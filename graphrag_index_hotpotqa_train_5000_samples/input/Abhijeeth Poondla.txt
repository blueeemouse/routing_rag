Abhijeeth Poondla

Abhijeeth Poondla is an Indian actor and model.

 He is known for his portrayal of Jai, a lead role in the film "LBW (Life before Wedding)" (2011).

 He played a lead role in "Chandamama Kathalu" (an anthology) released in 2014 and also played the lead in a British Asian feature film "Nice 2 meet U", a romantic drama on interracial relationships that was screened at the 2014 Cannes Film Festival.
