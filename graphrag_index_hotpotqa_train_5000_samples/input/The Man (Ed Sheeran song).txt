The Man (Ed Sheeran song)

"The Man" is a song by English singer-songwriter Ed Sheeran.

 It features production from frequent collaborator Jake Gosling.

 The song was released as an "instant grat" digital download to the iTunes Store on 19 June 2014, serving as the sixth of seven promotional singles from his second studio album, "x" (2014).

 It entered the UK Singles Chart at number 87.
