Distance (Christina Perri song)

"Distance" is a song by American singer-songwriter Christina Perri.

 The new version of the track featuring Jason Mraz is the third official single taken from the deluxe version of her debut studio album "Lovestrong" (2011) released on March 20, 2012.

 It was written by Perri and co-written and produced by David Hodges.

 It is a midtempo ballad about "loving someone at the wrong time in your life" and "being around that one whom your heart longs for without being able to show your true feelings".
