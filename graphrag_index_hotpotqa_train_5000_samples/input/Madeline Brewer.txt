Madeline Brewer

Madeline Brewer (born May 1, 1992) is an American actress, best known for her roles in the Netflix original series "Orange Is the New Black" and "Hemlock Grove".

 Prior to being cast in "Orange Is the New Black", Brewer was crowned Miss Pitman in 2010.

 In 2016, she appeared in "Men Against Fire", an episode of the anthology series "Black Mirror".
