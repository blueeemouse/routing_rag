Gouldsboro State Park

Gouldsboro State Park is a 2880 acre Pennsylvania state park in Coolbaugh Township, Monroe County and Lehigh Township, Wayne County, Pennsylvania in the United States.

 The park includes the 250 acre Gouldsboro Lake.

 Gouldsboro State Park is located very close to Tobyhanna State Park and Pennsylvania State Game Lands 127 and 312.

 It is on Pennsylvania Route 507 near the small village of Gouldsboro.
