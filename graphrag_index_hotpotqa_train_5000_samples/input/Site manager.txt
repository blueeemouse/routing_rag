Site manager

In the construction industry, site managers, often referred to as construction managers, site agents or building managers, are responsible for the day-to-day on site running of a construction project.

 Site managers are required to keep within the timescale and budget of a project, and manage any delays or problems encountered on-site during a construction project.

 Also involved in the role is the managing of quality control, health and safety checks and the inspection of work carried out.

 Many site managers will be involved before site activity takes place, and are responsible for managing communications between all parties involved in the on-site development of the project.

 Site managers are often required to deal with inquiries and communication with the public.

 Typically a site manager is employed by a construction company, contractor or civil engineering firm but are often employed by local authorities to oversee the refurbishment of council owned properties.
