Dylan Moran

Dylan William Moran ( ; born 3 November 1971) is an Irish comedian, writer, actor and filmmaker.

 He is best known for his observational comedy, the television sitcom "Black Books" (in which he starred and co-wrote) and his work with Simon Pegg in "Shaun of the Dead" and "Run Fatboy Run".

 He appeared as one of the two lead characters in the Irish black comedy titled "A Film with Me in It" in 2008.
