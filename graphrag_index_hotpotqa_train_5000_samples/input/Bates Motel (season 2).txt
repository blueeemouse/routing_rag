Bates Motel (season 2)

The second season of "Bates Motel" consisted of 10 episodes and premiered on A&E on March 3, 2014.

 The season aired on Mondays at 9 p.m. ET/PT on A&E, and concluded on May 5, 2014.

 The series itself is described as a "contemporary prequel" to the 1960 film "Psycho" and follows the life of Norman Bates and his mother Norma in the fictional town of White Pine Bay, Oregon prior to the events portrayed in the Hitchcock film.
