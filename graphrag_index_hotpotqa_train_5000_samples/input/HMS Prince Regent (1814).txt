HMS Prince Regent (1814)

HMS "Prince Regent was a 56-gun British warship that served on Lake Ontario during the War of 1812.

 "Prince Regent" was built at the Kingston Royal Naval Dockyard in Kingston, Upper Canada and launched on 14 April 1814.

 Rated as a fourth-rate frigate, "Prince Regent" took part in the Raid on Fort Oswego in 1814.

 Following the War of 1812 the frigate was renamed HMS "Kingston on 9 December 1814.

 In 1817, the vessel was placed in reserve following the Rush-Bagot Treaty that demilitarized all the lakes along the United States-Canada border.

 Discarded in 1832, the vessel found no buyer and sank in Deadman Bay off Kingston after 1832.
