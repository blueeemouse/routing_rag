Erol Togay

Erol Togay (1 February 1950 – 9 August 2012) was a former player of the Turkey national football team and Fenerbahçe SK, former caretaker coach of the Fenerbahçe football team.

 He was also played for Vefa (1968–1972), Altay (1972–1978) and Adana Demirspor (1981–1984).

 Togay played for Turkey in five qualifying matches for the 1978 FIFA World Cup.
