KMOT

KMOT, channel 10, is the NBC-affiliated television station for Minot, North Dakota.

 It broadcasts a high definition digital signal on VHF channel 10 (or virtual channel 10.1 via PSIP) from a transmitter at its studios on the intersection of 16th Street SW and 18th Avenue SW in Minot.

 The station can also be seen on Midcontinent cable channel 7 in the Minot area, cable channel 10 in most other areas and SRT cable channel 9.

 There is a high definition feed provided on Midcontinent digital channel 607 and SRT digital channel 509.
