Columbus Panhandles

The Columbus Panhandles were a professional American football team based in Columbus, Ohio.

 The club was founded in 1901 by workers at the Panhandle shops of the Pennsylvania Railroads.

 They were a part of the Ohio League from 1904 before folding after one season.

 Three years later, the team tried again and playing the Ohio League from 1907 to 1919, not winning a championship, before becoming charter members of the National Football League (NFL)—firstly named American Professional Football Association (APFA).
