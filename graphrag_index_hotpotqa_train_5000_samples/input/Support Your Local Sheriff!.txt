Support Your Local Sheriff!

 (a.k.a.

 The Sheriff) is a 1969 American Technicolor comedy western film distributed by United Artists, directed by Burt Kennedy, produced by William Bowers (who also wrote the screenplay) and Bill Finnegan.

 The film stars James Garner, Joan Hackett, Walter Brennan, Harry Morgan, Jack Elam and Bruce Dern.
