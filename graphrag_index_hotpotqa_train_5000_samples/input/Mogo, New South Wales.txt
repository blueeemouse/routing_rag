Mogo, New South Wales

Mogo (postcode: 2536) is a small heritage town in the South Coast region of the state of New South Wales, Australia.

 Mogo is administered by the Eurobodalla Shire council.

 The town is located on the Princes Highway (Highway 1), south of Batemans Bay and north of Moruya.

 At the 2016 census , Mogo had a population of 322.
