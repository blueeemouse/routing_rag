Yohio

Kevin Johio Lucas Rehn Eires (born July 12, 1995), better known by his stage name Yohio (ヨヒオ , stylized as YOHIO) , is a Swedish singer and songwriter currently active in Japan.

 He is best known for his performances as Yohio, wearing a lolita dress in previous years, with an androgynous appearance on stage.

 He is a former member of the Swedish rock band Seremedy, which disbanded in April 2013.

 Yohio has participated in Melodifestivalen both in 2013 and 2014, making it to the final on both occasions.

 He is one of the co-founders and current CEO of Keios Entertainment.
