Life and Times (Jim Croce album)

Life and Times is the fourth studio album by American singer-songwriter Jim Croce, released in 1973.

 (See 1973 in music.)

 The album contains the #1 "Billboard" chart hit "Bad, Bad Leroy Brown", one of Croce's most successful songs.

 Croce was nominated for two 1973 Grammy awards in the "Pop Male Vocalist" and "Record of the Year" categories for the song "Bad, Bad Leroy Brown".
