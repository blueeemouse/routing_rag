The Guardians (2017 TV series)

The Guardians (Hangul: 파수꾼 ; RR: "Pasuggun "; lit.

 Lookout ) is a South Korean television series starring Lee Si-young, Kim Young-kwang, Kim Tae-hoon, Kim Seul-gi and Key.

 The drama aired on MBC on Mondays and Tuesdays at 22:00 (KST) from May 22 to July 11, 2017.
