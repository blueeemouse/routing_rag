Scandalous!

"Scandalous" is the eighth track on Prince's soundtrack album "Batman" and was released as the album's fourth single, five months after the album was released.

 The music is attributed to Prince and his father, John L. Nelson.

 A maxi-single was released after the single, titled "The Scandalous Sex Suite", which contained a three part 19 minute suite of the song Scandalous, the three parts named "The Crime", "The Passion" and "The Rapture".

 Kim Basinger, who played the character Vicki Vale in "Batman", also appeared on the maxi-single.
