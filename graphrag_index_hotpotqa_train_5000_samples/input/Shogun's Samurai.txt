Shogun's Samurai

Shogun's samurai (柳生一族の陰謀 , Yagyū Ichizoku no Inbō ) , also known as "The Yagyu Clan Conspiracy" and "Intrigue of the Yagyu Clan", is a 1978 Japanese historical martial arts period film directed by Kinji Fukasaku.

 The film is the first of two period films by Fukasaku starring Shinichi "Sonny" Chiba as Jūbei Mitsuyoshi Yagyū, the other being "Samurai Reincarnation".

 The film was adapted into a teleplay called The Yagyu Conspiracy (柳生一族の陰謀 , Yagyū Ichizoku no Inbō ) that was broadcast by Kansai Telecasting Corporation for 39 one-hour TV episodes from 1978 to 1979.
