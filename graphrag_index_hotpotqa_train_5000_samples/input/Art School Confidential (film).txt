Art School Confidential (film)

Art School Confidential is a 2006 comedy-drama film directed by Terry Zwigoff, loosely based on the comic of the same name by Daniel Clowes.

 The film is Zwigoff's second collaboration with Clowes, the first being 2001's "Ghost World" (which was also released by United Artists).

 The cast includes Max Minghella, Sophia Myles, John Malkovich, Jim Broadbent, Matt Keeslar, Ethan Suplee, Joel Moore, Nick Swardson, Adam Scott, and Anjelica Huston.
