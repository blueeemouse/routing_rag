York Dispatch Newspaper Offices

The York Dispatch Newspaper Offices is a historic commercial building located at York, Pennsylvania, York County, Pennsylvania.

 It was built in 1887, and is a four-story, four bay brick building in the Italianate Revival style.

 The building consists of two independent sections connected by an overhead walkway.

 The front facade is built of cast iron and pressed metal and features decorative pilasters and long, slender windows.
