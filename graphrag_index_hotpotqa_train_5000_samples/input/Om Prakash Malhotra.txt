Om Prakash Malhotra

General Om Prakash "OP" Malhotra, PVSM (6 August 1922 – 29 December 2015) was an Indian Army General.

 He was the 13th Chief of Army Staff of the Indian Army from 19781981.

 He also served as the Ambassador of India to Indonesia from 19811984, and Governor of Punjab and Administrator of Chandigarh from 19901991.
