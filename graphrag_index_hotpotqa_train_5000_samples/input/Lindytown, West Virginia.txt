Lindytown, West Virginia

Lindytown is an unincorporated community in Boone County, West Virginia, United States.

 Lindytown was founded at the time Charles A. Lindbergh made his famous flight across the Atlantic and is based on a 50 acre tract of land.

 Lindytown is approximately 22 mi from Madison.

 Lindytown is accessible from Boone County Route 26, which is located right off of West Virginia Route 85 at the Van Bridge split.
