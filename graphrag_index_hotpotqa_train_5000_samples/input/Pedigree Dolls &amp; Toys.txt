Pedigree Dolls &amp; Toys

Pedigree Dolls & Toys, also known as Pedigree Toys, is a toy company located in Exeter, England.

 Best known for launching the Sindy doll in 1963, Pedigree also produced the Tommy Gunn action figure from 1966 to 1968.

 The Sindy doll was very popular from the 1960s to 1980s, and accounted for 80% of Pedigree's sales.
