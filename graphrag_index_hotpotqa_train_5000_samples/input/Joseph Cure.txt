Joseph Cure

Joseph O'Connell Cure (December 10, 1984 – November 8, 2015) was an American ice hockey player and actor.

 Cure made his film debut in Walt Disney Pictures' "Miracle" in 2004.

 Cure, a hockey player himself, was cast as Mike Ramsey, the youngest member of the "Miracle on Ice" U.S. ice hockey team at the 1980 Winter Olympics.
