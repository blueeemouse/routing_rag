A Little Less Talk and a Lot More Action

"A Little Less Talk And A Lot More Action" is a song written by Keith Hinton and Jimmy Alan Stewart, and recorded by American country music singer Hank Williams, Jr. for his 1992 album "Maverick".

 One year later, the song was recorded by American country music singer Toby Keith and released in November as the third single from his self-titled debut album.

 Keith's version peaked at number 2 on the Billboard Hot Country Singles & Tracks (now Hot Country Songs) chart and peaked at number 25 on the Canadian "RPM" country tracks.
