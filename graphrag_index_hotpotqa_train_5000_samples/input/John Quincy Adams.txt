John Quincy Adams

John Quincy Adams ( ; July 11, 1767 – February 23, 1848) was an American statesman who served as a diplomat, United States Senator, member of the House of Representatives, and the sixth President of the United States from 1825 to 1829.

 He was a member of the Federalist, Democratic-Republican, National Republican, and later the Anti-Masonic and Whig parties.

 He was the son of President John Adams and Abigail Adams and thus contributed to the formation of the Adams political family.
