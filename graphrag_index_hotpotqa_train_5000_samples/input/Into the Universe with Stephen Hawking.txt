Into the Universe with Stephen Hawking

Into the Universe with Stephen Hawking is a 2010 science documentary television mini-series written by British physicist Stephen Hawking.

 The series was created for Discovery Channel by Darlow Smithson Productions and features computer generated imagery of the universe created by Red Vision.

 The series premiered on 25 April 2010 in the United States and started on 9 May 2010 in the United Kingdom with a modified title, Stephen Hawking's Universe (not to be confused with the 1997 PBS series by the same name).

 In the series, Hawking appears on camera but, for the sections where there is voice-over, Benedict Cumberbatch, who played Hawking in a 2004 TV film about the scientist, performs the narration as Hawking himself.
