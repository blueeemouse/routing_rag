Ich hab' Dich lieb (song)

"Ich hab' Dich lieb" ("English": I love you) is the second hit single released by Schnuffel, on 4 July 2008 by Sony BMG Germany (Sony BMG).

 and is the first track in the homonym Album, Ich hab' Dich lieb and Ich hab' Dich lieb Gold Edition.

 The CD-single sold over 50.000 copies.

 The single is present in a digital release called "Häschenparty - Famous 5"(24 October 2008) as the 4th track.
