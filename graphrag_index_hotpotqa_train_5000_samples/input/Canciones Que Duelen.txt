Canciones Que Duelen

Canciones Que Duelen ("Songs That Hurt") is the fourth studio album by Mexican recording artist Espinoza Paz.

 It was released by Disa Records on September 27, 2011, one year after the release of his previous studio album.

 Espinoza Paz worked as producer and songwriter of all the tracks included.

 The album entered at number one in the "Billboard"' s Latin Albums and Regional Mexican Albums charts, while peaking atop the albums chart in Mexico.

 The first single, "Para No Perderte", reached the top twenty of the "Billboard" Top Latin Songs chart.

 A DVD with music videos to all the songs is included on the deluxe edition of the album.
