Suncook Lakes

The Suncook Lakes are a pair of lakes located in Belknap County in central New Hampshire, United States, in the town of Barnstead.

 Upper Suncook Lake encompasses 402 acre , while Lower Suncook Lake covers 295 acre .

 The lakes are connected by a 1000 ft channel, spanned by a road bridge.

 A dam at the outlet of Lower Suncook Lake controls the water level of both lakes.

 The lakes are located along the Suncook River, a tributary of the Merrimack River.

 There are three islands on Lower Lake.
