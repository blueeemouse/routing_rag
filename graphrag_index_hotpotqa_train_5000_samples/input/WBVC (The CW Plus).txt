WBVC (The CW Plus)

"WBVC" is the cable TV only CW-affiliated television station for the Northern Lower and Eastern Upper Peninsulas of Michigan.

 The call sign is fictional as it is a cable-only station.

 It is part of The CW Plus and can be seen exclusively on Charter.

 It is seen on cable channel 61 in Cadillac, Cheboygan, Gaylord, Manistee, Petoskey, Sault Ste. Marie, Traverse City, and surrounding areas.

 It is also seen on cable channel 17 in Big Rapids, Evart, Ludington, and Reed City and cable channel 13 in Grayling and Roscommon.

 DirecTV customers get The CW via its Green Bay affiliate WCWF, while DISH Network doesn't get a CW affiliate.

 AcenTek in Mesick, which is one of very few non-Charter cable systems in the Traverse City market, gets CW via WWMT-DT2.
