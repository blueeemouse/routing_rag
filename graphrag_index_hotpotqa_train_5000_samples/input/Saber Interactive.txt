Saber Interactive

Saber Interactive Incorporated is an American video game developer founded in 2001 by Andrey Iones, Matthew Karch and Anton Krupkin in Millburn, New Jersey.

 Now headquartered in Maplewood, New Jersey, it has additional offices in Saint Petersburg (Russia), Madrid (Spain), and Kiev (Ukraine).

 The studio's debut title was "Will Rock", which was released in 2003 by Ubi Soft.
