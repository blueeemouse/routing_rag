Derrybrusk

Derrybrusk is a civil parish and townland (of 204 acres) in County Fermanagh, Northern Ireland.

 The civil parish is mainly situated in the historic barony of Tirkennedy with a small portion (the two townlands of Aghnacarra and Gola) in the barony of Magherastephana.

 Derrybrusk townland is in the portion of the parish of the same name in Tirkennedy.
