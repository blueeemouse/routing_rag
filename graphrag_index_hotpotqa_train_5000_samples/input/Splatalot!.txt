Splatalot!

 is a Canadian/Australian/British internationally co-produced medieval-themed physical game show for kids, which made its debut in its English Canadian premiere on Monday, March 14, 2011 on YTV, where it is hosted by Jason Agnew and Matt Chin.

 The Australian and UK broadcasts of the show use the same filmed footage as the original Canadian show, but with their own local presenters providing commentary.

 The show premiered in the UK on CBBC on June 13, 2011, presented by Dick and Dom.

 The show premiered in Australia on ABC3 on November 5, 2011, hosted by Kayne Tremills and Scotty Tweedie.

 The series is a copy of "Wipeout", designed for children's audiences.
