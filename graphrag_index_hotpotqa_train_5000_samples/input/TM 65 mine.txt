TM 65 mine

The TM 65 (Telamiina 65) is a circular Finnish minimum metal anti-tank blast mine.

 It is broadly based on the Russian "TM-62 mine".

 The mines body is made from a thin shell of fibreglass over a cast block of explosive, with a central fuze well that holds the pressure fuze.

 The original "TM 65" used a "US-65" fuze as used in the "SM-65 anti-personnel mine", the later TM 65 77 uses a pressure fuze similar to the Russian "MV-5" fuze called "Painesytytin 77".
