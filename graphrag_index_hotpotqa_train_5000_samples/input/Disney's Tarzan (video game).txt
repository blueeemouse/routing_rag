Disney's Tarzan (video game)

Disney's Tarzan (also known as Tarzan Action Game) is an action platformer video game developed by Eurocom and published by Sony Computer Entertainment for the PlayStation in 1999 based on the Disney animated film "Tarzan".

 Konami published the game for its Japanese release.

 It was also released on the PC and Game Boy Color in 1999, and Nintendo 64 in 2000.
