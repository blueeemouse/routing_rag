Seven Tour

Seven Tour is a concert tour by British singer Lisa Stansfield, in support of her 2014 album "Seven".

 During the tour Stansfield performed her hit songs along the new tracks from her latest album, including "Can't Dance," "Stupid Heart," "Conversation" and others.

 The tour started on 16 May 2013 in Paris, France and ended on 4 November 2014 in Riga, Latvia.

 Stansfield played over 60 concerts in Europe.

 The show at the Bridgewater Hall in Manchester on 7 September 2014 was recorded for a forthcoming live DVD due for release on 25 August 2015.

 It is titled "Live in Manchester".
