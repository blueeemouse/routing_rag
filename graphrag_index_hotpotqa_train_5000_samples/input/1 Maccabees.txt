1 Maccabees

1 Maccabees is a book of the Bible written in Hebrew by a Jewish author after the restoration of an independent Jewish kingdom by the Hasmonean dynasty, about the latter part of the 2nd century BC.

 The original Hebrew is lost and the most important surviving version is the Greek translation contained in the Septuagint.

 The book is held as canonical scripture by the Catholic, Orthodox, and Oriental Orthodox churches (except for the Orthodox Tewahedo), but not by Anglican and Protestant denominations.

 Such Protestants consider it to be an apocryphal book (see also Deuterocanon).

 In modern-day Judaism, the book is often of great historical interest, but has no official religious status.
