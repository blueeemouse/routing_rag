Two Lanes of Freedom

Two Lanes of Freedom is the twelfth studio album by American country music artist Tim McGraw.

 It was released February 5, 2013, as his first album for Big Machine Records following a 20-year tenure with Curb Records.

 He co-produced the album with Byron Gallimore, producer of his previously released albums.

 The album includes the singles "Truck Yeah", "One of Those Nights", "Highway Don't Care" featuring new labelmate Taylor Swift, and "Southern Girl".
