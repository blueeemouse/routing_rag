Torture (The Jacksons song)

"Torture" is the second single released off the album "Victory" by the band The Jacksons.

 Written by Jackie Jackson and fellow Motown veteran Kathy Wakefield, the song is about someone ending a relationship and the torture that a person can receive while trying to end it.

 Jackie was originally going to sing the song with his brother, Michael, but Jackie's role instead went to Jermaine Jackson, whose availability for the album was in question until the last minute.

 The rest of the Jacksons sang the chorus along with Michael and Jermaine.
