I Love the '80s (UK TV series)

I Love the '80s is a BBC television mini-series that examines the pop culture of the 1980s.

 It was commissioned following the success of "I Love the '70s" and is part of the I Love... series.

 "I Love 1980" premiered on BBC Two on 13 January 2001 and the last, "I Love 1989", on 24 March 2001.

 Unlike with "I Love the '70s", episodes were increased to 90 minutes long.

 The series was followed later in 2001 by "I Love the '90s".

 The success of the series led to VH1 remaking the show for the US market: I Love the '80s USA, which is known simply as "I Love the '80s" in the US itself.
