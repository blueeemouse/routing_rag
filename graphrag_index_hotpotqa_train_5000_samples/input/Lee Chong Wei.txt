Lee Chong Wei

Dato' Lee Chong Wei {'1': ", '2': ", '3': 'DSPN DB DCSM PJN', '4': "} (born 21 October 1982) is a Malaysian professional badminton player.

 As a singles player, Lee was ranked first worldwide for 199 consecutive weeks from 21 August 2008 to 14 June 2012.

 He is the fourth Malaysian player after Rashid Sidek, Roslin Hashim and Wong Choong Hann to achieve such a ranking (since official rankings were first kept in the 1980s), and is the only Malaysian shuttler to hold the number one ranking for more than a year.
