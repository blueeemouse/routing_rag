For All I Am

For All I Am is an American metalcore band from Chicago, Illinois, originally known as “I, Artificial”.

 The band was formed in 2011.

 They currently don't have a record label, management, or booking agent.

 They released an EP called "Lone Wolf" in 2011.

 Their debut LP "Skinwalker" was released on January 22, 2013 and their album "No Home" was released on October 14, 2014.

 For All I Am was named as one of the "100 bands you need to know in 2012" by Alternative Press and has been featured in Rock Sound, Outburn, Hails & Horns, AMP, Substream Music Press magazines and more.
