Peter Barkworth

Peter Wynn Barkworth (14 January 1929 – 21 October 2006) was an English actor.

 He twice won the BAFTA TV Award for Best Actor; for "Crown Matrimonial" in 1975 and for "Professional Foul" and "The Country Party" in 1978.

 He also starred in the ITV series "Manhunt" (1970) and the BBC series "Telford's Change" (1979).

 His film appearances included "Where Eagles Dare" (1968), "Patton" (1970), "International Velvet" (1978) and "Champions" (1984).
