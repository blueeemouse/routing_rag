Mama Said (Metallica song)

"Mama Said" is a power ballad by American heavy metal band Metallica from their sixth album "Load", with music and lyrics by James Hetfield (music credited to Hetfield/Ulrich).

 The lyrics represent a man or boy who is learning to find his own way in life away from his mother.

 The song is directly written about Hetfield's difficult relationship with his mother, who died of cancer when he was 16 years old.
