Days Like These

Days Like These is a British TV remake of the popular American sitcom "That '70s Show".

 Directed by Bob Spiers, it was broadcast Fridays at 8.30 pm on ITV in 1999 and used many of the same names (Eric Forman, Kitty Forman), or slight alterations (Donna Palmer instead of Donna Pinciotti, Jackie Burget instead of Jackie Burkhart, etc.).

 It was set in the real-life town of Luton, England.

 Only 10 of the 13 produced episodes were aired.
