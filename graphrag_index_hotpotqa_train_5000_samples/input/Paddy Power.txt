Paddy Power

Paddy Power is an Irish bookmaker founded in 1988 in Dublin, Ireland.

 The company conducts business through a chain of licensed betting shops in Ireland and the United Kingdom, and by operating Ireland's largest telephone betting service.

 On the internet, it offers sports betting, online poker, online bingo, online casino and online games.

 It merged with Betfair to create Paddy Power Betfair on 2 February 2016.
