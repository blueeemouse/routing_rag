Dewan Farid Gazi

Dewan Farid Gazi (1924 – 19 November 2010) was a Bangladeshi politician born in Devpara, Nabiganj Upazila, Sylhet.

 He was elected as Member of Parliament for the Habiganj-1 (Nabigonanj-Bahuball) constituency in 1996, 2001 and 2008, representing the Awami League, of which he was an Advisory Council member.

 He served as chairman of the Parliamentary Standing Committee on the Ministry of Primary and Mass Education.
