2008 LPGA Tour

The 2008 LPGA Tour was a series of weekly golf tournaments for elite female golfers from around the world that took place from February through December 2008.

 The tournaments were sanctioned by the United States-based Ladies Professional Golf Association (LPGA).

 In 2008, prize money on the LPGA Tour was $60.3 million, the highest to date in the history of the tour.
