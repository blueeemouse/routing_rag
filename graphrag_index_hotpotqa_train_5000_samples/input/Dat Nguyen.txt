Dat Nguyen

Dat Tan Nguyen ( ; Vietnamese: "Nguyễn Tấn Đạt" , ] ) (born September 25, 1975) is a former football player, who was a linebacker in the National Football League (NFL) for seven seasons.

 He played college football for Texas A&M University, and earned All-American honors.

 He played professionally for the NFL's Dallas Cowboys and became the first Vietnamese-American to be drafted, play, and be recognized as an All-Pro in the NFL.

 In 2017, he was elected to the College Football Hall of Fame.
