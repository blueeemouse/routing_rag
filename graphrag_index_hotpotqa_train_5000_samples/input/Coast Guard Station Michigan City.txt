Coast Guard Station Michigan City

Coast Guard Station Michigan City is a United States Coast Guard station located in Michigan City, Indiana.

 The station is the only Coast Guard Station located on Lake Michigan in Indiana.

 Station Michigan City is a multi-mission unit that conducts missions in search and rescue, maritime law enforcement, homeland security and marine environmental protection.

 The station responds to an average of 125 search and rescue cases a year, assisting nearly two million dollars in property.
