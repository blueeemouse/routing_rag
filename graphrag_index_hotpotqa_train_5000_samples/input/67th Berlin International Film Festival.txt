67th Berlin International Film Festival

The 67th annual Berlin International Film Festival was held from 9 February to 18 February 2017 with Dutch filmmaker Paul Verhoeven as President of the Jury. "

Django", directed by Etienne Comar, opened the festival.

 The Golden Bear was awarded to the Hungarian film "On Body and Soul" directed by Ildikó Enyedi, which also served as closing film of the festival.
