Bee Gees' 1st

Bee Gees' 1st is the third studio album by English band Bee Gees, and their first international full-length recording after two albums distributed only in Australia and New Zealand.

 "Bee Gees' 1st" was the group's debut album for the UK Polydor label, and for the US Atco label.

 "Bee Gees 1st" was released on 14 July 1967 in the UK.

 On 9 August it entered the UK charts, on that same day, the album was released in the US, and it entered the US charts on 26 August.

 After its release, the band became more self-sufficient, producing and mixing many of their own releases.
