Edit (Regina Spektor song)

"Edit" is an Anti-folk/Indie rock song from Anti-folk singer Regina Spektor, released in the summer of 2006 on the album "Begin to Hope".

 The line "You don't have no Doctor Robert/You don't have no Uncle Albert" references the Beatles' song "Doctor Robert" as well as Paul and Linda McCartney's 1970 hit "Uncle Albert".

 "Edit" was covered by British anti-folk band The Red Army.
