Hozier (musician)

Andrew Hozier-Byrne (born 17 March 1990), known professionally by the mononym Hozier, is an Irish musician, singer and songwriter from County Wicklow.

 He released his debut EP, featuring the hit single "Take Me to Church", in 2013 and his second EP, "From Eden", in 2014.

 His debut studio album, "Hozier", was released in Ireland in September 2014 and globally in October 2014.
