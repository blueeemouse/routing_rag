Ashworth family

The Ashworth family are a fictional family in the long-running Channel 4 soap opera, "Hollyoaks".

 Introduced to the show in 2005, the family derives from Manchester and moved to Hollyoaks village in 2005.

 The characters have been involved in storylines such as secret siblings, anorexia, drug abuse, incest and paternity issues.

 With Phoebe McQueen, the deceased Rhys Ashworth's adoptive daughter`s recent murder in June 2015, there are officially no longer any Ashworth family members left in Hollyoaks, besides Darren Osborne, who between 2009 until 2011 was married to Hannah Ashworth; though, their marriage ended in divorce.
