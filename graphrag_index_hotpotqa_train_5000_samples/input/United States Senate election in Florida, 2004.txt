United States Senate election in Florida, 2004

The 2004 United States Senate election in Florida took place on November 2, 2004 alongside other elections to the United States Senate in other states as well as elections to the United States House of Representatives and various state and local elections.

 Incumbent Democratic U.S. Senator Bob Graham decided to retire instead of seeking a fourth term.

 The primary elections were held on August 31, 2004.

 Republican Mel Martínez won the open seat.
