Veera (1994 film)

Veera is a Tamil romantic comedy film directed by Suresh Krissna and music by Ilaiyaraaja.

 It stars Rajinikanth, Meena and Roja in the lead roles.

 The movie was dubbed in Telegu with the same title.

 It was a remake of superhit Telugu movie "Allari Mogudu" starring, Mohan Babu, Ramya Krishnan and Meena.

 It was a blockbuster and completed 150-day run at the box office.
