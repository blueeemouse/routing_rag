Kidulthood

Kidulthood (rendered as KiDULTHOOD) is a 2006 British drama film about the life of several teenagers in Ladbroke Grove and Latimer Road, two areas of inner west London.

 It was directed by Menhaj Huda and written by Noel Clarke, who also stars in the film and directed the sequel "Adulthood".

 The majority of the characters in the film generally behave in a violent and lawless manner, engaging in crime, sex, and recreational drug taking.
