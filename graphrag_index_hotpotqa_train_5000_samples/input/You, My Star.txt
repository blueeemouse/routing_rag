You, My Star

"You, My Star" (별, 그대 ; "Byeol, Geudae " ) is a song by South Korean musician Jung Yong-hwa of CNBLUE.

 Written in dedication to his fans, the song was released in December 23, 2013, under FNC Entertainment.

 Two days prior, Jung performed the song on the year-end music program "SBS Gayo Daejeon".

 "You, My Star" debuted and peaked at number 22 no South Korea's national Gaon Digital Chart; all proceeds went to the label's charity Love FNC.
