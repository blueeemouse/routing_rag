Strictly Come Dancing (series 7)

Strictly Come Dancing returned for its seventh series on 18 September 2009 on BBC One.

 Bruce Forsyth and Tess Daly returned as presenters of the main show on BBC One, whilst Claudia Winkleman presented spin-off show "" on BBC Two.

 Len Goodman, Bruno Tonioli and Craig Revel Horwood returned as judges.

 Series 5 winner Alesha Dixon joined the judging panel as a replacement for Arlene Phillips.

 Darcey Bussell was a guest judge from the quarter final onwards.
