Mark Lazarus

Mark Lazarus (born 5 December 1938) is an English, retired, professional footballer.

 He played as a right winger and made more than 400 Football League appearances, scoring over 100 goals.

 A prominent Jewish player, he initially chose football over boxing and followed manager Alec Stock first to Leyton Orient and then Queens Park Rangers.

 He transferred to Wolverhampton Wanderers for a club record fee, but due to a clash with manager Stan Cullis, he moved back to QPR after only nine games.

 He then played for Brentford before signing again for Queens Park Rangers.

 In his third stint with QPR he scored the winning goal for the club in the 1967 League Cup Final.

 He moved to Crystal Palace in December 1967.

 He moved back to Leyton Orient, before moving onto non-league football where he saw out his football career.
