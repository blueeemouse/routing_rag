Tony Saletan

Anthony D. "Tony" Saletan is an American folk singer and educator, who is responsible for the modern rediscovery of two of the genre's best-known songs, "Michael Row the Boat Ashore" and "Kumbaya."

 Born and raised in New York City, he attended the Walden School and received his bachelor's and master's degrees from Harvard University.

 For a brief period during his childhood, Saletan's piano teacher was a young Leonard Bernstein.

 He was involved as a teen in the Henry Wallace presidential campaign of 1948, in which original music in the folk style was important.

 Saletan settled in the Boston area, where for several years he appeared on educational television (WGBH), taught music in the Newton, Massachusetts public schools and gave private guitar lessons.

 He also became involved in folk dancing and calling of contra dances. Saletan has often taught at Pinewoods Camp, in Plymouth, Massachusetts.

 Later in life, Saletan moved to Tacoma, Washington.
