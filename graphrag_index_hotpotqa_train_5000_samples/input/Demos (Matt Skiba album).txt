Demos (Matt Skiba album)

Demos is the first full-length album by Alkaline Trio singer/guitarist Matt Skiba.

 It is composed of demos that Skiba has recorded into his computer.

 Skiba released the project on Asian Man Records, which was Alkaline Trio's label for their first two full-length albums, "Goddamnit" and "Maybe I'll Catch Fire".

 Four of the songs from this release, namely "Angel of Deaf," "Haven't You," "How The Hell Did We Get Here," and "Nausea (Cruel and Unusual)," were later rerecorded and released on "Babylon", Skiba's first album with his band The Sekrets.

 On that release, however, "Nausea (Cruel and Unusual)" underwent lyrical alterations and was renamed "Olivia."
