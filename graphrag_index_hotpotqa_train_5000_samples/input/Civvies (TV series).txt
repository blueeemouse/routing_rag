Civvies (TV series)

Civvies is a six-part thriller first broadcast on BBC1 on 22 September 1992.

 The series was written by Lynda La Plante - her first writing contribution for the BBC, after being snatched in a "golden handcuffs" deal following the success of "Prime Suspect".

 The series focuses on Frank Dillon (Jason Issacs), a former Army soldier who finds life outside the army tougher than being in it - and slowly falls under the power of east end gangster Barry Newman (Peter O'Toole), who is looking to hire a trained hitman.

 The complete series was released on DVD on 3 June 2013 on Acorn Media UK.
