Cathy Wilcox

Cathy Wilcox (born 1963) is an Australian cartoonist and children's book illustrator, best known for her work as a cartoonist for "The Sydney Morning Herald" and "The Age" newspapers.

 She has also twice won the Australian Children's Book Council's 'Picture Book of the Year' award.

 In 2007 she won the Walkley Award in Cartooning for a cartoon about Sheikh Taj el-Din al Hilaly's infamous 'uncovered meat' remarks on Australian women.
