Silence = Death

Silence = Death is a 1990 documentary film directed, written and produced by Rosa von Praunheim.

 The film centers on the response of some New York City artists to the AIDS epidemic.

 The interviewed includes East Village artist David Wojnarowicz, poet Allen Ginsberg, graffiti artist Keith Haring (who died from AIDS three months before the movie's release), Peter Kunz, Bern Boyle, and many others.

 It is the first part of von Praunheim and Phil Zwickler's trilogy about AIDS and activism it was followed by "Positive" (the third part, about the AIDS epidemic in Germany, was never released).
