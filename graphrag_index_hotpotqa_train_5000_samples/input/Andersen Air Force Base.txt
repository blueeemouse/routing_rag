Andersen Air Force Base

Andersen Air Force Base (AFB) (IATA: UAM, ICAO: PGUA, FAA LID: UAM) is a United States Air Force base located approximately 4 mi northeast of Yigo near Agafo Gumas in the United States territory of Guam.

 Along with Naval Base Guam, Andersen AFB was placed under the command of Joint Region Marianas on 1 October 2009.

 The two bases are about 30 miles apart at opposite ends of the island.

 Administration offices for Joint Region Marianas are about half-way in between, at Nimitz Hill.
