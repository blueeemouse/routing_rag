Holcomb High School

Holcomb High School is a fully accredited high school, serving students in grades 9-12, located in Holcomb, Kansas, USA.

 The current principal is Rob Schneeberger.

 Holcomb High School (HHS) is the only high school located within the city limits of Holcomb, KS.

 The school colors are black and orange and the enrollment for the 2009-2010 school year is approximately 269 students.
