This Little Wiggy

"This Little Wiggy" is the eighteenth episode of "The Simpsons"<nowiki>'</nowiki> ninth season.

 It originally aired on the Fox network in the United States on March 22, 1998.

 It was written by Dan Greaney and directed by Neil Affleck.

 The episode sees Ralph Wiggum becoming friends with Bart.

 Phil Hartman guest stars as recurring character Troy McClure.
