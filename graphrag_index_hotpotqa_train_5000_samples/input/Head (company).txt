Head (company)

Head N.V. is a Dutch sports and clothing company, which sells alpine sking and tennis equipment.

 The company includes parts of several previously independent companies, including Head Ski Company, founded in Delaware, United States, in 1950; Tyrolia, an Austrian ski-equipment manufacturer; and Mares, an Italian manufacturer of diving equipment.

 Head Ski Company produced one of the first successful metal-wood composite downhill ski, the Head Standard, and one of the first oversized metal tennis rackets.

 The company is headquartered in Amsterdam, Netherlands.
