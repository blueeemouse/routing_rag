Irene Zazians

Irene Zazians (Armenian: Իրեն Զազյանց ; Persian: ایرن زازیانس‎ ‎ ; August 20, 1927 — July 28, 2012), best known as Iren in Iran, was an Iranian-Armenian actress.

 She cooperated with some famous Iranian new wave directors in Iran, both before and after 1979 revolution such as Samuel Khachikian, Amir Naderi, Nosrat Karimi, Masoud Kimiyayi, and Alireza Davood Nejad.

 Her two films after the revolution were banned, The Red Line directed by Kimiyayi and The Reward by Davood Nejad.
