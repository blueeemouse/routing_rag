Stephen Boyd

Stephen Boyd (4 July 1931 – 2 June 1977) was an actor from Glengormley, County Antrim, Northern Ireland.

 He appeared in some 60 films, most notably as Messala in "Ben-Hur" (1959), a role that earned him the Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actor – Motion Picture.

 He received his second Golden Globe Award nomination for "Billy Rose's Jumbo" (1962).
