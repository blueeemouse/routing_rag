Miles M. O'Brien

Miles M. O'Brien (1852 – December 22, 1910) was a banker and former president of the New York City Board of Education, who was born in Ireland.

 His parents were Dr. Miles O'Brien and Fanny Casey O'Brien.

 He came to the United States when he was twelve.

 He was president of the New Amsterdam National Bank, vice-president of the Mercantile National Bank, and director of the American Ice Company.

 His great-great grandson (and namesake) Miles O'Brien is a correspondent for CNN based in New York City.
