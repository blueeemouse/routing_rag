Fozzie Bear

Fozzie Bear is a Muppet character known for his lack of innate and effective comedy skills.

 Fozzie is an orange-brown bear who often wears a brown pork pie hat and a red and white polka dot necktie.

 The character debuted on "The Muppet Show", as the show's stand-up comic, a role where he constantly employed his catchphrase, "Wocka Wocka Wocka!"

 Shortly after telling the joke, he was usually the target of ridicule, particularly from balcony hecklers Statler and Waldorf.

 Fozzie's characterization was developed by Frank Oz, who performed Fozzie until 2000.

 Eric Jacobson has since become the character's principal performer.
