Handel Society of New York

The Handel Society of New York (HSNY) was a New York City based musical organization that presented concert and semi-staged performances of operas and oratorios by George Frideric Handel from 1966-1974.

 The group mainly performed out of Carnegie Hall and was responsible for presenting the American and New York premieres of several works by Handel.

 The ensemble was also the first to record many of these works; releasing several LPs for RCA and Westminster Records.
