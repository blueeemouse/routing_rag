Malcolm Allen (swimmer)

Malcolm James Allen (born 29 May 1973) is a former freestyle swimmer who competed for Australia at the 1996 Summer Olympics.

 He placed 13th in the 400-metre freestyle, and ended up fourth with the men's 4x200-metre freestyle relay team.

 A year earlier, at the 1995 FINA Short Course World Championships in Rio de Janeiro, he won the bronze medal in the 400m Freestyle.
