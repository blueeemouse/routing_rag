The Mission (play)

The Mission: Memory of a Revolution (Der Auftrag: Erinnerungen an eine Revolution), also known as "The Task", is a postmodern drama by the (formerly East) German playwright Heiner Müller.

 The play was written and first published in 1979.

 Müller and his wife Ginka Cholakova co-directed its first theatrical production in 1980, at the intimate 'Theatre im 3.

Stock' studio space of the Volksbühne in Berlin (opening on 16 November).

 Müller also directed a full-house production in 1982 at the Bochum Theatre in West Germany.
