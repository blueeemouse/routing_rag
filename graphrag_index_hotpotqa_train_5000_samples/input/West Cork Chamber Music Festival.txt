West Cork Chamber Music Festival

The West Cork Chamber Music Festival is an internationally recognised music festival established in 1995 which takes place in the small Irish town of Bantry Ireland, usually in July.

 Performers at the festival have included, RTÉ Vanbrugh Quartet, Barry Douglas, Ruby Hughes, Nicola Benedetti, Tanja Becker-Bender and Natalie Clein and work by contemporary composers such as Thomas Larcher and Mathias Hinke.

 The performances take place at venues around the town, most notably at Bantry House and St Brendan's Church.
