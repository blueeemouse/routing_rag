Steve Callaghan

Steve Callaghan is an American screenwriter, producer and voice actor, best known for his work on "Family Guy".

 He is a graduate of the Department of Public Policy at the UCLA School of Public Affairs.

 Callaghan started his career in 1999 as a writers' assistant for "Family Guy", and has since worked as a writer and producer on a variety of televisions shows such as "3 South", "Yes, Dear" and "American Dad!
