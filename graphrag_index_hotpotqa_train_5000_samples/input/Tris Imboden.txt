Tris Imboden

Gregory Tristan "Tris" Imboden (born July 27, 1951) is an American rock and jazz drummer.

 As an educator, he has been a drum clinician and author of tutorial materials.

 As a performer, he has been in studio sessions and on tour with some of the most notable and highest-selling musicians of all time.
