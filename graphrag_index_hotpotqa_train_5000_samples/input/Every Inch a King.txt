Every Inch a King

Every Inch a King is a 2005 fantasy novel by Harry Turtledove, published by ISFiC Press.

 It is a fictional account of the story of Otto Witte, who allegedly spent five days pretending to be the King of Albania.

 The title is a quotation from Shakespeare's "King Lear", Act IV scene 6, wherein the insane King, crowned with weeds, makes futile assertions of his now usurped power.
