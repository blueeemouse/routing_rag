Evan Helmuth

Evan Hershey Helmuth (May 18, 1977 – July 17, 2017) was an American film and television actor.

 Helmuth was best known for his lead role as Father David Keane in the 2012 horror film, "The Devil Inside".

 His other film credits included "Fever Pitch" in 2005, in which he played Jimmy Fallon's best friend, "Ready or Not" in 2009, and "Jobs" in 2013.
