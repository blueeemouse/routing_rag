Katsuyori Shibata

Katsuyori Shibata (柴田勝頼 , Shibata Katsuyori , born November 17, 1979 in Kuwana, Mie) is a Japanese professional wrestler and mixed martial artist.

 In professional wrestling, he is known for his hard hitting offense.

 He is currently working for New Japan Pro-Wrestling, though on indefinite hiatus due to injury.

 In New Japan he is a former three-time NEVER Openweight Champion, one-time IWGP Tag Team Champion and the winner of the 2017 New Japan Cup.

 He is also a former holder of Revolution Pro Wrestling's British Heavyweight Championship.
