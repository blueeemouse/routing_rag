I Will Survive (Billie Jo Spears album)

I Will Survive is the 14th album by Billie Jo Spears an American country artist (born Billie Jean Spears in Beaumont, Texas, United States, January 14, 1937 – December 14, 2011) released in May 1979 on the United Artists label.

 The title track, "I Will Survive", was a cover of the chart topper for Gloria Gaynor in 1978.
