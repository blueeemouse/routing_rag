For Valour (1937 film)

For Valour is a 1937 British comedy film directed by Tom Walls and starring Walls, Ralph Lynn and Veronica Rose.

 It was made at Shepperton Studios, with sets designed by Oscar Werndorff.

 Unlike previous films starring Walls and Lynn, it was based on an original screenplay rather than one of the Aldwych Farces.

 Both Walls and Lynn played dual roles of two Boer War veterans and their son and grandson respectively.

 It was the last time the two actors, who had been one of the most popular film comedy teams of the decade, appeared together on screen.
