Archers of Loaf

Archers of Loaf is an American indie rock band originally formed in Chapel Hill, North Carolina in 1991.

 The group toured extensively and released four studio albums, one compilation, numerous singles and EPs, and a live album which was released after the band broke up in 1998.

 In 2011 the band began a reunion tour that coincided with the reissue of four of its albums by Merge Records.
