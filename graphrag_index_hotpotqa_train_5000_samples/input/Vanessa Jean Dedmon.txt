Vanessa Jean Dedmon

Vanessa Jean Dedmon (born 28 April 1987 in Braunfels, Hesse to Russell and Waltraud Dedmon) was a semifinalist on Germany's third season of "Deutschland sucht den Superstar" (the German Version of "Pop Idol") being voted out by the audience in the Top 3 Show.

 Dedmon was one of the favourites of the jury.
