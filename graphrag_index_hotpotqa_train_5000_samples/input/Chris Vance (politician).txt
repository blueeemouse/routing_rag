Chris Vance (politician)

Chris Vance (born May 1, 1962) is an American politician, a two-term member of the King County Council and a former member of the Washington State Legislature.

 Vance is also a former chair of the Washington State Republican Party.

 He and his wife Ann raised their son and daughter in Auburn, Washington.

 Vance lost his U.S. Senate bid to Patty Murray in 2016.
