Crystal Downs Country Club, Michigan

Crystal Downs Country Club is a private country club and lakefront community in Lake Township, Benzie County, near Frankfort, Michigan, in the United States.

 Designed by golf course architects Alister MacKenzie and Perry Maxwell in 1929, the par-70, 6518 yd course offers views of Lake Michigan and Crystal Lake.

 The Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore lies just to its east.

 The country club and surrounding housing comprise the "Crystal Downs Country Club" census-designated place (CDP) for federal statistical purposes.

 As of the 2010 census, the CDP had a population of 47.
