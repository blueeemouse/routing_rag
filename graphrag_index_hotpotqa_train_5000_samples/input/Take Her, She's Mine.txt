Take Her, She's Mine

Take Her, She's Mine is a 1963 comedy film starring James Stewart and Sandra Dee based on the 1961 Broadway comedy written by Henry Ephron and Phoebe Ephron.

 The film was directed by Henry Koster with a screenplay by Nunnally Johnson.

 It also features an early film score by prolific composer Jerry Goldsmith.

 The character of Mollie, played by Elizabeth Ashley on Broadway and in the film by Sandra Dee, was based on the then 22-year-old Nora Ephron.

 Ashley's performance won her a Tony Award for Best Featured Actress in a Play and served as the launchpad for her career.
