2016 SEC Championship Game

The 2016 SEC Championship Game was played on Saturday, December 3, 2016 in the Georgia Dome in Atlanta, Georgia, and determined the 2016 football champion of the Southeastern Conference (SEC).

 The game was played between the Eastern Division champion, Gators, and Western Division champion Alabama.

 The Eastern Division team was the designated home team, and the game was broadcast nationally by CBS for the 16th consecutive year.

 This was the final SEC Championship Game in the Georgia Dome, which is set to be demolished once the Dome's replacement, Mercedes-Benz Stadium, opens in 2017.

 The title game will move to the new stadium and will remain there through at least 2027.
