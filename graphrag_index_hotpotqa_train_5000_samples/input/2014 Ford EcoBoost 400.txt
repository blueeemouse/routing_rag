2014 Ford EcoBoost 400

The 2014 Ford EcoBoost 400 was a NASCAR Sprint Cup Series stock car race held on November 16, 2014, at Homestead-Miami Speedway in Homestead, Florida.

 Contested over 267 laps, it was the 36th and final race of the 2014 NASCAR Sprint Cup Series season, and the tenth and final race of the Chase for the Sprint Cup.

 This race marked the final NASCAR broadcast for ESPN, ending an eight-year stint covering the sport; as well as the final NASCAR broadcast for Allen Bestwick, who has covered the sport since 1986.

 Next season he will remain with ESPN and ABC calling IndyCar races.

 Kevin Harvick held off Ryan Newman to score the victory and score his first ever championship.

 Chevrolet clinched its twelfth consecutive manufacturers championship.

 Newman was second while Brad Keselowski, Paul Menard, and Jamie McMurray rounded out the top five.

 The top rookies of the race were Kyle Larson (13th), Justin Allgaier (15th), and Austin Dillon (25th).
