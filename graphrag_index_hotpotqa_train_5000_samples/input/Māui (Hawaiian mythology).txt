Māui (Hawaiian mythology)

In Hawaiian mythology, Māui is a culture hero and ancient chief who appears in several different genealogies.

 In the Kumulipo he is the son of ʻ Akalana and his wife Hina-a-ke-ahi (Hina).

 This couple has four sons, Māui-mua, Māui-waena, Māui-kiʻ ikiʻ i and Māui-a-kalana.

 Māui-a-kalana's wife is named Hinakealohaila; his son is Nanamaoa.

 Māui is one of the Kupua.

 His name is the same as that of the Hawaiian island Maui, although native tradition holds that it is not named for him directly, but instead named after the son of Hawaii's discoverer (who was named after Māui himself).
