Nelson Wheatcroft

Nelson Wheatcroft (1852-1897) was an English born actor and drama teacher.

 He famously ran a drama school, at the Charles Frohman Empire Theatre, in the late Victorian era.

 He was married to Adeline Stanhope(Wheatcroft)(1856-1935).

 Their son Stanhope Wheatcroft(1888-1966) was a silent film actor.
