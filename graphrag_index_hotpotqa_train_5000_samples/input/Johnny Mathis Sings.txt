Johnny Mathis Sings

Johnny Mathis Sings is an album by American pop singer Johnny Mathis that was released by Mercury Records on March 10, 1967 and was the last of his 11 studio projects for the label.

 Eight of the twelve tracks were recorded since the completion of his previous LP, "So Nice", while four of the tracks were leftovers from the recording sessions for previous Mercury albums.

 The finished product included a number from Broadway's "The Roar of the Greasepaint—the Smell of the Crowd", a cover of the Beatles hit "Eleanor Rigby", two offerings from songwriters Burt Bacharach and Hal David, and three cuts that originated in film scores but had lyrics added later: the melody for "Strangers in the Night" was written for "A Man Could Get Killed"; "Somewhere My Love" began as "Lara's Theme" from "Doctor Zhivago"; and "Lovers in New York" started out as the instrumental title track from "Breakfast at Tiffany's".
