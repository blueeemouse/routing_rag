Before I Disappear

Before I Disappear is a 2014 American drama film directed by Shawn Christensen.

 The film is a feature-length adaptation of his 2012 Oscar-winning short film, "Curfew".

 The film had its world premiere at South by Southwest Film on March 10, 2014.

 The film was acquired for distribution by IFC Films on August 5, 2014 and released on November 28, 2014.
