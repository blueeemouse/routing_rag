Robert Horwell

Robert "Bob" Horwell is a British actor who was born in Scarborough, North Riding of Yorkshire.

 His father, whom he was named after, was a coal merchant and Audrey, his mother, was also a successful small businesswoman.

 His sisters Angela and Sue run a small designer clothing outlet in his hometown.

 He is best known for having played the role of Nick Neeson in the British soap opera "Coronation Street".

 Horwell has also appeared on stage in many productions including "War Horse", "Henry V" (Royal National Theatre), "Cyrano de Bergerac" (RSC and West End), "Lysistrata" (Sir Peter Hall Company Tour and West End), "Edmond" (Royal National Theatre, alongside Kenneth Branagh), "Alice in Wonderland" (Royal Shakespeare Company).
