Farfalle

Farfalle (] ) are a type of pasta/noodle commonly known as bow-tie pasta.

 The name is derived from the Italian word "farfalla" (butterfly).

 The 'e' at the end of the word is the Italian feminine plural ending, making the meaning of the word "butterflies".

 In the Italian city of Modena, farfalle are known as "strichetti".

 A larger variation of farfalle is known as "farfallone", while the miniature version is called "farfalline".

 Farfalle date back to the 16th century in the Lombardy and Emilia-Romagna regions of Northern Italy.
