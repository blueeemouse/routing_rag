Gloria Gaynor (album)

Gloria Gaynor is the tenth studio album by Gloria Gaynor and her only to be released on Atlantic Records after her contract with Polydor Records expired.

 The lead single release was a cover of The Supremes' "Stop in the Name of Love", followed by the singles "America" and "Tease Me".

 The album failed to garner much attention in either the U.S. or in Europe.
