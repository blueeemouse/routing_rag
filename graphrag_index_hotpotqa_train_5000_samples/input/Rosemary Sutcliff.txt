Rosemary Sutcliff

Rosemary Sutcliff CBE (14 December 1920 – 23 July 1992) was an English novelist best known for children's books, especially historical fiction and retellings of myths and legends.

 Although she was primarily a children's author, the quality and depth of her writing also appeals to adults.

 In a 1986 interview she said, "I would claim that my books are for children of all ages, from nine to ninety."

 Some of her novels were specifically written for adults.
