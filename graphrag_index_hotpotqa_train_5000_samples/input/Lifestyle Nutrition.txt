Lifestyle Nutrition

"Lifestyle Nutrition LLC" is a sports nutrition speciality retailing company, with its head-office in Dubai.

 Founded in the year 1996, the company has since then has embarked on an aggressive expansion plan - which grew into a chain with several outlets across the UAE.

 The company sells health and nutrition related products, including vitamins, protein, branched-chain amino acids (BCAA), creatine, glutamine, meal replacement products, weight loss products and supplements for joint health.
