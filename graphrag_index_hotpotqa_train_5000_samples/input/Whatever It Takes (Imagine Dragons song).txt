Whatever It Takes (Imagine Dragons song)

"Whatever It Takes" is a song by American rock band Imagine Dragons.

 The song was released on May 8, 2017 through KIDinaKORNER and Interscope.

 It is a promotional single from the band's third studio album, "Evolve".

 It also has been the official theme song for WWE's PPV event, Battleground in addition to its inclusion in the video game "Madden NFL 18".
