Barq's

Barq's is an American soft drink.

 Its brand of root beer is notable for having caffeine.

<ref name="http://www.overcaffeinated.org/database/soda-pop/barqs-root-beer.php">Caffeine Database | Caffeine and Ingredients in Barqs Rootbeer, OverCaffeinated.org's Report on the Ingredients in Barq's Rootbeer</ref> Barq's, created by Edward Barq and bottled since the turn of the 20th century, is owned by the Barq family but bottled by the Coca-Cola Company.

 It was known as Barq's Famous Olde Tyme Root Beer until 2012.
