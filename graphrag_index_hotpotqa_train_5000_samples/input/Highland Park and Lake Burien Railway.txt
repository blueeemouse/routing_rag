Highland Park and Lake Burien Railway

The Highland Park and Lake Burien Railway was a branch of the Interurban System that went from Everett to Tacoma in Washington state.

 A branch came off about Spokane Street going west to near the Steel Mill.

 It went up nearly parallel to Delridge Way to White Center then on to Burien to end just north of "Kirks Feedstore".

 It had one passenger car and frequently had a flat car attached for it also carried freight.
