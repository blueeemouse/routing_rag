Video Retrospective

Video Retrospective is the first video album by American rock singer-songwriter Lenny Kravitz, released on November 25, 1991 in VHS format by Virgin Records America due to the success of his second album "Mama Said".

 The album contains all of Kravitz's first music videos filmed from 1989 to 1991 from his first two albums, "Let Love Rule" and "Mama Said".
