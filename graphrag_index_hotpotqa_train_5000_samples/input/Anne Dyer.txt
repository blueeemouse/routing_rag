Anne Dyer

Anne Catherine Dyer (born 1957) is a British Anglican priest and academic administrator.

 Since 2011, she has served as Rector of Holy Trinity Church, Haddington, East Lothian in the Diocese of Edinburgh of the Scottish Episcopal Church.

 Previously, from 2005 to 2011, she was the Warden of Cranmer Hall, Durham, an Evangelical Anglican theological college of the Church of England.
