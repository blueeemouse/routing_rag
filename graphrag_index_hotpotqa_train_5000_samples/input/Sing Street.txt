Sing Street

Sing Street is a 2016 musical coming-of-age comedy-drama film co-written, co-produced and directed by John Carney.

 Starring Ferdia Walsh-Peelo, Lucy Boynton, Maria Doyle Kennedy, Aiden Gillen, Jack Reynor, and Kelly Thornton, the story revolves around a boy starting a band to impress a girl in 1980s Ireland.

 It is an international co-production from Ireland, the United States, and United Kingdom.
