Oscar Polk

Oscar Polk (December 25, 1899 – January 4, 1949) was an American actor, best known for his portrayal as the servant "Pork" in the 1939 film "Gone with the Wind".

 His most memorable scene in that film comes when "Pork" discloses to Scarlett O'Hara, portrayed by Vivien Leigh, the back property taxes on Tara, are $300.

 He was married to Ivy V. Polk (née Ivy Parsons), who also had an uncredited role in "Gone with the Wind".

 Together they had a son, Oscar Polk Jr.
