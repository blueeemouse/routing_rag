Landfall Games

Landfall Games AB (formerly Wilnyls Game Company AB) is a video game developer based in Stockholm, Sweden, best known for developing "Clustertruck", which was published by tinyBuild on 27 September 2016.

 The company was founded by game designer Wilhelm Nylund on 27 May 2015 as Wilnyls Game Company, but changed its name to LandFall Games, trading as Landfall Games, on 14 August 2015.
