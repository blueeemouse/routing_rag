Bergamo

Bergamo (Italian: ]    ; ,    from "berg-heim", the hill-town) is a city in Lombardy, northern Italy, about 40 km northeast of Milan, 30 km from the lakes Como and Iseo, 80 km (50 mi) from the lakes Garda and Maggiore.

 The foothills of the Bergamo Alps begin immediately north of the town.
