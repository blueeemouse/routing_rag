Parts &amp; Labor

Parts & Labor was an American experimental rock/noise rock band.

 The group was formed in 2002 by B.J. Warshaw and Dan Friel in Brooklyn, NY.

 Drummer Joe Wong joined the band in 2007.

 Parts & Labor released five albums, two EPs, one split album (with Tyondai Braxton), and numerous 7"s and compilation tracks.

 Their music is influenced by DIY punk, experimental/psychedelic rock, and lo-fi electronic music.
