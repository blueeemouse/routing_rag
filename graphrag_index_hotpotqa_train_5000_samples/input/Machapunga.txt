Machapunga

The Machapunga were a small Native American tribe of the Algonquian language family, one of a number in the territory of North Carolina.

 Previously they were known under the name Secotan.

 They were a group who had migrated south from the Algonquian peoples of the Powhatan Confederacy in present-day Virginia.

 They are now extinct as a separate tribe.

 They spoke an Algonquian language and historically occupied a coastal area of northeastern North Carolina.
