Chesapeake Energy

Chesapeake Energy Corporation is an American petroleum and natural gas exploration and production company headquartered in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma.

 The company was founded in 1989 by Aubrey McClendon and Tom L. Ward with only a $50,000 initial investment.

 McClendon named the company due to his love of the Chesapeake Bay region.

 Ward later left the company to found SandRidge Energy.
