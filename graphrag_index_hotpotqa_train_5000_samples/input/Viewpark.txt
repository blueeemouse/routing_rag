Viewpark

Viewpark is a small urban development north-east of Uddingston in North Lanarkshire, Scotland.

 Viewpark is adjacent to Tannochside, and is 2 km west of Bellshill.

 Viewpark is often considered a district of Uddingston despite falling under the jurisdiction of a separate local authority.

 It has a population of 15,841 as of 2014, which is quite large for an urban neighbourhood in Scotland, it is a similar size to Bathgate in West Lothian and Calderwood in East Kilbride.
