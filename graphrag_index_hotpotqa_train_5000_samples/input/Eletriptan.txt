Eletriptan

Eletriptan (trade name Relpax, used in the form of eletriptan hydrobromide) is a second generation triptan drug intended for treatment of migraine headaches.

 It is used as an abortive medication, blocking a migraine attack which is already in progress.

 Eletriptan is marketed and manufactured by Pfizer Inc.

 It is sold in the US and Canada under the brand name Relpax, and in several other countries under the brand name Relert.
