Need for Speed (2015 video game)

Need for Speed is an online open world racing video game developed by Ghost Games and published by Electronic Arts, released for PlayStation 4 and Xbox One in November 2015, and released on Origin for Microsoft Windows on March 15, 2016.

 It is the twenty-second installment in the long-running "Need for Speed" series, and is a full reboot of the franchise.

 It marks the series' second eighth generation installment, and is the first "Need for Speed" title to only be on eighth generation hardware.

 It marks the franchise's first return to the import scene and tuner culture focus since the release of 2010's "".
