Fire Bomber

Fire Bomber is a fictional rock band from the anime series "Macross 7" (and related projects such as "Macross 7 Trash", "Macross 7 Encore", and "Macross Dynamite 7").

 In real life, Fire Bomber's music was performed by Yoshiki Fukuyama (as the singing voice and guitar of Basara), Chie Kajiura (as the singing voice of Mylene), and occasionally by Tomo Sakurai (the acting voice of Mylene).
