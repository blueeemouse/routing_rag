Julee Cruise

Julee A. Cruise (born December 1, 1956, Creston, Iowa) is an American singer, songwriter, actress and musician.

 She has recorded four albums, but is probably best known for "Falling", the theme song for the cult US television series "Twin Peaks".

 During the late 1980s and early 1990s, she collaborated with composer Angelo Badalamenti and film director David Lynch, who produced and wrote the lyrics for many of her released songs, and returned in the 2017 return of Twin Peaks.

 Cruise is also a songwriter in her own right.

 She is married to Edward Grinnan, Editor-in-Chief of "Guideposts" magazine and best-selling author of "The Promise of Hope" and "Always By My Side".
