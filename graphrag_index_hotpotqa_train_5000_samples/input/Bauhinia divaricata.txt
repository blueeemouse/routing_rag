Bauhinia divaricata

Bauhinia divaricata is a species of flowering shrub of the family Fabaceae.

 "B. divaricata" is the type species of the genus "Bauhinia".

 One common name for this species is pom pom orchid tree.

 It is native to China, Jamaica, India and also grows in the southern half of Florida.

 It has white bi-lobed leaves that alternate.
