Marvel Comics Super Special

Marvel Comics Super Special was a 41-issue series of one-shot comic-magazines published by American company Marvel Comics from 1977 to 1986.

 They were cover-priced $1.50 to $2.50, while regular color comics were priced 30 cents to 60 cents, Beginning with issue #5, the series' title in its postal indicia was shortened to "Marvel Super Special".

 Covers featured the title or a variation, including "Marvel Super Special", "Marvel Super Special magazine", and "Marvel Weirdworld Super Special" in small type, accompanied by large logos of its respective features.
