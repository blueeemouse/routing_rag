USS Brutus (AC-15)

USS "Brutus", formerly the steamer "Peter Jebsen", was a collier in the United States Navy.

 She was built in 1894 at South Shields-on-Tyne, England, by John Readhead & Sons and was acquired by the U.S. Navy early in 1898 from L. F. Chapman & Company.

 She was renamed "Brutus" and commissioned at the Mare Island Navy Yard on 27 May 1898, with Lieutenant Vincendon L. Cottman, commanding officer and Lieutenant Randolph H. Miner, executive officer.
