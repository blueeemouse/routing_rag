Adam Ondra

Adam Ondra (born February 5, 1993) is a Czech professional rock climber.

 He specializes in lead climbing and bouldering and is the only athlete to have won the World Championships in two disciplines, lead and bouldering.

 In addition, he won both medals in the same year, during the 2014 edition.

 He also has a similar record in the World Cup, being the only athlete to have won the World Cup in two disciplines, lead in 2009 and bouldering in 2010.
