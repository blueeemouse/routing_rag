Identity Evropa

Identity Evropa is a white supremacist group in the United States, established in March 2016.

 Part of the broader white supremacist alt-right movement, the group is identified as a racist white supremacist organization by the Anti-Defamation League and is designated by the Southern Poverty Law Center as a hate group.

 Relatively small in size, the group is seen as one of several groups contributing to a rising spike of white nationalism in the U.S. since 2015.

 The group particularly targets college campuses, by distributing white nationalist slogans on fliers, posters, and stickers.
