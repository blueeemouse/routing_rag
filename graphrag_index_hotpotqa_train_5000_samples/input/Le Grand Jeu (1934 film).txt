Le Grand Jeu (1934 film)

Le Grand Jeu is a 1934 French film directed by Jacques Feyder.

 It is a romantic drama set against the background of the French Foreign Legion, and the film was an example of poetic realism in the French cinema.

 The title "Le Grand Jeu" refers to the practice of reading the cards.

 Blanche asks whether her client wants the 'full works', the whole story: "Alors... je te fais le grand jeu?"
