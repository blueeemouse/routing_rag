Tufts Cove, Nova Scotia

Tufts Cove is a Canadian urban neighbourhood in the Dartmouth area of Nova Scotia's Halifax Regional Municipality.

 It is situated on the eastern shore of Halifax Harbour in the North End of Dartmouth.

 The neighbourhood boundaries of Tufts Cove are approximately from Albro Lake Road in the south to Highway 111 in the north, and from Victoria Road in the east with the harbour to the west.
