The Last Exorcism Part II

The Last Exorcism Part II is a 2013 American supernatural drama horror film co-written and directed by Ed Gass-Donnelly.

 It stars Ashley Bell, Julia Garner, Spencer Treat Clark, David Jensen, Tarra Riggs, Louis Herthum, and Muse Watson.

 It is a sequel to 2010's "The Last Exorcism", and released on March 1, 2013.
