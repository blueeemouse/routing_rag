Harsh Rajput

Harsh Jayesh Rajput (born 5 January 1988) is an Indian actor who has appears in the television soap operas. His first significant role on the television series "Dharti Ka Veer Yodha Prithviraj Chauhan" where he played the part of Pundir, one of the four childhood friends of the hero.

 This was followed with the role of Agni in the Imagine TV series "Dharam Veer".

 His first film role is in the film "", a sequel to "Krantiveer".

 He was seen playing 'Ishaan' in Channel V's Crazy Stupid Ishq.

 He was seen in Zee TV's "Sapne Suhane Ladakpan Ke" playing a complex character of a mentally challenged boy "Bittu".

He had also made significant appearances in Zing TV's "Pyar Tune Kya Kiya".
