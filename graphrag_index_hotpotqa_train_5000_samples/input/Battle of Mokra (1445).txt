Battle of Mokra (1445)

The Battle of Mokra took place on October 10, 1445 near mountain Mokra (today in Makedonski Brod, Macedonia).

 It was an Ottoman retaliation to a message sent by Skanderbeg to Murad II.

 The Albanian forces under Skanderbeg defeated the Ottoman forces under Firuz Pasha.

 It was the second major Albanian victory over the Ottoman Empire.
