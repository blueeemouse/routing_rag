Columbus Aces

The Columbus Aces were a proposed indoor football team based in Columbus, Ohio.

 The team was set to be the final team expansion team of the Continental Indoor Football League in 2010.

 The Aces would have been the third attempt at arena or indoor football in Columbus, the first were the Arena Football League's, following prior teams the Columbus Thunderbolts (played only the 1991 season before moving to Cleveland) and the Columbus Destroyers (started play in 2004, ended play in 2008 as the team never came back from the AFL's season long restructuring hiatu).

 The owners of the Aces were Michael Burtch and Stanley Jackson.

 The Aces were scheduled to play their home games at the Ohio Expo Center Coliseum in Columbus, Ohio.
