2015 New Orleans VooDoo season

The New Orleans VooDoo season was the ninth and final season and for the franchise in the Arena Football League (AFL).

 The team was coached by Dean Cokinos and played their home games at the Smoothie King Center.

 The team finished the regular season dead last in the league at 3–14-1, with one game cancelled and regarded officially as a tie, and failed to qualify for the playoffs for a third straight season.

 Following the season, the AFL, which had operated the team as owners since July 15, announced that the VooDoo would cease operations effective immediately.
