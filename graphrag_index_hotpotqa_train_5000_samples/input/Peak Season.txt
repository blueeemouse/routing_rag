Peak Season

Peak Season is an MTV Canada reality TV show set in Whistler, British Columbia.

 The premier was on October 19, 2009, and delivered the highest ratings for a series on MTV Canada.

 The show follows the lives of several young adult characters in the reality television style.

 The show concluded its first season December 14, 2009, aired on MTV Canada on Monday nights at 10 p.m. EST, and was followed by The After Show.
