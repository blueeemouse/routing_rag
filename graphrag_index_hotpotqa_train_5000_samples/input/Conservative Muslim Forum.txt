Conservative Muslim Forum

The Conservative Muslim Forum is a group within the British Conservative Party.

 It aims to increase Conservative Party's knowledge and comprehension of issues and circumstances that have particular relevance to Muslim communities and develop suitable responses.

 It also seeks to increase support for the Conservative Party within the Muslim community.

 Anyone who is eligible to vote in British general or local elections regardless of race, colour or creed may become a member of the Conservative Muslim Forum.

 It will however be necessary for a person applying for membership of the Conservative Muslim Forum to be an existing member of the Conservative Party or apply for such membership simultaneously.
