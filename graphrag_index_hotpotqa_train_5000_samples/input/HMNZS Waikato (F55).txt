HMNZS Waikato (F55)

HMNZS "Waikato" (F55) was a "Leander" Batch 2TA frigate of the Royal New Zealand Navy (RNZN).

 She was one of two "Leander"s built for the RNZN, the other being the Batch 3 HMNZS "Canterbury" .

 These two New Zealand ships relieved British ships of the Armilla patrol during the Falklands conflict, freeing British ships for deployment.
