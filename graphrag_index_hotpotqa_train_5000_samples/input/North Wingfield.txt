North Wingfield

North Wingfield is a large village and civil parish in the English county of Derbyshire, located approximately 4½ miles south-east of Chesterfield, and 1 mile north-east of Clay Cross.

 The population of the civil parish as of the 2011 census was 6,505.

 It is in the North East Derbyshire district.

 The A6175 road from the M1 motorway to the A61 road runs through the village.
