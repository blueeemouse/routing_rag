Ravina (Lordville, New York)

Ravina is a national historic district located at Lordville, a hamlet in the Town of Hancock in Delaware County, New York.

 The district contains six contributing buildings, one contributing site, and two contributing structures.

 It encompasses a small rural estate consisting of the main house, guest bungalow, garage, caretakers' dwelling, wood shed, and distinctive landscape features.

 The main residence is a three-by-three-bay, 2-story wood-frame building listed in the Sears catalog of prefabricated houses as "Shadow Lawn."

 It and the bungalow were built in 1926–1927.
