Kevin B. MacDonald

Kevin B. MacDonald (born January 24, 1944) is an American psychologist.

 He is a retired professor of psychology at California State University, Long Beach (CSULB), best known for his controversial application of evolutionary psychology to characterize Jewish behavior as a "group evolutionary strategy."

 He is currently the editor of the "Occidental Observer", which he says covers "white identity, white interests, and the culture of the West."

 He is described by the Anti-Defamation League as having "become a primary voice for anti-Semitism from far-right intellectuals" and by the Southern Poverty Law Center as "the neo-Nazi movement's favorite academic."

 He is also frequently referred to as part of the alt-right movement.
