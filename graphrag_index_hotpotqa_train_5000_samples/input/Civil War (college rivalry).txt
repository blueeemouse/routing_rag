Civil War (college rivalry)

The Civil War is a college rivalry between Oregon State University and the University of Oregon.

 The rivalry is one of the oldest in the nation, dating back to 1894 when the two universities' football teams first met.

 Although the college football game is the most popular rivalry, the two universities have Civil War games in every sport that both sponsor.

 There is also an academic rivalry between the schools on occasion, and competitions for community services such as blood drives take place often.
