Eddie Kaye Thomas

Eddie Kaye Thomas (born October 31, 1980) is an actor who rose to prominence as Paul Finch in the "American Pie" film series.

 He is also known for his roles as Andy Rosenberg in the "Harold & Kumar" series and as Jeff Woodcock in "'Til Death".

 He is currently starring in the CBS show "Scorpion" as Dr. Tobias "Toby" Curtis.
