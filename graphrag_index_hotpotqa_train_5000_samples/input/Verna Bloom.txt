Verna Bloom

Verna Bloom (born August 7, 1939) is an American actress.

 She co-starred in Clint Eastwood's 1973 film, "High Plains Drifter" and in the 1974 made-for-TV movie "Where Have All The People Gone?

" with Peter Graves and Kathleen Quinlan.

 She has had roles in more than 30 films and television episodes since the 1960s, including playing Mary, mother of Jesus, in "The Last Temptation of Christ" in 1988 and Marion Wormer in "Animal House" in 1978.
