Suzuki Katana AY50

The Suzuki Katana AY50 is a 50cc scooter produced by Suzuki since 1997 and sold primarily in Europe.

 It is named after the katana samurai sword, as well as the early 1980s motorcycle of the same name.

 As a result, the scooter was widely criticised by the motorcycle press and enthusiasts of the classic motorcycle over the name as for damaging credibility of the Katana name.
