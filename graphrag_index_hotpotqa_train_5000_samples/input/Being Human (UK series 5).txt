Being Human (UK series 5)

Being Human is a British supernatural drama series created and written by Toby Whithouse for Touchpaper Television.

 The fifth and final series began airing on BBC Three on 3 February 2013 and continued until mid-March 2013.

 The series follows the lives of a new ghost, vampire and werewolf trio living together and attempting to lead a normal life, and blends a mixture of flatshare comedy and horror drama.
