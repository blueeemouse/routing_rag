Code Lyoko: Evolution

Code Lyoko: Evolution is the sequel to the French animated television series "Code Lyoko".

 It premiered on December 19, 2012, on France 4 and repeated October 9, 2016, on Boing.

 It blends live-action with CGI, picking up where the original series left off.

 In addition to improving upon the CGI in the original series, the soundtrack has been overhauled as well.
