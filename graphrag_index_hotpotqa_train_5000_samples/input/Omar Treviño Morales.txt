Omar Treviño Morales

Óscar Omar Treviño Morales (born January 26, 1974) is a convicted Mexican drug lord and former leader of Los Zetas, a criminal organization.

 He was one of Mexico's most-wanted drug lords.

 His brother is Miguel Treviño Morales, a former leader of the group.

 The authorities believe he was the successor of his brother, who was arrested on July 15, 2013.
