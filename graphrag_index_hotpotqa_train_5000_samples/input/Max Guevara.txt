Max Guevara

Max Guevara (X5-452) is a fictional character and the protagonist of the cyberpunk science fiction television program "Dark Angel".

 During both seasons of the series Max was portrayed by Jessica Alba and by Geneva Locke during childhood flashbacks.

 Over one thousand actresses were considered for the part of Max before Alba was given the role.

 Max also appears in three canonical novels based on the series as well as an apocryphal video game adaptation.

 Created by James Cameron and Charles H. Eglee, Max followed a long line of strong female characters created by Cameron, including Sarah Connor and Ellen Ripley.
