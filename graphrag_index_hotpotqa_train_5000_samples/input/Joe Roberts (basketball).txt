Joe Roberts (basketball)

Joseph "Joe" Roberts (born May 18, 1936) is an American former professional basketball player.

 Born in Columbus, Ohio, Roberts played for Ohio State University's 1960 NCAA champions, and later played in the NBA for the Syracuse Nationals before going into coaching.

 Roberts also played with Hall-of-Famers Jerry Lucas, John Havlicek, and Bobby Knight.

 Coach Fred Taylor also was a Hall-of-Famer for Ohio State.
