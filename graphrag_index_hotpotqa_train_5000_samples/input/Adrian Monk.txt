Adrian Monk

Adrian Monk is a title character and the protagonist of the USA Network television series "Monk", portrayed by Tony Shalhoub.

 He is a renowned former homicide detective for the San Francisco Police Department.

 Monk has obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD) and multiple phobias, all of which intensified after the murder of his wife Trudy, resulting in his suspension from the department.

 He works as a private police homicide consultant and undergoes therapy with the ultimate goal of overcoming his grief, taking control of his phobias and disorder, and being reinstated as a police detective.
