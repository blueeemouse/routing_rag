Accrington

Accrington is a town in the Hyndburn borough of Lancashire, England.

 It lies about 4 mi east of Blackburn, 6 mi west of Burnley, 13 mi east of Preston, 20 mi north of Manchester city centre and is situated on the mostly culverted River Hyndburn.

 Commonly abbreviated by locals to "Accy", the town has a population of 54,800 according to the 2001 census and the urban area has a population of over 85,000.
