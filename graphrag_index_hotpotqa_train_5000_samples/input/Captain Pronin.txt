Captain Pronin

Captain Pronin (Russian: Капитан Пронин, "Kapitan Pronin" ) is a Russian animated cartoon parody series created by Mikhail Zaitsev.

 The series consists of four short animated films produced at Studio Ekran from 1992–1994, then expanded into a book series and a video game.

 Its story revolves around the eponymous protagonist, the grandson of Major Pronin, and his adventures which parody various film clichés.
