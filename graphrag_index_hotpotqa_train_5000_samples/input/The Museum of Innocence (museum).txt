The Museum of Innocence (museum)

The Museum of Innocence (Turkish: "Masumiyet Müzesi" ) is a museum in a 19th-century house in Istanbul created by novelist Orhan Pamuk as a companion to his novel "The Museum of Innocence".

 The museum and the novel were created in tandem, centred on the stories of two Istanbul families.

 On 17 May 2014, the museum was announced as the winner of the 2014 European Museum of the Year Award.
