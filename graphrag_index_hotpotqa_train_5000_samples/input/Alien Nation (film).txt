Alien Nation (film)

Alien Nation is a 1988 American buddy cop neo-noir science fiction action film directed by Graham Baker.

 The ensemble cast features James Caan, Mandy Patinkin and Terence Stamp.

 The film depicts the assimilation of the "Newcomers", an alien race settling in Los Angeles, much to the initial dismay of the local population.

 The plot integrates the neo-noir and buddy cop film genres with a science fiction theme, centering on a union between a veteran police investigator (Caan) and an extraterrestrial (Patinkin); the first Newcomer detective.

 The duo probe a criminal underworld attempting to solve a homicide.
