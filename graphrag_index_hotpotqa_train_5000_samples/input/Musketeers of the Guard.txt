Musketeers of the Guard

The Musketeers of the Guard (French: "Mousquetaires de la garde" ) or King's Musketeers (Mousquetaires du roi ); full name - Musketeers of the military household of the King of France (Mousquetaires de la maison militaire du roi de France ) were a fighting company of the military branch of the Maison du Roi, the Royal Household of the French monarchy.
