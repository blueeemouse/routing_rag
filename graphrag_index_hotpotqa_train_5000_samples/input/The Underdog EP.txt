The Underdog EP

The Underdog EP is an extended play (EP) released in 2002 by American pop punk band Yellowcard.

 Although publicized very little, it tried new styles in Yellowcard's music, making the transition between "One for the Kids" and "Ocean Avenue" and was overall well-accepted by fans.

 It is often mentioned by listeners that during "The Underdog EP" Yellowcard's style was "maturing".

 This is the final album to feature Warren Cooke on bass, as he left the band in July 2002.
