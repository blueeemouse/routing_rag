Beeba Boys

Beeba Boys is a 2015 Canadian crime thriller film written and directed by Deepa Mehta.

 It stars Randeep Hooda as Jeet Johar, a loving single father, a dutiful son, a proud, observant Sikh and a ruthless gangster managing a team of stylish, charismatic but brutal and unforgiving young men.

 The film is a fictional take based on real incidents, including Bindy Johal.
