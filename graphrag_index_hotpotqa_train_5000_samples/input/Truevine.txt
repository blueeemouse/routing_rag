Truevine

Truevine: Two Brothers, a Kidnapping, and a Mother's Quest: A True Story of the Jim Crow South is a 2016 non-fiction book by American author Beth Macy.

 The book tells the story of George and Willie Muse, two African-American brothers that were allegedly kidnapped and forced to perform as sideshow attractions because they were albinos.

 "Truevine" was released on October 18, 2016 through Little, Brown and Company.
