University of Hawaii–West Oahu

The University of Hawaiʻ i–West Oʻ ahu (UHWO), is a public university and one of ten branches of the University of Hawaiʻ i system.

 It offers baccalaureate degrees in liberal arts and professional studies.

 Hawaii–West Oahu opened in January 1976, and since 1981 has been fully accredited by the Western Association of Schools and Colleges.

 Until 2007 the school offered only third- and fourth-year courses, and became a four-year university in that year.
