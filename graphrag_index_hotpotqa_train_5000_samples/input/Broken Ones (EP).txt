Broken Ones (EP)

Broken Ones is the debut extended play recorded by American singer and runner-up of fifth season of "The Voice", Jacquie Lee.

 It was released through Atlantic Records and 300 Entertainment on October 21, 2014.

 A cover of Cyndi Lauper's 1983 hit, "Girls Just Want to Have Fun" is included along with four original songs co-written by popular songwriters such as Sia Furler and Andreas Carlsson.

 The EP failed to reach the "Billboard" 200, but did peak at 193 on the Top Current Albums component chart.
