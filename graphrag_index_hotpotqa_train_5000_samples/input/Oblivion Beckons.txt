Oblivion Beckons

Oblivion Beckons is the third studio album by Charleston heavy metal band Byzantine.

 It was released on January 22, 2008.

 According to vocalist Chris "OJ" Ojeda, the band "stripp[ed] down some things like the vocals for a more aggressive style and ... [went] out on a limb on some other things [they] have never tried before."

 Ojeda also described "Oblivion Beckons" as being the band's most varied album.

 The album reached #8 on CMJ.
