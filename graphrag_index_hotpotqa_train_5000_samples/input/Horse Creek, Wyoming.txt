Horse Creek, Wyoming

Horse Creek is an unincorporated community in western Laramie County, Wyoming, United States.

 It lies between Horse Creek to the north, and the South Fork of Horse Creek to the south, along WYO 211 northwest of the city of Cheyenne, the county seat of Laramie County.

 Its elevation is 6,506 feet (1,983 m).

 Although Horse Creek is unincorporated, it has a post office, with the ZIP code of 82061.

 As of the 2010 census, Horse Creek had an estimated population of 39.
