The Ren &amp; Stimpy Show: Fire Dogs

The Ren & Stimpy Show: Fire Dogs is a platform game developed by Argonaut Games, and published by THQ for the Super Nintendo Entertainment System in 1994.

 The game derives from "Fire Dogs" (1991), the eighth installment in the cartoon series "The Ren & Stimpy Show", created by Canadian animator John Kricfalusi.
