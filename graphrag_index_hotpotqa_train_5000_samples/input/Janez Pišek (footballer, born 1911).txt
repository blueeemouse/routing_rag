Janez Pišek (footballer, born 1911)

Janez Pišek was a Slovenian football manager and former player.

 He played for AŠK Primorje most of his career and later managed various clubs in the Yugoslav Second League.

 He became first professional Slovenian football coach and first president of Slovenian Football Coach Association in 1953.

 After his death he received a Blodek plaque for life work in Slovenian football.
