Kinky (Kinky album)

Kinky is the self-titled album by Mexican group Kinky.

 It was released on March 26, 2002 on Nettwerk.

 The most popular song, Cornman, is part of the soundtrack for the PlayStation 3 video game LittleBigPlanet.

 Another of their songs, "Más", is featured in the PS2 video game SSX 3 and in the 2004 film Man on Fire.
