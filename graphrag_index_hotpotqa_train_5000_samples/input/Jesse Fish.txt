Jesse Fish

Jesse Fish (1724 or 1726–1790) was a shipmaster, merchant, and realtor who lived in St. Augustine, Florida under both Spanish and British rule, and is infamous in the town's history to this day.

 He was a schemer involved in contraband trade and illegal real estate deals, and operated as a slaver, smuggler, and usurer.

 By his slaver activities Fish introduced most of the "bozales", or African-born slaves, registered in Spanish Florida during the decade (1752–1763) preceding Spain's cession of Florida to Great Britain.

 He has been accused of spying for England and Spain as a double agent during the Seven Years’ War, but there is no evidence to support the claim.
