Listed buildings in Noctorum

Noctorum is a suburb of Birkenhead, Wirral, Merseyside, England.

 It contains five buildings that are recorded in the National Heritage List for England as designated listed buildings, all of which are listed at Grade II.

 This grade is the lowest of the three gradings given to listed buildings and is applied to "buildings of national importance and special interest".

 The listed buildings are all large houses, or buildings associated with large houses.
