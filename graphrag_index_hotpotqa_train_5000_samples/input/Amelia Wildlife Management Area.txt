Amelia Wildlife Management Area

Amelia Wildlife Management Area is a 2217 acre Wildlife Management Area located in Amelia County, Virginia.

 Primarily upland habitat, it also preserves around 175 acre of bottomland hardwoods and beaver swampland along the Appomattox River.

 Much of the land was formerly used for farming; today it is managed to preserve wildlife habitat.

 The forest is mature, with gently rolling terrain, and an altitude between 200 and above sea level.

 Much of the area is bounded on the north by the Appomattox River.
