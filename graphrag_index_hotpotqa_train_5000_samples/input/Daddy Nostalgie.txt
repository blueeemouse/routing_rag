Daddy Nostalgie

Daddy Nostalgie, released as These Foolish Things in the UK and Daddy Nostalgia in the USA, is a 1990 French drama film directed by Bertrand Tavernier.

 It was entered into the 1990 Cannes Film Festival and is Dirk Bogarde's last film.

 Odette Laure was nominated for the César Award for Best Supporting Actress.
