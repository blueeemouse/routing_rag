Lose It (Cartel song)

"Lose It" is a single by the five-piece American pop-rock band Cartel, recorded for the MTV show "Band in a Bubble".

 This was the first song recorded by Cartel while they spent 20 days recording a new album in a glass bubble.

 It was released as the group's first single from their self-titled second album "Cartel", on June 12, 2007.

 The song featured female vocalist Juliet Simms of the band Automatic Loveletter and the ringtone reflected her portion of the song although her vocals were removed from the video.
