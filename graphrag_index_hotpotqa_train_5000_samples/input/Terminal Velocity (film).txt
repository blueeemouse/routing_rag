Terminal Velocity (film)

Terminal Velocity is a 1994 American action film starring Charlie Sheen, Nastassja Kinski, James Gandolfini, and Christopher McDonald.

 Directed by Deran Sarafian and written by David Twohy, it follows a daredevil skydiver (Sheen) who is caught up in a criminal plot by Russian mobsters (Gandolfini and McDonald), forcing him to team up with a freelance secret agent (Kinski) in order to survive.

 It was one of two skydiving-themed action films released in the fourth quarter of 1994 (the other being Paramount Pictures' "Drop Zone"), and received mostly negative reviews from critics.
