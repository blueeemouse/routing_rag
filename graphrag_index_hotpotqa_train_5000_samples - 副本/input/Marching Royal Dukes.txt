Marching Royal Dukes

The Marching Royal Dukes are James Madison University's official marching band.

 Formed in 1972 (the same year as the football team), the "MRD's" perform at pre-game, halftime and post-game at all home football games, and travel with the team occasionally to perform at away and post-season games.

 In 1994, the MRD's were the recipients of the Sudler Trophy, the highest honor available for a college marching band.
