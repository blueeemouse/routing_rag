Lisa Disbrow

Lisa Stephens Disbrow (born September 29, 1962) is the former Under Secretary of the Air Force.

 From January 20 to May 16, 2017, she served as the Acting United States Secretary of the Air Force until Heather Wilson assumed the office.

 Previously, Disbrow served as Acting Under Secretary of the Air Force from January 2015 until she was confirmed by the Senate as the Under Secretary in January 2015.

 She was confirmed by the Senate and served as the Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Financial Management and Comptroller from 2014 to 2015.
