Cherrie Ireland

Cheryl "Cherrie" Ann Dallas-Smith (née Loydstrom, formerly Ireland), MBE is an Australian former wheelchair athlete, swimmer and table tennis player who represented her country at two Paralympic Games and two Commonwealth Paraplegic Games in the 1960s and 1970s.

 Born in Darwin, She was on holiday in Melbourne when she was paralysed by polio at the age of five.

 Her family moved to Brisbane and she became involved in competitive sport through the Queensland Sports and Social Club for the Disabled.
