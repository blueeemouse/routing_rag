ULTRA Diamonds

ULTRA Diamonds was the largest seller of fine jewelry in factory outlet and value centers in the United States.

 ULTRA was a manufacturer as well as a direct importer of diamonds, gemstones, and gold jewelry.

 At one point, Ultra operated over 100 stores in outlet centers and 38 other format locations (primarily licensed jewelry departments).

 After its acquisition by Signet Jewelers, ULTRA Diamonds stores were re-branded as Kay Jewelers.
