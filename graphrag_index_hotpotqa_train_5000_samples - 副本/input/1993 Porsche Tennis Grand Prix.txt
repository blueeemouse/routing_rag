1993 Porsche Tennis Grand Prix

The 1993 Porsche Tennis Grand Prix was a women's tennis tournament played on indoor hard courts at the Filderstadt Tennis Centre in Filderstadt, Germany and was part of the Tier II of the 1993 WTA Tour.

 It was the 16th edition of the tournament and was held from 11 October to 17 October 1993.

 Third-seeded Mary Pierce won the singles title and earned $75,000 first-prize money as well as 300 ranking points.
