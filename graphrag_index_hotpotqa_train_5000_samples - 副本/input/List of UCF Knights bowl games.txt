List of UCF Knights bowl games

The UCF Knights football team competes as part of the National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) Division I Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS), representing The University of Central Florida in the American Athletic Conference.

 Since the establishment of the football program in 1979, the Knights have played in eight bowl games.

 UCF has appeared in four different bowl games, with multiple appearances in the St. Petersburg (3) and the Liberty Bowl (2).

 The Knights also played in the Hawai'i Bowl, the Cure Bowl, and the Fiesta Bowl which was a part of the Bowl Championship Series (BCS).

 With their most recent defeat in the 2016 Cure Bowl, UCF has an overall bowl record of 3–5.

 They are 1-0 in BCS and CFP bowl games.
