Jonas Malmsjö

Carl Jonas Love Malmsjö (born September 2, 1971) is a Swedish actor who has worked in theatre, TV and radio; he is the son of Swedish actor Jan Malmsjö and the Swedish actress Marie Göranzon.

 Apart from his work in his native Sweden, which has included a starring role in the TV series "Labyrint", he has been in several productions directed by Ingmar Bergman at the Brooklyn Academy of Music.

 He was also cast as Skorpa of the White Horse, a Viking warlord in the second half of "The Last Kingdom", a BBC adaptation of the first two books in Bernard Cornwell's "The Saxon Stories" series of historical novels.
