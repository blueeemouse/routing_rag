Har (Blake)

Har is a character in the mythological writings of William Blake, who roughly corresponds to an aged Adam.

 His wife, Heva, corresponds to Eve.

 Har appears in "Tiriel" (1789) and "The Song of Los" (1795) and is briefly mentioned in "The Book of Thel" (1790) and "Vala, or The Four Zoas" (1796-1803).
