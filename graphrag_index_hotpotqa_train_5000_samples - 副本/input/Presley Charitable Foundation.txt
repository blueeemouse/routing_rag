Presley Charitable Foundation

The Presley Charitable Foundation is a non-profit philanthropic organization created by Lisa Marie Presley in August 2007.

 The first endeavor of The Presley Charitable Foundation will be Presley Place-New Orleans; a transitional housing facility for homeless families.

 The foundation provides rent-free housing, child day care, career and financial counseling, family management guidance and other tools which help people learn how no longer be homeless again.

 Through this process they gain self-esteem and independence.
