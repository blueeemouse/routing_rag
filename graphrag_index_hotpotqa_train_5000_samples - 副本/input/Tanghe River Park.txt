Tanghe River Park

Tanghe River Park is located in the Chinese city of Qinhuangdao.

 It was created on the coast of Tanghe River in July 2006, and is home to the Red Ribbon, a red steel bench which stretches for half a kilometre through the park.

 The Ribbon has won an honor award from the American Society of Landscape Architects, and was chosen by the readers of "Condé Nast Traveler" magazine as one of the seven wonders of the architectural world.
