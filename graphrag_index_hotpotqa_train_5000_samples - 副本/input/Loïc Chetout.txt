Loïc Chetout

Loïc Chetout (born 23 September 1992) is a French professional racing cyclist riding for Cofidis .

 He made his Grand Tour début at the 2016 Vuelta a España, finishing 138th overall with one top ten placing.

 His first Monuments races came in 2017, riding the Tour of Flanders and Paris-Roubaix.
