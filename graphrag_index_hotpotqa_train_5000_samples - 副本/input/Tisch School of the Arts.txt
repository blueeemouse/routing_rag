Tisch School of the Arts

The New York University Tisch School of the Arts (known more commonly as Tisch or TSOA) is a center of study in the performing and media arts.

 Founded on August 17, 1965, Tisch is a training ground for artists, scholars of the arts, filmmakers, and creative entrepreneurs.

 The school merges the technical training of a professional school with the academic resources of a major research university to immerse students in their intended artistic disciplines.

 It is located at 721 Broadway in Manhattan, New York City.

 NYU has the most alumni working at Broadway theatre.
