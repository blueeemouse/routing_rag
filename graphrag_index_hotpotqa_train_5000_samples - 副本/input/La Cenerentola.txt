La Cenerentola

La Cenerentola, ossia La bontà in trionfo (Cinderella, or Goodness Triumphant) is an operatic "dramma giocoso" in two acts by Gioachino Rossini.

 The libretto was written by Jacopo Ferretti, based on the fairy tale "Cendrillon" by Charles Perrault.

 The opera was first performed in Rome's Teatro Valle on 25 January 1817.
