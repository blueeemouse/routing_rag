Baconian theory of Shakespeare authorship

The Baconian theory of Shakespearean authorship holds that Sir Francis Bacon, philosopher, essayist and scientist, wrote the plays which were publicly attributed to William Shakespeare.

 Various explanations are offered for this alleged subterfuge, most commonly that Bacon's rise to high office might have been hindered were it to become known that he wrote plays for the public stage.

 Thus the plays were credited to Shakespeare, who was merely a front to shield the identity of Bacon.
