Jami Bernard

Jami Bernard (born August 10, 1956) is an American author and media consultant, an award-winning film critic for "The New York Post" and "The New York Daily News", and the founder of Barncat Publishing.

 She has appeared in documentaries as herself, including the Independent Film Channel's "Indie Sex" series, on which she was a consultant.

 Bernard wrote a "Lois Lane" comic for DC Comics in which Lois is based on Bernard's early career at "The New York Post".

 Bernard is a frequent guest on TV and radio shows; appearances include on "Oprah", "The Today Show", and "Lynn Samuels" on Satellite Radio.

 Her work has been published in numerous magazines, including "Entertainment Weekly", "Seventeen", "Glamour", and "Self".
