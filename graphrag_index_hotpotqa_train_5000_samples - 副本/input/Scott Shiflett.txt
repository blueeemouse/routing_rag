Scott Shiflett

Scott David Shiflett (born August 22, 1966) is an American musician who has been the bassist in the punk rock band Face to Face since 1995.

 Born in Santa Barbara, California, he is the brother of Foo Fighters lead guitarist Chris Shiflett.

 Scott has also performed in the bands Viva Death, Me First and the Gimme Gimmes, Jackson United, and 22 Jacks.
