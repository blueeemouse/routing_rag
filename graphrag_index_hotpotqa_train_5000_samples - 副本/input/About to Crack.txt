About to Crack

About to Crack is the fifth studio album by Dutch punk hardcore band Vitamin X.

 Released through Tankcrimes on September 11, 2012 in the US, Agipunk in Europe and Peculio Discos in Brazil.

 The album was recorded at Electrical Audio in Chicago by Steve Albini who previously recorded Nirvana, Neurosis, PJ Harvey, High on Fire, Iggy Pop & The Stooges.

 Art is by John Dyer Baizley.
