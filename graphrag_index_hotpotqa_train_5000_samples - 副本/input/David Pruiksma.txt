David Pruiksma

David Pruiksma (born January 15, 1957) is an American animator, best known for his work for The Walt Disney Company.

 He has worked on "The Black Cauldron", "The Great Mouse Detective", "Oliver & Company", "The Little Mermaid", "The Rescuers Down Under", "Beauty and the Beast", "Aladdin", "The Lion King", "Pocahontas", "The Hunchback of Notre Dame", and "".
