Hatching Pete

Hatching Pete is a 2009 Disney Channel Original Movie which first aired on April 24, 2009 on Disney Channel UK and later Disney Channel and Family.

 It was released on DVD in America on May 12, 2009 together with "Dadnapped".

 It is the first Disney Channel Original Movie to air in the UK first.

 It first aired in America on April 24, 2009.
