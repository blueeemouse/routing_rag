Oldroyd Publishing Group

Oldroyd Publishing Group is a print and website publishing company based in Lytham St Annes, England.

 It was founded in 1974.

 Oldroyd Publishing Group produce advertising sponsored publications for doctors and estate agents.

 They also produce Estate Agency News (founded in 1986), a trade newspaper for estate agents, and websites for businesses.

 They have a head office at Keenan's Mill, Lytham St Annes.
