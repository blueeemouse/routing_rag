Yongjing County

Yongjing (, Xiao'erjing: ﻳْﻮ دٍ ﺷِﯿًﺎْ ) is a county in Linxia Hui Autonomous Prefecture in China's Gansu Province.

 The county seat, the town ("zhen") of Liujiaxia (), is located about 80 km south-west from the provincial capital city, Lanzhou.

 As is the case with most Chinese county seats, Liujiaxia Town is labeled on most less-detailed maps simply as "Yongjing" or "Yongjing County".
