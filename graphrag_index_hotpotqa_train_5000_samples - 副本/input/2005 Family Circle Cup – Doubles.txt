2005 Family Circle Cup – Doubles

Virginia Ruano Pascual and Paola Suárez were the defending champions, but had different outcomes.

 While Suárez did not compete this year, Ruano Pascual teamed up with Conchita Martínez and successfully defended her title, by defeating Iveta Benešová and Květa Peschke 6–1, 6–4 in the final.
