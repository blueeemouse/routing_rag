Kapiolani Community College

Kapiʻ olani Community College, formerly Kapiʻ olani Technical School, is a public, co-educational commuter college in Honolulu, Hawaiʻ i situated on the slopes of Diamond Head in Waikīkī.

 It is one of ten branches of the University of Hawaiʻ i system anchored by the University of Hawaiʻ i at Mānoa.
