Shuanggudui

Shuanggudui () is an archeological site located near Fuyang in China's Anhui province.

 Shuanggudui grave no. 1, which belongs to Xiahou Zao (), the second marquis of Ruyin (), was sealed in 165 BCE in the early Han dynasty (206 BCE – 220 CE).

 Excavated in 1977, it was found to contain a large number of texts written on bamboo strips, including fragments of the "Classic of Poetry" and the "Songs of the South", a text on breathing exercises, a "year table" () recounting historical events, a manual on dogs, a version of the "I Ching" ("Yijing") that differs from the received one, and artifacts including the oldest known cosmic board, a divinatory instrument.

 Like Mawangdui and Guodian, two other tombs from the area of the old state of Chu, the Shuanggudui find has shed great light on the culture and practices of the early Han dynasty.
