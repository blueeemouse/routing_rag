Rogers, Minnesota

Rogers is a city in Hennepin County, Minnesota, United States.

 The population was 8,597 at the 2010 census.

 However, the population increased to over 11,000 upon the annexing of surrounding Hassan Township in 2012.

 The population of Hassan Township was 2,600 in 2010, giving the current city a population base of 11,197 as of the census.

 The center of population of Minnesota is located in Rogers .

 The city is a northwest suburb of the Minneapolis–Saint Paul metropolitan area.
