Flandrien of the Year

The Flandrien of the Year (Dutch: Flandrien-Trofee) is an annual award presented by the Flemish newspaper "Het Nieuwsblad" to the best Belgian cyclist of the year.

 The prize has been awarded since 2003 and was originally awarded based on a vote by the public, and open to any nationality.

 Since 2008, people from within the world of cycling choose the winner from a jury's list of nominees, with only Belgian riders eligible to win the award.

 The first winner of the award was the Italian Paolo Bettini, and the current holder is Greg Van Avermaet.

 There are also separate awards for the best international cyclist, and best female cyclist.
