Michigan Sports Hall of Fame

The Michigan Sports Hall of Fame is a Hall of Fame to honor Michigan sports athletes, coaches and contributors.

 It was organized in 1954 by Michigan Lieutenant Governor Philip Hart, Michigan State University athletic director Biggie Munn, president of the Greater Michigan Foundation Donald Weeks, general manager of the Detroit Lions W. Nicholas Kerbawy and George Alderton of the "Lansing State Journal".

 The inaugural class was inducted in 1955.

 After Nick Kerbawy the President was Cyndy Winkler who was the owner of the Mt. Clemens Race Track.

 Scott Lesher is its current chairman.
