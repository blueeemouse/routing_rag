All Hallows, Bread Street

All Hallows Bread Street was a parish church in the Bread Street ward of the City of London.

 It stood on the east side of Bread Street, on the corner with Watling Street.

 First mentioned in the 13th century, the church was destroyed in the Great Fire of London in 1666.

 The church was rebuilt by the office of Sir Christopher Wren and demolished in 1878.
