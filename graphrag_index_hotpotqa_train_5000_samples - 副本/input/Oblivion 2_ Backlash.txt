Oblivion 2: Backlash

Oblivion 2: Backlash is a 1996 American science fiction Western film directed by Sam Irvin, written by Peter David and starring Richard Joseph Paul, Jackie Swanson, Maxwell Caulfield and Musetta Vander.

 It was produced by Full Moon Entertainment and was shot in Romania.

 It is a sequel to the 1994 film "Oblivion".
