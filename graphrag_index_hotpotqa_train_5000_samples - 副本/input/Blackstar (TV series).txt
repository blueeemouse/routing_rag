Blackstar (TV series)

Blackstar is an American animated television series, a science fantasy story sometimes reported as being inspired by "Thundarr the Barbarian".

 It was produced in 1981 by Lou Scheimer and Norm Prescott for Filmation.

 The series was Filmation's second fantasy epic, the first being "The Freedom Force", a segment of "Tarzan and the Super 7".

 "Blackstar" has many notable similarities to "He-Man and the Masters of the Universe", which was produced shortly afterwards.
