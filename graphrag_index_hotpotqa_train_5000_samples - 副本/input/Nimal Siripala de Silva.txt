Nimal Siripala de Silva

Nilenthi Nimal Siripala de Silva MP (born 6 September 1944) is Sri Lanka's Cabinet Minister of Transprt in 15th Parliament of Sri Lanka.

 He is a member of the Sri Lanka Freedom Party and a Member of Parliament representing the Badulla District.

 He was also the former Leader of the Opposition, former Cabinet Minister (Irrigation and Water Resources Management), former Leader of the House.
