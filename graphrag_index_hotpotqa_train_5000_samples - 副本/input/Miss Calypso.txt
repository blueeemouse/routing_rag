Miss Calypso

Miss Calypso is a 1957 album by singer, writer and poet Maya Angelou.

 The album was released during a craze for calypso music catalyzed by Harry Belafonte the previous year.

 Angelou sings every song on the album, and she composed five of them.

 Behind Angelou's voice, studio guitarist Tommy Tedesco and percussionist Al Bello created an exotic mood.

 Angelou toured in support of the album, performing calypso songs in nightclubs.

 The album was a modest success but Angelou did not make any further records as a singer.
