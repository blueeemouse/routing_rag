The Max Levine Ensemble

The Max Levine Ensemble is a DIY punk band from the Washington, DC metropolitan area.

 They play punk rock with various pop influences, and often have politically conscious lyrics.

 They used to have a rotating band roster that changed from tour to tour depending upon who could go.

 Though at one point they played regularly with 5-7 members, they generally play now as a three piece power trio consisting of David Combs, Ben Epstein, and Nick Popovici.
