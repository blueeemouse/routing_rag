Hey Ma (Pitbull and J Balvin song)

"Hey Ma" is a song recorded by American rapper Pitbull and Colombian singer J Balvin, taken from the soundtrack of the 2017 action film "" (2017), featuring vocals from Cuban-Mexican singer Camila Cabello.

 It was written by the artists along with Sermstyle, Tinashe Sibanda, Philip Kembo, Soaky Siren and Johnny Yukon, and produced by Sermstyle, T-Collar, Pip Kembo and Yukon.

 Vocals were produced by Matt Beckley.

 Originally, the song was scheduled to be a collab between Pitbull, Britney Spears and Romeo Santos, but Spears and Santos were replaced by Cabello and Balvin, respectively.

 There are two versions of the song; one in Spanish and one in English.
