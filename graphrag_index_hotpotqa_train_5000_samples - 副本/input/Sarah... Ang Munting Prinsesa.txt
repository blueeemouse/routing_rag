Sarah... Ang Munting Prinsesa

Sarah... Ang Munting Prinsesa (Filipino: "Sarah... The Little Princess") is a 1995 Filipino family-drama film adapted from the 1985 Nippon Animation anime, "Princess Sarah", which in turn was based on the children's novel "A Little Princess" by British playwright and author Frances Hodgson Burnett.

 The film was directed by Romy Suzara and starred Camille Prats as Sara Crewe, Angelica Panganiban as Becky and Jean Garcia as Ms. Minchin.
