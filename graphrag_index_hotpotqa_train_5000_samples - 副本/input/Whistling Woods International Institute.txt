Whistling Woods International Institute

Whistling Woods International is a film, communication and media arts institute located in Mumbai, India.

 The institute is promoted by Indian Filmmaker Subhash Ghai, Mukta Arts Limited and Film City Mumbai.

 In July 2014, "The Hollywood Reporter" named Whistling Woods on its list of "The best film schools in the world".

 The school was first ranked among the top 10 film schools by The Hollywood Reporter in 2010.
