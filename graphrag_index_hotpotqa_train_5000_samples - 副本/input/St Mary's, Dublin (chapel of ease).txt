St Mary's, Dublin (chapel of ease)

St Mary's Chapel of Ease, also known as "The Black Church", is a former chapel in Dublin, Ireland.

 Now deconsecrated, it was a church of the Church of Ireland located on St Mary's Place, Broadstone, Dublin.

 It is constructed from local calp limestone which takes on a dark hue when wet.

 This is the origin of the building's nickname.

 A chapel of ease is a church building, other than a parish church, that is located within the bounds of a parish for the convenience of those who cannot conveniently reach the main church.

 The parish's main church, now also deconsecrated, was St Mary's on Mary Street.
