List of Child's Play characters

Child's Play is an American horror franchise that consists of six slasher films and numerous comic books.

 The main antagonist is Chucky, also known as Charles Lee Ray, the notorious Lakeshore Strangler, whose soul was passed into a "Good Guy" Doll.

 Chucky pursues a variation of victims in each installment, usually with the purpose of possessing the body of a new human host.

 Recurring characters in the series include Chucky's first target, Andy Barclay, and his wife, Tiffany.
