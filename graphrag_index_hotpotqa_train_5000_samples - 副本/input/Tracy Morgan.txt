Tracy Morgan

Tracy Jamal Morgan (born November 10, 1968) is an American actor and comedian best known for his seven seasons as a cast member on "Saturday Night Live" (1996–2003), "30 Rock" (2006–2013) and his 2010 buddy cop film "Cop Out" and hosting "Scare Tactics".

 He was nominated for an Emmy Award in 2009 for his work on "30 Rock".

 He has appeared in numerous films as an actor and voice actor.
