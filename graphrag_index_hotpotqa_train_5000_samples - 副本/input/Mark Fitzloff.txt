Mark Fitzloff

Mark Fitzloff (born February 3, 1971 in Milwaukee, Wisconsin) is an American advertising creative director.

 He is most known for his work at Wieden+Kennedy on Nike and Coca-Cola, and for reviving Procter & Gamble's Old Spice brand.

 He is also the executive producer and screenwriter of Tempbot, Neill Blomkamp's cult short film.
