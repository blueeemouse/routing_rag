Blue Moon World Tour

2013 CNBLUE Blue Moon World Tour was the 2013 live concert tour of South Korean rock band CNBLUE in support of their fifth EP, "", which was released on 14 January 2013.

 The world tour commenced, at the conclusion of promotional activities in South Korea, with one show in Taiwan in April 2013 and scheduled concerts for Singapore, Thailand, Hong Kong, South Korea, Australia, Philippines and Malaysia, thru August 2013.

 Subsequent concert dates added China and Indonesia; and extended the tour into January 2014, for concerts in the United States,Mexico, Peru, and the final concert in Chile, for a total of 20 concerts in 14 countries and 18 cities.
