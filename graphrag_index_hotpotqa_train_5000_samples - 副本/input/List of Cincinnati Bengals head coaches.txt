List of Cincinnati Bengals head coaches

This is a complete list of Cincinnati Bengals head coaches.

 There have been 9 head coaches for the Cincinnati Bengals of the National Football League (NFL).

 The Bengals are a professional American football team based in Cincinnati, Ohio.

 They are a member of the North Division of the American Football Conference (AFC).

 The Bengals franchise was founded in 1968 as a member of the Western Division of the American Football League (AFL), before merging with the NFL in 1970.
