High School Rock

High School Rock is an album by the Huntingtons released in 1998 on Tooth & Nail Records, the band's first for the label.

 A remastered version was released in 2009, available as a digital download.

 According to the band, the album was remastered to get it sounding the way they always wanted it to sound.

 The remastered version includes a bonus track previously only available on vinyl.
