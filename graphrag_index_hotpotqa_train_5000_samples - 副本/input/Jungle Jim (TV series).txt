Jungle Jim (TV series)

Jungle Jim is a 26-episode syndicated adventure television series which aired from 1955 till 1956, starring Johnny Weissmuller, as Jim "Jungle Jim" Bradley, a hunter, guide, and explorer in, primarily, Africa.

 The program should not be confused with "Ramar of the Jungle", but is based on the "Jungle Jim" comic strip created by Alex Raymond and Don Moore.

 Starring with Weissmuller were Martin Huston as Jungle Jim's teenage son, Skipper; Dean Fredericks (also known as Norman Fredric) as Haseem, the Hindu manservant, and Neal, a chimpanzee from the World Jungle Compound, as Tamba.

 Paul Cavanagh played Commissioner Morrison in nine episodes.
