Resiutta

Resiutta (Friulian: "Resiùte" , Slovene: "Na Bili" ) is a "comune" (municipality) in the Province of Udine in the Italian region Friuli-Venezia Giulia, located about 100 km northwest of Trieste and about 35 km north of Udine.

 As of 31 December 2014, it had a population of 311 and an area of 20.0 km2 .
