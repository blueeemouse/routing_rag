Labour Party (Malta)

The Labour Party (Maltese: "Partit Laburista" , PL), formerly known as the Malta Labour Party (MLP), is a social-democratic political party in Malta.

 Along with the Nationalist Party (PN), the Labour Party is one of two major contemporary political parties in Malta.

 It is currently the governing party in the Maltese House of Representatives following the March 2013 general election.

 The Labour Party is a member of the Party of European Socialists, and was a member of the Socialist International until December 2014.
