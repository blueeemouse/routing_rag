1926 Navy Midshipmen football team

The 1926 Navy Midshipmen football team represented the United States Naval Academy in the 1926 college football season.

 The Midshipmen were coached by Bill Ingram in his first year and finished the season undefeated with a record of nine wins, zero losses and one tie (9–0–1).

 Although Alabama and Stanford have been named the 1926 national champion by most selectors, the 1926 Navy team was named as the national champion under Boand and Houlgate Systems.
