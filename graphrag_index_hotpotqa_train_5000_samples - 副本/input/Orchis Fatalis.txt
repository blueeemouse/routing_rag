Orchis Fatalis

"Orchis Fatalis" is the third episode of the eighth season of British television show "Midsomer Murders" and the thirty-eighth episode overall.

 It stars John Nettles as Detective Chief Inspector Tom Barnaby and John Hopkins as Detective Sergeant Dan Scott.

 It concerns the discovery of a very rare orchid, the yellow roth, and a number of murders seemingly committed in an attempt to acquire it.
