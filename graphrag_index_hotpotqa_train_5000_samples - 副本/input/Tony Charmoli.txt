Tony Charmoli

Tony Charmoli (born June 11, 1922) is an American dancer, choreographer, and director.

 He began dancing on Broadway in such shows as "Make Mine Manhattan" but soon began choreographing for television with "Stop the Music" in 1949.

 Charmoli then choreographed dance sequences for the popular "Your Hit Parade", winning his first Emmy Award in 1955.

 He went on to direct and choreograph for some of the biggest stars including Dinah Shore, Lily Tomlin, Danny Kaye, Julie Andrews, Cyd Charisse, Shirley MacLaine, Mitzi Gaynor, and others.

 On Broadway, Tony choreographed "Ankles Aweigh" (1955) and "Woman of the Year" (1981) with Lauren Bacall.
