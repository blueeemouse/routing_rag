Zara Durrani

Zara Durrani is a Pakistani-Canadian actress and model, who currently hosts and produces CHCH television series "Life & Style with Zara".

 She was born and grew up in Pakistan, and later moved to Canada.

 She appeared in many commercials and some films and in Neil Simon's "Barefoot in the Park".

 She also models in so many fashion shows and as a host in many shows.
