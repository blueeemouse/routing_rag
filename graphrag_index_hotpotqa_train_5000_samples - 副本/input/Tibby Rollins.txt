Tibby Rollins

Tabitha Anastasia "Tibby" Tomko-Rollins is a fictional character in the 2001 novel "The Sisterhood of the Traveling Pants" and the 2005 film based upon it.

 She is a member of the titular club, along with her friends Lena Kaligaris, Bridget Vreeland and Carmen Lowell.

 She was portrayed by Amber Tamblyn in the film.
