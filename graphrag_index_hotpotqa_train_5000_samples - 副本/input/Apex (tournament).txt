Apex (tournament)

Apex is an annual esports tournament held in New Jersey that focused on fighting games, specifically "Super Smash Bros." The event's first incarnation was in 2009 with Jesus "Jman" Fernandez as the champion of "Super Smash Bros.

 Melee" and Elliot "Ally" Carroza-Oyarce as champion of "Super Smash Bros.

 Brawl".

 Each year the event grew with more competitors entering.

 Apex 2014 garnered 629 entrants and was the 2nd largest tournament for "Melee" at the time after EVO 2013.

 Though the tournament initially focused on "Brawl", the feature game has since switched to "Melee" due to its popularity.

 In 2010, an event for "Super Smash Bros." for the Nintendo 64 was added.

 In 2014, the fan modification of "Brawl", "Project M" was added for singles only.

 "Project M" has recently been removed as an official event out copyright concerns under Nintendo of America sponsorship and Third Party relations.
