Chili Con Carnage

Chili Con Carnage is an action/adventure third-person shooter video game.

 It was developed by Deadline Games and published by Sci Entertainment in Europe and by Eidos in North America.

 Its predecessor "Total Overdose" was released September 27, 2005.

 "Chili Con Carnage" is not actually a true sequel to "Total Overdose", as it goes by a different title and has a story that has nothing to do with "Total Overdose".

 Many reviews of the game classify it as a remake of "Total Overdose" for the PlayStation Portable.
