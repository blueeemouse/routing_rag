Origin of the Snow White tale

"Snow White" is a German fairy tale known across much of Europe and is today one of the most famous fairy tales worldwide.

 The Brothers Grimm published it in 1812 in the first edition of their collection "Grimms' Fairy Tales".

 It was titled in German: "Sneewittchen" (in modern orthography "Schneewittchen") and numbered as Tale 53.

 The Grimms completed their final revision of the story in 1854.

 It has generally been regarded as purely a tale of fiction.

 However, recent research suggests that the story may have been inspired from a real story.
