Christelyn Karazin

Christelyn Karazin is an American writer, columnist, and blogger on the subject of interracial dating, particularly black women dating outside their race.

 She hosts the blog "Beyond Black & White" and has written for "Woman's Day", "Ebony", "Jet", and Reuters.

 Karazin attended Loyola Marymount University, where she wrote for "The Los Angeles Loyolan".
