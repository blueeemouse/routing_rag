Scadoxus

Scadoxus is a genus of African and Arabian plants in the Amaryllis family, subfamily Amaryllidoideae.

 The English name "blood lily" is used for some of the species.

 The genus has close affinities with "Haemanthus."

 Species of "Scadoxus" are grown as ornamental plants for their brilliantly coloured flowers, either in containers or in the ground in frost-free climates.

 Although some species have been used in traditional medicine, they contain poisonous alkaloids.
