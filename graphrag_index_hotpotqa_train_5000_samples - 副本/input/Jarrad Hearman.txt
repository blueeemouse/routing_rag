Jarrad Hearman

Jarrad Hearman (born 26 June 1980) is a Recording, Mixing and Mastering Engineer, based in London, England.

 He has worked with artists including Katy B, Sinéad Harnett, Nadia Rose, Tinie Tempah, Kali Uchis, Keys N Krates, Craig David, P Money, Novelist, Mr Mitch, Tinashe, Wretch 32, Ian Brown, Taj Mahal, Toumani Diabate, and Tom Odell.
