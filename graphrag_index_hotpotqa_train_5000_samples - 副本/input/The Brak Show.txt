The Brak Show

The Brak Show is an American adult animated sitcom that aired on Adult Swim.

 "The Brak Show" was created by Andy Merrill, Jim Fortier, and Pete Smith, serving as a spin-off of the animated television series, "Space Ghost Coast to Coast", and featured recurring characters from "Space Ghost Coast to Coast" and "Cartoon Planet".

 Both programs used stock footage from the Hanna-Barbera cartoon "Space Ghost".

 The protagonist is Brak, voiced by Andy Merrill, who developed a quirky persona for the character.
