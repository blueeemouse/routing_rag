Rampage (2018 film)

Rampage is an upcoming American action adventure monster film directed by Brad Peyton and written by Ryan Engle.

 It is based on the 1980s arcade video game of the same name.

 The film stars Dwayne Johnson, Naomie Harris, Malin Åkerman, Joe Manganiello, Jake Lacy, Marley Shelton, and Jeffrey Dean Morgan.

 New Line Cinema will release the film on April 20, 2018 in 3D and IMAX.
