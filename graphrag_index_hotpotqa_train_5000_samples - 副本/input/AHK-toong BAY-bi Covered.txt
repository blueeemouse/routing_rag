AHK-toong BAY-bi Covered

AHK-toong BAY-bi Covered, stylized as (Ăhk-to͝ong Ba͞y-bi) Covered or (Ăℎk-to͝ong Ba͞y-bi) Covered, is a tribute album featuring cover versions of the 12 songs from U2's 1991 record "Achtung Baby".

 It was released on 26 October 2011 as a pack-in CD with the December 2011 issue of "Q" (issue #305).

 The magazine commissioned the album to commemorate the 20th anniversary of "Achtung Baby"' s original release and "Q"' s 25th anniversary.

 The magazine's editor-in-chief Paul Rees said, "This is an entirely appropriate way to mark "Q"' s anniversary and that of "Achtung Baby", one of the pivotal albums in our lifetime."

 The performing artists are a mix of U2's contemporaries and successors, including Jack White, Patti Smith, Nine Inch Nails, Depeche Mode, The Killers, and Garbage.

 Although a tribute album, "AHK-toong BAY-bi Covered" contains a remix of "Even Better Than the Real Thing" that is credited as performed by U2.
