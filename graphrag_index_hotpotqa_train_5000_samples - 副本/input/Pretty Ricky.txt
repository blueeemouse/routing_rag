Pretty Ricky

Pretty Ricky is an American R&B/hip hop group originated from Miami, Florida.

 Pretty Ricky originally consisted of two brothers: Ala Diamond "Baby Blue" Smith, "Spectacular" Blue Smith, cousin Corey "Slick em" Mathis and Family friend Marcus "Pleasure P" Cooper who is the group's only singer since the rest of them take roles as rappers.

 brothers: Ala Diamond "Baby Blue" Smith, "Spectacular" Blue Smith have the same father, Joseph "Blue" Smith Sr., and the same mother.
