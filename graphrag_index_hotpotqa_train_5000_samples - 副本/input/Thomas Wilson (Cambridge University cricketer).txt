Thomas Wilson (Cambridge University cricketer)

Thomas Ward Wilson (1 April 1849 – 4 January 1924) was an English first-class cricketer active 1869–1871 who played for Cambridge University.

 He was born in Nocton and died in Broadstone, Dorset.

 He appeared in eight first-class matches as a right-handed batsman who bowled fast or medium pace roundarm.

 Wilson scored 171 career runs with a highest score of 50; he held one catch and took 17 wickets with a best return of five for 25.
