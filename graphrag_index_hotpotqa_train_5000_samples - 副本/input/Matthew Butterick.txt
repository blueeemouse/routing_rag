Matthew Butterick

Matthew Butterick is an American typographer, lawyer, writer, and computer programmer.

 He received the 2012 Golden Pen Award from the Legal Writing Institute for his book "Typography for Lawyers", which started as a website in 2008 based on his experience as a practicing attorney.

 He has worked for The Font Bureau and founded his own website design company, Atomic Vision (purchased by Red Hat in 1999).

 Expanding "Typography for Lawyers", Butterick published "Practical Typography" as a "web-based book" in July 2013.
