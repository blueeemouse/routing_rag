West Town Mall

West Town Mall is an upscale shopping mall located in Knoxville, Tennessee, United States.

 Opened in August 1972, this one-level mall is located in the western portion of Knoxville in the West Hills community.

 West Town Mall is located along Interstates 40/75 and Kingston Pike.

 The mall has over 1300000 sqft of Gross leasable area, making it the largest of any enclosed shopping mall in Tennessee.
