Natasha Dupeyrón

Natasha Elizabeth Dupeyrón Estrada (born 3 June 1991 in Mexico City, Mexico) known professionally as Natasha Dupeyron, is a Mexican actress and singer.

 She has acted in various Mexican telenovelas since childhood.

 Dupeyrón was a member of the Mexican-Argentine pop band, Eme 15 from 2011 until 2014.
