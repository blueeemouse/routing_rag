Matt Urban

Matt Louis Urban (August 25, 1919 – March 4, 1995) was a United States Army lieutenant colonel who was one of the most decorated American infantry officers of World War II.

 He performed valiantly in combat on many occasions despite being wounded in action several times.

 Urban received over a dozen personal decorations for combat from the U.S. Army, including seven Purple Hearts.

 In 1980, he received the Medal of Honor and four other personal decorations for combat belatedly for his actions in France and Belgium in 1944.
