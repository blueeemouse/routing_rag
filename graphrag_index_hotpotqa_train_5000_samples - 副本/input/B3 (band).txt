B3 (band)

B3 was an American pop boy band from New York City.

 They were formed in 2001 and were first produced by O-Jay of German dance band Real McCoy.

 Original members were Timothy Andrew Cruz (2001–2004), John Steven Sutherland (2001–2004) and Rod Michael (2001–2002).

 When Michael left in 2002, Blair Madison Late (2002–2004) joined in.

 Tim Cruz was previously, in the late 1990s, a member of the boy band React.
