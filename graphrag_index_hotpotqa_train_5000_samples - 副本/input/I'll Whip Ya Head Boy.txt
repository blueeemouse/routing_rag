I'll Whip Ya Head Boy

"I'll Whip Ya Head Boy" is a song by American hip hop recording artist 50 Cent, released as the fourth and final single from the soundtrack to the film "Get Rich or Die Tryin'."

 The song features Young Buck and is the closing track on the album.

 It is played in the intro of the film.

 The official remix features M.O.P. and was released as a promo single to USA radio stations.
