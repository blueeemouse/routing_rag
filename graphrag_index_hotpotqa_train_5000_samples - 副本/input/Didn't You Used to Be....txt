Didn't You Used to Be...

Didn't You Used to Be... is the only album David Cassidy released on Scotti Brothers Records.

 The album was released in 1992.

 Originally titled, "Didn't You Used to be"?

, is David Cassidy's ninth studio album.

 The album features ten tracks which are all written or co-written by David Cassidy's wife, Sue Shifrin.
