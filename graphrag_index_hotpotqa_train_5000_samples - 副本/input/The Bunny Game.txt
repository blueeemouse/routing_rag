The Bunny Game

The Bunny Game is a 2010 low budget horror film co-created by Rodleen Getsic and Adam Rehmeier.

 Set in the desert, the film is about a prostitute who is abducted by a truck driver and subjected to extreme "games" of torture.

 The screenplay was co-written by Rehmeier and the film's lead female actress, Rodleen Getsic
