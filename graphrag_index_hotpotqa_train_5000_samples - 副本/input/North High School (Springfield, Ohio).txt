North High School (Springfield, Ohio)

North High School was a public high school in Springfield, Ohio.

 It was built in 1960.

 It was one of two high schools in the Springfield City Schools district, the other school being South High School.

 On September 8, 2008 the two schools were combined into Springfield High School and moved into a new building behind the old North High School.

 The North High School building was demolished on May 30, 2008 to make way for the new Springfield High School building.
