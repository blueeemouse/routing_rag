William Cooper (judge)

William Cooper (December 2, 1754 – December 22, 1809) was an American merchant, land speculator and developer, the founder of Cooperstown, New York.

 A politician, he was appointed as a county judge and later served two terms in the United States Congress, representing Otsego County and central New York.

 He was the father of James Fenimore Cooper, who became a noted writer of historical novels related to the New York frontier.
