Rosicrucian Order Crotona Fellowship

The Rosicrucian Order Crotona Fellowship was a Rosicrucian group founded by George Alexander Sullivan in about 1924.

 It may have existed under the name Order of Twelve from 1911–1914 and again from 1920.

 The ROCF operated first from the Liverpool area of England and then after the mid-1930s from the Christchurch area.

 Its members studied esoteric subjects from lectures, plays and correspondence material prepared by George Alexander Sullivan.
