Texas A&amp;M University at Galveston

Texas A&M University at Galveston (TAMUG) is an ocean-oriented branch campus of Texas A&M University offering both undergraduate and graduate degrees that are awarded from Texas A&M University in College Station.

 Students enrolled at Texas A&M University at Galveston, known affectionately as 'Sea Aggies', share all the benefits of students attending Texas A&M University (TAMU) campus in College Station.

 TAMUG is strategically located on Pelican Island, offering many benefits the ocean has to offer for its maritime focused majors.
