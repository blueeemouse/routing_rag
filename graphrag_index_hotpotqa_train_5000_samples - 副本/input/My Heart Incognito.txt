My Heart Incognito

My Heart Incognito (French:Mon coeur incognito) is a 1931 comedy film directed by André-Paul Antoine and Manfred Noa and starring Mady Christians.

 It was made by Germany's Aafa-Film as the French-language version of "Lieutenant, Were You Once a Hussar?

".

 Such multi-language versions were common during the early years of sound.
