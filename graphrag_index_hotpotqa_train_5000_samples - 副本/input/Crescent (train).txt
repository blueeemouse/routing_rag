Crescent (train)

The Crescent is a passenger train operated by Amtrak in the eastern part of the United States.

 It operates 1377 mi daily between Pennsylvania Station in New York City and Union Passenger Terminal in New Orleans, as train numbers 19 and 20.

 Most of the route of the "Crescent" is on the Norfolk Southern Railway.

 The "Crescent" passes through more states, including the District of Columbia, than any other Amtrak route.
