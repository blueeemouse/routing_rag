The Glory Guys

The Glory Guys is a 1965 American film based on the novel "The Dice of God" by Hoffman Birney.

 Filmed by Levy-Gardner-Laven and released by United Artists, it stars Tom Tryon, Harve Presnell, Senta Berger, James Caan, and Michael Anderson, Jr. The film's screenplay was written by Sam Peckinpah long before the 1965 film was made.

 The director was Arnold Laven.

 Riz Ortolani composed the score and the title song.
