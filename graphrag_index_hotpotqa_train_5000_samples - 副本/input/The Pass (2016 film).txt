The Pass (2016 film)

The Pass is a 2016 film starring Russell Tovey and Arinze Kene.

 It was directed by Ben A. Williams, based on a play by John Donnelly.

 The film deals with the issue of a homosexual relationship between two English football players, and how their lives unfold over the course of a decade.

 The film was nominated at the 2017 BAFTA Awards, in the category of "Outstanding Debut by a British Writer, Director or Producer" for John Donnelly (writing) and Ben Williams (direction).
