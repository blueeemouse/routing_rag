Wimmera Regional Library Corporation

The Wimmera Regional Library Corporation is situated in the northwest of Victoria, Australia.

 It covers an area of approximately 36,800 km.

 and serves six local government bodies - Buloke Shire Council, Hindmarsh Shire Council, Horsham Rural City Council, Northern Grampians Shire Council, West Wimmera Shire Council and Yarriambiack Shire Council.

 The Shires have a combined population of approximately 52,000.

 As these figures indicate, its clientele is predominantly rural-based and often has considerable distances between centres.
