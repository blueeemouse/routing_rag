Get Me Bodied

"Get Me Bodied" is a song recorded by American singer Beyoncé for her second studio album, "B'Day" (2006).

 It was written by Beyoncé, Kasseem "Swizz Beatz" Dean, Sean Garrett, Makeba Riddick, Angela Beyincé, and Solange Knowles, while the production was handled by Dean, Beyoncé Knowles and Sean Garrett.

 Beyoncé was inspired by her sister, Solange, and former Destiny's Child band-mates Kelly Rowland and Michelle Williams for the writing process.

 Columbia Records released "Get Me Bodied" as the album's seventh and final single in the US on July 10, 2007.
