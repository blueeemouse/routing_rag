Andy Borowitz

Andy Borowitz (born January 4, 1958) is an American writer, comedian, satirist, and actor.

 Borowitz is a "New York Times"-bestselling author who won the first National Press Club award for humor.

 He is known for creating "The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air" and the satirical column "The Borowitz Report".

 In a profile on "CBS News Sunday Morning", he was called "one of the funniest people in America".
