Helmut Anheier

Helmut K. Anheier (born January 4, 1954) is a German-American academic currently serving as President and Dean of the Hertie School of Governance in Berlin.

 He holds a chair at the Max Weber Institute of Sociology, Heidelberg University, where he is also the Academic Director of the Center for Social Investment and Innovation.

 His research interests include civil society, social innovation, organizational theory, governance and policy research, social science methodology, including indicator models
