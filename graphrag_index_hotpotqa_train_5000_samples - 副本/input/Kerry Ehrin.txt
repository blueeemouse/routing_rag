Kerry Ehrin

Kerry Anne Ehrin (born October 8, 1960) is an American screenwriter, showrunner, and producer.

 In 1990, she was nominated for the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Comedy Series for her work on the ABC comedy-drama series "The Wonder Years".

 From 2006 until its ending in 2011, she worked as a consulting producer on the NBC drama series "Friday Night Lights".

 She was a showrunner of the A&E drama series "Bates Motel", which aired from 2013 to 2017.
