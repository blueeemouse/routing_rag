Gas turbine engine compressors

As the name suggests, gas turbine engine compressors provide the compression part of the gas turbine engine thermodynamic cycle.

 There are three basic categories of gas turbine engine compressor: axial compressor, centrifugal compressor and mixed flow compressor.

 A fourth, unusual, type is the free-piston gas generator, which combines the functions of compressor and combustion chamber in one unit.
