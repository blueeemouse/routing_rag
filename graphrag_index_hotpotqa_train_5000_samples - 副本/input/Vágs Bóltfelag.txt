Vágs Bóltfelag

Vágs Bóltfelag (VB) is a Faroese handball club.

 It started as a football club, which was founded on 5 June 1905, but later the sport handball became a part of the club.

 The handball club is based in Vágur in Suðuroy.

 They play their home matches in the sports hall, Vágshøll on Eiðinum in Vágur.

 The football club is now known as FC Suðuroy.
