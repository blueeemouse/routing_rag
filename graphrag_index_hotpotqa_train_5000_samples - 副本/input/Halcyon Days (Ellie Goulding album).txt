Halcyon Days (Ellie Goulding album)

Halcyon Days is the reissue of English singer Ellie Goulding's second studio album, "Halcyon" (2012).

 It was released on 23 August 2013 by Polydor Records.

 Following the release of "Halcyon", Goulding announced that she had plans to repackage the original album, with new songs that focused on electronic music.

 For the reissue, Goulding recorded 10 new songs and worked with a variety of producers, including Ryan Tedder, Calvin Harris and Greg Kurstin, to create her desired sound.
