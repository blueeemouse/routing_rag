Marius Petipa

Marius Ivanovich Petipa (Russian: Ма́риус Ива́нович Петипа́), born Victor Marius Alphonse Petipa (11 March 181814 July [O.S. 1 July] 1910 ) was a French and Russian ballet dancer, pedagogue and choreographer.

 Petipa is considered to be the most influential ballet master and choreographer in ballet history.
