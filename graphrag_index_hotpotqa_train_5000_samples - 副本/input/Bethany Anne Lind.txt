Bethany Anne Lind

Bethany Anne Lind is an American stage and screen actor in Atlanta, Georgia and New York City, New York.

 She is best known for creating roles in world premieres such as Margo in "Carapace"" (at Alliance Theatre), Olivia in "26 Miles" (at Alliance Theatre), and Dora in "The Storytelling Ability of a Boy" (at Florida Stage).
