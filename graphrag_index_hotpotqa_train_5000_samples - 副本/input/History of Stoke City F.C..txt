History of Stoke City F.C.

Stoke City Football Club was formed in 1863 as Stoke Ramblers Football Club by former pupils of the Charterhouse School whilst they were apprentices at the North Staffordshire Railway.

 The club dropped the Ramblers from their name in 1878, and in 1888 they were founding members of the Football League.

 In 1925, the club's name was changed for the final time to Stoke City Football Club when Stoke-on-Trent was granted city status.
