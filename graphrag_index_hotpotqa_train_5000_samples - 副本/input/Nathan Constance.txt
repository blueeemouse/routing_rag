Nathan Constance

Nathan Constance (born 7 January 1979 in London) is an English actor.

 He is best known for his role as Josh Mitchell in the TV series "Bad Girls", Ian Walmsley in the TV series "Footballers' Wives" and Leon Richards in the TV series Dream Team .

 Amongst his film roles is Zac in the 2008 film "Adulthood", the sequel to the 2006 film "Kidulthood", and Chum in the 2006 film "London to Brighton".

 In 2009, he portrayed DC Connor in the BBC One series "Hunter".
