Baaghi 2

Baaghi 2 is an upcoming Indian martial arts film directed by Ahmed Khan, written by Sanjeev Datta and produced by Sajid Nadiadwala under the banners Nadiadwala Grandson Entertainment and Fox Star Studios.

 The film features Tiger Shroff and Disha Patani in lead roles.

 It is a sequel to the 2016 film "Baaghi."
