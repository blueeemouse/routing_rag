Aidan Hawtin

Aidan Hawtin (born 13 June 1995) is an English former professional footballer who played for Oxford United in League Two in England, and for Valdres in the fourth tier of the Norwegian football league system.

 He made his English Football League debut as a substitute for Oxford United during a 3–0 win away at Tranmere Rovers.

 His contract was cancelled in March 2016 as he joined Valdres with fellow Oxford midfielder Sam Humphreys.
