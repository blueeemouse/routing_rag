Everytime We Touch (Cascada song)

“Everytime We Touch” is a cover song performed by the German Eurodance recording trio Cascada, taken from their 2006 debut album of the same name.

 It was written, composed, and produced by the band's DJs, Manian and Yanou; however, the only writing/composing credits were given to Maggie Reilly, Stuart Mackilliop, and Peter Risavy, as the song borrows the chorus from Reilly's single of the same name.

 "Everytime We Touch" was first released in the United States on August 16, 2005 by Robbins Entertainment.

 It was later released internationally in association with other dance music labels including Zooland Records and All Around the World and Universal Music Group following its success in the United States.

 Musically, the song was composed as a Eurodance track with a pulsating synthesizer, jackhammer beat, and Europop lyrics.
