Alice Cancel

Alice Cancel is the former Assembly member for the 65th District of the New York State Assembly.

 She is a Democrat.

 The district included the neighborhoods of the Lower East Side, East Village, Little Italy, Chinatown, SoHo, TriBeCa, the Financial District, Battery Park and Governor's Island in Manhattan.

 In 2016, Cancel was defeated in the Democratic primary for her seat by Yuh-Line Niou.
