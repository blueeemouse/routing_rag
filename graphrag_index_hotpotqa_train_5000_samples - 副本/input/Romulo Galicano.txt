Romulo Galicano

Romulo Galicano (born February 4, 1945, in Carcar Cebu) is a Filipino painter whose works are technically academic and philosophical approach.

 His paintings shows invoice and broad meaning that can be depicted through canvas.

 He had numerous one man show and won various awards.

 He had been judge for several competitions like in PLDT-DPC Telephone cover 8th visual art national and UST on-the-spot painting competition.
