Tata 407

The Tata 407 is a light commercial vehicle manufactured by Tata Motors.

 The 1986 407 model sold more than 500,000 units.

 The 407 has a payload of 2.25 tonnes, an overall length of 4.7 metres, and a turning circle radius of 5.5 metres.

 Tata purchased all of the pressing tools for Steyr's 591/691-series of trucks in the mid-eighties, a truck which was itself derived from the 1967 Hanomag F-series.
