Sanna Lundell

Sanna Cecilia Lundell (born 6 November 1978) is a Swedish freelance journalist and television presenter.

 She is the daughter of the singer Ulf Lundell.

 She has done journalistic work for the magazines Mama, M-Magasin, Hennes, and newspaper "Aftonbladet".

 She has also been working as a television presenter at TV3 with shows such as "Sanning och konsekvens" along with Robert Aschberg, and "Stalkers" with Hasse Aro.

 Since June 2013 she has been presenting the documentary series "TV3 Dokumentär".
