Héroes del Silencio discography

Héroes del Silencio is a Spanish rock band from Zaragoza (Aragon, Spain) formed by Juan Valdivia in 1984.

 In 1988 they released their official first studio album, "El Mar No Cesa", then in 1990 they released their second album "Senderos de Traición", "El Espíritu del Vino" was their third album released in 1993 and in 1995 they released their last studio album to date, the often praised "Avalancha".
