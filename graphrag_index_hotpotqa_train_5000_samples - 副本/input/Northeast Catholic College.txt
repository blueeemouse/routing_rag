Northeast Catholic College

Northeast Catholic College (formerly The College of Saint Mary Magdalen) is a non-profit, liberal arts, co-educational, four-year Catholic college located in Warner, New Hampshire, United States.

 It is recognized as a Catholic college by the Diocese of Manchester and recommended by the Cardinal Newman Society.

 NCC offers associates and bachelor's degrees in liberal studies.

 Its curriculum is based on the study of the Great Books, both in its core and in its majors.

 NCC possesses degree-granting authority from the State of New Hampshire and candidacy for accreditation from the New England Association of Schools and Colleges.
