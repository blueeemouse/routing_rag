2010 FINA Diving World Cup

The 2010 FINA Diving World Cup was held in Changzhou, Jiangsu, People's Republic of China from June 2 to June 6, 2010.

 It was the 17th FINA Diving World Cup competition.

 146 divers from 33 countries and regions competed in this World Cup.

 The 2010 FINA Diving World Cup is held one year after the 2009 FINA Diving World Championships which was held in Rome.
