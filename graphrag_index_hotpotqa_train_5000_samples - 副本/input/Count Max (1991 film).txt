Count Max (1991 film)

Count Max (Italian:Il conte Max) is a 1991 French-Italian comedy film directed by Christian De Sica and starring De Sica, Ornella Muti and Galeazzo Benti.

 It is a remake of the 1957 film "Count Max", which was itself a remake of the 1937 film "Il signor Max".

 Both films had starred Christian De Sica's father Vittorio De Sica.
