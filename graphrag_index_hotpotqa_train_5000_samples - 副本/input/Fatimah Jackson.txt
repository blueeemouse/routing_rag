Fatimah Jackson

Fatimah Linda Collier Jackson is an African American biologist and anthropologist.

 She received her B.A. (cum laude and with distinction in all subjects), M.A., and Ph.D. from Cornell University.

 She was a professor of biological anthropology at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (UNC).

 She now teaches at Howard University in Washington, D.C.

 She became professor emerita of applied biological anthropology at the University of Maryland after teaching there for 20 years.

 She is the recipient of the Distinguished Scholar Teacher Award from the University of Maryland in 1995.
