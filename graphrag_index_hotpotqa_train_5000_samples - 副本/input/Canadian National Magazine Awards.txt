Canadian National Magazine Awards

The National Magazine Awards Foundation (NMAF) is a bilingual, not-for-profit institution whose mission is to recognize excellence in the content and creation of Canadian magazines through an annual program of awards.

 The National Magazine Awards gala takes place each June in Toronto.

 Each year the NMAF relies on over 100 volunteer judges to evaluate the entries and award gold and silver winners in the written, visual, integrated and special categories.
