Shaunie O'Neal

Shaunie O'Neal is the current executive producer of VH-1’s reality TV series, "Basketball Wives", "Basketball Wives LA", and Baller Wives, and is the former wife of NBA center Shaquille O’Neal.

 Formerly a film marketer, O'Neal is, as of May 2012, a TV personality and producer, entrepreneur, philanthropist and mother of five children.
