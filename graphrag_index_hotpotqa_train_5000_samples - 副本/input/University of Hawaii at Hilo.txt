University of Hawaii at Hilo

The University of Hawaiʻ i at Hilo or UH Hilo is a public co-educational university in Hilo, Hawaiʻ i, United States.

 It is one of ten branches of the University of Hawaiʻ i system.

 It was founded as Hawaiʻ i Vocational College (Hawaiʻ i College) in 1941.

 In 1970 it was reorganized by an act of the Hawaiʻ i State Legislature.
