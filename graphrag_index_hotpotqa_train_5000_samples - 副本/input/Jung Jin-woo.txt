Jung Jin-woo

Jung Jin-woo (born January 17, 1938) is a South Korean film director and producer.

 Jung made his directorial debut with "The Only Son" (1963) at the age of 23, setting the Korean record for the youngest person to direct a movie.

 After finishing "Early Rain" (1966), one of the most famous teenage movies of the 1960s, he went on to direct "The Ran's Elegy" (1965), "The Secret Meeting" (1965), and "The Student Boarder" (1966), showcasing his superb skills as a film director.
