Neotango

Neotango is a distinct genre of Tango which goes beyond it both in music and in dance.

 The music is a container of tracks from all over the World, instrumental or vocal, clearly distinct from the tango that preceded it because it includes only modern music, everything which was recorded in the last 30/40 years and which can be danced using the tango biomechanics.

 As a dance form it is currently evolving.

 It is the 'living' globalized tango dance form of the 21st Century.
