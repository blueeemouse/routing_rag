Edward Leier

Edward "Eddie" Leier (born 3 November 1927) is a Polish-born former baseball player, track athlete, and ice hockey player.

 He played two seasons with the Chicago Black Hawks of the National Hockey League.

 He was named to the Manitoba Junior Hockey League Second All-Star Team in 1948.

 On 29 September 1948 he signed as a free agent to the Saskatoon Quakers of the Western Canada Senior Hockey League.
