Come What May (2001 song)

"Come What May" is a song composed by David Baerwald and Kevin Gilbert, originally intended for the film "William Shakespeare's Romeo + Juliet".

 However, it was first heard publicly, and is best known as the romantic love theme from Baz Luhrmann's 2001 film "Moulin Rouge!

", where it is sung by Ewan McGregor and Nicole Kidman in their respective roles as Christian and Satine.
