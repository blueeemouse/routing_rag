Pieter Cnoll

Pieter Cnoll, also Pieter Knoll (1672), was a Dutchman who became Director General in Batavia.

 He married Cornelia van Nijenroode, an Indo Eurasian who was the daughter of Cornelis van Nieuwroode (also Cornelis van Nijenroode, former VOC Opperhoofden in Japan in 1623-1631) with a Japanese woman named Surishia.

 His family was depicted in a painting by Jacob Coeman.
