Larry Santos

Larry Santos (born 2 June 1941 in Oneonta, New York) is an American pop music singer-songwriter.

 Santos wrote songs for several American pop bands in the 1960s, including the 1963 hit "Candy Girl" for The Four Seasons which reached number 3 on "Billboard" Hot 100 chart.

 In the mid-Sixties, he recorded singles as part of a group called The Madisons, and then under his own name.

 He released three albums in the 1970s and scored one pop hit single, produced by Don Davis, "We Can't Hide it Anymore", which peaked on 10 April 1976 at number 36 on the Billboard Hot 100.
