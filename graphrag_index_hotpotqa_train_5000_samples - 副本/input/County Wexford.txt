County Wexford

County Wexford (Irish: "Contae Loch Garman" , Yola: Weiseforthe) is a county in Ireland.

 It is in the province of Leinster and is part of the South-East Region.

 It is named after the town of Wexford and was based on the historic Gaelic territory of Hy Kinsella ("Uí Ceinnsealaigh"), whose capital was Ferns.

 Wexford County Council is the local authority for the county.

 The population of the county is 145,273 according to the 2011 census.
