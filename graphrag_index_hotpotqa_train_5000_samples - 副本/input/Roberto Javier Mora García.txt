Roberto Javier Mora García

Roberto Javier Mora García (c. 1962 – 16 March 2004) was a Mexican journalist and editorial director of "El Mañana", a newspaper based in Nuevo Laredo, Tamaulipas, Mexico.

 He worked for a number of media outlets in Mexico, including the "El Norte" and "El Diario de Monterrey", prior to his assassination.
