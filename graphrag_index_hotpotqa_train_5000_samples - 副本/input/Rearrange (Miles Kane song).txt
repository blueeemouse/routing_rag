Rearrange (Miles Kane song)

"Rearrange" is a song by the English musician Miles Kane from his debut studio album "Colour of the Trap".

 It was released on 25 March 2011 as a Digital download in the United Kingdom.

 It has peaked to number 149 on the UK Singles Chart.

 A remix by popular dubstep producer Skream was also included on the single's digital release.
