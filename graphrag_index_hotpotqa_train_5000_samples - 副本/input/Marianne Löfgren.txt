Marianne Löfgren

Marianne Löfgren (24 February 1910 – 4 September 1957) was a Swedish actress.

 She played in Åke Ohberg's "Elvira Madigan" (1943), director Ingmar Bergman's debut "Crisis" in 1946, Hasse Ekman's "Girl with Hyacinths" (1950) and in over a hundred other films in her native Sweden.
