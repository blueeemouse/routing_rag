List of Maho Girls PreCure! episodes

"Maho Girls PreCure!

" is the thirteenth anime television series in Izumi Todo's "Pretty Cure" franchise, produced by Asahi Broadcasting Corporation and Toei Animation.

 The series follows a girl named Mirai Asahina and a magician named Liko Izayoi, who both attend Magic School whilst also becoming the Maho Girls Pretty Cure to fight off the evil Dokuroxy.

 The series aired on ABC in Japan between February 7, 2016 and January 29, 2017, replacing "Go!
