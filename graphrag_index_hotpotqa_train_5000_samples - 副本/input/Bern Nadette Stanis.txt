Bern Nadette Stanis

Bern Nadette Stanis (born Bernadette Stanislaus, December 22, 1953) is an American actress and author.

 Stanis is best known for her role as Thelma Ann Evans–Anderson, the only daughter of Florida and James Evans, Sr. on the CBS sitcom "Good Times" which originally ran from 1974 to 1979.

 Stanis is the author of four books: "Situations 101: Relationships, The Good, The Bad & The Ugly"; "For Men Only"; "Situations 101: Finances"; and "The Last Night".
