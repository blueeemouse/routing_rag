Oleg Yashchuk

Oleg Yashchuk (Ukrainian: Олег Ростиславович Ящук , born 26 October 1977 in (Hrynky Rural-rada), Lanivtsi Raion, Ternopil Oblast then Ukrainian SSR) is a Ukrainian professional footballer.

 He ended his career as a striker for BX Brussels and also holds the Belgian nationality.

 His last name is sometimes transliterated as Iachtchouk or Jasjtsjoek.

 He now coaches the U14 division of R.S.C. Anderlecht.
