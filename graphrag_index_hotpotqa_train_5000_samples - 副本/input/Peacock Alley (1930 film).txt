Peacock Alley (1930 film)

Peacock Alley is a 1930 American musical romantic drama film directed by Marcel de Sano, and starring Mae Murray and George Barraud.

 The film is a remake of the 1922 silent film of the same name in which Murray also stars.

 Aside from Murray being cast in the lead, the remake was largely different from the 1922 silent film.

 While Murray's character in the 1922 film was named Cleo, she was renamed Claire Tree in this film.

 George Barraud replaced Monte Blue as the male lead, who is now named Clayton Stoddard.
