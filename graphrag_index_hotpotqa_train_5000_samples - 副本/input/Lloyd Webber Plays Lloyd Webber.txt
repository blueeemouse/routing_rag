Lloyd Webber Plays Lloyd Webber

Lloyd Webber Plays Lloyd Webber is a 2001 album by British cellist Julian Lloyd Webber.

 For the album, the musician chose to interpret songs written by his older brother, the popular musical theatre composer Andrew Lloyd Webber.

 The album was recorded with the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra.

 The album primarily featured classical musicians with the notable exception of Rod Argent of The Zombies.
