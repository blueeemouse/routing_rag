All Saints Church, Kedleston

All Saints Church, Kedleston, is a redundant Anglican church standing adjacent to Kedleston Hall, a country house in Derbyshire, England.

 It is recorded in the National Heritage List for England as a designated Grade I listed building.

 Kedleston Hall is owned by the National Trust, and the church is under the care of the Churches Conservation Trust.

 Kedleston Hall has been occupied by the Curzon family for over 700 years.
