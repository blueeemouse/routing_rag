Warrior on the Edge of Time

Warrior on the Edge of Time is Hawkwind's fifth studio album.

 It reached number 13 on the U.K. album charts and was their third and last album to make the U.S. "Billboard" chart, where it peaked at number 150.

 Many of the lyrics are by Michael Moorcock and the album is loosely based on the concept of Moorcock's 'Eternal Champion'.

 Reviews have been mixed, with "Melody Maker" panning the album and particularly criticizing the vocal work while the All Music Guide has praised the album for features such as the songwriting.
