Brush-tailed rock-wallaby

The brush-tailed rock-wallaby or small-eared rock-wallaby ("Petrogale penicillata") is a kind of wallaby, one of several rock-wallabies in the genus "Petrogale".

 It inhabits rock piles and cliff lines along the Great Dividing Range from about 100 km north-west of Brisbane to northern Victoria, in vegetation ranging from rainforest to dry sclerophyl forests.

 Populations have declined seriously in the south and west of its range, but it remains locally common in northern New South Wales and southern Queensland.
