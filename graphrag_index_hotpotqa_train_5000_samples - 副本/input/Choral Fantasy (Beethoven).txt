Choral Fantasy (Beethoven)

The Fantasy (Fantasia) for piano, vocal soloists, chorus, and orchestra, Op.

 80 , was composed in 1808 by Ludwig van Beethoven.

 It is known as the Choral Fantasy ("Chorphantasie").

 Beethoven wrote the piece to conclude the "Akademie", a benefit concert for him on 22 December 1808, performed by soloists, chorus and orchestra.

 The long program presented also the first performances of his Fifth and Sixth Symphonies and the Fourth Piano Concerto.

 Beethoven was the composer of all pieces, the pianist and the conductor.
