Cartoon Network (Japan)

Cartoon Network (Japanese: カートゥーン ネットワーク , Hepburn: Kātūn Nettowāku ) is a Japanese cable and satellite television channel that mostly airs children's animated television series.

 As a Japanese version of the eponymous television channel in the United States, Cartoon Network broadcasts original series from its U.S. counterpart, as well as several Japanese animated media and other non-Japanese programs.

 An amount of non-Japanese programs are also available in English via secondary audio feed.

 It is one of few versions of Cartoon Network outside U.S. that broadcasts its programs in High Definition.
