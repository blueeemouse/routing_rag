Seven Lives Many Faces

Seven Lives Many Faces is the seventh studio album by German musical project Enigma.

 It is Enigma's 7th album.

 The album was released on 19 September 2008 worldwide and on 30 September 2008 in North America.

 Michael Cretu stated that the new album will feature an omnicultural sound unlike anything on its previous releases.

 On 12 September, the album was released on Enigma's MySpace page for pre-listening.

 After enjoying a huge success on MySpace (with over 400,000 listens in 2 days), several international MTV and VH1 web sites started streaming the album until 22 September.
