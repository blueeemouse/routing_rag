Nantwich Town Council

Nantwich Town Council is the town council for the Cheshire market town of Nantwich.

 It was established in 1974 as a successor council to the Nantwich Urban District Council.

 The last elections were held in May 2015 which saw the Conservative Party win 8 of the 12 seats.

 The Council is split into two wards, Nantwich North and West (5) and Nantwich South (7).

 It primarily raises funds through a precept on Council Tax and has the highest Band D in Cheshire East.
