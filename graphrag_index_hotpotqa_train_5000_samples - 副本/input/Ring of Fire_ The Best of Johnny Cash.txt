Ring of Fire: The Best of Johnny Cash

Ring of Fire: The Best of Johnny Cash is the sixteenth album in total by the singer-songwriter Johnny Cash, released on Columbia Records in 1963 (see 1963 in music).

 This album collects tracks from singles and an EP released between 1959 and 1963, Cash's first years on the Columbia label, and marked the first release of these tracks in LP format, with the exception of "I Still Miss Someone," which had previously appeared on the 1958 album "The Fabulous Johnny Cash". "

Ring of Fire", one of Cash's most famous tracks, made its first LP appearance here.

 "Ring of Fire" was the first #1 album when Billboard debuted their Country Album Chart on Jan. 11, 1964.

 It was certified Gold on 2/11/1965 by the RIAA.

 It stands as the only Columbia "greatest hits" collection to be included in the "" box set.
