Kawasaki KH-4

The Kawasaki KH-4 was a light utility helicopter produced in Japan in the 1960s as a development of the Bell 47 that Kawasaki had been building under licence since 1952.

 The most visible difference between the KH-4 and its forerunner was its new and enlarged cabin.

 This was fully enclosed (although the side doors were removable) and provided seating for three passengers side-by-side on a bench seat behind the pilot's seat.

 The helicopter was provided with a new control system, revised instrumentation, and larger fuel tank.
