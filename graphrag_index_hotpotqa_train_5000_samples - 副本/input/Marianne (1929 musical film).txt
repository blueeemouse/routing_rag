Marianne (1929 musical film)

Marianne is a 1929 pre-Code romantic musical drama about a French farm girl who, despite already having a French fiancé, falls in love with an American soldier during World War I.

 It is a remake of a silent film that was made and released earlier in 1929.

 Although the films feature mostly different casts, Marion Davies starred in both versions.

 This was Davies' first released talking movie.
