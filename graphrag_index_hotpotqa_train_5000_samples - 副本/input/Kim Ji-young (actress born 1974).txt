Kim Ji-young (actress born 1974)

Kim Ji-young (born September 7, 1974) is a South Korean actress.

 She won several Best Supporting Actress awards for playing an indomitable handball player in the 2008 sports drama "Forever the Moment".

 Kim also starred in leading roles in the televisions series "My Lovely Fool" (2006), "Two Wives" (2009), "Marry Me, Please" (also known as "All About Marriage", 2010), and "Everybody, Kimchi!

" (2014), as well as the film "Touch" (2012).
