Billy the Kid (Charlton Comics)

Billy the Kid is a Western comic book series published by Charlton Comics, with stories of a fictional character based on the historical Billy the Kid.

 Taking over the numbering of a previous Western comic, Masked Raider, "Billy the Kid" was published from issues #9-153 (Nov. 1957 - March 1983).

 The Billy the Kid character made his first appearance in "Masked Raider" #6.
