Angel in Disguise (McCartney–Starr song)

"Angel in Disguise" is a song co-written by Paul McCartney and Ringo Starr that has never been released.

 The song was intended for Ringo's 1992 "Time Takes Time" album; however, it did not make it onto the album, and therefore remains an intriguing, publicly unheard song.

 Had it been released in 1992 it would have been the first McCartney-Starkey composition on record, although there were Beatles songs that were credited to Lennon–McCartney–Starkey and Lennon–McCartney–Harrison–Starkey.
