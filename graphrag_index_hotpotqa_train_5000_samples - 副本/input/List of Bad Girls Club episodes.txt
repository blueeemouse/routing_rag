List of Bad Girls Club episodes

The following is a list of episodes of the American reality television series "Bad Girls Club" broadcast on Oxygen.

 It was first shown on December 5, 2006 and ended on May 2, 2017.

 As of 2017, 275 episodes have aired, including specials.

 "Bad Girls Club" has spawned four spin-off series: "Bad Girls Road Trip", "Bad Girls All-Star Battle", "" and "Tanisha Gets Married".
