Mercury (toy manufacturer)

Mercury was a brand of diecast toy cars manufactured in Italy from about 1945 through the 1970s.

 Along with Dinky Toys in England, Mercury was a pioneer in 1:43 scale diecast toys made in Europe.

 Today, Mercury models are rather rare and not easy to find.

 The company logo was the word Mercury within a rectangle with a round toothed gear behind the company name.
