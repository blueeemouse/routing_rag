Suzuki RG150

The Suzuki RG150 was a 148 cc two-stroke road motorcycle produced by Suzuki in Thailand.

 The bike was produced from 1996 until 2000.

 In 1998, it was selected as the second-best new motorcycle of 1998 by "Motor Trend" magazine.

 It was produced almost exclusively for Thailand although around 100 were exported to New Zealand.
