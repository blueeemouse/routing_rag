Qila (film)

Qila (Hindi: क़िला, Urdu: قلعہ, translation: "Fort") is a 1998 Bollywood Hindi language film directed by Umesh Mehra and starring Dilip Kumar (playing a dual role), Rekha, Mukul Dev, Mamta Kulkarni, Smita Jaykar and Gulshan Grover.

 This film marked the final film appearance of veteran actor Dilip Kumar.

 The movie rights is now owned by Red Chillies Entertainment.
