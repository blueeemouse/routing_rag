DeMar DeRozan

DeMar Darnell DeRozan (born August 7, 1989) is an American professional basketball player for the Toronto Raptors of the National Basketball Association (NBA).

 He played college basketball for USC and was selected ninth overall by the Raptors in the 2009 NBA draft.

 In 2017, he was named an NBA All-Star for the third time in four years, and was named an All-NBA Team member.

 DeRozan has played for the United States men's national basketball team in two major tournaments: the 2014 World Cup and the 2016 Summer Olympics.
