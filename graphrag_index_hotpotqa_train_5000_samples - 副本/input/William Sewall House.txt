William Sewall House

The William Sewall House is a historic house at 1027 Crystal Road in Island Falls, Maine.

 Built in 1865, it is a well-preserved example of vernacular Greek Revival style in a rural setting.

 The house is notable as the longtime home of a second-generation resident of the community, William Wingate Sewall, who had a long and enduring friendship with Theodore Roosevelt, a regular guest until 1878.

 The house was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1982.

 It is still in the hands of Sewall descendants, operated as a yoga retreat center.
