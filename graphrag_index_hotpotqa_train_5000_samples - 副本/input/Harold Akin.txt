Harold Akin

Harold Dwayne Akin (born January 11, 1945 in McAlester, Oklahoma) was an American football player.

 An offensive tackle, he played college football at Oklahoma State University, and played professionally in the American Football League for the San Diego Chargers in 1967 and 1968.

 He later went on to found The Budget Floor Store in Oklahoma City, which now has 4 locations across Oklahoma.
