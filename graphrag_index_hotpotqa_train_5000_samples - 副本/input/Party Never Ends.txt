Party Never Ends

Party Never Ends is the third studio album recorded by Romanian singer Inna, released on 4 March 2013 by Roton.

 The singer collaborated with several producers on the record, including DJ Frank E, Orange Factory, Steve Mac, Lucas Secon, DJ Smash and Play & Win.

 "Party Never Ends" has been described as approaching dance music and its subgenres, with influences varying from Latin, hip hop and house to electropop, salsa and dubstep. "

The Times of India" gave the album a mixed review; while commending some tracks, other were deemed as passable and mediocre.
