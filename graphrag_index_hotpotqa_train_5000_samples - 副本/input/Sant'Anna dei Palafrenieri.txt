Sant'Anna dei Palafrenieri

The Church of Saint Anne in the Vatican (Italian: "Sant'Anna in Vaticano" ), known as "Sant'Anna de' Parafrenieri " (English: Saint Anne of the Grooms ), is a Roman Catholic parish church dedicated to Saint Anne in Vatican City.

 The church is the parish church of the State of Vatican City and is placed under the jurisdiction of the Vicariate of the Vatican City and is located beside the Porta Sant'Anna (Saint Anne's Gate), an international border crossing between Vatican City State and Italy.
