Jim the Penman (1921 film)

Jim the Penman is a 1921 American silent crime drama film produced by Whitman Bennett and distributed through Associated First National, later just First National Pictures.

 It is based on a well known play, "Jim the Penman" by Charles Lawrence Young about a forger in Victorian Britain.

 The film stars Lionel Barrymore and was directed by Kenneth Webb, the duo having worked on "The Great Adventure" previously.

 "Jim the Penman" is preserved though incomplete (reel 5 missing) at the Library of Congress.
