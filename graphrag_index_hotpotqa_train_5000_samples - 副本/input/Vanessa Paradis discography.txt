Vanessa Paradis discography

The discography of the French pop-singer Vanessa Paradis consists of six studio albums — "M&J" (1988), "Variations sur le même t'aime" (1990), "Vanessa Paradis" (1992), "Bliss" (2000),"Divinidylle" (2007) and Love Songs (2013).

 Simultaneously, she released four live albums, "Live" (1994), "Au Zénith" (2001), "Divinidylle Tour" (2008) and "" (2010).

 Her discography also features a greatest hits collection entitled "Best of Vanessa Paradis" (2009) and two soundtracks; namely "Atomik Circus" (2004) by and "Un monstre à Paris" (2011) recorded with -M-.
