Nothing Is Free

Nothing Is Free is the debut EP of Justin Broadrick under the moniker JK Flesh, released digitally for free on September 7, 2015 through 3BY3 and HHR.

 It is the third release under the JK Flesh pseudonym, following the debut studio album, "Posthuman", and split album with Prurient, "Worship Is the Cleansing of the Imagination".

 Despite being categorized as an EP, "Nothing Is Free" clocks in at 55:05, which is longer than many traditional studio albums, including JK Flesh's 2016 follow-up release "Rise Above".
