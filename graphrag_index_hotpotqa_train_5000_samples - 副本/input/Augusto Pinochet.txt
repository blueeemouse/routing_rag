Augusto Pinochet

Augusto José Ramón Pinochet Ugarte (] or ] ; 25 November 1915 – 10 December 2006) was a Chilean general, politician and the dictator of Chile between 1973 and 1990; he remained the Commander-in-Chief of the Chilean Army until 1998.

 He was also president of the Government Junta of Chile between 1973 and 1981.

 His rule of Chile was a dictatorship.
