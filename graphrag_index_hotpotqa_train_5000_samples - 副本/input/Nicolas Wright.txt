Nicolas Wright

Nicolas Wright (born March 23, 1982) is a Canadian actor.

 Wright has performed on stage, television and film.

 In 2004, he received the "most promising newcomer" award at the Just for Laughs film festival in Montreal for his short film, "Toutouffe".

 Recently he appeared in Mike Clattenburg's 2011 film "Afghan Luke".
