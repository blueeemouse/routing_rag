Columbian Iron Works and Dry Dock Co.

The Columbian Iron Works and Dry Dock Company (1872–1899), was located in Baltimore, Maryland on the Locust Point peninsula, adjacent to Fort McHenry.

 Founded by William T. Malster (1843–1907) who later partnered with William B. Reaney in 1879, it opened for business on 16 July 1880.

 The company was located on 8 acre adjacent to Ft. McHenry where it leased the property from the Baltimore Dry Dock Company.
