Westminster, Ellesmere Port

Westminster, Ellesmere Port is a suburb of the town of Ellesmere Port, Cheshire, England.

 It is located to the north of the town centre and it is the location of the M53 motorway.

 Westminster is famous for its terraced houses and the Joseph Groome Towers, three thirteen-storey tower blocks built as public housing .

 It is also known as the wezzy.
