Nureddin Pasha

Nureddin Ibrahim Pasha (Turkish: "Nurettin Paşa, Nureddin İbrahim Paşa" ; 1873 – 18 February 1932), known as Nureddin İbrahim Konyar after 1934 and often called Bearded Nureddin (Turkish: "Sakallı Nurettin" ), was a Turkish military officer who served in the Ottoman Empire during World War I and in the Turkish army during the Eastern Front of the Turkish War of Independence.

 To distinguish him from namesakes, he was called Beard Nureddin because he was the only high-ranking Turkish officer during the Turkish War of Independence sporting a beard.

 He is known as one of the most important commanders of the War.
