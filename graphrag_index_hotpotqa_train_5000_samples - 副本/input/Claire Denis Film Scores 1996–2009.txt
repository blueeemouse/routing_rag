Claire Denis Film Scores 1996–2009

Claire Denis Film Scores 1996–2009 is a compilation album by Tindersticks.

 Released in 2011, the albums compiles the soundtrack music scored by the band to six films by Claire Denis.

 This creative partnership began in 1996 when Denis approached the band about using the song "My Sister" from the second Tindersticks album in the film she was scripting, "Nénette et Boni".

 Singer Stuart A. Staples liked the idea, but suggested Tindersticks write something original for the film instead.
