Mount Moriah (New Hampshire)

Mount Moriah is a mountain located in Coos County, New Hampshire.

 The mountain is part of the Carter-Moriah Range of the White Mountains, which runs along the northern east side of Pinkham Notch.

 The summit is approximately 5 mi southeast of the center of the town of Gorham.

 Mount Moriah is flanked to the northeast by Middle Moriah Mountain and to the southwest by Imp Mountain.
