Sandman Midnight Theatre

Sandman Midnight Theatre is the title of a one-shot comic book in which two DC comics characters called the Sandman — Dream and Wesley Dodds — encounter each other.

 "Sandman Midnight Theatre" was co-written by "Sandman Mystery Theatre" author Matt Wagner (co-plot) and "The Sandman" author Neil Gaiman (co-plot/script), and featured painted artwork by Teddy Kristiansen and lettering by Todd Klein.

 It received the Comics Buyer's Guide Fan Award for Favorite Original Graphic Novel/Album for 1996.
