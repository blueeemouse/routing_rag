Staircase (film)

Staircase is a 1969 film adaptation of a two-character play, also called "Staircase", by Charles Dyer.

 The film, like the play, is about an aging gay couple who own a barber shop in the East End of London.

 One of them is a part-time actor about to go on trial for propositioning a police officer.

 The action takes place over the course of one night as they discuss their loving but often volatile past together and possible future without each other.
