Ferrybank, Waterford

Ferrybank (Irish: "Port an Chalaidh" ) is a suburb of Waterford City in Ireland.

 Much of Ferrybank is under the political jurisdiction of Waterford City and thus an area of administration for Waterford City and County Council, on the northern bank of the River Suir, extending into County Waterford.

 However, some parts of Ferrybank extend into County Kilkenny and are administered by Kilkenny County Council.

 There is an ongoing boundary dispute between both jurisdictions, with debates and proposals ongoing for many decades.
