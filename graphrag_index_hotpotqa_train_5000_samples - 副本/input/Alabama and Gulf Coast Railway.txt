Alabama and Gulf Coast Railway

The Alabama and Gulf Coast Railway (reporting mark AGR) is a Class II railroad owned by Genesee & Wyoming.

 It operates 348 mi of track from the Pensacola, Florida export terminals, west of downtown, north to Columbus, Mississippi, with trackage rights along BNSF Railway to Amory, Mississippi.

 A branch uses trackage rights along Norfolk Southern from Kimbrough, Alabama west and south to Mobile, Alabama, with separate trackage at the end of the line in Mobile.
