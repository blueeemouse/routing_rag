Literary influence of Hamlet

William Shakespeare's Hamlet is a tragedy, believed to have been written between 1599 and 1601.

 It tells the story of Hamlet, Prince of Denmark—who takes revenge on the current king (Hamlet's uncle) for killing the previous king (Hamlet's father) and for marrying his father's widow (Hamlet's mother)—and it charts the course of his real or feigned madness.

 "Hamlet" is the longest play—and Hamlet is the largest part—in the entire Shakespeare canon.

 Critics say that "Hamlet" "offers the greatest exhibition of Shakespeare's powers".
