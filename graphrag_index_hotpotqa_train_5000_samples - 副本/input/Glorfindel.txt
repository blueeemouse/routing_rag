Glorfindel

Glorfindel () is a fictional character in J.R.R. Tolkien's Middle-earth legendarium.

 He is introduced in various material relating to the First Age of Middle-earth, including "The Silmarillion".

 The name is also used for a character in "The Lord of the Rings", which takes place in Middle-earth's Third Age.

 In late writings, Tolkien works out how the two characters were one and the same, though this is not evident from the published versions of "The Silmarillion" and "The Lord of the Rings".
