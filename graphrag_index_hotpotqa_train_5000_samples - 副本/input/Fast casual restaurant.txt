Fast casual restaurant

A fast casual restaurant, found primarily in the United States, does not offer full table service, but promises higher quality food than other fast food restaurants (with fewer frozen or processed ingredients).

 It is an intermediate concept between fast food and casual dining, and usually priced accordingly.

 The category is exemplified by chains such as Boston Market, Bruegger's, Captain D's, Chipotle Mexican Grill, Dig Inn, El Pollo Loco, Newk's Eatery, Noodles & Co., Panera Bread, Pizza Ranch and Vapiano.
