Laren Bright

Laren Bright is a writer who worked at Hanna Barbera Productions and Warner Bros. Laren Bright worked on Hanna Barbera shows like "The Snorks", "A Pup Named Scooby-Doo", "Wake, Rattle, and Roll" (Fender Bender 500 segments), "Pirates of Dark Water", "Captain Planet", and other shows.

 Laren Bright was Story Editor/Writer for Batman Animated Series.

 Laren Bright was one of story editors for Emmy winning Batman Animated Series episode "Robin's Reckoning Part 1."
