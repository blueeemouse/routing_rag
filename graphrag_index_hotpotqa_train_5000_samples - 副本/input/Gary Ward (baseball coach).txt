Gary Ward (baseball coach)

Gary Ward (born September 9, 1940) is an American former baseball coach.

 He was the head baseball coach at Oklahoma State University from 1978 to 1996, compiling a record of 953–313–1.

 Ward won 17 Big Eight Conference championships at OSU, including 16 in a row from 1980 to 1996.

 He led his team to 18 40-win seasons, and 12 times his teams finished in the top 10.

 He later became the head baseball coach at New Mexico State University.

 During his tenure, he became only the 24th coach in college baseball history to gain 1,000 career wins.
