The Smashing Pumpkins discography

The discography of the Smashing Pumpkins, an American alternative rock band formed in Chicago, Illinois, consists of nine studio albums, four live albums, one digital live album series, seven compilation albums (including box sets and promotional releases), five extended plays (including promotional releases), 42 singles (including promotional releases), four video albums, 23 music videos, and contributions to five soundtrack albums.

 This list does not include material recorded by The Smashing Pumpkins members with other side projects.
