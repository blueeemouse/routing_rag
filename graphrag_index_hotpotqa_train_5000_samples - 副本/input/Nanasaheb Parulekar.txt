Nanasaheb Parulekar

Narayan Bhikaji Parulekar commonly referred as Nanasaheb Parulekar (Marathi: डॉ.

 नारायण भिकाजी उर्फ नानासाहेब परुळेकर ) (September 20, 1898 – January 8, 1973), was the Founding Editor of "Sakal", a Marathi daily newspaper, launched in January 1932.

 He also remained the Chairman of the Press Trust of India.
