Passion (2012 film)

Passion is a 2012 erotic thriller film written and directed by Brian De Palma, starring Rachel McAdams and Noomi Rapace.

 It is the English-language remake of Alain Corneau's 2010 thriller film "Love Crime", but with the ending greatly altered.

 The film is an international co-production between France, Germany, Spain, and the United Kingdom.
