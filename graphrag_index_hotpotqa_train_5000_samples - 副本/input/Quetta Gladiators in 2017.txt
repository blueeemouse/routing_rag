Quetta Gladiators in 2017

The Quetta Gladiators is a franchise cricket team that represents Quetta in the Pakistan Super League.

 They are one of the five teams that had a competition in the 2017 Pakistan Super League.

 The team was captained by Sarfraz Ahmed, and they stand on second position after winning four matches from their eight matches in the PSL 2017.

 They finished runners-up for the second time after losing the final against Peshawar Zalmi.
