C.L.I.F. 3

C.L.I.F. 3 ("Courage, Loyalty, Integrity, Fairness", or 警徽天职3) was a police procedural series produced by MediaCorp Singapore in 2014 in collaboration with the Singapore Police Force, following off from "C.L.I.F. 2".

 It was broadcast from 9 April to 13 May 2014 on free-to-air MediaCorp Channel 8 and consists of 25 episodes.

 The series stars casts from the second season, with Elvin Ng and Tracy Lee returning from the first season as the main characters of the series.The casts of the last sequel were Pierre Png, Thomas Ong , Andie Chen & Aileen Tan we're removed from the sequel.

 The series is co-sponsored by Gain City, Sheng Siong Supermarkets and Gold Roast Coffee.The sequel from C.L.I.F. 2.

It is shown on weekdays at 9pm.

 It was the second highest-rated drama series of 2014, only losing out to "Three Wishes" in October 2014, which was produced by Wawa Pictures and starred Huang Biren.
