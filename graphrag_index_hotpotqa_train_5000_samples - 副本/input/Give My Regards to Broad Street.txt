Give My Regards to Broad Street

Give My Regards to Broad Street is the fifth studio album by Paul McCartney, as well as the soundtrack album to his 1984 film of the same name.

 The album reached number 1 on the UK chart.

 The lead single, "No More Lonely Nights", was BAFTA and Golden Globe Award nominated.

 It was also to be his final album to be released under Columbia Records, which had been his US Label for over 5 years.
