Rose Red (miniseries)

Rose Red (also known as Stephen King's Rose Red) is a television miniseries scripted by horror novelist Stephen King.

 The series was premiered in the United States on ABC on January 27, 2002.

 The story centers on a mysteriously haunted Seattle mansion named Rose Red.

 Due to its long history of supernatural events and unexplained tragedies, the house is investigated by parapsychologist Dr. Joyce Reardon and a team of gifted psychics.
