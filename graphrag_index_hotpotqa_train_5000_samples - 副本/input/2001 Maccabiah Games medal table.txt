2001 Maccabiah Games medal table

The 2001 Maccabiah Games medal table, ranked by the number of gold medals won by their athletes during the 2001 Maccabiah Games, the "Jewish Olympics".

 The 16th Maccabiah Games were held from 16 July to 23 July 2001.

 Approximately 5,200 athletes from 46 counties participated.
