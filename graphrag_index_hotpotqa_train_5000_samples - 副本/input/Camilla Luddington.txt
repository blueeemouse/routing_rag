Camilla Luddington

Camilla Luddington (born December 15, 1983) is an English actress.

 She is known for her roles as Catherine Middleton in the Lifetime TV film "", and as Jo Wilson in the ABC medical drama "Grey's Anatomy".

 She provided the voice and motion capture work for Lara Croft in the 2013 video game "Tomb Raider" and its 2015 sequel "Rise of the Tomb Raider".
