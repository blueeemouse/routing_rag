Anna Alma-Tadema

Anna Alma-Tadema (1867–1943) was a British artist, daughter of painter Sir Lawrence Alma-Tadema.

 She created drawings and paintings of portraits, interior scenes, flowers and buildings.

 She was influenced by her father, and showed her works at exhibitions with her father and step-mother, Laura Theresa Alma-Tadema.

 Her work was shown at national exhibitions, like the Royal Academy of Arts, and the 1893 World's Columbian Exposition in Chicago where she won a prize.

 She also won a medal at the 1889 Paris exhibition.
