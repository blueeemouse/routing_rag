Grant Hart

Grant Vernon Hart (March 18, 1961 – September 13, 2017) was an American musician, best known as the drummer and co-songwriter for the alternative rock and hardcore punk band Hüsker Dü.

 After the band's breakup in 1988, he formed the alternative rock trio Nova Mob, where he moved to vocals and guitar.

 His solo career became his main focus after the dissolution of Nova Mob in 1997.
