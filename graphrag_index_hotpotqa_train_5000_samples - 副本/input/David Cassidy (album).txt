David Cassidy (album)

David Cassidy is David Cassidy's first U.S. album released in 14 years and was his only release on the now-defunct Enigma Records.

 The album was released in 1990 with the featured single, "Lyin' to Myself."

 "Lyin' to Myself" was the first top-30 hit for David Cassidy in eighteen years.

 The album reached 136 on the Billboard charts.
