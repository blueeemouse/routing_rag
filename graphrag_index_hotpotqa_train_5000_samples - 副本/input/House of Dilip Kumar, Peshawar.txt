House of Dilip Kumar, Peshawar

House of Dilip Kumar, Peshawar housed the Indian film actor Dilip Kumar.

 Born in Peshawar, British India on (1922--)11 1922 , Dilip Kumar relocated to Mumbai in the late 1930s with family.

 It was named as a Pakistani national heritage monument on 13 July 2014 by the Pakistani Prime Minister Nawaz Sharif.
