Judi Dench

Dame Judith Olivia "Judi" Dench, {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (born 9 December 1934) is an English actress and author.

 Dench made her professional debut in 1957 with the Old Vic Company.

 Over the following few years, she performed in several of Shakespeare's plays in such roles as Ophelia in "Hamlet", Juliet in "Romeo and Juliet", and Lady Macbeth in "Macbeth".

 Although most of her work during this period was in theatre, she also branched into film work and won a BAFTA Award as Most Promising Newcomer.

 She drew strong reviews for her leading role in the musical "Cabaret" in 1968.
