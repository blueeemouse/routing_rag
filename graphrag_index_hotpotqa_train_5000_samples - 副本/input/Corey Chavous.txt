Corey Chavous

Corey Lamonte Chavous ( ; born January 5, 1976) is a former American football safety who played 11 seasons in the National Football League (NFL).

 Chavous played for three teams, primarily as a safety but also as a cornerback.

 Chavous was known as one of the most instinctive safeties of his era and was a Pro Bowl selection in 2003.

 He is the founder of the media company and website DraftNasty.com, which specializes in coverage of the major sports with an emphasis on scouting and professional drafts.

 He is currently a color analyst for college football games as well as an NFL Draft analyst for CBS Sports.
