Langford House

Langford House (21 North Bailey, Durham, facing the west wing of Durham Cathedral) is one of nine houses that accommodate students who study at St Chad's College, Durham University, England.

 It is a grade II listed building built in the 18th century and is named after a former Judaism scholar and College Chaplain.

 For many decades, it was the home of the College’s chaplains, but today is used as a hall of residence for third year (and some first year) undergraduate students.
