Princess Fragrant

Princess Fragrant () is a Chinese 3-D cartoon series directed by Deng Jianglei and produced by the Shenzhen Qianheng Cultural Communication Company ().

 Its basis is the Fragrant Concubine, a Chinese legend about a Uighur girl from Kashgar who became a concubine of the Qianlong Emperor.

 The cartoon was produced to improve relations between the Han Chinese and the Uighurs.

 The series, which will be available in the Mandarin and Uighur languages, is scheduled to have 104 episodes.

 It will begin airing in 2015.
