George Weston

George Weston (March 23, 1864 – April 6, 1924) was a Canadian businessman and the founder of George Weston Limited.

 He became Toronto’s biggest baker with Canada’s largest bread factory.

 Weston began his career at the age of twelve as a baker's apprentice and went on to become a bread route salesman.

 By the turn of the century, he was known throughout the city for his "Weston’s Home-Made Bread" and years later for "Weston’s Biscuits."

 In addition to being a successful local businessman, he was also a prominent Methodist, as well as a municipal politician who served four years as alderman on Toronto City Council.
