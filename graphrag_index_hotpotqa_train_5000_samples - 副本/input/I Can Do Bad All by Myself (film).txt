I Can Do Bad All by Myself (film)

Tyler Perry's I Can Do Bad All by Myself is a 2009 romantic musical comedy-drama film which was released on September 11, 2009.

 The film was directed, produced, and written by Tyler Perry, who also makes an appearance in the film as his signature character Madea.

 Although the film and play share the same title, the film is not an adaptation of Perry's play of the same name; the two works have different storylines.
