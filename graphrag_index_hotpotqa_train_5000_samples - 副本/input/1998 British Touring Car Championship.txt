1998 British Touring Car Championship

The 1998 Auto Trader RAC British Touring Car Championship was a prominent BTCC season during the 'golden era' of touring car racing in Britain.

 It was eventually won by Rickard Rydell in a Volvo S40 with a round to spare.

 The runner-up was Anthony Reid in a Nissan Primera.

 The Autosport Cup for Independents, a championship for privateer entries was won by Tommy Rustad in a Renault Laguna.

 The video game TOCA 2 Touring Cars is based on this championship season.
