Haywood Mall

Haywood Mall is a upscale shopping mall in Greenville, South Carolina, United States.

 The mall is the largest in the state, with 1300000 sqft of retail space.

 The super-regional mall opened in 1980 at the intersection of Haywood Road and I-385/Golden Strip Freeway.

 Haywood's anchor stores include Belk, Dillard's, JCPenney, Macy's and Sears.

 The mall also features an Apple Store and a large Victoria's Secret Store.

 Since its 1995 renovation, the mall has been L-shaped.
