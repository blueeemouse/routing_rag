Dixie-Narco (EP)

The Dixie-Narco EP is an EP by the British band Primal Scream, released in February 1992 on Creation Records.

 Recorded at Ardent Studios in Memphis, this is the only official Primal Scream release to contain the song "Screamadelica", which was not used on their "Screamadelica" album ("Movin' on Up" appears on "Screamadelica").
