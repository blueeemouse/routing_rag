Jon Pollock

Jon Pollock (born 11 May 1977) is a British wheelchair basketball player.

 He was selected to train with the British wheelchair basketball squad in 1992.

 Pollock has played at four Paralympic games: the 2000 Summer Paralympics in Sydney, the 2004 Summer Paralympics in Athens, the 2008 Summer Paralympics in Beijing and the 2012 Summer Paralympics in London.

 He won a bronze medal at the 2004 Paralympics and the 2008 Paralympic Games.
