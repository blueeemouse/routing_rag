Hannah Montana: The Movie (soundtrack)

Hannah Montana: The Movie is the soundtrack for the .

 The film is an adaptation of the popular Disney Channel original series "Hannah Montana", which first aired in 2006.

 In the television series and film, American singer–songwriter and actress Miley Cyrus portrays Miley Stewart, a girl with a secret double life as the popstar Hannah Montana.

 Cyrus performs twelve of the songs on the album, seven of which are credited to Hannah Montana.

 American recording artists Billy Ray Cyrus, Taylor Swift, and Rascal Flatts and English recording artist Steve Rushton also have songs on the soundtrack.
