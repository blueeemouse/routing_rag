Kunzea

Kunzea is a genus of plants in the family Myrtaceae and is endemic to Australasia.

 They are shrubs, sometimes small trees and usually have small, crowded, rather aromatic leaves.

 The flowers are similar to those of plants in the genus "Leptospermum" but differ in having stamens that are longer than the petals. Most kunzeas are endemic to Western Australia but are few occur in eastern Australia and a few are found in New Zealand.

 The taxonomy of the genus is not settled and is complicated by the existence of a number of hybrids.
