Scott Z. Burns

Scott Z. Burns (born 1962) is an American screenwriter, producer, and director.

 Burns has written screenplays for "The Bourne Ultimatum" (2007), "The Informant!

" (2009), and "Contagion" (2011), all of which feature Matt Damon.

 His films "The Informant!"

, "Contagion," and "Side Effects" were directed by Steven Soderbergh.

 Burns also produced the Academy Award-winning "An Inconvenient Truth".
