The Damned (2013 film)

The Damned, also known as Gallows Hill, is a 2013 American horror film directed by Víctor García.

 The film stars Peter Facinelli, Sophia Myles, Nathalia Ramos, and Carolina Guerra.

 The film features a family and group of friends stranded in a storm and looking to seek refuge in a house with an ancient evil presence.

 The film was produced by Peter Block, Andrea Chung, and David Higgins, and is a joint Colombian and American production.

 The film had its world premiere at the Sitges Film Festival on October 17, 2014.

 and was released on video on demand on July 25, 2014, before a limited release on August 29, 2014, by IFC Midnight.
