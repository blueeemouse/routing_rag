Mohsen Amiryoussefi

Mohsen Amiryoussefi was born in 1972.

 He graduated with a degree in mathematics from Isfahan University.

 After writing several screenplays for both screen and stage, he completed his first short film in 1997, based on a story by Kafka.

 Amiryoussefi first came to prominence with his 2004 black comedy “Bitter Dream,” about a funeral director.

 It took home the Camera d’Or at that year’s Cannes as well as generous critical acclaim.

 He belongs to the third generation of "Iranian New Wave".
