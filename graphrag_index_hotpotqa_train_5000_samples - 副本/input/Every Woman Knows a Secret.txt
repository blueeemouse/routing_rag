Every Woman Knows a Secret

Every Woman Knows a Secret is a 1999 British television drama series written by William Humble based on the novel by Rosie Thomas.

 The series stars Siobhan Redmond as Jess, a woman whose son Danny dies in a drunk driving accident.

 The series aired on ITV from 18 March to 1 April 1999 is both set and filmed in Berkshire.
