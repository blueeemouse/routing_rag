Tactics and Air Combat Defence Establishment

Tactics and Air Combat Defence Establishment or TACDE is an Indian Air Force unit for training aerial combat to its top one percent fighter pilots.

 TACDE is based in Gwalior.

 It was conferred Presidential standard in 2009.

 The institution evolves tactical procedures for various aircraft, implements standard operating procedures and trains pilots in operational doctrines.

 It is similar to Topgun of the US Navy.
