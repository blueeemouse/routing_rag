Emily Kinney

Emily Rebecca Kinney (born August 15, 1985) is an American actress, singer, and songwriter.

 She is known for her role as Beth Greene on AMC's horror drama television series "The Walking Dead".

 Kinney has also appeared on several other television series, including Showtime's critically acclaimed "Masters of Sex," The CW's "The Flash", and later "Arrow".

 In 2016, she co-starred in the ABC series "Conviction", alongside Hayley Atwell.
