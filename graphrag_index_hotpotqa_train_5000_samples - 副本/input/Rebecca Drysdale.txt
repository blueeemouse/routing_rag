Rebecca Drysdale

Rebecca Drysdale (born 1978 or 1979 in Ohio) is an American comedian who was a member of the Second City Chicago E.T.C. cast.

 She won the 2005 Breakout Performer Award at the 2005 United States Comedy Arts Festival.

 She performed as part of the multi-arts group performance Synesthesia.

 She has written for sketch comedy shows such as "The Big Gay Sketch Show" and "Key & Peele".

 In 2011 she made a viral video for the "It Gets Better Project."
