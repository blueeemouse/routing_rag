Shed Seven

Shed Seven are an English alternative rock band, formed in York in 1990.

 One of the groups which contributed to the Britpop music scene of the 1990s, they never received the degree of mainstream success achieved by the likes of Oasis and Blur.

 They originally comprised singer Rick Witter, guitarist/keyboardist Joe Johnson, bassist Tom Gladwin and drummer Alan Leach.

 Johnson was later replaced by Paul Banks.
