Gillian Arnold (technologist)

Gillian Arnold is an influential British Information technology leader.

 She is the elected Chair of the BCSWomen Specialist Group that supports women in the IT industry.

 In 2015, she was identified as the 9th Most Influential Women in UK IT 2015, by "Computer Weekly".

 In 2016, Arnold was again identified as one of the 50 most influential women in UK IT 2016 by "Computer Weekly".
