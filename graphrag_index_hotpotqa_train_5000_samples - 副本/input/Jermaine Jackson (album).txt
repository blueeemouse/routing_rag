Jermaine Jackson (album)

Jermaine Jackson (released internationally as Dynamite) is the tenth studio album by United States singer-songwriter Jermaine Jackson, released in 1984.

 It was his debut album with Arista after leaving Motown.

 The album features Whitney Houston and his brothers Michael, Tito and Randy.

 Overall, it stands as one of Jermaine Jackson's most commercially successful albums, selling over 900,000 copies in the US to date and being certified Gold by the Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA).
