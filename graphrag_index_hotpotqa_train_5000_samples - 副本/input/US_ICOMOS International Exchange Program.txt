US/ICOMOS International Exchange Program

The US/ICOMOS International Exchange Program is an exchange program for young professions in historic preservation through the United States National Committee of the International Council on Monuments and Sites.

 The aim of the program is to promote an understanding of international preservation policies, methods and techniques that lead to a continuing dialogue between countries.

 As of 2007, nearly 600 young preservation professionals and over 70 countries have participated in this program since its creation in 1984.
