The Flys (American band)

The Flys were an American post-grunge group, formed in Hollywood in 1994 and on indefinite hiatus since 2012.

 They had success with the 1998 alternative top five hit "Got You (Where I Want You)", produced by David J. Holman, the video for which featured actors Katie Holmes and James Marsden.

 "Got You (Where I Want You)" was featured on their second album "Holiday Man" in 1998 and also appeared on the soundtrack for the 1998 MGM film "Disturbing Behavior", which starred Holmes.
