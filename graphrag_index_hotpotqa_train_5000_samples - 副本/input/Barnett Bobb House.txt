Barnett Bobb House

The Barnett Bobb House, also known as the Old Log House, is a historic building in downtown York, Pennsylvania, York County, Pennsylvania.

 It was originally located at the intersection of Pershing and College Avenues.

 In 1968, it was moved to its current location and restored.

 It is on the same site as the General Horatio Gates House and Golden Plough Tavern.

 It was built in 1811, and is a two-story log dwelling with dovetailed corners.

 It houses a museum operated by the York County Heritage Trust that showcases family life during the 1830s.
