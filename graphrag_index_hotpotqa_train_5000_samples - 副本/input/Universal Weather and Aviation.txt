Universal Weather and Aviation

Universal Weather and Aviation, Inc. (commonly called Universal or Universal Weather) is a privately held company headquartered in Houston, Texas that provides products and services for the general aviation industry.

 Its customers include owners and operators of business jets for corporate and personal air travel.

 The company offers products and services including international flight planning and scheduling, weather briefings, fueling programs, aircraft ground handling, and aviation security.

 It is the oldest company in the corporate flight planning industry.
