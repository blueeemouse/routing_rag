Matt Berry

Matthew Charles Berry (born 2 May 1974) is an English actor, writer, and musician.

 He is best known for his acting role as Douglas Reynholm in "The IT Crowd" from 2007 until the final episode in 2013.

 He is also well known for his appearances in "Garth Marenghi's Darkplace", "The Mighty Boosh", "Snuff Box", and "The Wrong Door".

 He plays Beef in "House of Fools" and the title character in Channel 4 sitcom "Toast of London".
