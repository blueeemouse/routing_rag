Justicia umbrosa

Justicia umbrosa (Brazilian plume, yellow jacobinia; syn.

 "Adhatoda umbrosa" Ness, and "Justicia aurea" Schltdl.

) is an ornamental shrub native of Cerrado vegetation of Brazil.

 This plant may be propagated by herbaceous stem cutting, and it can usually get to 1,50 - 2,50 m tall.

 They flourish in the shade, and will not do well if overwatered.
