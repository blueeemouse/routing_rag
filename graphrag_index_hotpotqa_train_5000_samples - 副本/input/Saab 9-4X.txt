Saab 9-4X

The Saab 9-4X is a compact luxury crossover SUV introduced by Swedish automaker Saab at the LA Auto Show 2010.

 It is based on the all-wheel-drive GM Theta Premium platform which also forms the basis for the Cadillac SRX.

 The 9-4X began production in 2011 and was built at Ramos Arizpe, Mexico but stopped before the end of the year due to the bankruptcy of Saab.
