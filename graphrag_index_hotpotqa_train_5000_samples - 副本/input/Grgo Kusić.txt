Grgo Kusić

Grgo Kusić (1892–1918) was a Croat soldier in the Austro-Hungarian Army.

 According to some accounts, Kusić was 2.37 m tall, and is frequently cited as the tallest Croat ever, as well as the tallest soldier of the Austro-Hungarian Army.

 However, other accounts put his height at 2.18 m , so the exact figure is not known for certain.
