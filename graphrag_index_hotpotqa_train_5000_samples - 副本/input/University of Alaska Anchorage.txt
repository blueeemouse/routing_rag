University of Alaska Anchorage

The University of Alaska Anchorage (UAA) is a public research university located in Anchorage, Alaska.

 UAA also administers four community campuses spread across Southcentral Alaska.

 These include Kenai Peninsula College, Kodiak College, Matanuska–Susitna College, and Prince William Sound College.

 Between the community campuses and the main Anchorage campus, over 20,000 undergraduate, graduate, and professional students are currently enrolled at UAA.

 This makes it the largest institution of higher learning in the University of Alaska System, as well as the state.
