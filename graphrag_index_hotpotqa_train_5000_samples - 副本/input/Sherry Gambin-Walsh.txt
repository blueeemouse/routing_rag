Sherry Gambin-Walsh

Sherry Gambin-Walsh is a Canadian politician, who was elected to the Newfoundland and Labrador House of Assembly in the 2015 provincial election.

 She represents the electoral district of Placentia-St.

 Mary's as a member of the Liberal Party.

 Following the 2015 election, she served as Minister of Children, Seniors and Social Development until moving to Service NL in a 2017 cabinet shuffle.
