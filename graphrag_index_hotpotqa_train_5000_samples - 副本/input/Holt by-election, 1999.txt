Holt by-election, 1999

The 1999 Holt by-election was held in the Australian electorate of Holt in Victoria on 6 November 1999.

 The by-election was triggered by the resignation of the sitting member, former Australian Labor Party deputy leader Gareth Evans on 30 September 1999.

 The writ for the by-election was issued on 1 October 1999.
