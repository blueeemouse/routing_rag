H. Brett Melendy

Howard Brett Melendy (May 30, 1924 – April 19, 2008) was a prominent American historian, writer, researcher, publisher, autobiographer, dean, history professor, and filipinologist.

 Melendy was a professor and administrator at the San José State University in California and the University of Hawai'i.

 As a professor, he taught about the history of California and United States history.

 He was the first chairman of the history department of San José State University.

 He was a life member of the American Historical Association.
