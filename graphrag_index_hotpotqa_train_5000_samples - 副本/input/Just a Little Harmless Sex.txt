Just a Little Harmless Sex

Just a Little Harmless Sex is a 1999 romantic comedy film which revolves around the offer of oral sex by a stranded motorist (and prostitute) to a monogamous man who stops to help her.

 Arrested by a passing police officer, the unlikely good Samaritan must telephone his wife to bail him out in the middle of the night.

 A few days later, she throws him out of the house and goes out with her friends to enjoy a sexy night on the town.

 The denouement takes place when all the parties meet at a local nightclub for explanations and apologies.

 The film was directed by Rick Rosenthal, and stars Alison Eastwood, Rachel Hunter, and Lauren Hutton.
