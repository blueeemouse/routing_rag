Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority

Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority (Urdu: ‎ ) (abbreviated as CAA) is a regulatory authority, which oversees and regulates all aspects of civil aviation in Pakistan.

 CAA's head office is situated in Terminal-1 of Jinnah International Airport in Karachi.

 CAA is a member of the International Civil Aviation Organization.

 Safety Investigation Board (SIB) conduct air safety investigations on direction of Federal Government.

 Nearly all civilian airports in Pakistan are owned and operated by the CAA.
