The Attack (novel)

The Attack is the 26th book in the Animorphs series, written by K.A. Applegate.

 It has the distinction of being the last book in the series written by Applegate on a regular schedule; with "The Separation", the final two books in the regular series; "The Answer" and "The Beginning", two Megamorphs books; "Elfangor's Secret" and "Back to Before", and the chronicles; "Visser", and "The Ellimist Chronicles" being the only other Animorphs related books written by her after this was published.

 It is narrated by Jake.
