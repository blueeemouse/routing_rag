Jordan Hastings

Jordan "Ratbeard" Hastings (born March 15, 1982) is a Canadian musician born in Hamilton, Ontario but raised in Burlington, Ontario .

 He is best known as the drummer of Alexisonfire, having replaced their former drummer Jesse Ingelevics.

 Hastings was originally intended to be a temporary replacement, but then became a full member after two months, with his first album with the band being "Crisis" in 2006.
