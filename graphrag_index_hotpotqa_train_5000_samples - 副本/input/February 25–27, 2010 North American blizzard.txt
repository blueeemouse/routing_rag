February 25–27, 2010 North American blizzard

The February 25–27, 2010 North American blizzard (also known as the "Snowicane") was a winter storm and severe weather event that occurred in the Mid-Atlantic and New England regions of the United States between February 24–26, 2010.

 The storm dropped its heaviest snow of 12 to (locally as much as 36 in ) across a wide area of interior New England, New York, and Pennsylvania.

 The storm also brought flooding rains to coastal sections of New England, with some areas experiencing as much as 4 in .

 Aside from precipitation, the Nor'easter brought hurricane-force sustained winds to coastal New England.
