Mending Fences (song)

"Mending Fences" is a song written by Jim Robinson and Andy Byrd, and recorded by American country music group Restless Heart.

 It was released in January 1993 as the second single from the album "Big Iron Horses".

 The song reached number 13 on the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles & Tracks chart and peaked at number 3 on the Canadian "RPM" Country Tracks chart.

 Bassist Paul Gregg sings lead vocals on this song.
