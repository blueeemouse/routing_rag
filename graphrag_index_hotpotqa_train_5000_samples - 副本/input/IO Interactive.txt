IO Interactive

IO Interactive A/S (IOI) is an independent Danish video game developer based in Copenhagen, Denmark, best known for developing the "Hitman" series.

 The company was founded under the name ASX Nr.

 14.811 ApS on 1 November 1990, and became Mermaid Invest ApS on 24 May 1992.

 On 16 September 1998, the Reto-Moto development group turned the company into IO Interactive ApS, and into IO Interactive A/S on 14 April 2000.

 Originally a private studio, they were purchased in 2004 by their publisher, Eidos Interactive; Eidos was subsequently taken over by Square Enix Europe, a subsidiary of Square Enix, in 2009.

 IO Interactive remained a subsidiary of Square Enix until 2017, when Square Enix started seeking buyers for the studio.

 IO Interactive completed a management buyout, regaining their independent status and retaining the rights for "Hitman", in June 2017.
