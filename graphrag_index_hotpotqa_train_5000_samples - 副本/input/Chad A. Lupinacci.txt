Chad A. Lupinacci

Chad A. Lupinacci (born February 28, 1979) is the Assembly member for the 10th District of the New York Assembly.

 He is a Republican.

 The district includes portions of Suffolk County including Lloyd Harbor, Huntington Bay, Cold Spring Harbor, Huntington, Greenlawn, Huntington Station, Elwood, South Huntington, West Hills, Melville and Dix Hills on Long Island.
