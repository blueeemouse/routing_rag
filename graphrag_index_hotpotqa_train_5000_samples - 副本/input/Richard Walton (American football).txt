Richard Walton (American football)

Richard Wade Walton is a former American football quarterback.

 He was the starting quarterback of the Texas Longhorns in early 1998.

 He was the backup quarterback for James Brown for three years before winning the starting job as a 5th year Senior.

 He started for the first two games in 1998 but broke his hand against UCLA and as a result lost his starting job to Major Applewhite.
