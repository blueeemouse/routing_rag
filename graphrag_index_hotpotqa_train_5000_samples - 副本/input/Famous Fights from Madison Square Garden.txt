Famous Fights from Madison Square Garden

Famous Fights from Madison Square Garden was a TV sports series broadcast by the DuMont Television Network from September 15, 1952 to December 22, 1952.

 The program aired famous past boxing matches at Madison Square Garden in New York City.

 The program aired Monday nights at 9:45pm ET, was 15 minutes long, and was preceded by another 15-minute show "Football Sidelines".
