Bruce Ingram

Sir Bruce Stirling Ingram MC DLit (5 May 1877 – 8 January 1963) was a publishing entrepreneur and philanthropist.

 He was the editor of "The English Illustrated Magazine" (September 1899 – September 1901), "The Sketch", and "The Illustrated London News" from 1900 to 1963.

 Ingram was credited with introducing greater use of photography in the News and introducing the Rembrandt Regalio process which enabled faster printing of the paper.
