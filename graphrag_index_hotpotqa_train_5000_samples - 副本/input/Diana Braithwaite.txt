Diana Braithwaite

Diana Braithwaite is a Canadian electric blues singer, songwriter and screenwriter.

 She is a multiple Maple Blues Award winner.

 More recently she has teamed up with Chris Whiteley and they have been acclaimed as "blues icons" by the "Toronto Star", and collectively have won nine Maple Blues Awards and had six Juno Award nominations.

 Although they are little known in the United States, Diana Braithwaite and Chris Whiteley are mainstays of the Canadian blues scene.
