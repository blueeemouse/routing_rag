Omar Killed Me

Omar Killed Me (French: Omar m'a tuer ) is a 2011 drama film directed by Roschdy Zem.

 The film was selected as the Moroccan entry for the Best Foreign Language Film at the 84th Academy Awards.

 On 18 January 2012, the film was named as one of the nine shortlisted entries for the Oscars.

 Zem, Olivier Gorce, Rachid Bouchareb and Olivier Lorelle were collectively nominated for the César Award for Best Adaptation and Sami Bouajila was nominated for the César Award for Best Actor.
