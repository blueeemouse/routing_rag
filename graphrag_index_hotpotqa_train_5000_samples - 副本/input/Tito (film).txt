Tito (film)

Tito (Arabic: تيتو‎ ‎ ) is an Egyptian action movie produced in 2004 starring Ahmed El Sakka, Hanan Tork, Amr Waked, Ashraf Dwedar, and Khaled Saleh.

 It was directed by Tarek Al Eryan.

 The movie is about a criminal who, as a young boy, didn't find anyone or anything to help or guide, turning into a vicious, yet kind hearted, criminal.
