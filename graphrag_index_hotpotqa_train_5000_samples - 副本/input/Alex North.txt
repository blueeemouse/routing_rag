Alex North

Alex North (December 4, 1910 – September 8, 1991) was an American composer best known for his many film scores, including "A Streetcar Named Desire" (one of the first jazz-based film scores), "Viva Zapata!

", "Spartacus", "Cleopatra", and "Who's Afraid of Virginia Woolf?

".

 He was the first composer to receive an Honorary Academy Award but never won a competitive Oscar despite fifteen nominations.
