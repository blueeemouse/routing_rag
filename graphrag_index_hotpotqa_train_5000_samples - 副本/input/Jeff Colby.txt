Jeff Colby

Jeffrey Broderick "Jeff" Colby is a fictional character on the ABC prime time soap opera "Dynasty", created by Richard and Esther Shapiro.

 Portrayed by John James, Jeff Colby was introduced in the show's first episode in 1981 and appeared as a series regular for six seasons until transferring to the spin-off companion series, "The Colbys".

 The character returned to "Dynasty" upon the cancellation of "The Colbys" in 1987 and remained with the series until its final episode in 1989.
