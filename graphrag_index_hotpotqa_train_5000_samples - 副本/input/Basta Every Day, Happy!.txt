Basta Every Day, Happy!

Basta Every Day, Happy!

 was a Filipino morning variety-talk show to be broadcast by GMA Network hosted by Gladys Reyes, Donita Rose, Alessandra de Rossi and Chef Boy Logro.

 It is set to premiere on May 12, 2014 on the network's morning block and worldwide, premiering the same day on GMA Life TV (instead of GMA Pinoy TV), and also airing just a few hours after it originally aired in the Philippines in the evening in the United States and Canada effectively having the show premiere on the night of May 11, 2014, as well as similar schedules "The Ryzza Mae Show" and "Eat Bulaga!

" on GMA Pinoy TV.

 The show ended on January 5, 2015.
