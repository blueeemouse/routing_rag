Z22 (computer)

The Z22 was the seventh computer model Konrad Zuse developed (the first six being the Z1, Z2, Z3, Z4, Z5 and Z11, respectively).

 One of the early commercial computers, the Z22's design was finished about 1955.

 The major version jump from Z11 to Z22 was due to the use of vacuum tubes, as opposed to the electromechanical systems used in earlier models.

 The first machines built were shipped to Berlin and Aachen.
