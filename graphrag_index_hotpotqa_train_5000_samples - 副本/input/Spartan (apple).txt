Spartan (apple)

The 'Spartan' is an apple cultivar developed by Dr. R. C Palmer and introduced in 1936 from the Federal Agriculture Research Station in Summerland, British Columbia, now known as the Pacific Agri-food Research Centre - Summerland.

 The 'Spartan' is notable for being the first new breed of apple produced from a formal scientific breeding program.

 The apple was supposed to be a cross between two North American cultivars, the 'McIntosh' and the 'Newtown Pippin', but recently, genetic analysis showed the 'Newtown Pippin' was not one of the parents and its identity remains a mystery.

 The 'Spartan' apple is considered a good all-purpose apple.
