Psy from the Psycho World!

Psy from the Psycho World!

 is the debut album by South Korean singer Psy.

 The album was released on January 12, 2001.

 The album contains 19 songs.

 Track 11 "I Love Sex" is a re-make of Lee Jung-hyun's song "I Love X", featured on her debut album "Let's Go to My Star".

 The album was also released worldwide through iTunes.
