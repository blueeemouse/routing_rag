Slobodan Backović

Slobodan Backović (born September 3, 1946 in Nikšić, Montenegro) is a Montenegrin politician and nuclear physicist.

 He is a member of the Doclean Academy of Sciences and Arts and the Montenegrin Academy of Sciences and Arts.

 A former Minister of Education and Science in the government of Montenegro as a member of the Democratic Party of Socialists of Montenegro, Backović is well known for a controversial decision to rename the elementary and secondary education subject "Serbian Language" to "First Language".
