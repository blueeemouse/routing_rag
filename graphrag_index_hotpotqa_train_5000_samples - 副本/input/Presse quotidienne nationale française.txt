Presse quotidienne nationale française

Presse quotidienne nationale française is a group of eighteen paid-for French daily newspapers, of which six have circulations in excess of , and four free newspapers, which have a much larger circulation: not only is the paid-for press more expensive, but there are fewer outlets from which to buy newspapers.

 In recent years many newsstands and newsagents in Paris that sold newspapers have closed, and customers would need to travel far to get some titles.
