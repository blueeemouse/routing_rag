Susan Cartsonis

Susan Cartsonis is an American film producer and the president of Storefront Pictures.

 She produced "Firelight", "Where the Heart Is", "What Women Want", "Aquamarine", "Beastly", and "Carrie Pilby", and was the executive producer of "The Mistress of Spices" and "No Reservations".

 In 2000, she was named one of the top five grossing producers of the year by "The Hollywood Reporter."

, She is President of Wind Dancer Films.

 She is also Senior Vice President at 20th Century Fox.
