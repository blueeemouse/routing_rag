East Coast (ASAP Ferg song)

"East Coast" is a song by American rapper ASAP Ferg.

 It was released as a single on April 7, 2017, by RCA Records.

 The track was produced by DJ Khalil and features a guest appearance by American rapper Remy Ma.

 Originally meant as the lead single from Still Striving, the song was excluded from the final track listing, as only the remix was included instead.
