Canal 5 Noticias

Canal 5 Noticias ("Channel 5 News", also known as C5N) is a 24-hours cable news channel in Argentina.

 It has been created by the entrepreneur and journalist Daniel Hadad Sold to the Indalo Group, owned by Cristóbal López.

 C5N began broadcasting on Monday, August 6, 2007 at 6:50 AM (9:50 AM GMT).
