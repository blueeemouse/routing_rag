Football NSW

Football NSW is the governing body for soccer in the Australian state of New South Wales with the exception of the northern regions of NSW (the governing body for which is Northern New South Wales Football).

 Football NSW is a member of the national governing body, the Football Federation Australia.

 Football NSW's premier football competition is the National Premier Leagues NSW.

 The premier futsal competition is the Futsal Premier League.

 Prior to 1 April 2007, Football NSW was known as Soccer NSW.
