Silver City (album)

Silver City is a 2013 Christmas album recorded by experimental Christian rock band Falling Up.

 It was digitally released on December 6, 2013, and contained 11 tracks.

 It is the first Christmas-related album that Falling Up has released.

 Although the band labeled it as an "extended play," at 11 tracks, it exceeds both "Billboard"'s and the RIAA's definition for an EP, which is capped at six and five maximum tracks, respectively.

 The iTunes Store and Amazon.com versions did not include the opening track, "Intro/Jingle Bells".
