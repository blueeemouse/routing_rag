Royal Centre tram stop

Royal Centre is a tram stop of Nottingham Express Transit (NET) in the centre of the city of Nottingham.

 It derives its name from the adjacent Theatre Royal and the Royal Concert Hall.

 The stop is also close to the Cornerhouse, the Victoria Centre and the Newton Building of Nottingham Trent University, although much of the university's City Campus is closer to the Nottingham Trent University stop.
