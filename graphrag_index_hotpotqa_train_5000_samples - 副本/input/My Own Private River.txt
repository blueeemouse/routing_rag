My Own Private River

My Own Private River is a re-contextualized adventure drama film of My Own Private Idaho (1991).

 Using footage shot and directed by Gus Van Sant in 1990, the new edit gives James Franco and Van Sant a shared director credit.

 The project is in tribute to Franco's favorite actor, River Phoenix.

 Franco called "Idaho" one of his favorite movies and praised River's performance as the actor’s best.
