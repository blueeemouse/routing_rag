Joris Voorn

Joris Voorn (born 25 February 1977 in Tilburg) is a Dutch DJ.

 Since 1997 Joris has been regularly booked for gigs and had a residency at the ATAK.

 However his first production was not until 2002 when he released his "Muted Trax" EP.

 This release, as well as support from Carl Craig, Derrick May and Laurent Garnier made him decide to (temporarily) give up interior architecture and focus on his DJing career.

 In 2003 his "Lost Memories" EP became another hit.

 In the same year Joris was crowned Talent of the Year at the Dutch DJ Awards.
