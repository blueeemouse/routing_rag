Adam Bakri

Adam Bakri comes from a cinematic family; he is the son of Mohammad Bakri, brother of Saleh Bakri and Ziad Bakri.

 After completing a bachelor's degree in English literature and theater arts at Tel Aviv University, Bakri trained at New York's Lee Strasberg Theatre and Film Institute.

 Shortly after his graduation, he landed the lead role in Hany Abu-Assad's drama thriller, "Omar".
