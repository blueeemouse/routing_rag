The Massacre

The Massacre is the 2nd major label studio album by 50 Cent.

 It was released on March 3, 2005, by Aftermath, Shady and Interscope.

 The album debuted and peaked at #1 on the US Billboard 200, selling 1.15 million copies in its 1st week.

 Upon its release, "The Massacre" received generally positive from music critics.

 The album was nominated for a Grammy Award for Best Rap Album, but lost to Kanye West's album "Late Registration" at the 48th Grammy Awards.
