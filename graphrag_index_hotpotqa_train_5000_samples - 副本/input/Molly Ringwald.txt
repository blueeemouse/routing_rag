Molly Ringwald

Molly Kathleen Ringwald (born February 18, 1968) is an American actress, singer, dancer, and author.

 She was cast in her first major role as Molly in "The Facts of Life" (1979–80) after a casting director saw her playing an orphan in a stage production of the musical "Annie".

 She and several other members of the original Facts of Life cast were let go when the show was reworked by the network.

 She made her motion picture debut in the independent film "Tempest" (1982), which earned her a Golden Globe nomination.
