Fuji Bamboo Garden

The Fuji Bamboo Garden (富士竹類植物園 Fuji Chikurui Shokubutsuen?)

 is Japan’s only botanical garden specializing in bamboo.

 Fuji Bamboo Garden is located in Nagaizumi, which is part of Japan's Shizuoka Prefecture (southwest of Tokyo).

 The garden is located at the base of Mount Fuji, from where the garden's name originated.

 The garden occupies over 2 acres of land.

 Fuji Bamboo Garden is said to be the world’s largest collection of bamboo, holding more than 450 varieties of bamboo from all around the world.

 The garden contains approximately 100,000 bamboo plants.

 The types of bamboo in the garden range from black bamboo to moso bamboo.

 The garden has benches to sit on, as well as bamboo sheds each holding a special type of bamboo.
