Adán Zapata

Adán Zapata Mireles or simply Adán Zapata was a Mexican singer within the genre of Hip-Hop / Rap.

 He was born and lived in the colony Mexico Lindo in San Nicolás de los Garza, Nuevo Leon.

 From 2006 he belonged to the group called Mente En Blanco (MEB) where he was lead vocalist and was the one that raised the group, until his murder in the colony "Los Morales" San Nicolás de los Garza, Nuevo Leon on June 1, 2012 At the age of 21 by an armed command of organized crime together with 3 other young members of the same group "Mind On Target", two of them were children of a former secretary of public security.
