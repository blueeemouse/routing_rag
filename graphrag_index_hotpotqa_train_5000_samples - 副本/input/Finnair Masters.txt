Finnair Masters

The Finnair Masters was a women's professional golf tournament on the Ladies European Tour.

 Held in Finland, the 54-hole event was founded in 2005 as the Ladies Finnish Masters.

 The title sponsor since 2006 has been Finnair, the country's largest airline.

 The tournament was played every year at the Helsinki Golf Club and had one of the smaller prize funds on the tour.

 The last event was played in 2011.
