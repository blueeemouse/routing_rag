Dancing with the Stars (Greece season 2)

Dancing with the Stars was a Greek reality show airing on ANT1 and filmed live in Athens.

 The show was based on the United Kingdom BBC Television series "Strictly Come Dancing" and was part of BBC Worldwide's "Dancing with the Stars" franchise.

 The theme song was "It's personal" performed by Swedish indie pop band The Radio Dept.

 The second season of the show started on 20 March 2011.

 Season's 1 winner Errika Prezarakou joined the judging panel, replacing Galena Velikova.

 ANT1 launched a daily spin off titled "Dancing with the Stars Daily" on March 21, hosted by former contestant Eugenia Manolidou.

 The show was canceled on April due to low ratings.
