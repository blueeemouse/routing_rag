The Kindred (film)

The Kindred is a 1987 American horror film directed by Jeffrey Obrow and Stephen Carpenter.

 Obrow also produced the film and co-wrote it along with Carpenter, Earl Ghaffari and John Penney.

 The film stars David Allen Brooks, Amanda Pays and Rod Steiger.

 It was released on January 9, 1987 and grossed just over $2 million.
