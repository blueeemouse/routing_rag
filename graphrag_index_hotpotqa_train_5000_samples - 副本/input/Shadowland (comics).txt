Shadowland (comics)

"Shadowland" is a 2010 Marvel Comics storyline centering on Daredevil and other "street-level" superheroes in the Marvel Universe.

 The storyline started in the Daredevil comic and was expanded upon in the "Shadowland" five-issue mini series as well as four tie-in mini series, four one-shots, and two issues of "Thunderbolts".

 The storyline was collected into seven individual hard cover and soft cover Trade paperbacks in 2011.
