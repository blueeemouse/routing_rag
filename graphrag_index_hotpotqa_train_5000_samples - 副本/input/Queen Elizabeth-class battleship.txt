Queen Elizabeth-class battleship

The "Queen Elizabeth"-class battleships were a class of five super-dreadnoughts of the Royal Navy commissioned in 1915–16.

 The lead ship was named after Elizabeth I of England.

 These battleships were superior in firepower, protection and speed to their Royal Navy predecessors of the "Iron Duke" class as well as preceding German classes such as the "König" class , although the corresponding "Bayern"-class ships were competitive except for being 2 kn slower.

 As such, the "Queen Elizabeth"s are generally considered the first fast battleships.
