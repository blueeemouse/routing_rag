Judi Dench filmography

Dame Judi Dench is an English actress who has worked in theater, television, and film.

 Dench made her professional debut in 1957 with the Old Vic Company.

 Over the following few years she played in several of Shakespeare's plays in such roles as Ophelia in "Hamlet", Juliet in "Romeo and Juliet" and Lady Macbeth in "Macbeth".

 She branched into film work, and won a BAFTA Award as Most Promising Newcomer; however, most of her work during this period was in theatre.
