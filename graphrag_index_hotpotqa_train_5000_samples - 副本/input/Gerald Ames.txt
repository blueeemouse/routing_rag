Gerald Ames

Gerald Ames (12 September 1880 – 2 July 1933) was a British actor, film director and Olympic fencer.

 Ames was born in Blackheath, London in 1880 and first took up acting in 1905.

 He was a popular leading man in the post-First World War cimema, appearing in more than sixty films between his debut in 1914 and his retirement from the screen in 1928 in a career entirely encompassing the silent era.

 He was also a regular stage actor who took on many leading roles in the theatre.
