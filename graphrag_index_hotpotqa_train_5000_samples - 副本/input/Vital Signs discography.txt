Vital Signs discography

Vital Signs was a Pakistani pop band formed in Rawalpindi in 1986 by keyboardist, Rohail Hyatt, and bassist, Shahzad Hasan, who were soon joined by guitarist, Nusrat Hussain and vocalist, Junaid Jamshed.

 The band released four studio albums, as well as two compilations, ten singles, one video album, one remix album and several music videos.

 Guitarist Nusrat Hussain left the band after the band's first single release and was replaced by Salman Ahmad.

 They released their self-titled debut album in 1989; which had the single hit "Dil Dil Pakistan".

 Salman was later replaced by lead guitarist Rizwan-ul-Haq who played on the second album "Vital Signs 2" released in 1991.

 In 1993, Rizwan-ul-Haq left the band to join Awaz and replaced by Aamir Zaki.

 Zaki played lead guitars on the third and fourth studio albums.
