National road 98 (Poland)

Droga krajowa nr 98 (translates from Polish as "national road 98") is route belonging to Polish national roads network.

 It runs entirely through Lower Silesian Voivodeships, leading from a crossing with national road 5 in Wrocław to junction "Wrocław Psie Pole" with motorway A8 and expressway S8 near the city, mostly old route of national road 8.

 Road 98 is an alternative route for motorway bypass, despite fact that A8 is not a toll road.
