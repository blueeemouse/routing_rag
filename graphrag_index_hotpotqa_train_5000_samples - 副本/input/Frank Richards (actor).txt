Frank Richards (actor)

Frank Richards (September 15, 1909– April 15, 1992) was an American actor.

 He was a character actor, typically playing a hoodlum or thug with a menacing appearance.

 He appeared in 150 films and televisions shows from 1940 into the mid 1980s.

 He appeared in a 1952 episode of "Superman" "The Night of Terror" and a 1953 episode of "The Lone Ranger".

 His first stage appearance was in 1938 and his last film was John Cassavetes' "A Woman Under the Influence" in 1974.
