Footsteps in the Dark: Greatest Hits, Vol. 2

Footsteps in the Dark: Greatest Hits Vol.

 2 is a compilation album released by Cat Stevens in 1984.

 Its fourteen songs include hits such as "Father and Son" and "Where Do the Children Play?

" as well as two previously unreleased tracks from the Hal Ashby and Colin Higgins black comedy "Harold and Maude" (1971).

 And the obscure B-side "I want to Live in a Wigwam" from the Teaser sessions.
