Laura Thompson (politician)

Laura Thompson was an Independent member of the Texas House of Representatives.

 She represented District 120 after winning a special election on August 2, 2016.

 In the November 2016 general election, she ran for election to a full term but was defeated by Barbara Gervin-Hawkins.

 Thompson's brief tenure in the Texas House ended on January 9, 2017.

 Thompson was the first Independent to serve in the Texas Legislature in over fifty years.
