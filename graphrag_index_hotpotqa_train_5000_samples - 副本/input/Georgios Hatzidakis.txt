Georgios Hatzidakis

Georgios Nicolaou Hatzidakis (Greek: Γεώργιος Νικολάου Χατζιδάκις ; 23 November [O.S. 11 November] 1843 , in Myrthios, Ottoman Crete – 28 June 1941, in Athens) was a Greek philologist, who is regarded as the father of linguistics in Greece.

 He was the first chair of Linguistics and Indian Philology at the University of Athens in 1890–1923.
