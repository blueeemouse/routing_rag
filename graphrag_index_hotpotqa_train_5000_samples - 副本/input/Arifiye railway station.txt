Arifiye railway station

Arifiye railway station is a railway station on the Istanbul-Ankara railway in the town of Arifiye, a suburb of Adapazarı.

 The station is served by the Turkish State Railways as well as Adaray commuter service.

 The State Railways operates its premier high-speed rail service from Istanbul to Ankara and Konya as well as a premier limited-stop regional train service to Istanbul.

 Adaray operates commuter rail service to Adapazarı from Arifiye, which is the southern terminus.
