Kinect Rush: A Disney-Pixar Adventure

Kinect Rush: A Disney-Pixar Adventure is a 2012 platform video game based on Pixar films, released for Kinect on Xbox 360.

 Announced on March 8, 2012 and released later that month, the game is similar to "", but players instead are taken through the worlds of eight of Pixar's movies: "Up", "Toy Story", "Toy Story 2", "Toy Story 3", "The Incredibles", "Cars, Cars 2", and "Ratatouille" with the game hub set in a theme park.
