Horten H.IV

The Horten H.IV was a German tailless flying wing glider in which the pilot lay in a prone position to reduce the frontal area, and hence drag.

 It was designed by Reimar and Walter Horten in Göttingen.

 Four were built between 1941 and 1943.

 They were flown in a number of unofficial competitions in Germany during World War II.

 After the war the flying examples were transported to the United Kingdom and the United States where several contest successes were achieved.
