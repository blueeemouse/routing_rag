Oksana Lada

Oksana Lada (Ukrainian: Oксана Лада ; born March 3, 1979) is a Ukrainian actress, probably best known for the role as Irina Peltsin, the mistress of Tony Soprano, on the hit HBO TV series "The Sopranos".

 It was this role that made Lada become familiar to national audiences.

 She also played Ulia, in the Netflix Original Series, "Orange Is The New Black".

 Lada initially gained recognition as the lead in the Off-Broadway production, Pentecost, for which was nominated for a Drama Desk Award in 1995.

 She also had a role as a wedding dress saleswoman in "30 Rock" in the first episode of show's second season, SeinfeldVision.
