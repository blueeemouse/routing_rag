1937/1938 Daily Mail Gold Cup

The 1937/1938 Daily Mail Gold Cup was a professional snooker tournament sponsored by the "Daily Mail".

 The cup was won for the second successive year by Joe Davis with Willie Smith finishing in second place in the final table.

 It was the fourth Daily Mail Gold Cup tournament, although only the second as a snooker event.

 The Daily Mail Gold Cup ran from 1935 to 1940.
