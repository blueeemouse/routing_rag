Maximilian Le Cain

Maximilian Le Cain (born 1978) is an Irish filmmaker, cinephile and film critic living in Cork City Ireland.

 His always-personal, formally experimental work has included narrative, documentary, and video art installation, although it mostly wanders restlessly somewhere between those categories.

 Le Cain has made more than sixty short and medium-length films and videos over the past decade.

 He's written for many magazines such as "Senses of Cinema", "Film Ireland" and "Rouge" and in several books, including "The Cinema of Roman Polanski: Dark Spaces of the World" (Wallflower Press, 2006).

 He is also the editor of the online magazine devoted to experimental cinema called "Experimental Conversations".
