Tug of War (Paul McCartney album)

Tug of War is the third solo studio album by Paul McCartney, released in April 1982.

 It was McCartney's first album released after the dissolution of Wings in April, 1981 and McCartney's first album after the murder of former songwriting partner John Lennon.

 The album was produced by former Beatles producer George Martin and was a number 1 hit in numerous countries.

 It was hailed as a return-to-form for McCartney upon release.

 Its remastered deluxe edition received a nomination for Best Boxed or Special Limited Edition Package at the 2017 Grammy Awards.
