Frank McEnulty

Frank Edward McEnulty (born 1956) is an American businessperson and politician.

 In the 2008 presidential election, he was the vice presidential nominee of the Reform Party of the United States of America, and the presidential nominee of the New American Independent Party.

 His running mate for the latter was Bobby Klingler.

 In total, he received 833 votes in the presidential election for his presidential candidacy and 481 for his vice-presidential candidacy.
