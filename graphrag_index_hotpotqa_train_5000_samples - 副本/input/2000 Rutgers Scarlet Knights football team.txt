2000 Rutgers Scarlet Knights football team

The 2000 Rutgers Scarlet Knights football team represented Rutgers University in the 2000 NCAA Division I-A football season.

 The Scarlet Knights were led by fifth-year head coach Terry Shea and played their home games at Rutgers Stadium.

 They were a member of the Big East Conference.

 They finished the season 3–8, 0–7 in Big East play to finish in last place.
