&quot;V&quot; Is for Vagina

"V" Is for Vagina is the debut studio album by Maynard James Keenan's side project Puscifer, released on  30, 2007 (2007--) .

 The album features a significantly different, electronic sound and arrangements than Keenan's other bands: the progressive Tool and alternative A Perfect Circle.

 Keenan himself compared the sound of ""V" Is for Vagina" to "driving around in your car listening to those old Motown hits, James Brown, and cool R&B stuff". "

"V" is for Vagina" has sold 112,000 copies.
