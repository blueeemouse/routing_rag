Necabettin Ergenekon

Colonel Necabettin Ergenekon (born 1926, in Erzurum) is a Turkish Army officer who retired in 1982, having been commander of the Turkish Gendarmerie in Adıyaman Province after the 1980 Turkish coup d'état.

 Born "Necabettin Baltacı", he changed his name "some time in the '60s" to avoid confusion with another person by the same name.

 At one time, Ergenekon was the commanding officer of Veli Küçük.

 Ergenekon was suspected by later-assassinated state prosecutor Cevat Yurdakul as being behind a string of mysterious deaths in the 70s, but no legal action was ever taken.
