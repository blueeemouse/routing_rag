WERT

WERT (1220 AM) is a radio station broadcasting an adult standards featuring soft oldies chiefly from the 1940s through today.

 Licensed to Van Wert, Ohio, United States, the station serves Van Wert primarily but is considered part of the Lima market.

 The station is currently owned by First Family Broadcasting and broadcasts from its studios on the Lincoln Highway (County Highway 418) just east of Van Wert.
