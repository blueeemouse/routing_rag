Lou Pearlman

Louis Jay "Lou" Pearlman (June 19, 1954 – August 19, 2016) was an American record producer and fraudster.

 He was the manager of successful 1990s boy bands such as Backstreet Boys and NSYNC.

 In 2006, he was accused of running one of the largest and longest-running Ponzi schemes in history, leaving more than $300 million in debts.

 After being apprehended, he pleaded guilty to conspiracy, money laundering, and making false statements during a bankruptcy proceeding.

 In 2008, Pearlman was convicted and sentenced to up to 25 years in prison.

 He died in federal custody in 2016.
