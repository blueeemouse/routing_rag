Cum On Feel the Noize

"Cum On Feel the Noize" is a song by the British rock band Slade, released in 1973 as a non-album single.

 It was written by lead vocalist Noddy Holder and bassist Jim Lea, and produced by Chas Chandler.

 It reached No. 1 in the UK, giving the band their fourth number one single, and remained in the charts for twelve weeks.

 The song would be included on the band's 1973 compilation album "Sladest".
