Lytham St Annes Art Collection

The Lytham St Annes Art Collection is a public art collection of over 240 artworks in Lytham St Annes, Lancashire.

 Fylde Borough Council are the custodians of the paintings, sculptures, prints and artefacts that are mostly held within the Town Hall in St Annes.

 The collection was started in 1925 by the donation of "The Herd Lassie", painted by Richard Ansdell, to the townspeople of Lytham St Annes, Lancashire.

 It was donated by John Booth, son of the founder of the grocery store chain Booths.

 The collection is now one of the largest public collections of Ansdell's paintings.
