United Freedom Front

The United Freedom Front (UFF) was a small American Marxist organization active in the 1970s and 1980s.

 It was originally called the Sam Melville/Jonathan Jackson Unit, and its members became known as the Ohio 7 when they were brought to trial.

 Between 1975 and 1984 the UFF carried out at least 20 bombings and nine bank robberies in the northeastern United States, targeting corporate buildings, courthouses, and military facilities.

 Brent L. Smith describes them as "undoubtedly the most successful of the leftist terrorists of the 1970s and 1980s."

 The group's members were eventually apprehended and convicted of conspiracy, murder, attempted murder, and other charges.

 Two, Tom Manning and Jaan Laaman, remain incarcerated today.
