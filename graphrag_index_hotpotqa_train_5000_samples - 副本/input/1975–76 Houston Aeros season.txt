1975–76 Houston Aeros season

The 1975–76 Houston Aeros season was the Aeros' fourth season of operation in the World Hockey Association (WHA).

 The Aeros again qualified first for the playoffs and made it to the Avco Cup Final but lost the championship to the Winnipeg Jets.

 This was the first season that the Aeros played in the Summit after three seasons at Sam Houston Coliseum.
