Demolition (2015 film)

Demolition is a 2015 USA drama film directed by Jean-Marc Vallée and written by Bryan Sipe.

 The film stars Jake Gyllenhaal, Naomi Watts, Chris Cooper, and Judah Lewis.

 The film opened the 2015 Toronto International Film Festival and was theatrically released on April 8, 2016, by Fox Searchlight Pictures.
