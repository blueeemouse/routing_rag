Mind Your Manners (Chiddy Bang song)

"Mind Your Manners" is a song by American indie hip hop duo Chiddy Bang, featuring Icona Pop.

 It was released in the United States on July 19, 2011 as a Digital Download as the lead single from their debut studio album "Breakfast".

 The song takes a sample of "Manners" from the Icona Pop's self-titled debut album.

 The song did not enter the "Billboard" Hot 100 but peaked on the Bubbling Under Hot 100 Singles chart at number 15, as well as reaching number 19 on the Top Heatseekers.

 The official remix features Gym Class Heroes frontman and rapper Travie McCoy.

 The single is featured in the soundtrack for the EA Sports video game "Madden NFL 12" and was also included on "Now That's What I Call Music!
