Bavarian Soviet Republic

The Bavarian Soviet Republic (German: "Bayerische Räterepublik" ) was the short-lived unrecognised socialist state in Bavaria during the German Revolution of 1918–19.

 It took the form of a workers' council republic.

 Its name is variously rendered in English as the Bavarian Council Republic or the Munich Soviet Republic (the German name "Räterepublik" means a republic of councils or committees; council or committee is also the meaning of the Russian word "soviet") after its capital of Munich.

 It was established in April 1919 following the demise of Kurt Eisner's People's State of Bavaria and sought independence from the also newly-proclaimed Weimar Republic.

 However, it was overthrown less than a month later by elements of the German Army and the paramilitary "Freikorps".
