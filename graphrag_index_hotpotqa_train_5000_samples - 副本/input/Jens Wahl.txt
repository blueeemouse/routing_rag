Jens Wahl

Jens Wahl (born July 24, 1966) is a German former footballer.

 Wahl began his career with FC Hansa Rostock in the DDR-Oberliga, and won one cap for East Germany.

 After reunification, Hansa won the final, transitional East German championship, and the cup, with Wahl scoring the winner in the final.

 He later played for Chemnitzer FC, FC St. Gallen and Dynamo Dresden.
