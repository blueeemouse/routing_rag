Royal Norwegian Order of Merit

The Royal Norwegian Order of Merit (Norwegian, "Den Kongelige Norske Fortjenstorden") was instituted by King Olav V in 1985.

 It is awarded to foreigners, Norwegian citizens living abroad, Ministry of Foreign Affairs diplomats, foreign civil servants in Norway, and Norway's honorary consuls for "outstanding service in the interests of Norway".

 Its counterpart, the Royal Norwegian Order of St. Olav, is generally only awarded to Norwegian citizens living in Norway.
