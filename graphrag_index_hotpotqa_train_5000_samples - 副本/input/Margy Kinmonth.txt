Margy Kinmonth

Margy Kinmonth is a film and television director whose credits include feature documentary "Revolution - New Art for a New World" (Foxtrot Films Ltd/Arts Alliance), marking the 1917 Russian Revolution, "War Art with Eddie Redmayne" ( 2015), which shows on ITV and Ovation, about artists of the First World War, Bafta nominated feature documentary "Hermitage Revealed" (Foxtrot Films Ltd/Arts Alliance, Moscow International Film Festival 2014), "Royal Paintbox" with HRH the Prince of Wales (Foxtrot Films Ltd/ ITV, MIFF 2013), "Looking for Lowry" with Ian McKellen, Noel Gallagher and Dame Paula Rego (Foxtrot Films/ ITV) ""Mariinsky Theatre," "Nutcracker Story"" and BBC series "Naked Hollywood", winner of BAFTA Best Documentary Series.
