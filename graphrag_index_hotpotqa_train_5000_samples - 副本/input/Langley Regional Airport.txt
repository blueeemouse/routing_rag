Langley Regional Airport

Langley Regional Airport (IATA: YLY, ICAO: CYNJ) is located in Langley Township, British Columbia, Canada.

 The airport serves mostly general aviation, and also provided scheduled passenger service to the Victoria Airport Water Aerodrome via Harbour Air Seaplanes before service was ended on May 20, 2011.

 Helicopter operations are a major part of Langley Airport's traffic; the airport has 3 helipads.
