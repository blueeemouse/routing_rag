Malcolm &amp; Eddie

Malcolm & Eddie is an American television sitcom that premiered August 26, 1996 on UPN, and ran for four seasons, airing its final episode on May 22, 2000.

 This series starred Malcolm-Jamal Warner and Eddie Griffin in the lead roles.

 The program was produced by Jeff Franklin Productions in association with TriStar Television in its first three seasons and by Columbia TriStar Television in its final season.
