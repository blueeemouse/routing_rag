Try Not to Look So Pretty

"Try Not to Look So Pretty" is a song co-written and recorded by American country music artist Dwight Yoakam.

 It was released in February 1994 as the fourth single from his album "This Time".

 This song peaked at number 14 in the United States and at number 4 in Canada.

 It was written by Dwight Yoakam and Kostas.
