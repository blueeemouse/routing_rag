Gryting, King of Orkdal

Gryting was according to Heimskringla a king of Orkdal in the period leading up to Harald Fairhair's unification of Norway.

 He was the first leader in Trøndelag to make a stand against King Harald's ravaging of the country.

 At the battle of Orkdal Gryting's men were defeated and Gryting taken prisoner.

 After this Gryting swore allegiance to king Harald.
