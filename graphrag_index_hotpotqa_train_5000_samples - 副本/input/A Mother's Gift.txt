A Mother's Gift

A Mother's Gift is a 2001 novel by pop music singer Britney Spears and her mother, Lynne Spears.

 It is their second book together, following 2000's "Heart-to-Heart".

 The novel is loosely based on Britney's life.

 Popular reactions to the novel in spaces like Amazon were mixed.

 In 2012, rumors of a third novel sequel surfaced.
