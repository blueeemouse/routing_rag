Deep (Nine Inch Nails song)

"Deep" is a song recorded by American industrial rock band Nine Inch Nails for the to the 2001 film "".

 Because this is a promotional single, it has never been featured with its own official halo and the song "Deep" has never been released on any Nine Inch Nails album, or on any halo-numbered release, although it has its own video directed by Enda McCallion.
