Geordie Shore

Geordie Shore is a British reality television series broadcast on MTV and based in Newcastle upon Tyne, England.

 It was first broadcast on 24 May 2011, and is the British version of the American show "Jersey Shore". "

Geordie" is the regional nickname and dialect given to the people of the Tyneside area in north-east England, and is closely associated with the city of Newcastle and its environment where the show is set.

 Despite this, the show includes cast members from various parts of North East England as well as one from Italy.
