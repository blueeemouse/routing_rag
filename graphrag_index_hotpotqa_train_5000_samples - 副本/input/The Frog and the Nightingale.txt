The Frog and the Nightingale

The Frog and the Nightingale is a poem composed by Indian poet Vikram Seth in 1994.

This poem is an allegory(a poem in which a moral is conveyed) The poem tells a story about a frog and a nightingale.

 The poem was originally published by Evergreen Publications, but it was later used by the Ministry of Education, India as a poem for school students.
