Elijah Ba'al Shem of Chelm

Elijah Ba'al Shem or Eliyahu Ba'al Shem of Chełm (government of Lublin) (born 1550; died at Chelm, 1583 ) was a Polish rabbi who served as chief rabbi of Chełm.

 He also studied Kabbalah, and, according to his descendant Tzvi Ashkenazi, created a golem.

 He is credited with creating the first golem with a ""shem"" (that is, using a combination of letters from one of the names of God to form a name; Elijah used ""Sefer Yezirah""), so he was known as a "Ba'al Shem."

 He is the first rabbi in history to be known by this title.
