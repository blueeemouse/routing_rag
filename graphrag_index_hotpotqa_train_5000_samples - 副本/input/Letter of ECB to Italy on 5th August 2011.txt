Letter of ECB to Italy on 5th August 2011

On 5th August 2011, during dramatic crisis of European banks, the former governor of ECB, Jean Claude Trichet, together with Mario Draghi, wrote a "secret" letter to Italian government, at the time led by Silvio Berlusconi, in which they pushed for a series of economic measures "to be implemented as soon as possible".

 By complying of these measures, the support of ECB was implicitly conditioned by massive purchase of Italian bonds on the secondary market.
