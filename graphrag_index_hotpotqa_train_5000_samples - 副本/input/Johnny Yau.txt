Johnny Yau

Johnny Yau Chung Ling (邱仲凌) is a Hong Kong businessman, the Managing Director of a Chinese based mass media company named Media Plus Hong Kong Limited in Hong Kong, China.

 Yau has been working for at least 35 years in TV production, advertising, sales, marketing planning and international business developments, and acquiring businesses both in Hong Kong and other overseas TV media.

 He worked at TVB and TVBI for more than 25 years and was the General Manager of Television Broadcast Satellite Limited.
