Fairmount Line

The Fairmount Line or Dorchester Branch is a line of the MBTA Commuter Rail system in Boston, Massachusetts, USA.

 Except for a short portion in Milton, it lies entirely within Boston, progressing in a southwesterly trajectory, passing through the neighborhoods of Dorchester, Mattapan and Hyde Park.

 Weekend service began on November 29, 2014.

 Most trains reverse direction at the south end at Readville, but a few continue onto the Franklin Line.
