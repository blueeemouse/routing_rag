Greg LaFleur

Gregory Louis LaFleur (born September 16, 1958) is a former American football tight end in the National Football League.

 LaFleur was selected in the third round by the Philadelphia Eagles out of Louisiana State University in the 1981 NFL Draft.

 LaFleur has also been a member of the Indianapolis Colts and St. Louis Cardinals.

 He had career statistics of 64 Receptions and 3 Touchdowns.

 He is the father of NBA center Robert Sacre.
