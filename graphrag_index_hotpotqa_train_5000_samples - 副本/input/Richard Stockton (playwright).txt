Richard Stockton (playwright)

Richard F. Stockton (Akron, Ohio February 3, 1932 – April 5, 1997) was an American playwright.

 He was the first American Playwright to receive a world premiere at the Abbey Theatre, Ireland’s National Theatre, for his play "Prisoner of the Crown", co-produced by Sir Alfred Drake.

 The play received its American premiere by the Repertory Company of the Virginia Museum Theater, under the title "The Royal Rape of Ruari Macasmunde", with Keith Fowler in the title role and under Alfred Drake's direction.

 The play dramatizes the jury deliberations in the treason trial of Roger Casement.
