The Eye (2002 film)

The Eye, also known as "Seeing Ghosts", is a 2002 Hong Kong-Singaporean horror film directed by the Pang brothers.

 The film spawned two sequels by the Pang brothers, "The Eye 2" and "The Eye 10".

 There are three remakes of this film, including "Naina", made in 2005 in Hindi, starring Urmila Matondkar and produced by Shripal Morakhia, Sagar Pandya, Anjum Rajabali, and Rakesh Mehra.
