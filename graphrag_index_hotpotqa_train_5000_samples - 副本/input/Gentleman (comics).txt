Gentleman (comics)

The Gentleman (Gustav Fiers) is a fictional character, a supervillain and enemy of the Marvel Comics superhero called Spider-Man.

 The Gentleman is the brother of the Finisher, first appearing in a trilogy of "Spider-Man" novels written by Adam-Troy Castro before appearing within the comic books themselves.

 The Gentleman made his film debut in 2012 in "The Amazing Spider-Man", portrayed by actor Michael Massee.
