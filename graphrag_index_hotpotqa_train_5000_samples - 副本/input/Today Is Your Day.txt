Today Is Your Day

"Today Is Your Day" is a song by the Canadian singer-songwriter Shania Twain.

 It was self-penned by Twain and co-produced by David Foster and Nathan Chapman.

 The song was released on June 12, 2011 by Mercury Nashville Records, as a promotional single to accompany the documentary television series "Why Not?

 with Shania Twain" (2011).

 The song marked Twain's first song release in over six years and actually even the only song release of her own within a timespan of twelve years.

 Twain wrote the track for self-inspiration, during the development of "Why Not?

 with Shania Twain".

 To her, "Today Is Your Day" became the theme song for the series, expressing the purpose behind it via music.

 Despite feeling apprehensive, Twain decided to record the track, which induced her to create her forthcoming fifth studio album.

 The track is midtempo ballad in the country pop genre.

 Lyrically, "Today Is Your Day" regards personal upliftment.

 "Today Is Your Day" is Twain's first piece of music to have had no involvement with now ex-husband Robert John "Mutt" Lange in 16 years and only her second self-penned song, the first one being "Leaving Is The Only Way Out" on the album "The Woman In Me"
