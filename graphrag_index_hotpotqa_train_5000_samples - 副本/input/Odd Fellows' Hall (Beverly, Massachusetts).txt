Odd Fellows' Hall (Beverly, Massachusetts)

The Odd Fellows' Hall in Beverly, Massachusetts occupies a prominent location on Cabot Street opposite city hall in Beverly Center.

 It is a 3.5 story High Gothic Revival building constructed in 1893 to a design by local architect J. Foster Ober.

 Its exterior is clad in brick with trim of granite and brownstone.

 Its roof is a cross gable style, steep roof with polychrome bands of slate tiles.

 The central portion of the roof is a deck surrounded by a wrought iron railing and is topped by a cupola and weathervane.

 One of its notable tenants was President William Howard Taft, who had offices in the building during summer residencies in Beverly.
