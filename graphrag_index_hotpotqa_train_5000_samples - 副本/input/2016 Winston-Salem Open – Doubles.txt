2016 Winston-Salem Open – Doubles

Dominic Inglot and Robert Lindstedt were the defending champions, but chose not to participate together.

 Inglot played alongside Marin Draganja, but lost in the quarterfinals to Lindstedt and Aisam-ul-Haq Qureshi.

 Lindstedt and Qureshi then lost in the semifinals to Andre Begemann and Leander Paes.
