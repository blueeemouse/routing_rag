Kim Wilde

Kim Wilde (born Kim Smith; 18 November 1960) is an English pop singer, author, DJ and television presenter who burst onto the music scene in 1981 with her debut single "Kids in America", which reached number two in the UK.

 In 1983, she received the Brit Award for Best British Female.

 In 1986, she had a UK number two hit with her version of the Supremes' song "You Keep Me Hangin' On", which also went on to be a major US hit, topping the charts there in 1987.

 Between 1981 and 1996, she had 25 singles reach the Top 50 of the UK singles chart.

 Her other hits include "Chequered Love" (1981), "You Came" (1988) and "Never Trust a Stranger" (1988).

 In 2003, she collaborated with Nena on the song "Anyplace, Anywhere, Anytime", which topped the Dutch charts.
