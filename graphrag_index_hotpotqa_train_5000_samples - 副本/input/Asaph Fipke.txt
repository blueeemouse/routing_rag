Asaph Fipke

Asaph Fipke is a Canadian writer/director/producer/executive producer and the creator of the animated series "Storm Hawks", which is made by the animation studio he co-founded, Nerd Corps Entertainment, in cooperation with Cartoon Network and YTV.

 He is also the creator of the animated series "Slugterra" which aired on Disney XD and "Endangered Species", which was also produced by Nerd Corps and currently airs on Teletoon as well as the executive producer in the onwards of "", which currently airs on Discovery Family and game show "Airmageddon", which airs on CBBC.
