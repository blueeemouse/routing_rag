Gertrude Lintz

Gertrude Ada Davies Lintz (died 1968) was an English-American dog breeder and socialite known for keeping exotic animals, including chimpanzees and gorillas, in her Brooklyn home.

 Her gorilla Buddy was sold to a circus and renamed Gargantua.

 Her gorilla Massa was sold to the Philadelphia Zoo, eventually becoming the longest-living documented gorilla.

 Her 1942 memoir "Animals Are My Hobby" inspired the 1997 American film "Buddy".

 Lintz was played by Rene Russo in the film.
