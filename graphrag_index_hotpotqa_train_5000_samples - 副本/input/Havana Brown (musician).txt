Havana Brown (musician)

Havana Brown or DJ Havana Brown (born Angelique Meunier; 14 February 1985) is an Australian DJ, recording artist, record producer and dancer.

 In 2008, Brown signed with Island Records Australia as a DJ for the record company, and began releasing her "Crave" compilation album series, which featured remixes of songs from other artists.

 This led to her touring with international artists, including Britney Spears, Rihanna, the Pussycat Dolls, Chris Brown and Enrique Iglesias.
