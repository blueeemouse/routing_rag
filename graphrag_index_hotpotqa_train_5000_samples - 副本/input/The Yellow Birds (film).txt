The Yellow Birds (film)

The Yellow Birds is a 2017 American war film directed by Alexandre Moors and based on the novel "The Yellow Birds" by Kevin Powers.

 The film stars Tye Sheridan, Jack Huston, Alden Ehrenreich, Jennifer Aniston, and Toni Collette.

 It premiered at the 2017 Sundance Film Festival on January 21, 2017.
