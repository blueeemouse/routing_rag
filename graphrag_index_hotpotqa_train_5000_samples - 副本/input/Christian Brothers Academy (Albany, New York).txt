Christian Brothers Academy (Albany, New York)

Christian Brothers Academy is a private Catholic college preparatory junior and senior high school for boys founded in 1859 by the De La Salle Christian Brothers (see Institute of the Brothers of the Christian Schools).

 Christian Brothers Academy is located in the town of Colonie, New York near the Albany International Airport on a 126 acre campus built in 1998.

 Christian Brothers Academy is independently run by a board of trustees.

 The school is known for its academic and athletic programs, and has had a 100% college acceptance rate since 1980.

 The school's athletic program also boasts numerous athletic titles.

 Christian Brothers Academy currently educates 553 students in grades 5-12.
