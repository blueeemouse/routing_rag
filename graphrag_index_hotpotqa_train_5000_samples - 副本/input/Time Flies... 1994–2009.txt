Time Flies... 1994–2009

Time Flies... 1994–2009 is a compilation album by the English rock band Oasis.

 Released on 14 June 2010, the album contains all 27 UK singles released by the band between 1994 and 2009, including "Whatever" and "Lord Don't Slow Me Down", which had previously never appeared on an Oasis studio album. "

Sunday Morning Call" is not listed anywhere on the artwork but appears as a hidden track on track 14 of the second disc.
