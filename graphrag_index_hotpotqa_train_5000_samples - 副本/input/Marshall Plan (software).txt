Marshall Plan (software)

The Marshall Plan software is a novel writing software to assist in the technical aspects of novel writing.

 The software automatically plots a novel based on literary agent Evan Marshall's novel writing system in his three book series, "The Marshall Plan for Novel Writing: A 16-Step Program Guaranteed to Take You From Idea to Completed Manuscript," "The Marshall Plan Workbook," and "The Marshall Plan for Getting Your Novel Published."

 Marshall Plan software is published by Evan Marshall and Martha Jewett, literary agent and consultant.
