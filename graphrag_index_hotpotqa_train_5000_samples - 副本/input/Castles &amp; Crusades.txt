Castles &amp; Crusades

Castles & Crusades (C&C) is a fantasy role-playing game published in 2004 by Troll Lord Games based upon a stripped-down variant of the d20 System by Wizards of the Coast.

 The game system is designed to emulate the play of earlier editions of the "Dungeons & Dragons" game while keeping the unified mechanics of the d20 System.
