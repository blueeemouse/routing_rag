Dave Luckett

Dave Luckett (born 1951) is an Australian children's writer born in Stanmore, New South Wales.

 He has written three non-fiction books about cricket and medieval weapons and armour.

 He has also written three series of fantasy books as well as a number of standalone fantasy books.

 One of the series, "The Rhianna Chronicles", has been reprinted in the United States and Poland.

 His "A Dark Winter" won the Aurealis Award for best fantasy novel in 1998.
