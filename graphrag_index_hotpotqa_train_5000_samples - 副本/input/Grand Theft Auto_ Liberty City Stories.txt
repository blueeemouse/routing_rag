Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories

Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories is an open world action-adventure video game developed by Rockstar Leeds in conjunction with Rockstar North and published by Rockstar Games.

 Released on 25 October 2005 for the PlayStation Portable, it is the ninth game in the "Grand Theft Auto" series and was preceded by "" and succeeded by "".

 It is a prequel to "Grand Theft Auto III".

 It was published and distributed by Capcom for the Japanese release.

 It was released on PlayStation 3 via the PlayStation Network on 2 April 2013.
