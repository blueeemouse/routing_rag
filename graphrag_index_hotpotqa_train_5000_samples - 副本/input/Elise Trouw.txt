Elise Trouw

Elise Ashlyn Trouw (born 1999) is an American singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist.

 She was signed in November 2015 to a one album record deal by Pacific Records of San Diego, California.

 As of October, 2016, she has released four singles, "X Marks the Spot", "She Talking", "Your Way", and "Burn".

 In September 2016, she left Pacific Records, buying out her contract, and retaining all rights to her music.
