Face Off (season 12)

The twelfth season of the Syfy reality television series Face Off (Styled as "Face Off: Divide & Conquer") premiered on June 13, 2017.

 This season divides contestants into two competing FX shops.

 Each week the teams designate one competitor the "foreman" or "forewoman", who designates group tasks, coordinates the project, and represents the team to the judges.

 At the end of each challenge, the most successful artist from the winning team is declared the challenge winner, and the least successful artist from the losing team is eliminated.
