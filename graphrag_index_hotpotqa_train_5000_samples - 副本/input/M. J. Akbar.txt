M. J. Akbar

Mobashar Jawed "M.J." Akbar (Bengali: মবাসের জাভেদ আকবর ) is an Indian politician who is the Minister of State (MoS) for External Affairs, and a Member of Parliament in the Rajya Sabha, from Madhya Pradesh.

 He was inducted into the Union Council of Ministers by PM Narendra Modi on 5 July 2016.

 He is also a veteran Indian journalist and author of several books.

 He first served as an elected Member of Parliament between 1989 and 1991, and returned to public life in March 2014, when he joined the BJP and was appointed national spokesperson during the 2014 general elections that brought the party back to office with a simple majority under the leadership of PM Narendra Modi.

 In July, 2015 he was elected to the Rajya Sabha from Jharkhand.

 During his long career in journalism, he launched, as editor, India’s first weekly political news magazine, Sunday, in 1976, and two daily newspapers, The Telegraph and The Asian Age in 1989 and 1994.

 He has also been editorial director of India Today and The Sunday Guardian.

 He was the Editor-in-Chief and then Editorial Director of "The Sunday Guardian", a weekly newspaper that he founded, until he left to join politics full-time.

 He has remained associated with leading media houses and periodicals in India including "India Today", "Headlines Today", "The Telegraph", "The Asian Age" and "Deccan Chronicle',' among others.
