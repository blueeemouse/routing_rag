Devonport Naval Base

Devonport Naval Base is the home of the Royal New Zealand Navy, located at Devonport, New Zealand on Auckland's North Shore.

 It is currently the only base of the navy that operates ships, and was a navy base from as far back as 1841.

 The base consists of HMNZS "Philomel" (the administration centre for the base), the Fleet Support Organisation, and the Fleet Personnel and Training Organisation.
