Kapasiteettiyksikkö

Kapasiteettiyksikkö (Finnish for "capacity unit") was a Finnish hip hop group from Helsinki.

 The group was formed in 1997 by Anders "Andu" Westerholm, Markku "Tasis" Wettenranta and Dan "Uniikki" Tolppanen.

 Along with Fintelligens, Kapasiteettiyksikkö was one of the first Finnish-language hip hop groups that gained popularity in the country.
