Eindra Kyaw Zin

Eindra Kyaw Zin (Burmese: အိန္ဒြာကျော်ဇင် , ] ; born 24 April 1977) is a Myanmar Academy Award winning film actress and TV commercial model.

 Kyaw Zin, an amateur painter, is one of the stars of the Burmese entertainment industry.

 She is best known for winning the 2004 Myanmar Academy Award as best actress in the film "Flirtatious Sky" (မျက်နှာများတဲ့ မိုးကောင်းကင် ).
