Saat Din Mohabbat In

Saat Din Mohabbat In (English: "Seven days in love" ) is an upcoming Pakistani romantic drama film directed by Meenu-Farjad, produced by Dawn Films and IMGC Global Entertainment and written by Fasih Bari Khan.

 The film features Mahira Khan and Sheheryar Munawar in lead roles and is also their second mutual film after "Ho Mann Jahaan".
