Hector Carlier

Hector Carlier (1884 – 1 January 1946 in Kalmthout) was a Belgian banker and industrialist who was the son of banker Jean Baptist Ferdinand Carlier and Marie de Roy.

 He was married with Amelia Goossens and together they had three children.

 He is one of the founders of the Belgian oil company Petrofina.

 Petrofina merged with Total of France to form "TotalFina" and with Elf Aquitaine in 2000 to form "TotalFinaElf".

 The company's current name is Total S.A.
