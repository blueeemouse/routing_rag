XBill

XBill is an arcade style game for the X Window System.

 The game features a bespectacled character known as "Bill".

 The goal is to prevent Bill's legions of clones from installing "Wingdows", a virus "cleverly designed to resemble a popular operating system", on a variety of computers running other operating systems.

 It was very popular among Linux gamers at the end of the 1990s, beating out "Quake", though not Quake II, as Linux Journal reader's favourite Linux game in 1999.
