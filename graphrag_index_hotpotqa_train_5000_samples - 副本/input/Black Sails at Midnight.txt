Black Sails at Midnight

Black Sails at Midnight is the second studio album by the Scottish power metal band Alestorm.

 As with their first album, all the drums tracks were recorded by session drummer Migo Wagner but touring drummer Ian Wilson added additional percussion.

 The album features string and brass arrangements and folk instruments as opposed to the more heavy metal instrumentation of Captain Morgan's Revenge.

 The special edition of the album included a live DVD of the band's performance at the 2008 Wacken Festival.
