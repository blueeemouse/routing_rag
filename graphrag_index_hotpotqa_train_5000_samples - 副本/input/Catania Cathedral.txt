Catania Cathedral

Catania Cathedral (Italian: "Duomo di Catania; Cattedrale di Sant'Agata" ), dedicated to Saint Agatha, is a Roman Catholic cathedral in Catania, Sicily, southern Italy.

 It was the seat of the Bishops of Catania until 1859, when the diocese was elevated to an archdiocese, and since then has been the seat of the Archbishops of Catania.
