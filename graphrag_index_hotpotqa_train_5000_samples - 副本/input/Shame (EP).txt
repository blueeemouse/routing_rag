Shame (EP)

Shame is the second EP by hip hop duo MadGibbs, which consists of Indiana rapper Freddie Gibbs and California hip hop musician Madlib.

 Preceded by their 2011 EP, "Thuggin'", it was released on July 10, 2012 through Madlib Invazion in both digital and 12-inch vinyl formats.

 The 8-track EP includes two vocal tracks, "Shame" featuring singer BJ the Chicago Kid and "Terrorist", as well as instrumentals, a cappellas and two bonus beats by Madlib.
