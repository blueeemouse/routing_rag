The Warriors (band)

The Warriors is an American hardcore punk band originally from Tehachapi, CA.

 Due to member changes, The Warriors are now primarily based in Oxnard, CA.

 Their work is heavily influenced by the work of '90s bands such as Rage Against the Machine and Snapcase, a unique sound during a period that had previously been saturated with heavy American metalcore.

 They followed their debut 7" release with a full-length album titled "War Is Hell", through Eulogy Recordings in 2003.

 In 2005 it was reissued with two extra tracks and computer accessible content titled "War Is Hell Redux".

 In 2006 their second album was released, titled "Beyond The Noise".

 They are now signed on to Victory Records.

 In 2007 they released their third album "Genuine Sense of Outrage".
