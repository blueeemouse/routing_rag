Nina Marie Fite

Nina Marie Fite is an American diplomat and a career member of the Senior Foreign Service.

 Currently serving as Principal Officer at the U.S. Consulate General in Montreal, Canada, she has been nominated by President Donald Trump to become the next United States Ambassador to Angola.

 Since becoming a diplomat in 1990, Fite has served at seven U.S. overseas missions.

 Fite's diplomatic assignments have included posts in Lahore as Consul General, in Kabul as Deputy Economic Counselor, and in Luanda as the Political/Economic Section Chief.

 She has also been on assignment with the Office of the United States Trade Representative.
