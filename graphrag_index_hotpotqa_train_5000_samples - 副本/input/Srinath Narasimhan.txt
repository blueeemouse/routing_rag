Srinath Narasimhan

Srinath Narasimhan (born July 8, 1962) is an Indian business executive.

 He is the current Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer of Tata Teleservices, an Indian broadband and telecommunications service provider and part of the business conglomerate Tata Group.

 Narasimhan joined the Tata Group in 1986, and has also served as the CEO of two other group companies, Tata Communications and Tata Internet Services.
