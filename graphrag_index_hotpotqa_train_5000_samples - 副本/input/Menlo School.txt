Menlo School

Menlo School, also referred to simply as Menlo, is an independent college preparatory school in Atherton, California, United States, near the heart of Silicon Valley.

 Menlo comprises a middle school that includes grades 6–8 and a high school that includes grades 9–12.

 Both the middle school and high schools are located in close physical proximity, but they operate as semi-autonomous units with select overlapping administration.
