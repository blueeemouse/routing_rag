Preluders

The Preluders were a pop girl group from Germany.

 The original group comprised singers Tertia Botha, Miriam Cani, Anh-Thu Doan, Rebecca Miro, and Anne Ross.

 Created through the ProSieben talent show "Popstars - Das Duell" (2003) in which pop groups of both genders competed against each other, Preluders lost out to boy band Overground in a public vote.

 Despite their defeat, the group achieved commercial success with their first single "Everyday Girl", which became a number-one hit in Germany, while their full-length debut album "Girls in the House" (2003) reached the top five in Germany and Switzerland.
