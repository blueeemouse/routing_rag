Belen Pass

The Belen Pass (Turkish: "Belen Geçidi" ), also known as the Syrian Gates, is a mountain pass located in the Belen District of Hatay Province in south-central Turkey.

 The narrow pass through the Nur Mountains (the ancient Amanus Mountains), said to be 300 paces across, is the most important route from the coastal region of Cilicia to inland Syria.

 It is one of two passes through the Amanus, the other being the Amanian Gate to the north.
