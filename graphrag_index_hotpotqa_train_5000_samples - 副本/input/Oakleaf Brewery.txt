Oakleaf Brewery

Oakleaf was a Gosport-based brewery founded in 2000 in Gosport, Hampshire, England.

 It brewed cask ales and bottled ales until filing for Administration in August 2016 due to the rise of microbreweries.

 In September 2016, the brewery was purchased, staff rescued from administration and the new brewery, Fallen Acorn Brewing Co, began.
