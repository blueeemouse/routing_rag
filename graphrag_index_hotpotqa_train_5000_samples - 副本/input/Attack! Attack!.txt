Attack! Attack!

Attack!

 Attack!

 were a Welsh rock band from Caerphilly and Aberdare, formed in 2006.

 They released their eponymous debut album in 2008, with a second album "The Latest Fashion" released on 27 September 2010 through Hassle Records.

 Their third album, "Long Road to Nowhere", was announced for a 1 April 2013 release, and this was later confirmed to be their final release.

 The band played a farewell April/May headliner in 2013 and then split.
