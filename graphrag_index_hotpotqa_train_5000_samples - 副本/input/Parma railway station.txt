Parma railway station

Parma (Italian: "Stazione di Parma" ) is a railway station serving the city of Parma, in the region of Emilia-Romagna, northern Italy.

 The station opened in 1859 and is located on the Milan–Bologna railway, Pontremolese railway (to La Spezia), Brescia–Parma railway and Parma–Suzzara railway.

 The train services are operated by Trenitalia, Trenord and Ferrovie Emilia Romagna.
