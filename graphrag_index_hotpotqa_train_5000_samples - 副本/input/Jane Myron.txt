Jane Myron

Jane Myron is an American politician and restaurateur who was the Mayor (from 2009 to 2011) and a city commissioner of Johnson City, Tennessee.

 She is Johnson City's second female mayor.

 She became mayor after Phil Roe's resignation to become a congressman.

 Prior to becoming mayor, Myron was vice mayor (2007–2009).

 On January 29, 2009, she announced her candidacy for re-election to a four-year term as City Commissioner.

 Two seats for four-year terms and one seat for a two-year term were on the ballot in the April 2009 election, to be concluded on Tuesday, April 28.
