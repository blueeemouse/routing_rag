Bald Knob, Arkansas

Bald Knob is a city in White County, Arkansas, United States.

 The population was 2,897 at the 2010 census.

 Located at the intersection of two of the state's natural regions, Bald Knob is often promoted as "where the Ozarks meet the Delta".

 Bald Knob is also a leading strawberry producer in the state, known for its yearly Strawberry Fest held during Mother's Day weekend.

 It was once known as the leading strawberry producer in the world.

 Bald Knob was established in 1881.
