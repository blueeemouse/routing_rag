The Last Word (Greene short story)

"The Last Word" is a short story by author Graham Greene, written in 1988 (see 1988 in literature).

 It first appeared in "The Independent" but can also be found in collections of his short fiction, notably the Penguin edition of "The Last Word and Other Stories", for which it is the lead story.

 The story, written toward the end of Greene's life, reflects his frustration at the declining influence of religion, particularly Catholicism, in the modern world.
