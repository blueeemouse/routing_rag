2008–09 Houston Cougars men's basketball team

The 2008–09 Houston Cougars men's basketball team, also known as the Houston Cougars, Houston, or UH, represented the University of Houston in the college basketball 2008–09 season.

 It was their 64th year of season play.

 The head coach for the Cougars was Tom Penders, who was serving in his 5th year in that position.

 The team played its home games at Hofheinz Pavilion on-campus in Houston, Texas.

 The Cougars played in the first game of the NCAA Division I season in the 2K Sports College Classic.
