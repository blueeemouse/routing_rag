Viktoriya Isakova

Viktoriya Evgenievna Isakova (Russian: Викто́рия Евге́ньевна Иса́кова ; born 12 October 1976) is a Russian theater and film actress.

 Her film credits include "Tochka (2006)", "The Island" (2006) and "" (2013).

 Her television credits include "Ottepel" (2013).

 In 2015 she starred in "Rodina", the Russian TV adaptation of "Homeland".
