Auld Ones

Auld Ones is an Irish animated television programme broadcast on RTÉ Two.

 It featured the antics of two Dublin pensioners, Bernie and Mary who sit at a bus stop reminiscing about their youthful adventures.

 The programme symbolises the tedium and meaninglessness of human life, which loosely connects the characters to one of the themes of existentialist philosophy.

 Divided into two series, it aired on Mondays at 22:40 and is a Wireless Production.
