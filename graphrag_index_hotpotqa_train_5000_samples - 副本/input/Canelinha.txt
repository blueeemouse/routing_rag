Canelinha

Canelinha is a municipality in the state of Santa Catarina in the South region of Brazil.

 Canelinha is a small town, located 15.2 km west of Tijucas and 9.9 km by road east of São João Batista.

 It has an altitude of 17 metres and its population according to the 2010 census is 10,603 inhabitants.

 It has land area of 151.409 km ².

 The municipality was established on 23 December 1962.
