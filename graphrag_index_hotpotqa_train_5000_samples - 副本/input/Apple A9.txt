Apple A9

The Apple A9 is a 64-bit ARM-based system-on-chip (SoC), designed by Apple Inc.

 Manufactured for Apple by both TSMC and Samsung, it first appeared in the iPhone 6S and 6S Plus which were introduced on September 9, 2015.

 Apple stated that it had 70% more CPU performance and 90% more graphics performance compared to its predecessor, the Apple A8, and was one of the most powerful and energy-efficient mobile chips on the market then along with the Samsung Exynos 8890 and Qualcomm Snapdragon 820.
