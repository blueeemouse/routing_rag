2012 BWF World Junior Championships – Teams event

The Team event tournament of the 2012 BWF World Junior Championships was the fourteenth tournament of the BWF World Junior Championships.

 It was held from October 25–28, 2012 in Chiba, Japan.

 According to the Badminton World Federation (BWF) 30 teams have confirmed their participation.

 The winner of the tournament would have Suhandinata Cup for about a year until the next BWF World Junior Championships Team Event is held.
