The Golden Girls (season 1)

The first season of the American television comedy series The Golden Girls originally aired on NBC in the United States between September 14, 1985 and May 10, 1986.

 Created by television writer Susan Harris, the series was produced by Witt/Thomas/Harris Productions and Touchstone Television.

 It stars Bea Arthur, Rue McClanahan, Betty White, and Estelle Getty as the main characters Dorothy Zbornak, Blanche Devereaux, Rose Nylund, and Sophia Petrillo.

 The series revolves around the lives of four elderly women living together in a house in Miami.
