1920 college football season

The 1920 NCAA football season had no clear-cut champion, with the "Official NCAA Division I Football Records Book" listing California, Georgia, Harvard, Notre Dame, and Princeton as national champions.

 Only California and Princeton claim national championships for the 1920 season.

 Andy Smith's Pacific Coast Conference champion California "Wonder Team" was the first national champion from the Pacific Coast.

 Princeton and Harvard were undefeated and with one tie to each other.

 Notre Dame was led by its first Walter Camp All-American, George Gipp, who died before the year was over.
