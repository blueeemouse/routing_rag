I Got a Thang for You

"I Got a Thang for You" is the second single from Miami-based rapper Trina's fourth album "Still da Baddest".

 The song features American R&B singer Keyshia Cole.

 It was chosen after much speculation and talk about the second single.

 The song was produced by Kane da Kameleon, while the chorus was written by Midus, both of Slick Salt Entertainment.
