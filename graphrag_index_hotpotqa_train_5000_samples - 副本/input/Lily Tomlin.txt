Lily Tomlin

Mary Jean "Lily" Tomlin (born September 1, 1939) is an American actress, comedian, writer, singer, and producer.

 Tomlin began her career as a stand-up comedian, and performing Off-Broadway during the 1960s.

 Her breakout role was performing as a cast member on the variety show "Rowan & Martin's Laugh-In" from 1969 until 1973.

 She currently stars on the Netflix series "Grace and Frankie" as Frankie Bergstein.

 Her performance as Frankie garnered her three consecutive nominations for the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Comedy Series in 2015, 2016, and 2017.
