Doctor and the Medics

Doctor and the Medics are an English glam rock band formed in London in 1981.

 The group received their great success during the 1980s and are best known for their cover of Norman Greenbaum's "Spirit in the Sky", the group's hit single which reached No. 1 in the UK singles charts.

 The band currently performs, with a different line-up, as a tribute act to various artists.

 The group's musical style includes neo-psychedelia, glam rock, new wave and pop rock.
