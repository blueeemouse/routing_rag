1919 SAFL Grand Final

The 1919 SAFL Grand Final was an Australian rules football competition.

 Sturt drew with North Adelaide 5.9 (19) to 5.9 (19).

 This was the second time in the competition's history that a Grand Final had been drawn, and the most recent.

 Sturt won the Grand Final Replay a week later, 3.5 (23) to 2.6 (18).
