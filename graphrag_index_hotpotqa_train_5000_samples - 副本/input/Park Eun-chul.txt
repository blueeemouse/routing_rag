Park Eun-chul

Park Eun-Chul (Hangul: 박은철, Hanja: 朴殷哲; ] or ] ; born January 18, 1981 in Cheongju, Chungcheongbuk-do) is a South Korean wrestler who won the Bronze medal in the Men's Greco-Roman 55kg in the 2008 Summer Olympics in Beijing.

 He is also a Three-time world championship medalist, receiving Bronze in 2006, and Silver in 2005 and 2007.

 He lost both his world championship finals matches to Hamid Sourian.

 Park eventually beat Sourian in the Bronze medal match at the 2008 Summer Olympics.
