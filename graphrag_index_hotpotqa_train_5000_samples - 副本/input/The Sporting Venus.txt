The Sporting Venus

The Sporting Venus (1925) is a silent romance film directed by Marshall Neilan.

 The film was the second MGM release of Neilan, and starred his wife, actress Blanche Sweet, who allegedly sported the lowest waistline of 1925.

 This is the first of two feature films that paired Ronald Colman with Blanche Sweet, the second being "His Supreme Moment", which was released in May 1925.

 This film is listed as being extant (surviving) at silentera database.
