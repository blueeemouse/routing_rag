2014 Morgan State Bears football team

The 2014 Morgan State Bears football team represented Morgan State University in the 2014 NCAA Division I FCS football season.

 They were led by first-year head coach Lee Hull and played their home games at Hughes Stadium.

 They were a member of the Mid-Eastern Athletic Conference (MAC).

 Morgan State finished the season 7–6 overall and 6–2 in MEAC play to finish in a five-way tie for a share of the conference championship.

 After tiebreakers, they received the conference's automatic bid to the FCS Playoffs, where they lost in the first round to Richmond.
