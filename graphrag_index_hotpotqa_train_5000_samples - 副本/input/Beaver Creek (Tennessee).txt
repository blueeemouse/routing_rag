Beaver Creek (Tennessee)

Beaver Creek is a tributary of the Clinch River in Knox County in East Tennessee.

 It drains a watershed area of 86.5 mi2 between Copper Ridge and Black Oak Ridge.

 It flows from northeast to southwest from the Gibbs High School area, through the Halls, Powell, Karns, Solway, and Hardin Valley communities, entering the Clinch downstream from Solway.
