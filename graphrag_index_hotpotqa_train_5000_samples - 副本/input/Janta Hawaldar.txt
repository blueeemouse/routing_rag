Janta Hawaldar

Janata Havaldar is a 1979 Bollywood film directed by Mehmood with Rajesh Khanna playing the title role and is paired opposite Yogita Bali.

 The film has Ashok Kumar and Hema Malini making extended guest appearance.

 This film was not commercially successful at the time of its release.

 However over the years, the film has been appreciated by the audiences in its screening in television and has gained cult following over the years.
