Melody Fairchild

Melody Fairchild (born as the twin to Germaine on September 10, 1973 in Boulder, Colorado) is an American long distance runner.

 She is primarily known for an exceptional high school career.

 She has been called the "Best American High School distance runner of all time."

 In 1991, she set the American Junior and National High School record in the indoor 3,000 meters.
