Tony Daniels

Tony Daniels (born January 23, 1963 in Toronto, Ontario) is a Canadian voice actor.

 He is well known for providing the voices of Uncle Flippy in JoJo's Circus and Wiseman in the "Sailor Moon" metaseries, but probably best known for providing the voice of Gambit in "X-Men: The Animated Series" and "Marvel vs. Capcom" series.

 He also provides voices for CBC News Network and appeared on camera in such shows and films as "Code Name: Eternity", "Gracie's Choice", "Get Ed" and "Eerie, Indiana: The Other Dimension".
