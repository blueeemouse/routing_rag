Home at Seven (film)

Home at Seven is a 1952 British mystery drama film directed by and starring Ralph Richardson.

 It also featured Margaret Leighton, Jack Hawkins, Campbell Singer and Michael Shepley.

 It was based on the play "Home at Seven" by R. C. Sherriff.

 The film remains Richardson's only work as a film director.

 It follows a man who returns to his suburban home one evening to discover that he has been missing for 24 hours, despite not remembering the 'lost' day.
