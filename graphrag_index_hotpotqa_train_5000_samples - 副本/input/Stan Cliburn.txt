Stan Cliburn

Stanley Gene Cliburn (born December 19, 1956, in Jackson, Mississippi) is a retired professional baseball player and the current manager of the New Britain Bees.

 He was previous the manager of the Rochester Red Wings, the Triple-A affiliate of the Minnesota Twins, from 2006 to 2009.

 Cliburn has also managed in the Arizona Fall League and at other levels in the minor leagues.

 He is the twin brother of former Major League pitcher Stu Cliburn, who remains a coach in the Twins' farm system.

 Cliburn is now in his first year as manager of the New Britain Bees of the Atlantic League, an independent baseball league.
