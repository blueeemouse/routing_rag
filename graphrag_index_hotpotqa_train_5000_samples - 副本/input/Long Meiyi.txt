Long Meiyi

Long Meiyi () is a woman from Guizhou province in China who alleged she was raped by mining magnate Zhou Shili.

 Long's allegations made domestic and international headlines after she and her parents began appealing to Beijing for justice and complaining of corruption in the courts.

 A significant factor in the case's popularity is that Long's mother and stepfather are government officials in China, who have generally found themselves on the other side of the politicised legal system that she now claims is obstructing the justice she seeks.
