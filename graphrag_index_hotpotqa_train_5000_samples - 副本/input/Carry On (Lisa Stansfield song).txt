Carry On (Lisa Stansfield song)

"Carry On" is a song recorded by British singer Lisa Stansfield for her 2014 album, "Seven".

 It was written by Stansfield and her husband Ian Devaney, and produced by Devaney and Snowboy.

 "Carry On" was announced as the second single on 10 January 2014 and premiered on Ken Bruce's BBC Radio 2 show on 13 January 2014.

 The music video premiered on 31 January 2014.

 The digital single was released in Germany on 14 February 2014 and the 7" single in the United Kingdom on 21 April 2014.

 In October 2014, Andy Lewis Remix of "Carry On" was included on the re-release of "Seven", titled "Seven+".

 Stansfield performed the song during her Seven Tour.
