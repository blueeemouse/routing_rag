Chinu Xxx

Chinu XXX (born 6 June 1987) is a British freestyle wrestler.

 Real name Chinu Chinu he competed for England in the men's freestyle 125 kg event at the 2014 Commonwealth Games where he won a bronze medal.

 He was named Chinu XXX at the Glasgow Commonwealth Games when his name was mistakenly obmitted with the XXX characters.

 The name later stuck as a comparison to Vin Diesels XXX character in the Hollywood Blockbuster Films.
