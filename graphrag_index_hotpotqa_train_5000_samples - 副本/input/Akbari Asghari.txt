Akbari Asghari

Akbari Asghari is a Pakistani drama based on Mirat-ul-Uroos.

 In 2011, the story of Mirat-ul-Uroos was adapted into a comedy television drama airing on Hum TV by the name of Akbari Asghari, in which the lead role of Akbari has been played by Sanam Baloch and Asghari by Humaima Malik along with Imran Abbas Naqvi and Fawad Khan playing the male leads.
