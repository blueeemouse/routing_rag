2008 All-Pacific-10 Conference football team

The 2008 All-Pacific-10 Conference football team consists of American football players chosen by various organizations for All-Pacific-10 Conference teams for the 2008 college football season.

 The USC Trojans won the conference, posting an 8–1 conference record.

 USC then beat the Big Ten champion Penn State Nittany Lions in the Rose Bowl 38 to 24.

 Oregon State running back Jacquizz Rodgers was voted Pac-10 Offensive Player of the Year.

 USC linebacker Rey Maualuga was voted Pat Tillman Pac-10 Defensive Player of the Year.
