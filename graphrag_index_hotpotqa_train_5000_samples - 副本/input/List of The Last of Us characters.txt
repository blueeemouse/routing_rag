List of The Last of Us characters

"The Last of Us", an action-adventure survival horror video game developed by Naughty Dog, deals with the relationship between an older man, Joel, and a teenaged girl, Ellie.

 Joel is tasked with escorting Ellie across a post-apocalyptic United States in an attempt to create a potential cure against an infection to which Ellie is immune.

 The relationship between the two characters became the basis of the game's development.
