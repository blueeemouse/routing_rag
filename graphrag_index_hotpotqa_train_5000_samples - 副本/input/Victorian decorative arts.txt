Victorian decorative arts

Victorian decorative arts refers to the style of decorative arts during the Victorian era.

 Victorian design is widely viewed as having indulged in a grand excess of ornament.

 The Victorian era is known for its interpretation and eclectic revival of historic styles mixed with the introduction of middle east and Asian influences in furniture, fittings, and interior decoration.

 The Arts and Crafts movement, the aesthetic movement, Anglo-Japanese style, and Art Nouveau style have their beginnings in the late Victorian era and gothic period.
