Blundell Park

Blundell Park is a football ground in Cleethorpes, North East Lincolnshire, England and home to Grimsby Town Football Club.

 The stadium was built in 1899, but only one of the original stands remains.

 The current capacity of the ground is 9,052, after being made all-seater in summer 1995, reducing the number from around 27,000.

 Several relegations in previous years meant the expansion seating was also taken away; that reduced the capacity further from around 12,000 to what it is now.
