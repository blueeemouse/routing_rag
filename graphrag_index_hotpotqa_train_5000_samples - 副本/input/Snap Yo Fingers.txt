Snap Yo Fingers

"Snap Yo Fingers" is a 2006 American hip hop single by Atlanta-based rapper/producer Lil Jon.

 It was originally intended to be the first single from Lil Jon's solo debut album "Step Up".

 The song was produced by Lil Jon and features E-40 and Sean P.

 The catchy, up-tempo and club oriented southern hip hop track allowed the song to peak at #7 on the U.S. Billboard Hot 100.
