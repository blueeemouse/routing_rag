Tinsel Town

Tinsel Town is a television drama co-produced by BBC Scotland and Raindog/Deep Indigo Productions.

 It ran for two series, the first debuting on BBC Two in 2000 and the second airing on BBC Choice the following year.

 Developed by Raindog, the series was created by Robbie Allen, Stuart Davids and Martin McCardie.

 Set throughout the city of Glasgow, Scotland, it deals with the lifestyles of eight main characters who each have the titular Tinsel Town nightclub in common.

 In Series One, six episodes were written by Martin McCardie, two by Ed McCardie and two by Dublin playwright, Jimmy Murphy.

 In the second series, the Tinsel Town name has extended to feature a clothing retail store in addition to the nightclub.
