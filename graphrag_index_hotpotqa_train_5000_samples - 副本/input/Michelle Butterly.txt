Michelle Butterly

Michelle Butterly (born 5 February 1970 English actress.

 She graduated from the Central School of Speech and Drama.

 She has played Julie Oldroyd in "Soldier Soldier" in 1997, Melanie Dyson in "Casualty" from 1999 to 2001, and she has appeared in "Pie in the Sky", "The Bill", "No Angels", "Midsomer Murders", and "Dangerfield".

 She appeared for two series as Ms Perin in the BBC2 sitcom "Beautiful People", and played Trudy in the fifth series of "Benidorm".
