2014 Navy Midshipmen football team

The 2014 Navy Midshipmen football team represented the United States Naval Academy in the 2014 NCAA Division I FBS football season.

 The Midshipmen were led by seventh year head coach Ken Niumatalolo and played their home games at Navy – Marine Corps Memorial Stadium.

 The Midshipmen competed as an Independent.

 This was the final year as an Independent before the school joins the American Athletic Conference.

 They finished the season 8–5.

 They were invited to the Poinsettia Bowl where they defeated San Diego State.
