Hensley Meulens

Hensley Filemon Acasio "Bam Bam" Meulens (] ; born June 23, 1967) is a retired Dutch professional baseball player and current hitting coach for the San Francisco Giants.

 A native of Curaçao, he played from 1989 to 2000 in Major League Baseball, Nippon Professional Baseball, and the Korea Baseball Organization.

 He was the first Curaçaoan to play in both Major League Baseball and the Dominican Professional Baseball League He speaks five languages: English, Spanish, Dutch, Papiamento and Japanese.
