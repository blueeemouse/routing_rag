2010 Nebraska Cornhuskers football team

The 2010 Nebraska Cornhuskers football team represented the University of Nebraska in the 2010 NCAA Division I FBS football season.

 The team was coached by Bo Pelini and played their home games at Memorial Stadium in Lincoln, Nebraska.

 They were members of the North Division of the Big 12 Conference.

 It was Nebraska's 102nd and last season in the Big 12 (including years in the MVIAA/Big Eight) as they will begin competing in the Big Ten Conference in 2011.
