Weight of the World (Ringo Starr song)

"Weight of the World" is a song performed by Ringo Starr, released on his 1992 album, "Time Takes Time".

 Written by Brian O'Doherty and Fred Velez, the song was released as a lead single backed with "After All These Years", and "Don't Be Cruel" (the latter was only on the CD single).

 The single reached 74 in the UK charts.

 It was released on 28 April 1992 in the US, and on 18 May in the UK.
