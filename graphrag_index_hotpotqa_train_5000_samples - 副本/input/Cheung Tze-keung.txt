Cheung Tze-keung

Cheung Tze-keung ((1955--)07 1955 – (1998--)05 1998 ) was a notorious Chinese gangster also known as "Big Spender" ().

 He was a kidnapper, robber, arms smuggler and was wanted for murder.

 He was best known for having masterminded the abduction of Walter Kwok and Victor Li, son of Li Ka Shing.
