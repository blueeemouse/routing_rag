Bleed American

Bleed American is the fourth studio album by American rock band Jimmy Eat World.

 It was released on July 24, 2001 by DreamWorks Records.

 It was the first recording launched after signing the band a few months prior.

 The album was re-released as Jimmy Eat World after the September 11 attacks on New York and Washington, D.C. that took place a month after its initial release.
