Ashley Hicks

Ashley Louis David Hicks (born 18 July 1963) is a British author, architect, and interior and furniture designer.

 He is the only son of Lady Pamela Hicks (née Mountbatten) and David Nightingale Hicks.

 Hicks designs architecture and interiors in Europe, the United States, and the United Kingdom.

 He divides his time between designing residential interiors and designing his fabric and furniture lines.
