A Força do Querer

A Força do Querer (English: Edge of Desire) is a Brazilian telenovela produced and aired by Rede Globo.

 It is created by Glória Perez, and directed by Rogério Gomes and Pedro Vasconcelos.

 It premiered on 3 April 2017, replacing "A Lei do Amor" at the traditional 9 pm timeslot.
