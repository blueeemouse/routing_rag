Club Atlético River Plate

Club Atlético River Plate is an Argentine sports club based in the Belgrano neighborhood of Buenos Aires, and named after the city's estuary, Río de la Plata.

 Although many sports are practiced at the club, River is best known for its professional football team, which has won the most domestic competitions in Argentina with 36 league titles in the top division, 2 second division championships and 9 national cups.

 At international level, River Plate has won a total of 16 official titles.
