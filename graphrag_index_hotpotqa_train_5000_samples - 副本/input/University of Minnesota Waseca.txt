University of Minnesota Waseca

The University of Minnesota Waseca (UMW) was a two-year technical college specializing in agriculture and located in Waseca, in the U.S. state of Minnesota.

 A part of the University of Minnesota system, it operated from 1971 to 1992 and served nearly 20,000 students during that time.

 Their mascot was the "Ramus" the ram.

 It maintained a college radio station with the call letters KOWO.

 Its campus was previously an agricultural boarding high school known as the Southern School of Agriculture.
