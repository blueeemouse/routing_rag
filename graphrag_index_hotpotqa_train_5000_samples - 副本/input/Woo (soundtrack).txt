Woo (soundtrack)

Woo is the soundtrack to the 1998 comedy film, "Woo".

 It was released on May 5, 1998 through Epic Records and consisted of hip hop and R&B music.

 The soundtrack was a moderate success, peaking at 52 on the "Billboard" 200 and 8 on the Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums and featured three charting single Charli Baltimore's "Money", which went to #50 on the Hot Rap Singles, Nate Dogg and Warren G's "Nobody Does It Better" and Cam'ron's "357".
