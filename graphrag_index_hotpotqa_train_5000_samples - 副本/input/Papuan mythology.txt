Papuan mythology

The Papuans are one of four major cultural groups of Papua New Guinea.

 The majority of the population lives in rural areas.

 In isolated areas there still remains a handful of the giant communal structures that previously housed the whole male population, with a circling cluster of huts for the women.

 The Papuan people are Melanesian people composed of at least 240 different peoples, each with its own language and culture.

 Sago is the staple food of the Papuan supplemented with hunting, fishing and small gardens.
