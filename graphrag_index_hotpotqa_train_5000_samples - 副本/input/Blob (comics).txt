Blob (comics)

The Blob (Frederick J. "Fred" Dukes) is a fictional supervillain appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics.

 The character is usually depicted as an adversary of the X-Men.

 A mutant originally depicted as an obese circus freak, the Blob claims to be immovable when he so desires.

 He possesses an extreme amount of pliable body mass, which grants him superhuman strength.

 Possessing the mindset of a bully, he mostly uses his powers for petty crime and as a member of the Brotherhood of Mutants and Freedom Force.

 He is one of the most prominent, long-running mutants to be depowered as a result of the "Decimation" storyline.
