Walk Through Exits Only

Walk Through Exits Only is the debut solo album by former Pantera frontman Phil Anselmo.

 It was released in July 16, 2013 under Anselmo's own label, Housecore Records.

 This album has been compared to Pantera's 1996 album The Great Southern Trendkill due to Anselmo's extreme vocals and very heavy guitar riffs.
