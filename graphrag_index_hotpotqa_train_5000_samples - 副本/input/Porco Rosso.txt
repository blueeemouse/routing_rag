Porco Rosso

Porco Rosso (Japanese: 紅の豚 , Hepburn: Kurenai no Buta , lit.

 "Crimson Pig") is a 1992 Japanese animated comedy-adventure film written and directed by Hayao Miyazaki.

 It is based on "Hikōtei Jidai" ("The Age of the Flying Boat"), a three-part watercolor manga by Miyazaki.

 The film stars the voices of Shūichirō Moriyama, Tokiko Kato, Akemi Okamura and Akio Ōtsuka.

 Toshio Suzuki produced the film for Studio Ghibli.

 Joe Hisaishi composed the music.
