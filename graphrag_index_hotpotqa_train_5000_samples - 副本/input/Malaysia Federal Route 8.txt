Malaysia Federal Route 8

The Federal Route 8, also known as the Kuala Lumpur–Kota Bharu Highway (Malay: "Jalan Kuala Lumpur–Kota Bharu"), is a 402.7-km federal highway in Malaysia.

 It connects Bentong in the south until Kota Bharu in the north.

 The highway had two parts - Kota Bharu–Kuala Krai Road (Malay: "Jalan Kota Bharu–Kuala Krai") and Bentong–Kuala Lipis Road (Malay: "Jalan Bentong–Kuala Lipis") until the completion of the missing link from Kuala Krai to Kuala Lipis, causing the entire FT8 highway to take 99 years to be completed.
