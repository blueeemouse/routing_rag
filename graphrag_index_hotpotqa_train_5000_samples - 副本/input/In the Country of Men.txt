In the Country of Men

In the Country of Men is the debut novel of Libyan writer Hisham Matar, first published in 2006 by Viking, an imprint of Penguin Books.

 It was nominated for the 2006 Man Booker Prize and the Guardian First Book Award.

 It has so far been translated into 22 languages and was awarded the 2007 Royal Society of Literature Ondaatje Prize as well as a host of international literary prizes.

 The book was also nominated for the 2007 National Book Critics Circle Award in the U.S.
