Glaiza Herradura

Glaiza Herradura-Agullo (born February 24, 1978) is a Filipino former child actress.

 She was the first-ever grand winner of the Little Miss Philippines segment of "Eat Bulaga!

" in 1984.

 She starred in RPN-9's television series "Heredero" with Manilyn Reynes and Richard Arellano.

 She won the 1988 FAMAS Best Child Actress award for her role in "Batas Sa Aking Kamay" starring Fernando Poe, Jr..
