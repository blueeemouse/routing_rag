Callin' All Dogs

<nowiki>Callin' All Dogs</nowiki> is a 1995 album by Louisville, Kentucky rock band Bodeco.

 This, the rockabilly band's second album, made a considerable impact on the Louisville music scene, finding a place at #80 on WFPK's "top 1000 best albums ever". "

Trouser Press" asserted that the album reinforced "Bodeco's simple genius by turning up the slop right from the get-go".
