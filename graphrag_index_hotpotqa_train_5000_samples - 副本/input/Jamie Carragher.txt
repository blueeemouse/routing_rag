Jamie Carragher

James Lee Duncan Carragher ( ; born 28 January 1978) is an English retired footballer who played as a defender for Premier League club Liverpool during a career which spanned 17 years.

 He is currently a pundit and commentator for Sky Sports.

 A one-club man, he was Liverpool's vice-captain for 10 years, and is the club's second-longest ever serving player, making his 737th appearance for Liverpool in all competitions on 19 May 2013.

 Carragher also holds the record for the most appearances in European competition for Liverpool with 150.
