John Boyega

John Adedayo B. Adegboyega (born 17 March 1992), known professionally as John Boyega, is an English actor and producer best known for playing Finn in the 2015 film "", the seventh film of the "Star Wars" series.

 Boyega rose to prominence in his native United Kingdom for his role as Moses in the 2011 sci-fi comedy film "Attack the Block".
