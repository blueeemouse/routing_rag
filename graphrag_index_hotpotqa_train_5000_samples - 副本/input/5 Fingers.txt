5 Fingers

5 Fingers, known also as "Five Fingers", is a 1952 American spy film directed by Joseph L. Mankiewicz and produced by Otto Lang.

 The screenplay by Michael Wilson was based on the book "Operation Cicero" (Original German: "Der Fall Cicero") (1950) by Ludwig Carl Moyzisch, Nazi commercial attaché at the German Embassy in Ankara (1943-44).

 In the film, James Mason plays Ulysses Diello (Cicero), the character based on Elyesa Bazna.

 The rest of the cast includes Danielle Darrieux, Michael Rennie, Herbert Berghof and Walter Hampden.
