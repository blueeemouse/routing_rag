Michael Tippett (businessman)

Michael Tippett (born February 23, 1970) is a Canadian entrepreneur, columnist and educator.

 He was the founder of NowPublic which is in citizen journalism.

 In 2009 the site was nominated for an Emmy in Advanced Technology.

 In August 2009 NowPublic was acquired by billionaire, Phillip Anschutz for a reported 25 million dollars.

 In 2009, Tippett was recognized by the British Columbia New Media Association with an award for visionary leadership.
