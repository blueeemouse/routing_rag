924th Fighter Group

The 924th Fighter Group is a group of the United States Air Force Reserve.

 It assigned to Tenth Air Force and stationed at Davis-Monthan Air Force Base, Arizona as a Geographically Separated Unit (GSU) of the Air Force Reserve Command's 442d Fighter Wing at Whiteman Air Force Base, Missouri.

 On 1 October 2012, the 924 FG was reassigned to the 944th Fighter Wing at Luke Air Force Base, Arizona.

 The 924th flies the A-10 Thunderbolt II aircraft and is gained by Air Combat Command when mobilized.
