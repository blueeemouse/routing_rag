Changchun Subway

Changchun Subway or Changchun Rail Transit is rail mass transit system in the city of Changchun, Jilin province, China.

 Its first line, Line 3, is the first true light rail line in Mainland China.

 The initial phase of Line 3 was completed in 2001, the 2nd phase was completed in 2006.

 It officially opened to revenue service in October 2002.

 The current total length of the route is 50.63 km with 49 stations.

 4 sections of 4 Lines are under construction.
