2016–17 Denver Pioneers men's basketball team

The 2016–17 Denver Pioneers men's basketball team represented the University of Denver during the 2016–17 NCAA Division I men's basketball season.

 The Pioneers, led by first-year head coach Rodney Billups, played their home games at Magness Arena and were members of The Summit League.

 They finished the season 16–14, 8–8 in Summit League play to finish in a three-way tie for fourth place.

 They lost in the quarterfinals of the Summit League Tournament to South Dakota State.
