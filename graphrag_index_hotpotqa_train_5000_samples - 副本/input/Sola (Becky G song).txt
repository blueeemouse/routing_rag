Sola (Becky G song)

"Sola" is a song recorded by Mexican-American singer and actress Becky G.

 It was released on June 24, 2016 through Kemosabe Records as the lead single from her forthcoming Spanish debut album.

 "Sola" was written by Gomez alongside Saul Alexander Castillo Vasquez, Steven Dominguez, and Martin Rodriguez Vincente; it is her first song to be recorded entirely in Spanish.

 The song features elements of reggaeton, urban, and Latin music, and lyrically speaks of gaining freedom after the conclusion of a negative relationship.

 It was also produced by Saul Alexander Castillo Vasquez, taking the name A.C. Gomez has confirmed that an English version of the song has been recorded, and is set to be released in the future.
