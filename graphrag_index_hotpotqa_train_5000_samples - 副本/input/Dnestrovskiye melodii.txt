Dnestrovskiye melodii

Dnestrovskiye melodii (Russian: Днестровские мелодии ) (Romanian: "Melodii nistrene" ) is a 1973 Soviet Moldavian musical film starring Sofia Rotaru in the main role, as well as Ion Suruceanu, Nadezhda Chepraga and Maria Cudreanu.

 The movie symbolizes the propaganda and ideology of the Soviet regime.

 The movie features songs in Romanian and Russian of Sofia Rotaru and other singers, as well as behind the scenes background voice monologues in Russian between the songs.
