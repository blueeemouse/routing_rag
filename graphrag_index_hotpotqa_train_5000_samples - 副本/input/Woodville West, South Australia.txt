Woodville West, South Australia

Woodville West is a suburb of Adelaide, situated about 9 kilometres northwest of the Central Business District.

 It lies within the City of Charles Sturt.

 The postcode of Woodville West is 5011.

 Woodville West is bounded by the railway line and Port Road in the north, Findon Road in the east, Trimmer Parade, Todville Street and Minns Street East in the south, and Frank Mitchell Park, Agnes Street and the railway line in the west.

 As of 2011, Renewal SA and the City of Charles Sturt have been implementing the Woodville West Neighbourhood Renewal Project, which involves an $130m property development.
