Kurupt

Ricardo Emmanuel Brown (born November 23, 1972), better known by his stage name Kurupt, is an American rapper, actor and former Executive Vice President of Death Row Records.

 He is a member of the rap group Tha Dogg Pound, and is mostly known for his work with Death Row Records.

 He is also a member of the hip-hop supergroups The HRSMN and Diirty OGz.
