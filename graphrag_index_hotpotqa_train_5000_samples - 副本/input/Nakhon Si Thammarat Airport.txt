Nakhon Si Thammarat Airport

Nakhon Si Thammarat Airport (Thai: ) (IATA: NST, ICAO: VTSF) is an airport serving Nakhon Si Thammarat, a town in the Nakhon Si Thammarat Province of Thailand.

 The airport is 14 km from the city.

 The capacity of the arrivals terminal is 188 persons per hour and that of the departure terminal is 215 people per hour.

 Nakhon Si Thammarat Airport has 2 Boeing 737 parking spaces, 2 ATR 72 parking spaces, and 2 helipad spaces.

 Total space, 2,902,400 square meters.
