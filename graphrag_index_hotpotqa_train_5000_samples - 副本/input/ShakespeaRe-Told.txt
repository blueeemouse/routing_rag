ShakespeaRe-Told

ShakespeaRe-Told is the umbrella title for a series of four television adaptations of William Shakespeare's plays broadcast on BBC One during November 2005.

 In a similar manner to the 2003 production of "The Canterbury Tales", each play is adapted by a different writer, and relocated to the present day.

 The plays were produced in collaboration by BBC Northern Ireland and the central BBC drama department.

 In August 2006 the four films aired on BBC America.
