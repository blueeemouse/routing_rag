The Collection (Black Sabbath album)

The Collection is a compilation album released by English heavy metal band Black Sabbath in 1992.

 The album was released on the label Castle, who released two CD versions of this album in the UK, both with the same cover art and songs.

 The album includes greatest songs of Black Sabbath with Ozzy Osbourne prior to his dismissal in 1979, from the eponymous album to "Never Say Die!

".

 The album has 15 tracks, two from "Black Sabbath", two from "Paranoid", one from "Master of Reality", two from "Black Sabbath Vol.

 4", two from "Sabbath Bloody Sabbath", two from "Sabotage", two from "Technical Ecstasy" and two from "Never Say Die!

".
