Stranger by the Lake

Stranger by the Lake (French: L'Inconnu du lac ) is a 2013 French drama-thriller film written and directed by Alain Guiraudie.

 The film premiered in the Un Certain Regard section at the 2013 Cannes Film Festival where Guiraudie won the award for Best Director.

 The film also won the Queer Palm award, and was mentioned on multiple top ten lists of the best films of 2014.
