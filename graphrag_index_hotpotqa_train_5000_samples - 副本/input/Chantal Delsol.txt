Chantal Delsol

Chantal Delsol ("a.k.a.:"Chantal Millon-Delsol), born 16 April 1947 in Paris, is a French philosopher, political historian and novelist.

 Founder of the Hannah Arendt research institute founded in 1993.

 She is openly catholic, and a disciple of Julien Freund and Pierre Boutang, describes herself as a "liberal-conservative".
