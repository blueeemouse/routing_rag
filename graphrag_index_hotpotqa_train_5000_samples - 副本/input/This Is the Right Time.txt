This Is the Right Time

"This Is the Right Time" is a song recorded by British singer Lisa Stansfield for her 1989 album, "Affection".

 It was written by Stansfield, Ian Devaney and Andy Morris, and produced by Coldcut.

 The song received favorable reviews from music critics.

 "This Is the Right Time" was released as the first European single on 31 July 1989.
