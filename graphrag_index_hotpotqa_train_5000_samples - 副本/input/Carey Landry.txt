Carey Landry

Carey Landry (born 1944) is an American composer of Catholic liturgical music.

 Among his better-known compositions are "Abba, Father", "Hail Mary, Gentle Woman", "Only a Shadow", and a musical accompaniment to the traditional verses "Peace Is Flowing Like a River".

 He and his wife, Carol Jean Kinghorn, have released several albums and collections of music through their publisher, Oregon Catholic Press.

 He has a master's degree in theology from The Catholic University of America and lives in Indianapolis, Indiana.
