Colby College

Colby College is a private liberal arts college in Waterville, Maine.

 Approximately 1,800 students from more than 60 countries are enrolled annually.

 The college offers 54 major fields of study and 30 minors.

 It was founded in 1813 as the Maine Literary and Theological Institution until it was renamed after the city it resides in with Waterville College.

 The donations of Christian philanthropist Gardner Colby saw the institution renamed again to Colby University before concluding on its final and current title, reflecting its liberal arts college curriculum.
