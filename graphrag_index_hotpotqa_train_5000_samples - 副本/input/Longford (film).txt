Longford (film)

Longford is a 2006 British television drama film directed by Tom Hooper and written by Peter Morgan.

 The film centres on Labour Party peer Lord Longford and his campaign for the parole of Moors Murderer Myra Hindley.

 It was produced by Granada Productions for Channel 4, in association with HBO, and stars Jim Broadbent and Samantha Morton.

 The film was first broadcast on Channel 4 on 26 October 2006 and was an Official Selection at the 2007 Sundance Film Festival.

 Broadbent won the British Academy Television Award for his role.
