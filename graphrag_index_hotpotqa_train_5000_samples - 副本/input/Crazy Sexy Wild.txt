Crazy Sexy Wild

"Crazy Sexy Wild" is a song recorded by Romanian singer Inna from her third studio album, "Party Never Ends" (2013).

 It was digitally released on 14 September 2012 by DIY, while a Romanian-language version titled "Tu și eu" (Romanian: "You and I"/"You and me") was previously made available on 12 June 2012 through Roton.

 The track was written by Henri Lanz, Kimberly Cole, Will Rappaport and Inna, while production was handled by Sebastian Barac, Radu Bolfea and Marcel Botezan.

 Musically, it is a dance recording featuring 90s sounds in its instrumentation.

 One music critic compared it to Nicki Minaj's "Starships" (2012).
