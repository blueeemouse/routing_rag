1923 Women's World Games

The 1923 Women's Olympiad ( ) was the fourth international event in women's sports, the tournament was held 4 to 7 April 1923 in Monte Carlo, Monaco.

 The tournament was formally called ""Les Jeux Athlétiques Féminins à Monté Carlo"".

 The games were a runner-up to the 1921 Women's Olympiad and 1922 Women's Olympiad.
