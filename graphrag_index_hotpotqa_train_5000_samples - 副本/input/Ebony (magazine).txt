Ebony (magazine)

Ebony is a monthly magazine for the African-American market.

 It was founded by John H. Johnson in Chicago and has published continuously since the autumn of 1945.

 A digest-sized sister magazine, "Jet", was founded by the Johnson Publishing Company.

 After 71 years, in 2016, Johnson sold the publications to private equity firm Clear View Group.

 The new publisher will be known as Ebony Media Corporation.
