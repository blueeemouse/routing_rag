2009–10 Penn State Lady Lions basketball team

The 2009–10 Penn State Lady Lions basketball team represented Pennsylvania State University in the 2009–10 NCAA Division I women's basketball season.

 It was the 46th season of Lady Lions basketball.

 The Lady Lions, a member of the Big Ten Conference, finished the season tied for sixth in the conference.

 They advanced to the WNIT, losing in the first round to Hofstra.
