2014 Portland State Vikings football team

The 2014 Portland State Vikings football team represented Portland State University in the 2014 NCAA Division I FCS football season.

 They were led by fifth year head coach Nigel Burton and played their home games at Providence Park.

 They were a member of the Big Sky Conference.

 They finished the season 3–9, 2–6 in Big Sky play to finish in a three way tie for tenth place.
