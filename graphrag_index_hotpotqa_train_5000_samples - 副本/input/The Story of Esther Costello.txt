The Story of Esther Costello

The Story of Esther Costello is a 1957 British drama film starring Joan Crawford, Rossano Brazzi, and Heather Sears (who won a BAFTA as Best British Actress for her performance).

 The film is an exposé of large-scale fundraising.

 "The Story of Esther Costello" was produced by David Miller and Jack Clayton, with Miller directing.

 The screenplay by Charles Kaufman was based on the 1952 novel by Nicholas Monsarrat.

 It was distributed by Columbia Pictures.
