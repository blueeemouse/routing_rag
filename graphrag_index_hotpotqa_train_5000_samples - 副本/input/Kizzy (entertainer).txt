Kizzy (entertainer)

Kizzy Yuanda Constance Getrouw (born 14 March 1979) is a Dutch actress, singer-songwriter, poet and television host who performs under her stage name Kizzy.

 She became a household name in the Netherlands Antilles with a hit songs and TV shows.

 In the United States she is best known for hosting TV shows on both "The Gossip Swapp" on XY TV and CN8.
