Toyland (comics)

Toyland is an 8-page funny animal comic book short story drawn and lettered by Carl Barks.

 Barks may have written the tale, but this is uncertain.

 "Toyland" features Donald Duck, his nephews Huey, Dewey, and Louie, and Santa Claus.

 It was first published by Dell as a Firestone Giveaway entitled Donald and Mickey Merry Christmas #1948 (December 1948).

 Also in the volume is an 8-page Mickey Mouse story entitled "A Day with Santa".
