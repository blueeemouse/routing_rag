Robert S. Nelsen

Robert S. Nelsen (born January 21, 1952) is the eighth President of California State University, Sacramento in Sacramento, California.

 He assumed leadership of the university on July 1, 2015.

 Prior to becoming president of CSUS, he was serving as special adviser to the University of Texas System's Executive Vice Chancellor for Academic Affairs.

 Before taking on this position, Nelsen served as the eighth President of the University of Texas–Pan American from January 2010 to August 2014.
