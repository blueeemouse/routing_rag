Boingo Alive

Boingo Alive is a double live album by Oingo Boingo, released on September 26, 1988.

 During recordings, the band performed live in a studio with no audience; hence it is not a traditional "live" album.

 It was released to celebrate the 10th anniversary of the band's inception.

 The song selection spans the group's entire discography, as well as including several previously unreleased pieces.
