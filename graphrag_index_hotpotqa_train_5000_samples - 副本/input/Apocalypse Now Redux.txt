Apocalypse Now Redux

Apocalypse Now Redux is a 2001 extended version of Francis Ford Coppola's epic war film "Apocalypse Now", which was originally released in 1979.

 Coppola, along with editor/long-time collaborator Walter Murch, added 49 minutes of scenes that had been cut out of the original film.

 It represents a significant reedit of the original version.
