Andrea Casiraghi

Andrea Albert Pierre Casiraghi (born 8 June 1984) is the elder son of Caroline, Princess of Hanover, and her second husband Stefano Casiraghi.

 He is the eldest grandchild of Rainier III, Prince of Monaco, and American actress Grace Kelly.

 Casiraghi is currently fourth in the line of succession to the Monegasque throne, following his twin cousins and mother.
