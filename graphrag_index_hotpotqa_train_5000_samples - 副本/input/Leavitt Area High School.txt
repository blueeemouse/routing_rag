Leavitt Area High School

Leavitt Area High School is a public secondary school that serves grades 9–12 in Turner, Maine.

 It is a regional high school and serves the communities of Turner, Leeds, and Greene and is run by Maine School Administrative District 52.

 It is bordered by the school districts of Buckfield Junior-Senior High School, Edward Little High School, Lewiston High School, Winthrop High School, Monmouth Academy (Maine), and Cony High School.
