WWF Forceable Entry

WWF Forceable Entry is a soundtrack album by WWE (then known as the World Wrestling Federation, or WWF).

 Released on March 26, 2002 by Columbia Records, it features entrance music of WWE wrestlers re-recorded by various hard rock and heavy metal artists and bands.

 It is also the last album released under the "WWF" name, as the company changed its name to "WWE" in May 2002 (after a British court ruled in favor of the World Wide Fund for Nature for ownership of the "WWF" name and branding (the World Wrestling Federation and the World Wide Fund for Nature had used the "WWF" name and branding since 1979 at the time)).

 The album was a commercial success, charting at number three on the US "Billboard" 200.
