William E. Ford

William E. Ford (born June 18, 1961) is an American businessman.

 He is the CEO of General Atlantic, a global growth equity firm with $19.6 billion in assets as of June 30, 2016.

 The former Morgan Stanley investment banker was ranked by "Forbes" magazine at #5 on the "Midas List" in 2009.

 He lives in New York City.
