Knock Knock (Doctor Who)

"Knock Knock" is the fourth episode of the tenth series of the British science fiction television series "Doctor Who".

 It is written by Mike Bartlett and was broadcast on 6 May 2017 on BBC One.

 "Knock Knock" received mixed reviews from critics, receiving criticism regarding the strength of the writing, but praise on the quality of the performances in the story, particularly Suchet's.
