Whole Lotta Holes

"Whole Lotta Holes" is a song written by Jon Vezner and Don Henry, and recorded by American country music artist Kathy Mattea.

 It was released in July 1991 as the second single from the album "Time Passes By".

 The song reached #18 on the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles & Tracks chart.

 The Roches provide background vocals.
