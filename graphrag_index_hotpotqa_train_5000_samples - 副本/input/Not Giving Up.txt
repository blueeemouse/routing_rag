Not Giving Up

"Not Giving Up" is a song by English-Irish girl group The Saturdays.

 It was released on 6 April 2014 as the fifth and final single from their fourth studio album "Living for the Weekend" (2013).

 The song was written by Antonio Egizii, Celetia Martin, David Musumeci, Carl Ryden and Carla Marie Williams, and produced by Ryden.
