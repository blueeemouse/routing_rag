Lilli Cooper

Lilli Cooper is a New York City-born actress who played Elphaba in the Australian Tour of Wicked as the standby.

 She was also in the OBC of Spring Awakening as Martha.

 She is the daughter of artist Chuck Cooper.

 She attended LaGuardia High School of Music & Art and Performing Arts.

 She will be starring in the upcoming Broadway production of"SpongeBob SquarePants".
