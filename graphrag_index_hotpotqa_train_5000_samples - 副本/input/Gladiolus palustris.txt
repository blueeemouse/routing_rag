Gladiolus palustris

Gladiolus palustris, common name Marsh gladiolus or Sword Lily, is a herbaceous perennial plant belonging to the genus Gladiolus of the Iridaceae family.

 The Genus nane "Gladiolus" is the Latin diminutive of gladius, a sword, while the specific Latin name "palustris", meaning "growing in marshes", refers to the alleged environment of this species.
