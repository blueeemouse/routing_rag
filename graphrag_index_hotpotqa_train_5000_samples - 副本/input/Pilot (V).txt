Pilot (V)

"Pilot" is the series premiere of the 2009 reimagining of the 1983 miniseries "V" created by Kenneth Johnson.

 The episode's teleplay was written by Scott Peters, with story credit going to Johnson and Peters.

 Yves Simoneau directed the episode, which originally aired in the United States on ABC on November 3, 2009.

 The episode sees spaceships appear over 29 of the world's major cities.

 Though the alien "Visitors" claim to come in peace, it transpires that they have been infiltrating the planet for decades, and are planning on enslaving the human species.
