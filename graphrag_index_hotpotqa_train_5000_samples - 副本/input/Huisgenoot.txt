Huisgenoot

Huisgenoot (Afrikaans for "House Companion") is a weekly Afrikaans-language general interest family magazine.

 It has the highest circulation figures of any South African magazine and is followed by sister magazine "YOU", its English-language version.

 A third magazine, "Drum", is directed at the black market.

 The magazines have a combined circulation of about 550 000 copies a week.

 Henriëtte Loubser is the current editor of "Huisgenoot".
