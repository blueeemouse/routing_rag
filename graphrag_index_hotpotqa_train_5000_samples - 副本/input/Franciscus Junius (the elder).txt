Franciscus Junius (the elder)

Franciscus Junius the Elder (born François du Jon, 1 May 1545 – 13 October 1602) was a Reformed scholar and theologian.

 Born in Bourges in central France, he initially studied law, but later decided to study theology in Geneva under John Calvin and Theodore Beza.

 He became a minister in Antwerp, but was forced to flee to Heidelberg in 1567.

 He wrote a translation of the Bible into Latin with Emmanuel Tremellius, and his "De Vera Theologia" was a text in Reformed scholasticism.
