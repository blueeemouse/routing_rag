The Rubberbandits

The Rubberbandits are an Irish comedy hip-hop duo from Limerick city.

 They consist of Blindboy Boatclub (real name Dave Chambers) and Mr Chrome (sometimes Bobby Chrome; real name Bob McGlynn).

 During performances and interviews, they conceal their identities with masks made from plastic shopping bags.

 They are often accompanied by DJ Willie O'DJ, a silent masked caricature of local politician Willie O'Dea (portrayed by several consecutive men, including Spin South West DJ Paul Webb).

 The group describe themselves as artists, and have dubbed their movement as "Gas Cuntism".
