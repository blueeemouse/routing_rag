List of songs recorded by Sy Smith

Sy Smith is an American recording artist.

 Smith rose to fame in the late 1990s when she signed a recording contract with Hollywood Records.

 Smith released her first studio album, "Psykosoul", in 2000, which spawned the singles "Gladly" and "Good N Strong".

 During the hiatus of her career in 2001, Smith joined The Brand New Heavies.

 In 2004, she embarked on her solo career that she pursued following the release of the group's album "We Won't Stop" in 2002.

 She has written and recorded material for her four studio albums, namely "Psykosoul" (2000), "The Syberspace Social" (2005), "Conflict" (2008), and "Fast and Curious" (2012).

 In 2011, Smith toured with duo The Foreign Exchange and a temporary member of the group.

 In June 2011, the group released a live album "".

 Songs included in this list are from her studio albums, extended plays, soundtrack albums, live albums, mixtapes, and collaborations with other recording artists on their respective albums.

 Also included in this list are songs that Smith recorded, but remain unreleased or were never officially released.
