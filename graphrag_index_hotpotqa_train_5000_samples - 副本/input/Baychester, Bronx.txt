Baychester, Bronx

Baychester is dominated by one and two family homes of various styles.

 The total land area is 2.61 square miles.

 The area is low laying and flat; some parts of the East Bronx are built on marshland and valleys.

 The area was formerly home to the amusement park Freedomland U.S.A., opened in 1959 and closed in 1964.
