Coalition 2016

Gambia Coalition 2016, was a coalition of seven Gambian political parties and one independent candidate created to field and support a unity candidate for the Gambian opposition in the 2016 Gambian presidential election.

 The coalition selected real estate developer and chairman of the United Democratic Party (UDP) Adama Barrow as their candidate.

 Barrow officially left the UDP to allow him to run as an independent candidate, although his candidacy continued to be supported by the UDP through its membership in the coalition.
