Signs of Life (Poets of the Fall album)

Signs of Life is the debut album by the Finnish alternative rock band Poets of the Fall.

 It was released on 19 January 2005 and debuted at number-one on the Finnish charts.

 The album was certified platinum on 26 April 2006 and remained in the Top 40 for more than one year (56 weeks).

 The album was released on the iTunes Music Shop in Finland, Norway, Denmark and Sweden in August 2005.

 It was released on iTunes for worldwide purchase on 12 April 2008.

 A limited vinyl-edition was released on the band's official webshop on 19 January 2011.
