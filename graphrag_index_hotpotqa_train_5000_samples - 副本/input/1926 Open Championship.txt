1926 Open Championship

The 1926 Open Championship was the 61st Open Championship, held 23–25 June at Royal Lytham & St Annes Golf Club in Lytham St Annes, England.

 Amateur Bobby Jones won the first of his three Claret Jugs, two strokes ahead of runner-up Al Watrous.

 Jones was the first amateur to win the title in 29 years, last by Harold Hilton in 1897.

 Americans finished in the top four spots and it was the fifth victory by an American in six years.
