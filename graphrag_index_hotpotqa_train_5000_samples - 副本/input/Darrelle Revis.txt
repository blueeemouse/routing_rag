Darrelle Revis

Darrelle Shavar Revis (born July 14, 1985) is an American football cornerback who is currently a free agent.

 He played college football for the University of Pittsburgh and was drafted in the first round (14th overall) by the New York Jets in the 2007 NFL Draft.

 He has also played for the Tampa Bay Buccaneers and New England Patriots, with whom he won Super Bowl XLIX over the Seattle Seahawks.
