West Hills, New York

West Hills is a hamlet and census-designated place (CDP) in the Town of Huntington, Suffolk County, New York, United States.

 The population was 5,592 at the 2010 census.

 Residents have a Huntington postal address but much earlier in its history, West Hills had its own post office located on Jericho Turnpike (formerly Middle Post Road).

 Also, it is addjacent to the hamlet of Woodbury.
