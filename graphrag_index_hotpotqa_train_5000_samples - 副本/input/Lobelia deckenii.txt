Lobelia deckenii

Lobelia deckenii (syn.

 "Lobelia keniensis") is a species of giant lobelia of the mountains of East Africa.

 It grows in moist areas, such as valley bottoms and moorland, in contrast to "Lobelia telekii" which grows in a similar, but drier habitat.

 These two species produce occasional hybrids.

 "Lobelia deckenii" plants usually produce multiple rosettes.

 Each rosette grows for several decades, produces a single large inflorescence and hundreds of thousands of seeds, then dies.

 Because individual plants have multiple rosettes, they survive to reproduce repeatedly, and plants with more rosettes flower more frequently.

 It is iteroparous.
