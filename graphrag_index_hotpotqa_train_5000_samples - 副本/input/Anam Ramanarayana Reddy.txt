Anam Ramanarayana Reddy

Ramanarayana Reddy Anam (born 10 July 1952) is a politician in India.

 s of 2012 , he is the Finance and Planning Minister for the state of Andhra Pradesh, with a seat in the cabinet of the government led by Nallari Kiran Kumar Reddy.

 He is a member of the Telugu Desam Party political party.
