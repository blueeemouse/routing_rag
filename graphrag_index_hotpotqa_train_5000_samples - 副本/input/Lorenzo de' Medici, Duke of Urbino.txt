Lorenzo de' Medici, Duke of Urbino

Lorenzo di Piero de' Medici (] ), (12 September 1492 – 4 May 1519) was the ruler of Florence from 1516 to his death in 1519.

 He was also Duke of Urbino from 1516 to 1519.

 His daughter, Catherine de' Medici, became Queen Consort of France, while his illegitimate son, Alessandro de' Medici, became the first Duke of Florence.
