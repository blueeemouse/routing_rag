Ryan Jones (baseball)

Ryan Matthew Jones (born November 5, 1974) is an American former professional baseball player who spent 14 seasons in minor league baseball and the independent baseball leagues.

 He hit over 200 professional home runs in his career.

 Once considered a potential 30 home run hitter at the major league level, he peaked briefly at Triple-A but never reached the big leagues.

 He had been compared to Mark McGwire.
