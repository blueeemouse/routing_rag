1999 San Marino Grand Prix

The 1999 San Marino Grand Prix was a Formula One motor race held on 2 May 1999 at the Autodromo Enzo e Dino Ferrari, Imola, Italy.

 It was the third race of the 1999 Formula One season.

 The 62-lap race was won by Ferrrari driver Michael Schumacher after he started from third position.

 David Coulthard finished second for the McLaren team with Stewart driver Rubens Barrichello third.
