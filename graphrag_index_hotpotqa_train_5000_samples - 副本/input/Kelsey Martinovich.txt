Kelsey Martinovich

Kelsey Martinovich (born 27 November 1990) is an Australian model, best known for being the runner up in the sixth cycle of "Australia's Next Top Model" where she was accidentally announced the winner first by host Sarah Murdoch.

 Kelsey was covered on Harper's Bazaar Australia Magazine after she was announced the winner first.

 The editor decided to publish half the November issue with winner Amanda Ware and half with Kelsey Martinovich.
