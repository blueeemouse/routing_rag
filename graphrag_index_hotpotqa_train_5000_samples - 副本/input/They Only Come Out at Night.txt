They Only Come Out at Night

They Only Come Out at Night is the third studio album by Edgar Winter and the first by the Edgar Winter Group.

 A commercial hit, the album reached the #3 slot on the "Billboard" 200 chart and also features two of the band's biggest songs: "Frankenstein" (#1 on the "Billboard" Hot 100) and "Free Ride" (#14 on that same chart).

 Musically, the album features a mixture of blues rock and pop rock influences in a generally carefree, upbeat sound.

 The album eventually sold two million copies.
