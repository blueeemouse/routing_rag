Baby by Me

"Baby By Me" is the official 1st single by US rapper 50 Cent from his 4th studio album "Before I Self Destruct".

 It was commercially released on September 10, 2009.

 The song is produced by Polow Da Don and William Tyler, and written by Curtis Jackson, Jamal Jones, William Tyler and Shaffer Smith.

 It features vocals by R&B singer Ne-Yo.
