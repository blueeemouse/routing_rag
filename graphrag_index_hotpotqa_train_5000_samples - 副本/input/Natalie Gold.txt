Natalie Gold

Natalie Gold is an American actress who has appeared in film, television, and stage productions, including on Broadway.

 She is perhaps best known for playing Julia Harwell on the TV show "Rubicon", and she has appeared in many films including "Before the Devil Knows You're Dead", "I Don't Know How She Does It", "Love & Other Drugs".

 She has performed opposite Jake Gyllenhall, Naomi Watts, Cynthia Nixon, and she has been directed by Sidney Lumet.

 Gold grew up in Miami, Florida and studied theatre at the New World School of the Arts and at Emerson College.
