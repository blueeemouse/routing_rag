Suzanne Ashworth

Suzanne Ashworth (also Dick) is a fictional character from the British Channel 4 soap opera, "Hollyoaks", played by Suzanne Hall.

 She made her first on-screen appearance on 3 October 2005.

 Suzanne was introduced by series producer David Hanson as part of the Ashworth family.

 In February 2010, Suzanne was axed from the serial by new series producer Paul Marquess.

 Suzanne made her final appearance on 14 July 2010.

 Marquess stated in an interview that he was a big fan of Hall's work in the serial.

 In late 2010 it was revealed that Hall had returned to filming and Suzanne would be pregnant with twins.

 Suzanne's storylines have included trying to keep her family together, fathering a son Rhys Ashworth secretly with another man, other affairs and a feud with Kathy Barnes (Sarah Jane Buckley).

 Suzanne returned to "Hollyoaks" on 14 January 2011 for a period of 2 months.

 In 2012 it was announced that Hall had reprised the role once again.
