Radoslav Pavlović

Radoslav (Lale) Pavlović (Serbian: Радослав Павловић , born in Aleksandrovac, Serbia, 8 September 1954) is a Serbian writer.

 Pavlovic authored numerous theatre plays and film/TV scripts popular with the audience and acclaimed by the critics.

 He is best known for his theatre plays Šovinistička farsa, performed more than a thousand times across ex-Yugoslavia, "Mala", and "Moja Draga" performed for hundreds of times in Belgrade theaters, as well as movies Balkan Rules (1997), Living like the rest of us (1983), Hajde da se volimo 3 (1990) featuring ex-Yugoslavia mega-star Lepa Brena, and TV series Moj Rodjak sa Sela (2008) scoring record viewership of over 3 million viewers per episode.
