Beatrice Fairfax

Beatrice Fairfax is an American silent film serial directed and produced by Leopold Wharton and Theodore Wharton.

 First released on August 7, 1916, the series consists of 15 weekly episodes and features the character of "Beatrice Fairfax" (Grace Darling).

 The character was inspired by the popular newspaper advice column "Ask Beatrice Fairfax", which had been the world's first column of its kind when launched in 1898.
