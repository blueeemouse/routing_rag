Maritz, LLC

Maritz is a sales and marketing services company that designs and operates employee recognition and reward programs, sales channel incentive programs, (including incentive travel rewards) and customer loyalty programs.

 It also plans corporate trade shows, meetings and events, and offers a customer experience technology platform.

 Subsidiaries and segments include Maritz Motivation Solutions, MaritzCX, the Maritz Institute, Maritz Travel and Maritz Research.
