The Book of Masters

The Book of Masters (Russian: Книга Мастеров , "Kniga Masterov" ) is a Russian language fantasy film produced by the CIS division of the Disney company and directed by Vadim Sokolovsky.

 It was released in Russia on October 29, 2009.

 The story is based on Russian fairy tales, such as "The Stone Flower" and other stories from "The Malachite Casket" collection.

 It is Disney's first film made in Russia.
