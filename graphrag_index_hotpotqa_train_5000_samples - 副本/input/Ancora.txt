Ancora

Ancora is the second studio album released by classical crossover vocal group Il Divo.

 The album was released in the United Kingdom and other parts of Europe on 7 November 2005, excluding the United States and Latin America.

 The album was later released in the United States and Latin America on 24 January 2006.

 It debuted at number one on the "Billboard" 200 on the week of releases.

 The album contains one song partially in Latin, one song performed in Italian, two songs performed in French, two songs performed in English, and six songs performed in Spanish, excluding the bonus track.

 The album features the single "I Believe in You", performed with Céline Dion, that is also featured on her international album, "On Ne Change Pas".
