Jim Dombrowski

James Matthew Dombrowski (born October 19, 1963) is a former American college and professional football player who was a guard and offensive tackle in the National Football League (NFL) for eleven seasons.

 Dombrowski played college football for the University of Virginia, and earned All-American honors.

 A first-round pick in the 1986 NFL Draft, he played his entire eleven-year pro career for the NFL's New Orleans Saints.

 He was elected to the College Football Hall of Fame in 2008 and the Virginia Sports Hall of Fame in 2010.
