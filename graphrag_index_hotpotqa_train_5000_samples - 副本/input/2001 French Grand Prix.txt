2001 French Grand Prix

The 2001 French Grand Prix (formally the Mobil 1 Grand Prix de France) was a Formula One motor race held at the Circuit de Nevers Magny-Cours, Magny-Cours, Burgundy, France on 1 July 2001.

 It was the tenth race of the 2001 Formula One season and the 87th French Grand Prix.

 The 72-lap race was won by Michael Schumacher driving a Ferrari car after starting from second place.

 Ralf Schumacher finished second for the Williams team with Rubens Barrichello third in the other Ferrari.
