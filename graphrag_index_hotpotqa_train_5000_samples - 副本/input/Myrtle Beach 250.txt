Myrtle Beach 250

The Myrtle Beach 250 was a NASCAR Busch Series stock car race held at Myrtle Beach Speedway, in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina.

 Added to the Busch Series schedule in 1988, Myrtle Beach Speedway hosted one race per year through the 2000 season, after which it was removed from the schedule.

 The first three races held were 200 laps, covering 107.6 mi .

 The distance was extended to 250 laps starting in 1991, where it remained for the rest of the race's history.

 Jimmy Spencer and Jeff Green were the only drivers to win twice in this race.

 Spencer won at both the 200 and 250 laps race lengths, while Green won the final two races ever held at Myrtle Beach.
