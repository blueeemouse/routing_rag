John Schneider (screen actor)

John Richard Schneider (born April 8, 1960) is an American actor and country music singer.

 He is best known for his portrayal of Beauregard "Bo" Duke in the American television action/comedy series "The Dukes of Hazzard", (opposite Tom Wopat and James Best), Jonathan Kent in the 2001–11 TV series "Smallville" (an adaptation of "Superman"), and James "Jim" Cryer on the television series "The Haves and the Have Nots", created by Tyler Perry.
