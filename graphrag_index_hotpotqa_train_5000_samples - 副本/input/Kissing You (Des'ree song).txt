Kissing You (Des'ree song)

"Kissing You" (or "I'm Kissing You") is a song by British singer Des'ree.

 It was written by the singer with Timothy Atack for Baz Luhrmann's 1996 film "Romeo + Juliet".

 The song was included on the film's soundtrack album and Des'ree's third studio album, "Supernatural" (1998).

 A pop ballad set in the key of A minor, the record uses a simple instrumentation consisting only of piano and string instruments. "Kissing You" featured in "Romeo + Juliet" when the title characters meet at a ball.

 The song was well received by critics for its emotional melody and toned-down production.

 First released as a single in Australia on 24 February 1997, it appeared on the ARIA Singles Chart and the UK Singles Chart.

 A music video accompanied the single, which included scenes from "Romeo + Juliet".
