The Voice Kids (UK TV series)

The Voice Kids is a British television talent show created by John de Mol and its first premiere was on 10 June 2017 on ITV.

 Presented by Emma Willis, the programme is a junior version of "The Voice UK" and is part of "The Voice Kids"' worldwide franchise.

 The coaches are will.i.am, Danny Jones and Pixie Lott .

 Repeats also air on CITV and ITV2.
