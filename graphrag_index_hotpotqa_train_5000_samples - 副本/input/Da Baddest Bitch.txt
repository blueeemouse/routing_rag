Da Baddest Bitch

Da Baddest Bitch is the debut album by American rapper Trina.

 It was released on March 21, 2000 through the label Atlantic/Slip-N-Slide Records.

 The album debuted at number thirty-three on the US The Billboard 200 and number eleven on the Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums chart, and entered the "Top R&B/Hip-Hop Catalog Albums" in 2002.
