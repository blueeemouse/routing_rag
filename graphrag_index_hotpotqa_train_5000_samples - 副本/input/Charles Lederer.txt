Charles Lederer

Charles Lederer (December 31, 1906 – March 5, 1976) was an American screenwriter and film director.

 He was born into a prominent theatrical family in New York, and after his parents divorced, was raised in California by his aunt, Marion Davies, mistress to newspaper publisher William Randolf Hearst.

 A child prodigy, he entered college at age 13, but dropped out after a few years to work as a journalist with Hearst's newspapers.
