Victor Amuso

Vittorio "Little Vic" Amuso (born 1934 Canarsie, Brooklyn) is a New York mobster and boss of the Lucchese crime family.

 Amuso was described as a "Deadly Don", by Assistant United States Attorney Charles Rose.

 He is currently serving a life sentence at the Federal Correctional Institution, Cumberland, in Maryland, on murder and racketeering charges.
