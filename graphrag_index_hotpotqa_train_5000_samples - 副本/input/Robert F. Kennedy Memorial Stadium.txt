Robert F. Kennedy Memorial Stadium

Robert F. Kennedy Memorial Stadium, commonly known as RFK Stadium, is a multi-purpose stadium in Washington, D.C., located about two miles (3 km) due east of the U.S. Capitol building.

 It is the current home of D.C. United of Major League Soccer and the AT&T Nation's Football Classic.

 The U.S. men's national soccer team has played 22 matches there from 1977 to 2013, more than any other stadium.
