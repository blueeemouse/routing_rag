The General's Daughter (novel)

The General's Daughter is a 1992 novel by American author Nelson DeMille.

 The novel introduces protagonist Paul Brenner, who is also featured in DeMille's novels "Up Country" and "The Panther".

 "The General's Daughter" was made into a 1999 film of the same name, starring John Travolta and Madeleine Stowe.

 In the movie, Captain Ann Campbell's first name was changed to Elisabeth.
