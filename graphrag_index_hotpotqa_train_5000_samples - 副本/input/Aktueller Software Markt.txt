Aktueller Software Markt

Aktueller Software Markt (literally "Current Software Market"), commonly known by its acronym, ASM, was a German multi-platform video game magazine that was published by Tronic Verlag from 1986 until 1995.

 It was one of the first magazines published in Germany focused on video games, though the very first issues of "ASM" covered the software market in general for almost all platforms at this time, hence the magazine's full name.

 According to the magazine itself, it was the first computer software journal in Germany.

 However, it soon evolved into a video game magazine.
