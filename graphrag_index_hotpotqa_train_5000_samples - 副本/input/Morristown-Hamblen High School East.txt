Morristown-Hamblen High School East

Morristown-Hamblen High School East (also known as Morristown East High School and formerly known as Morristown High School) is a secondary school located in Morristown, Tennessee.

 MHHSE is the oldest accredited high school in the state of Tennessee.

 The school incorporates grades 9-12.

 The school mascot is "Storm" the Ibis, students and faculty are known as the Hurricanes and the school colors are orange and black.

 The school had an enrollment of nearly 1,600 students as of the 2013-2014 school year.

 Gary Johnson is the head principal.
