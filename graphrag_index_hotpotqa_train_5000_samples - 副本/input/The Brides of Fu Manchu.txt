The Brides of Fu Manchu

The Brides of Fu Manchu is a 1966 British/German Constantin Film co-production adventure crime film based on the fictional Asian villain Fu Manchu, created by Sax Rohmer.

 It was the second film in a series, and was preceded by "The Face of Fu Manchu". "

The Vengeance of Fu Manchu" followed in 1967, "The Blood of Fu Manchu" in 1968, and "The Castle of Fu Manchu" in 1969.

 It was produced by Harry Alan Towers for Hallam Productions.

 Like the first film, it was directed by Don Sharp, and starred Christopher Lee as Fu Manchu.

 Nigel Green was replaced by Douglas Wilmer as Scotland Yard detective Nayland Smith.
