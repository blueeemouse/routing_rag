Johann Durand

Johann Durand (born 17 June 1981, in Évian-les-Bains) is a French football player who plays as a goalkeeper for French club Evian in Ligue 2.

 He began his career with Swiss club Servette and joined Evian in 2000 when the club was known as "FC Gaillard".

 Durand is the club's all-time leader in appearances having made over 200 since Gaillard merged with "FC Ville-la-Grand" in 2003 to form the current club.
