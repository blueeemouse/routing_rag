The All Star Talent Show

The All Star Talent Show was a 2006 UK television programme that was broadcast on Five.

 It was presented by Andi Peters and Myleene Klass, with Julian Clary making up the judging panel alongside two guest judges.

 Each show had six celebrities performing, with the winner of each episode going into the final at the end of the series.

 In addition, the runner up with the most votes at the end of the series also performed again in the final.
