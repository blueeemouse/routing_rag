Crash Here Tonight

"Crash Here Tonight" is a song written and recorded by American country music artist Toby Keith.

 It was released in August 2006 as the third and final single from Keith's album "White Trash with Money".

 A shorter all-acoustic version of the song was featured on the soundtrack for "Broken Bridges" and was played by Keith in the movie.

 It peaked at number 15 on the United States Country Charts.
