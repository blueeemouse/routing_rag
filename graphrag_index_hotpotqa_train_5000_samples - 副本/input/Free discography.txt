Free discography

The discography of Free, an English blues rock band, consists of six studio albums, two live albums, 18 compilation albums, one extended play, 16 singles and two video albums. Formed in London in 1968 by vocalist Paul Rodgers, guitarist Paul Kossoff, bassist Andy Fraser and drummer Simon Kirke, the group signed with Island Records and released their debut album "Tons of Sobs" in 1969.

 The album entered the US "Billboard" 200 chart at number 197.

 Free's self-titled second album failed to chart, before the 1970 follow-up "Fire and Water" peaked at number 2 on the UK Albums Chart, supported by the popular single "All Right Now" which reached the same position on the UK Singles Chart.

 The single also reached the top ten in a number of other regions, including the United States where it peaked at number 4 on the "Billboard" Hot 100.
