WWE Music Group

WWE Music Publishing, Inc.; operating publicly as WWE Music Group, is an American record label funded and operated by WWE.

 It was manufactured and co-marketed by Columbia Records and was distributed by Sony Music Entertainment until 2013 when the distribution moved to Warner Music Group.

 The label specializes in compilation albums of the WWE wrestlers' entrance themes, often by contributing performing artists, but also releases titles that have been actually performed by the wrestlers themselves, including the various-artists album "WWE Originals" and John Cena's "You Can't See Me".
