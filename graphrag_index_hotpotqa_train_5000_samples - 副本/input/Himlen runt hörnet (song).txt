Himlen runt hörnet (song)

"Himlen runt hörnet" is a song first released as a single by Lisa Nilsson in 1992.

 In 1995 it was released in English with the title, "Ticket to Heaven".

 The song was given a Grammis award for "Song of the year 1992" and won the Rockbjörnen award in the "Swedish song of the year 1995" category
