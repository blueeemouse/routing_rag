GovX

GovX is an American internet retailer for current and former members of the US Armed Forces, law enforcement, firefighting, emergency medical services, and related government agencies.

 The company operates GovX.com, where qualified members with service-related backgrounds can shop for tactical gear, outdoor equipment and apparel, athletic attire, and more.

 Membership also includes access to a variety of ticketing deals, such as major league sports, concerts, endurance races, and theme parks.
