Mexican Hairless Dog

The Xoloitzcuintli ( ; Nahuatl pronunciation: ), or Xolo for short, is a hairless breed of dog, found in toy, miniature, and standard sizes.

 The Xolo also comes in a coated variety and coated and hairless can be born in the same litter.

 It is also known as Mexican hairless dog in English-speaking countries, and is one of several breeds of hairless dog.
