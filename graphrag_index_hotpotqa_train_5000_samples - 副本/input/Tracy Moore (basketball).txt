Tracy Moore (basketball)

Tracy Lamont Moore (born December 28, 1965, in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma) is a retired American professional basketball player who played in the NBA.

 A 6'5" (1.96 m), 200 lb (91 kg) shooting guard, Moore played collegiately at the University of Tulsa.

 He was not drafted, but played with the Dallas Mavericks, Detroit Pistons, and the Houston Rockets from 1992 to 1997.
