Michael Douglas on stage and screen

American actor and producer Michael Douglas began his film career with a brief uncredited role in "Cast a Giant Shadow" (1966).

 In the same year he played a small role in the play "Bedford Forrest".

 His performance in "Hail, Hero!

" (1969) earned him a nomination for the Golden Globe Award for Most Promising Male Newcomer.

 He won the 1971 Theatre World Award for "Pinkville".

 During 1972–76, he played the lead role in the TV series "The Streets of San Francisco".

 In 1975, Douglas produced "One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest" which won the Academy Award for Best Picture, Golden Globe for Best Picture and BAFTA Award for Best Film.
