The Everly Brothers (album)

The Everly Brothers is the 1958 eponymous debut album of close harmony rock and roll duo The Everly Brothers.

 The album peaked at number 16 on "Billboard's" "pop albums" chart and launched three very successful singles.

 Originally on the Cadence label, (CLP-3003), the album was re-released on LP in 1988 by EMI and on CD in 2000 by Emporio Records.

 It was re-released again in 2009 on 180-gram vinyl by Doxy music.
