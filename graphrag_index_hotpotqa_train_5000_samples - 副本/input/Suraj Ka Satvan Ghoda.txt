Suraj Ka Satvan Ghoda

Suraj Ka Satvan Ghoda (Translation: Seventh Horse of the Sun) is a 1992 Indian Hindi film directed by Shyam Benegal and based on a novel of the same name by Dharmavir Bharati.

 It won the 1993 National Film Award for Best Feature Film in Hindi.

 The self-reflexive film is also known for its subversive take on the 'Devdas' syndrome.

 It later inspired Tamil film autograph.
