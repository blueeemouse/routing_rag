Hinduja Bank (Switzerland)

Hinduja Bank (Switzerland) Ltd was founded as a finance company in 1978 and became a Swiss regulated bank in 1994.

 Hinduja Bank has its headquarters in Geneva and has a developed network in Switzerland including offices in Zurich, Lugano, St Margrethen, and Basle.

 Additionally, it has a global presence in London, Dubai, Paris, New York City, Mauritius, and Chennai.
