Be My Lover (Inna song)

"Be My Lover" is a song recorded by Romanian singer Inna for the deluxe edition of her third studio album, "Party Never Ends" (2013).

 It was released on 26 July 2013 through Roton.

 The track was written by Uli Brenner, Lane McCray, Gerd Amir Saraf and Melanie Thornton, with production handled by Afrojack and METI.

 Musically, "Be My Lover" encompasses dubstep, electro dance, club and house genres, sampling the 1995 song of the same name by La Bouche, for which Brenner, McCray, Saraf and Thornton received writing credits.
