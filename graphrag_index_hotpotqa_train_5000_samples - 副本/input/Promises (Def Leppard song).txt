Promises (Def Leppard song)

"Promises" is a 1999 song by British hard rock band Def Leppard from their album "Euphoria".

 It was released as a single later that year and reached No. 1 on the "Billboard" Hot Mainstream Rock Tracks chart and No. 38 on the Top 40 Mainstream.

 The song was also featured on the second discs of their compilations "Best of Def Leppard" and "", released in 2004 and 2005.

 A live version is featured on their live album "Viva!

 Hysteria", released in October 2013.
