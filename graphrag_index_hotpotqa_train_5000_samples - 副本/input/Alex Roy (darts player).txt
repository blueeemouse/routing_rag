Alex Roy (darts player)

Alex Roy (born 4 September 1974 in Watford, Hertfordshire) is a darts player who competes in Professional Darts Corporation events.

 He uses the nickname Ace of Herts for this matches and is with Dave Holland Management.

 Roy's entrance music is "Burn in My Light" by Mercy Drive, which was the former theme of professional wrestler Randy Orton.

 Roy even does Orton's trademark pose to the song once he steps onto the oche.
