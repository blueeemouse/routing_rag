Kimberley Town F.C.

Kimberley Town Football Club was a football club based in Kimberley, Nottinghamshire, England.

 They joined the Midland League in 1971.

 They reached the Second Round of the FA Vase three times.

 In the summer of 2012 the club resigned from the Central Midlands League South Division and folded.

 Their last manager was ex-pro Paul Rawden.
