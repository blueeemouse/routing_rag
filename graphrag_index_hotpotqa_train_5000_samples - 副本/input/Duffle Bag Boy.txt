Duffle Bag Boy

"Duffle Bag Boy" is a song by American hip hop duo Playaz Circle, released as the debut lead single from their debut album, "Supply & Demand" (2007).

 The song features a guest appearance from fellow American rapper Lil Wayne and was produced by M16 and Liam Kantwill.

 The song peaked in the Top 40 of the U.S. "Billboard" Hot 100, reaching number 15.
