Bowling Green Falcons football

The Bowling Green Falcons football team is the intercollegiate football team of Bowling Green State University.

 The team is a member of the NCAA, playing at the Division I Football Bowl Subdivision, formerly Division I-A, level; BGSU football competes within the Mid-American Conference in the East Division.

 The Falcons have played their home games in Doyt Perry Stadium since 1966.

 The stadium currently holds 24,000 spectators.

 In their 93-year history, the Falcons have won 12 MAC championships and a College Division national championship – as voted by the UPI in 1959.

 The current head coach is Mike Jinks.
