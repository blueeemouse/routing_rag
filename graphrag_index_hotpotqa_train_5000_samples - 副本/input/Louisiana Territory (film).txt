Louisiana Territory (film)

Louisiana Territory is a 1953 American historical drama film directed by Harry W. Smith (who also photographed the film), from an original screenplay by Jerome Brondfield.

 Produced by RKO-Pathé, it was distributed by its sister company, RKO Radio Pictures, who premiered the film in New Orleans on October 14, 1953, with a national release two days later, on October 16.

 The film stars Val Winter, Leo Zinser, Julian Miester, and Phyliss Massicot.
