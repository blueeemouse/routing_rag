UFC 55

UFC 55: Fury was a mixed martial arts event held by the Ultimate Fighting Championship on October 7, 2005 at the Mohegan Sun Arena in Uncasville, Connecticut.

 The event was broadcast live on pay-per-view in the United States, and later released on DVD.

 This would be the last UFC to air live on a Friday until UFC 141, which aired on December 30, 2011.
