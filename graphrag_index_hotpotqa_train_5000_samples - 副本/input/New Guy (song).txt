New Guy (song)

"New Guy" is a song recorded by Ghanaian rapper Sarkodie featuring American hip pop artist Ace Hood.

 Although intended to be included on his first live album, "Mary" (2015), the inclusion was ultimately scrapped.

 It was released for digital consumption on 10 June 2015 through Sarkcess Music and Ivy League Records.

 Written by the two artists and produced by CedSolo, "New Guy" is an African hip hop and hip hop song that contains alternating rap verses, while its lyrics deal with Sarkodie's desire and motivation for success.
