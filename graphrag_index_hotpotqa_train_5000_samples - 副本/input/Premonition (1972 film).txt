Premonition (1972 film)

Premonition (also known as Head or The Impure) is a 1972 film written and directed by Alan Rudolph.

 The film was Rudolph's first after being an assistant director of 11 episodes of the 1960s sitcom "The Brady Bunch", as well as the films "The Big Bounce" (1969) and "Riot" (1969).

 Originally titled "Head", the distributors first changed the title to "Premonition" and later for a time to "Impure".
