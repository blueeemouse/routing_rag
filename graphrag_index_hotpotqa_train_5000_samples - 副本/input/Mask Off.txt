Mask Off

"Mask Off" is a song by American rapper Future for his eponymous fifth studio album "Future" (2017).

 The track was later released as the second single after initially charting as an album track and receiving more popularity than the lead single "Draco".

 The song samples "Prison Song" by Tommy Butler.

 It is the highest-charting song on the album and also Future's best-charting single to date.
