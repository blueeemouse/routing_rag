Alice's Birthday

Alice's Birthday (Russian: Де́нь рожде́ния Али́сы , translit.

 Den' rozhdeniya Alisy), is a 2009 Russian traditionally animated children's science fiction film, directed by Sergey Seryogin and produced by Master-film studio.

 The film is based on a novella of the same name by Kir Bulychov about Alisa (Alice) Selezneva, a teenage girl from the future.

 It is a spiritual successor to 1981 animated film "The Mystery of the Third Planet", from which it draws a heavy influence.
