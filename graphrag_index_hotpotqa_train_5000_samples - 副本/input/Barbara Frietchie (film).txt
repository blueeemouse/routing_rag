Barbara Frietchie (film)

Barbara Frietchie is a 1924 silent film about an old woman who helps out soldiers during the American Civil War.

 It is based on the play that had starred Julia Marlowe at the turn of the century which in turn was taken from the real-life story of Barbara Fritchie.

 There were two silent film versions, a 1915 version and 1924 version.

 The 1915 version, directed by Herbert Blaché, starred Mary Miles Minter and Anna Q. Nilsson.

 The 1924 version, directed by Lambert Hillyer, starred Florence Vidor and Edmund Lowe.
