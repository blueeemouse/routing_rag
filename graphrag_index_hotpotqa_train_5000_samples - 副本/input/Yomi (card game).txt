Yomi (card game)

Yomi: Fighting Card Game (often shortened to Yomi) is a designer card game created by David Sirlin, inspired by "Super Street Fighter II Turbo".

 “Yomi” is Japanese for “reading,” as in reading the mind of the opponent.

 The Yomi card game is designed to distill the high-level mind games from fighting game tournaments into a simple card game that, itself, stands up to serious tournament play.

 It features asymmetric gameplay, and makes use of cards with multiple options.
