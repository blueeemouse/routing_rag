Wan Chai Sports Ground

Wan Chai Sports Ground () is a stadium in Wan Chai North, Hong Kong Island, Hong Kong, with a seating capacity of 2,401.

 It is a government-run sports ground primarily used by local schools for sports days and other athletic activities.

 Situated by the Victoria Harbour, it is at a convenient location next to Hong Kong's Central business district, Wan Chai, and the shopping district of Causeway Bay.
