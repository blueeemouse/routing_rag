Oh-OK

Oh-OK was an American musical group from Athens, Georgia formed in 1981 with singer/lyricist Linda Hopper, bassist/vocalist/lyricist Lynda Stipe, and drummer David Pierce.

 Other members later included drummer David McNair and guitarist Matthew Sweet.

 The trio began practicing together at parties in the college community in the spring of 1981.

 Their first club performance break came when Stipe's brother, Michael Stipe of R.E.M., needed a backing band for a show at the 40 Watt Club.
