Snow White: A Tale of Terror

Snow White: A Tale of Terror is a 1997 American dark fantasy horror television film based on the fairy tale "Snow White".

 Also known as Snow White in the Black Forest it was directed by Michael Cohn and stars Sigourney Weaver, Sam Neill and Monica Keena.

 The original music score was composed by John Ottman.

 The film was marketed with the tagline "The fairy tale is over".
