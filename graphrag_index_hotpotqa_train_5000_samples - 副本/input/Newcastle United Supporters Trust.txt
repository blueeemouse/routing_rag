Newcastle United Supporters Trust

The Newcastle United Supporters Trust, or the NUST, is a supporters' trust consisting of fans of Newcastle United (NUFC), an English football club.

 The prime role of the group is to "represent the interests of and provide a voice for the fans of Newcastle United".

 The group was set up as an initiative by the fanzines "True Faith" and "The Mag", and the fan website NUFC.com, aiming to be independent of but maintain a working relationship with the owners of the football club.

 A later stated aim was to achieve stakeholder representation on the board of the football club
