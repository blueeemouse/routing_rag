Wild Chives and Soy Bean Soup: 12 Years Reunion

Wild Chives and Soy Bean Soup: 12 Years Reunion () is a 2014 South Korean television series starring Lee So-yeon, Namkoong Min, Lee Tae-im, Yoon So-hee, Lee Won-keun and Ryu Hyoyoung.

 It aired on jTBC from March 22 to June 29, 2014 on Saturdays and Sundays at 20:45 for 26 episodes.
