Deadline: Sirf 24 Ghante

Deadline: Sirf 24 Ghante is a Bollywood film released in 2006 featuring Konkona Sen Sharma, Irfan Khan and Rajit Kapur in the lead roles.

 The film is a Bollywood adaptation of the Charlize Theron-Kevin Bacon film "Trapped", which was based on the Greg Iles novel "24 Hours".
