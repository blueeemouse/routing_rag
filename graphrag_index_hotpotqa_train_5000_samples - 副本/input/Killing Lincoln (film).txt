Killing Lincoln (film)

Killing Lincoln is an American television film inspired by the 2011 book of the same name by Bill O'Reilly and Martin Dugard.

 The two-hour political docudrama was originally broadcast on National Geographic Channel on February 17, 2013.

 Narrated and hosted by American actor Tom Hanks, the film stars Billy Campbell as United States President Abraham Lincoln and Jesse Johnson as John Wilkes Booth.

 It was written and executive produced by Erik Jendresen ("Band of Brothers"), directed by Adrian Moat ("Gettysburg"), produced by Chris Cowen, Mark Herzog, Ridley Scott, Tony Scott, Mary Lisio, David Zucker, and Terri Weinberg.
