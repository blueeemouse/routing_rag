Laurene Landon

Laurene Landon (born March 17, 1957 as Laurene Landon Coughlin) is an American film and television actress.

 Laurene first began appearing in movies in the 1980s.

 She is best known for her roles in "Maniac Cop" and "Maniac Cop 2", "Hundra", "", and "...All the Marbles" with Peter Falk.

 She is half Irish and half Polish and describes herself as being "bi-Polish".
