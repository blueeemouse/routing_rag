Mark McGuckin

Mark McGuckin is the co-host, writer, creator, and creative producer of the Canadian television series "Road Hockey Rumble".

 In British Columbia, the McGuckin name has long been synonymous with athletic ingenuity and cataclysmic road hockey play.

 As a young boy, Mark grew up idolizing the legacy of his forefathers and their long tradition of psychological warfare on the ‘asphalt jungle’ – affectionately known as the road hockey court.

 In his teens, Mark put his admired tradition into practice and got beaten badly … several times over.

 Now, having grown six inches taller and stacking on 40 plus pounds of raw twisted Canadian steel, Mark feels it’s payback time!

 He is also a Taurus and very, very bald.
