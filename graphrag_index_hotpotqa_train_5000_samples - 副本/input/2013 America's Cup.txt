2013 America's Cup

The 34th America's Cup was a series of boat races held on San Francisco Bay between the defender Oracle Team USA representing the Golden Gate Yacht Club, and the challenger Emirates Team New Zealand representing the Royal New Zealand Yacht Squadron.

 Oracle Team USA defended the America's Cup by a score of 9 to 8.

 Oracle had to win the last eight races to come from behind and once again win the oldest trophy in international sport.

 Team New Zealand won the right to challenge for the cup by winning the 2013 Louis Vuitton Cup.

 The 34th America's Cup was the longest ever cup by both number of days and races, and the first since the 25th America's Cup to feature a winner-take-all final race.
