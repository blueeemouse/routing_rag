Bergnart Carl Lewy

Bergnart (Bernhard) Carl Lewy was a Danish chemist; born in Copenhagen on 5 July 1817.

 He died there on 1 January 1863.

 He obtained the degree of graduate of pharmacy in 1835, and then studied chemistry for three years at the polytechnic school.

 In 1839 he studied in Berlin (Ph.D.), and spent the winter of 1839-40 in Rome.

 He then obtained a position as assistant in the private laboratory of J. B. Dumas in Paris.
