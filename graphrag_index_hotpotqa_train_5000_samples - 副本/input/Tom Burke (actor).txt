Tom Burke (actor)

Tom Liam Benedict Burke (born 30 June 1981) is an English actor.

 He is best known for his role as Athos in the BBC series "The Musketeers" (2014–2016), as Dolokhov in the BBC literary-adaptation miniseries "War & Peace" and most recently for his role as the titular character Cormoran Strike in the BBC series "Strike" (2017).
