Besieged (band)

Besieged is a Christian metal band from Bay City, Michigan.

 The band was formed by Drummer Josh Schroeder, the only member in every line-up, who later became a producer and has recorded albums for Those Who Fear, A Plea for Purging, and The Burial.

 Former Vocalist Mattie Montgomery left to join the band For Today in 2007.

 The band was supposed to do a Super Mario Bros. 3 cover for the game, but it is uncertain if that happened or not.

 The band played on the Blood and Ink Fest, along with, xLooking Forwardx, Burden of a Day, and Saints Never Surrender.
