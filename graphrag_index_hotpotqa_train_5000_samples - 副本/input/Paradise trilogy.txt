Paradise trilogy

Paradise (German: Paradies ) is the collective name of three films directed by Ulrich Seidl: "" (2012), "" (2012) and "" (2013).

 They focus on three women from one family; one of them travels to Kenya as a sex tourist, one has to spend time at a weight loss camp, and one tries to propagate Catholicism.

 The project is an Austrian majority production with co-producers in Germany and France.

 It was conceived as one feature film, but after a long gestation became three entries forming a trilogy.

 The first installment, "", competed for the Palme d'Or at the 2012 Cannes Film Festival.

 Seidl originally planned to premiere all three films at the same event, but after the Cannes selection decided to roll out parts two and three, "" and "", at other major film festivals.

 The individual films are named after the three theological virtues, and focus on how the protagonists conceive their view of paradise.
