Kris Letang

Kristopher Allen "Kris" Letang (born April 24, 1987) is a Canadian professional ice hockey defenceman and alternate captain for the Pittsburgh Penguins of the National Hockey League (NHL).

 He played juniors in the Quebec Major Junior Hockey League (QMJHL) for three seasons, during which time he was selected 62nd overall by the Penguins in the 2005 NHL Entry Draft.

 In his second full NHL season, Letang won the Stanley Cup with Pittsburgh, became a two time Stanley Cup Champion when the Penguins beat the Sharks in 2016, and became a three time Stanley Cup Champion when the Penguins beat the Predators in 2017.

 Internationally, he has competed for Canada at the under-18 and under-20 levels, winning back-to-back gold medals at the World Junior Championships in 2006 and 2007.
