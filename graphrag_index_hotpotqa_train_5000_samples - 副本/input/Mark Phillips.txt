Mark Phillips

Captain Mark Anthony Peter Phillips {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (born 22 September 1948) is an English Olympic gold-medal-winning horseman for Great Britain and former husband of Anne, Princess Royal, with whom he has two children.

 He remains a leading figure in British equestrian circles, a noted eventing course designer, and a columnist in "Horse & Hound" magazine.
