Langston Wilde

Langston Wilde is a fictional character from ABC soap opera "One Life to Live" portrayed by Brittany Underwood from May 17, 2006, to April 12, 2011.

 Langston is Dorian Lord's adoptive daughter and very similar to her.

 In January 2011, it was announced that Underwood would be exiting the show in the spring in a "storyline dictated" exit.

 She was slated to appear in the "Vicker Man" premiere episodes alongside Markko in July 2011 but could not due to scheduling conflicts.

 She made a return appearance during the final ABC episode of the show on January 13, 2012.
