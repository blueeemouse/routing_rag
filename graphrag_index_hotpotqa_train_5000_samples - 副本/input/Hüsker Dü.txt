Hüsker Dü

Hüsker Dü was an American rock band formed in Saint Paul, Minnesota, in 1979.

 The band's continual members were guitarist/vocalist Bob Mould, bassist Greg Norton, and drummer/vocalist Grant Hart.

 Hüsker Dü first gained notability as a hardcore punk band, later crossing over into alternative rock.

 Mould and Hart were the principal songwriters for Hüsker Dü, with Hart's higher-pitched vocals and Mould's baritone taking the lead in alternating songs.
