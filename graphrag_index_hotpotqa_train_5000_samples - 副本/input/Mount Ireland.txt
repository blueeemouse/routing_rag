Mount Ireland

Mount Ireland is a mountain in Grant County of the U.S. state of Oregon.

 It is about 5 mi east-northeast of Granite, Oregon.

 It is the second highest peak in the Blue Mountains at 8,325 ft after Strawberry Mountain, and Oregon's 71st highest peak.

 For a period of several years, this mountain peak was referred to as Bald Mountain but in 1975, it was changed to Ireland Mountain before formally becoming known as Mount Ireland.

 The name change was brought about by a cooperative effort of local citizens and the US Forest Service to honor Henry Ireland, a forest supervisor for many years, who had died in 1916.
