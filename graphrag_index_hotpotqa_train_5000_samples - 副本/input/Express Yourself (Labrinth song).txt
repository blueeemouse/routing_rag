Express Yourself (Labrinth song)

"Express Yourself" is a song by English musician Labrinth.

 Written by Labrinth and Charles Wright, it was released as the fourth single from the his debut studio album, "Electronic Earth" on 7 May 2012.

 It interpolates "Express Yourself" by Charles Wright & the Watts 103rd Street Rhythm Band, which was the same song sampled by N.W.A. in their 1988 song of the same name, and the famous drum break from James Brown's "Funky Drummer".
