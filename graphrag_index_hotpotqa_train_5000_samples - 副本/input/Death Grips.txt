Death Grips

Death Grips is an American experimental hip hop band from Sacramento, California, formed in 2010.

 The group consists of rapper/vocalist MC Ride (Stefan Burnett), drummer/producer/songwriter Zach Hill, and recording engineer Andy Morin.

 The band has featured guitarist Nick Reinhart of Tera Melos on their albums Fashion Week, Jenny Death and Bottomless Pit.

 Their musical style draws on hip hop, punk, electronic, noise, and industrial.

 In 2012, "The Skinny" called them "the first truly important band of the twenty-first century".
