I Know (Yo Gotti song)

"I Know" is a song by American hip hop recording artist Yo Gotti.

 The song was released on November 15, 2013, as the fourth single from his fourth studio album, "I Am" (2013).

 "I Know" features a guest appearance from rapper Rich Homie Quan who produced the song along with Trauma Tone.

 The song samples Club Nouveau's "Why You Treat Me So Bad", the same song sampled on Luniz's prolific "I Got 5 On It".

 Following its release, the song peaked at number 31 on the US "Billboard" Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart.
