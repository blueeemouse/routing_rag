Take It Like a Man (Michelle Wright song)

"Take It Like a Man" is a song written by Tony Haselden, and recorded by Canadian country music artist Michelle Wright.

 It was released in February 1992 as the first single from Wright's album "Now and Then".

 The song became Wright's first Number One on the Canadian "RPM" Country Tracks charts that year.

 It was also her only top 10 hit on the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles & Tracks chart in the United States.

 Country singer Lari White is featured on background vocals.
