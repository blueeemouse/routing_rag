Kameshwara

Kameshwara, Bamesvara, or Kameçvara was the second monarch of Kediri kingdom and ruled circa 1117–1130 CE.

 His formal stylized name was "Çri Maharaja Rake Sirikan çri Kameçvara Sakalabhuvanatustikarana Sarvanivaryyaviryya Parakrama Digjayottunggadeva".

 The "Lanchana" (royal seal) of his reign was a skull with a crescent moon called "chandrakapala", the symbol of Shiva.
