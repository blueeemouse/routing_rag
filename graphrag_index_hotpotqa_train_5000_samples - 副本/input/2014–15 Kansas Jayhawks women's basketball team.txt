2014–15 Kansas Jayhawks women's basketball team

The 2014–15 Kansas Jayhawks women's basketball team will represent the University of Kansas in the 2014–15 NCAA Division I women's basketball season.

 This was head coach Bonnie Henrickson's eleventh season at Kansas.

 They play their home games at Allen Fieldhouse in Lawrence, Kansas and were members of the Big 12 Conference.

 They finished the season 15–17, 6–12 in Big 12 play to finish in ninth place.

 They lost in the first round of the Big 12 Women's Tournament to their in-state rival Kansas State.
