Jyrki (TV show)

Jyrki (sometimes stylised as "JYRK:.")

 was a Finnish TV show shown on the MTV3 channel from 1995 to 2001.

 "Jyrki" was an afternoon show about popular music for youths.

 The idea for the show came from the Canadian "MuchMusic" show by the CHUM network, whose material "Jyrki" borrowed and cooperated with to improve the concept.

 "Jyrki" was produced by Funny-Films Oy.
