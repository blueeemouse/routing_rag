"V" Is for Viagra.

 The Remixes is a remix album by Puscifer, a side project of Maynard James Keenan, which was released on April 29, 2008.

 It contains ten remixes of tracks from the first Puscifer album ""V" Is for Vagina", as well as two remixes of the non-album single "Cuntry Boner".
