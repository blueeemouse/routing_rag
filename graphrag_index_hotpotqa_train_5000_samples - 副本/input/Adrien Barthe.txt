Adrien Barthe

Adrien Barthe (born Grat-Norbert Barthe, June 7, 1828 – August 13, 1898) was a French classical composer.

 Born in Bayonne, Barthe studied music under Fernand Le Borne at the Paris Conservatory.

 An early composition, "Francesca de Rimini", was lauded by the Rome Institute, and subsequent cantatas "Judith" and "Don Carlos" were well received.

 Performances of "Aubade" in 1893 by conductor Paul Taffanel were popular with audiences to the extent that Taffanel published praise of Barthe - "your delightful work earned us our first success."

 Moving from classical composition to theatre work, Barthe's "la Fiancée d'Abydos" appeared on stage in Paris and Rome starring Jean-Vital Jammes, however flaws in the composition persuaded Barthe to retire shortly after December 1865, becoming a teacher.

 He died in Asnières, leaving behind several unpublished manuscripts.
