Touch the World

Touch the World is the fourteenth studio album by Earth, Wind & Fire, produced by the band's leader Maurice White for Kalimba Productions, and released in 1987 on Columbia Records.

 "Touch the World" has been certified gold in the US for sales of over 500,000 copies by the RIAA.

 "Touch The World" also marked the debut of former Commodores guitarist Sheldon Reynolds to the lineup.

 The album ended a four-year hiatus for the band, with vocalists Maurice White and Philip Bailey reforming the group, bringing back longtime members Verdine White, Andrew Woolfolk and Ralph Johnson.

 Drummer Sonny Emory filled the drum chair vacated by Fred White.
