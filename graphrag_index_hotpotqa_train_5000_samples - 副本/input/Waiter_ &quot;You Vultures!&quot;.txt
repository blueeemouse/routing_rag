Waiter: "You Vultures!"

 is the debut full length album by the Alaskan experimental rock band Portugal.

 The Man.

 It is lead singer John Gourley's first album released after leaving the band Anatomy of a Ghost.

 It is the band's only album before 2009's "The Satanic Satanist" to use a heavy amount of electronic drums and synthesizers.
