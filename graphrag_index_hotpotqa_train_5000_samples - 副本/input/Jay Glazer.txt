Jay Glazer

Jason Charles "Jay" Glazer (born December 26, 1969) is an American sportswriter and National Football League (NFL) insider for Fox Sports.

 He is also a business partner with mixed martial arts fighter Randy Couture.

 Glazer trains NFL players in mixed martial arts during the off-season Glazer also hosts UFC shows for FOX Sports, including weigh-ins and pre/post coverage.
