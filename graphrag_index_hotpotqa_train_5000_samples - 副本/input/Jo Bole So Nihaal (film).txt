Jo Bole So Nihaal (film)

Jo Bole So Nihaal (also transliterated as "Jo Bole So Nihal"; literally meaning "Whoever utters shall be fulfilled") is a 2005 Bollywood action comedy film, directed by Rahul Rawail.

 It stars Sunny Deol, Shillpi Sharma and Kamaal Khan in lead roles, whilst Nupur Mehta and Surekha Sikri appear in major supporting roles; the director also plays an antagonistic role in the film.

 Singer Kamaal Khan appeared first time on big screen in a negative role.

 This movie was his debut movie as actor.
