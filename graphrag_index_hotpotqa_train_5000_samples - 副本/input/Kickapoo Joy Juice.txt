Kickapoo Joy Juice

Kickapoo Joy Juice is a citrus-flavored soft drink brand owned by the Monarch Beverage Company.

 The name was introduced in "Li'l Abner", a comic strip that ran from 1934 through 1977.

 Although "Li'l Abner"<nowiki>'</nowiki>s Kickapoo Joy Juice was an alcoholic drink, the real world beverage is a lightly carbonated soft drink.
