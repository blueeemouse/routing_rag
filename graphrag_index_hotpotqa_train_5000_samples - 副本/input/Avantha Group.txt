Avantha Group

Avantha Group is an Indian business conglomerate led by Gautam Thapar.

 The company is one of India’s largest business conglomerates.

 Its businesses include power generation and distribution, power transmission and distribution equipment and services, paper and pulp, food processing, farm forestry, chemicals, infrastructure, Information Technology and Information Technology Enabled Service (ITeS), also referred to as Business Process Outsourcing (BPO).
