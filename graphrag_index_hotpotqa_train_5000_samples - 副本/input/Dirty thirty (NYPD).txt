Dirty thirty (NYPD)

The Dirty Thirty scandal took place in the New York Police Department between 1992–1995, and was the largest collection of police officers charged with corruption in almost a decade.

 A group of rogue officers, led by Sergeant Kevin P. Nannery, participated in various unlawful activities, including civil rights conspiracy, perjury, extortion, grand larceny and the possession and distribution of narcotics.

 The scandal led to a number of arrests of police officers and two suicides.
