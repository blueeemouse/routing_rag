Madhav Yeshwant Gadkari

Madhav Yeshwant Gadkari (1928–2006) was an Indian journalist and the editor of Loksatta, a popular Marathi daily published by the Indian Express Group.

 He was also associated with the All India Radio, "Maharashtra Times", "Sakal" and their Goa edition, "Gomantak Times" during his career.

 The Government of India awarded him the fourth highest civilian award of Padma Shri in 1990.
