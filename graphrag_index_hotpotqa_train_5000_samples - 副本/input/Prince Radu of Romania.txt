Prince Radu of Romania

Prince Radu of Romania (born Radu Duda 7 June 1960) is the son-in-law of former King Michael I of Romania.

 He was born in Iași, Romania.

 In 1996, he married Crown Princess Margareta of Romania, the King's eldest daughter.

 On 1 January 1999, he was given the name, not title of "Prince of Hohenzollern-Veringen" by Friedrich Wilhelm, Prince of Hohenzollern, the Head of the Sigmaringen branch of the Hohenzollern family.

 He has also called himself "Radu Hohenzollern Veringen Duda".

 Since 2007, when he had his legal name changed from "Radu Duda" to "Radu al României Duda", Radu no longer uses the name of Hohenzollern.
