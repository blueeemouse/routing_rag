205th Tactical Fighter Squadron (JASDF)

The 205th Tactical Fighter Squadron (第205飛行隊 (dai-ni-byaku-go-hikoutai ) ) was a squadron of the 6th Air Wing of the Japan Air Self-Defense Force.

 It was based at Komatsu Air Base, in Ishikawa Prefecture, Japan.

 When the squadron disbanded it was equipped with Lockheed F-104J/DJ aircraft.

 It was replaced by the Mitsubishi F-15J-equipped 306th Tactical Fighter Squadron.
