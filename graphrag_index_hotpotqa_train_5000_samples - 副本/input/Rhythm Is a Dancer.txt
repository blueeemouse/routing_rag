Rhythm Is a Dancer

"Rhythm Is a Dancer" is a song recorded by German group Snap!

.

 It was released in March 1992 and achieved huge success in many countries around the world, topping the charts in France, Ireland, Italy, Netherlands, Germany and the United Kingdom.

 It also reached the top 5 on the US "Billboard" Hot 100 and number one on the US Hot Dance Music/Club Play Singles.

 The single spent six weeks at the top in the UK where it was the second biggest selling single of 1992, surpassed only by Whitney Houston's version of "I Will Always Love You".
