Shadow Cabinet of Jim McLay

New Zealand political leader Jim McLay assembled a "shadow cabinet" system amongst the National caucus following his election to the position of Leader of the Opposition in 1984.

 He composed this of individuals who acted for the party as spokespeople in assigned roles while he was Leader of the Opposition (1984–86).

 McLay was plagued by interference from previous leader Robert Muldoon, who was denied a place on National's frontbench which he desired, unlike McLay who wished him to retire to the backbenches as an 'elder statesmen'.
