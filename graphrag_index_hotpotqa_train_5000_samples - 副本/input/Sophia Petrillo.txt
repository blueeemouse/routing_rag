Sophia Petrillo

Sophia Petrillo is a fictional character from the TV series "The Golden Girls", and its spin-offs "The Golden Palace" and "Empty Nest" and one episode of the series, "Blossom".

 She was portrayed by Estelle Getty for 10 years and 259 episodes.

 Bea Arthur, who played her daughter Dorothy Zbornak on the show, was in real life a year older than Getty.
