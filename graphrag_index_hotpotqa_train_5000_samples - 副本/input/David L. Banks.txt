David L. Banks

David L. Banks is an American statistician.

 He is a former editor of the "Journal of the American Statistical Association"; a founding editor of "Statistics, Politics and Policy"; and a co-editor of the monograph "Statistical Methods for Human Rights".

 He is a fellow of the Institute of Mathematical Statistics and the Royal Statistical Society.

 He has been a member of the Board of Directors of the American Statistical Association, and he is a past-President of the Classification Society.

 He has taught at the University of Cambridge and at Carnegie Mellon University; he was also Chief Statistician of the U.S. Department of Transportation.

 Additionally, he has served on six National Academies panels.
