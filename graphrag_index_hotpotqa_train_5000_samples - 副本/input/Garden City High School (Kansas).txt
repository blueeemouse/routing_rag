Garden City High School (Kansas)

Garden City High School (known locally as GCHS) is a fully accredited high school, serving students in grades 9–12, located in Garden City, Kansas, USA.

 Garden City High School (GCHS), a part of Garden City Public Schools, is the only high school within the city limits of Garden City, KS.

 The school colors are brown and white, although gold is generally considered a third unofficial color.

 Approximately 2,111 students are enrolled for the 2016-2017 school year.

 The current principal is Steve Nordby.
