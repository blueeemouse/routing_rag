1979 Indiana Hoosiers football team

The 1979 Indiana Hoosiers football team represented the Indiana Hoosiers in the 1979 Big Ten Conference football season.

 The Hoosiers played their home games at Memorial Stadium in Bloomington, Indiana.

 The team was coached by Lee Corso, in his seventh year as head coach of the Hoosiers.

 The Hoosiers participated in the Holiday Bowl, in a post-season matchup against BYU.

 The Hoosiers won 38–37.
