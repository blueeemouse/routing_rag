Kids' Court

Kids' Court is a children's television/nontraditional court show aired by Nickelodeon.

 First airing on September 10, 1988 and ending in 1989, it was hosted by actor Paul Provenza.

 It was created and executive produced by Alan Goodman, Albie Hecht, and Fred Seibert; produced by Chauncey Street Productions, a division of Fred/Alan, Inc., in New York City.
