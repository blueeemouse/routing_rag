I Am Gloria Gaynor

I Am Gloria Gaynor is the eleventh studio album by Gloria Gaynor, released in 1984 by Chrysalis Records.

 The most notable song on the album was Gloria Gaynor's cover of "I Am What I Am" from the musical "La Cage Aux Folles".

 It reached #82 on the Billboard Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs charts.
