Old School Flava

Old School Flava is the second album by the old school rap group, Treacherous Three.

 It was released in 1994 for Wrap Records and was produced by DJ Easy Lee, DJ Clark Kent, T La Rock, Ced Gee, Rahiem, Special K, Kool Moe Dee and LA Sunshine.

 Guests include Big Daddy Kane, Chuck D and Heavy D.
