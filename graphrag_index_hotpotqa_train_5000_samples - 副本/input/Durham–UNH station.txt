Durham–UNH station

Durham–University of New Hampshire station, also known as Durham–UNH station or simply Durham station, is a passenger rail station in Durham, New Hampshire, served by Amtrak's "Downeaster" line.

 The historic depot, which now houses the UNH Dairy Bar, is situated just west of downtown Durham on the campus of the University of New Hampshire (UNH).

 The station is owned by the university, but an adjacent parking area is managed by the town of Durham.

 On average, about 172 rail passengers board or detrain daily at Durham, making it the second-busiest Amtrak stop in New Hampshire.
