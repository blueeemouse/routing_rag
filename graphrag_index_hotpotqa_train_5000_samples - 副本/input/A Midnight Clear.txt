A Midnight Clear

A Midnight Clear is a 1992 American war drama film written and directed by Keith Gordon and starring an ensemble cast that features Ethan Hawke, Gary Sinise, Peter Berg, Kevin Dillon and Arye Gross.

 It is based on the eponymous novel by William Wharton.

 Set towards the end of World War II, the film tells the story of an American intelligence unit which finds a German platoon that wishes to surrender.
