Arūnas Visockas

Arūnas Visockas (born December 7, 1965 in Kaunas) is a retired Lithuanian basketball player for the Lithuanian national basketball team and Žalgiris Kaunas.

 He was a power forward 200 cm tall and weighed 108 kg.

 Visockas is currently the assistant coach of the Lithuanian basketball team BC Kaunas Triobet.

 His former clubs include BC Zalgiris (1985–1996), Atomeromu Paks (1996–2000) and Statyba Jonava (2000–2001).
