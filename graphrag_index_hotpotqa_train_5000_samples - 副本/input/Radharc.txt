Radharc

Radharc was an Irish television documentary series broadcast by RTÉ Television from 1962 until 1996.

 The documentaries were created by a film unit funded by the Catholic Archbishop of Dublin, John Charles McQuaid, in anticipation of the inauguration of television broadcasting in Ireland by RTÉ in December 1961.

 The film unit was staffed exclusively by Catholic priests, including Joe Dunn, Desmond Forristal, and Peter Lemass.

 The Irish language word "radharc" means vision, view, or panorama.
