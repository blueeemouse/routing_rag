Mikhail Porechenkov

Mikhail Evgenevich Porechenkov (Russian: Михаи́л Евге́ньевич Поре́ченков , born 2 March 1969, Leningrad, Soviet Union) is a Russian film actor, producer, director.

 He became famous after his lead role as FSB Agent Alexey Nikolayev in the TV series "National Security Agent" (1999–2005).

 In 2008, Porechenkov produced, directed and starred in "D-Day" (День Д), a Russian remake of the 1985 American action film "Commando".
