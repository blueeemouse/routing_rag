Aladdin (Disney character)

Aladdin is a fictional character and the protagonist of Disney's 1992 animated feature film "Aladdin", and its two direct-to-video sequels "The Return of Jafar" (1994) and "Aladdin and the King of Thieves" (1996).

 He also stars in the animated television series based on the film.

 In all animated productions, Aladdin is voiced by American actor Scott Weinger, while his singing voice is provided by Brad Kane.

 Mena Massoud is set to play a live-action version of the character in a live action adaptation of the 1992 film.
