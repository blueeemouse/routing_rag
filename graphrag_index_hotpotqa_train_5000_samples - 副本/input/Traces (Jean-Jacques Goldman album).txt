Traces (Jean-Jacques Goldman album)

Traces is a 1989 album recorded by French singer-songwriter Jean-Jacques Goldman.

 It was his second live album and his seventh album overall.

 It was recorded during the singer's tour 1988, between March and December, in various countries (France, Congo, Belgium).

 The album was released on 13 March 1989 and spawned two singles which achieved success in France : "Il changeait la vie" (#14), which precedeed the album's release, and "Peur de rien blues" (#17).

 The album was successful on the French chart.
