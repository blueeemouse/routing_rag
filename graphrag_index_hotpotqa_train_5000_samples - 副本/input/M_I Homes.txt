M/I Homes

M/I Homes, Inc. is a United States homebuilding company based in Columbus, Ohio.

 Founded in 1976 by Melvin and Irving Schottenstein as M/I Schottenstein Homes, the company now operates as M/I Homes.

 As of 2013, M/I Homes is the 14th largest builder of single-family homes in the nation.

 M/I Homes' headquarters is located near Easton Town Center in Columbus, Ohio.

 In 1992, M/I Homes launched its advertising campaign "Home is a Two Letter Word...M/I".

 The current slogan for M/I Homes is "Move Up".
