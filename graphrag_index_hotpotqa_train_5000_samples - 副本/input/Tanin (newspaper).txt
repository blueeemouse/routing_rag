Tanin (newspaper)

Tanin ("resonance" in Turkish) was a Turkish newspaper.

 It was founded in 1908, after the Young Turk Revolution, by Tevfik Fikret (1867–1915), the Ottoman poet who is considered the founder of the modern school of Turkish poetry, began publishing the newspaper "Tanin", which became a strong supporter of the new progressive ruling party Committee of Union and Progress (CUP; Turkish: "Ittihat ve Terakki Cemiyeti" ).

 Pluralism and diversity were reflected on the pages of "Tanin".
