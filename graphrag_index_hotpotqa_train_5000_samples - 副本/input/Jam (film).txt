Jam (film)

Jam (or Jam: When Lives Collide) is a 2006 drama film directed by Craig E. Serling.

 Written by Serling and Nicole Lonner, the film was executive produced by Dianne Burnett for Burnett Entertainment in association with Thanksgiving Films.

 As Serling's first feature length project, "Jam" is based upon a short film by the same name that Serling shot in 2004.

 Starring Elizabeth Bogush, Dan Byrd, Julie Claire, and David DeLuise, "Jam" premiered at the Vail Film Festival on April 1, 2006, aired on television on the Starz!

 TV channel, and was released on DVD on July 3, 2007, by the Starz!

 distribution branch of Anchor Bay Entertainment.
