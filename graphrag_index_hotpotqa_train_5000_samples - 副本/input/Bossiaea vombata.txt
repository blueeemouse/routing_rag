Bossiaea vombata

Bossiaea vombata, commonly known as Wombat Bossiaea, is a species of flowering plant in the pea family (Fabaceae).

 It is endemic to the Wombat State Forest in Victoria, Australia.

 It has flat, blue-green leafless branches and grows up to 2 metres in height.

 Flowers appear between November and December in its native range.

 These are yellow with a red-brown centre.

 The seed pods are oblong in shape and around 40 mm long.

 The species was first formally described in 2008.
