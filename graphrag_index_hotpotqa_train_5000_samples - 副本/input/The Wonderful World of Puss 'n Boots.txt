The Wonderful World of Puss 'n Boots

The Wonderful World of Puss 'n Boots (Japanese: 長靴をはいた猫 , Hepburn: Nagagutsu o Haita Neko , literally "Cat Who Wore Cavalier Boots") is a 1969 Japanese traditional animation action-comedy musical feature film, the 15th cinema feature produced by Tōei Animation (then Tōei Dōga) and the second to be directed by Kimio Yabuki.

 The screenplay and lyrics, written by Hisashi Inōe and Morihisa Yamamoto, is based on the European literary fairy tale of the same name by Charles Perrault, expanded with elements of Alexandre Dumas-esque swashbuckling adventure and funny animal slapstick, with many other anthropomorphic animals ("kemono" in Japanese) in addition to the title character.

 The Tōei version of the character himself is named Pero, after Perrault.
