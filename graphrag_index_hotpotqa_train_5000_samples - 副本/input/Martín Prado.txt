Martín Prado

Martín Manuel Prado Bustamante (born October 27, 1983) is a Venezuelan professional baseball third baseman for the Miami Marlins of Major League Baseball (MLB).

 He has also played for the Atlanta Braves, Arizona Diamondbacks and New York Yankees.

 During his time with the Braves, Prado played in the 2010 All-Star Game.

 Primarily a third baseman, second baseman, and left fielder, Prado has started at every position during his MLB career except for pitcher, catcher, and center field.

 For the Marlins, Prado has primarily been a third baseman.
