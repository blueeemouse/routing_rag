Roger Avary

Roger Avary (born August 23, 1965) is a Canadian film and television producer, screenwriter and director in the American mass media industry.

 He worked on "Reservoir Dogs" and "Pulp Fiction", for which he and Quentin Tarantino were awarded the Oscar for Best Original Screenplay at the 67th Academy Awards.

 He wrote the screenplays for "Silent Hill" and "Beowulf".

 He also directed "Killing Zoe" and "The Rules of Attraction".
