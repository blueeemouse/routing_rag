In the Morning (Razorlight song)

"In the Morning" is a song by English indie rock band Razorlight, and is the opening track to their self-titled second album, "Razorlight".

 It was released on 3 July 2006 as the lead single from that album, peaking at number 3 in the UK Singles Chart (see 2006 in British music).

 To date, it is their third biggest single after "Somewhere Else", which peaked a position higher at number 2, and "America", which peaked at number 1.
