Lance Shilton

He was born in late 1921 in Elsternwick, Victoria, a suburb of Melbourne, and educated at the University of Melbourne and ordained in 1950.

 His first post was as a curate at Hawthorn.

 Then he was the incumbent at Carlton from 1951 to 1954.

 After this he was at the University of London for two years completing a B.D. From 1957 to 1973 he was the Rector of Holy Trinity, Adelaide.
