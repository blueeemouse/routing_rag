Williamsburg Community School District

The Williamsburg Community School District is a small, rural, public school district in Blair County, Pennsylvania.

 It serves the borough of Williamsburg, plus the townships of Woodbury and Catharine.

 the district encompasses approximately 65 sqmi .

 According to 2000 federal census data, it serves a resident population of 3,740.

 According to District officials, in school year 2007–08 the Williamsburg Community School District provided basic educational services to 543 pupils through the employment of 50 teachers, 31 full-time and part-time support personnel, and 3 administrators.

 Williamsburg Community School District received more than $4.3 million in state funding in school year 2007-08.
