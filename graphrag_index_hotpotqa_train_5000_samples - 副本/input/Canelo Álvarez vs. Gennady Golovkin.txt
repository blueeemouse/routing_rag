Canelo Álvarez vs. Gennady Golovkin

Canelo Álvarez vs. Gennady Golovkin, billed as "Supremacy", was a professional boxing superfight contested for the unified WBA (Super), WBC, IBF, "Ring" magazine, and lineal middleweight championship.

 The bout was on September 16, 2017 at T-Mobile Arena on the Las Vegas Strip.

 It was televised on HBO pay-per-view in the United States, on BoxNation Box Office in the United Kingdom, and on Space in Latin America.
