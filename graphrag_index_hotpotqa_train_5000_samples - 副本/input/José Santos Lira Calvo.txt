José Santos Lira Calvo

José Santos Lira Calvo (1809 – 25 October, 1886) was a Chilean lawyer and politician.

 He was born on the San Jose de Toro estate, Chimbarongo in 1809 and died in the city of Santiago on October 25, 1885.

 He was the son of José Santos Lira Contreras and Carmen Calvo Argomedo.

 He was married to Martina Rencoret y Cienfuegos.

 He was the father of one of the four great masters of Chilean painting, the painter Pedro Lira.
