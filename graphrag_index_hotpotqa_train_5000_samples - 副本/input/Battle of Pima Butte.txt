Battle of Pima Butte

The Battle of Pima Butte, or the Battle of Maricopa Wells, was fought on September 1, 1857 at Pima Butte, Arizona near Maricopa Wells in the Sierra Estrella.

 Yuma, Mohave, Apache and Yavapai warriors attacked a Maricopa village named Secate in one of the largest battles in Arizona's history.

 It was also the last major battle fought by the Yumas and the last major battle fought solely between native Americans in North America.
