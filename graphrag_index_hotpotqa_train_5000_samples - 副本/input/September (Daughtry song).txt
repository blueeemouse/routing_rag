September (Daughtry song)

"September" is the third and final single from Daughtry's second album "Leave This Town" (2009).

 This song was co-written by Chris Daughtry and Josh Steely.

 It was first released June 1, 2010 through RCA Records.

 The mid-tempo ballad is inspired by Chris's childhood memories growing up with his brother in a small town in North Carolina.
