Copy of a

"Copy of a" is a song by American industrial rock band Nine Inch Nails, released as the second single from their eighth studio album, "Hesitation Marks" (2013).

 It was originally released as a free digital download on Amazon.com in the United States and the United Kingdom for a limited time starting August 13, 2013.

 On August 20, the song was made available on the iTunes Store.

 It was also made available to those who had pre-ordered the album from Nine Inch Nails' official online store, together with "Came Back Haunted" in a zip file labeled ""Hesitation Marks" Singles".
