T.R. Dallas

T.R. Dallas is an Irish country singer.

 Dallas appeared on the Irish music scene in 1970 and has since become known across the British isles.

 The singer's real name is Tom Allen and he is the brother of Foster and Allen's, Tony Allen.

 He was born in Mount Temple Moate, Co.

 Westmeath, Ireland and first came to prominence in 1970 when he joined the Finnavons Showband which had previously been fronted by Gerry Black.
