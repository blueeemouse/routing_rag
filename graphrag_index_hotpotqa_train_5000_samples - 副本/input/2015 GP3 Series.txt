2015 GP3 Series

The 2015 GP3 Series was the sixth season of the GP3 Series, a motor racing feeder series that was run in support of the FIA Formula One World Championship and sister series GP2.

 Thirty-one drivers represented eight teams over the course of the season's nine rounds, starting in Spain on 9 May and ending in Abu Dhabi on 29 November.

 It was the final season for the Dallara GP3/13-AER P57 V6 chassis and engine package that debuted in 2013.

 A new Dallara-built chassis was introduced for 2016 along with a Mecachrome normally-aspirated V6 engine.
