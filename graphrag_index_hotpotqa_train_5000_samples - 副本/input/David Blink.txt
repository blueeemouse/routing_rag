David Blink

David Blink is the Yakima Valley Community College director of instrumental music and jazz studies.

 He is a trumpet player and he directs the Yakima Valley Community College Salsa Band.

 Blink is also a composer.

 Under his direction, the YVCC Salsa Band has successfully released a CD of original music.

 Blink is the organizer of the Yakima Valley Community College Latin Music Festival, a yearly celebration that began in 2007.

 The Latin Music Festival has a community-outreach portion.

 Professional musicians, such as Memo Acevedo from various parts of the United States teach students of all ages in Yakima and the surrounding region about music.
