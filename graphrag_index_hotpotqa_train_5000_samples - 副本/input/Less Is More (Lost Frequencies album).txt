Less Is More (Lost Frequencies album)

Less Is More is the debut studio album by Belgian DJ and record producer Lost Frequencies.

 It was released on 21 October 2016, by Lost & Cie under exclusive license to Armada Music and Ultra Music.

 The album was shortlisted by IMPALA (The Independent Music Companies Association) for the Album of the Year Award 2016, which rewards on a yearly basis the best album released on an independent European label.
