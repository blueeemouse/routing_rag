Judges' Lodgings, Monmouth

The Judges' Lodgings, located in Whitecross Street, Monmouth, south east Wales, is an eighteenth-century building, with earlier origins, on the edge of St James' Square.

 It has its origins as an early 16th-century town house, becoming the 'Labour in Vain' inn around 1756.

 It was in use as the Judges' Lodgings for the Monmouth Assizes before 1835, and as the Militia Officers' Mess in the 1870s.

 Today it is a private house, with modern mews cottages built into the rear.

 It is a Grade II listed building and is one of 24 blue plaque buildings on the Monmouth Heritage Trail.
