Ringo Starr and Friends

Ringo Starr and Friends is a 2006 live album by rock musician and ex-Beatle Ringo Starr, following his 2005-2006 successful tour.

 The album features the tracks from the All-Starr Band's 2001 tour.

 (Tracks from the 2001 edition of the All-Starr Band also appeared on the album, "King Biscuit Flower Hour Presents Ringo & His New All-Starr Band", released in 2002.

 Ringo's friends included on the album are Ian Hunter, Howard Jones, Roger Hodgson, Sheila E, Greg Lake and Mark Rivera.
