Fortuna Sittard Stadion

The Fortuna Sittard Stadion is a multi-use stadium on the Milaanstraat, Sittard, Netherlands.

 Currently used mostly for football matches, it is the home stadium of Fortuna Sittard.

 Built on the site of an industrial estate in 1999, it replaced Fortuna Sittard's former stadium, De Baandert.

 In 2013 they began to expand the stadion with an extra sports centrum next to it, a new hotel in the stadium and an whole new look.
