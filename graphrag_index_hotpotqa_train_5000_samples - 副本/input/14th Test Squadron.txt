14th Test Squadron

The 14th Test Squadron is a United States Air Force unit located at Schriever Air Force Base, Colorado.

 It is an Air Force Reserve unit that augments the 17th Test Squadron.

 The squadron is responsible for testing and evaluating space systems and associated support equipment.

 The unit was originally established in 1972 as the 14th Missile Warning Squadron.

 The missile warning squadron was an active duty unit that operated early warning radars at eight locations around the United States until it was inactivated in 1980.

 The squadron was reactivated and given its current space test mission in 2000.
