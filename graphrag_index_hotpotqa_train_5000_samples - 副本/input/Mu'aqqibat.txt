Mu'aqqibat

The Arabic term al-mu'aqqibat (الْمُعَقِّبَات - commonly encountered as the definite plural in Arabic: "those who follow one upon another"; "Sahih Int'l transl."

: "successive [angels]"; "Yusuf Ali transl."

: "angels in succession") is a term occurring in the Quran (Q.13:11) which some Islamic commentators consider to refer to a class of guardian.

 Therefore, these angels are also called "'al-ḥafaẓah"/ "'al-hafazzah"/ "'al-hafazwah" (الْحَفَظَة) which means "guarding angels".

 They protect mankind from the harm of the evil "jinn" (جِنّ) and "shayāteen" (شَيَاطِين).
