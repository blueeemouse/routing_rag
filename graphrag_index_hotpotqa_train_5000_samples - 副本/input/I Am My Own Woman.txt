I Am My Own Woman

I Am My Own Woman (German: Ich bin meine eigene Frau ) is a 1992 German film directed by Rosa von Praunheim.

 The film, a documentary-drama, follows the life story of Charlotte von Mahlsdorf, an East German transsexual who survived the Nazis; the repression of the Communists and helped start the German gay liberation movement.

 The film is based on Mahlsdorf's autobiography: "Ich bin meine eigene Frau", published in 1992, published in English as "I Am My Own Woman" (1995) and "I Am My Own Wife" (2004).
