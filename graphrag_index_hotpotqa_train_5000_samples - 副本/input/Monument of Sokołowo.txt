Monument of Sokołowo

Monument of Sokołowo - a monument commemorating the victims of the Battle of Sokołowo on May 2, 1848 during the Greater Poland Uprising.

 In the battle the forces of the Prussian led by Gen. A. Hirschfeld were against the Polish insurgents who were helped by local peasants and their leader was Ludwik Mierosławski.

 The victorious battle more than 300 insurgents were killed.

 This event also marks the Song of 1848.

 The monument was built according to Cyprian Norwid's idea.
