West Indian cricket team in Australia in 1979–80

The West Indies cricket team toured Australia from November 1979 to January 1980 and played a three-match Test series against the Australia national cricket team.

 The West Indies won the Test series 2–0.

 The West Indies were captained by Deryck Murray (first Test) and Clive Lloyd; Australia by Greg Chappell.

 In addition, the teams played in a triangular Limited Overs International (LOI) tournament with the England national cricket team.

 The West Indies won this tournament, defeating England in the final.
