Jay Pharoah

Jared Antonio Farrow (born 14 October, 1987) better known by his stage name Jay Pharoah, is an American actor, stand-up comedian, impressionist, rapper, and voice actor.

 He joined the cast of NBC's "Saturday Night Live" in 2010, for its thirty-sixth season.

 In 2015, he was ranked the 55th greatest "Saturday Night Live" cast member by "Rolling Stone" magazine.
