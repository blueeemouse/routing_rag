Mimulus

Mimulus is a plant genus in the family Phrymaceae, which was traditionally placed in family Scrophulariaceae.

 The genus now contains only seven species, two native to eastern North America and the other five native to Asia, Australia, Africa, or Madagascar.

 In the past, about 150 species were placed in this genus, most of which have since been assigned to other genera, the majority to genus "Erythranthe".
