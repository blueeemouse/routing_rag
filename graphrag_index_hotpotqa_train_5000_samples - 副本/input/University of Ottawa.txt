University of Ottawa

The University of Ottawa (uOttawa or U of O) (French: "Université d'Ottawa" ) is a bilingual public research university in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada.

 The main campus is located on 42.5 ha in the residential neighbourhood of Sandy Hill, adjacent to Ottawa's Rideau Canal.

 The university offers a wide variety of academic programs, administered by ten faculties.

 It is a member of the U15, a group of research-intensive universities in Canada.

 The University of Ottawa is the largest English-French bilingual university in the world.
