Dharam Singh (field hockey)

Dharam Singh (19 January 1919 in Gandiwind – 5 December 2001 in Chandigarh) was an Indian field hockey player who played as a right back in the gold medal winning team at the 1952 Helsinki Olympic Games.

 He was the team coach of the Indian team that won the gold medal at Tokyo in 1964.

 He also coached the silver medal winning team at 1978 Asian games Bangkok.

 He studied at Khalsa college, Amritsar.

 He also served on the selection panel of the Indian Hockey Federation from 1970–1982.
