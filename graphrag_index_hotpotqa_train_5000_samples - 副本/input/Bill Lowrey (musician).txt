Bill Lowrey (musician)

Bill Lowrey (born January 29, 1963) is an American musical entertainer and banjoist from California.

 He has been a featured performer or headliner at a variety of jazz festivals around the U.S. for over fifteen years.

 Lowrey has established himself in the four-string banjo community as one of its key figures as compared to the likes of Sean Moyses, Steve Peterson, and Buddy Wachter.
