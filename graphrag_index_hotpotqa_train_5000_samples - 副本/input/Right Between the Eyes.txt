Right Between the Eyes

Right Between the Eyes is a 1989 album by the band Icon.

 It marked a number of changes for the band Icon.

 The first of which was a new record label, Megaforce Worldwide/Atlantic Records.

 Then a new vocalist for the band.

 Jerry Harrison, who brought a new vocal and writing style to the band.

 Third was a new guitar player, Drew Bollmann, who was listed as a member, pictured and toured with the band although he did not actually play on the album which was finished before he joined.

 The album was produced by guitarist Dan Wexler along with radio personality Ed Trunk and Dan Zelisko, and featured cameo vocals by shock rock star Alice Cooper on "Two for the Road" and "Holy Man's War".
