Cartoon

A cartoon is a type of two-dimensional illustration, possibly animated.

 While the specific definition has changed over time, modern usage refers to (a) a typically non-realistic or semi-realistic artistic style of drawing or painting, (b) an image or series of images intended for satire, caricature, or humor, or (c) a motion picture that relies on a sequence of illustrations for its animation.

 An artist who creates cartoons is called a cartoonist.
