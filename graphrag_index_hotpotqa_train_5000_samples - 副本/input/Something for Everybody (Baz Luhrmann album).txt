Something for Everybody (Baz Luhrmann album)

Something for Everybody is an album released by Baz Luhrmann in 1998.

 It contains new ambient versions (either newly recorded or remixed) of music from his films and plays, including hits from "William Shakespeare's Romeo + Juliet", "Strictly Ballroom" and "La bohème".

 The most popular single from the album is the spoken word song "Everybody's Free (To Wear Sunscreen)".
