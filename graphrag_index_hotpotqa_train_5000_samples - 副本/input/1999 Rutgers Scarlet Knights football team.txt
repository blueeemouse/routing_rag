1999 Rutgers Scarlet Knights football team

The 1999 Rutgers Scarlet Knights football team represented Rutgers University in the 1999 NCAA Division I-A football season.

 The Scarlet Knights were led by fourth-year head coach Terry Shea and played their home games at Rutgers Stadium.

 They were a member of the Big East Conference.

 They finished the season 1–10, 1–6 in Big East play.
