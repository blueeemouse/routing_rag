The Arab-Hebrew Theater

The Arab-Hebrew Theater is a multilingual theater located in the Old Saraya House in the Old City of Jaffa.

 It serves as a stage for two theater companies working independently and together in two languages: Hebrew and Arabic.

 The Hebrew company is "Teatron Mekomi (Local Theater)", which was established in 1990 by Yigal Ezrati and Gabi Eldor, and the Arab company is "Al-Saraya", which was established in 1998.

 The theater is partially funded by the Ministry of Culture and the Tel Aviv Municipality.

 The theater has three arts directors: Mohammad Bakri, Yigal Ezrati, and Gabi Eldor.
