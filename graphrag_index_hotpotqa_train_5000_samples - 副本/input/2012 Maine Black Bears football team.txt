2012 Maine Black Bears football team

The 2012 Maine Black Bears football team represented the University of Maine in the 2012 NCAA Division I FCS football season.

 They were led by 20th-year head coach Jack Cosgrove and played their home games at Alfond Stadium.

 They are a member of the Colonial Athletic Association.

 They finished the season 5–6, 4–4 in CAA play to finish in seventh place.
