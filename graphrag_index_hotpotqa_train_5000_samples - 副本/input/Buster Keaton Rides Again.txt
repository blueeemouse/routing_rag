Buster Keaton Rides Again

Buster Keaton Rides Again is a 55-minute 1965 documentary film directed by John Spotton and narrated by Michael Kane.

 The film is a behind-the-scenes documentary shot while Buster Keaton's film "The Railrodder" (1965), was being produced.

 Although it is a production documentary, the film is actually longer than "The Railrodder", which was only 24 minutes long.

 Both films were produced by the National Film Board of Canada (NFB).

 In addition, a French version of "Buster Keaton Rides Again", "Avec Buster Keaton" was released.
