Red, White &amp; Crüe

Red, White & Crüe is an anthology album by the heavy metal band Mötley Crüe, released on February 1, 2005 by Mötley Records and charted at number 6 on The Billboard 200.

 To coincide with the album's release, the band reunited with drummer Tommy Lee, who left the band in 1999.

 Bassist Nikki Sixx commented on the band's reunion, comparing it to "seeing Mike Tyson fight.

 He probably won’t bite anyone’s ear off — but you’re there in case he does."
