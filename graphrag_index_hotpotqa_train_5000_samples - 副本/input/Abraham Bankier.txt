Abraham Bankier

Abraham Bankier (1910-1956) was one of the owners of the "Rekord" factory on Lipowa street in Cracow, Poland, that Oskar Schindler took over during World War II.

 Schindler then hired Bankier to manage the factory, which was renamed "Deutsche Emailwarenfabrik Oskar Schindler", "Emalia" for short.

 Bankier was able to leverage black market dealings with extra scrap metal to bring additional Jews to work at the factory, thereby giving them a reprieve and ultimately saving many lives.
