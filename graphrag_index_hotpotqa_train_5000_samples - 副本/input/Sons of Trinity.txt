Sons of Trinity

Sons of Trinity (Italian: Trinità & Bambino... e adesso tocca a noi, also known as "Trinity & Babyface" and "Trinity & Bambino: The Legend Lives On") is a 1995 Italian spaghetti western.

 The film was intended to be a continuation of the Trinity series starring Terence Hill and Bud Spencer, but although it was directed and produced by the same people as the original film, it failed to attract any interest in the audience.

 It was the last film directed by Enzo Barboni.
