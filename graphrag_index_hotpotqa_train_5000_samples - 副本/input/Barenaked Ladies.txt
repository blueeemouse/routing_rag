Barenaked Ladies

Barenaked Ladies (often abbreviated BNL or occasionally BnL) is a Canadian rock band.

 The band is currently composed of Jim Creeggan, Kevin Hearn, Ed Robertson, and Tyler Stewart.

 Barenaked Ladies formed in 1988 in Scarborough, Ontario (now incorporated into the City of Toronto) as a duo of Robertson and Steven Page.

 Brothers Jim and Andy Creeggan joined at the end of 1989, and Stewart was added in 1990 while Andy was on hiatus from the group.

 Andy departed permanently in 1995 citing musical differences and was replaced by Hearn.

 Page left in 2009, leaving the group as a quartet.
