Golden Greats (Ian Brown album)

Golden Greats is the second solo album released by Ian Brown, a first made famous by his role as frontman in The Stone Roses.

 Despite the title, the album is not a compilation, but rather, a studio album.

 It was described by "NME" as "a left-field masterpiece and Brown's best work for a decade", contains soaring sonic textures and electronic dance beats; it is less song-oriented than "Unfinished Monkey Business".

 "Golden Greats" showcases a diverse usage of instruments including strings, mellotron and organs.

 A number of the tracks on the album were written by Brown whilst he was imprisoned for two months following a fracas with a flight attendant.
