No Hands

"No Hands" is a song recorded by the American rapper Waka Flocka Flame for his debut album, "Flockaveli" (2010).

 The song features guest appearances from rappers Roscoe Dash and Wale.

 It was produced by Drumma Boy with co-production and writing credits from N. Cobey.

 It was leaked in May 2010 before it was officially released in August.

 The single entered the "Billboard" Hot 100 charts at #45 and peaked at #13.
