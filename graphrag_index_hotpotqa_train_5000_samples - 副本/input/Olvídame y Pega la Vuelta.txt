Olvídame y Pega la Vuelta

"Olvídame y Pega la Vuelta" is a song written and recorded by Argentine sibling duo Pimpinela from their 1982 self-titled album.

 Pimpinela recorded an English-language version of the song titled "Get Out of My Life, Now!"

 In 2005, a reggaeton remix was made for the song.
