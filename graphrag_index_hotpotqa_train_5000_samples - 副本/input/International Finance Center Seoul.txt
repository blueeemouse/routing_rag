International Finance Center Seoul

International Finance Centre Seoul(서울국제금융센터), commonly known IFC Seoul(아이 에프 시 서울), is a mixed-use integrated commercial development in Seoul, South Korea.

 It is located in Yeouido-dong, Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul, South Korea.

 The IFC project is one of the first large-scale developments in Korea, led by an international consortium.

 It was officially launched in 2005, and is part of Seoul Metropolitan Government's plan to rejuvenate the Yeouido area as a regional financial hub.
