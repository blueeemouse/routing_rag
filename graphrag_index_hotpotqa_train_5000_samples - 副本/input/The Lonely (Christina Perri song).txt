The Lonely (Christina Perri song)

"The Lonely" is a song by American singer-songwriter Christina Perri.

 The song was first released to iTunes on March 29, 2011, the same day that its accompanying lyric video premiered on "Teen Vogue".

 The video was uploaded onto her official YouTube account the following day.

 The song is included on Perri's debut album, "Lovestrong".

 It was released as the first promotional single of the album.
