Chris Messina

Chris Messina (born August 11, 1974) is an American actor and film director.

 He has appeared in supporting roles in films such as "Vicky Cristina Barcelona", "Argo", "Julie & Julia", "Ruby Sparks", "Celeste and Jesse Forever", and "You've Got Mail".

 He won critical acclaim for his lead performance in the film "The Giant Mechanical Man".

 On television, he is known for his roles as Chris Sanchez in "Damages" and as Danny Castellano in "The Mindy Project".
