Just a Game Stakes

The Just A Game Stakes is an American Thoroughbred horse race run annually in early June at Belmont Park in Elmont, New York.

 Open to fillies and mares age four and up, it is contested on turf over a distance of one mile (8 furlongs).

 A Grade I event since 2008, it offered a purse of $750,000 in 2014.

 In 2015, the purse was changed to $700,000.
