Baby's Got Her Blue Jeans On

"Baby's Got Her Blue Jeans On" is a song written by Bob McDill and recorded by American country music artist Mel McDaniel.

 It was released in October 1984 as the lead-off single from his album "Let It Roll".

 It was a number-one hit on the U.S. "Billboard" Hot Country Songs chart in February 1985, and Mel McDaniel's only number-one single.
