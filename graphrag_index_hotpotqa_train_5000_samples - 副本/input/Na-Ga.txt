Na-Ga

Na-Ga is a male Japanese artist who is employed as a graphic designer and illustrator for the company Key known for such famous visual novels as "Kanon", "Air", and "Clannad" among others.

 Na-Ga has been working for Key since the production of "Air" as one of the computer graphic artists, but was able to majorly contribute to character design in Key's sixth visual novel "Little Busters!

" with Itaru Hinoue, along with the later released "Little Busters!

 Ecstasy" and "Kud Wafter".

 For Key's ninth title "Rewrite", Na-Ga contributed to the game's computer graphics.

 Na-Ga worked in collaboration with Jun Maeda and ASCII Media Works' "Dengeki G's Magazine" to produce the mixed media projects "Angel Beats!

" and "Charlotte" as the original character designer.

 Na-Ga once worked for the company Pearlsoft R between 1997 and 1999 where he contributed to two visual novels, "Hakanai Omoi: Anemone", and "Sweet Days", as the main artist before coming to Key.

 He also participates in a dōjinshi circle named "from-D".
