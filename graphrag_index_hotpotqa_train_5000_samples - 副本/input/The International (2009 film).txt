The International (2009 film)

The International is a 2009 German–American political thriller drama film directed by Tom Tykwer.

 The film follows an Interpol agent and an American district attorney who investigate corruption within the IBBC, a fictional merchant bank based in Luxembourg.

 It serves organized crime and corrupt governments as a banker and as an arms broker.

 The bank's ruthless managers assassinate potential threats including their own employees.
