Wesley Gibbs

Wesley Gibbs (July 24, 1842 - May 29, 1917) was an American soldier who fought in the American Civil War.

 Gibbs received his country's highest award for bravery during combat, the Medal of Honor.

 Gibbs's medal was won for his capturing the flag during the Third Battle of Petersburg on April 2, 1865.

 He was honored with the award on May 10, 1865.

 Following his Medal of Honor his rank was subsequently reduced to Private, for reasons unknown.
