Avi Resort &amp; Casino

Avi Resort & Casino is a hotel and casino located near the southern tip of Nevada on the banks of the Colorado River in Laughlin, Nevada next to Fort Mohave, Arizona.

 Within walking distance of the California and Arizona borders, it is owned by the Fort Mojave Tribe and operated under an agreement with the state of Nevada.

 It has a 465-room hotel, a 25000 sqft casino and a 260-space RV park with internet access.
