Tame Impala

Tame Impala ( ) is an Australian psychedelic rock band founded by Kevin Parker in 2007.

 The group began as a home recording project for Parker, who writes, records, performs, and produces the music.

 As a touring act, the project consists of Parker (guitar, vocals), Jay Watson (synthesiser, vocals, guitar), Dominic Simper (guitar, synthesiser), Cam Avery (bass guitar, vocals), and Julien Barbagallo (drums, vocals).

 Previously signed to Modular Recordings, Tame Impala is signed to Interscope Records in the US, and Fiction Records in the UK.
