Ursuline College (Chatham)

Ursuline College Chatham (AKA The Pines, UCC) is a Catholic secondary school in Chatham-Kent, Ontario, Canada.

 It is one of two secondary schools in the St. Clair Catholic District School Board (the other being located in the city of Sarnia, Ontario), and is one of seven secondary schools located in the regional municipality of Chatham-Kent.

 The school has more than 1400 students enrolled, making it the largest high school in Chatham-Kent.
