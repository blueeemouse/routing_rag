Let's Get Crazy (song)

"Let's Get Crazy" is a song by American singer–songwriter and actress Miley Cyrus, performing as Hannah Montana – the alter ego of Miley Stewart – a character she played on the Disney Channel television series "Hannah Montana".

 It was released to Radio Disney on January 19, 2009 as promotion for "" and .

 The song is also included on the "Hannah Montana 3" soundtrack.

 A karaoke version is available in "Disney's Karaoke Series: Hannah Montana 3".

 The song is musically dance-rock based.

 Lyrically, the track speaks about having fun and cutting loose.
