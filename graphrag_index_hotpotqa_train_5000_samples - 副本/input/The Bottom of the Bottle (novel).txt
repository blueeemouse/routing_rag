The Bottom of the Bottle (novel)

The Bottom of the Bottle is a novel by the Belgian writer Georges Simenon.

 The original French version "Le Fond de la Bouteille", written in 1948 when Simenon was living in Arizona, appeared in 1949.

 The novel is among his "romans durs", a term roughly translated as hard, or harrowing, novels; it was used by Simenon for what he regarded as his serious literary works.
