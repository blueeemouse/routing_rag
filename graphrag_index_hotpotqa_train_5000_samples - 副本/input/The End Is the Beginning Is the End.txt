The End Is the Beginning Is the End

"The End Is the Beginning Is the End" is a Grammy Award-winning song by American alternative rock band The Smashing Pumpkins.

 It is the first full-band song released as a single by the Smashing Pumpkins in the aftermath of their 1995 album, "Mellon Collie and the Infinite Sadness".

 It is their first release with drummer Matt Walker, who later drummed on several tracks of "Adore" and all of James Iha's "Let It Come Down".

 The song won the Grammy Award for Best Hard Rock Performance.
