Shri Damubhai Shukla Shaikshanik Sankul

Shri Damubhai Shukla Educational Campus (Gujarati: શ્રી દામુભાઈ શુક્લ શૈક્ષણિક સંકુલ) is educational institution founded by Shri Damubhai Shukla in 1935.

 It is popularly known as Navchetan High School, is an educational institution at Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

 Shri Damubhai Shukla was founder of the institution and therefore the name is Shri Damubhai Educational Campus.

 Navchetan trust is running the educational institution that is why it is also known as Navchetan High School.

 Shri Damubhai Shukla Educational Campus is affiliated with Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Board along with Sikkim Manipal University for Business Administration.

 Navchetan High School providing both Gujarati as well as English medium education facilities.

 For higher Education Navchetan is also providing education in Commerce as well as Science Streams.

 The campus is at Paldi Road, Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

 The school serve the education to more than 1700 students per year.
