Nelson Liriano

Nelson Arturo Liriano Bonilla (born June 3, 1964) is a Dominican former professional baseball baseball player.

 He played for various teams in Major League Baseball (MLB) from 1987 to 1998 batting a career average of .260 over those 11 seasons.

 In 1999, he played in one game for the Chunichi Dragons of Nippon Professional Baseball (NPB).
