Hüsker Dü discography

The discography of Hüsker Dü, an American punk rock band, consists of six studio albums, two live albums, one compilation album, two extended plays, and ten singles.

 The band was formed by Bob Mould (guitar, vocals), Grant Hart (drums, vocals), and Greg Norton (bass guitar) in 1979.

 Their first album release was "Land Speed Record", a live album released through New Alliance Records.

 The band released its first studio album, "Everything Falls Apart" on its own label (Reflex Records) the following year.

 Hüsker Dü signed with SST Records in 1983, and released its next three albums with that label.

 The Warner Music Group released the band's last two studio albums.

 Hüsker Dü broke up in 1987.

 The band released 5 albums, including two double albums, between January 1984 and January 1987.
