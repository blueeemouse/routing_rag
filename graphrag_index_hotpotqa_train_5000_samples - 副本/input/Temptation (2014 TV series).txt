Temptation (2014 TV series)

Temptation () is a 2014 South Korean television series starring Kwon Sang-woo, Choi Ji-woo, Lee Jung-jin and Park Ha-sun.

 It aired on SBS from July 14 to September 16, 2014 on Mondays and Tuesdays at 21:55 for 20 episodes.

 The drama reunites Choi Ji-woo and Kwon Sang-woo who both starred in the 2003 hit melodrama "Stairway to Heaven".
