My Heart Belongs to Daddy (Desperate Housewives)

"My Heart Belongs to Daddy" is the 27th episode of the ABC television series, "Desperate Housewives".

 It was the fourth episode of the show's second season.

 The episode was written by John Pardee and Joey Murphy and was directed by Robert Duncan McNeill.

 It originally aired on Sunday, October 16, 2005.
