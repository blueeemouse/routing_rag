Paul Revere (song)

"Paul Revere" is a song by American hip hop group Beastie Boys, released as the third single from their debut album "Licensed to Ill" (1986).

 It was written by Adam Horovitz, Joseph Simmons, Darryl McDaniels, and Rick Rubin.

 It was produced by Rick Rubin and the Beastie Boys.

 The song tells a fictional story of how the Beastie Boys met.
