Sheryl Gascoigne

Sheryl Gascoigne (née Failes; born 24 September 1965) is a British television personality and author.

 She is the former wife of footballer Paul Gascoigne and the mother of glamour model Bianca Gascoigne.

 Her television career includes a presenting role on ITV1's "Loose Women", and she appeared as a contestant on the tenth series of the UK version of "I'm a Celebrity...Get Me Out of Here!
