Kingsman (film series)

The Kingsman film series consists of spy action-comedy films based on "The Secret Service" comics by Scottish writer Mark Millar and English writer Dave Gibbons.

 Produced by Matthew Vaughn, David Reid and Adam Bohling and distributed by 20th Century Fox, the films star Taron Egerton, Colin Firth, Mark Strong and Sophie Cookson.

 Vaughn who directed and co-wrote the first two films is writing the script for the third film.
