Good Friday Agreement

The Good Friday Agreement (GFA) or Belfast Agreement (Irish: "Comhaontú Aoine an Chéasta" or "Comhaontú Bhéal Feirste"; Ulster-Scots: "Guid Friday Greeance" or "Bilfawst Greeance") was a major political development in the Northern Ireland peace process of the 1990s.

 Northern Ireland's present devolved system of government is based on the agreement.

 The agreement also created a number of institutions between Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland, and between the Republic of Ireland and the United Kingdom.
