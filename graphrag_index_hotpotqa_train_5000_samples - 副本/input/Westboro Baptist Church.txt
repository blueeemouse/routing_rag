Westboro Baptist Church

Westboro Baptist Church (WBC) is a Baptist church which is known for allegations of hate speech, especially against LGBT people (homophobia), Catholics (anti-Catholicism), Orthodox Christians (anti-Orthodoxy), Muslims (Islamophobia), Jews (antisemitism), American soldiers and politicians.

 The church is widely known as a hate group and is monitored as such by the Anti-Defamation League and the Southern Poverty Law Center.

 The church has been involved in actions against gay people since at least 1991, when it sought a crackdown on homosexual activity at Gage Park six blocks northwest of the church.

 In addition to conducting anti-gay protests at military funerals, the organization pickets celebrity funerals and public events.

 Protests have also been held against Jews and Catholics, and some protests have included WBC members stomping on the American flag or flying the flag upside down on a flagpole.

 The church also has made statements such as, "thank God for dead soldiers," "God blew up the troops," "thank God for 9/11," and "God hates America."
