Chomana Dudi

Chomana Dudi (Kannada: ಚೋಮನ ದುಡಿ , English: "Choma's Drum" ) is a feature film in the Kannada language.

 It is based on a novel of the same name, written by Shivaram Karanth.

 The film was released in the year 1975 and won the "Swarna Kamal", India's National Award for the best film.
