Cocijo

Cocijo (occasionally spelt Cociyo) is a lightning deity of the pre-Columbian Zapotec civilization of southern Mexico.

 He has attributes characteristic of similar Mesoamerican deities associated with rain, thunder and lightning, such as Tlaloc of central Mexico, and Chaac (or "Chaak") of the Maya civilization.

 In the Zapotec language, the word "cocijo" means "lightning", as well as referring to the deity.
