2011 Nebraska Cornhuskers football team

The 2011 Nebraska Cornhuskers football team represented the University of Nebraska–Lincoln in the 2011 NCAA Division I FBS football season.

 The Cornhuskers were coached by Bo Pelini and played their home games at Memorial Stadium in Lincoln, Nebraska.

 This season was Nebraska's first in the Big Ten Conference in the Legends Division as they moved from the Big 12 Conference to the Big Ten following the conclusion of the 2010 season.

 They finished the season 9–4, 5–3 in Big Ten play to finish in third place in the Legends Division.

 They were invited to the Capital One Bowl where they were defeated by South Carolina 13–30.
