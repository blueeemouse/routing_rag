Queen of the Netherlands (ship)

Queen of the Netherlands is a Dutch Trailing suction hopper dredger ship constructed in 1998.

 After lengthening in 2009, she was the largest and most powerful dredger in the world.

 The vessel has been used in high-profile salvage and dredging operations including the investigation into the Swissair Flight 111 crash and in the Port Phillip Channel Deepening Project.

 It has been called "the world's largest floating vacuum cleaner".
