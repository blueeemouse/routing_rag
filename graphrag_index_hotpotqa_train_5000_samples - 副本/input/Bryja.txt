Bryja

Bryja - thin kasza or mash, a Germanic, Celtic and Slavic dish, based on overcooked oat or kasza, that formulated the basis of their respectable cuisine.

 It is also the name of a traditional Silesian dish served during Wigilia.

 The dish's names: "bryja", "breja", "brejka", "breha", the German "Brei" have their roots in Celtic languages.
