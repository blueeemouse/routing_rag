Heart Beats Again

"Heart Beats Again" is a song recorded by Australian singer and songwriter Dami Im for her third studio album, "Heart Beats".

 It was written by Im and produced by Carl Dimitaga.

 The song was released on 30 September 2014, as a promotional single from "Heart Beats".

 Upon its release, "Heart Beats Again" debuted at number 92 on the ARIA Singles Chart.
