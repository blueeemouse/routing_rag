Katia Margaritoglou

Katia Marie Margaritoglou, in Greek: "Κάτια Μαργαρίτογλου", is a Greek fashion model and beauty contestant.

 In 1998, she won the title Miss Hellas (Greek: Μις Ελλάς ) at the Miss Star Hellas pageant and represented Greece at the Miss World 1998 pageant event which took place in the Seychelles.

 Her face has appeared on the covers of such magazines as "Life & Style", "Men Magazine" and "Money & Life" as well as in numerous fashion catalogue books and advertisements.

 She is currently represented by Ace Models.
