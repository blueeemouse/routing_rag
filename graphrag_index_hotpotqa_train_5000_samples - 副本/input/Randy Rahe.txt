Randy Rahe

Randy Michael Rahe (born (1960--) 12, 1960 ) is an American college basketball coach and the current head men's basketball coach at Weber State University.

 Randy Rahe was hired on March 23, 2006, as the 9th coach in Weber State's 47-year history.

 Rahe came to WSU from the University of Utah where he was an assistant under former coach Ray Giacoletti.

 Rahe was also an assistant under Stew Morrill for 13 seasons, first at Colorado State University and then at Utah State University.

 Rahe posted a record of 54–17 as a high school coach in Colorado from 1985 to 1988.

 He has also been an assistant coach at Colorado College, Colorado and Denver.
