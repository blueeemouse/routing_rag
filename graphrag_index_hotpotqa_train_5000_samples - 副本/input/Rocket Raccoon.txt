Rocket Raccoon

Rocket Raccoon is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics.

 Created by writer Bill Mantlo and artist Keith Giffen, the character first appeared in "Marvel Preview" #7 (Summer 1976).

 He is an intelligent, anthropomorphic raccoon, who is an expert marksman and master tactician.

 His name and aspects of his character are a nod to The Beatles' 1968 song "Rocky Raccoon".
