2014 Austrian Grand Prix

The 2014 Austrian Grand Prix (formally the Formula 1 Grosser Preis Von Österreich 2014) was a Formula One motor race held on 22 June 2014 at the Red Bull Ring in Spielberg, Austria.

 It was the eighth round of the 2014 season and the 28th Austrian Grand Prix and the first to be held since 2003.

 The 71-lap race was won by Mercedes driver Nico Rosberg after starting from third position.

 His teammate Lewis Hamilton finished second with Valtteri Bottas third for the Williams team.
