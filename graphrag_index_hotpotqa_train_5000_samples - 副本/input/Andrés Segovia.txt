Andrés Segovia

Andrés Segovia Torres, 1st Marquis of Salobreña (] ) (21 February 18932 June 1987), known as Andrés Segovia, was a virtuoso Spanish classical guitarist from Linares, Spain.

 He is regarded as one of the greatest guitarists of all time and the grandfather of the classical guitar.

 Many professional classical guitarists today are students of Segovia, or students of his students.
