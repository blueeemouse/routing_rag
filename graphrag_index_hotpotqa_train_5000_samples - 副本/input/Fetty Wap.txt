Fetty Wap

Willie Maxwell II (born June 7, 1991) is an American rapper better known by his stage name Fetty Wap.

 He rose to prominence after his debut single "Trap Queen", reached number two on the U.S. "Billboard" Hot 100 chart in May 2015.

 This helped Fetty Wap secure a record deal with 300 Entertainment, a record label distributed by Atlantic Records.

 He subsequently released two Top 10 singles in the U.S., "679" and "My Way".

 Fetty Wap's eponymously titled debut album was released in September 2015 and reached number one on the U.S. "Billboard" 200 chart.
