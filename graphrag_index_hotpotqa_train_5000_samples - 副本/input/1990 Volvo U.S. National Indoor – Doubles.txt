1990 Volvo U.S. National Indoor – Doubles

Paul Annacone and Christo van Rensburg were the defending champions.

 Annacone competed alongside Kelly Evernden but lost in the semifinals, while van Rensburg played with Kevin Curren and lost in the quarterfinals.

<br>Darren Cahill and Mark Kratzmann won the title, defeating Udo Riglewski and Michael Stich 7–5, 6–2, in the final.
