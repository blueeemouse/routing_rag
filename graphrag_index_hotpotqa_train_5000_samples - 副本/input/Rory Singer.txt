Rory Singer

Rory Michael Singer (born May 28, 1976) is an American mixed martial artist.

 Although Rory had begun making a name for himself as a 185-pound prospect; his big break came after meeting the producers of TUF.

 They were filming Forrest Griffin at The HardCore Gym for his fight against Stephan Bonnar in the finals of season 1.

 Forrest went on to win Season 1 and Rory was approached soon after to appear on Season 3.
