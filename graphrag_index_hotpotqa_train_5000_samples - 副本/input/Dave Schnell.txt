Dave Schnell

David "Dave" Schnell (July 15, 1966 - May 22, 2011) was an American football player.

 He attended Elkhart Central High School in Elkhart, Indiana, and he was selected by "Sports Illustrated" in 1985 as the best high school football player in the United States.

 He then played college football at the quarterback position for the Indiana University Hoosiers football team.

 He led the 1987 Indiana Hoosiers football team to victories over both Michigan and Ohio State, becoming the only Indiana quarterback to record victories against both teams in the same year.

 The following year, he was selected by the conference coaches as the second-team quarterback on the 1988 All-Big Ten Conference football team.

 Schnell was diagnosed with leukemia in 2007 and died from the disease in May 2011 at age 44.

 He was inducted into the Indiana Football Hall of Fame in May 2010.
