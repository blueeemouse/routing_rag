Lauren London

Lauren Nicole London (born December 5, 1984) is an American film actress, model, television personality and occasional television actress.

 Beginning her career in music videos and later transitioning into film and television acting, London earned recognition for her performance in the 2006 film "ATL", as well as the television shows "90210" and "Entourage", and as Kiera Whitaker on the BET Comedy-drama, "The Game".
