Joe Connelly (producer)

Joe Connelly (August 22, 1917 – February 13, 2003) was a television and radio scriptwriter born in New York City.

 He was best known for his work on "The Amos 'n' Andy Show", "Meet Mr. McNutley", "Leave It To Beaver", "Ichabod and Me", "Bringing Up Buddy", and "The Munsters", along with his co-writer Bob Mosher who hails from Auburn, New York.
