Didn't They Do Well

Didn't They Do Well is a short-lived BBC television quiz show presented by Bruce Forsyth that ran from 15 January to 18 March 2004.

 It consisted of archive television clips, many of which were from previous quiz show episodes, in which modern day contestants were shown a question and then asked to answer it.

 Its title was one of Forsyth's catchphrases when hosting "The Generation Game" in the 1970s and again in the 1990s.
