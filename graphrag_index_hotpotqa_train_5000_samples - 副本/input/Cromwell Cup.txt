Cromwell Cup

The Cromwell Cup was the second ever football competition (after the Youdan Cup) and was held in Sheffield, England.

 It was held in February 1868 and named after Oliver Cromwell, manager of the local Alexandra Theatre (not the famous Lord Protector), who donated the cup.

 He also played for the Garrick club.

 The tournament was only open to teams under two years old.

 The final was held at Bramall Lane, Sheffield and was won by the world's first ever Golden Goal.

 The trophy is still held in the Sheffield Wednesday trophy cabinet.
