If Ever I See You Again

"If Ever I See You Again" is the title of a 1978 hit single by Roberta Flack.

 The song was composed by Joseph "Joe" Brooks and served as the title song for the eponymous 1978 film "If Ever I See You Again" that Brooks directed and also starred in with Shelley Hack as his leading lady.

 Male vocalist Jamie Carr sang the theme song on the film's soundtrack.
