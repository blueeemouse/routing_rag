Lakshmi Narasimha Entertainments

Lakshmi Narasimha Entertainments (Telugu: లక్ష్మీ నరసింహ ఎంటర్టైన్మెంట్స్) is an Indian film production company established by Chakri Chigurupati, an Indian film producer.

 The company is based in Hyderabad.

Telugu movies produced by the company Veedu Theda, Swamy Ra Ra and Mosagallaku Mosagadu (2015 film).
