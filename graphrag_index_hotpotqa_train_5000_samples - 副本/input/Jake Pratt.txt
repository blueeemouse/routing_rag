Jake Pratt

Jake Stephen Pratt (born 25 September 1996) is an English actor.

 He entered the public eye when he appeared on the first series of the ITV1 television show "Britain's Got Talent" in June 2007 where he reached the semi-finals, and lost a place in the grand final.

 He has since appeared in the British children's television programme, "Green Balloon Club", and also worked as a voice-over artist.

 He stars as Alfie in the BBC comedy film "Nativity!

", which was released on 27 November 2009.
