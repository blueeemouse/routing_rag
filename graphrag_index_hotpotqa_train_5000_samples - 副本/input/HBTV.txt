HBTV

HBTV (Hanna-Barbera Television) is a series of animated music videos created by Hanna-Barbera Productions in 1985–1986 featuring clips from various Hanna-Barbera cartoon shows.

 The animated videos were similar to Walt Disney Productions' "D-TV" and Nickelodeon's sister network MTV and were originally broadcast as interstitials between segments of the syndicated programming block "The Funtastic World of Hanna-Barbera".
