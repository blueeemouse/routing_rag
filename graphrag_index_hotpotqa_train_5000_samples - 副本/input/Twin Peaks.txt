Twin Peaks

Twin Peaks is an American mystery drama television series created by Mark Frost and David Lynch that premiered on April 8, 1990, on ABC.

 It was one of the top-rated series of 1990, but declining ratings led to its cancellation after its second season in 1991.

 It nonetheless gained a cult following and has been referenced in a wide variety of media.

 In subsequent years, "Twin Peaks" has often been listed among the greatest television series of all time.
