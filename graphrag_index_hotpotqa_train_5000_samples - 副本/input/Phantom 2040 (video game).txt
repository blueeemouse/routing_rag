Phantom 2040 (video game)

Phantom 2040 is a side-scrolling action-adventure video game developed by Hearst Entertainment and published by Viacom New Media in 1995 for the Genesis, Super NES and Game Gear.

 The game is directly based upon the animated television series "Phantom 2040" but follows a plotline not strictly taken by the show.

 The game plays similarly to the "Metroid" and "Castlevania" series.
