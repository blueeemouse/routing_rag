Vaadu Nenu Kaadhu

Vaadu Nenu Kaadhu is an upcoming Telugu legal thriller film directed by Vinod Vijayyan.

 The film stars starring Sairam Shankar in the lead role as a public prosecutor.

 Acclaimed cinematographer Rajeev Ravi wields the camera, Vivek Harshan handles the editing, while award winning composer Rahul Raj composes the film's original songs and original background score.
