Let's Be Cops

Let's Be Cops is a 2014 American action comedy film co-written and directed by Luke Greenfield.

 The film stars Jake Johnson and Damon Wayans, Jr. as two friends who pretend to be Los Angeles police officers.

 Co-starring Nina Dobrev, Rob Riggle, James D'Arcy and Keegan-Michael Key, the film was released on August 13, 2014.
