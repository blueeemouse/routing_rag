Plot 3a Princes Dock

Plot 3a Princes Dock is a 33-storey mixed use building that has been approved for construction alongside Prince's Dock at the waterfront of Liverpool, England.

 If and when built, it will stand alongside the likes of 1 Princes Dock and Alexandra Tower.

 Besides West Tower, Radio City Tower and Liverpool Cathedral, Plot 3a Princes Dock is the only structure over 100 m that has been approved for construction in the city's history.

 Planning permission has been granted for the building on two occasions (2007 and 2011), despite this construction is still yet to commence.
