Our Town (1940 film)

Our Town is a 1940 American drama romance film adaptation of a play of the same name by Thornton Wilder starring Martha Scott as Emily Webb, and William Holden as George Gibbs.

 The cast also included Fay Bainter, Beulah Bondi, Thomas Mitchell, Guy Kibbee and Frank Craven.

 It was adapted by Harry Chandlee, Craven and Wilder, and directed by Sam Wood.
