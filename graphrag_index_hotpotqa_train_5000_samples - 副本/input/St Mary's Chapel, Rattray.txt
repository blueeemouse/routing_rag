St Mary's Chapel, Rattray

St Mary's Chapel (known also as "the Chapel of the Blessed Virgin") is a late 12th/early 13th century chapel found in Rattray, Aberdeenshire, Scotland.

 It was built by William Comyn, jure uxoris Earl of Buchan during the same period as the Castle of Rattray and was ""private chapel for the castle"" and its residents. "

"Dedicated to the Virgin Mary"" it was possibly constructed after the ""drowning of a"..." [unknown] ""son of Comyn in the well near by.""
