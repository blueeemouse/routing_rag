Ode to Billie Joe (album)

Ode to Billie Joe is the debut solo studio album by Bobbie Gentry.

 It was released in 1967.

 It was produced by Kelly Gordon and arranged by Jimmie Haskell and Shorty Rogers.

 It was the only album to displace The Beatles "Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band" from its 15-week reign at the top of the "Billboard" 200 album chart.
