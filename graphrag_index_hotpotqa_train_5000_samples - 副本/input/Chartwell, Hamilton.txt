Chartwell, Hamilton

Chartwell is a suburb in north-eastern Hamilton, New Zealand.

 The suburb was named after Chartwell, the country home of Sir Winston Churchill.

 The area became a part of Hamilton in June 1962 and was officially defined as a suburb in 1974.

 Most of the housing is private single or 2 level dwellings with little state housing.

 Streets near the square are used for parking.

 Private dwellings are being purchased by professionals to be used as business premises close to the square.

 The streets are well planted with trees.
