After the Disco (song)

"After the Disco" is a song by American alternative rock band Broken Bells.

 Written by band members James Mercer and Brian Burton and produced by the latter, it was originally recorded by the band for their second studio album, "After the Disco", where it appears as the second track.

 It was released as the second single from "After the Disco" in the United Kingdom on January 7, 2014.

 This song also used in the 15th edition of PES, "Pro Evolution Soccer 2016" as 2nd track.
