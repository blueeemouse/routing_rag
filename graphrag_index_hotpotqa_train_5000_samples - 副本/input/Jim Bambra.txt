Jim Bambra

Jim Bambra (born 1956) is a British designer and reviewer of fantasy roleplaying games (RPG), and a former company director.

 He is particularly known for his contributions to "Dungeons and Dragons", "Fighting Fantasy", "Warhammer", and "" which was based on the "Star Wars" films.

 Later he became head of design at MicroProse, then managing director of Pivotal Games, a publisher of video games including "".
