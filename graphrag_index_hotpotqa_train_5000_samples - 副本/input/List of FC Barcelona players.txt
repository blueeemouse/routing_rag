List of FC Barcelona players

FC Barcelona is a professional association football club based in Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain.

 The club was founded in 1899 by a group of Swiss, English and Spanish men led by Joan Gamper.

 Barcelona is one of only three clubs never to have been relegated from La Liga and the most successful club in Spanish football, having won a total of 70 domestic titles; 24 La Liga titles, a record 29 Spanish Cups, 12 Spanish Super Cups, a record 3 Copas Eva Duarte and 2 League Cups.

 Barcelona is the only European side to have played continental football in every season since its inception in 1955 and one of the most successful clubs in European football, having won 14 official UEFA trophies in total.
