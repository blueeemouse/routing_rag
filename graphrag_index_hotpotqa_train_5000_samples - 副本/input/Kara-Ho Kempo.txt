Kara-Ho Kempo

Kara-Ho Kempo (the unity of spirit, mind, soul and body, fist law) is a martial art created in the 1930s by the late William Kwai Sun Chow of Hawaii (July 3, 1914 - September 21, 1987).

 The system can best be described as a series of rapid strikes (punches and kicks) to the vital areas of an opponent.

 The system calls for an equal use of both the hands and feet and, also, teaches self-defense techniques against weapons and multiple attackers.
