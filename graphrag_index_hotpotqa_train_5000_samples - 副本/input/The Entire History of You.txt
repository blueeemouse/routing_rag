The Entire History of You

"The Entire History of You" is the third and final episode of the first series of British science fiction anthology series "Black Mirror".

 It was written by the creator of "Peep Show" and "Fresh Meat", Jesse Armstrong, making it the only episode of the series not written or co-written by creator and showrunner Charlie Brooker.

 It was directed by Brian Welsh, and first aired on Channel 4 on 18 December 2011.
