Ligustrum sinense

Ligustrum sinense (Chinese privet; syn.

 "L. villosum"; in Mandarin: 杻; pinyin: chǒu) is a species of privet native to China, Taiwan and Vietnam.

 It is also naturalized in Réunion, the Andaman Islands, Norfolk Island, Costa Rica, Honduras, Panamá and much of the eastern and southern United States (from Texas and Florida north to Kansas, Illinois, New Jersey, Massachusetts and Connecticut). "

Ligustrum lucidum" is sometimes also called "Chinese privet".
