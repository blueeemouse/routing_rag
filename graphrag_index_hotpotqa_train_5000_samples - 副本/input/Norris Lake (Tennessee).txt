Norris Lake (Tennessee)

Norris Lake is a reservoir that is located in Anderson, Campbell, Claiborne, Grainger, and Union counties in Tennessee.

 The lake was created by the Norris Dam at the Cove Creek Site on the Clinch River in 1936 by the TVA for flood control.

 The dam was the first major project taken on by the TVA.

 The lake, dam and the town of Norris, Tennessee are named after George W Norris, who was a United States senator from Nebraska and wrote the legislation that created the TVA.
