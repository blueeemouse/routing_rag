Liberty Bowl Memorial Stadium

Liberty Bowl Memorial Stadium, originally Memphis Memorial Stadium, is a football stadium located at the former Mid-South Fairgrounds in the Midtown area of Memphis, Tennessee, United States.

 The stadium is the site of the annual AutoZone Liberty Bowl, and is the home field of the University of Memphis Tigers football team of the American Athletic Conference.

 It has also been the host of several attempts at professional sports in the city, as well as other local football games and other gatherings.
