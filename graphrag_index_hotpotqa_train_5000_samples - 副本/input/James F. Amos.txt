James F. Amos

James F. "Jim" Amos (born November 12, 1946) is a retired United States Marine Corps four-star general who served as the 35th Commandant of the Marine Corps.

 As a Naval Aviator, Amos commanded the 3rd Marine Aircraft Wing during the Iraq War in 2003 and 2004.

 He served as the 31st Assistant Commandant of the Marine Corps.

 He is the first Marine Corps aviator to serve as commandant.
