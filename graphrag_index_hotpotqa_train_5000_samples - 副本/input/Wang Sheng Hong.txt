Wang Sheng Hong

Wang Sheng Hong was born in Hemei village in Zhanghua County, Taiwan in 1970, growing up in a farming family with an illiterate mother and a father who only received basic education.

 Though his parents did not excel at explaining the world, their actions served as a model for honesty, warmth and kindness.

 As a youth, Wang enjoyed reading the works of Qi Jun and Yukio Mishima, and exchanged letters with the former for twenty years, discussing the giving and receiving among family and friends, as well as their shared love for various other topics.

 Meanwhile, the author Yukio sparked his interest in passion, immorality, and life and death.
