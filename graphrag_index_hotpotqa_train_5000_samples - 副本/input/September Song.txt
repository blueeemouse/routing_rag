September Song

"September Song" is an American pop standard song composed by Kurt Weill with lyrics by Maxwell Anderson, introduced by Walter Huston in the 1938 Broadway musical production "Knickerbocker Holiday".

 After being used in the 1950 film "September Affair", the song has since been recorded by numerous singers and instrumentalists.

 It was also used during screen credits in the British television series "May to December", the name of which quotes the opening line of the song.
