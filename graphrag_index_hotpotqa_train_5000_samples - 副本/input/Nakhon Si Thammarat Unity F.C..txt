Nakhon Si Thammarat Unity F.C.

Nakhon Si Thammarat Unity Football Club (Thai สโมสรฟุตบอลนครศรีธรรมราช ยูนิตี้), is a Thai professional football club based in Nakhon Si Thammarat Province.

 The club was founded in 2014.

 They currently play in Thai League 3 Southern region.

 This club rename from Ratchaphruek Muangnont United to Rajapruk University and they changed logo in 2016.
