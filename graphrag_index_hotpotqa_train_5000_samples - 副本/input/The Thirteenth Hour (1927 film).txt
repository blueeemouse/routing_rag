The Thirteenth Hour (1927 film)

The Thirteenth Hour (aka:The 13th Hour) is a 1927 American silent film mystery produced and distributed by Metro Goldwyn Mayer and directed by Chester Franklin.

 The film stars Lionel Barrymore in a role where, as noted criminologist Professor Leroy, he dons a weird series of disguises to hide a dark secret.

 This was the first film where Barrymore was cast opposite talented dogs, and the first where he was cast as a serial killer.
