Ya Viene el Sol

Ya Viene el Sol ("The sun is coming") is the third album recorded by Spanish synthpop band Mecano, in 1984.

 The album was the beginning of a more sophisticated and mature band.

 It included new sounds in the band's music, using the sampler/workstation Fairlight CMI.

 After this album the band assumed the production of their records from the start.

 This album includes the only song that Ana Torroja wrote with the band.

 The importance of the songs written by José María Cano and the fact that for the first time one of his songs was released as a single - and became the biggest hit of the summer - kept the band together, since at this point he was considering leaving the band.
