Pilot (The Drew Carey Show)

"Pilot" is the first episode and the series premiere of the American sitcom "The Drew Carey Show".

 It first aired on September 13, 1995 on the ABC network in the United States.

 The premise of the show revolves around the life Drew Carey would have lived if he had not become a stand-up comedian.

 The pilot introduces the main characters of Drew (Carey), Kate (Christa Miller), Lewis (Ryan Stiles) and Oswald (Diedrich Bader), as well as Drew's workplace, the fictional Winfred-Lauder department store, and enemy Mimi Bobeck (Kathy Kinney).
