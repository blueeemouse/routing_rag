Roger Price (television producer)

Roger Damon Price (born 1941) is a retired British television producer, who was also active in North America.

 He created the children's science fiction series "The Tomorrow People", "Junior Points of View", the American sketch comedy "Don't Look Now", the pilot episode "UFO Kidnapped", the teen sketch comedy "Turkey Television" and the Canadian sketch comedy "You Can't Do That on Television", which became hugely successful on Nickelodeon in the United States.

 He also produced the British children's television programmes, "You Must Be Joking" and "Pauline's Quirkes", both of which also featured the band Flintlock.
