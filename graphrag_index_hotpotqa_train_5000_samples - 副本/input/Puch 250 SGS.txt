Puch 250 SGS

The Puch 250 SGS (Schwing-Gabel-Sport) was a motorcycle manufactured by the Austrian Steyr Daimler Puch AG in Thondorf near Graz.

 The motorcycle was powered by a split-single two-stroke engine (two pistons sharing a single combustion chamber).

 It was marketed in the United States by Sears as the "Allstate 250" or "Twingle", and sold primarily via the Sears catalog.

 It was a common "first motorcycle" for many riders.
