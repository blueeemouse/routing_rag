MBK Entertainment

MBK Entertainment (Hangul: MBK 엔터테인먼트), is a South Korean entertainment company established in 2007 by Kim Kwang-soo.

 The label was formerly known as Core Contents Media, established on January 9, 2007 as the entertainment division of CJ E&M Music Performance Division created after the acquisition of Poibos (in July 2006), MAXMP3 and Mediopia (in September 2006).
