Chevrolet Lakewood

The Chevrolet Lakewood is a 4 door station wagon produced by Chevrolet in the 1961 and 1962 model years.

 It was the station wagon version of the Corvair and therefore the entry level station wagon series.

 In size, appearance, and technical respects it resembled the Volkswagen Type 3 Squareback, and power came from the Corvair's rear engine with 146 cu.in.

 displacement which developed 80 bhp @ 4,400 rpm.

 Like the Corvair it had a 108 in.

 wheelbase and was equipped with a 3 speed manually shifted gearbox.
