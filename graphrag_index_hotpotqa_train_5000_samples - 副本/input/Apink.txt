Apink

Apink (Korean: 에이핑크 , Japanese: エーピンク ; also written as A Pink) is a South Korean girl group formed by Plan A Entertainment (formerly A Cube Entertainment) in 2011.

 The group consists of Park Cho-rong, Yoon Bo-mi, Jung Eun-ji, Son Na-eun, Kim Nam-joo and Oh Ha-young.

 Hong Yoo-kyung left the group in April 2013 to focus on her studies.
