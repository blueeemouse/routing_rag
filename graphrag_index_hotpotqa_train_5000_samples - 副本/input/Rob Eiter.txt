Rob Eiter

Rob Eiter (born September 12, 1967 in Chicago, Illinois) is an American Olympic wrestler.

 He competed in the 1996 Summer Olympics in Atlanta, Georgia, where he wrestled in the 48 kilogram (105 pound) weight class.

 He wrestled for Sunkist Wrestling Club and attended Arizona State University, where he wrestled under Bobby Douglass.

 He became head wrestling coach of the University of Pennsylvania in 2008.

 He is currently the assistant wrestling coach at the University of Maryland.
