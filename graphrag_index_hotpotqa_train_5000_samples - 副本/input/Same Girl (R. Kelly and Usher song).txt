Same Girl (R. Kelly and Usher song)

"Same Girl" is a song released by R&B singer R. Kelly, from his 2007 album "Double Up", with R&B singer, Usher.

 The song was recorded over a year by upcoming R&B group Nephu.

 R. Kelly and Usher's version leaked to the Internet on May 2, 2007 and later leaked to radio stations.

 This song was number 26 on "Rolling Stone"' s list of the 100 Best Songs of 2007.
