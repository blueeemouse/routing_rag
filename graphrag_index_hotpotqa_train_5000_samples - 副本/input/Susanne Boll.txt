Susanne Boll

Susanne Boll-Westermann is a Professor for Multimedia and Internet in the Department of Computing Science at the University of Oldenburg, Germany.

 She is the vice dean for computer science in the school of computing science, business administration, economics, and law.

 Her main research interests are semantic information retrieval, intelligent user-interfaces and mobile systems.

 Susanne Boll received her PhD in 2001 from the Vienna University of Technology with distinction.

 She graduated in Computer Science (Diploma with distinction) from the Technical University of Darmstadt.

 She contributed to about 200 scientific papers and organized a number of conferences and workshops.

 Susanne Boll is an active member of SIGMM of the ACM and is a member of the board at the research institute OFFIS.
