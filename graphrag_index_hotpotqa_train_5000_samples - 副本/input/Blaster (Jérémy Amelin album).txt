Blaster (Jérémy Amelin album)

Blaster is the unreleased debut studio album of French electropop recording artist and entertainer Jérémy Amelin, finalist of French music TV show "Star Academy 5" - French version of American Idol broadcast on French TV channel TF1.

 The album was originally scheduled to be released on October 24, 2011, but X-Cite Records cancelled the album release.
