Bruce Scott (boxer)

Bruce "Lionheart" Scott ( (1969--) 16 1969 (age (2017)-(1969)-((11)<(08)or(11)==(08)and(30)<(16)) ) ) is a Jamaican/British professional light heavy/cruiserweight boxer of the 1990s and 2000s, who won the British Boxing Board of Control (BBBofC) Southern Area cruiserweight title, BBBofC British cruiserweight title, and Commonwealth cruiserweight title (twice), and was a challenger for the Commonwealth light heavyweight title against Nicky Piper, World Boxing Organization (WBO) cruiserweight title against Johnny Nelson, World Boxing Council (WBC) cruiserweight title against Juan Carlos Gómez, Commonwealth cruiserweight title against Adam Watt, and World Boxing Union (WBU) cruiserweight title against Enzo Maccarinelli, his professional fighting weight varied from 173 lb , i.e. light heavyweight to 199+1/4 lb , i.e. cruiserweight.
