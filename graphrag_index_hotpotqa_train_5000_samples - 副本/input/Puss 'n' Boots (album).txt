Puss 'n' Boots (album)

Puss 'N' Boots is the sixth studio album by Crash Test Dummies, released in 2003.

 The album began life as a Brad Roberts solo project.

 While the lyrics were written by Brad Roberts, most of the music was written by Stuart Cameron.

 Ellen Reid sang backing vocals and Dan Roberts played bass, though much of the music was performed by other musicians.
