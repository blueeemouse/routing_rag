Ethna Carbery

Ethna Carbery (born Anna Johnston, 3 December 1864 – 2 April 1902) was an Irish journalist, writer and poet.

 She is best known for the ballad "Roddy McCorley" and the "Song of Ciabhán"; the latter was set to music by Ivor Gurney.

 Along with Alice Milligan she published two Irish nationalist magazines.
