Endless Mic

Endless Mic is a Chapel Hill-based hip hop group composed of Zano, Stu!

, and Deez.

 They have released one album, titled "Cave Livin'" on the small independent Trekky Records.

 They have performed the throughout the state of North Carolina and have performed with both RJD2 and One Be Lo.

 They have relocated to Greensboro and are finalizing their follow-up album, "Baby Geniuses".
