Challenge Tour

The Challenge Tour is the second-tier men's professional golf tour in Europe.

 It is operated by the PGA European Tour and, as with on the main European Tour and the European Senior Tour, some of the events are played outside Europe.

 World ranking points are awarded for high finishes in Challenge Tour events.

 Most Challenge Tour events give 12 OWGR points to the winner, with those co-sanctioned by the European Tour earning 18 points.

 The Challenge Tour Grand Final gives 17 points to the winner.
