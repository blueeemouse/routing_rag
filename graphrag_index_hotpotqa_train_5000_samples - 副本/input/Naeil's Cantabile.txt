Naeil's Cantabile

Naeil's Cantabile () is a South Korean television series adapted from the Japanese manga "Nodame Cantabile" by Tomoko Ninomiya.

 Starring Joo Won and Shim Eun-kyung with Park Bo-gum, Go Kyung-pyo and Baek Yoon-sik, it aired on KBS2 from October 13 to December 2, 2014 for 16 episodes.
