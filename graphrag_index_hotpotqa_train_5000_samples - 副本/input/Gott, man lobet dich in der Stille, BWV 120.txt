Gott, man lobet dich in der Stille, BWV 120

Gott, man lobet dich in der Stille (God, You are praised in the stillness), BWV 120 , is a sacred cantata by Johann Sebastian Bach.

 He composed it in Leipzig for the occasion of "Ratswechsel ", the inauguration of a new town council in a church service, probably in 1742.

 Parts of the cantata appeared in a wedding cantata (BWV 120a ) and a cantata (BWV 120b ) commemorating the Augsburg Confession in 1730.

 Bach reworked the choral second movement for the "Symbolum Nicenum" of his Mass in B minor.
