SS Principessa Jolanda (1907)

The SS Principessa Jolanda was an Italian transatlantic ocean liner built by Cantiere Navale di Riva Trigoso for the Navigazione Generale Italiana (NGI) shipping company.

 Named after Princess Iolanda Margherita di Savoia, the eldest daughter of King Victor Emmanuel III, the ship was intended for the NGI's South American service.

 At 9,210 tons and 141 m (463 ft) in length, she was the largest passenger ship built in Italy up to that time.

 Constructed at a cost of 6 million lire to designs by Erasmo Piaggio, the "Principessa Jolanda" has also been called the first true Italian luxury liner.

 She was among the first transatlantic vessels fitted with Marconi Wireless telegraphy, electric lighting throughout and telephones in each cabin.
