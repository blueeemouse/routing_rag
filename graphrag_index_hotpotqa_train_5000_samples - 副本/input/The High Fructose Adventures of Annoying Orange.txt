The High Fructose Adventures of Annoying Orange

The High Fructose Adventures of Annoying Orange is an American live-action/animated TV series based on the characters from the popular web series "The Annoying Orange", created by Dane Boedigheimer and Spencer Grove, which is made for the television by Boedigheimer himself with Tom Sheppard for Cartoon Network.

 It was produced by Daneboe's company Annoying Orange, Inc., alongside management company The Collective and the studios 14th Hour Productions.

 A preview aired on May 28, 2012, and the official premiere was on June 11, 2012.

 The show is rated TV-PG in the United States, rated PG in Canada, and rated G in Australia.
