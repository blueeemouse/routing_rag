Straftanz

Straftanz was a German electronic dance music project, established in the summer of 2006.

 Straftanz consisted of a loose group of artists (j-ing, k-x, -jl- and cyberdominator) who called themselves the Straftanz ZK.

 Additional artists involved were Carsten Jacek (SITD), Mille Petrozza, and Myk Jung (The Fair Sex) who were involved in the recording of the first album "Forward Ever".

 Straftanz also collaborated with the futurepop project Rotersand by way of sharing ideas and equipment.

 In interviews, Straftanz described its musical style as "Industrial-Streetfighting-Dance".

 In fact, the sound of Straftanz is best described as a mixture of EBM, futurepop, trance, rave, metal, and rhythm noise elements that are tied together with a present dance groove.
