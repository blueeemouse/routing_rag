Caldwell County Courthouse Historic District

The Caldwell County Courthouse Historic District is a historic district located in Lockhart, Texas, the seat of Caldwell County.

 The historic district encompasses 67 buildings on 250 acre across downtown Lockhart.

 One building included in the historic district, the Emmanuel Episcopal Church, was previously listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1974.

 The historic district was added to the National Register of Historic Places on January 3, 1978.
