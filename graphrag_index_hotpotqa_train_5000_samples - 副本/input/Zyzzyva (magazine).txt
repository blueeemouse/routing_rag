Zyzzyva (magazine)

Zyzzyva is a triannual magazine of writers and artists.

 It places an emphasis on showcasing emerging voices and never before published writers in addition to the already established.

 Based in San Francisco, it began publishing in 1985.

 "ZYZZYVA"'s slogan is "The Last Word," referring to "zyzzyva", the last word in the American Heritage Dictionary.

 A zyzzyva is an American weevil.

 The accent is on the first syllable.
