The Little Drummer Girl

The Little Drummer Girl is a spy novel by John le Carré, published in 1983.

 The story follows the manipulations of Martin Kurtz, an Israeli spymaster who intends to kill Khalil, a Palestinian terrorist who is bombing Jewish-related targets in Europe, particularly Germany, and Charlie, an English actress and double agent working on behalf of the Israelis.

 Despite the plot, some reviewers thought it transcends the spy novel genre. "

"The Little Drummer Girl" is about spies", said William F. Buckley, writing in "The New York Times", "as "Madame Bovary" is about adultery or "Crime and Punishment" about crime."
