White Limozeen (song)

"White Limozeen" is a song co-written and recorded by American country music artist Dolly Parton.

 It was released in May 1990 as the fifth single and title track from the album "White Limozeen".

 The song reached #29 on the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles & Tracks chart.

 The song was written by Parton and Mac Davis.
