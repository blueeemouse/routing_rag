Skiatook Lake

Skiatook Lake is a federally-owned and operated reservoir located in Osage County, Oklahoma, 4 mi west of the town of Skiatook, 11 mi east of Hominy, Oklahoma and about 18 mi from Tulsa.

 The Skiatook Dam is located on Hominy Creek, 14 miles upstream of the confluence of Hominy and Bird Creeks.

 The project purposes include flood control, water quality, water supply, recreation, and fish and wildlife management.

 The cities of Skiatook, Sand Springs, Sapulpa, and Tulsa use Skiatook Lake as a municipal water supply.

 Gentle rolling hills of Blackjack and Post Oak interspersed with Tallgrass prairie surround the lake.

 The lake is under the management of the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Tulsa District.
