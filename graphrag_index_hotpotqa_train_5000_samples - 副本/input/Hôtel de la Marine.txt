Hôtel de la Marine

The hôtel de la Marine (also known as the hôtel du Garde-Meuble) is a building on place de la Concorde in Paris, to the east of Rue Royale.

 It was built between 1757 and 1774 on what was then known as "place Louis XV", with a façade by Ange-Jacques Gabriel, Premier architecte du Roi and designer of the square.

 The identical building to its west now houses the Hôtel de Crillon.
