Uh Huh (B2K song)

"Uh Huh" is the first single by R&B group B2K, from their self-titled debut album.

 The song was released in July 2001 and it peaked at number 37 on the "Billboard" Hot 100 and number 20 on the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs.

 It also peaked at number 35 in the UK on its first entry and reached a new peak at number 31 on a re-release.
