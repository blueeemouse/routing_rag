Rizvan Geliskhanov

Rizvan Geliskhanov (Russian: Ризван Гелисханов , born 1963) is a retired Soviet super-heavyweight weightlifter of Chechen origin.

 He won a silver medal at the 1989 World Championships and a bronze at the 1990 European Championships.

 Domestically he held the Soviet title in 1987; in 1988 he won the snatch and clean and jerk events, but placed second in the total.
