Joint Task Force 2

Joint Task Force 2 (JTF2) (French: "Force opérationnelle interarmées 2, FOI 2" ) is an elite special operations force of the Canadian Armed Forces.

 JTF2 serves alongside the Canadian Special Operations Regiment, the Canadian Joint Incident Response Unit and 427 Special Operations Aviation Squadron as part of the Canadian Special Operations Forces Command.

 The unit is currently based at Dwyer Hill, near Ottawa, Ontario, and plans to move to a new facility to be built at CFB Trenton by 2019.
