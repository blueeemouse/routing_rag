Rob &amp; Big

Rob & Big is an American reality television series that follows the lives of professional skateboarder, actor, producer Rob Dyrdek and his best friend and bodyguard Christopher "Big Black" Boykin.

 It premiered on November 4, 2006, and concluded on April 15, 2008, after three seasons.

 Reruns of the series air on the MTV sister network, MTV2.

 The series was followed-up with "Fantasy Factory".
