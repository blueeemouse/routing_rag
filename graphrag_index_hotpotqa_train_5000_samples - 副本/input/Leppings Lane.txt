Leppings Lane

Leppings Lane is a street in Hillsborough, Sheffield.

 It provides a link between the A61 Penistone Road and the A6102 Middlewood Road.

 It has a Supertram stop, and gives its name to the away supporters' stand at Hillsborough Stadium, the home of Sheffield Wednesday F.C..

 Extra trams serve Leppings Lane on matchdays.
