No Better

"No Better" is a song by New Zealand singer Lorde, taken from the extended version of her debut studio album "Pure Heroine" (2013).

 The track was released on 13 December 2013 by Universal Music Group as a promotional tool for the album.

 "No Better" is an electropop and trip hop ballad with elements of hip hop, in which Lorde discusses an infatuation.

 Music critics opined that the single has similar musical style to songs from "Pure Heroine".
