Garrick Theatre (Leman St)

The Garrick Theatre, also known as "Garrick's Subscription" was a small theatre located in Leman St, Whitechapel.

 The theatre opened in 1831, and closed in about 1881.

 The theatre was named for the actor, David Garrick, who had made his début at the nearby Goodman's Fields Theatre on 9 October 1741, playing the role of "Richard III".
