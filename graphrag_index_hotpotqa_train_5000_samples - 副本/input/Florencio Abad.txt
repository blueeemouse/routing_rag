Florencio Abad

Florencio Barsana Abad (born July 13, 1954) is a Filipino lawyer and politician.

 Having held many cabinet-level ranks in the past, he was appointed by President Benigno Aquino III as Secretary of the Philippine Department of Budget and Management.

 Abad held various cabinet-level positions in the past, particularly as Secretary of the Department of Education and Secretary of the Department of Agrarian Reform.
