Loïc Rémy

Loïc Rémy (born 2 January 1987) is a French professional footballer who plays as a forward for Spanish side Las Palmas of La Liga.

 He was described by his former manager Didier Deschamps as "a young international who is fast and powerful with huge potential".

 He was sent out on loan to Crystal Palace on 30 August 2016 but returned almost immediately on 10 September 2016 to Chelsea after sustaining an ankle injury.
