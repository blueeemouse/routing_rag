Vladimir Antipin

Vladimir Yurevich Antipin (Russian: Влади́мир Ю́рьевич Анти́пин ; born April 18, 1970) is a former Kazakhstani professional ice hockey defenceman who participated at the 2010 IIHF World Championship and the 2006 Winter Olympics as a member of the Kazakhstan men's national ice hockey team.

 On September 15, 2012, Vladmir's wife was killed in an automobile accident in rural Kazakhstan.

 His son Viktor Antipin is also hockey player, but plays for Russia men's national ice hockey team.

 He is currently an ice hockey coach.
