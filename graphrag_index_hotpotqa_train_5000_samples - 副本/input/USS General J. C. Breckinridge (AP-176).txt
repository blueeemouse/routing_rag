USS General J. C. Breckinridge (AP-176)

USS "General J. C. Breckinridge" (AP-176) was a troopship that served with the United States Navy in World War II, the Korean War and the Vietnam War.

 In October 1949 she was redesignated T-AP-176 but retained her Navy crew.

 Her namesake was Confederate Major General John C. Breckinridge who served as Vice President of the United States from 1857 to 1861.
