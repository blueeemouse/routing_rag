Soundtrack from Twin Peaks

Soundtrack from Twin Peaks (also known as Music from Twin Peaks) is a soundtrack album by the American composer Angelo Badalamenti.

 It was released on September 11, 1990 on Warner Bros.

 Records and is the official soundtrack to the television series "Twin Peaks" (1990–91).

 The series' co-creator David Lynch produced the album alongside Badalamenti and wrote lyrics for several tracks, including the series theme song "Falling".
