Ravenala

Ravenala is a genus of flowering plants with a single species, Ravenala madagascariensis, commonly known as traveller's tree or traveller's palm, from Madagascar.

 It is not a true palm (family Arecaceae) but a member of a monocotyledonous flowering plant family, Strelitziaceae.

 The genus is closely related to the southern African genus "Strelitzia" and the South American genus "Phenakospermum".

 Some older classifications include these genera in the banana family (Musaceae).

 Although it is usually considered to be a single species, four different forms have been distinguished.
