Kickboxer: Vengeance

Kickboxer: Vengeance is a 2016 American martial arts film directed by John Stockwell, and starring Alain Moussi, Jean-Claude Van Damme (who played Kurt Sloane in the original "Kickboxer" film series), Dave Bautista, Gina Carano, Georges St-Pierre and Darren Shahlavi (in his final film role).

 It is a reboot of the original series, and has a similar premise as the first film in the franchise.

 The film was released in select theaters and Video on Demand on September 2, 2016, by RLJ Entertainment.
