Georg von Tiesenhausen

Georg F. von Tiesenhausen (born May 18, 1914) is a retired German-American rocket scientist.

 After being brought to the United States in 1953 as part of Operation Paperclip, he was part of Wernher von Braun's team at the U.S. Army, and later, NASA.

 He is credited with the first complete design of the Lunar Roving Vehicle and made a variety of other contributions to the space program.
