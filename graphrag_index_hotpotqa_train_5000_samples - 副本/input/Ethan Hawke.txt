Ethan Hawke

Ethan Green Hawke (born November 6, 1970) is an American actor, writer, and director.

 He has been nominated for four Academy Awards and a Tony Award.

 Hawke has directed two feature films, three Off-Broadway plays, and a documentary, and written the novels "The Hottest State" (1996), "Ash Wednesday" (2002), and "Rules for a Knight" (2015).
