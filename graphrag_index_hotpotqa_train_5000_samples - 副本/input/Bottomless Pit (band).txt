Bottomless Pit (band)

Bottomless Pit is an indie rock band from Chicago.

 Guitarist Andy Cohen and baritone guitarist Tim Midyett, both formerly of Silkworm, formed the band with drummer Chris Manfrin of Seam and bassist Brian Orchard of .22 in 2005.

 Since inception the group has issued three critically acclaimed records and toured in the United States and Canada.

 The band also played one show in Paris to open the Villette Sonique Festival in June 2008 at the invitation of fellow Chicago-based rock trio Shellac.
