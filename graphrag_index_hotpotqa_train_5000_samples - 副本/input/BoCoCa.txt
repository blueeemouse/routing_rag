BoCoCa

BoCoCa is a portmanteau word combining the names of three adjacent neighborhoods in the Brooklyn borough of New York City: Boerum Hill, Cobble Hill, and Carroll Gardens.

 BoCoCa is not an actual neighborhood, but an umbrella term for the small region of Brooklyn directly south and southwest of Downtown Brooklyn.

 The relatively new name is not widely used, but is gaining greater currency, even outside the United States.

 Among the publications using BoCoCa to classify neighborhoods are the Not for Tourists guide and "New York" magazine, both of which list BoCoCa together with neighboring Red Hook.
