The Vanishing Duck

The Vanishing Duck is a 1958 one reel animated "Tom and Jerry" short, directed and produced by William Hanna and Joseph Barbera with music by Scott Bradley.

 The cartoon was animated by Lewis Marshall, Kenneth Muse, Carlo Vinci and James Escalante, with backgrounds by Robert Gentle and layouts by Richard Bickenbach.

 Red Coffey, June Foray and George O'Hanlon provided the voices for this film.

 It was released on May 2, 1958 by Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer and marks the final appearance of Quacker, who appeared in seven previous "Tom and Jerry" shorts.

 As such, "The Vanishing Duck" is the antepenultimate "Tom and Jerry" short of the Hanna and Barbera era.

 O'Hanlon would go on to star as the voice of George Jetson on the ABC-TV animated series, "The Jetsons," also produced by Hanna-Barbera four years later.

 The cartoon has a similar premise to an earlier film, The Invisible Mouse, released in 1947.
