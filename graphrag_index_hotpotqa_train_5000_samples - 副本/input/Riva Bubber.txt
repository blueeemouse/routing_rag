Riva Bubber

Riva Bubber is an Indian television actress.

 She made her debut with the role of Nikita in Kyun Hota Hai Pyarrr.

 Afterwards, she joined Kyunki Saas Bhi Kabhi Bahu Thi, where she played the role of the oldest Virani bahu, Damini.

 She also played the role of Shabana Ghulam Haider in Beintehaa
