Independent Wrestling Federation

The Independent Wrestling Federation (IWF) is a professional wrestling promotion based in Nutley, New Jersey.

 The IWF features upcoming talent, in some cases, which have made it to the World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE).

 Along with producing live events for the public, the IWF also gives youth clinics and acts as a wrestling school.

 The wrestling school trains teens and adults, and former WWE star Tito Santana is among the instructors.
