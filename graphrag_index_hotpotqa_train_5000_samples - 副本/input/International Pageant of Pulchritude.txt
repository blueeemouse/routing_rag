International Pageant of Pulchritude

The International Pageant of Pulchritude, also known as the "International Beauty Contest" or "Miss Universe", was a beauty contest that began in 1920, featuring contestants from multiple nations.

 This pageant originated the title "Miss Universe" and was the first international contest.

 The last pageant event in the USA was held in 1931 although additional "Miss Universe" events were held until 1935.

 This contest served as a model for modern contests that began after the war.
