Jeff F. King

Jeff F. King (sometimes credited simply as Jeff King) is a Canadian screenwriter, comic writer, television producer and film director.

 King served as co-executive (1994–95) and then executive producer (1995–96) for the Canadian television series "Due South" and was the co-recipient of three Gemini Awards.

 He then worked as the producer of the CBS show "EZ Streets" and as co-executive producer for "Stargate SG-1".

 His other television credits include "", "Models Inc.", "Relic Hunter", "Strange Days at Blake Holsey High", "Mutant X", "Dinosapien" and "White Collar".
