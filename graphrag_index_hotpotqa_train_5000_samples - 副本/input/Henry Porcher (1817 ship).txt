Henry Porcher (1817 ship)

Henry Porcher was launched in 1817 at Bristol, England.

 Between 1818 and 1831 she made three voyages to India for the British East India Company (EIC).

 On the second she first transported convicts to Sydney, New South Wales.

 Between these voyages for the EIC "Henry Porcher" traded privately to India as a licensed ship.

 She made two further voyages as a convict transport, one to Sydney in 1834-35, and one to Hobart in 1836.

 She grounded in 1858 and was broken up in 1860.
