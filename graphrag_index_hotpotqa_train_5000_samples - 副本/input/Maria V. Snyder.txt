Maria V. Snyder

Maria V. Snyder is an American fantasy author best known for her "Study Series".

 Her first novel, "Poison Study", was published on October 2005, and won the 2006 Compton Crook Award for Best First Novel.

 the next two books in the series are "Magic Study" and "Fire Study".

 Snyder then released another trilogy of books, the "Glass" series, following the character Opal Cowan from the "Study Series".

 The books in the series are "Storm Glass", "Sea Glass" and "Spy Glass" respectively.

 On 24 February, Snyder went back to the main characters of the "Study Series" and released the first book of he next trilogy, the "Soulfinder Series", the first book of which is "Shadow Study".

 Aside from writing, her interests include "traveling, photography, making jewelry, and volleyball."
