Taiwan Supermodel No. 1

Taiwan Supermodel No. 1 ("決戰第一名 Supermodel No. 1") also known as Taiwan's Next Top Model is based on CW's hit reality TV show "America's Next Top Model", which was created by model Tyra Banks.

 "Taiwan Super Model No. 1" is the second Top Model franchise that features both genders.

 The first was Malaysia's "I Wanna Be A Model".
