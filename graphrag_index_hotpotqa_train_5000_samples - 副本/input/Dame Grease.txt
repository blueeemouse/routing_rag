Dame Grease

Damon Blackman (born August 9, 1974), better known by his stage name Dame Grease, is an American hip hop recording artist and record producer from Harlem, New York.

 Born and The Bronx, raised in Harlem he began his musical career as a producer and has since added rapper, record executive, creative director and fashion designer, to his repertoire.

 In 1996 he gained recognition in the hip hop industry, through his work with Bad Boy Records and The Lox.

 He also established himself as a prominent New York Producer while working with East Coast rapper DMX and the Ruff Ryders Entertainment record label.
