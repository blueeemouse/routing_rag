Kavin Dave

Kavin Dave (born 12 November 1984) is an Indian film and television actor.

 Dave made his big screen debut in the movie "Mumbai Meri Jaan" in 2008.

 This was followed by further successful films including "My Name Is Khan", "I Hate Luv Storys", "Crook", "Shirin Farhad Ki Toh Nikal Padi" and "Kick".

 Dave recently played the lead role in the film "Bumboo".

 After featuring in several television commercials for popular brands including Sprite, Vodafone, Dish TV, Fortune oil, Sil jam and Center shock, he landed the lead role in the television sitcom "Rishta.com".

 Dave made his Tollywood debut with the Telugu film "Money Money, More Money".
