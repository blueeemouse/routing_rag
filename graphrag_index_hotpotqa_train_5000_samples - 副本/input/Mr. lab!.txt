Mr lab!

 is a French rock group, founded in Paris in 2002, by a French musician Yves Labbe, in whose honor and named group.

 The first EP "And now it time to go..." was recorded and released in 2005.

 The first EP is now released in Germany, Austria, Switzerland too, and the band had a lot of touring in France, Germany and Hungary.

 In 2007, the band began recording their second album "Post Industrial Ceremony", which included the group's most popular song "Anyway."

 This song later was heard in advertising for Nescafe.

 This album also featured the songs "Gravy machine" and "Would You like?"
