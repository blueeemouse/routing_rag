2017 Supercheap Auto Bathurst 1000

The 2017 Supercheap Auto Bathurst 1000 will be a motor racing event for Supercars, held on the weekend of 5 to 8 October 2017.

 The event will be held at the Mount Panorama Circuit near Bathurst, New South Wales, and will consist of one 1000 kilometre race.

 It is scheduled to be the eleventh event of fourteen in the 2017 Supercars Championship and will be Race 20 of the season.

 It is also the second event of the 2017 Enduro Cup.
