Monkeys, Go Home!

Monkeys, Go Home!

 is a 1967 Disney film directed by Andrew V. McLaglen.

 The movie stars Maurice Chevalier and Yvette Mimieux.

 Dean Jones plays Hank Dussard, the new owner of an olive grove, who brings in chimp labor, upsetting the other workers.

 Hank eventually gains the town's confidence with the kind aid of Father Sylvain and his neighbor Maria Riserau.
