Susan Mosher

Susan Mosher is an American stage and film actress.

 She starred as the Female Authority figure in the Vegas run of "Hairspray" and is best known for portraying Pepper Cole in the show "Cashino".

 She also was in the original cast of the Broadway show SUDS.

 Mosher is currently performing as the Female Authority Figure in Broadway's run of "Hairspray" and as an understudy for Velma Von Tussle for Isabel Keating to present.
