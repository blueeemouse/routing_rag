Seoul Olympic Park Tennis Center

Seoul Olympic Park Tennis Center is a tennis venue in Seoul, South Korea, located in the Olympic Park.

 It hosted the tennis events for the 1988 Summer Olympics and has hosted several South Korea Davis Cup team and South Korea Fed Cup team ties.

 The center currently hosts the Hansol Korea Open Tennis Championships.

 The main stadium has a capacity of 10,000 people.

 The No.1 court has a capacity of 3,500, and the other 12 courts have a capacity of 900.
