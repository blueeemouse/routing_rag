Jacob ben Ephraim

Jacob ben Ephraim (died in Lublin 1648) was a Polish rabbi.

 At first he occupied the post of rabbi and instructor at the yeshivah of that city, whence he was called to officiate as rabbi in Brest.

 There he entertained in 1631 R. Yom-Ṭob Lipman Heller, who speaks of him with great respect, and mentions his officiating as rabbi in the two cities cited ("Megillat Ebah," p. 28).

 From Brest he returned to Lublin as rabbi, and remained there till his death.
