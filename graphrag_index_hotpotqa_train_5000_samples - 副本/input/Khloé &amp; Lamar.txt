Khloé &amp; Lamar

Khloé & Lamar is an American reality television series that debuted on E! in the United States and Canada on April 10, 2011.

 The series is the third spin-off of the show "Keeping Up with the Kardashians" and features reality star Khloé Kardashian and her then-husband, basketball player Lamar Odom.

 The series returned for its second and final season on February 19, 2012.
