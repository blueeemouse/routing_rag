Cohousing

Cohousing is an intentional community of private homes clustered around shared space.

 Each attached or single family home has traditional amenities, including a private kitchen.

 Shared spaces typically feature a common house, which may include a large kitchen and dining area, laundry, and recreational spaces.

 Shared outdoor space may include parking, walkways, open space, and gardens.

 Neighbors also share resources like tools and lawnmowers.
