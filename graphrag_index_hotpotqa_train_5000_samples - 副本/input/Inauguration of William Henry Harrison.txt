Inauguration of William Henry Harrison

The inauguration of William Henry Harrison as the ninth President of the United States was held on Thursday, March 4, 1841, on the East Portico of the United States Capitol in Washington, D.C..

 The inauguration marked the commencement of William Henry Harrison's only term as President and John Tyler's only term as Vice President.

 The presidential oath of office was administered to Harrison by Supreme Court Chief Justice Roger B. Taney.

 Harrison died days into this term, and Tyler succeeded to the presidency.
