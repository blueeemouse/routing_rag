2017 Sun Belt Conference Baseball Tournament

The 2017 Sun Belt Conference Baseball Tournament will be held at J. I. Clements Stadium on the campus of Georgia Southern University in Statesboro, Georgia from May 24 to May 28, 2017.

 The tournament will again use a double-elimination format.

 The winner of the tournament will earn the Sun Belt Conference's automatic bid to the 2017 NCAA Division I Baseball Tournament.
