Laceby

Laceby is a village and civil parish in North East Lincolnshire, England.

 It is situated on the A46 road, just outside the western boundary of Grimsby.

 Laceby's population at the 2001 Census was 2,886, increasing to 3,259 at the 2011 Census.

 The village is noted for its parish church, parts of which date to the 12th century.
