John Mitchinson (researcher)

John Mitchinson is the head of research for the British television panel game "QI", and is also the managing director of Quite Interesting Limited.

 He is co-writer of the "QI" series of books with the show's creator John Lloyd.

 The two men are normally referred to as "The Two Johns" and are seen as the main controllers of "QI", as they do most of the research of the show.

 His most recent work, "1,411 Quite Interesting Facts to Knock You Sideways", a collaboration with John Lloyd and James Harkin, will be released in 2015 with W.W. Norton and Company.
