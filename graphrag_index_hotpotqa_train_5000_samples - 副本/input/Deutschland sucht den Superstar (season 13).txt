Deutschland sucht den Superstar (season 13)

During the live-shows of season 12, it was announced that the castings for season 13 will start in August 2015.

 In Fall 2014, RTL gave closer details to the changes of Season 13.

 The liveshows are replaced with pre-recorded concerts in clubs in Germany.

 Only the announcement of the results will be broadcast live.

 Like the season before the final will not take place in a TV studio in Cologne, but in the ISS Dome in Düsseldorf.
