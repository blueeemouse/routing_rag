I Could Break Your Heart Any Day of the Week

"I Could Break Your Heart Any Day of the Week" is a song by American recording artist Mandy Moore from her sixth studio album, "Amanda Leigh".

 It was released on June 15, 2009 by Storefront Recording as the lead single of the album.

 The song was written by Moore and Mike Viola.

 Its lyrics are based on a girl who is holding a boy's love, which she says she could break his heart any day of the week.
