Rongmei Naga

The Rongmei (pronounced "Ruang-mei") are a major Naga tribe indigenous to Assam, Manipur and Nagaland in North-East India.

 The Rongmei Naga are a scheduled tribe under the Constitution of India.

 Like any other Naga tribe, the Rongmei have their own rich culture, customs, and traditions.

 The Gaan-Ngai festival (post-harvest festival) is celebrated annually between December and January.

 Among Naga tribes, they are known for their colorful dances and exquisite traditional attire.
