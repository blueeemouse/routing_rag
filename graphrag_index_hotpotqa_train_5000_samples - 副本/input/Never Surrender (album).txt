Never Surrender (album)

Never Surrender is the sixth studio album by Canadian hard rock band Triumph, released in 1982.

 The album reached #26 on the Billboard Albums chart assisted by the singles "All the Way", "A World of Fantasy" and "Never Surrender" which hit #2, #3 and #23, respectively, on the Billboard's Mainstream Top Rock Tracks chart in 1983.

 Interestingly, "All the Way" (the third of the three tracks to chart), was Triumph's highest charting song on the Top Rock Tracks chart, but did not sustain that level of popularity with Triumph fans as the song is not included on their 1985 live album "Stages", the later "Classics" (a Greatest Hits album) or 2005's "Livin' for the Weekend: The Anthology" album.
