Saint-Jean-Chrysostome, Lévis, Quebec

Saint-Jean-Chrysostome is a sector within the Les Chutes-de-la-Chaudière-Est borough of the city of Lévis in central Quebec, Canada south of Quebec City on the south bank of the Saint Lawrence River.

 It was a separate town, incorporated in 1828, but in 2002 was amalgamated into Lévis.
