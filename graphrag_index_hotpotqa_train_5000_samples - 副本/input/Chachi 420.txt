Chachi 420

Chachi 420 (English: The trickster aunt ) is a 1997 Indian Hindi comedy film, co-written, co-produced and directed by Kamal Hassan.

 The film is a remake of the Tamil film "Avvai Shanmughi" (released in the previous year) which was inspired from the Hollywood film "Mrs. Doubtfire" (1993).

 It was the first film directed by Kamal Haasan who also starred in the film playing double roles.

 The film also starred Tabu, Amrish Puri, Om Puri, Johnny Walker, Paresh Rawal, Rajendranath Zutshi, Ayesha Jhulka, Nassar and Baby Sana (Fatima Sana Shaikh).
