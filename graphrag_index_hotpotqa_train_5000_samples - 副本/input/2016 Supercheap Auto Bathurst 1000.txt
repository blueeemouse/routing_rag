2016 Supercheap Auto Bathurst 1000

The 2016 Supercheap Auto Bathurst 1000 was a motor racing event for Supercars, held on the weekend of 6 to 9 October 2016.

 The event was held at the Mount Panorama Circuit near Bathurst, New South Wales, and consisted of one race of 1000 kilometres in length.

 It was the eleventh event of fourteen in the 2016 International V8 Supercars Championship and hosted Race 21 of the season.

 It was also the second event of the 2016 Enduro Cup.

 The event marked ten years since the first awarding of the Peter Brock Trophy, which was introduced following the death of nine-time race winner Peter Brock in September 2006.
