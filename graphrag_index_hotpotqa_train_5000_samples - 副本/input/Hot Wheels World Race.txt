Hot Wheels World Race

Hot Wheels World Race is a computer-animated, feature-length made-for-TV movie based on the Hot Wheels television show and toy line made by Mattel.

 The film was distributed on DVD by Family Home Entertainment and distributed on TV by Warner Bros.

 Television.

 The video game "Hot Wheels World Race" was based on the television series film.

 It premiered on Cartoon Network on July 12, 2003 and was released on VHS and DVD on December 2, 2003.
