Ol' Red

"Ol' Red" is a song written by James "Bo" Bohan, Don Goodman and Mark Sherrill.

 The song was originally recorded by George Jones on his 1990 album "You Oughta Be Here with Me" and covered by Kenny Rogers on his 1993 album "If Only My Heart Had a Voice".

 Rogers' version was released as a single in August 1993.

 It was later recorded by artist Blake Shelton, and released in 2002 as the third and final single released from his self-titled debut album, becoming a top 20 hit.
