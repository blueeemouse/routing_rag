Stephanie Longfellow

Stephanie Longfellow was an American stage and film actress who appeared in ca. 40 silent films between 1909 and 1911.

 Longfellow was a popular ingenue for D.W. Griffith, and starred in 16 of his short films.

 She also starred in films of Mack Sennett and Frank Powell.

 In 1909, the Dubuque Telegraph-Herald called Longfellow a star of "singular beauty and charm."

 She was the grand-niece of the poet Henry Wadsworth Longfellow.
