Jonathan Freeman (cinematographer)

Jonathan Freeman, ASC is a Canadian-American cinematographer.

 A multi-award-winning director of photography for motion pictures, television and commercials, he is best known for his work on "Game of Thrones" and "Boardwalk Empire".

 Freeman’s motion picture credits include "Remember Me", "Fifty Dead Men Walking", "The Edge of Love", "Hollywoodland" and "The Prize Winner of Defiance, Ohio".
