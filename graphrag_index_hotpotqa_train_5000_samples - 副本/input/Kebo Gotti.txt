Kebo Gotti

Gotti appeared on the Waka Flocka Flame single ,"Grove St. Party", the fourth and final single from Flame's debut album, "Flockaveli" (2010).

 The music video for "Grove St. Party" includes Kebo, Gucci Mane and YC.

 On February 27, 2011, "Grove St. Party" entered at #38 on the U.S. "Billboard" R&B/Hip-Hop Tracks chart, peaking at #12.

 It also climbed the "Billboard" Rap Songs charts, reaching #10, and on the U.S. "Billboard" Hot 100 at #74.
