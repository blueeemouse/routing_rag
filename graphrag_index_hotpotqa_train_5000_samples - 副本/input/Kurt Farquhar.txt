Kurt Farquhar

Kurt Farquhar is a Los Angeles based–television and film composer.

 Farquhar is a seven-time BMI award winner, including four for "The King of Queens".

 one for The Game and two for Being Mary Jane.

 He is best known for composing the scores for "The King of Queens", "Girlfriends", "Sister, Sister", "Moesha", "Being Mary Jane", "The Game", "Black Lightning", and "Real Husbands of Hollywood".

 Farquhar's career is notable for having scored more primetime television series than any other African-American composer to date.
