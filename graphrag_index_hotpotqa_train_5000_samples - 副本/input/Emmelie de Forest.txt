Emmelie de Forest

Emmelie Charlotte-Victoria de Forest (born 28 February 1993) is a Danish singer and songwriter.

 She was born in Randers, Denmark, and raised in Mariager, Denmark, and in Stockholm, Sweden.

 Emmelie de Forest is of partial Swedish descent as her father is Swedish.

 She represented Denmark with the song "Only Teardrops" in the Eurovision Song Contest 2013 in Malmö, Sweden, winning the contest.

 Her debut album "Only Teardrops" was released on the Universal Music label on 6 May 2013.

 She participated in the Eurovision Song Contest 2017 as the songwriter for the song "Never Give Up On You" representing the United Kingdom.
