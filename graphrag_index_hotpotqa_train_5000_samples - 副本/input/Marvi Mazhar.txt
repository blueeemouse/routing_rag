Marvi Mazhar

Marvi Mazhar (Sindhi: ماروي مظهر ‎ ) was a member of Pakistan Peoples Party in Sindh Assembly in 2003 to 2008.

 She was elected from PS-74 Dadu-IV in Dadu, Sindh, Pakistan.

 Marvi Mazhar is the daughter of Pir Mazhar Ul Haq the parliamentary leader of Pakistan Peoples Party in Sindh Assembly and present Senior Minister with the Portfolio of Education in the Sindh Cabinet.

 She is the great grand daughter of former Chief Minister of Sindh, Pir Ilahi Bux, and of Qazi Akbar, long serving Sindh Provincial Minister.

 She is the great niece of Dr. Fahmida Mirza, the ex-Speaker of the National Assembly of Pakistan and great grand niece of ex-Federal Minister Qazi Abdul Majeed Abid.

 She was a member of Sindh Assembly's Standing Committee on Education.
