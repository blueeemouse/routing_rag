Stay (Wasting Time)

"Stay (Wasting Time)" is a song by the Dave Matthews Band, released as the second single off their album "Before These Crowded Streets".

 As a single, it reached #8 on the Modern Rock Tracks chart, #33 on the Top 40 Mainstream, and #20 on the Adult 40.

 The song features The Lovely Ladies (Tawatha Agee, Cindy Myzell, and Brenda White King) on background vocals.
