Robin Houston

Robin Houston (born 1948?)

 is a British former newsreader and game show host.

 He has read the news for both LWT and Thames Television and has fronted both 100% and One to Win for Channel 5 and has provided the voiceover for All Star Secrets, Bruce Forsyth's Big Night, Child's Play, Game for a Laugh, Newlyweds, the 1980s version of Play Your Cards Right, Punchlines, the Challenge TV version of Sale of the Century, Strike It Lucky, Tell the Truth, 100%, The Pyramid Game and We Love TV.
