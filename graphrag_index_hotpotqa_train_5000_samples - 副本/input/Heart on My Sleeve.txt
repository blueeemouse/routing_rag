Heart on My Sleeve

"Heart on My Sleeve" is a song written by James Morrison and John Shanks.

 It was first released by "American Idol" season 7 contestant Michael Johns from his post-"Idol" album, "Hold Back My Heart".

 The single was released on iTunes, on 14 April 2009.

 The song was later recorded by Olly Murs and released on 6 March 2011 as his third single from his debut album "Olly Murs".
