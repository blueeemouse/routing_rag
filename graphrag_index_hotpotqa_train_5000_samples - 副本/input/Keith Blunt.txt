Keith Blunt

Keith Blunt (c. 1939 – 12 August 2016) was an English football coach.

 He managed Sutton United from the late seventies until moving onto Malmö and then Viking in 1984.

 In 1987, he was coach of Tottenham Hotspur FC's youth team.

 In 1997, Blunt was head coach at the Centre of Excellence in Lilleshall in England.

 From 1998, he has worked as a football coach at various teams in China, including the national U-23 and U-19.
