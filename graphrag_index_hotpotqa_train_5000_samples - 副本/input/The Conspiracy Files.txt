The Conspiracy Files

The Conspiracy Files is a British documentary television series broadcast on BBC Two, investigating various modern-day conspiracy theories.

 So far in two series and 13 programmes, the show has investigated the theories surrounding the September 11 attacks (twice), the Pan Am Flight 103 bomb, the Oklahoma City bombing, the 7 July 2005 London bombings, the deaths of David Kelly and Diana, Princess of Wales, the Malaysian Airlines flight MH17, and those perpetrated by Donald Trump.
