Gangster (2014 film)

Gangster is a 2014 Malayalam gangster film directed by Aashiq Abu.

 Mammootty plays the lead role, along with Nyla Usha and Aparna Gopinath as the female leads.

 The film also features John Paul, T. G. Ravi, Sekhar Menon, Kunchan, Alexx O'Nell, Hareesh Peradi and Dileesh Pothan in supporting roles.

 The film was written by Ahmed Sidhique and Abhilash Kumar, with music by Deepak Dev.

 The film released on 11 April 2014.
