Guido Brunner

Guido Brunner (born 27 May 1930 in Madrid, died 2 December 1997) was a German diplomat and politician of the liberal Free Democratic Party.

 He served as European Commissioner for Energy, Research and Science in the Jenkins Commission from 1977 to 1981.

 He was a Member of the Bundestag from 1980 to 1981, Senator for the Economy in the government of West Berlin in 1981 and Ambassador to Spain from 1981 to 1992.

 Tam Dalyell described him as "one of the unsung architects of the Europe we have today."
