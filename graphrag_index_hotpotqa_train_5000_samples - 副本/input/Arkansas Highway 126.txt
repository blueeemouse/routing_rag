Arkansas Highway 126

Highway 126 (AR 126, Ark. 126, and Hwy.

 126) is a designation for two north–south state highways in Baxter County, Arkansas.

 A southern route of 11.66 mi runs from Buffalo City north to US Route 62/US Route 412 (US 62/US 412) near Mountain Home.

 A second route of 7.03 mi begins at US 62/US 412 in Gassville and runs north to Highway 5/Highway 178 at Midway.
