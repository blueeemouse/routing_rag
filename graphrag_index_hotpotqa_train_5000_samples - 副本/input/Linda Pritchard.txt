Linda Pritchard

Linda Norrgård Pritchard (born 19 May 1983) is a Swedish singer and professional dancer, who has worked with Magnus Uggla and Ace of Base.

 As a student she attended the Adolf Fredrik's Music School in Stockholm.

 She participated in "Idol 2008" where she got to the qualifying round but was then eliminated, although she was considered one of the favourites.

 During the summer of 2009, Pritchard participated in Allsång på Skansen as a dancer for Magnus Uggla and she also choreographed a video for Ace of Base.

 Pritchard also accompanied Ugglas on his revue tour.

 Pritchard's first single "Fast Car", originally recorded by Tracy Chapman, was included in the compilation album "Absolute Music 61".

 She had previously sung backing vocals for Celine Dion and for the Swedish singer Velvet.
