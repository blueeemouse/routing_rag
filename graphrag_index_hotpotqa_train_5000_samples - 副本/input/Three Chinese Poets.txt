Three Chinese Poets

Three Chinese Poets is a book of poetry by the titular poets Wang Wei, Li Bai and Du Fu translated into English by Vikram Seth.

 The Three Poets were contemporaries and are considered to be amongst the greatest Chinese poets by many later scholars.

 The three have been described as a Buddhist recluse, a Taoist immortal and a Confucian sage respectively.

 Though this trichotomy has been criticised as simplistic and artificial, it can act as a guiding approximation.

 They lived in the Tang Dynasty and the political strife at that time affected all of their lives very much and this impact is evident in the poetry of all three.
