5-7-9

5-7-9 is a low to mid-end fashion retailer, commonly found in malls throughout the continental United States and Puerto Rico, and formerly Hawaii.

 It is a sister company to Rainbow Shops.

 The chain offers mid to low-priced fashions for young teens and young women between 13 and 22 years of age.

 The store's name refers to the junior miss clothing sizes the store specializes in.
