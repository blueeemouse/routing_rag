Rino Romano

Rino Romano (born December 1, 1969) is a Canadian voice actor who has voiced Batman in the animated TV series "The Batman", Spider-Man in the animated TV series "Spider-Man Unlimited", Eduardo Rivera in "Extreme Ghostbusters", Luis Sera in "Resident Evil 4", Scorp in the "Skylanders" franchise" and the original Tuxedo Mask in the 90's English dub of the anime series "Sailor Moon".
