Akiva Grunblatt

Akiva Grunblatt is an Orthodox rabbi who as co-rosh yeshiva (dean) heads the Yeshivas Rabbeinu Yisrael Meir HaKohen.

 (Yeshivas Chofetz Chaim) in Flushing, New York.

 (The other co-rosh yeshiva is Rabbi Dovid Harris.)

 Before being appointed to his current position in 2000, he was a rosh yeshiva at Yeshiva Toras Chaim in Miami, Florida.
