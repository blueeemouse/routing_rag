Eli ben Yehudah ha-Nazir

Eli ben Yehudah ha Nazir ben Zechariah, Arabic Abu Kathir Yahya al Katib ("Father of Kathir, Yahya the Scribe" fl.

Tiberias, 910s) was a grammarian and philologist of the Hebrew, Arabic and Aramaic languages.

 He may have been among the teachers in Tiberias of Saadia.
