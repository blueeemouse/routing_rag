Placebo discography

The discography of Placebo, an English alternative rock band, consists of seven studio albums, three compilation albums, six extended plays, and thirty-one singles.

 Their self-titled debut album was released in 1996 and peaked at number five on the UK Albums Chart.

 A single from the album, "Nancy Boy", peaked at number four on the UK Singles Chart.

 Placebo's next studio album, 1998's "Without You I'm Nothing", peaked at number seven in both the UK and France.

 The singles "Pure Morning" and "You Don't Care About Us" both reached the top five on the UK Singles Chart.

 Those were Placebo's last singles to make the top five in the UK.
