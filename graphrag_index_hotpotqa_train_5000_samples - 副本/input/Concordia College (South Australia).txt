Concordia College (South Australia)

Concordia College is an independent and co-educational Lutheran school located in Adelaide, South Australia.

 Established in 1890, Concordia caters for around 1300 students from ELC to Year 12.

 The school has been an International Baccalaureate (IB) World School since January 2001 and offers the IB Primary Years, Middle Years and Diploma Programmes.

 "Concordia" is a Latin word meaning "harmony".
