2017–18 Houston Cougars men's basketball team

The 2017–18 Houston Cougars men's basketball team will represent the University of Houston during the 2017–18 NCAA Division I men's basketball season.

 The Cougars will be led by fourth-year head coach Kelvin Sampson as members of the American Athletic Conference.

 Due to renovations to the Cougars home arena, Hofheinz Pavilion, the Cougars will play their home games in the Toyota Center.
