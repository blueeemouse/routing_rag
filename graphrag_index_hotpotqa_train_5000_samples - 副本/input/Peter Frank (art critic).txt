Peter Frank (art critic)

Peter Frank (born 1950, New York) is an American art critic, curator, and poet who lives and works in Los Angeles.

 He was the Senior Curator at the Riverside Art Museum and an art critic for "Angeleno" magazine.

 He is a frequent contributor to "The Huffington Post".

 Until July 9, 2008, he was a long-time critic for "LA Weekly".

 He was a past editor of "Visions Art Quarterly" and was an art critic for "The Village Voice" and "The SoHo Weekly News" in New York.
