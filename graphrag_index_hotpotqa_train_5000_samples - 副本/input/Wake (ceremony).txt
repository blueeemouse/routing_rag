Wake (ceremony)

A wake is a social gathering associated with death, usually held before a funeral.

 Traditionally, a wake takes place in the house of the deceased with the body present; however, modern wakes are often performed at a funeral home or another convenient location.

 In the United States and Canada it is synonymous with a viewing.

 It is often a social rite that highlights the idea that the loss is one of a social group and affects that group as a whole.
