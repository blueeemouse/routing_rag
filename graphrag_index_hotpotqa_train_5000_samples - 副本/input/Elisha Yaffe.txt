Elisha Yaffe

Elisha Yaffe (born August 13, 1983) is a comedian, actor and writer.

 He has co-created webseries, including "Downers Grove", "Minor Stars" and "Remember When".

 He has appeared in commercials for Mike's Hard Lemonade, Time Warner Cable, Samsung, Wendy's, McDonald's, Nintendo 3DS and Hanes.

 He has appeared in television shows including Better Call Saul, The Newsroom, Mad Men, Southland, Up All Night, The New Normal and and regularly voices the character of Jimmy Olsen and B'dg on DC Nation Shorts's Tales of Metropolis and Super-Pets.
