St. Theresa's Cathedral, Changchun

St. Theresa's Cathedral of Changchun (in ) is a Roman Catholic cathedral in Changchun, Jilin Province, China.

 It is also called Changchun City Catholic Church or St. Theresa's Diocesan Cathedral () in Changchun because the bishop of Jilin Diocese moved here in 1994, from Sacred Heart of Jesus Cathedral of Jilin City.
