Hamayouni Decree

The Hamayoni Decree (also "Hamayonic", "Hamayouni") (Arabic: الخط الهمايونى‎ ‎ ) or "Hamayony Khat" is a clause in the Egyptian law that dates back to the Hatt-ı Hümayun of February 1856 issued during Ottoman rule, which regulates Christian church construction and maintenance.

 It is currently a cause of much controversy due to the conditions that need to be fulfilled in order for the permit to be granted.

 These same restrictions do not apply to mosques.

 The law formerly required that each permit must be issued by the President of Egypt.

 In 1998, under the administration of Hosni Mubarak, the law was changed to also allow Egyptian Governors to grant permits.
