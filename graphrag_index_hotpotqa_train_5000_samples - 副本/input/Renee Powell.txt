Renee Powell

Renee Powell (born May 4, 1946) is an American professional golfer who played on the U.S.-based LPGA Tour and is currently head professional at her family's Clearview Golf Club in East Canton, Ohio.

 She was the second African-American woman ever to play on the LPGA Tour.

 Althea Gibson was the first African-American LPGA Tour member, joining in 1963.

 Powell joined in 1967, and played on Tour from 1967 to 1980.

 It was not until 1995 that another African-American woman played on the tour.

 LaRee Sugg played on Tour from 1995 to 1997, and from 2000 to 2001.
