Nicotinamide

Nicotinamide (NAA), also known as niacinamide, is a vitamin found in food, used as a dietary supplement, and used as a medication.

 As a supplement, it is used by mouth to prevent and treat pellagra (niacin deficiency).

 While nicotinic acid (niacin) may be used for this purpose, nicotinamide has the benefit of not causing skin flushing.

 As a cream, it is used to treat acne.
