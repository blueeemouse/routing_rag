Erica Ash

Erica Ash (born September 19, 1977) is an American actress, comedian, singer and model.

 She was a cast member on the sketch comedy programs "MadTV" and "The Big Gay Sketch Show".

 Ash also has a recurring role as Bridgette Hart on the fictional reality series, "Real Husbands of Hollywood".

 And stars as Mary Charles "MChuck" Calloway on the STARZ hit series "Survivor's Remorse".
