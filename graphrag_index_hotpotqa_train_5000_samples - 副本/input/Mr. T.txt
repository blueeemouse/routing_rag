Mr. T

Mr. T (born Lawrence Tureaud; May 21, 1952) is an American actor and retired professional wrestler known for his roles as B. A. Baracus in the 1980s television series "The A-Team" and as boxer Clubber Lang in the 1982 film "Rocky III".

 Mr. T is known for his distinctive African Mandinka warrior hairstyle, his gold jewelry, and his tough-guy image.

 In 2006, he starred in "I Pity the Fool", a reality show shown on TV Land.

 The title of the show comes from the famous catchphrase used by his character, Clubber Lang.
