2017 PGA Tour Canada

The 2017 PGA Tour Canada season ran from June 1 to September 17 and consisted of 12 official golf tournaments.

 This was the 48th season of PGA Tour Canada (previously known as the Canadian Professional Golf Tour), and the fifth under the "PGA Tour Canada" name.

 It was also the third under the Mackenzie Tour – PGA Tour Canada name after Mackenzie Investments signed a six-year sponsorship deal.
