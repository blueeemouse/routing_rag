Ex on the Beach (series 4)

The fourth series of "Ex on the Beach", a British television programme, began airing on 19 January 2016 on MTV.

 The series concluded on 8 March 2016 after eight episodes.

 The group of cast members for this series include "Geordie Shore" star Scotty T, and "Judge Geordie" guest Lewis Good.

 Star of "Magaluf Weekender" Jordan Davies also made his return to the series having appeared during the third series, as well as Megan McKenna from the previous series.

 The series was filmed in Portugal.
