Nell Gwynn (1934 film)

Nell Gwynn is a 1934 British historical drama film directed by Herbert Wilcox and starring Anna Neagle, Cedric Hardwicke, Jeanne de Casalis, Miles Malleson and Moore Marriott.

 The film portrays the historical romance between Charles II of England and the actress Nell Gwynn.

 In the opening credits, the screenplay is attributed to Miles Malleson, "in collaboration with King Charles II, Samuel Pepys and Nell Gwyn."

 It was also released as Mistress Nell Gwyn.
