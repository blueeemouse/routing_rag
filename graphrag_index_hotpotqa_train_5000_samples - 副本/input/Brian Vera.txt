Brian Vera

Bryan Lee Vera (born December 28, 1981) is an American professional boxer who fights at super middleweight.

 He is best known for his memorable and notable victories over then-undefeated Andy Lee in 2008, former world champion Sergio Mora (twice, in 2011 and 2012) and Serhiy Dzinziruk in 2013.

 Vera currently trains in Cedar Park, Texas.
