Andreas Ioannides (naval officer)

Captain Andreas Ioannides (Greek: Ανδρέας Ιωαννίδης ; 1958 – 11 July 2011) was a Greek Cypriot naval officer and head of the Cyprus Navy until his death in the Evangelos Florakis Navy Base explosion, which took place at the Evangelos Florakis Navy Base on July 11, 2011.
