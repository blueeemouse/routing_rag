Drank in My Cup

"Drank in My Cup" is a song by American rapper Kirko Bangz.

 It is taken from his mixtape "Progression 2: A Young Texas Playa".

 The Sound M.O.B. produced single is his highest charting song to date and has sold 1,000,000 copies digitally.

 The song uses the word 'drank' a number of times as a clear reference to purple drank, a codeine-promethazine cocktail served in Sprite, a drink symbolic of the H-Town hip-hop scene, classically associated with chopped and screwed music.

 The song is full of references to this music scene, including the slow, relaxed rhythm of the song.
