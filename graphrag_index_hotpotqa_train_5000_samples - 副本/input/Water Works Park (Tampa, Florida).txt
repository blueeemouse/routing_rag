Water Works Park (Tampa, Florida)

Water Works Park is a park area in Tampa, Florida.

 It was redeveloped and opened to the public in August 2014.

 The park includes a section of the Tampa Riverwalk, which is extended alongside it and the Hillsborough River.

 A restaurant Ulele was built at the site and opened August 2014 in the nearby Waterworks building as part of urban redevelopment efforts and design with the purpose of integrating the Tampa Heights neighborhood with downtown Tampa.

 Water Works Park is located at 1710 North Highland Avenue.
