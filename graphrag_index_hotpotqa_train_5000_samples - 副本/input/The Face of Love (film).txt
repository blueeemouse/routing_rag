The Face of Love (film)

The Face of Love is a 2013 American romantic drama film directed by Arie Posin and co-written by Matthew McDuffie.

 The film stars Annette Bening, Ed Harris, Robin Williams, Amy Brenneman, Jess Weixler and Linda Park.

 It was screened in the Special Presentation section at the 2013 Toronto International Film Festival.

 This was Robin Williams' final role before his death in 2014.
