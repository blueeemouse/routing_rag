Amna Babar

Amna Babar (born December 28, 1992) is Pakistani supermodel.

 She is regarded as one of the most "recognised" model of Pakistan winning the Best Emerging Talent - Fashion award at 13th Lux Style Awards in 2014, and received her first Lux Style Award nomination as Best Model Female at 14th Lux Style Awards and Best Model Female at 3rd Hum Awards and 4th Hum Awards respectively.
