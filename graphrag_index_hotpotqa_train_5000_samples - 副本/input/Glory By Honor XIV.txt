Glory By Honor XIV

Glory By Honor XIV was a two night, two city professional wrestling event produced by the U.S.-based wrestling promotion Ring of Honor.

 The first night of the event took place on October 23, 2015 at the Wing's Event Center in Kalamazoo, Michigan.

 The second part of the event took place on October 24, 2015 at Montgomery County Fairgrounds Coliseum in Dayton, Ohio.
