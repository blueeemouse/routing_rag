Holding Patterns (film)

Holding Patterns is a 2016 American comedy-drama film written and directed by Jake Goldberger.

 The film stars Freddie Highmore, Odeya Rush, Haley Joel Osment, Christopher Meloni, and Marg Helgenberger.

 Filming began during the summer of 2015 in Mobile, Alabama.

 The film premiered on October 14, 2016 at the Austin Film Festival.
