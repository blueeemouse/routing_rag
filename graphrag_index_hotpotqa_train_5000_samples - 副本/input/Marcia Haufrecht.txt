Marcia Haufrecht

Marcia Haufrecht (born January 3, 1937 as Marcia Haufreucht) is an American actress, playwright and director, as well as a noted acting teacher and coach.

 A life member of The Actors Studio, and a longtime member of The Ensemble Studio Theatre, she is also the founder and artistic director of the Off-Off-Broadway company (and venue), The Common Basis Theatre (originally The Common Ground Theatre).
