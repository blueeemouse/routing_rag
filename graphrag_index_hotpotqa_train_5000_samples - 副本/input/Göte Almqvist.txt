Göte Almqvist

Göte Augustin Almqvist (25 June 1921 in Skellefteå, Sweden - 21 December 1994 in Skellefteå, Sweden) was a Swedish ice hockey player who represented his country at the 1952 Winter Olympics in Oslo, winning the bronze medal in the team competition.

 He was born on 25 July 1921 and died on 21 December 1994 in Skellefteå, Västerbotten County.

 He played his club hockey for Skellefteå AIK.
