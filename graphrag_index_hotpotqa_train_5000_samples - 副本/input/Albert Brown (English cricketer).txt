Albert Brown (English cricketer)

Albert Brown (10 July 1911 – 27 April 1995) was an English cricketer and snooker player.

 He made just one first-class appearance for Warwickshire County Cricket Club in 1932.

 As a snooker player he was twice runner-up in the English Amateur Championship and reached the semi-final of the World Snooker Championship four times between 1948 and 1953.
