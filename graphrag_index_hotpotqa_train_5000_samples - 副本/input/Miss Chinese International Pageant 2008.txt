Miss Chinese International Pageant 2008

Miss Chinese International Pageant 2008 was held on January 26, 2008 in Foshan, China, for the second consecutive year.

 The pageant was organized and broadcast by TVB in Hong Kong.

 Miss Chinese International 2007 Sarah Song of Sydney, Australia crowned Océane Zhu of Paris, France as the 20th Miss Chinese International.

 Zhu is the first ever winner from Europe.
