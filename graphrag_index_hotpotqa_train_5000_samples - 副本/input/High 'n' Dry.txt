High 'n' Dry

High 'n' Dry is the second studio album by the English rock band Def Leppard, released on July 11, 1981.

 "High 'n' Dry" was Pete Willis' last full-time album with Def Leppard.

 It charted at No. 38 on the "Billboard" 200 and No. 26 on the UK Albums Chart. "

High 'n' Dry (Saturday Night)", ranked No. 33 on VH1's 40 Greatest Metal Songs.

 Following the success of "Pyromania", the album re-entered in the US chart and reached No. 72 in 1983.
