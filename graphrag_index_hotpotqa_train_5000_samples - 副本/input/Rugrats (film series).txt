Rugrats (film series)

The "Rugrats" film series is a series of animated comedy adventure films based on the popular Nickelodeon cartoon, "Rugrats", created by Arlene Klasky, Gábor Csupó, and Paul Germain.

 The TV series, one of Nickelodeon's all-time most popular and longest-running cartoon series, ran from 1991 until 2004, while three films were released in 1998, 2000, and 2003.

 The three films received mostly mixed reviews from film reviewers, but were all commercial successes, collectively grossing nearly $300 million worldwide.
