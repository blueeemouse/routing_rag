To Sail Beyond the Sunset

To Sail Beyond the Sunset is a science fiction novel by American writer Robert A. Heinlein, published in 1987.

 It was the last novel published before his death in 1988.

 The title is taken from the poem "Ulysses", by Alfred Lord Tennyson.
