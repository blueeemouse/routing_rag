Stephen Brown (Jesuit)

Stephen James Meridith Brown (born 24 September 1881 in Holywood, County Down, Ireland, died 8 May 1962, in Kilcrony, Wicklow, Ireland) was an Irish Catholic Jesuit priest, writer, bibliographer and librarian.

 He founded the Central Catholic Library (Irish: "Leabharlann an Chreidimh" ) in Dublin.
