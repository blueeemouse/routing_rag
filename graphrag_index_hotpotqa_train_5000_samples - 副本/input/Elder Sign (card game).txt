Elder Sign (card game)

Elder Sign is a cooperative card and dice game, based on the Cthulhu Mythos of horror writer H.P. Lovecraft and Chaosium's Call of Cthulhu roleplaying game.

 It is published by Fantasy Flight Games, which also produces the Cthulhu Mythos games "Arkham Horror", "", "Mansions of Madness", and "Eldritch Horror".
