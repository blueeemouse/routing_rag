Pennsylvania Route 452

Pennsylvania Route 452 (PA 452) is a state highway in Delaware County, Pennsylvania.

 The route runs from U.S. Route 13 (US 13) in Marcus Hook north to PA 352 in Lima.

 The route runs through suburban areas, passing through Linwood and Aston Township.

 PA 452 intersects Interstate 95 (I-95) and US 322 in Upper Chichester Township and US 1 in Lima.

 PA 452 was first designated by 1928 between its current termini.

 A portion of the route in Aston Township was realigned by 1950.
