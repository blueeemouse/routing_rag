Karl Lintner

Karl Lintner (1917 - 11.2.2015) is an Austrian nuclear physicist.

 During World War II, he worked on the German nuclear energy project, also known as the Uranium Club; he did research on the inelastic dispersion of fast neutrons in uranium.

 After the war, he taught and did nuclear research at the University of Vienna.

 He was a full member of the Austrian Academy of Sciences.
