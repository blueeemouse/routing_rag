Cadillac Ranch (Chris LeDoux song)

"Cadillac Ranch" is a song written by Chuck Jones and Chris Waters, and recorded by American country music artist Chris LeDoux.

 It was released in October 1992 as the second and final single from his album "Whatcha Gonna Do with a Cowboy".

 The song reached number 18 on the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles & Tracks chart and number 16 on the Canadian "RPM" Country Tracks.
