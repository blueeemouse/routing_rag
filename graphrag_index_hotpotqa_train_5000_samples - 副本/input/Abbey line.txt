Abbey line

The Abbey line, also called the St Albans Abbey branch line, is a railway line from Watford Junction to St Albans Abbey.

 The 6.5 mi route passes through town and countryside in the county of Hertfordshire, just outside the boundaries of the Oyster Card and London fare zones.

 Its northern terminus in St Albans Abbey is located in the south of the city, around 1.2 km away from the larger St Albans City railway station on the Thameslink line.
