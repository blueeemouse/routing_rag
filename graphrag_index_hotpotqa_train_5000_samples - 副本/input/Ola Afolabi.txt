Ola Afolabi

Olawale O. "Ola" Afolabi (born 15 March 1980) is a British professional boxer.

 He is a two-time former WBO interim cruiserweight champion, as well as a two-time former IBO cruiserweight champion.

 Nicknamed "Kryptonite", Afolabi was known in the ring for his slickness, counterpunching skills and durability.

 Born to Nigerian parents, Afolabi resides in California, where he also trained for the majority of his career.
