Stephen Devassy

Stephen Devassy (Malayalam:സ്റ്റീഫൻ ദേവസ്സി, born 23 February 1981) is a musician hailing from Palakkad, Kerala.

 He started his career at an early age, and has performed on stage around the world.

 He also holds the record for being the highest Asian scorer in Piano exams from Trinity college of music,london
