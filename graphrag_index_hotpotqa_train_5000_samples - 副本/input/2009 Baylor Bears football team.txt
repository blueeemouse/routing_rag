2009 Baylor Bears football team

The 2009 Baylor Bears football team represented Baylor University in the 2009 NCAA Division I FBS football season.

 The team was coached by Art Briles.

 The Bears played their home games at Floyd Casey Stadium in Waco, Texas.

 Baylor finished the season with a record of 4–8 and 1–7 in Big 12 play.
