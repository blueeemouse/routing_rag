University of Queensland Business Association

The University of Queensland Business Association Inc. ("UQBA") is an organisation established to facilitate the professional development of the students of the University of Queensland.

 UQBA works with the University of Queensland Faculty of Business, Economics and Law, as well as several industry partners including Boston Consulting Group, Chartered Accountants Australia and New Zealand, CPA Australia, Deloitte, Ernst & Young, KPMG and PwC.
