Deborah Ann Woll

Deborah Ann Woll (born February 7, 1985) is an American actress.

 She is known for her roles as Jessica Hamby on the HBO drama series "True Blood", and currently portrays Karen Page in the Netflix drama series "Daredevil", "The Defenders", and "The Punisher".

 She has also starred in the films "Mother's Day", "Someday This Pain Will Be Useful to You", "Catch .44", "Ruby Sparks", and "Meet Me in Montenegro".
