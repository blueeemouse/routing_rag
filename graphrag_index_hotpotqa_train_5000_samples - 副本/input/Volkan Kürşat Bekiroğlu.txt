Volkan Kürşat Bekiroğlu

Volkan Kürşat Bekiroğlu (born December 30, 1977 in Kadirli-Osmaniye, Turkey) is a Turkish retired footballer who last played for Adanaspor.

 He is playing leftback and left midfield.

 Standing at 175 cm, he wears the number 47 jersey.

 His brother Serkan Bekiroğlu is also footballer and currently plays for Karşıyaka S.K..
