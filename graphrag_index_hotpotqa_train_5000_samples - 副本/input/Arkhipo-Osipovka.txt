Arkhipo-Osipovka

Arkhipo-Osipovka (Russian: Архи́по-О́сиповка ) is a rural locality (a "selo") and a Black Sea resort under the jurisdiction of the Town of Gelendzhik in Krasnodar Krai, Russia.

 Arkhipo-Osipovka (many people call this place Arkhipka) is located 50 kilometers away from the city of Gelendzhik.

 This place has one of the softest climates on the Russian sea coast.
