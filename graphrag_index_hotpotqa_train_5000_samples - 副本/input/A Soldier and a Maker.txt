A Soldier and a Maker

A Soldier and a Maker is a piece of musical theatre by Iain Burnside on the life story of the First World War poet and composer Ivor Gurney.

 It was premiered in April 2012 at the Guildhall School of Music and Drama, with Richard Goulding as Gurney.

 The production was designed by Giuseppe and Emma Belli.

 It was later adapted into a radio play broadcast on BBC Radio 3 on 29 June 2014 as part of the "Music in the Great War" season.

 The radio adaptation retained many of the stage cast.
