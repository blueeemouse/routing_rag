Gå upp och pröva dina vingar

Gå upp och pröva dina vingar ("Go up and try your wings") is a 1944 Swedish song written by Lasse Dahlquist.

 It was written for the 1944 Swedish film "Örnungar", a film about gliding, where Dahlquist, Alice Babs and Kaj Hjelm sang it.

 The song is considered one of Dahlqvist's most important compositions.
