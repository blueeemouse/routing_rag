Wichita South High School

Wichita South High School, known locally as South, is a fully accredited high school, serving students in grades 9-12, located in Wichita, Kansas.

 It is also known as Wichita High School South.

 The school colors are blue and red.

 The schools enrollment was 1,922 during the 2016–2017 school year, making it the 9th largest high school in Kansas.
