Skynyrd Frynds

Skynyrd Frynds is a tribute album to the American Southern rock band Lynyrd Skynyrd, released in 1994 on MCA Records.

 It features cover versions of ten Lynyrd Skynyrd songs, as performed by various country music artists.

 The album reached #8 on Top Country Albums upon its release.

 The album was executive produced by Gary Rossington.
