How I Live Now (film)

How I Live Now is a 2013 Canadian-British speculative drama film based on the 2004 novel of the same name by Meg Rosoff.

 It was directed by Kevin Macdonald and script written by Tony Grisoni, Jeremy Brock and Penelope Skinner.

 The film stars Saoirse Ronan, Tom Holland, Anna Chancellor, George MacKay and Corey Johnson.

 It was screened in the Special Presentation section at the 2013 Toronto International Film Festival.
