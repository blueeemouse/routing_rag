Leto II Atreides

Leto II Atreides ( ) is a fictional character from the "Dune" universe created by Frank Herbert.

 Born at the end of "Dune Messiah" (1969), Leto is a central character in "Children of Dune" (1976) and is the title character of "God Emperor of Dune" (1981).

 The character is brought back as a ghola in the Brian Herbert/Kevin J. Anderson sequels which conclude the original series, "Hunters of Dune" (2006) and "Sandworms of Dune" (2007).

 Leto also appears as a child in the prequel "The Winds of Dune" (2009).
