Muhammad ibn Muslim and Ibraheem ibn Muslim

Muhammad ibn Muslim (Arabic: محمد بن مسلم‎ ‎ ) and Ibraheem ibn Muslim (Arabic: إبراهيم بن مسلم‎ ‎ ) were the sons of Muslim ibn Aqeel and the grandsons of Aqeel ibn Abu Talib.

 Muslim ibn Aqeel was the messenger of Hussain ibn Ali to the people of Kufa, while Aqeel ibn Abu Talib was the brother of Ali ibn Abu Talib and the cousin of Muhammad.

 These children are also included among the martyrs of the Battle of Karbala.
