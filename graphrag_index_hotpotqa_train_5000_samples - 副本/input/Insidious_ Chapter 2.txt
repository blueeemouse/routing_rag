Insidious: Chapter 2

Insidious: Chapter 2 is a 2013 American supernatural horror film directed by James Wan.

 It is a sequel to "Insidious" and the second (chronologically, the fourth) installment in the "Insidious" franchise.

 The film stars Patrick Wilson and Rose Byrne reprising their roles as Josh and Renai Lambert, a husband and wife who seek to uncover the secret that has left them dangerously connected to the spirit world.
