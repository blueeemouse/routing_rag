Parish magazine

A parish magazine is a periodical produced by and for an ecclesiastical parish, generally within the Anglican Church.

 It usually comprises a mixture of religious articles, community contributions and parish notices, including the previous month‘s christenings, marriages and funerals.

 Magazines are sold or are otherwise circulated amongst the parishioners of the relevant church or village.

 They are almost invariably produced by volunteers, usually working alongside the resident clergy.

 From their earliest days they have frequently been augmented by the inclusion of a nationally-produced magazine supplement or a regionally produced insert, such as a diocesan news leaflet or similar publication (and sometimes they might include both).

 It has been estimated that the collective readership of parish magazines exceeds that of many national newspapers.

 Similar magazines have also been produced by other religious denominations, including the Church of Scotland.
