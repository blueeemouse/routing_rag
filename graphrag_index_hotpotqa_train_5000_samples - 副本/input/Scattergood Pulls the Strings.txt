Scattergood Pulls the Strings

Scattergood Pulls the Strings is a 1941 American comedy film directed by Christy Cabanne and written by Christy Cabanne and Bernard Schubert.

 It is the sequel to the 1941 film "Scattergood Baines".

 The film stars Guy Kibbee, Bobs Watson, Susan Peters, James Corner, Emma Dunn, Dink Trout and Monte Blue.

 The film was released on May 23, 1941, by RKO Pictures.
