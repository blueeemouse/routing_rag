Deoxyepinephrine

Deoxyepinephrine, also known by the common names N-methyldopamine and epinine, is an organic compound and natural product that is structurally related to the important neurotransmitters dopamine and epinephrine.

 All three of these compounds also belong to the catecholamine family.

 The pharmacology of epinine largely resembles that of its "parent", dopamine.

 Epinine has been found in plants, insects and animals.

 It is also of significance as the active metabolic breakdown product of the prodrug ibopamine, which has been used to treat congestive heart failure.
