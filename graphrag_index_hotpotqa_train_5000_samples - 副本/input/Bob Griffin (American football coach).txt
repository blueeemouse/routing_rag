Bob Griffin (American football coach)

Bob Griffin (born October 22, 1940) was the head coach of the Idaho State and Rhode Island Rams football teams.

 Griffin spent 17 seasons as the head coach of the Rams and is the winningest coach in URI history.

 He compiled a 100–127–1 overall record.

 Griffin was born and raised in Milford, CT and graduated from Southern Connecticut State College in 1963, where he played as a quarterback and still holds the record for longest completed pass (87 yards).

 He has fifty years of coaching experience at the high school, college and professional level.

 Griffin has three children and seven grandchildren, and currently resides in Narragansett, RI with his wife, Rosanne.
