Dean Lowry

Dean Vincent Lowry (born June 9, 1994) is an American football defensive end for the Green Bay Packers of the National Football League (NFL).

 He played high school football at Boylan Central Catholic High School in Rockford, Illinois before attending Northwestern and playing for their football team on a scholarship.

 At Northwestern, he was a four-year starter at defensive end.

 He was drafted after his senior year of college by the Green Bay Packers in the 2016 NFL Draft.
