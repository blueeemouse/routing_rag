2000 St. Louis Rams season

The 2000 St. Louis Rams season was the team's 63rd year with the National Football League and the sixth season in St. Louis.

 The Rams finished the regular-season with a record of 10–6 but would go on to lose to the New Orleans Saints in the Wild Card round of the playoffs.

 They led the NFL in scoring for a second straight year with 540 points.

 The Rams became the first team in NFL history to score more than 500 points on offense, while allowing more than 450 points on defense.
