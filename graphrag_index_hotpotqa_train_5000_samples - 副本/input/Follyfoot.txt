Follyfoot

Follyfoot is a children's television series co-produced by the majority-partner British television company Yorkshire Television (for transmission on ITV) and the independent West German company "TV Munich" (for transmission on the ZDF channel).

 It aired in the United Kingdom between 1971 and 1973, repeated for two years after that and again in the late 1980s.

 The series starred Gillian Blake in the lead role.

 Notable people connected with the series were actors Desmond Llewelyn and Arthur English and directors Jack Cardiff, Stephen Frears, Michael Apted and David Hemmings.
