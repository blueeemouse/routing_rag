A Wild Pear

"A Wild Pear" is a split EP from the Canadian garage rock band The Evaporators and American rock artist Andrew W.K..

 It was released on June 23, 2009, and features one original song and one cover by The Evaporators on side 1 and two covers by Andrew W.K. and an interview by Nardwuar the Human Serviette (lead singer and keyboardist of The Evaporators) with Andrew W.K. on side 2.

 The EP features cover art by cartoonist Mitch Clem.
