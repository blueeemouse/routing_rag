List of Star Wars theme parks attractions

The "Star Wars" franchise has spawned several attractions at multiple Disney parks, starting even before Disney's acquisition of the franchise from George Lucas.

 Lucas had established a partnership in 1986 with the company's Imagineering division to create an attraction at Disney parks.

 In addition to the attractions at the various Disney parks, the Museum of Science collaborated with Lucasfilm Ltd. to create an exhibition on the science behind "Star Wars".
