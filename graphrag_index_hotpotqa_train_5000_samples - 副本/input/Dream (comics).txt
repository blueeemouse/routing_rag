Dream (comics)

Dream is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics' imprint Vertigo.

 The character is the protagonist of the comic book series "The Sandman", written by Neil Gaiman.

 One of the seven Endless, inconceivably powerful beings older and greater than gods, Dream is both lord and personification of all dreams and stories, all that is not in reality (which, in turn, Dream may define by his existence).

 He has taken many names, including Morpheus and Oneiros, and his appearance can change depending on the person who is seeing him.

 Dream was named the sixth-greatest comic book character by "Empire Magazine".

 He was also named fifteenth in IGN's 100 Top Comic Book Heroes list.
