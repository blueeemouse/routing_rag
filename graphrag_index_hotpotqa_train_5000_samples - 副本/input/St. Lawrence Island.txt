St. Lawrence Island

St. Lawrence Island (Central Siberian Yupik: "Sivuqaq" ) is located west of mainland Alaska in the Bering Sea, just south of the Bering Strait.

 The village of Gambell is an island located on the northwest cape, 36 miles (58 kilometers) from the Chukchi Peninsula in the Russian Far East.

 The island is part of Alaska, but closer to Siberia than to the Alaskan mainland.

 St. Lawrence Island is thought to be one of the last exposed portions of the land bridge that once joined Asia with North America during the Pleistocene period.

 It is the sixth largest island in the United States and the 113th largest island in the world.

 It is considered part of the Bering Sea Volcanic Province.
