The Rain People

The Rain People is a 1969 film directed by Francis Ford Coppola.

 Alongside Shirley Knight, leading players are James Caan and Robert Duvall, both of whom would later work with Coppola in "The Godfather".

 Future film director and Coppola friend George Lucas worked as an aide on this film, and made a short making-of documentary film, "Filmmaker", about it.

 The film also won the Golden Shell at the 1969 San Sebastian Film Festival.
