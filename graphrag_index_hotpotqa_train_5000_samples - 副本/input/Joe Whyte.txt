Joe Whyte

Joseph "Joe" Whyte (born April 18, 1961) is an American actor and voice actor.

 He attended California State University, where he performed in acting groups.

 Whyte has also been working for Disney.

 He is best known for voicing Mermaid Man in the "SpongeBob SquarePants" video games series, as well as voicing Mr. Krabs in some of the SpongeBob video games, when Clancy Brown was not available.
