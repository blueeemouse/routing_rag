Battles of the Spanish–American War

During the Spanish-American War, United States Army and United States Navy fought 30 battles significant against the Spanish Army and Navy.

 Of these, 26 occurred in the Caribbean Theater and 3 in the Pacific Theater.

 The Caribbean theater had two campaigns: Puerto Rican Campaign, which had 10 battles, and the Cuban Campaign, which had 17 battles, while the Pacific theater had one campaign: the Philippine theater, which had two battles, but there was a battle in Guam, which was not significant enough to warrant as an individual campaign.

 This list includes battles involving the U.S. Army, the U.S. Marines, and the U.S. Navy.
