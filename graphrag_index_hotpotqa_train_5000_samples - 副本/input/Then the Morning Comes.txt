Then the Morning Comes

"Then the Morning Comes" is a song by American band Smash Mouth.

 It was released in September 1999 as the third single from the band's second studio album, "Astro Lounge".

 The song was a successful follow up to the band's international hit "All Star", peaking at number 11 on the "Billboard" Hot 100 and number 2 in Canada.

 In comparison to "Walkin' on the Sun", "Then the Morning Comes" also has a 1960s-inspired psychedelic vibe.

 This song was also featured on the 2000 compilation album "Now That's What I Call Music!
