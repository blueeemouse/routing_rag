Michael Hemschoot

Michael "Ffish" Hemschoot (born 1972, Aurora, Colorado) is a director, animator and has held a variety of roles as a visual effects artist in major Hollywood films.

 Since 1996, Hemschoot has worked on films such as "Mars Attacks!

", "What Dreams May Come", "The Matrix", "Hollow Man", "Black Hawk Down", "Minority Report" and "National Treasure".
