Kalanidhi Narayanan

Kalanidhi Narayanan (December 7, 1928 – February 21, 2016) was an Indian dancer and teacher of Indian classical dance form of Bharatnatyam, who was the early non-devadasi girl to learn the dance form and perform it on stage in the 1930s and 1940s.

 After a brief career in the 1940s, she returned to dance in 1973 and became a notable teacher of "abhinaya".
