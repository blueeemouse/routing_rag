Nine Mile Prairie

Nine Mile Prairie (named for its location 5 mi west and 4 mi north of downtown Lincoln) is a 230 acre tract of conserved tallgrass prairie in Lancaster County, Nebraska, United States.

 Except for one small portion of it that was farmed as recently as the 1950s, Nine-Mile Prairie has never been plowed (some of the land has at times been grazed as recently as the 1960s).

 As such, it is one of the largest areas of virgin tallgrass prairie in the state of Nebraska.

 It has been listed on the National Register of Historic Places since 1986.
