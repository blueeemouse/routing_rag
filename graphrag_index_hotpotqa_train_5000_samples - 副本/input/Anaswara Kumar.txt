Anaswara Kumar

Anaswara Kumar is an Indian film actress, who works in the Tamil film industry.

 She started her career with Arivazhaghan's sports thriller "Vallinam" (2014), though the romantic comedy "Ego" (2013) released first.

 She made her breakthrough portraying Mohini in the black comedy film "Yaamirukka Bayamey", which became a commercial success.
