Finnair

Finnair (Finnish: "Finnair Oyj" , Swedish: "Finnair Abp" ) is the flag carrier and largest airline of Finland, with its headquarters in Vantaa on the grounds of Helsinki Airport, its main hub.

 Finnair and its subsidiaries dominate both domestic and international air travel in Finland.

 Its major shareholder is the government of Finland, which owns 55.8% of the shares.

 Finnair is a member of the Oneworld airline alliance.

 In 2015, it transported over 10 million passengers to over 60 European, 13 Asian and 4 North-American destinations.

 At the end of 2016, the airline employed 4,937 people.
