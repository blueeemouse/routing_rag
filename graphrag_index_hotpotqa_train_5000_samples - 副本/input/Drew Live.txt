Drew Live

"Drew Live" is the eighth episode of the fifth season of the American sitcom "The Drew Carey Show", and the 109th overall.

 The episode's plot focuses on Drew (Drew Carey) trying to stop his friend, Kate (Christa Miller), from pursuing a relationship with a mystery guy, as Drew is in love with her.

 However, Drew is unaware that he is stopping Kate from pursuing him.

 Meanwhile, Drew's brother Steve (John Carroll Lynch) and his fiancée Mimi Bobeck (Kathy Kinney) hold a joint bachelor and bachelorette party.
