Lancang Lahu Autonomous County

Lancang Lahu Autonomous County () is an autonomous county under the jurisdiction of Pu'er City, in southwestern Yunnan province, China.

 Lancang is the same as Lan Xang, and refers to the Mekong River (known in Chinese as the Lancang) on its eastern borders and adopted by modern Laos, a Tai word meaning "Million Elephants".
