Destiny (DC Comics)

Destiny is a DC Comics character created by Marv Wolfman and Bernie Wrightson, first appearing in "Weird Mystery Tales" #1 (1972), and was regular host of that series for the first fourteen issues, after which he hosted "Secrets of Haunted House".

 He is one of the Endless in Neil Gaiman's comic book series, "The Sandman".

 In "The Sandman Special" #1, he was also referred to by his Greek name, Potmos.

 In 1997, he starred in an eponymous three-issue limited series written by Alisa Kwitney.
