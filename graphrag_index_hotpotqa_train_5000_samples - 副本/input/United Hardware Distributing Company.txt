United Hardware Distributing Company

United Hardware Distributing Co. is a dealer-owned corporation based in Plymouth, Minnesota which provides distribution services for about 610 member-owned stores, the majority of which operate under the Hardware Hank trade name.

 In addition, the company services about 800 other stores owned by non-members.

 2006 sales were $183 million.

 It was founded in 1945 as a furniture and hardware distributor.

 The furniture division was sold in 1953, and it became a dealer-owned retailers' cooperative in 1957 when majority control was assumed by a group of hardware store owners.

 In addition to servicing the Hardware Hank stores, United Hardware also services TrustWorthy Hardware Stores, Golden Rule Lumber Centers, and Ranch & Pet Supply stores.

 It is a member of the Distribution America buying group.
