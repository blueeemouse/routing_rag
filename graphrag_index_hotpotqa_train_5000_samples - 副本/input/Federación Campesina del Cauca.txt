Federación Campesina del Cauca

The Federación Campesina del Cauca is a non-profit, member-owned federation of coffee grower associations based in the Cauca Department of Colombia.

 It was found in 1971 as a congress of farmers, but has since formalized into a cooperative serving the interests of 630 coffee farmers from six local sub-groups.

 The federation was licensed FLO Cert in 2004, and has since acquired certification for producing both conventional and organic coffee.
