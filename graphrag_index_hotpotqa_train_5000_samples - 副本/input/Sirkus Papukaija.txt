Sirkus Papukaija

Sirkus Papukaija (Finnish for "Circus Parrot"), later simply Papukaija, was a Finnish children's TV show shown from 1961 to 1968.

 The show was shown every other Sunday, and it involved numerous "parrot clubs" in various Finnish municipalities.

 The show spawned the popular catchphrase ""10 pistettä ja papukaijamerkki"" ("10 points and a parrot badge"), which is used to congratulate someone on a correct answer, and is so popular that even people who have never heard of the show use it in everyday conversation.
