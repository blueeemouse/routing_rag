Indrans Jayan

Indrans Jayan is an Indian film costume designer working predominantly in Malayalam films.

 He won the National Film Award for Best Costume Design in 2009 (Kutty Srank) and 2010 (Namma Gramam).

 Born in Kumarapuram, Trivandrum, he is the cousin of the Malayalam film actor Indrans.

 He has worked as costume designer for close to 300 films.
