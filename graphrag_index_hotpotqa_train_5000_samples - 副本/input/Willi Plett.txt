Willi Plett

Willi Plett (born June 7, 1955) is a Canadian former professional ice hockey right winger who played 834 games in the National Hockey League (NHL) for the Atlanta Flames, Calgary Flames, Minnesota North Stars and Boston Bruins.

 He was a fifth-round selection of the Atlanta Flames in the 1975 NHL Amateur Draft, 80th overall.

 Plett was a member of the Tulsa Oilers' Adams Cup championship team in 1975–76 and won the Calder Memorial Trophy in 1977 as the NHL's top rookie.

 He transferred with the Flames franchise to Calgary in 1980 and a 1982 trade sent him to Minnesota where he played five seasons.

 Plett retired in 1988 following one season in Boston.
