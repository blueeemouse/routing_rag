Little auk

The little auk or dovekie ("Alle alle") is a small auk, the only member of the genus "Alle".

 "Alle" is the Sami name of the long-tailed duck; it is onomatopoeic and imitates the call of the drake duck.

 Linnaeus was not particularly familiar with the winter plumages of either the auk or the duck, and appears to have confused the two species.
