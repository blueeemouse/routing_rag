Kim Sung-ryung

Kim Sung-ryung (born February 8, 1967) is a South Korean actress.

 After winning the Miss Korea beauty pageant in 1988 (she later represented her country at the Miss Universe 1989 pageant), Kim began her career as a reporter on KBS's showbiz news program "".

 In 1991, she made a memorable film acting debut in Kang Woo-suk's "Who Saw the Dragon's Claws?"

, though she became more active in television in the next decade.

 She returned to the big screen in 2007, with notable supporting roles in "Shadows in the Palace", and "Rainbow Eyes", followed by "The Client" (2011), "Mr. XXX-Kisser" (2012), "The Fatal Encounter" (2014), and "The Target" (2014).

 As she entered her forties, Kim also became known for the television dramas "You're Beautiful" (2009), "The Chaser" (2012), "Yawang" (2013), "The Heirs" (2013), and "Queen's Flower" (2015).
