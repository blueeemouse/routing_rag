Mameshiba (character)

Mameshiba (豆しば ) is a Japanese merchandise franchise created by copywriter and Korean Japanese national Kim Sukwon.

 The Mameshiba are different varieties of beans (and other legumes) that have dog-like faces and tell trivia.

 Their name derives from a series of puns.

 The Japanese word for "bean" is mame (豆) , a toy version of the Shiba Inu is a mameshiba (豆柴) , and the Japanese word for "trivia" is mamechishiki (豆知識, literally "beans of knowledge") .
