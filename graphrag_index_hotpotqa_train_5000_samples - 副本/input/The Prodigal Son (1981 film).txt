The Prodigal Son (1981 film)

The Prodigal Son is a 1981 Hong Kong martial arts film starring Yuen Biao, and also written and directed by Sammo Hung, who also co-stars in the film.

 The film was released on 22 December 1981 and grossed HK$9,150,729.

 "The Prodigal Son" was nominated for two Hong Kong Film Awards and won the award for Best Action Choreography.
