Burn After Reading

Burn After Reading is a 2008 American black comedy film written, produced, edited and directed by Joel and Ethan Coen.

 The film stars George Clooney, John Malkovich, Frances McDormand, Tilda Swinton, and Brad Pitt.

 The film had its premiere on August 27, 2008, when it opened the 2008 Venice Film Festival.

 It was released in the United States on September 12, 2008, and in the United Kingdom on October 17, 2008.
