David Christopher Davies (missionary)

David Christopher Davies, known as Christy Davies (1878–1958) was a Welsh missionary, and representative of the Baptist Missionary Society (B.M.S.).

 His parents were John and Elizabeth Davies of Clydach, Glamorganshire.

 He attended a local Calfaria Baptist Church where his father was the church treasurer, and a deacon.

 The church pastor was T. Valentine Evans (father of Sir (David) Emrys Evans).
