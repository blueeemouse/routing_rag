Jason Gonzalez

Jason Gonzalez (born September 30, 1973) is an attorney in Tallahassee, Florida.

 He is credited with reshaping the Florida Supreme Court while serving as General Counsel to the Governor of Florida.

 He was the chief advisor to the Governor on the appointment of four of the seven Justices currently serving on the Florida Supreme Court.

 He handles civil and administrative litigation, appeals, and government affairs.
