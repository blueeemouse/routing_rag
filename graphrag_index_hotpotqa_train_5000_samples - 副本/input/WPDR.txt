WPDR

WPDR (1350 AM) is a radio station broadcasting an oldies format.

 Prior to oldies, WPDR was News/Talk.

 Before that, WPDR played oldies and also played country music for many years.

 Licensed to Portage, Wisconsin, United States, the station serves the Wisconsin Dells, Portage, and North Madison Metro.

 The station is currently owned by Magnum Broadcasting.

 Co-owned WDDC, formerly WPDR-FM, is located at 100.1 MHz.
