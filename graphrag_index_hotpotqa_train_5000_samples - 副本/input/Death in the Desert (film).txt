Death in the Desert (film)

Death in the Desert is a 2015 American crime-drama film directed and produced by Josh Evans and starring Michael Madsen and Shayla Beesley, with John Palladino, Paz de la Huerta, and Roxy Saint appearing in supporting roles.

 Principal filming completed in February 2014 in Las Vegas, Nevada.

 It is the second film collaboration between Madsen and Evans, who produced "The Price of Air" in which Madsen starred.
