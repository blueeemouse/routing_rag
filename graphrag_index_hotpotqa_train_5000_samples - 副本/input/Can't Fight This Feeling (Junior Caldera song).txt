Can't Fight This Feeling (Junior Caldera song)

"Can't Fight this Feeling" is a song written by Junior Caldera, Sophie Ellis-Bextor and Roselyn Della Sabina, and produced by Junior Caldera for his first album, "Début", released in 2009.

 It was released as the album's fourth single in February 2010.

 It was also the second single from Ellis-Bextor's fourth studio album, "Make A Scene".

 The single was released in France prior to the release of Ellis-Bextor following single, "Bittersweet".

 The video for the song was shot in Paris on 10 March 2010.

 It was believed that Roselyn Della Sabina stopped writing for Madonna to complete this song.

 The single was released in Australia on 26 March 2011.
