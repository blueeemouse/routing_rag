The Grand Maneuver

The Grand Maneuver (French: Les Grandes Manœuvres ) is a 1955 French drama film written and directed by René Clair, and starring Michèle Morgan and Gérard Philipe.

 It was released in the United Kingdom and Ireland as Summer Manoeuvres, and in the United States under the title The Grand Maneuver.

 It is a romantic comedy-drama set in a French provincial town just before World War I, and it was René Clair's first film to be made in colour.
