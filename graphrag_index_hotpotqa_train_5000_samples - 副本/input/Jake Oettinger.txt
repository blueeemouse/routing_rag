Jake Oettinger

Jakob Oettinger (born December 18, 1998) is an American collegiate ice hockey player who currently plays as a goaltender for Boston University for the 2016–17 season.

 After being part of the USA Hockey National Team Development Program, he has been widely considered as a top prospect for 2017 NHL Entry Draft.

 The Dallas Stars traded up from pick 29 to selected Oettinger in the first round, 26th overall, of the 2017 NHL Entry Draft.
