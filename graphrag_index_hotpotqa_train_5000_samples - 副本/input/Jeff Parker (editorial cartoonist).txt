Jeff Parker (editorial cartoonist)

Jeff Parker has been editorial cartoonist for "Florida Today" which serves Florida's Space Coast, since 1992.

 He also assists Pulitzer Prize–winning cartoonist Mike Peters (cartoonist) with his comic strip, "Mother Goose & Grimm" and worked with Denis Lebrun on the daily "Blondie" comic strip from 1996 until 2005.

 With "New Orleans Times-Picayune" editorial cartoonist Steve Kelley, Parker produces the strip "Dustin", centered on an unemployed 23-year-old living with his parents.

 "Dustin" was launched in papers nationwide in early 2010.
