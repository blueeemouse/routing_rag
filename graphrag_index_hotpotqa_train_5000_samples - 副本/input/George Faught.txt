George Faught

George Faught (born July 14, 1962) is an American businessman and Republican politician from Oklahoma.

 Faught was Representative for District 14 in the Oklahoma House of Representatives from 2007 to 2012.

 House District 14 encompasses Muskogee, Fort Gibson, Braggs, Hulbert, and outlying areas.

 When the 51st Legislature was opened on February 5, 2007, Faught became the only Republican in state history to represent the historically Democratic 14th House District.

 He announced his candidacy for Oklahoma's 2nd congressional district on July 14, 2011.
