Svetlana Radzivil

Svetlana Radzivil (Russian: Светлана Михайловна Радзивил ; born 17 January 1987 in Tashkent, Uzbekistan) is an Uzbekistani high jumper.

 She is among Asia's top female high jumpers.

 She won the Asian Games title two times running (2010 to 2014) and was the Asian champion in 2015.

 She has also won two silver and one bronze medal at the Asian Athletics Championships.

 She is a two-time champion at the Asian Indoor Athletics Championships (2006 and 2014).

 She is a three-time Olympian (2008, 2012 and 2016).
