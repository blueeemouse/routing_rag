Matthew H. Carpenter

Matthew Hale Carpenter (born Decatur Merritt Hammond Carpenter; December 22, 1824 – February 24, 1881) was an American attorney and U.S. Senator representing the state of Wisconsin.

 He served in the Senate from 1869 to 1875 and again from 1879 to 1881.

 Recognized as an authority on constitutional law, he made some of the most important legal arguments of 19th-century America.

 Carpenter presented cases before the U. S. Supreme Court involving such matters as states' rights and regulation of corporations.
