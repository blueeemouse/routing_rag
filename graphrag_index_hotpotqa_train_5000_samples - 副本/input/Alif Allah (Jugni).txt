Alif Allah (Jugni)

Alif Allah (Jugni) (Punjabi: ), also known as Alif Allah Chambe Di Booti (Punjabi: ) is a popular Punjabi sufi song that was composed and sung by Arif Lohar and Meesha Shafi in Coke Studio Pakistan.

 The lyrics of the song were based on the works of 17th century Sufi poet Sultan Bahu.
