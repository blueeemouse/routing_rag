Loophole (1981 film)

Loophole is a 1981 British heist film, directed by John Quested, and starring Albert Finney, Martin Sheen, Susannah York, Jonathan Pryce, Colin Blakely and Tony Doyle.

 It was written by Jonathan Hales, based upon the novel by Robert Pollock.

 Music is by Lalo Schifrin.
