Julian Sinclair Smith

Julian Sinclair Smith (1921 - April 19, 1993) was an American electrical engineer and television executive who founded Sinclair Broadcast Group in 1971 as a single local television station; namely, Baltimore, Maryland's WBFF-TV.

 In 1986, he and his family incorporated Sinclair Broadcast Group.

 As of December 2016, his four sons are the majority shareholders of Sinclair Broadcast Group.

 His son David D. Smith became CEO of Sinclair Broadcast Group in 1988.
