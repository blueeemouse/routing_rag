4 3 2 1 (novel)

4 3 2 1 is a novel by Paul Auster published in January 2017.

 At the time of its publication, it was the first new Auster novel to have appeared in seven years.

 Auster worked on the book seven days a week for three years and wrote it in long hand.

 At 866 pages, the novel is much longer than any of his previous works.

 In September 2017 it was shortlisted for the 2017 Man Booker Prize.
