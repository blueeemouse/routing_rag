2015 Morgan State Bears football team

The 2015 Morgan State Bears football team represented Morgan State University in the 2015 NCAA Division I FCS football season.

 They were led by second-year head coach Lee Hull and played their home games at Hughes Stadium.

 Morgan State was a member of the Mid-Eastern Athletic Conference(MEAC).

 They finished the season 4–6, 4–4 in MEAC play to finish in a tie for sixth place.
