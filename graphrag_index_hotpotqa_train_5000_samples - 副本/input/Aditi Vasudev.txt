Aditi Vasudev

Aditi Vasudev is an Indian film actress, known for her roles in the comedy-drama film "Do Dooni Chaar" (2010) and the comedy film "Sulemani Keeda" (2014).

 She has made her TV debut in the serial "Meri Aawaaz Hi Pehchaan Hain" in February 2016 with her role as the young Ketaki along with Amrita Rao, who also made her TV debut in the same serial playing the role of Kalyani, the elder sister of Ketaki.

 The TV serial is telecast in &TV channel.
