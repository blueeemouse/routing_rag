Civil Conflict

The Civil Conflict (sometimes styled as the conFLiCT), is the name given by former University of Connecticut football head coach Bob Diaco to Connecticut's annual matchup against the UCF Knights football team of the University of Central Florida.

 The teams first met in 2013 as members of the American Athletic Conference (The American).

 Diaco was fired at the end of the 2016 season, to be replaced by Randy Edsall.
