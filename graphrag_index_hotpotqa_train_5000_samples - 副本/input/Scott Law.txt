Scott Law

Scott Law is an American singer-songwriter, record producer, and multi-instrumentalist known for his work with guitar and mandolin.

 Based in Portland, Oregon, he has been a professional musician since 1992, performing within genres such as rock, blues, bluegrass, and Americana with groups such as The String Cheese Incident.

 In 1999 Law founded Scott Law Music.

 After performing with numerous bands, Law released his first solo album as a singer-songwriter, "Deliver" with the Scott Law Band, in 2005.

 This was followed by several other albums, including the acoustic album "Black Mountain" in 2013.
