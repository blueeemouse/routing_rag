Level 5 Motorsports

Level 5 Motorsports was an American auto racing team founded by racing driver and technology entrepreneur Scott Tucker in 2006.

 Level 5 Motorsports currently competes in the United SportsCar Championship and Ferrari Challenge, having won multiple American Le Mans Series championships and endurance races including the 24 Hours of Daytona, 12 Hours of Sebring and Petit Le Mans.

 Level 5 Motorsports began with Tucker competing in the Sports Car Club of America and Ferrari Challenge Series.

 In 2007, while continuing with SCCA and Ferrari Challenge, Tucker entered several events in the Rolex Sports Car Series and Koni Challenge Series with co-driver Ed Zabinski.

 Tucker also competed in the IMSA GT3 Cup Challenge.
