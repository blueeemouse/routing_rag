1987 Austrian Grand Prix

The 1987 Austrian Grand Prix was a Formula One motor race held at Österreichring on 16 August 1987.

 It was the tenth round of the 1987 Formula One season.

 It was the twentieth Austrian Grand Prix and the last to be held at the Österreichring for ten years.

 It was held over 52 laps of the six kilometre circuit for a race distance of 309 kilometres.
