Lolita Milyavskaya

Lolita Markovna Milyavskaya (Russian: Лоли́та Ма́рковна Миля́вская , née Горелик (Gorelik); Ukrainian: Лоліта Марківна Мілявська ; born 14 November 1963) is a Russian singer, actress, TV and film director of Ukrainian origin.

 She is better known under her stage name Lolita.

 She was born in Mukacheve, Zakarpattia Oblast, Ukraine.
