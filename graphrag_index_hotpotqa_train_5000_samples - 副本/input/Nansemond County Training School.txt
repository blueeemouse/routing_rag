Nansemond County Training School

Nansemond County Training School, also known as Southwestern High School, is a historic Rosenwald School for African-American students located at Suffolk, Virginia.

 It was built in 1924, and is a one-story building consisting of a central block with a recessed covered porch and flanking wings.

 It is capped with a tin hipped roof.

 Also on the property is the contributing cafeteria building that was later used as an extra classroom.

 It was built to house the first public black high school in Nansemond County, Virginia, the school included both the primary and secondary grades.

 The school closed following the 1969–70 school year.
