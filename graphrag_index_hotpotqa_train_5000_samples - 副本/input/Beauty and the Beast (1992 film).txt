Beauty and the Beast (1992 film)

Beauty and the Beast is a 48-minute animated film originally released on May 4, 1992, and based on the classic fairy tale, "Beauty and the Beast", by Gabrielle-Suzanne Barbot de Villeneuve.

 Like all other Golden Films productions, the film features three original songs and an underlying track of fully orchestrated classical music.

 Produced by Golden Films and the "American Film Investment Corporation", it was distributed to DVD in 2002 by "GoodTimes Entertainment", as part of its "Collectible Classics" line.
