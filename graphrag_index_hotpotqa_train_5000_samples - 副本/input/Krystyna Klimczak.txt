Krystyna Klimczak

Krystyna Klimczak (born September 24, 1992 in Oświęcim, Poland) is a Polish figure skater who has competed as a single skater and pair skater.

 As a pair skater, she competed with Janusz Karweta.

 They were the 2007 Polish national silver medalists.

 They received the host wildcard entry to the 2007-2008 Junior Grand Prix Final, where they placed 9th.

 Their partnership ended in 2009.
