2016–17 Penn State Lady Lions basketball team

The 2016–17 Penn State Lady Lions basketball team will represent Pennsylvania State University during the 2016–17 NCAA Division I women's basketball season.

 The Lady Lions, led by tenth year head coach Coquese Washington, play their home games at the Bryce Jordan Center and were members of the Big Ten Conference.

 They finished the season of 21–11, 9–7 in Big Ten play to finish in a tie for sixth place.

 They lost in the second round of the Big Ten Women's Tournament to Minnesota.

 They were invited to the Women's National Invitation Tournament where they defeated Ohio and Fordham in the first and second rounds before losing to Virginia Tech in the third round.
