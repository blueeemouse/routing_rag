Polish Basketball Supercup

The Polish Basketball Supercup (Polish: "Superpuchar Polski w Koszykówce") is the supercup game of Polish men's professional club basketball.

 Each season, the winner of the Polish League, plays against the winner of the Polish Cup, to cap off the new season.

 The first Polish Supercup game was played on September 4, 1999, and it was won by Śląsk Wrocław.
