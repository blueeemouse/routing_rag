Sabine Auken

Sabine Auken née Zenkel (born 4 January 1965) is a German bridge player.

 She has also played as Sabine Zenkel.

 Sometime prior to the 2014 European and World meets (summer and October), she ranked 24th among 73 Women World Grand Masters by world masterpoints (MP) and 4th by placing points that do not decay over time.
