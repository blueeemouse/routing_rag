Mustang (film)

Mustang is a 2015 internationally co-produced drama film directed by Turkish-French film director Deniz Gamze Ergüven.

 The film is set in a remote Turkish village and depicts the lives of five young orphaned sisters and challenges they face growing up as girls in a conservative society.

 The event that triggers the family backlash against the five sisters at the beginning of the film is based on Ergüven's personal life.
