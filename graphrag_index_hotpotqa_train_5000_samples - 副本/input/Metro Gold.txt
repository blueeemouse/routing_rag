Metro Gold

Metro Gold, also known as 9 Gold, was a Hindi-language terrestrial television channel in India.

 The channel was a joint venture of both DD Metro and Nine Gold of Kerry Packer and Vinay Maloo HFCL - Nine Broadcasting India; hence known as Metro Gold.

 During its first launch, the channel was more likely known as DD Metro channel's "primetime block" from 7 pm to 10 pm IST, where it showed its programmes only in the given three-hour span on DD Metro Channel.

 But when the programmes started becoming popular in the primetime slot, DD Metro tried to set up some realistic terms with Nine Gold, which created disagreement between the companies and led up the channel's closing.
