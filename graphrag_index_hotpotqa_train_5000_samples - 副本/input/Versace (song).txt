Versace (song)

"Versace" is the debut single by American hip hop group Migos.

 It was released on October 1, 2013, by Quality Control Music, 300 Entertainment and Atlantic Records.

 The track, which was included on their mixtape "Y.R.N. (Young Rich Niggas)" (2013) The song was produced by Zaytoven.

 Following a remix by Canadian rapper Drake, the song went viral and peaked at number 99 on the US "Billboard" Hot 100 chart.

 Drake performed it at the 2013 iHeartRadio Music Festival.
