Daily Record (Morristown)

The Daily Record is a seven-day morning daily newspaper located in Parsippany-Troy Hills, New Jersey.

 Sometimes called the Morristown Daily Record because of its historical ties to nearby Morristown, New Jersey, the paper serves the greater Morris County area of northern New Jersey.

 It is owned by Gannett Company, who purchased it from the Goodson Newspaper Group in 1998.

 Goodson had owned the paper since 1987.
