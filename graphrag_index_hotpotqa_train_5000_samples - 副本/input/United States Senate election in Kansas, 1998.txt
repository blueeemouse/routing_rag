United States Senate election in Kansas, 1998

The 1998 United States Senate election in Kansas was held November 3, 1998.

 Incumbent Republican U.S. Senator Sam Brownback won re-election to his first full term.

 Brownback was first elected in a special election held in 1996, when then-Senator Bob Dole resigned to campaign for U.S. President, after 27 years in the Senate.

 This would've been Dole's seventh term in office had he remained in his seat.
