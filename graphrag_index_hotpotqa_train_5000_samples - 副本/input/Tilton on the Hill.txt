Tilton on the Hill

Tilton on the Hill is a village and civil parish (called Tilton) in the Harborough district of Leicestershire.

 The population of the civil parish at the 2011 census was 601.

 It lies 2 miles north of the A47, on the B6047 to Melton Mowbray.

 Halstead civil parish (grid reference [ SK750056] ) was merged with Tilton in 1935, while the deserted medieval village of Whatborough ([ SK772059] ) was merged in 1994.

 Marefield remains a separate civil parish, but is part of the Tilton Electoral Ward.

 St Peter's Tilton, the Parish Church is in the parish of Halstead, as is the vicarage.
