Brian Ralph

Brian Ralph (born 1973) is a U.S. alternative cartoonist.

 His illustrations have appeared in "Wired" and the "New York Post".

 His debut graphic novel, "Cave-In", was nominated for three Harvey Awards, one Eisner Award, and listed as one of the "Comics Journal"'s "five best comics of 1999".

 His second graphic novel, "Climbing Out", was awarded a Xeric Grant in 2001.

 His third graphic novel, "Daybreak", was published by Drawn and Quarterly in September 2011.
