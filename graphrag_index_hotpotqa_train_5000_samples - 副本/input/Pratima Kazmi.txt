Pratima Kazmi

Pratima Kazmi (Hindi: प्रतिमा काज़मी , Urdu: پرتما کاظمی‎ ) is an Indian television actress who has worked in many Bollywood movies and Hindi television drama series.

 Although she now works in Hindi movies & dramas, she started her career in 1997 with an English film called Sixth Happiness, produced by an Indian director Waris Hussein, in which she plays the role of a 'brothel madam'.
