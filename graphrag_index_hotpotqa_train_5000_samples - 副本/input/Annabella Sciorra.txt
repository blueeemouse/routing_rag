Annabella Sciorra

Annabella Gloria Philomena Sciorra ( , ; born March 29, 1960) is an Italian-American actress.

 Her film roles include "Cadillac Man" (1990), "Jungle Fever" (1991), "The Hand That Rocks the Cradle" (1992), "Cop Land" (1997) and "What Dreams May Come" (1998).

 She was nominated for a 2001 Emmy Award for playing Gloria Trillo in "The Sopranos" (2001–04).
