2010 Ulster Rally

2010 Ulster Rally incorporating Rally Northern Ireland was held between 20–21 August 2010.

 The Rally HQ located in Antrim, Co Antrim, Northern Ireland and organised by the Northern Ireland Motor Club (NIMC) The 2010 Ulster Rally was the fifth round of the 2010 MSA British Rally Championship and the sixth round of the 2010 MSA British Historic Rally Championship.
