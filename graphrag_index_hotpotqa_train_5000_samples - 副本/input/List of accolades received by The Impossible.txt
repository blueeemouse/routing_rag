List of accolades received by The Impossible

"The Impossible" (Spanish: Lo Imposible ) is a 2012 English-language Spanish disaster drama directed by Juan Antonio Bayona and written by Sergio G. Sánchez.

 It is based on the experience of María Belón and her family in the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami.

 The cast includes Naomi Watts, Ewan McGregor, and Tom Holland.
