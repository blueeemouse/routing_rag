Pari Aike Formation

The Pari Aike Formation is a Late Cretaceous geologic formation of Cenomanian (formerly believed to be Maastrichtian) age in southern Patagonia, Argentina.

 The giant titanosaur "Puertasaurus", the megaraptoran "Orkoraptor", and the ornithopod "Talenkauen" have been recovered from the formation alongside turtles and crocodiles.
