Jim Fixx

James Fuller Fixx (April 23, 1932 – July 20, 1984) was an American who authored the 1977 best-selling book "The Complete Book of Running".

 He is credited with helping start America's fitness revolution, popularizing the sport of running and demonstrating the health benefits of regular jogging.

 He died of a heart attack while jogging at 52 years of age; his father Calvin Fixx had died even younger of the same.

 His genetic predisposition for heart problems and other previous lifestyle factors may have caused his heart attack.
