2005 All-Pacific-10 Conference football team

The 2005 All-Pacific-10 Conference football team consists of American football players chosen by various organizations for All-Pacific-10 Conference teams for the 2005 college football season.

 The USC Trojans won the conference, posting an undefeated 8–0 conference record (though this was later vacated).

.

 USC then lost to the Texas Longhorns in the Rose Bowl BCS National Championship Game 41 to 38.

 USC running back Reggie Bush was voted Pac-10 Offensive Player of the Year.

 Oregon defensive tackle Haloti Ngata and Arizona State linebacker Dale Robinson were voted Pat Tillman Pac-10 Co-Defensive Players of the Year.
