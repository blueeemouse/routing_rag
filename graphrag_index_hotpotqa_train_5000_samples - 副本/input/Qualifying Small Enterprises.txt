Qualifying Small Enterprises

A Qualifying Small Enterprise (QSE) is one of the categories of South African businesses as per the Broad Based Black Economic Empowerment Act.

 "The Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment Act (53/2003): Codes of Good Practice on Black Economic Empowerment" was gazetted on 9 February 2007.

 A Qualifying Small Enterprise is a business with a turnover between R10 million and R50 million and is measured using the QSE Scorecard.
