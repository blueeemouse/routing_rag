Hookah (song)

"Hookah" is a song by American rapper Tyga.

 It was released on April 9, 2014 as the second promotional single prior to "".

 The song, produced by London on da Track, features a guest appearance from fellow American rapper Young Thug.

 "Hookah" debuted at number 94 on the US "Billboard" Hot 100 and has peaked at number 85.

 In December 2014, the song was certified Gold by the Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA).
