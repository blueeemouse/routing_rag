Jonathan Spector

Jonathan Michael Paul Spector (born March 1, 1986) is an American soccer player who plays as a defender for Orlando City and the United States national team.

 He started his professional career in England when he was signed by Manchester United as a teenager, but rarely appeared for the club.

 He played on loan at Charlton Athletic, then in 2006 joined West Ham United, for whom he played over 100 games before he was released at the end of the 2010–11 season.

 He then spent five-and-a-half seasons with Birmingham City.
