Rough Landing, Holly

"Rough Landing, Holly" is a song by the American pop punk band Yellowcard.

 The song was written collaboratively by all the band members for their fifth album, "Lights and Sounds" (2006).

 The track is built around an introductory guitar sound, followed by a soaring chorus, and heavy beating drums.

 "Rough Landing, Holly" is one of the songs from the album that is based on a character, Holly Wood, that Yellowcard had developed while working on "Lights and Sounds".
