Matt Maiellaro

Matthew Gerard Maiellaro (Born August 17, 1966) is an American voice actor, filmmaker and musician, best known as the co-creator and writer of the cult television animated Adult Swim shows, "Aqua Teen Hunger Force" and "Perfect Hair Forever", and creator of "12 oz.

 Mouse".

 He is a native of Pensacola, Florida, and a graduate of Pensacola Catholic High School.
