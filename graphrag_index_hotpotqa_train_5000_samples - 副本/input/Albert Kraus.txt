Albert Kraus

Albert “The Hurricane” Kraus (born August 3, 1980 in Oss) is a Dutch welterweight kickboxer.

 He was the first K-1 World MAX Tournament Champion in 2002 and also the first SUPERKOMBAT Middleweight Champion.

 Kraus has also held four separate world titles in kickboxing and Muay Thai.

 He is currently fighting out of Super Pro Gym in GLORY.
