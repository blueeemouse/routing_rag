JaQuavis Coleman

JaQuavis Coleman is an American author.

 He is a member of the YMCMB book division, and along with his wife Ashley Antoinette, via the pen name Ashley & Jaquavis, is a New York Times bestselling author.

 He is married to New York Times Best Selling Author Ashley Antoinette.

 Ashley Antoinette and Jaquavis were named by "Ebony" magazine as one of the most powerful couples in 2012.
