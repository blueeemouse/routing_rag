Raymond C. Hoiles

Raymond Cyrus "R.C." Hoiles (November 24, 1878 – October 31, 1970) was an American newspaper publisher.

 He was born in Alliance, Ohio and started his career as a subscription solicitor in the local newspaper "The Alliance Review".

 Hoiles and his brother bought among many newspapers "Santa Ana Daily Register" in 1935.

 Hoiles became president of Freedom Newspapers in 1950, in which position he stayed until his death in 1970.
