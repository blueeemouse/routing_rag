John Phillips House

John Phillips, born in 1814, was a native of Wiltshire England who came to the U.S. in 1834 and settled in Florida.

 After living in New Orleans—where he met and married Elizabeth Hibbard in 1839—and St. Louis, he came to Oregon and bought the Turner donation land claim in Polk County for $100.

 The locale was once known as Spring Valley Ranch.

 John Phillips hired carpenter Samuel Coad to build a house for him there.
