Namaste Tower

Namaste Tower is a 316 m tall skyscraper currently under construction in Mumbai, India.

 It will be a mixed use skyscraper with 63 floors that will house a 380-room W Hotel, office and retail space.

 It has been designed by Atkins, Dubai.

 The design resembles the Namaste gesture: two wings of the hotel are clasped together like hands greeting.
