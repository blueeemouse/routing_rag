Exo (novel)

Exo is the fourth novel in the "Jumper" series by Steven Gould and the fifth in the "Jumper" universe.

 The first two novels—"Jumper" and "Reflex"—tell a connecting story of David and Millie, which is continued with their daughter, Cent, in "Impulse" and now "Exo"; while the fifth novel, "", is the back-story for a character from the 2008 movie "Jumper" and is not associated with the story or characters in the novels.
