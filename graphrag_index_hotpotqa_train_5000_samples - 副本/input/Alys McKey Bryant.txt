Alys McKey Bryant

Alys McKey Bryant (1880–1954) was an American aviator.

 She was the first woman to fly on the Pacific Coast and in Canada, and one of the few female members of the Early Birds of Aviation—individuals who had solo piloted an aircraft prior to December 17, 1916.

 She cut short her brief career as a pilot after her husband, John Bryant, died while performing an air show in Victoria, Canada, in August 1913.

 During World War I she trained pilots.
