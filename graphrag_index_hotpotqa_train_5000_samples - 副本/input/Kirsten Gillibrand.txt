Kirsten Gillibrand

Kirsten Elizabeth Gillibrand (née Rutnik; ; born December 9, 1966) is an American attorney and politician who since 2009, has served as the junior United States Senator from New York, alongside the Democratic Leader of the United States Senate, Chuck Schumer.

 Before the Senate, she served for two years (2007-09) in the United States House of Representatives, representing New York's 20th congressional district.

 She is a member of the Democratic Party.
