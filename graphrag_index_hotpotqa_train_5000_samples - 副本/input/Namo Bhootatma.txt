Namo Bhootatma

Namo Bhootatma is a 2014 Kannada horror comedy film directed by ace choreographer Murali.

 A remake of the 2014 Tamil film "Yaamirukka Bayamey" (which itself was based on the Korean movie"The Quiet Family"), it stars Komal Kumar, Iswarya Menon, Anaswara Kumar, Gayathri Iyer, and Harish Raj in the lead roles.

 Telugu comedian Ali makes his second Kannada movie with this film.

 Daler Mehndi has also sung a song in the film.
