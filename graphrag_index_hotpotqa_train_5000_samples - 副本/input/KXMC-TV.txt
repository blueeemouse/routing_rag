KXMC-TV

KXMC-TV, channel 13, is the CBS-affiliated television station for Minot, North Dakota.

 It broadcasts a high definition digital signal on VHF channel 13 (or virtual channel 13.1 via PSIP) from a transmitter southwest of Minot.

 The station can also be seen on Midcontinent cable channel 3 in the Minot area, cable channel 13 in most other areas and SRT cable channel 3.
