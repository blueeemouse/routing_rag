Miley Cyrus videography

American actress, singer, and songwriter Miley Cyrus has appeared in music videos, films, and television series.

 From 2006 to 2011, Cyrus starred in the television series "Hannah Montana" as Miley Stewart and her alter ego, Hannah Montana.

 In 2009, Cyrus starred in the feature film "", whose soundtrack included her lead single "The Climb".

 Her other films included voicing the role of Penny in the animated film "Bolt" in 2008, "The Last Song" with Liam Hemsworth in 2010, and "LOL" with Demi Moore in 2012.

 Her song "When I Look at You" was part of "The Last Song" soundtrack with the music video directed by the film's producer, Adam Shankman.
