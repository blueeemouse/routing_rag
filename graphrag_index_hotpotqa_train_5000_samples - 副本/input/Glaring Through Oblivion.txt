Glaring Through Oblivion

Glaring Through Oblivion is a 2011 book of poetry written by Serj Tankian, the Armenian-American lead singer of the band System of a Down.

 It is his second book of poetry, after "Cool Gardens" (2002).

 "Glaring Through Oblivion" was published by HarperCollins Publishers and printed in China, and released on March 22, 2011.
