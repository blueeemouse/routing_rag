Alphabet of Sirach

The Alphabet of ben Sirach ("Alphabetum Siracidis", "Othijoth ben Sira") is an anonymous medieval text inspired by the Wisdom of Sirach.

 It is dated to anywhere between 700 and 1000 CE.

 It is a compilation of two lists of proverbs, 22 in Aramaic and 22 in Hebrew, both arranged as alphabetic acrostics. Each proverb is followed by an Haggadic commentary.

 The work has been characterized as satirical, and it contains references to masturbation, incest and flatulence.

 The text has been translated into Latin, Yiddish, Judeo-Spanish, French and German.

 A partial English translation appeared in Stern and Mirsky (1998).
