Born to Kill (1947 film)

Born to Kill is a 1947 American film noir starring Lawrence Tierney and directed by Robert Wise.

 It was the first film noir to be directed by Wise, who later directed "The Set-Up" (1949), "The Captive City" (1952), and "Odds Against Tomorrow" (1959).

 The film also features Claire Trevor, Walter Slezak, and Elisha Cook Jr.
