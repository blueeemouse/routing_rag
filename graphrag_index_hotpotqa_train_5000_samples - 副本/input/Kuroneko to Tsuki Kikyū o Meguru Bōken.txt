Kuroneko to Tsuki Kikyū o Meguru Bōken

Kuroneko to Tsuki Kikyū o Meguru Bōken (黒猫と月気球をめぐる冒険 ) is Yui Horie's second album.

 It contains an alternate version of the song Love Destiny from the show Sister Princess.

 The name of the album could be translated "The adventures of the Black Cat with the Moon Balloon."

 The name of the Black Cat League, Yui Horie fan club, probably originates itself from this album.
