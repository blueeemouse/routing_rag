1996 San Marino Grand Prix

The 1996 San Marino Grand Prix was a Formula One motor race held on 5 May 1996 at the Autodromo Enzo e Dino Ferrari, Imola, Emilia-Romagna, Italy.

 It was the fifth round of the 1996 Formula One season.

 The 63-lap race was won by Damon Hill driving for the Williams team after starting from second position.

 Michael Schumacher finished second driving a Ferrari, with Gerhard Berger third driving for the Benetton team.

 Jacques Villeneuve retired near the end of the race after being hit by Jean Alesi.
