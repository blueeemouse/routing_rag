Neopolitan Dreams

"Neopolitan Dreams" is a song performed and written by Australian singer–songwriter Lisa Mitchell from her second extended play "Welcome to the Afternoon" (2008) and first studio album, "Wonder" (2009).

 It was released in August 2008 by Scorpio Music and peaked at number 70 on the Australian ARIA chart in August 2009, a year after it was first released.

 Sonnengruss ft. Jana recorded a cover of the song.
