W. E. B. Du Bois High School

W. E. B. Du Bois High School of Environmental Science (officially referred to as W. E. B. Du Bois High School) is a public high school located in northeast Baltimore, Maryland.

 The school is named after sociologist and civil-rights activist Dr. W. E. B. Du Bois.

 The school is situated in the former Northern High School and shares the structure with the Reginald F. Lewis High School.
