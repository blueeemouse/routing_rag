Hollywood Hills (song)

"Hollywood Hills" is a song recorded by Finnish rock band Sunrise Avenue for their third studio album, "Out of Style" (2011).

 It was written by the lead vocalist of the band, Samu Haber, while the production was done by Finish producer and musician Jukka Immonen.

 It was released as the lead single from the album on 25 February 2011; the song was made available for Digital download via Amazon.

 "Hollywood Hills" it's a emo-pop song, which according to Jon O'Brien of AllMusic helps to the band to sound tighter than ever did.

 Following its release it charted in several European countries.

 It was more successful in Finland where it reached number two.

 It also reached number three in Austria, Germany and Switzerland.
