Terrified

Terrified is the seventh album by American heavy metal band Quiet Riot.

 It is the band's first album in five years, and marks the return of singer Kevin DuBrow after his firing in 1987.

 It is bassist Kenny Hillery's only studio album with the band, and drummer Bobby Rondinelli plays on several songs.

 Many of the album's songs were featured in Charles Band's movie "Dollman vs. Demonic Toys", with the album itself being released on Moonstone Records, the soundtrack offshoot of Band's film company Full Moon Entertainment.
