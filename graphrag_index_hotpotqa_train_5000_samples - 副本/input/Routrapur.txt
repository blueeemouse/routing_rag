Routrapur

Routrapur (Odia: ) is a village of the 42 Mouza island situated at on the banks of Kathajodi river of Cuttack Sadar Tehsil, Cuttack district, Odisha under Cuttack Sadar Block, Near about 8 km from Cuttack Town.

 It is known as the birth place of legend odia singer Sikandar Alam formerly known as "Salabega of Modern Odisha".
