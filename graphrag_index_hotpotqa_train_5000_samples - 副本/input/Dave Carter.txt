Dave Carter

Dave Carter (August 13, 1952 – July 19, 2002) was an American folk singer-songwriter who described his style as "post-modern mythic American folk music."

 He was one half of the duo Dave Carter and Tracy Grammer, who were heralded as the new "voice of modern folk music" in the months before Carter's unexpected death in July 2002.

 They were ranked as number one on the year-end list for "Top Artists" on the "Folk Music Radio Airplay Chart" for 2001 and 2002, and their popularity has endured in the years following Carter's death.
