Withoutabox

Withoutabox is a website founded in January 2000 by David Straus, Joe Neulight and Charles Neulight which allows independent filmmakers to self-distribute their films.

 The first product launched was the International Film Festival Submission system.

 Withoutabox works with film festivals and filmmakers all over the world.

 In January 2008, Withoutabox was acquired by IMDb, a subsidiary of Amazon.com.
