T. S. Eliot

Thomas Stearns Eliot, {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (26 September 1888 – 4 January 1965) was a British essayist, publisher, playwright, literary and social critic, and "one of the twentieth century's major poets".

 He moved from his native United States to England in 1914 at the age of 25, settling, working, and marrying there.

 He eventually became a British subject in 1927 at the age of 39, renouncing his American citizenship.
