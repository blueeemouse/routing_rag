Aaron Pearson

Aaron Dantianto Pearson (born August 22, 1964) is a former American football linebacker who played three seasons with the Kansas City Chiefs of the National Football League.

 He was drafted by the Kansas City Chiefs in the eleventh round of the 1986 NFL Draft.

 He first enrolled at Itawamba Junior College before transferring to Mississippi State University.

 Pearson attended Gadsden High School in Gadsden, Alabama.
