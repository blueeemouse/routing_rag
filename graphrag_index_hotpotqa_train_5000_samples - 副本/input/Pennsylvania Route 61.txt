Pennsylvania Route 61

Pennsylvania Route 61 (PA 61) is an 81.801 mi -long state highway in the U.S. state of Pennsylvania.

 The route is signed on a north–south direction, from U.S. Route 222 Business in Reading to U.S. Route 11 and U.S. Route 15 in Shamokin Dam.

 PA 61 meets up with Pennsylvania Route 54 in Ashland and these highways split just outside Ashland.

 PA 54 goes towards Mount Carmel and PA 61 heads towards Centralia.
