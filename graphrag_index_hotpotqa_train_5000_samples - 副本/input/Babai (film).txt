Babai (film)

Babai is a 2015 internationally co-produced drama film directed by Visar Morina.

 Morina won the award for Best Director at the 2015 Karlovy Vary International Film Festival.

 The film was selected as the Kosovan entry for the Best Foreign Language Film at the 88th Academy Awards but it was not nominated.

 It is the most expensive Kosovan film produced, with a budget of €1.7 million.
