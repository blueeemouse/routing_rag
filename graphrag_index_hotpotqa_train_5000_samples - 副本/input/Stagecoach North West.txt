Stagecoach North West

Stagecoach North West was a major operator of bus services in North West England.

 It was a subsidiary of the Stagecoach Group, and had its origins in the purchase of Cumberland in 1987 and Ribble Motor Services in 1988 from the National Bus Company.

 The head office of Stagecoach North West was in Carlisle.

 Although the cities of Liverpool and Manchester are in the North West of England, Stagecoach Manchester and Stagecoach Merseyside were run as separate divisions.
