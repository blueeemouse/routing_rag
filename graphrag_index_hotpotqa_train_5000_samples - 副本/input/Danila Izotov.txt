Danila Izotov

Danila Sergeevich Izotov (Russian: Данила Сергеевич Изотов ; born 2 October 1991) is a Russian swimmer, a member of the Russian National team since 2008, and a multiple medalist at the Olympic Games and World Championships, as well as a European champion.

 He is coached by his father, Sergey Alexandrovich Izotov.

 He is the nephew of soviet swimmer Elvira Vasilkova.
