Canada–South Korea relations

Canada–South Korea relations are foreign relations between Canada and the Republic of Korea.

 Canadian soldiers participated in the defense of South Korea during the Korean War.

 Full diplomatic relations between Canada and South Korea were established on January 14, 1963.

 Canada has an embassy in Seoul, and a consulate in Busan.

 South Korea has an embassy in Ottawa and three Consulates-General, in Montreal, Toronto and Vancouver.

 Both nations are full members of APEC, OECD and the G20.
