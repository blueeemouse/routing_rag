Military College Jhelum

Military College Jhelum or MCJ, is a military high school, located at Sarai Alamgir (Jhelum District) but now in (Gujrat District), Pakistan.

 Though it is physically located in Gujrat District, due to its very close proximity it is still associated with the City of Jhelum, and is considered to be in the cantonment limit of Jhelum.

 The institution feeds the Pakistan Military Academy, Kakul.

 Military College Jhelum is one of three military colleges in Pakistan; the others being Military College Murree and Military College Sui.
