2003 Seattle Seahawks season

The 2003 Seattle Seahawks season was the team's 28th season with the National Football League.

 After going 31–33 in Mike Holmgren's first four years as head coach, the Seahawks clawed to 10–6 and made the NFC playoffs as a wild card team, the first of nine playoff appearances in twelve seasons.

 However, it was not to be as they fell 33-27 to the Green Bay Packers in the opening round thanks to an interception returned for a touchdown by Green Bay's Al Harris in overtime.

 Following the season, Hall of Fame defensive tackle John Randle retired.
