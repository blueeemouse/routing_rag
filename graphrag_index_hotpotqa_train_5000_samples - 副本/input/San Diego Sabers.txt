San Diego Sabers

The San Diego Sabers are a junior ice hockey team.

 They are a member of the Western Division in the Western States Hockey League based in Escondido, California and plays at the Iceoplex-Escondido.

 The team was the fourth team to be known as the San Diego Gulls from 2008 to 2015 until it gave up the nickname to the American Hockey League San Diego Gulls before the 2015–16 season.
