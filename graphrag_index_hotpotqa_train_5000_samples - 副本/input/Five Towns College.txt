Five Towns College

Five Towns College is a for-profit institution of higher learning located in Dix Hills, Long Island, New York.

 The College's fully accredited status was reaffirmed by the Middle States Commission on Higher Education in 2014.

 The Five Towns College is also accredited by the National Council for Accreditation of Teacher Education (NCATE).

 The College holds an Absolute Charter issued by the New York State Board of Regents, and all of its educational programs are registered by the New York State Education Department, Office of College and University Evaluation.
