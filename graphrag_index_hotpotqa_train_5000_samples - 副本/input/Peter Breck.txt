Peter Breck

Joseph Peter Breck (March 13, 1929 – February 6, 2012) was an American character actor.

 The rugged, dark-haired Breck played the gambler and gunfighter Doc Holliday on the ABC/Warner Bros. television series "Maverick" but is best known for his role as Victoria Barkley's (Barbara Stanwyck) hot-tempered, middle son Nick in the popular 1960s ABC/Four Star Western, "The Big Valley".

 Breck was also the leading man in an earlier ABC/Four Star Western television series entitled "Black Saddle".
