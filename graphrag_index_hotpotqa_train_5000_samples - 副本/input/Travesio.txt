Travesio

Travesio (Friulian: "Travês") is a "comune" (municipality) in the Province of Pordenone in the Italian region Friuli-Venezia Giulia, located about 100 km northwest of Trieste and about 30 km northeast of Pordenone.

 As of 31 December 2004, it had a population of 1,816 and an area of 28.8 km2 .
