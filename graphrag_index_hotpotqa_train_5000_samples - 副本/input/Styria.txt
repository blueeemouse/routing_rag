Styria

Styria (German: Steiermark , ] , Slovene/Croatian: "Štajerska" , Hungarian: "Stájerország" , ) is a state or "Bundesland", located in the southeast of Austria.

 In area it is the second largest of the nine Austrian federated states, covering 16401 km2 .

 It borders Slovenia and the Austrian states of Upper Austria, Lower Austria, Salzburg, Burgenland, and Carinthia.
