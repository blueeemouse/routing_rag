Stadio Ennio Tardini

Stadio Ennio Tardini, commonly referred to as just Il Tardini, is a football stadium in Parma, Italy, located near the centre of Parma, between the town centre and the city walls.

 It is the home of Parma Calcio 1913.

 The stadium was built in 1923 and was named after one of Parma's former presidents, Ennio Tardini.

 The stadium is the nineteenth largest football stadium in Italy and the second largest in Emilia–Romagna with a capacity of 22,352 spectators.

 The stadium is the sixth oldest Italian football ground still in use.
