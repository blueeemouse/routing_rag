Donnie Steele

Donald S. "Donnie" Steele (born October 6, 1971) is an American heavy metal musician.

 Born in Des Moines, Iowa, he is best known as one of two original guitarists in Slipknot.

 Steele co-founded Slipknot in 1995, performing on the band's debut demo "Mate.
