Girder Records

Girder Records is a Christian Metal label started by Greg Hays of GirderMusic.com.

 Girder Records has re-issued classic releases such as the re-recording of the Whitecross "Self-Titled" album, now called Nineteen Eighty Seven.

 That release was followed by the first ever re-issue of Strkyen "First Strike" on CD.

 In 2008 Girder Records release Sin Dizzy "He's Not Dead" which was started by Oz Fox and Tim Gaines or 80's platinum selling artist Stryper.
