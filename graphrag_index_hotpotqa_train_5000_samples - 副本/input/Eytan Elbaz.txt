Eytan Elbaz

Eytan Elbaz is an American entrepreneur and investor best known for co-founding, along with his brother Gil Elbaz and Adam Weissman, Applied Semantics (ASI), which would later become Google AdSense.

 He is an angel investor and founder of many Los Angeles companies, most notably Scopely, a mobile gaming startup.

 He is also the founder and Chairman of the Board of Render Media, a new-age digital media start up.
