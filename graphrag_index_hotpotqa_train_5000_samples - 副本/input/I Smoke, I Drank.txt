I Smoke, I Drank

"I Smoke, I Drank", also known as "Do It Big", is the lead single from Body Head Bangerz's debut album, "".

 The original track features Lil' Boosie and Young Bleed but only the remix of the song, "I Smoke, I Drank (Remix)" featuring YoungBloodZ, charted.

 Though only the latter charted, it is often credited as simply "I Smoke, I Drank".

 This single, along with Body Head Bangerz's second single, "Can't Be Touched" featuring Trouble Tha Truth, helped the group earn widespread recognition among hip hop enthusiasts.
