Quiet Riot

Quiet Riot is an American heavy metal band.

 The band was founded in 1973, by guitarist Randy Rhoads and bassist Kelly Garni under the name Mach 1.

 They then changed the name to Little Women, before settling on Quiet Riot in May 1975.

 The band's name was inspired by a conversation with Rick Parfitt of the British band Status Quo, who expressed desire to name a band "Quite Right," and his thick English accent made it sound like he was saying "Quiet Riot."

 The band is ranked at No. 100 on VH1's 100 Greatest Artists of Hard Rock.
