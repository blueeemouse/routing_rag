Willie the Kid (rapper)

William Jackson (born September 25, 1981), better known by his stage name Willie the Kid, is an American rapper from Grand Rapids, Michigan, United States.

 He is most famous for being DJ Drama's protégé and appearing on both DJ Drama albums "" and "" as well as Lil Wayne's "Dedication 2" and "Dedication 3" mixtape.

 He is the younger brother of Wu-Tang Clan affiliate La the Darkman.

 He released his debut album, "Absolute Greatness", in September 2008.
