2008 French Grand Prix

The 2008 French Grand Prix (formally the XCIV Grand Prix de France) was a Formula One motor race held on 22 June 2008 at the Circuit de Nevers Magny-Cours, France.

 This race is (to date) the last French Grand Prix.

 It was the eighth race of the 2008 Formula One season.

 The 70-lap race was won by Felipe Massa for the Ferrari team after starting from second position.

 Kimi Räikkönen, who started from pole position, finished second in the other Ferrari car; Jarno Trulli was third in a Toyota.
