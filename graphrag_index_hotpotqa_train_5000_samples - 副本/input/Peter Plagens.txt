Peter Plagens

Peter Plagens (born 1941 in Dayton, Ohio) is an American art critic, painter, and novelist.

 From 1989 until 2003 Plagens was a senior writer and art critic for "Newsweek".

 As a novelist, Plagens is the author of "Time for Robo" (1999, Black Heron Press, ISBN  ) and "The Art Critic", which was and is serialized online at artnet.

 As a painter, he has been represented by the Nancy Hoffman Gallery, New York.
