Old Ottawa West

(Old) Ottawa West (also known as Champlain Park) is an older urban neighbourhood in Kitchissippi Ward in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada.

 It was considered in the 1930s as the west end of town.

 The boundaries of the neighbourhood are the Ottawa River (to the north) and Scott Street (to the south).

 To the east is Tunney's Pasture and to the west is Island Park Drive.

 Westboro Beach is also to the west.

 The population as of the 2011 Census was 1008.
