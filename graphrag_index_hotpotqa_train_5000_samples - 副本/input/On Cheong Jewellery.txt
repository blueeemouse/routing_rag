On Cheong Jewellery

On Cheong Jewellery was founded in 1936 by Mr Ho Yew Ping, an emigrant of Guangdong Shunde province of China, who left Guangdong at age 14 to seek his fortune in Malaysia in 1925.

 In Penang and later Perak, he apprenticed as a goldsmith before marrying in Guangdong and moving to Singapore with his newly-wedded wife.

 On Cheong started a small goldsmith shop known as On Cheong Goldsmith on North Bridge Road.

 The name "On Cheong" or "安昌" in Chinese is derived from the phrase "平安昌盛", meaning peace and prosperity.

 On Cheong moved to its current location, a shophouse on South Bridge Road, in 1941.

 In the 1940s, Mr Ho Yew Ping also acquired Kwong Yik and Nan Jing Goldsmiths to expand the company.

 The company was incorporated as On Cheong Co Pte Ltd in 1949.

 Mr Ho Yew Ping’s wife, Mdm Leung Chew Fun, and the eldest son, Dr Ho Nai Kiong, took over the operations of the business after Mr Ho Yew Ping's passing in 1965.

 Its current Managing Director, Mr Ho Nai Chuen, the youngest of Mr Ho Yew Ping’s children, began managing the business in 1985.

 On Cheong Jewellery is one of the pioneer members of the Singapore Jewellers Association (SJA), a non-profit organisation established in 1939 representing jewellery operators in Singapore.

 Mr Ho is currently the President of SJA.
