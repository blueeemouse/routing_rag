Lino DiSalvo

Lino DiSalvo, is an American animator and film director.

 In February 2016, Lino signed on to develop and direct the upcoming Playmobil movie.

 The animated feature will be produced by ON Animation, known for "The Little Prince", and is set to release at the end of 2018.

 Previously, he is known for serving as Head of Animation on Academy Award for Best Animated Feature winning film "Frozen".
