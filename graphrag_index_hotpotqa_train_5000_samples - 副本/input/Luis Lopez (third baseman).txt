Luis Lopez (third baseman)

Luis Lopez (born October 5, 1973 in Brooklyn, New York) is a former Major League Baseball third baseman and first baseman.

 He played parts of two seasons in the major leagues: 2001 for the Toronto Blue Jays and 2004 for the Montreal Expos.

 In 2005, he played in Nippon Professional Baseball for the Tohoku Rakuten Golden Eagles.

 He last played for the Bridgeport Bluefish of the Atlantic League in 2013.
