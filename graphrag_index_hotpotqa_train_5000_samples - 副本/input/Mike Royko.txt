Mike Royko

Michael Royko Jr. (September 19, 1932 – April 29, 1997) was a Chicago newspaper columnist.

 Over his 30-year career, he wrote over 7,500 daily columns for three newspapers, the "Chicago Daily News", the "Chicago Sun-Times", and the "Chicago Tribune".

 Originally a humorist focused on life in Chicago, he authored "Boss", a scathing negative biography of Chicago Mayor Richard J. Daley in 1971.

 He was the winner of the 1972 Pulitzer Prize for commentary
