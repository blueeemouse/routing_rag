Holding On to You (Twenty One Pilots song)

"Holding On to You" is a song written and recorded by American musical duo Twenty One Pilots, for their second studio album "Regional at Best".

 A partially re-recorded version of the song appeared on their third studio album, "Vessel".

 "Holding On to You" was released as the first single from "Vessel" in the iTunes Store worldwide on September 11, 2012.

 A video for the song was released on November 15.

 The video was directed by Jordan Bahat.

 The song impacted alternative radio on December 4, 2012, and released to mainstream radio on June 11, 2013.

 It is the band's debut single.
