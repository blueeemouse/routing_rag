Jason Bourne (film)

Jason Bourne is a 2016 American action thriller film directed by Paul Greengrass and written by Greengrass and Christopher Rouse.

 It is the fifth installment of the "Bourne" film series and a direct sequel to "The Bourne Ultimatum" (2007).

 Matt Damon reprises his role as the main character, former CIA assassin and psychogenic amnesiac Jason Bourne, following his absence in the previous installment "The Bourne Legacy" (2012).
