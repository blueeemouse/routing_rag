The Word Exchange

The Word Exchange is a science fiction novel by Alena Graedon, published in 2014.

 It is a dystopian thriller set in the not very distant future when the printed word has nearly vanished, technology dominates, and language has become a commodity.

 An online "Word Exchange" has taken the effort out of looking up words, a mere convenience until real dictionaries are no longer available.

 Then it becomes possible to corner the market for words.

 The novel has been translated into eight different languages.
