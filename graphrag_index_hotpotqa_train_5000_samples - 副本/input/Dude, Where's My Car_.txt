Dude, Where's My Car?

Dude, Where's My Car?

 is a 2000 American comedy film directed by Danny Leiner.

 The film stars Ashton Kutcher and Seann William Scott as two young men who find themselves unable to remember where they parked their vehicle after a night of recklessness.

 Supporting cast members include Kristy Swanson, Jennifer Garner, and Marla Sokoloff.
