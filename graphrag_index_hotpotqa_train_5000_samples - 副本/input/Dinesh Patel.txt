Dinesh Patel

Dinesh Kumar Patel (born May 8, 1989 in Lucknow, India) is an Indian right-handed baseball pitcher who played in the Pittsburgh Pirates organization.

 Along with Rinku Singh, he was the first Indian national ever to sign a contract with a major American sports club.

 Neither Patel nor Singh had ever thrown a baseball before beating over 37,000 competitors in "The Million Dollar Arm", an Indian reality television show designed to find new baseball talent.
