2007 Baylor Bears football team

The 2007 Baylor Bears football team (variously "Baylor", "BU", or the "Bears") represented Baylor University during the 2007 NCAA Division I FBS football season.

 They played their home games at Floyd Casey Stadium in Waco, Texas.

 The team was led by head coach Guy Morriss until he was fired on November 18, 2007 and replaced by Houston coach Art Briles.
