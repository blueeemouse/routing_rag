Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center

The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center, formerly called The Ohio State University Medical Center (OSUMC), is a multidisciplinary academic medical center located in Columbus, Ohio, United States, on the main campus of The Ohio State University (OSU).

 In 2010, the center was ranked one of "America's Best" by U.S. News & World Report in 11 specialties.

 OSU Medical Center was the only central Ohio hospital ranked as "America's Best" in 2010.

 However, as of 2016, the center's health-related programs garnered the best rankings of all at the medical center, including nursing-midwifery (#28), occupational therapy (#12), physical therapy (#10), and pharmacy (#6).

 In 2016, OSUMC was ranked in 7 specialties, with only one medicine-related area breaking into the Top 15 rankings (ear, nose, and throat).
