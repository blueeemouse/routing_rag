Cops vs. Thugs

Cops vs. Thugs (県警対組織暴力 , Kenkei tai Soshiki Bōryoku , lit.

 "Police vs. Violence Groups") is a 1975 Japanese yakuza film directed by Kinji Fukasaku.

 It won two Blue Ribbon Awards in 1976: Best Director (Fukasaku) and Best Actor (Sugawara). "

Complex" named it number 6 on their list of The 25 Best Yakuza Movies.

 Kino International released the film on DVD in North America in 2006.
