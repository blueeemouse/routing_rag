Paul Margulies

Paul Eli Margulies (1935–2014) was an American writer, philosopher and advertising-industry creative director.

 Margulies was famous for writing jingles ("Plop, Plop, Fizz, Fizz, Oh What a Relief it Is!

") and inventing tag lines such as "I Can't Believe I Ate the Whole Thing."

 A graduate of Dartmouth, with a degree in philosophy, Margulies retired at an early age to write about philosophy.
