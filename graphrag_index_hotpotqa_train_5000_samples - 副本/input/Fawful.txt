Fawful

Fawful, known in Japan as Gerakobits (ゲラコビッツ , Gerakobittsu ) , is a fictional character appearing in the "Mario & Luigi" series of role-playing video games developed by AlphaDream.

 The character is considered to be "insane," and often serves as either the main antagonist or the sidekick to the main antagonist in the games.
