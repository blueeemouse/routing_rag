Stan Bush

Stan Bush is an American singer-songwriter and musician whose most notable works include the songs "The Touch" and "Dare" from the soundtrack to the 1986 animated film "", "Hearts vs. Heads" from the soundtrack to the 1986 action/supernatural film "The Wraith", and "She's Got the Power", featured in the American voice dub of the animated series "Sailor Moon".

 He also had one track on the children's album "Take My Hand – Songs from the 100 Acre Wood" entitled "That's What Tiggers Do Best," released in 1995.

 Other notable works include the songs "Never Surrender," "Streets of Siam," and "Fight for Love" from the movie "Kickboxer", and "Fight to Survive" and "On My Own – Alone", the theme from "Bloodsport".

 In 1987, Bush (and back-up band Barrage) wrote and recorded the ballad "Love Don't Lie", which became a minor MTV hit when covered a year later by House of Lords.
