Battle of Arcis-sur-Aube

The Battle of Arcis-sur-Aube (20–21 March 1814) saw an Imperial French army under Napoleon face a much larger Allied army led by Karl Philipp, Prince of Schwarzenberg during the War of the Sixth Coalition.

 On the second day of fighting, Emperor Napoleon suddenly realized the long odds against him and hurriedly ordered a retreat.

 The French were able to disengage and withdraw to the north because of the hesitations of the Austrian Field Marshal Schwarzenberg.

 This was Napoleon's penultimate battle before his abdication and exile to Elba, the last being the Battle of Saint-Dizier.
