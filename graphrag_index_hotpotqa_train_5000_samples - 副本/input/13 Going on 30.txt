13 Going on 30

13 Going on 30 (released as Suddenly 30 in some countries) is a 2004 American romantic comedy fantasy film written by Josh Goldsmith and Cathy Yuspa and directed by Gary Winick.

 Starring Jennifer Garner, the film was produced by Revolution Studios for Columbia Pictures, and was released on April 23, 2004.

 It follows a 13-year-old girl who dreams of being popular.

 During her birthday party, she is humiliated by classmates and wishes that she was thirty years old.

 When she eventually does emerge, she finds herself five days shy of her 30th birthday, uncertain to how she got there.
