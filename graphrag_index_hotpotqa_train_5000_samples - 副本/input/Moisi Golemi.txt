Moisi Golemi

Moisi Golemi, also known as Moisi of Dibra (Albanian: "Moisiu i Dibrës" ), was an Albanian nobleman and a commander of the League of Lezhë.

 In 1443–44 he captured all Ottoman holdings in the area of Debar.

 For a brief period in the 1450s he joined the Ottomans, but soon abandoned them and returned to the League.

 He died in 1464, when he was executed publicly in Constantinople after being captured by the Ottoman army.

 In Albanian folk tradition, Golemi became a popular hero mostly through the "Song of Moisi Golemi" ("Kënga e Moisi Golemit").
