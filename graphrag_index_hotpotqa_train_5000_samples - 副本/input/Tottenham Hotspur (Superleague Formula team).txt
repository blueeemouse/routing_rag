Tottenham Hotspur (Superleague Formula team)

Tottenham Hotspur Superleague Formula team is the racing team of Tottenham Hotspur F.C., a football team that competes in England in the Premier League.

 The Tottenham Hotspur F.C. racing team competes in the Superleague Formula.

 It was operated in 2008 by GTA Motor Competición and will be operated by Alan Docking Racing during the 2009 season.
