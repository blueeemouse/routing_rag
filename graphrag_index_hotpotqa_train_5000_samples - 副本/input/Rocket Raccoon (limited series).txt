Rocket Raccoon (limited series)

Rocket Raccoon was a four-issue comic book limited series that was published in 1985 and featuring the eponymous character.

 It was written by Bill Mantlo, penciled by Mike Mignola and inked by Al Gordon (Issue # 3 was inked by Al Milgrom).

 All covers by Mignola and Gordon.

 The series took place on half world where Rocket and his anthropomorphic allies fought killer clowns with lethal juggling balls and deadly unicycles and who spoke in a stilted language full of alliteration and rhyme.
