Buakaw Banchamek

Sombat Banchamek (Thai: สมบัติ บัญชาเมฆ , born May 8, 1982) AKA Buakaw Banchamek (Thai: บัวขาว บัญชาเมฆ , Buakaw meaning "white lotus") is a Thai welterweight Muay Thai kickboxer, who formerly fought out of Por.

 Pramuk Gym, in Bangkok, Thailand, under the ring name Buakaw Por.

 Pramuk (Thai: บัวขาว ป.ประมุข ).

 He is the former two-time Omnoi Stadium champion, Lumpini Stadium Toyota Marathon champion, Thailand Featherweight champion and two time K-1 World MAX champion.

 As of 1 August 2017, he is ranked the #5 lightweight in the world by CombatPress.com.
