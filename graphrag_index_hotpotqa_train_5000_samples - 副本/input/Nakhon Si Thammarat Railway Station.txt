Nakhon Si Thammarat Railway Station

Nakhon Si Thammarat Railway Station is a railway station located in Tha Wang Subdistrict, Nakhon Si Thammarat City, Nakhon Si Thammarat.

 It is a class 1 railway station located 816.025 km from Thon Buri Railway Station.

 This station opened in October 1914 as part of the Southern Line section Khao Chum Thong Junction-Nakhon Si Thammarat.
