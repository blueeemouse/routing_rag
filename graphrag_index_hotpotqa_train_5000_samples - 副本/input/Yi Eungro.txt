Yi Eungro

Yi Eungro (in Hangul:이응로, in Hanja:李應魯, or Ung-no Lee, January 12, 1904 – January 10, 1989) was a Korean-born French painter whose works were chiefly focused on Easter art, Korean paintings and printmaker.

 He was born in Hongseong County, Chungcheongnam-do and spent his childhood in Yesan county.
