Steptoe and Son (film)

Steptoe and Son is a 1972 British comedy drama film and a spin-off from the popular British television comedy series of the same name about two father and son rag and bone men.

 It starred Wilfrid Brambell and Harry H. Corbett as the eponymous characters, Albert and Harold Steptoe respectively.

 It also features Carolyn Seymour.

 A sequel "Steptoe and Son Ride Again" was released the following year.
