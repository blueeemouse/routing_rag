Mookajjiya Kanasugalu

Mookajjiya Kanasugalu (English: Dreams of Mookajji) is a 1968 Kannada epic novel written by K. Shivaram Karanth.

 It won Jnaanapeeta award for best novel in the year 1977.

 The novel is about the thoughts of human being of today's generation.

 It deals with the beliefs, the origin of tradition etc.
