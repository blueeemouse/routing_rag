Leshi Singh

Leshi singh (Hindi: लेशी सिंह ) is a JDU member of the Legislative Assembly from the Dhamdaha seat in Bihar.

 She has been made the minister in nitish kumar cabinet, and has been the president of Bihar state women commission.

 In the 2010 state assembly elections, Leshi Singh of JD(U) won the Dhamdaha assembly defeating his nearest rival Irshad Ahmad Khan of Congress.

 When Nitish Kumar became chiefminister she was again inducted as minister.
