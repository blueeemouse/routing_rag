No Favors (Big Sean song)

"No Favors" is a song by American rapper Big Sean featuring American rapper Eminem for his fourth studio album "I Decided" (2017).

 The song was written by Big Sean, Francis Nguyen-Tran, Mark Sebastian, Starrah, The Lovin' Spoonful, Eminem, WondaGurl and John Sebastian and was produced by WondaGurl, Big Sean, and FrancisGotHeat.

 This song contains samples from "Summer in the City", performed by Quincy Jones featuring Valerie Simpson and was written by Mark Sebastian, Steve Boone and John Sebastian.

 The song debuted at number 22 on the Billboard Hot 100.
