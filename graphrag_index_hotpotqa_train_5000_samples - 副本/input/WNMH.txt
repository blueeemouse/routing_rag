WNMH

WNMH is a high school radio station broadcasting a Variety format.

 The station is owned by Northfield Mount Hermon School.

 From 1984 until 2010, the station was licensed to operate on 91.5 FM from Northfield, Massachusetts, United States and featured programming from ABC Radio.

 From 2014 until 2017, Northfield Mount Hermon School held a construction permit for a low-power FM station, WNMH-LP (106.7 FM) in Mount Hermon (a section of Gill), which would have restored WNMH's programming to the FM dial.

 s of 2017 , WNMH continues to operate as an Internet radio station.
