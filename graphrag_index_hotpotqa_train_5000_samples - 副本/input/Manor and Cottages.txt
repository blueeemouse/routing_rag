Manor and Cottages

Manor and Cottages is a historic resort complex and national historic district located at Asheville, Buncombe County, North Carolina.

 The complex encompassed 36 contributing buildings that were built starting in 1898.

 The main hotel, The Manor, was built starting in 1898-1899, and consists of a rambling group of interconnected wings with elements of the Colonial Revival, Shingle Style, and Tudor Revival styles.

 Wings were added to the original building in 1903 and 1913-1914.

 Located on the property are the contributing Club House (c. 1903) and a 19 guest cottages built between 1899 and 1920.

 The hotel was later converted for use as a retirement hotel for elderly persons with limited incomes.
