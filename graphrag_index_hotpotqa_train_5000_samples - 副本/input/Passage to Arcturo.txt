Passage to Arcturo

Passage to Arcturo is the first EP by Greek extreme metal band Rotting Christ, released in 1991.

 It was originally an MLP-pressing only: first edition in gatefold cover; second edition with normal cover.

 The EP was re-released in February 2011 with "The Old Coffin Spirit (Live)", "The Mystical Meeting (Live)" and "The Forest of N'Gai (Live)" as bonus tracks on MCD.
