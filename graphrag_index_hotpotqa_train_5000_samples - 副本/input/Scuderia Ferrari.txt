Scuderia Ferrari

Scuderia Ferrari S.p.A. competing as Scuderia Ferrari (] ) is the official name of the racing division of luxury Italian auto manufacturer, Ferrari, and competes in Formula One racing.

 The team is also nicknamed "The Prancing Horse", with reference to their logo.

 It is the oldest surviving and most successful Formula One team, having competed in every world championship since the 1950 Formula One season.
