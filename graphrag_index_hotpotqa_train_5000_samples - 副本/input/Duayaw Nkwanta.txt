Duayaw Nkwanta

Duayaw Nkwanta is a town and the capital of Tano North District, a district in the Brong-Ahafo Region of Ghana, located close to the capital of Brong-Ahafo, Sunyani.

 The infrastructure of Duayaw Nkwanta is well structured.

 Duayaw Nkwanta has a settlement population of 17,476.
