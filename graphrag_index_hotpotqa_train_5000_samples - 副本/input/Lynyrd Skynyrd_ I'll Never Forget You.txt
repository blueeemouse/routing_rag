Lynyrd Skynyrd: I'll Never Forget You

Lynyrd Skynyrd: I'll Never Forget You is a book written by former Lynyrd Skynyrd bodyguard Gene Odom.

 It details the childhood memories Gene shared with Lynyrd Skynyrd vocalist Ronnie Van Zant.

 It was followed by another book co-written with Frank Dorman, entitled ""Lynyrd Skynyrd: Remembering the Free Birds of Southern Rock"".
