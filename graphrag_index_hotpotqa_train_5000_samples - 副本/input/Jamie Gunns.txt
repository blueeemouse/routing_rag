Jamie Gunns

Jamie Elizabeth Gunns (30 November 1985 in Lambeth, London, England) is a British fashion model.

 Early in her career, Gunns was an editorial model whose clients included the like of Roberto Cavalli and Chanel.

 Currently, Gunns has become popular on the internet due to her appearance in online retailers such as ASOS.com, Bon Prix, Nasty Gal, Nordstrom, Nelly, and Littlewoods.
