Me, Myself and I (Beyoncé song)

"Me, Myself and I" is a song recorded by American singer Beyoncé.

 It was written by Beyoncé, Scott Storch and Robert Waller for her debut solo album "Dangerously in Love" (2003).

 The recording was produced by Storch with guitars performed by musician Aaron Fishbein.

 Beyoncé decided that she wanted a deeper and more personal song for the third single; the song was sent by Columbia Records to US radio on October 19, 2003.

 "Me, Myself and I" is an R&B song about dealing with a philandering boyfriend and learning from the consequences.
