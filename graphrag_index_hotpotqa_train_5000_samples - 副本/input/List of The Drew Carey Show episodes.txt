List of The Drew Carey Show episodes

The following is a complete list of episodes for the television show sitcom "The Drew Carey Show", which first aired on ABC on September 13, 1995.

 Throughout the show's run, nine seasons were filmed amassing 233 episodes, with the final episode airing on September 8, 2004.

 The sitcom follows assistant personnel director Drew Carey, in his romances and relationships to friends Lewis, Oswald, and Kate.

 There have been two DVD releases for "The Drew Carey Show": the first was a six-episode compilation released on February 28, 2006 and the first season was released on April 24, 2007.
