La Bohème (2009 film)

La Bohème is a 2009 short film from directed by Werner Herzog.

 The four-minute film features images of harsh life in Ethiopia set to the duet "O soave fanciulla " from Puccini's opera "La bohème", sung by Peter Auty and Mary Plazas.

 It was part of a series of short films commissioned by Sky Arts and English National Opera.
