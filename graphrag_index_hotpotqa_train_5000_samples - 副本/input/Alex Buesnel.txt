Alex Buesnel

Alexander Thomas Harry Buesnel (born 8 October 1992) is a British artistic gymnast.

 He is a nine-time British Disability Gymnastics Champion as well as a gold medalist in the 2011 Special Olympics World Summer Games in Athens, Greece.
