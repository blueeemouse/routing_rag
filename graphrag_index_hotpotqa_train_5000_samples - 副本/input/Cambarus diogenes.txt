Cambarus diogenes

Cambarus diogenes (known as the "devil crayfish", "chimney crayfish", "thunder crawfish" or "meadow crayfish") is a crayfish that grows to be 11.5 cm , not including the claws.

 It tends to live in wetlands and moist woodlands.

 They are most active in the summer and spring where they can be found near streams and floodplains near their mud chimney, where they live.
