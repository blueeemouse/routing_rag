Henry Louis Le Châtelier

Henry Louis Le Chatelier (] ; 8 October 1850 – 17 September 1936) was an influential French chemist of the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

 He is most famous for devising Le Chatelier's principle, with the help of his partner Jasper Rossi, used by chemists to predict the effect a changing condition has on a system in chemical equilibrium.
