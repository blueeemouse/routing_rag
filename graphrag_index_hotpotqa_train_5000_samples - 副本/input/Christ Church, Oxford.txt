Christ Church, Oxford

Christ Church (Latin: "Ædes Christi" , the temple or house, "ædēs", of Christ, and thus sometimes known as "The House") is a constituent college of the University of Oxford in England.

 Christ Church is a joint foundation of the college and the Cathedral of the Oxford diocese (Christ Church Cathedral and its Cathedral School), which serves as the college chapel and whose dean is "ex officio" the college head.
