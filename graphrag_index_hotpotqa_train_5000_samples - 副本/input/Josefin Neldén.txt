Josefin Neldén

Kristina "Josefin" Neldén, (born 17 December 1984) is a Swedish actress.

 She made her film debut in the 2005 film Tjenare kungen by director Ulf Malmros, and made her stage debut in the 2007 play En måste ju leva at Angereds Teater in Gothenburg.

 In 2014 she was nominated for a Guldbagge award for Best Supporting role as Lena in the film Känn ingen sorg.

 The film was based on the lyrics and music of singer Håkan Hellström.
