Olimpiada Neapolis FC

Olimpiada Neapolis FC FC (Greek: Ολυμπιάδα Νεάπολης ) was a football team based in Neapoli, Nicosia, Cyprus.

 It had been a refugee club since the 1974 Turkish invasion of Cyprus, when Turkey occupied the northern part of the island.

 The team was temporarily based in Ayioi Omoloyites, Nicosia.

 Olimpiada Neapolis FC FC is part of Olimpiada Neapolis.
