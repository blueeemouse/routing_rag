Ride (The Vines song)

"Ride" was the second official single from The Vines' second album, "Winning Days".

 Although it wasn't a big chart success, "Ride" is one of the band's best known songs because it was featured in a number of advertisements, including commercials for Apple's iPod, Nissan, "American Chopper", "NASCAR Hot Pass", "WKCF", The WB, and .

 Written by Craig Nicholls.
