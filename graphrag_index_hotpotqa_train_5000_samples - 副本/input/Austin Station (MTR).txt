Austin Station (MTR)

Austin () is an underground MTR rapid transit station on the West Rail Line in Hong Kong, situated perpendicular to Wui Cheung Road and Austin Road West, and formerly adjacent to Jordan Road Ferry Pier Bus Terminus.

 It is the only intermediate station along the Kowloon Southern Link project, built to connect the West Rail Line and East Rail Line.

 Construction was finished in July 2009 and the station opened on 16 August 2009.
