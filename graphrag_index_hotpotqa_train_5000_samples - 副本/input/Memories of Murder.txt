Memories of Murder

Memories of Murder () is a 2003 South Korean crime-drama film co-written and directed by Bong Joon-ho.

 It is based on the true story of Korea's first serial murders in history, which took place between 1986 and 1991 in Hwaseong, Gyeonggi Province.

 Song Kang-ho and Kim Sang-kyung star as Detective Park and Detective Seo, respectively, two of the detectives trying to solve the crimes.
