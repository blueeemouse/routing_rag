Adam Rayner

Adam Chance Angus Rayner (born on 28 August 1977 in Shrewsbury, Shropshire) is an English actor, who has appeared on television in "Mistresses", "At Home with the Braithwaites" and "Making Waves".

 He has also appeared on stage in "The Rivals" (Bristol Old Vic, 2004), "Romeo and Juliet" (Royal Shakespeare Theatre, 2006) and "Much Ado About Nothing" (Novello, 2006).
