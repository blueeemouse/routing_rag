La damnation de Faust

La damnation de Faust (English: "The Damnation of Faust"), Op.

 24 is a work for four solo voices, full seven-part chorus, large children's chorus and orchestra by the French composer Hector Berlioz.

 He called it a "légende dramatique" (dramatic legend).

 It was first performed at the Opéra-Comique in Paris on 6 December 1846.
