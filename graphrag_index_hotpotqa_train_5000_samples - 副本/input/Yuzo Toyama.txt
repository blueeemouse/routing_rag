Yuzo Toyama

Yūzō Toyama (外山雄三 , Toyama Yūzō ) (born 10 May 1931) is a Japanese composer and conductor.

 A native of Tokyo, he was a pupil of Kan'ichi Shimofusa; he studied conducting with Kurt Wöss and Wilhelm Loibner and, like them, later became a conductor of the NHK Symphony Orchestra.

 As a conductor he has served with numerous orchestras throughout Japan; as a composer his prime influences are Béla Bartók and Dmitri Shostakovich.

 His best-known work is a "Rhapsody for Orchestra" based on Japanese folk songs.
