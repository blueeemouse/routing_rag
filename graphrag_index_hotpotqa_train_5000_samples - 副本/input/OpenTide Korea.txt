OpenTide Korea

OpenTide Korea(오픈타이드 코리아 ) is a South Korean IT and counsulting corporation.

 It is a subsidiary of Samsung SDS that is an affiliate of the Samsung group.

 OpenTide Korea was founded in 2,000 by Lee Jae-yong who is the vice chairman of Samsung Electronics.

 He is the son of Lee Kun-hee, Chairman of Samsung.
