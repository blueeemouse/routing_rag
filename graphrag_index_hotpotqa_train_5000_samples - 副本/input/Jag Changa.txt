Jag Changa

Jag Changa ("The World is Beautiful") is the second studio album by the Indian band The Raghu Dixit Project released on November 23, 2013 through Wandering Minstrel Records.

 The album features songs in Hindi, Kannada and Tamil.

 The album features artists including Raghu Dixit as the lead vocalist and others he met during his concerts in the UK.

 It includes members of the British folk band, Bellowhead, American clawhammer banjo player, Abigail Washburn and sarod player Soumik Datta.
