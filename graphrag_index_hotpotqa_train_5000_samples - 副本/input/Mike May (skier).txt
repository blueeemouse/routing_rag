Mike May (skier)

Michael G. "Mike" May (born 1953) is an American business executive, skier and enthusiast of other sports who was blinded by a chemical explosion at the age of three, but regained partial vision in 2000, at the age of 46, after cornea transplants and a pioneering stem cell procedure by San Francisco ophthalmologist, Daniel Goodman.

 In 1999, May founded the Sendero Group in Davis, California, United States (US), which employs many individuals who are blind or visually impaired.

 Sendero has assisted those with sight disabilities by producing the first accessible GPS solution for blind persons.

 The Sendero Group's GPS technology received the Consumer Electronics Show's "Innovation Honoree" title in 2004 and 2009.
