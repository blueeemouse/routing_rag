Hosgri Fault

The Hosgri Fault (also Hosgri Fault Zone, San Gregorio-Hosgri Fault Zone) is an offshore fault zone located near the Central Coast of California in San Luis Obispo County.

 The main fault stretches for about 87 mi , and is located nearest to the coastal communities of Cambria, San Simeon, Morro Bay, Baywood Park-Los Osos and Avila Beach.

 The fault system (including its branches) is some 260 mi long, and is a right-lateral strand of the San Andreas Fault system.
