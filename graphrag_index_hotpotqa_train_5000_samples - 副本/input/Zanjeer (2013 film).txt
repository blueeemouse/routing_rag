Zanjeer (2013 film)

Zanjeer (English: "Shackles" ) in Hindi, Thoofan (English: Storm) in Telugu is a 2013 Indian bilingual action-drama film directed by Apoorva Lakhia, shot simultaneously in Hindi and Telugu.

 It is a remake of the 1973 Hindi film of the same name.

 The film marked the Bollywood debut of Tollywood actor Ram Charan in the lead role along with Priyanka Chopra.
