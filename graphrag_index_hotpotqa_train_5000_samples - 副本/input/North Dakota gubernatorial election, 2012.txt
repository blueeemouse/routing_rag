North Dakota gubernatorial election, 2012

The 2012 North Dakota gubernatorial election was held on November 6, 2012, to elect a governor and lieutenant governor of North Dakota.

 Incumbent Governor Jack Dalrymple succeeded to the office when then-Governor John Hoeven resigned to take a seat in the United States Senate.

 Dalrymple, a member of the Republican Party, won election to a first full term.

 Ryan Taylor was the Democratic nominee.

 Jack Dalrymple prevailed with 63% of the vote.
