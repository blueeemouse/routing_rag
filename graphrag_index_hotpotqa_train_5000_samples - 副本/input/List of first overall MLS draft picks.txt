List of first overall MLS draft picks

The Major League Soccer SuperDraft's first overall pick is the player who is selected first among all eligible draftees by a team during the annual Major League Soccer (MLS) SuperDraft.

 The first pick is awarded to the club with the poorest regular season record during the previous MLS campaign.

 Exceptions are when there is an expansion franchise, where they expansion side has the opportunity to select the first overall draft pick.
