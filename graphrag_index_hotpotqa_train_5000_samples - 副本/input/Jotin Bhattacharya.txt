Jotin Bhattacharya

Jotin Bhattacharya (Hindi: ज्योतिन भट्टाचार्य ), also known as Pandit Jotin Bhattacharya is an Indian classical musician and one of the most renowned sarod players and composers from Varanasi, India.

 Jyotin Bhattacharya was a disciple of Baba Allauddin Khan and received his formal training from him.

 Bhattacharya has created over 40 ragas, released Indian classical music albums and has written books about Baba Allauddin Khan.
