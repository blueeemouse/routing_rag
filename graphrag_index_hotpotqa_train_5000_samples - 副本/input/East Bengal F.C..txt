East Bengal F.C.

East Bengal Football Club is a professional football club, based in Kolkata, West Bengal, India.

 It currently competes in the I-League, the top tier of Indian football.

 The club has won three National Football League (India) (later named as I league) titles, eight Federation Cups, and three Indian Super Cups, among others.

 The club is the current holder of the Calcutta Football League.
