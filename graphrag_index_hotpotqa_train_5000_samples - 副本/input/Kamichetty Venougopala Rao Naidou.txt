Kamichetty Venougopala Rao Naidou

Kamichetty Venougopala Rao Naidu better known as "The Grand Old Man of Yanaon" was Mayor and MLA of Yanaon during French rule in first half of 20th century.

 He was one of the two powerful political leaders in Yanam during French rule.

 His opponent was Bezawada Bapa Naidou, the first mayor of Yanam.

 After death of Bapa Naidou, he became Mayor of Yanam (Maire de Yanaon) and even his family became very dominant in Yanam politics for at least 50 years thence.
