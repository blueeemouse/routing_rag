Nsawam

Nsawam is a town in south Ghana and is the capital of the Akuapim South Municipal District, a district in the Eastern Region of south Ghana.

 The main ethnic group is Akan, followed by Ga and then Ewe.

 Nsawam is controlled by Akuapim South Municipal District (ASMD).

 As of 2013, Nsawam has a settlement population of 44,522 people.

 Nsawam is situated on a main railway and highway to Kumasi.
