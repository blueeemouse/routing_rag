List of Keeping Up with the Kardashians episodes

"Keeping Up with the Kardashians" is an American reality television series that has aired on E! since October 14, 2007.

 The series has aired thirteen seasons, and focuses on the personal and professional lives of the Kardashian–Jenner family.

 Its premise was originated with Ryan Seacrest, who additionally serves as an executive producer.

 The series focuses on sisters Kourtney, Kim, and Khloé Kardashian.
