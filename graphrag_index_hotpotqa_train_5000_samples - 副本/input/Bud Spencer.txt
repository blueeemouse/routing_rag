Bud Spencer

Carlo Pedersoli (31 October 1929 – 27 June 2016), professionally known as Bud Spencer, was an Italian actor, professional swimmer and water polo player.

 He is known for action-comedy roles with his long-time film partner Terence Hill.

 The duo "garnered world acclaim and attracted millions to theater seats".

 Spencer and Hill appeared in, produced and directed over 20 films together.
