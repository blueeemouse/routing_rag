Sammy Kershaw discography

The discography of American country music singer Sammy Kershaw comprises sixteen studio albums, two Greatest Hits packages, an album of cover songs, and two Christmas albums.

 Three of his studio albums are certified platinum by the RIAA, while two of his studio albums and his first greatest hits package have been certified gold.

 Although only one of his singles — 1993's "She Don't Know She's Beautiful" — reached the top of the "Billboard" charts, twenty-five of his singles have been Top 40 hits.

 Besides "She Don't Know She's Beautiful", ten more of these have reached Top Ten, including three songs which reached #2.
