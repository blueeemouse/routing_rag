The Ward (film)

The Ward is a 2010 American supernatural psychological horror film directed by John Carpenter.

 It stars Amber Heard, Mamie Gummer, Danielle Panabaker and Jared Harris.

 The film is a period piece set in 1966, and chronicles a young woman who is institutionalized after setting fire to a house, and who finds herself haunted by the ghost of a former inmate at the psychiatric ward.
