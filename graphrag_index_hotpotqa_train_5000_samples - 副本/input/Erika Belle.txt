Erika Belle

Erika Belle is an American socialite, art curator, fashion designer, and dancer, most known for her friendship and work with pop singer Madonna in the 1980s.

 She has been featured dancing in Madonna's videos to "Everybody", "True Blue", "Papa Don't Preach", "Lucky Star", and "Holiday".

 She collaborated on choreography to the videos and live shows, and performed with Madonna in various New York clubs and television programs such as "Top of the Pops".
