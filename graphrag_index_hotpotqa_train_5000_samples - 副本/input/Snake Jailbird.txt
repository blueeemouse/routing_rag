Snake Jailbird

Snake Jailbird (usually referred to as simply Snake) is a recurring fictional character in the animated television series "The Simpsons", who is voiced by Hank Azaria.

 Snake's first appearance was in the episode "The War of the Simpsons".

 His catchphrase is "Bye!"

, which he usually says when he's in trouble.

 His real name is Albert Knickerbocker Aloysius Snake, although a Simpsons card says his name is Chester Turley.

 He was named the 19th (out of 25) of IGN's Top 25 Simpsons Peripheral Characters.
