Aquarius (U.S. TV series)

Aquarius is an American period crime drama television series created by John McNamara for NBC.

 The show aired from May 28, 2015, to September 10, 2016.

 In 2015, the series was among seven others to receive the Critics' Choice Television Award for Most Exciting New Series.

 On October 1, 2016, NBC cancelled the series after two seasons.
