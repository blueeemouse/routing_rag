1971 Fresno State Bulldogs football team

The 1971 Fresno State Bulldogs football team represented Fresno State College during the 1971 University Division football season as a member of the Pacific Coast Athletic Association.

 The team was led by head coach Darryl Rogers, in his sixth year, and they played their home games at Ratcliffe Stadium on the campus of Fresno City College in Fresno, California.

 They finished the season with a record of six wins and five losses (6–5, 3–2 PCAA).
