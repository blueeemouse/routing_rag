1998 Argentine Grand Prix

The 1998 Argentine Grand Prix (formally the XXI Gran Premio Marlboro Argentina) was a Formula One motor race held at Autódromo Oscar Alfredo Gálvez, Buenos Aires, on 12 April 1998.

 The race is (to date) the last Argentine Grand Prix.

 It was the third race of the 1998 Formula One season.

 The 72-lap race was won by Michael Schumacher for the Ferrari team, from a second position start.

 Mika Häkkinen finished second in a McLaren, with Eddie Irvine third in the other Ferrari car.
