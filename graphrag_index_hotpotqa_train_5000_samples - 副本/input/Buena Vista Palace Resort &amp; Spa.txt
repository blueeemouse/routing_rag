Buena Vista Palace Resort &amp; Spa

The Hilton Orlando Buena Vista Palace is a 1,014-room resort located on 27 acre directly across from the Disney Springs Marketplace.

 The 23 acre hotel, which opened in March 1983, is among seven hotels that make up the Disney Springs Resort Area.

 The Disney Springs Resort Area Hotels are located on the property of the Walt Disney World Resort in Lake Buena Vista, Florida, United States, but are not operated by Disney.
