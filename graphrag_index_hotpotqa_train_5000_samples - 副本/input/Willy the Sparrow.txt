Willy the Sparrow

Willy the Sparrow (Hungarian: "Vili, a veréb" ) is a 1989 Hungarian animated film directed by József Gémes.

 It was released in Hungary to critical acclaim and it won the Prize of the Audience at the 3rd Kecskemét Animation Film Festival.

 The English adaptation was directed by Scott Murphy.

 Voice actors included Sarah Schaub, Barta Heiner, Rick Macy and Aaron Bybee.

 The film was released on DVD in 2004.
