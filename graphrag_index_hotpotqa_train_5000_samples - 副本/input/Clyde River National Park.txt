Clyde River National Park

Clyde River is a national park in south-eastern New South Wales (Australia) between Batemans Bay and Nelligen.

 It includes 9 km of river frontage to the Clyde River, bounded on three sides by the Clyde River and on the northeast by the Kings Highway.

 It was created from a part of Benandarah State Forest; in 2000 10.91 km of the state forest was set aside as a national park.

 The park forms part of the Ulladulla to Merimbula Important Bird Area, identified as such by BirdLife International because of its importance for swift parrots.
