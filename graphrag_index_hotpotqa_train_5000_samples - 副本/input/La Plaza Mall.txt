La Plaza Mall

La Plaza Mall is a regional shopping mall located in McAllen, Texas, at the intersection of Interstate 2 (Expressway 83) and 10th Street.

 It has 1215000 sqft of gross leasable area and features more than 150 specialty stores, many of which are flagship stores, and restaurants.

 The mall is one of the highest-grossing operated by Simon, and the largest mall in south Texas.
