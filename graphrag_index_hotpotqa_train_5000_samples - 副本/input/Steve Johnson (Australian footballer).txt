Steve Johnson (Australian footballer)

Steven Leigh "Stevie" Johnson (born 4 July 1983) is a former professional Australian rules footballer who played for the Geelong and Greater Western Sydney football clubs in the Australian Football League (AFL).

 He was selected by Geelong with pick 24 in the 2001 AFL Draft.

 His first few years were plagued with inconsistency, injury and off field problems.

 A medium-sized forward, Johnson is renowned for his freakish ability on the field, where he has consistently wowed fans with his penchant for extraordinary goals.
