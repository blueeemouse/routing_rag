Today (Mel B song)

"Today" is a song by British singer-songwriter Melanie Brown.

 The song was written by Brown and produced by Kevin Malpass.

 It was the first and only single released from her second solo album, "L.A. State of Mind".

 Released on 13 June 2005 in the United Kingdom, it entered and peaked at #41, charting for only one week.

 The music video was directed by Mark McConnell and filmed in Los Angeles, California.

 To promote the single, Brown performed the song on "GMTV".
