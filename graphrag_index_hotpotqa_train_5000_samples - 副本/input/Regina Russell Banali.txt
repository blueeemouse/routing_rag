Regina Russell Banali

Regina Russell Banali is a film producer, director, television presenter, and actress.

 Her first role as an actress was playing one of the three mermaids that saves Robin Williams's character in Steven Spielberg's "Hook" (1991).

 She has appeared in over 50 films and television shows.

 She produced and directed a commissioned comedy short for Crackle and PSAs for the HSUS on animal welfare issues.

 She directed and produced a feature-length documentary called "Quiet Riot - Well Now You're Here" about the hard rock band Quiet Riot.

 It premiered on the Showtime Network January 29, 2015.
