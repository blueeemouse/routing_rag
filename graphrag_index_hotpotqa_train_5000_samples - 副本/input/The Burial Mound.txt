The Burial Mound

The Burial Mound ("Kjæmpehøjen") was Henrik Ibsen's second play and his first play to be performed.

 It is a three-act verse drama, written in 1850 when Ibsen was 22 years old.

 The play was first performed at the Christiania Theater on 26 September 1850, under Ibsen's pseudonym Brynjolf Bjarme.
