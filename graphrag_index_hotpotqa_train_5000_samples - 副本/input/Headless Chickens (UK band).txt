Headless Chickens (UK band)

Headless Chickens were a melodic punk band from Exeter, England whose members included Thom Yorke (guitar and vocals), Simon Shackleton (bass and vocals), John Matthias (violin), Laura Forrest-Hay (violin), Martin Brooks (drums), Andy Hills (bass) and Lindsey Moore (drums).

 In June 1989 they released their first single, "I Don't Want To Go To Woodstock", on the "Hometown Atrocities" EP, along with tracks from three other bands (Beaver Patrol, Jackson Penis and Mad At The Sun).

 This is believed to be Thom Yorke's first ever released recording, on which he played lead guitar and sang backing vocals; Yorke later became famous as the singer of Radiohead.
