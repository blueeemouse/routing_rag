Thomas Capehart House

Thomas Capehart House is a historic home located near Kittrell, Vance County, North Carolina.

 It was built between 1866 and 1870, and is a small two-story, "L"-shaped frame board-and-batten, dwelling in the Downingesque Gothic style.

 It features ornate bargeboards, sawn ornament, and traceried windows.

 Also on the property is a contributing small outbuilding, also of board-and-batten.
