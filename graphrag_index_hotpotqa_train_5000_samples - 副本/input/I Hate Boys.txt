I Hate Boys

"I Hate Boys" is a song recorded by American recording artist Christina Aguilera for her sixth studio album, "Bionic" (2010).

 The song was written by Aguilera, Ester Dean, William Tyler, Bill Wellings, J. J. Hunter and Jamal Jones, who also handled the production of the track.

 "I Hate Boys" is a glam rock and pop song, containing elements of urban pop and synthpop.

 Lyrically, it is a hate-driven song about ridiculing bad boys.
