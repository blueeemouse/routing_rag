Mumbai Indians in 2017

The Mumbai Indians are a franchise cricket team based in Mumbai that play in the Indian Premier League (IPL).

 They are one of eight teams that are competing in the 2017 Indian Premier League.

 Rohit Sharma is captaining the team for the fifth season in succession, while Mahela Jayawardene is in his first season as the team's coach.

 The Mumbai Indians drew an average home attendance of 26,000 in the 2017 Indian Premier League.
