Snorri Snorrason

Snorri Snorrason (born July 14, 1977 in Reykjavík, Iceland) is an Icelandic singer who rose to popularity after winning "Idol Stjörnuleit 3", the Icelandic version of "Pop Idol".

 Snorri compares his vocal style to Axl Rose & includes Robert Plant as his biggest influence, his favourite Icelandic artist is Jet Black Joe.
