Olga Festival

The Olga Festival is a beer fest held occasionally around the year in honour of the Santo Olga Goretskaja, concluding on the next day when people are kicked out from the bar, in the city of København in the Denmark.

 The name "Olga Fest" means "to be like Olga" or "make believe in parties".

 Olgarians, the people who celebrate Olga Festival were the primary settlers in Copenhagen according to history books during early 2013.

 There are some early settlers who came before.
