Khlong Chandi Railway Station

Khlong Chandi Railway Station is a railway station located in Chandi Subdistrict, Chawang District, Nakhon Si Thammarat.

 It is a class 2 railway station located 727.953 km from Thon Buri Railway Station.

 This station is the alighting point for Lan Saka District, Chang Klang District, Phipun District and Nakhon Si Thammarat City (for passengers on trains not terminating at Nakhon Si Thammarat), continuing the journey by road transport.
