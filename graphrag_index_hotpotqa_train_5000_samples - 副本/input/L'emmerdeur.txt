L'emmerdeur

L'emmerdeur (English title: "A Pain in the ...") is a 1973 French-Italian black comedy film starring Jacques Brel, appearing in his tenth and final feature film.

 Directed by Édouard Molinaro and co-starring Lino Ventura, Caroline Cellier, and Jean-Pierre Darras, "L'emmerdeur" is an adaptation of Francis Veber's 1971 play "Le contrat".
