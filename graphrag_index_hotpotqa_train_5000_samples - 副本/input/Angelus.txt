Angelus

The Angelus ( ; Latin for "angel") is a Catholic devotion commemorating the Incarnation.

 As with many Catholic prayers, the name "Angelus" is derived from its incipit: "Angelus Domini nuntiavit Mariæ" ("... the Angel of the Lord declared unto Mary ...") and is practised by reciting as versicle and response three Biblical verses narrating the mystery; alternating with the prayer "Hail Mary".

 The Angelus exemplifies a species of prayers called the "prayer of the devotee".
