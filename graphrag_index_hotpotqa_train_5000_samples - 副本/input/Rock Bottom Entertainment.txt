Rock Bottom Entertainment

Rock Bottom Entertainment is a record label founded in Detroit, Michigan in June 1997 by founder and C.E.O., Rafael "Rock" Howard.

 Rock Bottom released the "Blow: Based On True Stories" album in late 2005.

 The group/label released the most independently sold album in Detroit hip hop music history in 2000, with their album "Alligator City".

 Their other artist's continue to make moves while Big Herk releases his "Over Dose" album.
