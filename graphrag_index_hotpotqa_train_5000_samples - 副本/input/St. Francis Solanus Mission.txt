St. Francis Solanus Mission

St. Francis Solanus Mission is a historic mission on W. Lake Street in Petoskey, Michigan.

 It is the only existing building in the Arbre Croche district dating from the time of Bishop Frederic Baraga, and is the oldest building still standing in Petoskey, as well as one of the oldest in northern lower Michigan.

 The mission was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1972.
