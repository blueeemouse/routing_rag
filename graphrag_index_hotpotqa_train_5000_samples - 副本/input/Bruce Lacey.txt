Bruce Lacey

Bruce Lacey (1927–2016) was a British artist, performer and eccentric.

 After completing his national service in the Navy he became established on the avantgarde scene with his performance art and mechanical constructs.

 He has been closely associated with "The Alberts" performance group and "The Goon Show".

 He made the props and had an acting part in Richard Lester's "The Running Jumping & Standing Still Film".
