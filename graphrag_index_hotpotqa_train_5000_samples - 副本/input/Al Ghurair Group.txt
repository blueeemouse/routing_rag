Al Ghurair Group

Al Ghurair Group (Arabic: مجموعة سيف الغرير) (also known as Saif Al Ghurair Group) is a Dubai, United Arab Emirates-based business group founded by the Al Ghurair family.

 A diverse conglomerate, it has operations in the Middle East, North Africa, Europe and North America.

 The grandsons of Al Ghurair Group founder Ahmad Al Ghurair run the group.

 Abdul Rahman Saif Al Ghurair serves as group chairman and Majid Saif Al Ghurair is the group's chief executive officer.
