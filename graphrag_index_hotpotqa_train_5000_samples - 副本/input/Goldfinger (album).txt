Goldfinger (album)

Goldfinger is the first album by punk rock band Goldfinger, released on Mojo Records in February 1996 and produced by Mojo founder Jay Rifkin.

 The album was a hit on college radio.

 The single "Here in Your Bedroom" was a top 5 rock hit in the U.S. in the summer of 1996, and also reached #47 on the Billboard Hot 100 Airplay chart, making it their highest charting single ever.

 The album was certified Gold in Canada (50,000 copies) in 2002.

 It is the only album by the band not to be produced by frontman John Feldmann in any capacity.
