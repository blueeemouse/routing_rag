Michał Elwiro Andriolli

Michał Elwiro Andriolli, Lithuanian: "Mykolas Elvyras Andriolis" , Italian: "Elviro Michele Andriolli" (2 November 1836, in Vilnius 23 August 1893, in Nałęczów) was a Polish - Lithuanian painter and architect of Italian descent.

 He is notable for his illustrations to Mickiewicz's "Pan Tadeusz", as well as a distinctive style of villas built outside Warsaw.
