WCOO

WCOO (105.5 FM) is a radio station broadcasting an adult album alternative format.

 Licensed to Kiawah Island, South Carolina, USA, it serves the Charleston, South Carolina area.

 The station is currently owned by L.M. Communications II of South Carolina, Inc..

 The station's studios are located in Charleston due west of downtown (west of the Ashley River) and the transmitter site is on Johns Island.
