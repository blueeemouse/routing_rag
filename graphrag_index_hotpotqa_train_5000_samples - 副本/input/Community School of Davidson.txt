Community School of Davidson

Community School of Davidson (CSD) is a public charter school located in Davidson, North Carolina.

 Their mascot is the Spartans.

 The school opened in 2001 as Children's Community School and adopted its current name in 2009.

 The school's founding principles center around The Basic School, and research done by Dr. Ernest Boyer.

 The school operates an elementary school, middle school, and high school on two separate campuses.

 Grades kindergarten through 5th "loop" with their teachers for 2 years.

 With both campuses combined, the student body is approximately 1,300 pupils, with about 100 per grade level.
