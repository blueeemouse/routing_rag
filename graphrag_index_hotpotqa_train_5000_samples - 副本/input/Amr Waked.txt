Amr Waked

Amr Waked (Arabic: عمرو واكد; born April 12, 1973) is an Egyptian film, television, and stage actor.

 He is best known to international audiences and in Hollywood for his role in the 2005 film "Syriana".

 Other prominent roles include the Yemeni Sheikh Muhammad in "Salmon Fishing in the Yemen", and Pierre Del Rio in Luc Besson's "Lucy".
