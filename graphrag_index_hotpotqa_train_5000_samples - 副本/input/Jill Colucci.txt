Jill Colucci

Jill Colucci is an American songwriter and vocalist.

 She initially rose to prominence in 1988, singing the main scores to the film "Mystic Pizza".

 She sang the ABC promo campaigns "Something's Happening" (the 1988 version with Bill Champlin and the 1989 edition solo) and "America's Watching ABC" (1990 version only).

 She also performed "The Funny Things You Do", which was the theme song for "America's Funniest Home Videos" for the first seven seasons.

 Colucci's prolific credits even extended to Toyota, where she was the original vocalist of the auto maker's ""I Love What You Do For Me"" campaign in 1989 and she also performed the title theme song of the short-lived NBC-TV series "Brand New Life" (1989-90).

 The following year, as she continued to sing for ABC, she sang for The Disney Channel's "Celebrate Me Home" promotions.

 She also sang for promotional videos for the Apple II entitled "Apple ][ Forever" in 1984.
