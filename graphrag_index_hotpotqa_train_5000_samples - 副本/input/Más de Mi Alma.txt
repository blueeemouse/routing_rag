Más de Mi Alma

Más de Mi Alma (Eng.: "More of My Soul"') is the fourth studio album released by Marco Antonio Solís on May 29, 2001.

 This album became his second number-one hit on the "Billboard" Top Latin Albums chart and received a nomination for Best Pop Vocal Album at the Latin Grammy Awards of 2002.

 "Cuando Te Acuerdes de Mi" was featured in the novela "Salomé".

 The album received a 2002 Premio Lo Nuestro Award nomination for Pop Album of the Year.
