Mercy Thompson

Mercedes Athena "Mercy" Thompson is a fictional character and the protagonist of the Mercy Thompson series, written by Patricia Briggs.

 The main series, which currently consists of the novels "Moon Called", "Blood Bound", "Iron Kissed", "Bone Crossed", "Silver Borne", "River Marked", "Frost Burned", "Night Broken", "Fire Touched", the short story "Hollow", and the most recent novel "Silence Fallen", are narrated primarily from Mercy's point of view.

 The character also has a comic series by Dynamite which includes the prequel "Homecoming", a comic adaptation of "Moon Called", and an original story "Hopcross Jilly".
