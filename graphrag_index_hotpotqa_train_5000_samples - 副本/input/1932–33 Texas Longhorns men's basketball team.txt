1932–33 Texas Longhorns men's basketball team

The 1932–33 Texas Longhorns men's basketball team represented The University of Texas at Austin in intercollegiate basketball competition during the 1932–33 season.

 The Longhorns were led by second-year head coach Ed Olle and captained by future Longhorn football head coach Ed Price.

 The team finished the season with a 22–1 record and was retroactively named the national champion by the Premo-Porretta Power Poll.
