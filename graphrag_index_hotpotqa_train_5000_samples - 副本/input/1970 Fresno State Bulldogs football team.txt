1970 Fresno State Bulldogs football team

The 1970 Fresno State Bulldogs football team represented Fresno State College during the 1970 University Division football season as a member of the Pacific Coast Athletic Association.

 The team was led by head coach Darryl Rogers, in his fifth year, and they played their home games at Ratcliffe Stadium on the campus of Fresno City College in Fresno, California.

 They finished the season with a record of eight wins and four losses (8–4, 4–2 PCAA).
