The MySpace Transmissions: Manchester Orchestra

The MySpace Transmissions is the fifth EP released by the band Manchester Orchestra, available for free download on MySpace.com.

 The EP is the twelfth release in the MySpace Transmissions series, preceded by bands such as Thrice, City And Colour, James Blunt and Nada Surf.

 The band also opted to mix it themselves having guitarist Robert do the final work.
