Polikarpov SPB (D)

The Polikarpov SPB (D) ("Skorostnoy Pikiruyushchy Bombardirovshchik (Dalnost)"—High Speed Dive Bomber (Distance)) was a Soviet twin-engined dive bomber designed before World War II.

 A single prototype and five pre-production aircraft were built, but two crashed and the program was cancelled in favor of the Petlyakov Pe-2.
