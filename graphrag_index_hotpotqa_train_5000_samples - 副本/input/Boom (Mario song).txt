Boom (Mario song)

"Boom" is an R&B single by Mario.

 It is the fourth and final single from his second studio album "Turning Point".

 The single was released on October 3, 2005.

 The song features rapper Juvenile, who co-wrote the song with Lamarquis Jefferson, Johnta Austin, Craig Love and Lil Jon; the latter is also the producer.

 The song peaked on the Rhythmic Top 40 chart at number 24.
