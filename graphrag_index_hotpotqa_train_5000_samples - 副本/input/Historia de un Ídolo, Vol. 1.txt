Historia de un Ídolo, Vol. 1

Historia de Un Ídolo, Vol.

 1 (Eng.: "History of an Idol, Vol.

 1") is a compilation album released in 2000 by ranchero music performer Vicente Fernández.

 This album achieve a rare feat on the "Billboard" Top Latin Albums chart, it peaked at number-one in 2001 and 2007.
