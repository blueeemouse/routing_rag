In Your Eyes (Inna song)

"In Your Eyes" is a song recorded by Romanian singer Inna for her third studio album, "Party Never Ends" (2013).

 It was released on 3 November 2013 through Roton, featuring the vocal collaboration of American reggaeton performer Yandel.

 The track–recorded at the Rokstone Studios–was written by Steve Mac, Yandel and Ina Wroldsen, while production was solely handled by Mac.

 Musically, "In Your Eyes" is inspired by Latin music and encompasses a hip-hop style because of Yandel's contribution.
