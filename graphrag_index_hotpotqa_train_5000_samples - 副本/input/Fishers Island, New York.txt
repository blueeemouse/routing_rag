Fishers Island, New York

Fishers Island is located at the eastern end of Long Island Sound, 2 mi off the southeastern coast of Connecticut across Fishers Island Sound, about 9 mi long and 1 mi wide.

 It is about 11 mi from the tip of Long Island at Orient Point, 2 mi from Napatree Point at the southwestern tip of Rhode Island and from Groton Long Point in Connecticut, and about 7 mi southeast of New London, Connecticut, from which it is accessible by plane or regular ferry service.
