Rabbit Card

The Rabbit Card is a rechargeable contact-less stored value smart card used to transfer electronic payments in systems in Thailand.

 It was launched in May 2012 to collect fares for the BTS Skytrain and the Bangkok BRT.

 The card can be recharged at minimum THB100 at a time.

 It can be recharged at all BTS and BRT ticket offices and most of McDonald's branches in Thailand.
