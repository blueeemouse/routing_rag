2015 Texas Longhorns football team

The 2015 Texas Longhorns football team, known variously as "Texas", "UT", the "Longhorns", or the "Horns", was a collegiate American football team that represented the University of Texas at Austin as a member of the Big 12 Conference in the 2015 NCAA Division I FBS football season.

 The team played its home games at Darrell K Royal–Texas Memorial Stadium in Austin, Texas, where the team is based.

 The Longhorns were led by second-year head coach Charlie Strong.

 Shawn Watson and Joe Wickline ran the offense.

 Vance Bedford served as defensive coordinator.

 They finished the season 5–7, 4–5 in Big 12 play to finish in a three way tie for fifth place.
