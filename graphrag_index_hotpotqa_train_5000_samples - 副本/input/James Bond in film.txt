James Bond in film

The James Bond film series is a British series of spy films based on the fictional character of MI6 agent James Bond, "007", who originally appeared in a series of books by Ian Fleming.

 It is one of the longest continually-running film series in history, having been in on-going production from 1962 to the present (with a six-year hiatus between 1989 and 1995).

 In that time Eon Productions has produced 24 films, most of them at Pinewood Studios.

 With a combined gross of over $7 billion to date, the films produced by Eon constitute the fourth-highest-grossing film series.

 Six actors have portrayed 007 in the Eon series, the latest being Daniel Craig.
