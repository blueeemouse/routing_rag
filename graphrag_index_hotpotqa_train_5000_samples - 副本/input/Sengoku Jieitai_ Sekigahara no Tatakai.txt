Sengoku Jieitai: Sekigahara no Tatakai

Sengoku Jieitai Sekigahara No Tatakai (戦国自衛隊 関ケ原の戦い , "Warring States JSDF Battle of Sekigahara" ) is a 2006 television series mainly based on the Sengoku Jieitai movie in 1979 and the 2005 movie Sengoku Jieitai 1549.

 Its director, Mitsumasa Saito, had been involved in the creation of the Sengoku Jietai 1979 movie.
