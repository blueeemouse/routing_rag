Dʿmt

Dʿmt (South Arabian alphabet: 𐩩𐩣𐩲𐩵 ; Unvocalized <a href="Ge%27ez">Ge'ez</a>: ደዐመተ, "DʿMT" theoretically vocalized as ዳዓማት "Daʿamat" or ዳዕማት Daʿəmat) was a <a href="monarchy">kingdom</a> located in <a href="Eritrea">Eritrea</a> and northern <a href="Ethiopia">Ethiopia</a> that existed during the 10th to 5th centuries BC.

 Few inscriptions by or about this kingdom survive and very little archaeological work has taken place.

 As a result, it is not known whether Dʿmt ended as a civilization before Aksum's early stages, evolved into the Aksumite state, or was one of the smaller states united in the Aksumite kingdom possibly around the beginning of the 1st century.
