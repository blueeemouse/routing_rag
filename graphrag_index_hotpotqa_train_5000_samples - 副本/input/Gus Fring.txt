Gus Fring

Gustavo "Gus" Fring is a fictional character in "Breaking Bad" and "Better Call Saul", played by Giancarlo Esposito.

 Fring is a prominent methamphetamine distributor in the Southwestern United States, who uses several legitimate businesses, including a chain of successful fast food fried chicken restaurants called Los Pollos Hermanos and an industrial laundry facility called Lavanderia Brillante, as fronts for a vast drug operation.

 Gus maintains a positive exterior; he takes an active role in managing his front businesses and is a booster for the DEA, making large donations to the agency's Albuquerque office.

 However, Gus is ruthless and Machiavellian in managing his vast drug empire.

 He employs a number of enforcers and has personally killed rivals and associates.
