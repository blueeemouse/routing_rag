Ben Ray Luján

Ben Ray Luján (born June 7, 1972) is an American politician and the U.S. Representative for New Mexico 's 3 congressional district , serving since 2009.

 He is a member of the Democratic Party.

 The district is based in Santa Fe, the state capital, and includes most of the northern portion of the state.

 Luján became the Chairman of the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee on November 18, 2014.
