Central Coast FC

Central Coast FC, formerly known as "Central Coast Lightning" is a semi-professional soccer club, based and located on the Central Coast of New South Wales.

 They are the main "feeder" club for the professional A-League club, the Central Coast Mariners !

Central Coast Mariners .

 As well as fielding a team in the NSW National Premier Leagues Division 2, they also have junior clubs that have teams as young as Under 8, which play in the various club competitions along the Central Coast.
