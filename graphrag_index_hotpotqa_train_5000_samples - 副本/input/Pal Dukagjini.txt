Pal Dukagjini

Pal Dukagjini or Paul Ducagin (Italian: "Paolo Ducagini" , 1411–1458) was an Albanian nobleman, a member of the Dukagjini family.

 He and his kinsman Nicholas Dukagjini were initially subjects of Lekë Zaharia, a Venetian vassal who had possessions around Shkoder.

 Nicholas murdered Lekë, and the Dukagjini continued to rule over their villages under Venetian vassalage.

 Pal and Nicholas were part of the League of Lezhë, a military alliance that sought liberation of Albania from the Ottoman Empire, founded by the powerful Skanderbeg.

 In 1454, the Dukagjini accepted vassalage of Alfonso V of Aragon, as other chieftains had done three years earlier.

 Pal later abandoned Skanderbeg's army and deserted to the Ottomans.
