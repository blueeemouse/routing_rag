Young Blood (Sophie Ellis-Bextor song)

"Young Blood" is a song by English recording artist Sophie Ellis-Bextor from her fifth studio album "Wanderlust" (2014).

 The song was released as the album's lead single on 21 November 2013.

 It was co-written by Ed Harcourt and Ellis-Bextor; the former also produced it.

 The song is a chamber pop piano ballad, which features instrumentation from subdued drums and various string instruments.

 In the track, Ellis-Bextor sings with restrain, incorporating a low register in the verses and hitting her highest note in the chorus.

 A demo version of the track was offered online in March 2013.
