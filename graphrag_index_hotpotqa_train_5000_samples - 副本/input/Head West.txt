Head West

Head West is a music album released in 1970 by the American band Head West, who were based in Paris, France.

 The band are best known today for their vocalist / guitarist Bob Welch, who went on to join Fleetwood Mac in 1971.

 Since 1973, by which time Welch had become more well-known, the album was repackaged as more of a solo album, credited to Bob Welch with Head West.

 Singles were also released but with little success.
