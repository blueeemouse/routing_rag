Santa Clara Broncos baseball

The Santa Clara Broncos baseball team is the varsity intercollegiate baseball team that represents Santa Clara University in NCAA Division I college baseball.

 Santa Clara Baseball competes in the West Coast Conference, of which the Santa Clara Broncos were a charter member.

 The Broncos play their home games on campus at Stephen Schott Stadium, which opened in 2005.

 The Broncos and are led by head coach Rusty Filter, in his first season.
