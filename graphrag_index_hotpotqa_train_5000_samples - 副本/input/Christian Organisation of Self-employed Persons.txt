Christian Organisation of Self-employed Persons

Action for the Small Farmer (in Dutch: "Actie voor de Kleine Boer", better known as "Actie Bouwman") was a political party in the Netherlands.

 The party was led by Alphons Bouwman, a RKSP member.

 The party drew its support from rural Catholic sectors.

 It emerged after large-scale dissatisfaction amongst Catholic farmers towards the Northern Brabant Christian Farmers League (NCB) during the agrarian economic crisis of 1933.
