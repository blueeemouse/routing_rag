Ali Safina

Muhammad Ali Safina (born 18 December 1983) is a VJ-turned Pakistani actor.

 He is famous for his comedy acting and has hosted many shows rendering him a popular local hero.

 He started off his career DJing at a local radio station in Glasgow, Scotland while stuyding and later on by starring in sitcoms in his homeland of Pakistan such as "Takkay Ki Ayegi Baraat" among others.

 He is currently acting in a sitcom by ARY Digital and plans are for him to be cast in an upcoming movie "Jalaibee."

 He is the owner of FM 96.

 He and Hira Tareen became engaged on April 25, 2013.
