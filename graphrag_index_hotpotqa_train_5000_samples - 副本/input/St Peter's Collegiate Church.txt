St Peter's Collegiate Church

St Peter's Collegiate Church is located on the northern side of central Wolverhampton, England.

 For many centuries it was a chapel royal, and from 1480 a royal peculiar, independent of the Diocese of Lichfield and even the Province of Canterbury.

 The collegiate church was central to the development of the town of Wolverhampton, much of which belonged to its dean.

 Until the 18th century, it was the only church in Wolverhampton and the control of the college extended far into the surrounding area, with dependent chapels in several towns and villages of southern Staffordshire.
