Whitefield's Tabernacle, Tottenham Court Road

Whitefield's Tabernacle on Tottenham Court Road, a church in London, England, also called Tottenham Court Road Chapel, was built in 1756 for George Whitefield.

 It was enlarged in 1759.

 John Wesley preached a sermon "On the death of the Rev Mr George Whitefield" both here and at Whitefield's Tabernacle, Moorfields, in 1770.
