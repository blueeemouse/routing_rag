Ryan Seacrest

Ryan John Seacrest (born December 24, 1974) is an American radio personality, television host, and producer.

 Seacrest is known for hosting the competition show "American Idol", the syndicated countdown program "American Top 40", and the KIIS-FM morning radio show "On Air with Ryan Seacrest".

 He began co-hosting "Live with Kelly and Ryan" on a permanent basis May 1, 2017.
