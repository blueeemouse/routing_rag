2010 ASB Classic

The 2010 ASB Classic was a women's tennis tournament played on outdoor hard courts.

 It was the 25th edition of the ASB Classic, and was part of the WTA International tournaments of the 2010 WTA Tour.

 It took place at the ASB Tennis Centre in Auckland, New Zealand, from 4 January through 9 January 2010.

 Yanina Wickmayer won the singles title.
