D'atra Hicks

D'Atra Hicks (born Deitra Hicks; December 28, 1968 in Harlem, New York) is an American actress and singer.

 She is best known for her role as Jackie Simmons in Tyler Perry's 2002 stage play Madea's Family Reunion.

 She has also performed as Nurse Trudy in the 2006 stage play What's Done in the Dark and as Niecy in the 2009 play Laugh to Keep from Crying.
