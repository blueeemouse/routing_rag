Lisa Lopez

Lisa Lopez is a Tejano music singer who had a United States "Billboard" Regional Mexican Airplay number one single with "Si Quieres Verme Llorar" (1982).

 Lopez's core audience was targeted towards Mexicans, and became the first female Tejano singer to appear on the "Billboard" Top Latin Albums chart in October 1986.

 Historically, female musicians were commercially less successful than male performers.

 Lopez became a successful female Tejano singer before the genre's 1990s golden age, and was among the seven female singers who became popular artists; including Chavela Ortiz, Patsy Torres, Laura Canales, Shelly Lares, Elsa Garcia, and Selena.
