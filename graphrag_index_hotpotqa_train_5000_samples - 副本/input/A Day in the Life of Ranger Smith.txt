A Day in the Life of Ranger Smith

A Day in the Life of Ranger Smith is a stand-alone special parody of the Hanna-Barbera cartoon series, "The Yogi Bear Show", which revolves around Ranger Smith.

 "A Day in the Life of Ranger Smith" was made by "The Ren & Stimpy Show" creator John Kricfalusi and his company Spümcø. "

 A Day in the Life of Ranger Smith" originally aired on Cartoon Network on September 24, 1999, along with "Boo Boo Runs Wild", a similar Yogi Bear-themed stand alone special.
