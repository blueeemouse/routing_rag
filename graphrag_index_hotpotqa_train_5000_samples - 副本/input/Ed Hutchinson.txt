Ed Hutchinson

Edwin Forrest Hutchinson (May 19, 1867 – July 19, 1934), was a Major League Baseball player who played second base for the Chicago Colts of the National League.

 He appeared in four games for the Colts in the 1890 season.

 He remained active in the minor leagues, mostly in western leagues, through 1906, and was briefly a player/manager for the Spokane Indians in 1906.
