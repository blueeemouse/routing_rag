2013 AFL Grand Final

The 2013 AFL Grand Final was an Australian rules football game contested between Hawthorn Football Club and Fremantle Football Club at the Melbourne Cricket Ground on 28 September 2013.

 It was the 117th annual Grand Final of the Australian Football League (formerly the Victorian Football League), staged to determine the premiers for the 2013 AFL season.

 The match, attended by 100,007 spectators, was won by Hawthorn by a margin of 15 points, marking the club's eleventh VFL/AFL premiership victory.

 Hawthorn's Brian Lake was awarded the Norm Smith Medal as the best player on the ground.
