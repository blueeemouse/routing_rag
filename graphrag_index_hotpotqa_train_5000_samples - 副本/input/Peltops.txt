Peltops

Peltops is a genus of bird in the family Artamidae.

 The genus contains two species endemic to the island of New Guinea.

 The genus was once placed with the monarch flycatchers, but molecular and morphometric studies place it closer to the butcherbirds, possibly as a sister taxon to this group.

 The genus is named for the Greek "pelte" meaning small shield and "ops" meaning face.

 The species have also had the common name of shieldbill.
