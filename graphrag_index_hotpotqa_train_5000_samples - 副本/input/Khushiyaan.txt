Khushiyaan

Khushiyaan is an Indian family film shot in Punjabi with little English, directed by Tirlok Malik,written by Rajan Gangahar starring Jasbir Jassi, Tisca Chopra, Kulbushan Kharbanda, Rama Vij, Master Shrey Bawa and Deep Dhillon.

 The film is produced by Darshan Garg, D.P.Goyal and Pradeep Bansal.

 The theatrical trailer premièred with Deol’s Yamla Pagla Deewana and the film is releasing soon.

 It is in the news for its impressive quote "Every family has a journey…What’s Yours?"
