Love Letters (Leslie Satcher album)

Love Letters is the debut album from American country music artist Leslie Satcher.

 It was released in 2000 on Warner Bros.

 Records.

 Although it produced four singles, none of them charted.

 Satcher wrote or co-wrote eleven of its twelve tracks, with the only outside contribution being her cover of Bobbie Gentry's "Ode to Billie Joe".
