Donna Hayward

Donna Hayward is a fictional character in the "Twin Peaks" franchise.

 She was portrayed by Lara Flynn Boyle in the television series "Twin Peaks" (1990-1991) and by Moira Kelly in the films "" (1992) and "" (2014).

 She was created by David Lynch and Mark Frost.

 Making her debut as a main character in the original series, Donna was the best friend of Laura Palmer and she is determined to figure out who Laura's killer is.

 Donna has a supporting role in the prequel film "", which depicts the final week of Laura's life.

 Donna is referenced numerous times in Jennifer Lynch's novel "The Secret Diary of Laura Palmer".
