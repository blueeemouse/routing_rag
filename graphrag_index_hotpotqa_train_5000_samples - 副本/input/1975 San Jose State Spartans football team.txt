1975 San Jose State Spartans football team

The 1975 San Jose State Spartans football team represented San Jose State University during the 1975 NCAA Division I football season as a member of the Pacific Coast Athletic Association.

 The team was led by third year head coach Darryl Rogers.

 They played home games at Spartan Stadium in San Jose, California.

 The Spartans finished the season as champions of the PCAA, with a record of nine wins and two losses (9–2, 5–0 PCAA).
