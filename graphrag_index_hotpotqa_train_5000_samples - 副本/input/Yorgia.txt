Yorgia

Yorgia waggoneri is a discoid Ediacaran, and possibly represents a transition organism between "Dickinsonia" and "Spriggina".

 However, their growth form indicates that may not be the case.

 It has a low, segmented body consisting of a short wide "head", no appendages, and a long body region, reaching a maximum length of 25 cm .

 It is classified within the extinct animal phylum Proarticulata.
