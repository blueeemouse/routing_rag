The Foster Photoplay Company

The Foster Photoplay Company was a film production company based in Chicago, Illinois.

 It was founded in 1910 by William Foster (also known as Juli Jones).

 It is widely considered to be the first film production company established by an African-American featuring all African-American casts.

 The company released a number of critically acclaimed films, including "The Railroad Porter" (1913), "The Fall Guy" (1913), and "The Butler" (1913).

 After the release of "The Railroad Porter" and "The Fall Guy", the company also became known for their films' slapstick-style comedy.
