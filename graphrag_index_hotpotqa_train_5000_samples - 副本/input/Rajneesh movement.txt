Rajneesh movement

The Rajneesh movement comprises persons inspired by the Indian mystic Bhagwan Shree Rajneesh (1931–1990), also known as Osho, particularly initiated disciples who are referred to as "neo-sannyasins" or simply "sannyasins".

 They used to be known as "Rajneeshees" or "Orange People", because of the orange and later red, maroon and pink clothes they used from 1970 until 1985.

 Members of the movement are sometimes called "Oshoites" in the Indian press.
