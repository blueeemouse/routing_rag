Igor Dmitriev (ice hockey)

Igor Dmitriev (19 October 1941 – 21 December, 1997) was a Russian ice hockey player and coach.

 He was inducted into the Russian and Soviet Hockey Hall of Fame in 1974 as a player, and in 1988 as a builder.

 He was posthumously inducted into the IIHF Hall of Fame as a builder in 2007.
