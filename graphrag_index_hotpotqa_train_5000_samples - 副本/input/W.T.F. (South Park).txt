W.T.F. (South Park)

"W.T.F." is the 10th episode of the 13th season of the American animated television series "South Park".

 The 191st overall episode of the series, it originally aired on Comedy Central in the United States on October 21, 2009.

 In the episode, the South Park boys form their own backyard wrestling league, drawing droves of fans more interested in the acting and scripted dramatic storylines than athletic elements.
