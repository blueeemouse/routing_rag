Brendan DuBois

Brendan DuBois is an American mystery fiction and suspense writer.

 In this field he has twice won a Shamus Award for Best Short Story of the Year.

 He also had his short story "The Dark Snow'" published in "Best American Mystery Stories of the Century", edited by Otto Penzler and Tony Hillerman (ISBN  ).

 Despite success in his primary genres, he is best known for his alternate history novel "Resurrection Day", which won the Sidewise Award for Alternate History.

 He was the champion on the September 28, 2012 episode of "Jeopardy!" and defeated the Beast on the February 24, 2015 episode of "The Chase".
