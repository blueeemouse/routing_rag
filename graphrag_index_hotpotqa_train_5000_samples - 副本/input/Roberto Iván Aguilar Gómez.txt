Roberto Iván Aguilar Gómez

Roberto Ivan Aguilar Gómez (born 19 May 1958 in La Paz, Bolivia) is an economist, former university official, politician, and Bolivia's Minister of Education since 2 June 2011.

 Aguilar served as docent, general secretary, and rector in the Universidad Mayor de San Andres in La Paz.

 In 2006, he was elected as a plurinominal member of the Bolivian Constituent Assembly for the Movement for Socialism and served as its vice president.

 In November 2008, he was appointed by Evo Morales as Minister of Education, succeeding Magdalena Cajías.
