Moon Publications

Moon is a travel guidebook publisher founded in 1973 in Chico, California as a collective of world travelers and writers.

 The company started with travel guides to Asia and later became the top publisher of guides to the Americas.

 Moon was an early advocate of independent travel, and their authors often live in the areas they write about.

 The company is now based in Berkeley, California and published by Avalon Travel, a member of the Perseus Books Group.
