Dragon Haven

Dragon Haven is a novel by Robin Hobb, the second novel in "The Rain Wild Chronicles".

 In a blog post Robin Hobb wrote: "The untitled book I am working on now picks up the tale of the Tarman expedition in search of Kelsingra.

 It’s my work in progress and threatens to be a long book!"

.

 Dragon Haven is written in third-person narrative from the viewpoint of several key characters.

 The narrative joins these separate threads together as a party of malformed dragons, their human keepers and other supporters are on a quest for the legendary Elderling city of Kelsingra.
