Gocoo

Gocoo, or GOCOO (ゴクウ ) are originally seven female and four male Taiko drummers from Tokyo (Japan).

 On stage Gocoo performs with up to 16 artists.

 Gocoo performed at major Music & Arts, Blues & Roots, Rock & Pop and alternative Rock festivals, in live clubs, classic theatres and concert halls or at techno events.

 Lead drummer, Kaoly Asano (浅野香 ), attracts much attention as a female Taiko artist.

 Whenever not performing with Gocoo on stage, she teaches the art of playing the Taiko at her Tawoo Taiko Dojo in Tokyo.
