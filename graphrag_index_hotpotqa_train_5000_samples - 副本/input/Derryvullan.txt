Derryvullan

Derryvullan (from Irish: "Doire Mhaoláin" , meaning "Mullan's oak wood" ) is a civil parish and townland (of 296 acres) in County Fermanagh, Northern Ireland.

 The civil parish is situated in the historic baronies of Tirkennedy and Lurg.

 Derryvullan townland is in the portion of the parish of the same name in Tirkennedy.
