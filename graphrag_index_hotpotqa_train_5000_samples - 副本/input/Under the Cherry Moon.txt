Under the Cherry Moon

Under the Cherry Moon is a 1986 American musical comedy drama film directed by and starring Prince in his directorial debut.

 The film also stars former The Time member Jerome Benton, Steven Berkoff, Kristin Scott Thomas (in her feature film debut), and Francesca Annis.

 The film was a critical and commercial failure, winning five Golden Raspberry Awards, including Worst Picture, tying with "Howard the Duck".
