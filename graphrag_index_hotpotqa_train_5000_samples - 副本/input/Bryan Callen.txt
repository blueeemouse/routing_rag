Bryan Callen

Bryan Christopher Callen (born January 26, 1967) is an American actor, comedian, and podcaster.

 He studied acting at the Beverly Hills Playhouse, and got his start as one of the original cast members on the sketch comedy series "MADtv".

 Callen is co-host of the successful independent podcast "The Fighter & The Kid," alongside entertainer and former UFC Heavyweight contender Brendan Schaub.

 He regularly appears on the Joe Rogan podcast The Joe Rogan Experience.
