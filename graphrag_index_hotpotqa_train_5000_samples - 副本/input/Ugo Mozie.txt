Ugo Mozie

Ugo Mozie (born November 16, 1991), is a fashion stylist and creative director of Nigerian descent.

 Known mainly for his work with "Sex and the City 2", his work includes television programs such as "Dateline NBC", "51st Grammy Awards", "Diddy - Dirty Money", and at the Tribeca Film Festival.

 He is currently the fashion editor at large at "360" magazine, the New York fashion editor of "Ghubar" magazine, and fashion contributor at "WAD" magazine in Paris.
