Daytona USA (video game)

Daytona USA is a racing video game developed by Sega AM2 and released by Sega, with a limited release in 1993 followed by a full release in 1994.

 One of the highest grossing arcade games of all time, "Daytona USA" was Sega's first title to debut on the Sega Model 2 arcade board, and, at the time of its release, was considered the most visually detailed 3D racing game.

 Compared to the flat-shaded polygons of its predecessor, "Virtua Racing", "Daytona's" 3D-world was fully texture-mapped, giving it a more realistic appearance.

 "Daytona" was one of the first video games to feature filtered, texture-mapped polygons, giving it the most detailed graphics yet seen in a video game up until that time.

 In single-player mode, Daytona maintained a consistent 60fps (frames per second) rate, even with multiple opponents on screen, surpassing the motion smoothness of the only other racing game in a comparable graphical arena, Namco's "Ridge Racer".
