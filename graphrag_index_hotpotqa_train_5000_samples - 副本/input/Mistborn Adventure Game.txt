Mistborn Adventure Game

The Mistborn Adventure Game is a pen and paper role playing game that was published in 2011 by Crafty Games and is a licensed release based on American author Brandon Sanderson's "Mistborn" novel series.

 Sanderson was involved in the game's creation and the game is set in Scadrial; the World created by the "Mistborn" series.

 The initial "Mistborn Adventure Game" book was released on December 16, 2011.

 Since 2011, two supplementary material books have been published.

 The first supplement was released March 2014 and is called "Terris: Wrought of Copper".

 The second supplement was released on August 3, 2014 and is called "Alloy of Law", not to be confused with the fourth "Mistborn" book called "".
