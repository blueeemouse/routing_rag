Bunge Limited

Bunge Limited (formerly Bunge International, and prior to that Bunge y Born) is a global agribusiness and food company, incorporated in Bermuda, and headquartered in White Plains, United States.

 As well as being an international soybean exporter, it is also involved in food processing, grain trading, and fertilizer.

 It competes with Cargill and Archer Daniels Midland.

 The company has over 35,000 employees at 400 facilities in 40 countries.
