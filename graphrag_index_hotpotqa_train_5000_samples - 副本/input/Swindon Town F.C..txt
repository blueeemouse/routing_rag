Swindon Town F.C.

Swindon Town Football Club is a professional football football club in Swindon, Wiltshire, England.

 Founded as Swindon AFC in 1879, they became Spartans in 1880 and Swindon Town in 1883.

 The team compete in League Two, the fourth tier of the English football league system.

 The club's home ground, where it has played since 1896, is the 15,728 capacity County Ground.
