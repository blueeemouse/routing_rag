Evelyn De Morgan

Evelyn De Morgan ( ; 30 August 1855 – 2 May 1919) was an English painter whose works were influenced by the style of the Pre-Raphaelite movement.

 She was a follower of Pre-Raphaelist Edward Burne-Jones.

 Her paintings exhibit spirituality; use of mythological, biblical, and literary themes; the role of women; light and darkness as metaphors; life and death; and allegories of war.
