Cheerful Sensibility

Cheerful Sensibility is the first studio album by South Korean band F.T. Island, released on 5 June 2007.

 The album contains 13 songs divided into two sections: "Emotional Chapter" consists of rock ballads, while "F.T. Island Chapter" is made up of pop-rock songs written by Japanese composers.

 The album sold 79,000 copies and became the sixth most sold album of the year in South Korea.

 It was re-released on 3 December by the title "The Refreshment", with three new songs.

 The package included a Music 2.0 program, which enabled changes to the levels of vocals and instruments.

 It sold 25,724 copies.
