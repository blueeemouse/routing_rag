Bluetory

Bluetory is the debut mini-album by South Korean pop-rock idol band CNBLUE.

 It was released on January 14, 2010, under FNC Music and distributed by Mnet Media.

 After being signed to the record label, the quartet was sent to Japan, where it independently released two mini-albums.

 While working with the band, frontman Jung Yong-hwa was cast in the television drama "You're Beautiful" (2009), where he first garnered recognition.
