Fritzi Burr

Fritzi Burr (May 31, 1924 – January 17, 2003) Was an American character/comedic actress who appeared as various comic foils to Fred Sanford on the NBC sitcom TV series "Sanford and Son".

 A versatile character actress with a flair for comedy, and could do characterizations in various dialects, Fritzi appeared in both motion pictures and on television, in both comedic and dramatic roles.

 Fritzi was also the sister-in-law of "Sanford and Son" producer Saul Turteltaub and the aunt of Saul's son Jon, who also works in Hollywood as a producer/director/writer.
