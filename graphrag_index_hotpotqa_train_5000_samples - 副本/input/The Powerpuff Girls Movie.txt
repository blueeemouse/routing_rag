The Powerpuff Girls Movie

The Powerpuff Girls Movie is a 2002 American animated superhero action-comedy film based on the Cartoon Network animated television series of the same name.

 The film was directed by series creator Craig McCracken, and was released in theaters on July 3, 2002, by Warner Bros.

 Pictures.

 It is a prequel to the series that tells the origin story of how the Powerpuff Girls were created, and how they came to be the defenders of Townsville.

 In theaters, a "Dexter's Laboratory" short titled "Chicken Scratch" was shown prior to the film, which later aired as part of the series' fourth season.
