Michael Davitt

Michael Davitt (Irish: "Mícheál Mac Dáibhéid" ; 25 March 184630 May 1906) was an Irish republican and agrarian campaigner who founded the Irish National Land League.

 He was also a labour leader, Home Rule politician and Member of Parliament (MP).

 He campaigned for Home Rule and was a close ally of Charles Stuart Parnell, the leader of the Irish Parliamentary Party, until the party split over Parnell's divorce and Davitt joined the anti-Parnellite Irish National Federation.
