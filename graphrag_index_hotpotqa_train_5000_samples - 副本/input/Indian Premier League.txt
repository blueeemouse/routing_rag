Indian Premier League

The Indian Premier League (IPL), officially Vivo Indian Premier League for sponsorship reasons, is a professional Twenty20 cricket league in India contested during April and May of every year by teams representing Indian cities.

 The league was founded by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) in 2007, and is regarded as the brainchild of Lalit Modi, the founder and former commissioner of the league.

 Vivo, the smartphone multinational company, is serving as the title sponsor since the ninth season of the league.

 The current IPL title holders are the Mumbai Indians, who won the 2017 Indian Premier League.
