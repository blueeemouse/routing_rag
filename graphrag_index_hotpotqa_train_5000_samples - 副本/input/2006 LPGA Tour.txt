2006 LPGA Tour

The 2006 LPGA Tour was a series of weekly golf tournaments for elite female golfers from around the world which took place from February through December 2006.

 The tournaments were sanctioned by the United States-based Ladies Professional Golf Association (LPGA).

 In 2006, prize money on the LPGA Tour exceeded US$50 million for the first time in the history of the LPGA Tour.
