Robert Clark Morgan

Robert Clark Morgan (13 March 1798 – 23 September 1864) was an English sea captain and diarist.

 He was captain of the ship that brought the first settlers to South Australia in the "Duke of York" in 1836.

 The diaries that he kept are held in the collection of the State Library of New South Wales.

 His life as a whaling captain has been recorded in the book "The Man Who Hunted Whales" (2011) by Dorothy M. Heinrich.
