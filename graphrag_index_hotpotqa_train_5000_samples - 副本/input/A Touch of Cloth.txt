A Touch of Cloth

A Touch of Cloth is a British television comedy police procedural series created and written by Charlie Brooker and Daniel Maier.

 It is a parody of British detective and crime dramas, and stars John Hannah as title character Jack Cloth, a police detective with personal issues, and Suranne Jones as Anne Oldman, his colleague.

 The title is a play on the title of another detective series, "A Touch of Frost", as well as the British euphemism "to touch cloth".

 To date there have been three series.

 The first series was released on DVD in the UK on September 3, 2012.

 Both the second and third series were released on September 1, 2014.
