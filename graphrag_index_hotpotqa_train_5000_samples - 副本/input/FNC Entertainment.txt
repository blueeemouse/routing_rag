FNC Entertainment

FNC Entertainment (; stands for "fish and cake") is a South Korean entertainment company established in 2006 by singer-turned-producer Han Seong-ho.

 The label operates as a record label, talent agency, music production company, event management and concert production company, and music publishing house.

 The label previously known as FNC Music, which only managing musicians and later changed its name to FNC Entertainment in 2012 and began to broaden their business field of entertainment.

 It has since January 2012, been based in its own company offices in Cheongdam-dong.
