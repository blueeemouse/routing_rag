Lloyd Anoaʻi

Lloyd Anoaʻi (born May 17, 1970) is an American professional wrestler and professional wrestling manager.

 He is best known for his appearances with the World Wrestling Federation (WWF) under the ring names Tahitian Savage, Fred Williams, and Lloyd Lanui and for his appearances with Extreme Championship Wrestling (ECW) under the ring name L.A. Smooth.

 He is one of the three sons of professional wrestling patriarch Afa Anoaʻi and a member of the Anoaʻi family.
