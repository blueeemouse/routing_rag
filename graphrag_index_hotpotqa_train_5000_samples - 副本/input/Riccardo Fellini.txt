Riccardo Fellini

Riccardo Fellini ( 21 February 1921 - 26 March 1991) was an Italian film actor.

 He also worked as a director on documentaries for RAI.

 He was the brother of the director Federico Fellini and starred in his 1953 film "I Vitelloni".

 His younger sister was the actress Maddalena Fellini.
