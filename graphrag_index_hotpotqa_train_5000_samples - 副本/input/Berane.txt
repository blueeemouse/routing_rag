Berane

Berane (Cyrillic: Беране, ] ) is one of the largest towns of northeastern Montenegro and a former administrative centre of the Ivangrad District.

 It is a seat of one of many multi-ethnic and multi-religious municipalities in Montenegro.

 The town is located on the Lim river, being part of Sandžak region.

 From 1949 until 1992 it was named as Ivangrad (Иванград) in honour to people's hero Ivan Milutinović.

 The town has a population of over 11 thousand, whereas its municipality area reaches nearly 30 thousand people, making it one of the largest centres of Polimlje area.
