Municipality of Annandale

The Municipality of Annandale was a local government area of Sydney, New South Wales, Australia.

 The municipality was proclaimed on 29 December 1893 as the Borough of Annandale when the East Ward of Leichhardt Council separated, and, with an area of 1.4 square kilometres, covered the entire suburb of Annandale, excepting a small block between Johnstons Creek, Booth Street and Parramatta Road.

 The council was amalgamated with the Municipality of Leichhardt (now the Inner West Council) to the west with the passing of the "Local Government (Areas) Act 1948".
