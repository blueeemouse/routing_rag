Campus for Finance

Campus for Finance Association is a student association at the WHU – Otto Beisheim School of Management in Vallendar, Germany.

 It was founded in 2001 in cooperation with the Endowed Chair of Finance, held by Prof. Dr. Markus Rudolf, to support and organize the student-run “Campus for Finance”-conferences.

 The association annually hosts the “WHU New Year’s Conference” and the “WHU Private Equity Conference”.

 The mission of the association is entitled “Uniting the World of Finance”.
