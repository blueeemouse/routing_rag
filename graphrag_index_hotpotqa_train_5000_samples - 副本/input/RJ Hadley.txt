RJ Hadley

RJ Hadley (born Rakeim Jerhal Hadley on January 14, 1969) is a Democratic Party activist and current Tax Commissioner of Rockdale County, Georgia.

 In 2008 he was a delegate to the Democratic National Convention representing Georgia.

 He has been a member of the Rockdale County Democratic Party since 2006 and was elected to its executive board in 2010.
