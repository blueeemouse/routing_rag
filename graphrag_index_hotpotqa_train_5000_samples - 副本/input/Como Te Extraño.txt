Como Te Extraño

"Como Te Extraño" (English: How Much I Miss You ) is a song recorded by American Tejano singer Pete Astudillo for his second studio album of the same name (1995).

 The song was released as the lead single by EMI Latin.

 It was composed by Astudillo and former Selena y Los Dinos band members; A.B. Quintanilla and Joe Ojeda.

 The recording is a tribute song for American Tejano singer Selena, who was killed in March 1995.

 Selena was the lead vocalist of Selena y Los Dinos, while Astudillo was the backup dancer of the group and occasional duet partner with Selena.
