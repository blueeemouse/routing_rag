Toy Story Toons

Toy Story Toons is a series of short animated films based on the "Toy Story" franchise.

 The plotline of the series begins after "Toy Story 3".

 The films are set at Bonnie's house, the new home of Andy's toys.

 As of 2015, three shorts have been released as part of the series – "Hawaiian Vacation" and "Small Fry" in 2011 and "Partysaurus Rex" in 2012.

 A fourth short, titled "Mythic Rock", was rumored in 2013, but was scrapped.
