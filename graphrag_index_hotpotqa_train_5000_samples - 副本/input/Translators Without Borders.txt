Translators Without Borders

Translators without Borders is a non-profit organization set up to provide translation services for humanitarian non-profits.

 It was established in 2010 as a sister organization of Traducteurs Sans Frontières , founded in 1993 by Lori Thicke and Ros Smith-Thomas of Lexcelera.

 As of 2012 it had about 1600 vetted volunteer translators.

 Translators without Borders aims to close the language gaps that hinder critical humanitarian efforts by connecting non-profit humanitarian organizations with a volunteer community of professional translators, building language translation capacity at the local level and raising awareness globally about language barriers.
