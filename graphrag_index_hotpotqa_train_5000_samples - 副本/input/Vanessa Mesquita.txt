Vanessa Mesquita

Vanessa Andréa Mesquita (born April 1, 1986 in Sao Paulo) is a Brazilian model, bodybuilder, activist and reality television personality best known for being the winner of the fourteenth season of the Brazilian version of the Big Brother, broadcast by Rede Globo in 2014.

 After the program, Mesquita made the cover of Playboy (Brazil)
