Bodies (Drowning Pool song)

"Bodies" (often called "Let the Bodies Hit the Floor") is a song by the American rock band Drowning Pool and also is the lead single from their debut album "Sinner".

 Released in May 2001, the song is Drowning Pool's signature song and has been featured in various films, TV programs, and advertisements since its release.

 It was also the theme song for the 2001 WWF SummerSlam pay-per-view event, as well as that of the ECW brand in 2006 to early 2008.

 During 2001, the song got popular, but the song was taken off radio stations after the September 11 attacks because the song would've been inappropriate for the terrorist attack.
