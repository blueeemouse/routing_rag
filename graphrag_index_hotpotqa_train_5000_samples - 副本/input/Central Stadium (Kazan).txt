Central Stadium (Kazan)

The Central Stadium (Russian: Центральный стадион , Tatar: Үзәк стадион , "Üzäk stadion " ) is a multi-purpose stadium in Kazan, Russia.

 It is currently used mostly for football matches and is the home ground of FC Rubin Kazan.

The western half of the tribunes is covered with a canopy.

 In 2010, the stadium gained Four stars classification from UEFA.
