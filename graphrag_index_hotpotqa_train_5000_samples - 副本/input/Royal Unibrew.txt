Royal Unibrew

Royal Unibrew is a brewing and beverage company headquartered in Faxe, Denmark.

 Its brands include Ceres, Faxe, Albani and Royal.

 Royal Unibrew also has a strong presence in the Baltic region, where it owns Vilniaus Tauras, Kalnapilis (both in Lithuania), and Lāčplēša Alus (in Latvia).

 It also brews and markets Heineken in Denmark.
