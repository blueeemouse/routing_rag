Springtime!

 is a boutique entertainment company established in the UK in 1977 and based in the United States since 1983.

 The company's activities encompass film, TV, radio, record and stage show production, entertainment marketing & publicity and talent management.

 The company has produced and marketed audio recordings, films, TV specials and music videos.

 It is periodically active as a record company releasing its own audio productions and acquisitions.
