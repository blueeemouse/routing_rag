Little Man, What Now? (1933 film)

Little Man, What Now?

 (German:Kleiner Mann - was nun?)

 is a 1933 German drama film directed by Fritz Wendhausen and starring Hermann Thimig, Hertha Thiele and Viktor de Kowa.

 It is an adaptation of the novel of the same name by Hans Fallada.

 It was well received by contemporary critics, many of whom considered it the best German film of 1933.

 A separate American film adaptation of Fallada's novel, "Little Man, What Now?

", was released in 1934.
