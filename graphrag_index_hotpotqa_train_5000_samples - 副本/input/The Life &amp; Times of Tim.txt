The Life &amp; Times of Tim

The Life & Times of Tim is an HBO comedy animated television series, which premiered on September 28, 2008.

 The series was created by Steve Dildarian, and is about a hapless man in his mid-20s named Tim (voiced by Dildarian) who lives in New York City with his girlfriend Amy.

 Throughout the series, Tim constantly finds himself in increasingly awkward situations in both his work and personal life.
