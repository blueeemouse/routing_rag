Eugene Patterson Harris

Pat Harris has been a leading American criminal defense and civil rights attorney for over 25 years based in Los Angeles, California.

 In addition, he has co-written two non-fiction books, "Mistrial: An Inside Look at How the Criminal Justice System Works ... and Sometimes Doesn't" and the Susan McDougal autobiography, "The Woman Who Wouldn't Talk", which spent four weeks on the New York Times' bestseller list"."

 As a lawyer, he has been involved in a number of high-profile cases including representing Susan McDougal in her fight against Whitewater prosecutor Kenneth Starr, and Will Lynch who was accused of assaulting a priest who had abused him forty years earlier.
