Amanda Carrington

Amanda Carrington is a fictional character on the ABC prime time soap opera "Dynasty", created by Richard and Esther Shapiro.

 Originated by actress Catherine Oxenberg early in the series' fifth season in 1984, Oxenberg left the series after its sixth season in 1986.

 The role was immediately recast with Karen Cellini, who appeared in the series' seventh season, though no explanation was given as to why Amanda now spoke with an American accent.
