Personal Care Products Council

The Personal Care Products Council (PCPC) was founded in 1894 as the Manufacturing Perfumers' Association.

 In 1922 it was renamed to the American Manufacturers of Toilet Articles (AMTA) in 1922; in 1970 the association adopted the name Cosmetic, Toiletry, and Fragrance Association; in November 2007, the name was changed to the Personal Care Products Council.
