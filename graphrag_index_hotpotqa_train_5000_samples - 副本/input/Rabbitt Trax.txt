Rabbitt Trax

Rabbitt Trax is the tenth studio album by the country artist Eddie Rabbitt, released in 1986 by RCA Records.

 The album produced four singles including "A World Without Love", "Repetitive Regret", "Both to Each Other (Friends and Lovers)" (a duet with the country-pop star Juice Newton) and "Gotta Have You".

 All of thse singles reached the top ten on country charts, with the duet reaching number one.
