Kourtney and Kim Take Miami

Kourtney and Kim Take Miami (previously titled Kourtney and Khloé Take Miami) is an American reality television series.

 It premiered on E! on August 16, 2009, as the first "Keeping Up with the Kardashians" spin-off.

 The series originally followed sisters Kourtney and Khloé Kardashian as they opened a second D-A-S-H location in Miami, Florida.

 From the third season onward, sister Kim Kardashian replaced Khloé, who had other work commitments.

 The third season began filming in October 2012, and premiered on January 20, 2013.
