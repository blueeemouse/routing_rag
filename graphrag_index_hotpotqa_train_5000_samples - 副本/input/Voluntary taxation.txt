Voluntary taxation

Voluntary taxation is a theory that states that taxation should be a voluntary act.

 Under the theory, people should have the option to pay taxes instead of being forced to pay taxes by their government.

 Under this theory, people would control how much they pay and where they spend it.

 The theory is a part of Objectivist politics and many libertarian ideologies.

 Proponents of some studies assert that individuals will give to government, paying voluntary taxes to support specific functions.

 Donations average 22 percent of an endowment to government, and 27 percent to private nonprofits, and are influenced by their cause, level, and perceptions of effectiveness and efficiency.

 State lotteries are an example of a voluntary taxation system.
