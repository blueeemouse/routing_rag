Tom Wopat

Thomas Steven Wopat (born September 9, 1951) is an American actor and singer.

 He first achieved fame as Lucas K. "Luke" Duke on the long-running television action/comedy series "The Dukes of Hazzard".

 Since then, Wopat has worked regularly, most often on the stage in musicals and in supporting television and movie roles.

 He was a semi-regular guest on the 1990s comedy series "Cybill", and had a small role as U.S. Marshal Gil Tatum in "Django Unchained" (2012).

 Wopat also has a recurring role as Sheriff Jim Wilkins on the television series "Longmire".

 Additionally, Wopat has recorded several albums of country songs and pop standards, scoring a series of moderately successful singles in the 1980s and 1990s.
