Taekkyeon

Taekkyeon is a traditional Korean martial art first explicitly recorded during the Joseon Dynasty.

 Taekkyeon is characterized by fluid, dynamic footwork called ""pum balgi"" or Stepping-on-Triangles.

 Taekkyeon has many leg and whole-body techniques with fully integrated armwork.

 Although taekkyeon primarily utilizes kicking, punching, and arm strikes thrown from a mobile stance and does not provide a framework for groundfighting, it does incorporate a variety of different throws, takedowns, and grappling techniques to complement its striking focus.

 The martial art is frequently romanized informally as Taekgyeon, Taekkyon, or Taekyun.
