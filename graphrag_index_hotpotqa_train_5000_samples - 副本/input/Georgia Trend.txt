Georgia Trend

Georgia Trend (tagline: "The Magazine of Georgia Business, Politics & Economic Development Since 1985") is a monthly business magazine covering business and finance in Georgia.

 It was established in 1985 by Times Publishing Company, which published the "Tampa Bay Times" (then known as the "St. Petersburg Times").

 It was modeled on the business magazine "Florida Trend", because Eugene Patterson, the then-president of Times Publishing Company, wanted to start a similar magazine in his home state of Georgia.

 The magazine's first issue was published in September of that year.

 It is distributed in every county in Georgia.

 In February 2017, Morris Publishing Group acquired the magazine's publisher, Trend Publications LLC.
