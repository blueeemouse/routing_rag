Sumo (band)

Sumo was a 1980s Argentine alternative rock band, merging "post-punk" with "reggae".

 Headed by Italian-born Luca Prodan, it remained underground for most of its short activity, but was extremely influential in shaping contemporary Argentine rock.

 Sumo introduced British post-punk to the Argentine scene, with many lyrics in English.

 And provided a visceral counterpoint to the progressive and nueva canción influences then dominant in "Rock en Español".
