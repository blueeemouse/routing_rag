Logie Awards of 1969

The 11th Annual TV Week Logie Awards were presented on Friday 21 March 1969 at Southern Cross Hotel in Melbourne and broadcast on the Nine Network.

 Bert Newton from the Nine Network was the Master of Ceremonies.

 American television actors Dennis Cole, Barbara Anderson, William Shatner, Ty Hardin, British actor Barry Morse and Skippy appeared as guests.

 This article lists the winners of Logie Awards (Australian television) for 1969.
