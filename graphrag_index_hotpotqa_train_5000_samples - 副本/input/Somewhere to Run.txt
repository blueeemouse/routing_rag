Somewhere to Run

Somewhere to Run is a 1989 British made-for-television film about teenage runaways.

 Directed by Carol Wiseman and written by Matthew Jacobs, it starred Robin Weaver and Vicky Murdock as two teenage girls who run away from home and find themselves on the streets of London.

 The film also starred Angela Pleasence and Michael Jayston.

 It was produced by Thames Television for the ITV network, and received its ITV debut on 11 July 1989.
