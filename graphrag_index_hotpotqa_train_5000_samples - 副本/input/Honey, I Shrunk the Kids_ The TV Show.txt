Honey, I Shrunk the Kids: The TV Show

Honey, I Shrunk the Kids: The TV Show (truncated to Honey, I Shrunk the Kids in the show's title sequence) is an American syndicated comic science fiction sitcom based on the 1989 film, "Honey, I Shrunk the Kids".

 It expands upon the original film's concept of a shrinking experiment gone wrong to include a myriad of experiments gone awry.

 It debuted in syndication on September 1, 1997 and ran for three consecutive seasons, concluding with the 66th episode on May 20, 2000.
