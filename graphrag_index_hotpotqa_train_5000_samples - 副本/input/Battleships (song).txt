Battleships (song)

"Battleships" is a song recorded by American rock band Daughtry for their fourth studio album, "Baptized" (2013).

 The song was written by Daughtry frontman Chris Daughtry, Sam Hollander, and Martin Johnson, while production was handled by Johnson.

 It was serviced to hot adult contemporary radio in the US through RCA Records on May 12, 2014, as the third overall single from the album and the second to be promoted in North America.

 The song debuted at number 38 on the "Billboard" Adult Pop Songs chart.
