Thomas Blomberg

Thomas G. Blomberg is an American criminologist.

 an expert in criminology research and public policy; delinquency, education and crime desistance; penology and social control; and victim services and focuses in examining the relationship between educational achievement among incarcerated youthful offenders and successful community reintegration, currently the Dean and Sheldon L. Messinger Professor of Criminology at Florida State University College of Criminology and Criminal Justice.
