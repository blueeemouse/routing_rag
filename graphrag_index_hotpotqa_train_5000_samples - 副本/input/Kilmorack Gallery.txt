Kilmorack Gallery

Kilmorack Gallery is a converted church building, in a rural location near Beauly in Inverness-shire, Scotland.

 It was established as a commercial gallery in 1997 after the building was purchased by art dealer Tony Davidson, believing that inspirational art could sit in an inspirational building just as well as in a white cube.

 By putting on consistently ambitious solo and mixed shows of some of the country’s finest artists and sculptors, Kilmorack Gallery has established itself as one of Scotland’s leading contemporary art galleries.

 The success of non-urban, destination galleries like Kilmorack 'has made "remote" more than ever a relative term', wrote art critic Duncan Macmillan of the Scotsman.

 Davidson and art critic Jan Patience featured on the The Janice Forsyth Show in May 2013, speaking about the gallery and the works on exhibition.
