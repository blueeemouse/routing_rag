Pendleton Heights (Bethany, West Virginia)

Pendleton Heights, also known as the William K. Pendleton House and Christman Manor at Pendleton Heights, is a historic home located on the campus of Bethany College, at Bethany, Brooke County, West Virginia.

 It was built in 1841, as a small, box like dwelling.

 It was altered in 1872 by college president William K. Pendleton to take on a Gothic Revival-style of architecture like other buildings on campus.

 It is a two story brick residence with characteristic steep gable roofs and arched windows.
