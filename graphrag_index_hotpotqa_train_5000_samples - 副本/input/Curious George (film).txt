Curious George (film)

Curious George is a 2006 American German animated adventure comedy film based on the Curious George books by H.A. Rey and Margret Rey.

 It was directed by Matthew O'Callaghan, who replaced Jun Falkenstein.

 Ken Kaufman wrote the screenplay based on a story by him and Mike Werb.

 Ron Howard, David Kirschner, and John Shapiro produced.

 It was released on February 10, 2006 by Universal Pictures.

 It stars Will Ferrell, Drew Barrymore, Dick Van Dyke, David Cross, Eugene Levy, Joan Plowright, and Frank Welker.

 It was Universal's first theatrically-released animated film since 1995's "Balto", and Imagine Entertainment's first fully animated film.
