L.A.M.B.

L.A.M.B. was a fashion line by American singer Gwen Stefani, the lead vocalist of the rock band No Doubt.

 The line manufactured apparel and fashion accessories.

 It was founded in 2003 and made its runway debut in 2004.

 The fashion line manufactured accessories like shoes, watches, bags and a fragrance called "L." The name is an acronym of her debut solo album "Love.
