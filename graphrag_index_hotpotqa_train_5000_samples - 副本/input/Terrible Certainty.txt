Terrible Certainty

Terrible Certainty is the third studio album by German thrash metal band Kreator released in 1987 through Noise Records.

 It was released on cd, cassette, black vinyl, and as a limited edition red vinyl.

 The remastered version of 2000 contains the tracks of the 1988 Out of the Dark ... Into the Light EP as bonus tracks.

 A remastered version was released on June 9, 2017.
