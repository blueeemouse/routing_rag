Changchun Film Festival

The Changchun Film Festival is a biennial international film festival held in the Chinese city of Changchun.

 Ostensibly international, its award for best film, the Golden Deer has nevertheless primarily been awarded to Chinese and other East Asia-region films.

 It was first held in 1992 and was founded in part by the Changchun Film Studio (now the Changchun Film Studio Group Corporation).
