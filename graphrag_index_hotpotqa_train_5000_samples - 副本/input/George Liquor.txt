George Liquor

George Liquor (often taking his epithet as George Liquor, American) is a cartoon character created by John Kricfalusi.

 Liquor is most famous for his appearances on "The Ren & Stimpy Show".

 He is a mascot for Kricfalusi's defunct animation studio, Spümcø.

 Kricfalusi portrayed George Liquor as a patriotic, outspoken, politically conservative blowhard.

 Kricfalusi described Liquor as his favorite character to animate.
