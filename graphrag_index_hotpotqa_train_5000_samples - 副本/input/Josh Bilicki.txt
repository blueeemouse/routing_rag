Josh Bilicki

Josh Bilicki (born June 3, 1995) is an American professional racing driver.

 He currently competes part-time in the NASCAR Xfinity Series, driving the No. 40 Chevrolet Camaro for MBM Motorsports and the Nos. 8 and 78 Camaros for B. J. McLeod Motorsports, and part-time in the Monster Energy NASCAR Cup Series, driving the No. 51 Chevrolet SS for Rick Ware Racing.
