Cocky (album)

Cocky is Kid Rock's third major release under Atlantic Records, and his studio fifth studio album overall, released in 2001.

 The album is Kid Rock's first to feature material in the genres of country and blues.

 The album is known for featuring the ballad "Picture", recorded as a duet with Sheryl Crow.

 In May 2011, the album was certified 5× platinum by the RIAA and has sold 5,344,000 copies in the US as of December 2013.
