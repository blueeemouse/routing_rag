Single-Handed (TV series)

Single-Handed is an Irish television drama series, first broadcast on RTÉ Television in 2007.

 Set and filmed in the west of Ireland, it focuses on the life of a member of the Garda Síochána (police), Sergeant Jack Driscoll (played by Owen McDonnell).

 Three two-episode, single-story series aired one each on consecutive nights in 2007, 2008 and 2009.

 Series Four, consisting of three stories told over six episodes, began in RTÉ One November 2010.
