Mulan Jr.

Mulan Jr. is a one-act stage musical based on the 1998 Disney animated film "Mulan", which in turn was based on the Chinese legend of Hua Mulan) and the story "Fa Mulan" by Robert D. San Souci.

 The adaptation mixes songs featured in the 1998 film as well as deleted songs from the film, including the revival of songs written by Stephen Schwartz, who was the original composer for the 1998 film before leaving to work on "The Prince of Egypt".

 The musical is G-rated, any mature content from the sources it is based on having been deleted in the adaptation.

 The cast includes 26 roles plus an ensemble.
