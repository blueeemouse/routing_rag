De Nova

De Nova is the second album by Chicago-based rock band The Redwalls, released in the United States on June 21, 2005 through Capitol Records.

 Produced by Rob Schnapf (Elliott Smith, The Vines), "De Nova" was the first album the band released on Capitol since initially signing with the label in 2003.

 "It's Alright" was featured on an episode of the HBO Original Series "Entourage".
