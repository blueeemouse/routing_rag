Wor–Wic Community College

Wor–Wic Community College is a two-year institution located in Salisbury in the Eastern Shore region of Maryland, offering both credit and continuing education classes.

 The college's name is a portmanteau of Maryland's Worcester and Wicomico counties.

 It was founded in 1975, and operated as a "college without walls" in the two counties for 20 years before constructing a campus in Salisbury in 1994.

 In 1989, state legislation was enacted to allow Somerset County residents to attend Wor-Wic at the in-county tuition rate.
