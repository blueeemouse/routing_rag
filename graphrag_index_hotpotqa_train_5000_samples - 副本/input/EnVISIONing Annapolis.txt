EnVISIONing Annapolis

EnVISIONing Annapolis was a privately funded lecture series and visioning charrette for Annapolis, Maryland, that aimed to generate dialogue on responsible, long-term development of the city.

 Activities for enVISIONing Annapolis ran from 2007 through 2008.

 The organization backing the process, the EnVISIONing Annapolis Foundation, was formed solely for this purpose.

 Important contributors and co-sponsors include the University of Maryland, Annapolis Charter 300, and St. Johns College.

 The primary organizers of the project included MS&R Architects (Garth Rockcastle and Troy Urman), Miriam Gusevich, and Glenn Smith.
