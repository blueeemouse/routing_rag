Ralf Zumdick

Ralf Zumdick (born 10 May 1958) is a former German football goalkeeper.

 After his career (21 2.

 Bundesliga, 282 Bundesliga games; one goal) Zumdick was assistant coach at VfL Bochum from 1995 till 1999.

 Zumdick is often cited as the player who pioneered the practice of the penalty-taking-goalkeeper when he scored against Andreas Köpke in 1988.

 He is currently manager Thomas Doll's assistant coach at Ferencváros.
