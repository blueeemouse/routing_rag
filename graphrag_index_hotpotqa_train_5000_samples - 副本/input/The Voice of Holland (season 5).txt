The Voice of Holland (season 5)

The Voice of Holland (season 5) is the fifth season of the Dutch reality singing competition, created by media tycoon John de Mol.

 It aired during 2014 on RTL4.

 Martijn Krabbé and Wendy van Dijk returned as co-hosts, while Jamai Loman replaced Winston Gerschtanowitz as host of the red room.

 Marco Borsato, Trijntje Oosterhuis, Ilse DeLange and Ali B returned as coaches.
