Kristy Swanson

Kristen Noel "Kristy" Swanson (born December 19, 1969) is an American actress, best known for her portrayal of Buffy Summers in the 1992 cult film "Buffy the Vampire Slayer".

 Swanson began her career appearing in several teen films, including "Pretty in Pink" (1986) and "Ferris Bueller's Day Off" (1986).

 Her first starring role was in Wes Craven's horror film "Deadly Friend" (1986), followed by her portrayal of Catherine "Cathy" Dollanganger in the controversial film adaptation of V.C. Andrews's "Flowers in the Attic" (1987).
