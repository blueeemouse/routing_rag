Against Me! Is Reinventing Axl Rose

Against Me!

 Is Reinventing Axl Rose is the debut album by the Gainesville, Florida punk rock band Against Me!

, released on March 5, 2002 under No Idea Records.

 It was the group's first release as a full band with electric guitars, bass guitar, and drums.

 The album was produced by Rob McGregor, who would also produce the band's second album "Against Me!

 as the Eternal Cowboy" (2003).

 The album's title references Guns N' Roses singer Axl Rose, illustrated on the cover.
