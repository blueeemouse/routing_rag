Culture of New York City

The culture of New York City is reflected in its size and ethnic diversity.

 Many American cultural movements first emerged in the city.

 The Harlem Renaissance established the African-American renaissance in the United States.

 Large numbers of Italian Americans and Jews emigrated to New York throughout the twentieth century, significantly influencing the culture and image of New York City.

 American modern dance developed in New York in the early 20th century.

 The city was the top venue for jazz in the 1940s, expressionism in the 1950s and home to hip hop, punk rock, and the Beat Generation.
