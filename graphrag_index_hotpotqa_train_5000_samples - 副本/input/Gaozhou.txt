Gaozhou

Gaozhou is a county-level city in southwestern Guangdong Province, China.

 Formerly the primary city in the area, it is now administered as part of the prefecture-level city of Maoming.

 At the time of the 2010 census, Gaozhou had a population of 1,288,665 living in its 3270.8 sqkm territory.

 The locals speak a variation of the Gaozhou dialect.

 It is best known in China for being the ancestral home of Leo Ku.
