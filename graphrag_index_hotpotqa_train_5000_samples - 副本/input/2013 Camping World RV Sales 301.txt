2013 Camping World RV Sales 301

The 2013 Camping World RV Sales 301 was a NASCAR Sprint Cup Series stock car race held on July 14, 2013, at New Hampshire Motor Speedway in Loudon, New Hampshire, United States.

 Contested over 302 laps, it was the nineteenth race of the 2013 NASCAR Sprint Cup Series season.

 Brian Vickers of Michael Waltrip Racing won the race, his third career Sprint Cup win, while Kyle Busch finished second.

 Jeff Burton, Brad Keselowski, and Aric Almirola rounded out the top five.
