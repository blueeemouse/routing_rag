Bill Bateman

William Augustus 'Bill' Bateman (11 September 1866 – 27 July 1935) was a businessman, an Australian sportsman who played first-class cricket for Western Australia and Australian rules football in the Western Australian Football Association (WAFA).

 He was the captain of the Swans Football Club - the first Australian Rules Football team in Western Australia.

 He came from one of the founding families of Western Australia and his grandson Alan Bateman created the TV soap Home and Away.
