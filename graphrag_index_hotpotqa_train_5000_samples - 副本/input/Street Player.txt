Street Player

Street Player is the gold-selling sixth studio album by funk band Rufus (and their fourth album featuring singer Chaka Khan), released on the ABC Records label in 1978.

 "Street Player" was the band's third album to top "Billboard"'s R&B Albums chart and also reached #14 on Pop.

 The album includes the singles "Stay" (US R&B #3, US Pop #38) and "Blue Love" (US R&B #34).
