1974 San Jose State Spartans football team

The 1974 San Jose State Spartans football team represented San Jose State University during the 1974 NCAA Division I football season as a member of the Pacific Coast Athletic Association.

 The team was led by second year head coach Darryl Rogers.

 They played their home games at Spartan Stadium in San Jose, California.

 The Spartans finished the season with a record of eight wins, three losses and one tie (8–3–1, 2–2–0 PCAA).
