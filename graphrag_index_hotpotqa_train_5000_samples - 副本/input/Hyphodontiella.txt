Hyphodontiella

Hyphodontiella is a genus of two species of wood-inhabiting corticioid fungi.

 The genus contains two species found in Nordic countries.

 "Hyphodontiella" was circumscribed by Swedish mycologist Åke Strid in 1975.

 Originally classified in the family Corticiaceae along with most other corticioid fungi, it was moved to the Clavariaceae in 2007 .
