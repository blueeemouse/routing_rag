Sunday Morning (Maroon 5 song)

"Sunday Morning" is a song by American pop rock band Maroon 5.

 It was released on December 2, 2004 as the fourth single from their debut studio album, "Songs About Jane" (2002).

 Released in 2004, the single peaked at number 31 in the United States becoming Maroon 5's fourth Top 40 single; it also peaked at number 27 in the United Kingdom and Australia.
