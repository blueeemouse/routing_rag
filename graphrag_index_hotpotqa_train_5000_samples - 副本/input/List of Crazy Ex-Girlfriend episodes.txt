List of Crazy Ex-Girlfriend episodes

"Crazy Ex-Girlfriend" is an American musical comedy-drama television series created by Rachel Bloom and Aline Brosh McKenna, that premiered on The CW on October 12, 2015.

 The series stars Bloom as Rebecca Bunch, a depressed young woman who decides to follow her ex-boyfriend from New York City to West Covina, California in the hopes of finding real happiness.

 All episode titles follow the pattern of referring to Rebecca's ex-boyfriend, Josh.

 On March 11, 2016, The CW renewed the series for a second season which premiered on October 21, 2016.
