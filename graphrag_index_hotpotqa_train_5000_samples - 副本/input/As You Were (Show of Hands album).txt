As You Were (Show of Hands album)

As You Were is the fourth live album by English acoustic roots duo Show of Hands.

 Following the release of their acclaimed tenth studio album "Country Life" in 2003, which itself was promoted by a tour, the duo were named the "Best Live Act" at the 2004 BBC Radio 2 Folk Awards.

 The live interest in the duo ultimately lead to a greater interest when the band announced their Autumn Tour 2004.

 The tour itself ran from November–December and included Miranda Sykes as a collaborator, her first collaboration with the duo.

 With the band's producer and engineer of the era Mick Dolan, the entire tour was recorded onto MiniDisc, with the duo subsequently ploughing through each concert recording the following day to identify the best performances and then collating and working through the "A list again" to find the best tracks to create the live album.
