Kinohimitsu

Kinohimitsu (木の秘密 , Kinohimitsu ) is a functional beauty and health brand owned by Kino Biotech, a leading integrated bio-nutraceutical and cosmeceutical public listed company in the Taiwan`s GreTai Securities Market (GTSM) with its head office in Singapore.

 According to Euromonitor International, Kinohimitsu was the top selling beauty drink and collagen drink in Malaysia and Singapore between 2008 and 2010.

 The brand name, (木の秘密) means the secret of Nature.

 The brand name stays true to the company’s practices in using natural ingredients in its products.
