The Flying Sorceress

The Flying Sorceress is a 1956 one reel animated "Tom and Jerry" short directed and produced by William Hanna and Joseph Barbera with music by Scott Bradley.

 The cartoon was animated by Kenneth Muse, Ed Barge, Irven Spence and Lewis Marshall, with backgrounds by Robert Gentle and layouts by Richard Bickenbach.

 It was produced in CinemaScope and released to theatres on January 27, 1956 by Metro-Goldwyn Mayer.
