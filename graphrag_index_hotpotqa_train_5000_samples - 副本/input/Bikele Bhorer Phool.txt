Bikele Bhorer Phool

Bikaler Bhorer Phool is an Indian Bengali series which airs on Zeebangla channel.

 It stars Sudipta Chakraborty and Amitabh Bhattacharya in the lead roles and Sneha Chatterjee in a pivotal role.

 The show is written and directed by Snehasish Chakraborty.

 It premiered on 27 February 2017 and airs at 9:00pm from Monday to Saturday.
