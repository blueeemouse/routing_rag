List of Ghost Adventures episodes

"Ghost Adventures" is an American television series about the paranormal created by Zak Bagans and Nick Groff, airing on the Travel Channel.

 The series follows ghost hunters Zak Bagans, Nick Groff (seasons 1-10), and Aaron Goodwin as they investigate locations that are reported to be haunted.

 The show is introduced and narrated by Bagans.
