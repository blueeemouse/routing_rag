Tiendesitas

Tiendesitas ("a collection of little stores") is a shopping complex located along the C-5 road corner Eulogio Rodriguez, Jr.

 Avenue in Ugong, Pasig.

 It began its operations on September 26, 2005 and was developed by Ortigas & Company Limited Partnership (OCLP).

 It rivals the highly successful and world-renowned Chatuchak Market of Bangkok.

 Constructed at a cost of P200 million, Tiendesitas is a part of Frontera Verde, an interim 18.5 hectare Ortigas Center development project.

 The complex offers more than 300 parking slots, and is served by jeepneys on C-5 and by buses which stop a short walk from the Tiendesitas gate.
