History of the Jews in Metro Detroit

As of 2012, about 116,000 Jewish Americans live in Metro Detroit.

 In 2001, about 96,000 Jewish Americans lived in Metro Detroit.

 That year, 75% of them lived in Oakland County.

 Many are in walking distances to their synagogues.

 In 2006 the Jews living in Windsor, Ontario, lived closer to Downtown Detroit than the Jewish communities within Metro Detroit.
