Grand Theft Auto: The Ballad of Gay Tony

Grand Theft Auto: The Ballad of Gay Tony is the second of the two episodic expansion packs available for the PlayStation 3, Microsoft Windows and Xbox 360 versions of "Grand Theft Auto IV", developed by Rockstar North.

 It was first released for the Xbox 360 on 29 October 2009, before then being released for the PlayStation 3 and Microsoft Windows on 13 April 2010.

 It is the fourth expansion pack in the "Grand Theft Auto" series and the 14th release on the series.
