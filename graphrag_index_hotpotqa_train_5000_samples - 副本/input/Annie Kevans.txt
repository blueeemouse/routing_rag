Annie Kevans

Annie Kevans (born Cannes, France) is an English artist who paints series of 'portraits' that explore sometimes controversial concepts and alternative histories.

 They are "portraits only in a loose sense... her works being a composite of existing images, research and imagination".

 Kevans has been described as "sensitive yet fearless, an artist who tackles controversial subjects head on".

 She was named number 19 in "Harper's Bazaar" magazine's "Forty Under 40" chart of hot new British talent, September 2007 and was named number 32 in "New Woman" magazine's "Brit Hit List" and was described as the "new Tracey Emin".
