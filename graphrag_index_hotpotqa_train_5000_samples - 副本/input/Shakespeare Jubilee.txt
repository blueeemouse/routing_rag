Shakespeare Jubilee

The Shakespeare Jubilee was staged in Stratford-upon-Avon between 6–8 September 1769.

 The jubilee was organised by the actor and theatre manager David Garrick to celebrate the Jubilee of the birth of William Shakespeare, although it was in 1616 when he died.

 It had a major impact on the rising tide of bardolatry that led to Shakespeare becoming established as the English national poet.

 Thomas Arne composed the song "Soft Flowing Avon" for the Jubilee.
