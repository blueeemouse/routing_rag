The Dreamer of Oz: The L. Frank Baum Story

The Dreamer of Oz: The L. Frank Baum Story is a 1990 made-for-television biographical film starring John Ritter as Lyman Frank Baum, the man who wrote "The Wonderful Wizard of Oz" and thirteen of the other Oz books.

 Also starring in this TV movie was Annette O'Toole as Baum's supportive wife, Maud, and Rue McClanahan as Baum's tough mother-in-law, Matilda Gage.

 John's son, Jason Ritter, makes his debut in this telefilm as Harry Neal Baum.

 At the time of the film's debut, O'Toole and Ritter also starred in "It", a TV miniseries where they played a couple.
