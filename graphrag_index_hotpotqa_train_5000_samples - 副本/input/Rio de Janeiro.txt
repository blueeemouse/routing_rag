Rio de Janeiro

Rio de Janeiro ( ; ] ; "River of January"), or simply Rio, is the second-most populous municipality in Brazil and the sixth-most populous in the Americas.

 The metropolis is anchor to the Rio de Janeiro metropolitan area, the second-most populous metropolitan area in Brazil and sixth-most populous in the Americas.

 Rio de Janeiro the capital of the state of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil's third-most populous state.

 Part of the city has been designated as a World Heritage Site, named ""Rio de Janeiro: Carioca Landscapes between the Mountain and the Sea"", by UNESCO on 1 July 2012 as a Cultural Landscape.
