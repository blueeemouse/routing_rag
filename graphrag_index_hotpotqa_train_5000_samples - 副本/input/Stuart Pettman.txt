Stuart Pettman

Stuart Pettman (born April 24, 1975) is an English professional snooker player.

 The Preston native has qualified for the World Championship three times, in 2003, 2004 and 2010.

 He beat 2005 champion Shaun Murphy in qualifying to reach the 2004 World Championships.

 He has spent 11 seasons on the professional snooker tour, with a highest ranking of 35 (2009/10 season).
