Konstantia Sofokleous

Konstantia (Dina) Sofokleous, "Greek": Κωνσταντία (Ντίνα) Σοφοκλέους is a Greek Cypriot artist.

 She has exhibited drawings and two animated movies, 'Popular Children's Poem' and 'Alice's Adventures in Wonderland' at the 51.

 Venice Biennale International Art Exhibition.

 She lives and works in Limassol, Cyprus.
