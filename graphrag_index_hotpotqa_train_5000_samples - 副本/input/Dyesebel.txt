Dyesebel

Dyesebel (] ) is a popular mermaid character in the Philippines.

 The comic book character was originally conceived by the Filipino comic book illustrator, Mars Ravelo and drawn by Elpidio Torres.

 "Dyesebel" is a prominent character in Philippine cinema and television.

 It was first serialized in Pilipino Komiks in 1952-53, and was later adapted into the big screen that same year.

 The film was produced under Manuel Vistan Productions, Inc. and released through Premiere Productions.

 It was directed by Gerardo 'Gerry' De Leon.
