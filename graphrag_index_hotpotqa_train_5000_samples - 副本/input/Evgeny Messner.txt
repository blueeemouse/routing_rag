Evgeny Messner

Evgeny Messner (Russian: Евгений Эдуардович Месснер , German: "Eugen Messner" ; 1891–1974) was a Russian professional soldier and military theorist.

 A Russian German, he became an officer of the Imperial Russian Army.

 During the Russian Civil War he sided with the White movement and fought against the Bolsheviks, notably as the last chief of staff of Kornilov Division of the Army of General Wrangel.
