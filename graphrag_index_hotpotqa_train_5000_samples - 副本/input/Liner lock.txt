Liner lock

The Linerlock is a locking mechanism for folding pocket knives.

 A Linerlock is a folding knife with a side-spring lock that can be opened and closed with one hand without repositioning the knife in the hand.

 The lock is self-adjusting for wear.

 The modern Linerlock traces its lineage to the late 19th century, but in the 1980s the design was improved by American custom knifemaker Michael Walker.
