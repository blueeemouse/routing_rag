Life Sentence (TV series)

Life Sentence (stylized as l;fe sentence) is an American comedy-drama series that will debut on The CW as a midseason entry during the 2017–18 television season that was created by Erin Cardillo & Richard Keith, who will executive produce with Oliver Goldstick, Bill Lawrence, Jeff Ingold, and director Lee Toland Krieger.

 The show is produced by In Good Company, Doozer Productions and Warner Bros.

 Television.

 The CW officially ordered "Life Sentence" to series on May 10, 2017.

 In August 2017, star Lucy Hale revealed that the series is set to premiere in January 2018.
