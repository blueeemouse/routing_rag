The Sunken Yacht

"The Sunken Yacht" is a 10-page funny animal comic book short story written, drawn, and lettered by Carl Barks.

 Characters in the story include Donald Duck, his nephews Huey, Dewey and Louie, Uncle Scrooge, an Artist, and a Boxer.

 The story was first published in "Walt Disney's Comics and Stories" #104 (May 1949).

 The story has been reprinted several times since.
