Maryland Route 124

Maryland Route 124 (MD 124) is a state highway in the U.S. state of Maryland.

 The highway runs 17.03 mi from MD 28 in Gaithersburg north to MD 108 in Damascus.

 MD 124 connects the central and northern Montgomery County communities of Gaithersburg, Montgomery Village, Redland, Laytonsville, and Damascus.

 The route is a major conduit on the western and northern sides of Gaithersburg, where the highway serves the National Institute of Standards and Technology and Lakeforest Mall and connects with Interstate 270 (I-270) and MD 355.

 MD 124 continues north past the Montgomery County Airpark, beyond which the route changes from a four- to six-lane divided highway to a two-lane road as it passes west of Laytonsville on its way to Damascus.
