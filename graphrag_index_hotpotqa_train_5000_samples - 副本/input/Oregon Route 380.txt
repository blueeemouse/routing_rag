Oregon Route 380

Oregon Route 380 (OR 380) is an Oregon state highway running from Prineville to Paulina.

 OR 380 is known as the Paulina Highway No. 380 (see Oregon highways and routes).

 It is 55.49 mi long and runs east–west, entirely within Crook County.

 OR 380 was assigned to the Paulina Highway in 2002.
