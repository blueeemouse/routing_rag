Varaždin Generalate

The Varaždin Generalate (German: "Warasdiner Generalat" , Croatian: "Varaždinski generalat" ), also known as the "Windische Grenze" ("Wendian/Wendish [Slavic] Border") in German, was a Habsburg Monarchy Military Frontier province centred in Warasdin (Varaždin), Kingdom of Croatia within Habsburg Monarchy, that existed between 1531 and the 18th century.
