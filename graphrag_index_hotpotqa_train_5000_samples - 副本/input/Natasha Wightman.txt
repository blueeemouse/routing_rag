Natasha Wightman

Natasha Wightman (b. 1973) is an actress who appeared in British and American films and British television productions from 1999 until 2005.

 Productions in which she has co-starred include "Gosford Park" (2001), "Revelation" (2001), "Shoreditch" (2003), "Mouth to Mouth" (2005), and "V for Vendetta" (2005).

 She has also appeared in several episodes of the British TV serial "State of Play".
