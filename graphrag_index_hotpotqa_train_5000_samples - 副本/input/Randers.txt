Randers

Randers (] ) is a city in Randers Municipality, Central Denmark Region on the Jutland peninsula.

 It is Denmark's sixth-largest city, with a population of 61,163 (1 January 2014).

 Randers is the municipality's main town and the site of its municipal council.

 The municipality is also a part of the East Jutland metropolitan area, which has 1.2 million inhabitants.
