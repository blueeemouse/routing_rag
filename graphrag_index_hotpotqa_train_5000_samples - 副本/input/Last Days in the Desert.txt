Last Days in the Desert

Last Days in the Desert is an American drama film about the temptation of Christ, directed and written by Rodrigo García.

 It stars Ewan McGregor, Tye Sheridan, Ciarán Hinds and Ayelet Zurer.

 The film premiered at the 2015 Sundance Film Festival on January 25, 2015.

 The limited release date was May 13, 2016.
