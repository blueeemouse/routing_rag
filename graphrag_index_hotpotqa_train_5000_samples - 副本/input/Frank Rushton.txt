Frank Rushton

Frank Rushton (21 April 1906 – 15 October 1975) was an English cricketer active from 1928 to 1929 who played for Lancashire.

 He was born and died in Bolton.

 He appeared in six first-class matches as a righthanded batsman who bowled right arm fast-medium pace.

 He scored 59 runs with a highest score of 28 and held two catches.

 He took ten wickets with a best analysis of four for 30.
