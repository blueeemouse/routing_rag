Cenap Samet

Cenap Samet a.k.a. Dzenap Samet, or Џенап Самет, (born 2 April 1980, Skopje, Macedonia) is Macedonian-Turkish actor.

 Samet has acted on the TV series "Elveda Rumeli" (Farewell Rumelia), "Balkan Düğünü" (The Balkan wedding), "Son Yaz - Balkanlar 1912" (Last Summer - Balkans 1912), "Büyük Sürgün Kafkasya" (Caucasian Exile) He also acts at the Turkish Theatre in Skopje.

 Samet has acted in more than 20 plays including: "La Dame de chez Maxim" (as Faik), "Storm" (as Trinculo – Stephano), "Noises Off" (as Loyd), "Poor Women" (as Apo), "Leonce and Lena" (as King Peter), "Romeo & Juliet" (as Capulet (member of all family)), "Waiting for Godot" (as Pozzo), "A Midsummer Night's Dream" (as Nick Bottom and Oberon)... and others.

 Also he had a role in the movie "A Misleaded" and some others
