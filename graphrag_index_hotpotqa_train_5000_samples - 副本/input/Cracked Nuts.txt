Cracked Nuts

Cracked Nuts is a 1931 American Pre-Code comedy film directed by Edward F. Cline, from an original screenplay written by Al Boasberg and Ralph Spence.

 The film stars the comedy duo Wheeler & Woolsey (Bert Wheeler and Robert Woolsey), as well as Dorothy Lee.

 It also features Boris Karloff in a small supporting role.

 The film was one of RKO's only financial successes of the year.
