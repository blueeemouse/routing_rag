Landon Arena

Landon Arena, better known as the Kansas Expocentre and formerly known as the Sunflower State Expocentre, is a 10,000-seat multi-purpose arena built in 1987 in Topeka, Kansas.

 It is currently home to the Topeka Roadrunners ice hockey team.

 Previously, Topeka Sizzlers of the Continental Basketball Association, Topeka Tarantulas and Topeka ScareCrows ice hockey teams played there.

 Many other shows, including concerts, perform here.
