2014 Liberty Bowl

The 2014 Liberty Bowl was an American college football bowl game played on December 29, 2014 at Liberty Bowl Memorial Stadium in Memphis, Tennessee.

 The 56th edition of the Liberty Bowl, it featured the Texas A&M Aggies of the Southeastern Conference and the West Virginia Mountaineers of the Big 12 Conference.

 It began at 1:00 p.m. CST and aired on ESPN.

 It was one of the 2014–15 bowl games that concludes the 2014 FBS football season.

 Sponsored by automobile parts and accessories store AutoZone, it is officially known as the AutoZone Liberty Bowl.
