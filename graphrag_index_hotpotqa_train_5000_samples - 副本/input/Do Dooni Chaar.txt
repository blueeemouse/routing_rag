Do Dooni Chaar

Do Dooni Chaar (English: "Two Twos Are Four" ) is a 2010 Indian Hindi comedy-drama film produced by Arindam Chaudhuri (Planman Motion Pictures), directed by Habib Faisal, and stars Rishi Kapoor, Neetu Singh, Aditi Vasudev and Archit Krishna in lead roles.

 The film is about a middle-class school teacher who tries to keep his wife and children happy in inflationary times and dreams of buying a car.

 The movie also marks the return of the Kapoor pair as a lead couple on the silver screen.

 Although the duo has not acted in a film in over 30 years, they had previously acted in numerous hits during the 1970s.

 The directorial debut film was also the first live action Hindi film to be distributed by Disney World Cinema.

 The film was declared a hit by Box Office India.
