Province of Catania

The Province of Catania (Italian: "Provincia di Catania" ; Sicilian: "Pruvincia di Catania" ) was a province in the autonomous island region of Sicily in southern Italy.

 Its capital was the city of Catania.

 It was replaced by the Metropolitan City of Catania starting from 4 August 2015.

 The province had a total population of 1,054,778.
