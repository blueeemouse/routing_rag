Jacques Rozier

Jacques Rozier (] ; born 1926 in Paris) is a French film director and screenwriter.

 He is one of the lesser known members of the French New Wave movement and has collaborated with Jean-Luc Godard.

 Three of his films have been screened at the Cannes Film Festival.

 In 1978, he was a member of the jury at the 28th Berlin International Film Festival.
