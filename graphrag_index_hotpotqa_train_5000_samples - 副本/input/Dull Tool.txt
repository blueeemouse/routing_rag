Dull Tool

"Dull Tool" is a song by American singer-songwriter Fiona Apple, released on the soundtrack to the comedy film "This Is 40" (2012).

 It is the first track Apple wrote solely for a film.

 "Dull Tool" was produced by Apple's long-time collaborator Jon Brion, and leaked online in November 2012, several weeks before the soundtrack hit stores on December 11.

 Apple recorded a second song for "This Is 40", though this was not included in the film nor its soundtrack.
