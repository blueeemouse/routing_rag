Periodical literature

Periodical literature (also called a periodical publication or simply a periodical) is a published work that appears in a new edition on a regular schedule.

 The most familiar example is the magazine, typically published weekly, monthly, or as a quarterly.

 Newspapers, often published daily or weekly, are, strictly speaking, serials, not periodicals.

 Other examples of periodicals are newsletters, literary magazines (literary journals), academic journals (including scientific journals), science magazines, and yearbooks.
