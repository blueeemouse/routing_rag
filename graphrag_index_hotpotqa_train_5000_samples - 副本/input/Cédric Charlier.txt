Cédric Charlier

Cédric Charlier (born November 27, 1987) is a Belgian international field hockey player.

 At the 2008, 2012 and 2016 Summer Olympics, he competed for the national team in the men's tournament.

 At the 2016 Olympics, he was part of the Belgian men's team that won the silver medal.

 Charlier also won silver with Belgium at the 2013 European Championship on home ground in Boom.
