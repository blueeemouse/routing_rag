Piggy Bank (song)

"Piggy Bank" is the fifth track from 50 Cent's second album, "The Massacre".

 It was not released as a single, but charted at eighty-eight on the "Billboard" Hot 100 due to controversy over its attack on long-time rival Ja Rule, as well as Jadakiss and Fat Joe, who had worked with Ja Rule on his song "New York".

 The song also takes lyrical swipes at rappers Shyne, Kelis, Sheek Louch, Cassidy, Lil' Kim (who featured 50 Cent on her song "Magic Stick") and Nas.

 He also mentions Tupac Shakur, Jay-Z, Mobb Deep and Michael Jackson in the song, but not negatively.
