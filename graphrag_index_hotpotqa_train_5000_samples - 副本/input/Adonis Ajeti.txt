Adonis Ajeti

Adonis Afrim Ajeti (born 26 February 1997) is a Swiss professional footballer of Albanian descent who plays as a defender for Swiss club FC St. Gallen.

 He is the twin brother of Albian Ajeti who plays for German club Augsburg and the younger brother of Arlind Ajeti who plays for Italian Serie A club Torino.

 Adonis hold dual citizenship, the Swiss where he was born and grown up and the Albanian citizenship due to his parents origin.
