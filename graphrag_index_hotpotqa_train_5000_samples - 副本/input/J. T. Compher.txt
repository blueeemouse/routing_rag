J. T. Compher

Joseph Taylor Compher (born April 8, 1995) is an American professional ice hockey player, who is currently playing with the Colorado Avalanche organization of the National Hockey League (NHL).

 He previously played college ice hockey and was captain for the Michigan Wolverines of the Big Ten Conference.

 Compher was originally drafted 35th overall by the Buffalo Sabres in the 2013 NHL Entry Draft.
