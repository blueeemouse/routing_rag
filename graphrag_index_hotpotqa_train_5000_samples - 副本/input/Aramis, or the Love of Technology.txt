Aramis, or the Love of Technology

Aramis, or the Love of Technology, was written by French sociologist/anthropologist Bruno Latour.

 "Aramis" was originally published in French in 1993; the English translation by Catherine Porter, copyrighted in 1996, ISBN  , is now in its fourth edition (2002).

 Latour describes his text as "scientifiction," which he describes as "a hybrid genre... for a hybrid task" (p. ix).

 The genre includes voices of a young engineer discussing his "sociotechnological initiation," his professor's commentary which introduces Actor-network theory (ANT), field documents - including real-life interviews, and the voice of Aramis—a failed technology ( p. x).
