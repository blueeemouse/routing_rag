Hôtel de Crillon

The Hôtel de Crillon in Paris is a historic luxury hotel that opened in 1909 — in a building dating to 1758.

 Located at the foot of the Champs-Élysées, the Crillon along with the Hotel de la Marine is one of two identical stone palaces on the Place de la Concorde.

 With 124 guest rooms and 46 suites, ranging in price from USD $1,350 to USD $15,000 per night.

 the facility also features three restaurants, a bar, outdoor terrace, gym and health club on the premises.
