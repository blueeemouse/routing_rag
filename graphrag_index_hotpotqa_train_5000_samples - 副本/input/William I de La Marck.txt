William I de La Marck

William de la Marck (1446–1485) was an adventurer of German extraction.

 He became an important character in the late 15th century in the Prince-Bishopric of Liège.

 William's was nicknamed "Le Sanglier des Ardennes" (The Wild Boar of the Ardennes)— because he was as fierce as the wild boar which he delighted to hunt.
