1971 Rothmans International Tennis Tournament

The 1971 Rothmans International Tennis Tournament was a men's professional tennis tournament held on indoor carpet courts in the Royal Albert Hall in London, England.

 It was the second edition of the tournament and was held from 28 February through 4 March 1971.

 It was an independent event, i.e. not part of either the 1971Grand Prix or 1971 World Championship Tennis circuit.

 Rod Laver won the singles title.
