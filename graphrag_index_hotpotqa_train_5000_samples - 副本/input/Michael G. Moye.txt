Michael G. Moye

Michael George Moye (born August 11, 1954) is an American photographer and a former television writer and producer.

 In his television career he has written for shows such as "The Jeffersons", "Diff'rent Strokes", "Good Times", and "227", and produced "It's Your Move" and "The Jeffersons".

 His best-known work is likely the 1987 series "Married... with Children", created with Ron Leavitt for the fledgling Fox network.
