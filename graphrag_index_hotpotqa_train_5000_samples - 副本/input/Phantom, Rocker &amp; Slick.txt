Phantom, Rocker &amp; Slick

Phantom, Rocker & Slick was an American rock band active in the mid-1980s.

 The bandmembers were drummer Slim Jim Phantom, bassist Lee Rocker, and guitarist Earl Slick.

 Phantom and Rocker had previously played together as members of the Stray Cats.

 They released two albums, "Phantom, Rocker & Slick" and "Cover Girl", on EMI Records before disbanding.
