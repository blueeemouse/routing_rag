Tom Ricketts

Thomas B. Ricketts (January 15, 1853 – January 19, 1939), often credited as Tom Ricketts, was an English stage and film actor and director who was a pioneer in the film industry.

 He portrayed Ebenezer Scrooge in the first American film adaptation of "A Christmas Carol" (1908), and directed one of the first motion pictures ever made in Hollywood.

 After directing scores of silent films, including the first film to be released by Universal Pictures, Ricketts became a prominent character actor.
