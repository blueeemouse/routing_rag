Pork Pie (film)

Pork Pie is a 2017 comedy road movie written and directed by New Zealander Matt Murphy and produced by Tom Hern.

 The film is a remake of 1981 movie "Goodbye Pork Pie", the first New Zealand film to win a substantial local audience.

 The remake stars Dean O'Gorman, James Rolleston and Ashleigh Cummings as a trio of accidental outlaws who travel the length of New Zealand in a stolen yellow New Mini.

 The film was scored by Jonathan Crayford
