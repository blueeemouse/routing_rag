Prince Ali (song)

"Prince Ali" and its reprise are two musical numbers from the 1992 Disney animated film "Aladdin".

 The first part was performed by Robin Williams in his role as the Genie and the reprise is performed by Jonathan Freeman in his role as Jafar.

 The song performed by Williams was nominated for the Golden Globe Award for Best Original Song at the 50th Golden Globe Awards in 1993.
