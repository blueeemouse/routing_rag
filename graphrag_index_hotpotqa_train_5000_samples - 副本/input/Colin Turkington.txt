Colin Turkington

Colin Henry Turkington (born 21 March 1982) is a Northern Irish auto racing driver, who is currently competing in the British Touring Car Championship for Team BMW WSR in a BMW 125i M Sport .

 His most notable successes to date include becoming 2009 British Touring Car Champion driving for Team RAC and 2014 British Touring Car Champion driving for eBay Motors.

 He has also competed in the World Touring Car Championship and the inaugural Scandinavian Touring Car Championship.

 In 2013 he returned to the British Touring Car Championship with his current team, West Surrey Racing.
