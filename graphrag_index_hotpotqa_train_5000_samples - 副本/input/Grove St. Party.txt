Grove St. Party

"Grove St. Party" is the fourth single by American rapper Waka Flocka Flame, which was released February 15, 2011 under the record label 1017 Brick Squad Records.

 It features rapper Kebo Gotti and is the fourth single from his debut album "Flockaveli", which was released on October 5, 2010.

 The song was produced by Lex Luger.
