Lincoln Motor Car Works

Lincoln Motor Car Works was an automobile company in Chicago, Illinois.

 It produced cars for Sears Roebuck from 1908 until 1912.

 Nine models were offered, priced between US$325 and $475, with the Model L advertised at $495 complete.

 They were sold by mail, out of the Sears catalog.

 Sears had a very lenient return policy: cars were sold on a ten-day trial basis.
