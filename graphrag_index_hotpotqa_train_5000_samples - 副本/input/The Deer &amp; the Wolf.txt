The Deer &amp; the Wolf

"The Deer & the Wolf" is a song by English recording artist Sophie Ellis-Bextor from her fifth studio album "Wanderlust" (2014).

 The song was released as the fourth and final single of the album on 25 August 2014.

 It was co-written by Ed Harcourt and Ellis-Bextor; whilst production was handled by the former.

 The music video for the song was directed by Harry Cauty of Kode Media, and features Ellis-Bextor performing the song in a forest set with her band.
