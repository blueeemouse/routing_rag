Stay Together (Suede song)

"Stay Together" is a non-album single by Suede, released on 14 February 1994 on Nude Records.

 It is the last single released while guitarist Bernard Butler was in the band, though subsequent singles from "Dog Man Star" feature his music.

 Although lead singer Brett Anderson considers the single and the video that accompanies it the worst the band has released.

 It is tied with "Trash" as the highest charting single the band has released, charting at number three on the UK Singles Chart.

 The song also charted in Ireland, peaking at no. 18.

 The single was released in the US on 26 April as a six song EP, and was the first release by the band as The London Suede.

 This followed from the successful lawsuit of Suzanne deBronkart, who had been performing and recording in the US under the name Suede.
