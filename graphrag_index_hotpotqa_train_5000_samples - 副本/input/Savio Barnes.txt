Savio Barnes

Savio Barnes (Hindi: सवियो बार्न्स) is a popular Bollywood Dance choreographer, actor and dancer.

 He currently resides in Mumbai, Maharashtra.

 He came to fame after choreographing many dances for Jhalak Dikhhla Jaa, a dance-competition.

 Currently Savio is judging the Marathi dance reality show Maharashtracha Dancing Superstar - Chhotte Masters.
