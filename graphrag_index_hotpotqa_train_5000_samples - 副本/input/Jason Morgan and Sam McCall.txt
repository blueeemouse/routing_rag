Jason Morgan and Sam McCall

Jason and Sam Morgan (commonly known by their portmanteau "JaSam") are fictional characters and a supercouple from the ABC daytime drama, "General Hospital."

 They are known to be one of daytime's most popular supercouples.

<ref name="Soap Opera Weekly 03/01/05"> </ref><ref name="Soaps In Depth 09/27/05"> </ref> Jason was originally portrayed by actor Steve Burton; currently, the role is being played by Billy Miller.

 Sam is portrayed by Kelly Monaco.
