Tim Malchak

Tim Malchak (born June 25, 1957 in Binghamton, New York) is an American country music singer-songwriter.

 Malchak partnered with Dwight Rucker in 1982 to form the country music duo Malchak & Rucker.

 Together, they became the first black/white duo in country music history with a charting single when "Just Like That" debuted on the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles & Tracks chart in 1984.
