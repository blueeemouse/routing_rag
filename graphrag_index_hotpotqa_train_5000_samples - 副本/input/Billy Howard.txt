Billy Howard

Billy Howard is an English comedy impressionist, who appeared on the ITV series "Who Do You Do", alongside other impressionists such as Faith Brown.

 In 1976, his single, "King Of The Cops", a send-up of the hit "King of the Road", reached #6 in the UK Singles Chart; it featured his impressions of TV cops including Kojak, Columbo, Steve McGarrett in Hawaii Five-O, McCloud, Ironside and Cannon.

 A follow-up was also released: "The Disco Cops".
