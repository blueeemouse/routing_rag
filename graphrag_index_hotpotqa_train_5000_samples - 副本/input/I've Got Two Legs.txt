I've Got Two Legs

"I've Got Two Legs" is a song by the British comedy troupe Monty Python that was composed by Terry Gilliam.

 It most prominently appears in the concert film "Monty Python Live at the Hollywood Bowl", the concert LP "Monty Python Live at Drury Lane" and the album "Monty Python Sings".

 It also appeared at the 2014 reunion shows in O2 Arena titled "Monty Python Live".
