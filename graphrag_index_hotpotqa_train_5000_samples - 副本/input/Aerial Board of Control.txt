Aerial Board of Control

The Aerial Board of Control is a fictional supranational organization world to manage air traffic for the whole world.

 It was described in the early science fiction stories of Rudyard Kipling, "With The Night Mail" ("The Windsor Magazine", December 1905; "McClure's Magazine", November 1905) and "As Easy as ABC" ("The London Magazine" 1912).

 The organisation was able to limit the influence of national states and create a de facto world government.
