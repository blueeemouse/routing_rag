Nõiduse õpilane

Nõiduse õpilane (English: Sorcerer's Apprentice / Sorcerer's Disciple / Student of Witchcraft ) is a novel by Estonian author Karl Ristikivi.

 It was first published in 1967 in Lund, Sweden by Eesti Kirjanike Kooperatiiv ("Estonian Writers' Cooperative").

 In Estonia it was published in 1994.
