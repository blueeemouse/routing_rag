Le Beau Serge

Le Beau Serge (] , meaning "Handsome Serge") is a French film directed by Claude Chabrol, released in 1958.

 It has been cited as the first product of the Nouvelle Vague, or French New Wave, film movement.

 The film is often compared with Chabrol's subsequent film "Les Cousins", which also features Jean-Claude Brialy and Gérard Blain.
