Gong Yoo

Gong Ji-chul (; born July 10, 1979), better known by his stage name Gong Yoo (), is a South Korean actor.

 He is best known for his roles in television dramas "The 1st Shop of Coffee Prince" (2007) and "" (2016-2017), and the films "Silenced" (2011), "The Age of Shadows" (2016), and "Train to Busan" (2016).
