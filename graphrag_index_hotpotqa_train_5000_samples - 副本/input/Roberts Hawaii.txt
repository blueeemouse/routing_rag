Roberts Hawaii

Roberts Hawaii Tours and Transportation is a tour bus operator in the state of Hawaii founded in 1941 by Robert Iwamoto Sr. as a one-man taxi company in Hanapepe, Kauai.

 It has operations on 4 of Hawaii's major Islands: Kauaʻ i, Oʻ ahu, Maui, and the Island of Hawaiʻ i.

 It has an employee-owned company with 900 vehicles and 1,400 employees.
