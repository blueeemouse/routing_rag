Kaito Kuroba

Kaito Kuroba (Japanese: 黒羽 快斗 , Hepburn: Kuroba Kaito ) , the true identity of the gentleman thief "Kaito Kid" (怪盗キッド , Kaitō Kiddo , "Phantom Thief Kid") , is a fictional character and protagonist of the "Magic Kaito" manga series created by Gosho Aoyama.

 His father Toichi Kuroba was the original Kaito Kid before being killed by an unknown organization, while his mother was a former phantom thief known as the Phantom Lady.

 Kaito Kuroba then takes on the role of Kid after learning the organization is after a gemstone called Pandora and decides to find and destroy it.
