Rue du Sergent Blandan

The Rue du Sergent Blandan is one of the oldest streets of Lyon.

 It connects Saint Vincent and the slopes of the Croix-Rousse quarters, in the 1st arrondissement of Lyon.

 The street starts rue Pareille, runs along the Place Sathonay, crosses the rue Hippolyte Flandrin, the rue Louis Vitet and the rue du Terme, and becomes the rue des Capucins just after the square of the same name.

 The street belongs to the zone classified World Heritage Site by UNESCO.

 It is named in honour of Sergent Blandan (Jean Pierre Hippolyte Blandan), who participated in the conquest of Algeria.
