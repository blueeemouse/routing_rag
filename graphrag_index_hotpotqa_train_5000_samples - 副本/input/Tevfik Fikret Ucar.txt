Tevfik Fikret Ucar

Prof. Dr. Tevfik Fikret Ucar (born 28 January 1966) is a full-time Professor, chairman of the Graphic Design Department and design consultant Anadolu University of Fine Arts and Design.

 In addition, design director of Anadolu University School of Distance Education textbook publication.
