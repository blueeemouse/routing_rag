All Grown Up!

 is an American animated television series created by Arlene Klasky and Gábor Csupó for Nickelodeon.

 It is a spinoff of "Rugrats" and takes place about ten years after the original series where the characters are now pre-teen to teenage.

 Tommy, Dil, Chuckie, Phil, Lil, Kimi, Angelica and Susie now have to deal with teenage and pre-teen issues and situations.
