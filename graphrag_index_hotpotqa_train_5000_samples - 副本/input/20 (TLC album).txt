20 (TLC album)

20 is a compilation album by American girl group TLC.

 It was released by Epic Records on October 15, 2013, marking both the group's 20-plus year legacy in entertainment business and the release of their biographical VH1 original movie, "", which largely inspired the track listing for "20".

 Many of their hits are featured, included their four number-one singles: "Creep", "Waterfalls", "No Scrubs", and "Unpretty", plus a new track written by singer Ne-Yo, "Meant to Be".

 Most of the tracks are shorter edits of their original album versions, like on their previous compilation album "", and "No Scrubs" is featured in its video version with an accompanying rap of Left Eye.
