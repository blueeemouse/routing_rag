Paul Harris (film critic)

Paul Harris, born in 1950 in Melbourne and educated at Assumption College, Kilmore is a film critic, who appears on radio stations 3RRR and 3AW.

 Known for his vast knowledge of film, music and theatre, and for his elaborate puns and impressions, he has fronted the radio show "Filmbuff's Forecast" since 1982.

 For the first ten years he co-anchored with John Flaus to produce what became a memorable double act.

 He has directed the St Kilda Short Film Festival since 1999.
