Falcatifolium

Falcatifolium is a genus of conifers belonging to the podocarp family Podocarpaceae.

 The genus includes evergreen dioecious shrubs and large trees of up to 36 m .

 Five species are presently recognized.

 The genus was first described by de Laubenfels in 1969, and is composed of species formerly classified in genus "Dacrydium".
