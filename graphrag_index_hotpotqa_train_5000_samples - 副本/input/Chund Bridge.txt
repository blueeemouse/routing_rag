Chund Bridge

Chund Bridge (locally known as Chund Pull) (Urdu: چنڈ پل‎ ) is a bridge over the Chenab river.

 It is one of the oldest bridges in Pakistan.

 It is located near Chund Bharwana, Jhang District.

 Bridge has 750 meters length.

 Its name is after the nearest town "Chund Bharwana".

 It is 14 km from Jhang.
