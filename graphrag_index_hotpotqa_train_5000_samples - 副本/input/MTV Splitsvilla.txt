MTV Splitsvilla

MTV Splitsvilla is an Indian television reality show that airs on MTV India.

The show is presented by Rannvijay Singh and Sunny Leone.

The show is running on its 10th Season (Catch Your Match).

The first premiere of Splitsvilla X was telecast on 23 July 2017 at 7:00 PM IST, followed by every weekend on Sunday.The series is loosely based on the American dating reality show "Flavor of Love".
