Gangnam Blues

Gangnam Blues (; lit.

 Gangnam 1970) is a 2015 South Korean noir action film written and directed by Yoo Ha, and starring Lee Min-ho and Kim Rae-won.

 The film is set in the 1970s against the backdrop of the real estate development of Seoul's Gangnam district amidst socio-political turmoil and terrorism.

 The friendship of two childhood friends is tested as they find themselves entangled in the collusion and battles between political powers and criminal organizations.
