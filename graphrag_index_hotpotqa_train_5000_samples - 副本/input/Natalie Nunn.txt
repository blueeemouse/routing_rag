Natalie Nunn

Natalie Tynika Nunn (born December 26, 1984) is an American reality television personality best known for being a castmate on the fourth season of the "Bad Girls Club" in 2009-10.

 She subsequently appeared on "Bad Girls Club" season 13 as well as "Hair Battle Spectacular".
