Barbara Bestor

Barbara Bestor is an American architect based in Los Angeles, California.

 She is the principal of Bestor Architecture, founded in 1992.

 Examples of her work include the Beats Electronics Headquarters in Culver City, the Nasty Gal Headquarters in downtown Los Angeles, Intelligentsia Coffee & Tea in Los Angeles, and the Toro Canyon House in Santa Barbara.

 In 2015 she married director Tom Stern.

 In 2017 she was elected to the AIA's College of Fellows.
