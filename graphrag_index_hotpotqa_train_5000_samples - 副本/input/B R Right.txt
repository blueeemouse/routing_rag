B R Right

"B R Right" is a song recorded by American rapper Trina for her second studio album, "Diamond Princess" (2002).

 It features American rapper Ludacris.

 The song was released as the third single from the album.

 the song peaked at a moderately successful peak position of number 83 on the "Billboard" Hot 100.

 But on the U.S. Hot Rap Tracks was at Top 30 hit.
