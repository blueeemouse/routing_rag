Satoshi Kojima

Satoshi Kojima (小島 聡 , Kojima Satoshi ) is a Japanese professional wrestler, currently signed to New Japan Pro Wrestling.

 As a singles wrestler, he was the first wrestler to hold NJPW's IWGP Heavyweight Championship and All Japan Pro Wrestling's Triple Crown Championship simultaneously, and one of three wrestlers to hold the IWGP Heavyweight Championship, Triple Crown Championship and NWA World Heavyweight Championship (the other two being Keiji Mutoh and Shinya Hashimoto).

 As a team, he and Hiroyoshi Tenzan are six-time IWGP Tag Team Champions and became the first team to win G1 Tag League and World's Strongest Tag Determination League on the same year.

 He and Tenzan are also former National Wrestling Alliance (NWA) World Tag Team Champions.

 Between the NWA, AJPW, and NJPW, Kojima has held 19 total championships.
