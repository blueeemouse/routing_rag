Tsinghua University School of Law

Tsinghua University School of Law is the law school of Tsinghua University founded in 1929.

 It is a part of Tsinghua University.

 The restructuring of the higher education system in China led to the Law School’s merger with Peking University and other universities in 1952.

 It was reestablished in 1995 and has operated continuously since.

 As of 2016, it is ranked 2nd among law schools in China.

 Notable alumni include Xi Jinping, the current General Secretary of the Communist Party of China, as well as former General Secretary Hu Jintao.
