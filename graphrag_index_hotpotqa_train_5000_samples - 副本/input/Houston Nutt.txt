Houston Nutt

Houston Dale Nutt Jr. (born October 14, 1957) is a former American football coach and former player.

 He currently works for CBS Sports as a college football studio analyst.

 Previously, he served as the head football coach at Murray State University (1993–1996), Boise State University (1997), the University of Arkansas (1998–2007), and University of Mississippi (2008–2011).

 Nutt's all-time career winning percentage is just under 59 percent.
