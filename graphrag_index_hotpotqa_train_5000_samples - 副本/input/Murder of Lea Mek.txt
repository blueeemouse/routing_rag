Murder of Lea Mek

Lea Mek (  1975 – December 3, 1993) was a Cambodian refugee living in the United States who was a member of the Asian Boyz street gang.

 On December 3, 1993, Mek was murdered in a gang shooting by the Wah Ching gang, at a pool hall in El Monte, California.

 The murder was caught on camera by four surveillance cameras installed within the hall.

 After the murder, the Asian Boyz declared war on the Wah Ching.

 As a result, it influenced a number of other gang murders and shootings in the LA area, and created a strong feud between both Asian gangs.

 Authorities claim the murder inspired a chain reaction of gang violence which led to ten other murders in the LA area.
