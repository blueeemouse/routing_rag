Keep Running (TV series)

Keep Running (), previously known as Running Man () before 2017, is a Chinese variety show broadcast on ZRTG: Zhejiang Television.

 It is a spin-off from the popular original South Korean variety show "Running Man" by SBS.

 It was first aired on 10 October 2014.

 The show is classified as a game-variety show, where the MCs and guests complete missions at a landmark to win a race.
