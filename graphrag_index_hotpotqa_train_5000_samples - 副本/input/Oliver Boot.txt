Oliver Boot

Oliver Boot (born 1979) is an English actor.

 He trained at the RADA, and has appeared on both stage and screen.

 His theatre credits include "Antony and Cleopatra", "In Extremis" (in the role of Abelard), "Three Musketeers", "Hayfever", "Tartuffe", "Jamaica Inn" and an award winning world tour of "Othello" with Cheek by Jowl.

 He has starred as Demetrius in Shakespeare's "Midsummer's night dream" and as Ventidius in "Timon of Athens", at the Globe, in London.
