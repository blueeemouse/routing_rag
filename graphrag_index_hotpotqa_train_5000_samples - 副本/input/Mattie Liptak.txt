Mattie Liptak

Mattie Liptak (born April 10, 1996) is an American actor best known for his role as George in the horror film, "", a sequel to the 2008 film, "Quarantine".

 He was born and raised in Walker, Louisiana to Brenda Tedder and Robert Liptak.

 Mattie graduated from Walker High School, in Walker, Louisiana in May 2014.
