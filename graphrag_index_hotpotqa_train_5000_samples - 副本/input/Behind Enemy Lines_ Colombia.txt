Behind Enemy Lines: Colombia

Behind Enemy Lines: Colombia is a 2009 American war film directed by Tim Matheson and starring Joe Manganiello, former WWE and TNA wrestler Ken Anderson, Keith David and Tim Matheson.

 It is the third installment in the series, as well as the sequel to "Behind Enemy Lines", and "".

 The film was co-produced by WWE Studios, written by Tobias Iaconis and released direct-to-video on January 6, 2009.
