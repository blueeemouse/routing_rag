Daniel Levy (political analyst)

Daniel Levy is an Israeli political scientist, policy advisor and diplomat.

 He is the current president of the U.S./Middle East Project.

From 2012 to 2016, he was Director for the Middle East and North Africa at the European Council on Foreign Relations.

 Prior to that he was a senior Fellow and Director of the New America Foundation’s Middle East Taskforce in Washington DC and a Senior Fellow at The Century Foundation.

 Earlier he worked as an analyst for the International Crisis Group’s Middle East Program.
