Extended Versions (10,000 Maniacs album)

Extended Versions is a live album by 10,000 Maniacs released in 2009 as a part of the Extended Versions collection of discounted albums.

 The album was recorded during the band's 2006 tour with Oskar Saville as lead vocalist.

 By the time of the album's release, Saville had been replaced as lead vocalist by the former member Mary Ramsey who is pictured on the cover and listed as a member of the band.
