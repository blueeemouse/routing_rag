WFTU (AM)

WFTU (1570 AM) is a college radio station licensed to Riverhead, New York and is owned and operated by Five Towns College in Dix Hills, New York on Long Island.

 It broadcasts a Variety format featuring programming produced by current students and faculty of Five Towns College.

 The station is also operated by a student management staff under the guidance of the general manager, a staff professor.

 The studio is located on the campus in Dix Hills and transmitter is in Riverhead, New York.

 The station also streams on the internet from its website.
