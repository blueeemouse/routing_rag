Big Dreams &amp; High Hopes

Big Dreams & High Hopes is the eighth studio album by American country music singer Jack Ingram.

 It was released on August 25, 2009 via Big Machine Records as his third release for the label.

 The album includes the singles "That's a Man" (a Top 20 hit) and "Barefoot and Crazy," which is Ingram's first Top 10 hit on the U.S. "Billboard" Hot Country Songs charts since 2005's "Wherever You Are".
