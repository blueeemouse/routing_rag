Dan Laustsen

Dan Laustsen (born June 15, 1954 in Aalborg, Denmark) is a Danish cinematographer.

 He graduated from the Danish National Film School in 1979, and is a member of the Danish Society of Cinematographers.

 His career spans four decades and he has been nominated and won many prestigious awards, chiefly the Robert Awards.

 Laustsen is best known for his work on the films "Silent Hill", "The League of Extraordinary Gentlemen", and "Crimson Peak," where the visual side of "The League of Extraordinary Gentlemen" was hailed as a "visual treat" in Variety.

 Dan Laustsen has repeatedly collaborated with director "Guillermo Del Toro," notably on the films "Mimic" and "Crimson Peak".
