Umbrella Ultra Marathon

The Umbrella Ultra Marathon, or Umbrellaultra, was a 102 km distance running event held on 29 October 2014.

 It was not a competitive race, but a running event to support the Umbrella Movement of Hong Kong.

 The course covered New Territories, Kowloon and Hong Kong Island, which shows an umbrella shape on the map.

 Thus the trail is also known as the Umbrella Trail.
