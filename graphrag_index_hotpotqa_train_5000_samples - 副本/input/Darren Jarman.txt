Darren Jarman

Darren Jarman (born 28 January 1967) is a former Australian rules footballer who played in the Australian Football League.

 An elite midfielder-come-forward, Jarman amassed a total of 386 games of senior football at AFL, SANFL and representative level.

 He was widely recognised as one of the most skillful players of his era.

 He is the younger brother of Andrew.
