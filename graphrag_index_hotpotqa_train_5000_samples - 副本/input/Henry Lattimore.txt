Henry Lattimore

Henry C. "Hank" Lattimore (February 25, 1934 – May 18, 2010) was an American football coach.

 He was the 10th head football coach at Morgan State University, coaching the Bears during the 1976 and 1977 seasons.

 Lattimore was also the 15th head coach for the North Carolina Central University Eagles located in Durham, North Carolina and he held that position for twelve seasons, from 1979 until 1990.

 His coaching record at North Carolina Central was 72–53–3.
