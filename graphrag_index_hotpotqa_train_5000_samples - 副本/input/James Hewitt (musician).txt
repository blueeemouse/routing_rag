James Hewitt (musician)

James Hewitt (June 4, 1770 – August 2, 1827) was an American conductor, composer and music publisher.

 Born in Dartmoor, England, he was known to have lived in London in 1791 and early 1792, but went to New York City in September of that year.

 He stayed in New York until 1811, conducting a theater orchestra and composing and arranging music for local ballad operas and musical events.

 He also gave lessons and sold musical instruments and publications in his “musical repository”.
