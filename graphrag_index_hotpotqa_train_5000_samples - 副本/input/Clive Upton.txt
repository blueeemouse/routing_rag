Clive Upton

Clive Upton is a professor of English language at the University of Leeds, England, specializing in dialectology and sociolinguistics.

 He has acted as a consultant on British pronunciation for the English-language dictionaries published by Oxford University Press, including the "Oxford English Dictionary", the "Shorter Oxford English Dictionary", and the "Concise Oxford Dictionary".

 He was also responsible for the British element of the "Oxford Dictionary of Pronunciation for Current English" (2001).
