2013 Ford EcoBoost 400

The 2013 Ford EcoBoost 400 was a NASCAR Sprint Cup Series stock car race that was held on November 17, 2013, at Homestead Miami Speedway in Homestead, Florida.

 Contested over 267 laps, it was the thirty-sixth and final race in the 2013 NASCAR Sprint Cup Series, as well as the final race in the ten-race Chase for the Sprint Cup, which ends the season.

 Denny Hamlin won the race.

 Matt Kenseth finished second while Dale Earnhardt, Jr., Martin Truex Jr. and Clint Bowyer rounded out the top five.

 Jimmie Johnson finished ninth to take his sixth Sprint Cup title.
