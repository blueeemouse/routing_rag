Liturgical Battle

The Liturgical Battle (Swedish: "Liturgiska striden") was the name for the period from 1574 until 1593 in Sweden, when there was a struggle about the confession of faith and liturgy of the Church of Sweden, brought about by the attempts of King John III of Sweden to make the Swedish church take a position in between Catholicism and Protestantism.

 The battle began in 1574, when the king introduced some new rules in the liturgy which was not in accordance with Protestant opinion, followed by his publication of the "Liturgia Svecanæ Ecclesiæ catholicæ & orthodoxæ conformia" commonly called the "Red Book", which re-introduced a number of Catholic customs.

 The Liturgical Battle ended with the Protestant confession of faith at the Uppsala Synod in 1593.
