3000 Nights

3000 Nights is a 2015 internationally co-produced drama film directed by Mai Masri.

 It was screened in the Contemporary World Cinema section of the 2015 Toronto International Film Festival.

 The film focuses on a Palestinian woman, who whilst in jail, gives birth to a son.

 It was selected as the Jordanian entry for the Best Foreign Language Film at the 89th Academy Awards but it was not nominated.
