Van A. Harvey

Van A. Harvey is George Edwin Burnell Professor of Religious Studies (Emeritus) at Stanford University.

 Born in Hankow, China, he served in the U.S. Navy (1943–46), and was awarded a BA in Philosophy from Occidental College (1948, Phi Beta Kappa).

 After attending Princeton Theological Seminary for one year, he received a B.D. from Yale Divinity School in 1951 and a PhD.

 from Yale University in 1957 in post-Enlightenment religious thought.

 His thesis was entitled "Myth, Faith, and History" and his thesis supervisor was H. Richard Niebuhr.
