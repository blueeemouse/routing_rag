The Mystic Warrior

The Mystic Warrior is a 1984 American TV movie about a band of Sioux and the efforts of one man to save his people from destruction through the use of mysterious powers handed down by ancestors.

 The movie was originally a nine-hour mini-series entitled "Hanta Yo" to be aired in 1980, instead aired in 1984 as a five-hour mini series with the new name.

 The mini series has been released on DVD in Germany, but no international release as yet.
