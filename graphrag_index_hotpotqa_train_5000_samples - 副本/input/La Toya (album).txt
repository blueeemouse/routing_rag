La Toya (album)

La Toya (also known as You're Gonna Get Rocked!)

 is the fifth studio album by American singer La Toya Jackson released on October 25, 1988 by RCA.

 While the cover and box states the album's name as "La Toya", the actual disc and cassette tape state the name as "You're Gonna Get Rocked!"

.

 Therefore, the album is interchangeably referred to by both names.

 The album includes "(Ain't Nobody Loves You) Like I Do" and "You're Gonna Get Rocked", which are, to date, two of only five songs by La Toya Jackson to have an accompanying music video.

 The album was re-released as an expanded 2-CD set under the name "You're Gonna Get Rocked!"

 by Cherry Pop Records in December 2013.
