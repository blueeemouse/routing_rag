Trap Queen

"Trap Queen" is the debut single by American rapper Fetty Wap from his self-titled debut album (2015).

 Following its online premiere in March 2014, it was released independently on April 22, 2014 before being re-released in conjunction with 300 Entertainment on December 15, 2014.

 The song was well received by critics who praised the vocals and production for being cheery and infectious.
