Dos Mundos (Rick Trevino album)

Dos Mundos is the debut album of country music artist Rick Trevino, released in 1993 on Columbia Records.

 Its title is Spanish for "Two Worlds".

 The album produced no chart singles, although "Bastante Cordón" would be re-recorded in English as "Just Enough Rope" and serve as the first single to Trevino's self-titled second album.

 Also included is a Spanish-language cover of Bill Anederson's "Walk Out Backwards", which Trevino later covered in English on his 1994 self-titled album.
