2007 Alamo Bowl

The 2007 Valero Alamo Bowl was a college football bowl game played on December 29, 2007 in the 65,000-seat Alamodome in San Antonio, Texas, and nationally televised by ESPN.

 The game was one of the 2007–08 NCAA football bowl games that concluded the 2007 NCAA Division I FBS football season.

 It was the 15th Alamo Bowl and the first Alamo Bowl sponsored by the Valero Energy Corporation.
