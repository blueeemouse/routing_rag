Audi 5 Series DTM

The Audi 5 Series DTM (also known as the Audi A5 DTM (2012) later Audi RS5 DTM (2013-present)) is a DTM touring car constructed by the German car manufacturer Audi AG.

 It was developed in 2011 and has been raced in DTM seasons 2012-present.

 It was designed by former Audi Head of Research and Development Wolfgang Dürheimer.

 The A5 DTM replaced the retired Audi A4 DTM at the end of the 2011 season and based on the production Audi A5.
