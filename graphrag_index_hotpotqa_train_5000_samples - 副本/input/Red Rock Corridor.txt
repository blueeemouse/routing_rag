Red Rock Corridor

The Red Rock Corridor is a proposed bus rapid transit transitway in the Twin Cities of Minneapolis and Saint Paul.

 The route connects the metropolitan area's southeastern suburbs to downtown St. Paul, and through it to downtown Minneapolis.

 The transitway would originate in Hastings with planned stops in Cottage Grove, Newport and Saint Paul.

 The route will terminate at Saint Paul's Union Depot.

 From Union Depot, riders will connect with express buses, local buses, and the METRO Green Line to Minneapolis, Minneapolis–Saint Paul International Airport, and the Mall of America.
