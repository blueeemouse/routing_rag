R717 road (South Africa)

The R717 is a Regional Route in South Africa.

 From Colesberg to Reddersburg, it follows the older route of the N1.

 It south-western terminus is the N1 at Colesberg, Northern Cape.

 From there, it heads north-north-east, crossing the Orange River into Free State.

 The first town it comes to is Philippolis.

 From this town, it heads north-east to Trompsburg.

 At Trompsburg, it receives the south-eastern terminus of the R704.

 From Trompsburg until Edenburg, the route parallels the N1.

 After passing through Edenburg, it veers west-south-west, crossing the N1 to reach the N6 at Reddersburg.

 It is briefly cosigned with the N6, heading north, before continue in an easterly direction, reaching its north-eastern terminus of Dewetsdorp, where it ends at a junction with the R702.
