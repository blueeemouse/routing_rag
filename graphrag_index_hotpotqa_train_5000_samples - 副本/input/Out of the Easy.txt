Out of the Easy

Out of the Easy is a 2013 novel by Ruta Sepetys.

 It is her second published novel.

 It features Josie Moraine, a young woman in the 1950s French Quarter of New Orleans who struggles to escape her family and become the author of her own destiny.

 The novel became a New York Times bestseller and was chosen as an Editor’s Choice in the New York Times on February 15, 2013.
