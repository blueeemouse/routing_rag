Dubey Schaldenbrand

Dubey & Schaldenbrand is a luxury watch company dating back to 1946 and named after founders Georges Dubey and René Schaldenbrand.

 In 1995, the revival of Dubey & Schaldenbrand started as a homage to the past with Cinette Robert deciding to open her vault containing her fabulous old movement collection.

 (During the 1970s and early 1980s, Cinette Robert was one of the few visionaries that toured all manufacturers in order to purchase watch movements and components.

 Recasing each movement according to the era when it was created, the Dubey & Schaldenbrand designs rapidly found a niche with collectors.

 Dubey & Schaldenbrand continues to produce small quantities of automatic and mechanical movement watches.

 The company is located in La Chaux-de-Fonds in Switzerland.
