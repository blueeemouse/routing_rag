Trevor Goodchild

Trevor Goodchild is a fictional character featured in the 1990s animated television series, "Æon Flux", the 2005 "Æon Flux" live-action film, and the 2005 "Æon Flux" video game.

 He is played by voice actor John Rafter Lee in the half-hour series (the "Liquid Television" series of shorts had no dialogue).

 In the 2005 film, he is portrayed by Marton Csokas.

 In the 2005 video game, he is voiced by Troy Baker.
