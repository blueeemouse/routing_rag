Vengeance (2005)

Vengeance (2005) was a professional wrestling pay-per-view (PPV) event produced by World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE) and presented by THQ's "Juiced", which took place on June 26, 2005, at the Thomas & Mack Center in Las Vegas, Nevada.

 It was the fifth annual Vengeance event.

 Professional wrestling is a type of sports entertainment in which theatrical events are combined with a competitive sport.

 The buildup to the matches and the scenarios that took place before, during, and after the event were planned by WWE's script writers.

 The event starred wrestlers from the Raw brand: storyline expansions of the promotion where employees are assigned to wrestling brands under the WWE banner.
