The Interpreter (Kim novel)

The Interpreter (2003) is Suki Kim’s first novel.

 In "The Interpreter", Kim creates a twenty-nine-year-old Korean American court interpreter named Suzy Park who makes a startling and ominous discovery during one court case which ultimately reveals the mystery of her parents' homicide.

 The award winning novel, mainly a murder mystery, breaks through the stereotypical images of the happy immigrant experience with a story of pain, loss, and murder.
