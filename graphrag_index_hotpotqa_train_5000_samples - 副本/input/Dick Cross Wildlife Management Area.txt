Dick Cross Wildlife Management Area

Dick Cross Wildlife Management Area is a 1400 acre Wildlife Management Area in Mecklenburg County, Virginia.

 Formerly known as the Elm Hill Wildlife Management Area, it sits on the north side of the Roanoke River just below the John H. Kerr Dam.

 The terrain is gently rolling and fairly low, between 200 and above sea level, and is typified by open fields maintained for the benefit of wildlife.

 Much of the land was once used to support the farming of cattle, and some evidence of this remains.

 Close to 300 acre of broad flood plain has been preserved along the river and along Allen Creek, which forms the area's eastern boundary before joining the Roanoke River.

 Around 165 acre of the area includes impounded wetlands specifically managed for waterfowl.
