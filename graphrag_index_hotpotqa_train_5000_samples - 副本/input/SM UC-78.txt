SM UC-78

SM "UC-78" was a German Type UC II minelaying submarine or U-boat in the German Imperial Navy (German: "Kaiserliche Marine" ) during World War I.

 The U-boat was ordered on 12 January 1916 and was launched on 8 December 1916.

 She was commissioned into the German Imperial Navy on 10 January 1917 as SM "UC-78".

 In twelve patrols "UC-78" was credited with sinking 1 warship, either by torpedo or by mines laid.

 "UC-78" was rammed and sunk by the British steamer "Queen Alexandra" west of Cherbourg on 9 May 1918.
