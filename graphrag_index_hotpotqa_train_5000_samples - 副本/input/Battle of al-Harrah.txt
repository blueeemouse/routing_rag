Battle of al-Harrah

The Battle of al-Harrah (Arabic: وقعة الحرة‎ ‎ ) was a battle fought at al-Harrah on 26 Dhu al-Hijjah 63 H./26 August 683, then lying to the northeast of Medina.

 The battle was fought against the armies of Yazid ibn Muawiyah by Abdullah ibn Zubayr and his allies, the people of Medina and several notable Sahabas, many of whom were killed in the battle.

 It is the second most infamous battle during the reign of Yazid bin Muawiyah, the 2nd Umayyad Caliph.
