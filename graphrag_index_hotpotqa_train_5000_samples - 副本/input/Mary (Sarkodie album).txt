Mary (Sarkodie album)

Mary is a live album by Ghanaian rapper Sarkodie.

 It is the rapper's fourth overall album and the follow-up to his third studio album, "Sarkology" (2014).

 The album was released through his imprint Sarkcess Music.

 It is dedicated to his grandmother who died in 2012.

 As the executive producer of his project, Sarkodie enlisted Akwaboah to produce and write the album.

 The live recorded album features guest appearances from Akwaboah, Efya, Mugeez, Obrafour and Chase.
