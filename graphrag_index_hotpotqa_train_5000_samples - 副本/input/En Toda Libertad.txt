En Toda Libertad

En Toda Libertad (in English: In All Freedom) is the thirteenth album released by the Mexican rock band Maná.

 It is a remix/tribute album, fanatically constructed and includes songs from Falta Amor to Exiliados En La Bahia.

 Being very similar to The Beatles’ "Love", the intent of the mashup album was to bring to the fans and audience a remix like no other, for the real fans to listen to Maná like they never have.
