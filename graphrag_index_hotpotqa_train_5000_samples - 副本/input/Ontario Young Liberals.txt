Ontario Young Liberals

The Ontario Young Liberals (OYL) is the official youth wing of the Ontario Liberal Party, and until 2017, of Liberal Party of Canada in Ontario.

 In 2017, the Liberal Party of Canada created the Young Liberals of Canada (Ontario), effectively ending the federal Liberal Party's role in the OYL.

 The OYL's membership includes all members of the Ontario Liberal Party aged 25 and under.

 The organization is divided into campus and riding clubs and led by a 16-member executive board.

 The OYL is the largest youth political organization in Ontario, and the largest provincial Young Liberal organization in Canada.
