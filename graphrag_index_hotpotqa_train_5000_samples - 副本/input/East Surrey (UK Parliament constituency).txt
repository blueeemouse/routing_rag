East Surrey (UK Parliament constituency)

East Surrey is a constituency represented in the House of Commons of the UK Parliament since 2010 by Sam Gyimah of the Conservative Party.

 Its record is that of a Conservative safe seat based on time and opposition.

 It has elected a Conservative Party MP on an absolute majority since the seat's establishment, in 1918, and it's greatest share of the vote for any opposition candidate was 33.75% in February 1974.
