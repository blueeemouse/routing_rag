Make It Hot

Make It Hot is the debut studio album by R&B singer Nicole Wray.

 The album was released in August 1998 through Missy Elliott's Elektra-distributed vanity label, The Goldmind.

 The album was produced by Missy Elliott and Timbaland.

 Some editions of the release credit Nicole as "Nicole Ray", an artist name she only used here and on her single "I Can't See".
