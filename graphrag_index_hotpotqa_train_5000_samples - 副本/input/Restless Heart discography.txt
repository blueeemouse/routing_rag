Restless Heart discography

Restless Heart is an American country music band founded in 1984 by John Dittrich, Paul Gregg, Dave Innis, Greg Jennings, and Larry Stewart.

 Active from 1984 to 1994, in 1998, and from 2002 onward, the band has recorded seven studio albums and two greatest hits albums.

 Its second through fifth studio albums — "Wheels", "Big Dreams in a Small Town", "Fast Movin' Train", and "Big Iron Horses" — have all been certified gold by the Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA).
