Ammonium perchlorate

Ammonium perchlorate ("AP") is an inorganic compound with the formula NHClO.

 It is a colorless or white solid that is soluble in water.

 Perchlorate is a powerful oxidizer and ammonium is a good fuel.

 This combination explains the usefulness of this material as a rocket propellant.

 Its instability has involved it in a number of accidents, such as the PEPCON disaster.
