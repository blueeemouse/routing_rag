Pavlos, Crown Prince of Greece

Pavlos, Crown Prince of Greece, {'1': ", '2': ", '3': 'RE', '4': "} (Greek: Παύλος ; born 20 May 1967) is the eldest son and second child of Constantine II, the last King of Greece from 1964 to 1973 and his wife, Anne-Marie of Denmark.

 Pavlos was heir apparent to the throne of Greece and was its crown prince from birth, remaining so during his father's reign until the monarchy's abolition.
