Platz der Luftbrücke (Berlin U-Bahn)

Platz der Luftbrücke is a Berlin U-Bahn station located on the U6 .

 It is located under Platz der Luftbrücke and the south end of Mehringdamm on the border between Kreuzberg and Tempelhof, near the former Tempelhof International Airport, and is now named for the square there with its memorial to the victims of the Berlin Airlift.
