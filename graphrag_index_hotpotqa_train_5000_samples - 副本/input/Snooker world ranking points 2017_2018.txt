Snooker world ranking points 2017/2018

The official 2017/2018 snooker world ranking points for the professional snooker players on the World Snooker Main Tour in the 2017/2018 season are based on performances in ranking and minor-ranking tournaments over a two-year rolling period.

 The rankings at the start of 2017/2018 season are determined by prize money earned in the 2015/2016 and 2016/2017 seasons and are updated after every tournament carrying ranking status.

 As points are accrued from tournaments throughout the current season, the points from the corresponding tournaments from two seasons earlier are dropped.

 The rankings set the official seedings at the start of the season and at various further stages.

 The total points accumulated by the cut-off dates for the revised seedings are based on all the points up to that date in the 2017/2018 season, all of the points from the 2016/2017 season, and the points from the 2015/2016 season that have not yet been dropped.
