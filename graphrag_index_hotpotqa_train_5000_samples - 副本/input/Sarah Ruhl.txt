Sarah Ruhl

Sarah Ruhl (born January 24, 1974) is an American playwright, professor, and essayist.

 Among her most popular plays are "Eurydice" (2003), "The Clean House" (2004), and "In the Next Room (or the Vibrator Play)" (2009).

 She is the recipient of a MacArthur Fellowship and the PEN/Laura Pels International Foundation for Theater Award for a distinguished American playwright in mid-career.

 Two of her plays have been finalists for the Pulitzer Prize for Drama and she received a nomination for Tony Award for Best Play.
