Vermont Air National Guard

The Vermont Air National Guard (VT ANG) is the air force militia of the State of Vermont, United States of America.

 It is, along with the Vermont Army National Guard, an element of the Vermont National Guard.

 The 158th Fighter Wing is its sole unit.

 It is under the jurisdiction of the Governor of Vermont through the office of the Vermont Adjutant General unless they are federalized by order of the President of the United States.

 The Vermont Air National Guard is headquartered at Burlington Air National Guard Base.
