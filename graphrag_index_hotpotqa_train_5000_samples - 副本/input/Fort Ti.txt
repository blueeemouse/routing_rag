Fort Ti

Fort Ti is a 1953 American 3-D Western film directed by William Castle, and starring George Montgomery and Joan Vohs.

 Written by Robert E. Kent, the film is the first Western to be released in 3-D and the first 3-D feature to be released in Technicolor by a major studio.

 "Fort Ti" was produced by Esskay Pictures Corporation, and was distributed by Columbia Pictures in the United States.
