The Missing (TV series)

The Missing is a British television drama series written by brothers Harry and Jack Williams first broadcast on BBC One in the UK on 28 October 2014 and in the US on Starz on 15 November 2014.

 The first eight-part series was about the search for a missing boy in France, and was directed by Tom Shankland.

 It stars Tchéky Karyo as Julien Baptiste, the French detective who leads the case with James Nesbitt and Frances O'Connor as the boy's parents.

 "The Missing" is a co-production between the BBC and Starz.
