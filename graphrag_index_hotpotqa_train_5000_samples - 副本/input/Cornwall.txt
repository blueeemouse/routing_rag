Cornwall

Cornwall ( , ; Cornish: Kernow ] ) is a ceremonial county in the United Kingdom.

 It is also a unitary authority area of England, administered by Cornwall Council.

 The county is bordered to the north and west by the Celtic Sea, to the south by the English Channel, and to the east by the county of Devon, over the River Tamar which forms most of the border between them.

 Cornwall has a population of 556,000 and covers an area of 3563 km2 .

 The ceremonial county of Cornwall also includes the Isles of Scilly, which are administered separately.

 The administrative centre of Cornwall, and only city in the county, is Truro.
