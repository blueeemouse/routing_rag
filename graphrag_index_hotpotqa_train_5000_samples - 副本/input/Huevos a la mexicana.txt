Huevos a la mexicana

Huevos a la mexicana is a popular breakfast dish in Mexican cuisine.

 Finely chopped tomato, green chili pepper and onion is lightly fried in a hot skillet.

 Eggs are added and stirred until set.

 The heat is turned off and the coriander leaves are mixed in the eggs, adding salt.

 Refried beans is a common accompaniment.
