Stevan Faddy

Stevan Faddy (Стеван Фeди; born 2 September 1986 in Kotor) is a Montenegrin singer.

 He took part in Montevizija 2006, singing "Cipele".

 He also participated in Sunčane Skale 2003 singing "Poslednja obala".

 On 25 February 2007, he won a landslide victory in MontenegroSong 2007 with his song "Ajde Kroči" and won a ticket to represent Montenegro in the Eurovision Song Contest 2007 held in Helsinki, Finland.

 Stevan was the first singer representing Montenegro after its independence.

 He is currently living in Croatia.
