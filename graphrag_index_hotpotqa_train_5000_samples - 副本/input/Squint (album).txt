Squint (album)

Squint was the 1993 critically acclaimed return of Steve Taylor as a solo artist after his stint as the lead singer of Chagall Guevara.

 Highlights of the album include "The Lament of Desmond RG Underwood Frederick IV," "Easy Listening," "Jesus is for Losers," "The Finish Line," "Bannerman," and "Curses."

 It was the last studio album released by Steve Taylor as a solo artist.

 It peaked at position 17 on the "Billboard" "Top Contemporary Christian Album" Chart.
