Qemal Stafa Stadium

The Qemal Stafa Stadium (Albanian: "Stadiumi Qemal Stafa" ), named after Qemal Stafa a World War II hero, was a national stadium and the largest football stadium in Tirana, Albania.

 Construction started in 1939 and the stadium was inaugurated in 1946 for the Balkan Cup, which was won by the Albania national football team.

 The stadium has been used for football matches of the Albanian Superliga and the national team, athletic events, and the six Albanian Spartakiads.

 Although it was enlarged in 1974 to accommodate up to 35,000 spectators, in the 1990s it became an all-seater stadium, and its capacity was reduced to the current 19,700.
