Hillel Fendel

Hillel Fendel (Hebrew: הלל פנדל‎ ‎ ) was, for 16 years, senior editor and co-founder of Arutz Sheva's "Israel National News" and also works as an author and editor.

 He worked as a teacher and rabbi in the past.

 He is a son of Rabbi Meyer Fendel, founder of Hebrew Academy of Nassau County, and the brother of Rabbi Dovid Fendel, founder of the Hesder Yeshiva of Sderot.

 He is also a nephew of the late Orthodox Jewish educator and author, Rabbi Zechariah Fendel.
