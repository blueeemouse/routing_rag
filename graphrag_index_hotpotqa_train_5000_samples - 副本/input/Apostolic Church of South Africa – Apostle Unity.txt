Apostolic Church of South Africa – Apostle Unity

The Apostolic Church (Apostle Unity) is the South African branch of the United Apostolic Church.

 It has roots in the Catholic Apostolic Church of the early 19th century.

 It was founded in 1955 as a schism of the New Apostolic Church.

 It is part of a branch of Christianity called Irvingism and is separate from Protestantism.
