Yoon Hyun-min

Yoon Hyun-min (born April 15, 1985) is a South Korean actor.

 After graduating from Cheongwon high school, Yoon was a baseball player for the Hanwha Eagles in 2005 and the Doosan Bears in 2006.

 He fell in love with the stage after watching "Finding Kim Jong-wook", and quit sports to start acting in 2007, starring in the musical "Spring Awakening".

 Yoon later transitioned to television dramas, with notable supporting roles in "Cruel City" (2013), "Discovery of Love" (2014), and "Falling for Innocence" (2015).

 He has also played leading roles in the dramas My Daughter, Geum Sa-wol (2015) and Tunnel (2017).
