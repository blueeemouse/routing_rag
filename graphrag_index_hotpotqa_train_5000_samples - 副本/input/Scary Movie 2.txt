Scary Movie 2

Scary Movie 2 is a 2001 American horror comedy film and the second film in the "Scary Movie" franchise.

 The film stars Anna Faris, Regina Hall, Shawn Wayans, and Marlon Wayans (all reprising their roles from the first film, despite their characters having been killed off), as well as Tim Curry, Tori Spelling, Chris Elliott, Chris Masterson, Kathleen Robertson, David Cross, and James Woods.
