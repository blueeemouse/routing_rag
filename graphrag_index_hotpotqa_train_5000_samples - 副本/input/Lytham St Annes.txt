Lytham St Annes

Lytham St Annes ( ) is a conurbation in the Fylde district of Lancashire, England.

 The neighbouring towns of Lytham and St. Annes-on-the-Sea (nearly always abbreviated to St Annes) have grown together and now form a seaside resort.

 The towns are situated on the Fylde coast, south of Blackpool at the point where the coastline turns east to form the estuary of the River Ribble leading inland to Preston.

 St Annes is situated on the northern side of the turning and, like Blackpool, overlooks the Irish Sea, whereas Lytham is on the eastern side and overlooks the Ribble Estuary.

 The population of Lytham St Annes taken at the 2011 census was 42,954.
