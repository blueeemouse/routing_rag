Paul Weitz (filmmaker)

Paul John Weitz (born November 19, 1965) is an American film director, film producer, screenwriter, playwright actor.

 He is the older brother of filmmaker Chris Weitz.

 He is best known for his work with his brother, Chris Weitz, on the comedy films "American Pie" and "About a Boy", for which the brothers, who co-directed, were nominated for an Oscar.

 He also serves as a writer, executive producer, and director of the television series "Mozart in the Jungle".
