East Rail Line

The East Rail Line () is one of eleven railway lines of the Mass Transit Railway (MTR) system in Hong Kong.

 It used to be one of the three lines of the Kowloon–Canton Railway (KCR) network.

 It was known as the KCR British Section (九廣鐵路英段) from 1910 to 1996, and the KCR East Rail (九廣東鐵) from 1996 to 2007.

 The East Rail was the only railway line of the Kowloon-Canton Railway Corporation (KCRC) before the construction of "KCR West Rail" (now known as West Rail Line).
