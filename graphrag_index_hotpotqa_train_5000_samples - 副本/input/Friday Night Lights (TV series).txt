Friday Night Lights (TV series)

Friday Night Lights is an American drama television series about a high school football team in the fictional town of Dillon, Texas.

 It was developed by Peter Berg and executive produced by Brian Grazer, David Nevins, Sarah Aubrey and Jason Katims, based on by H. G. Bissinger, adapted as the 2004 film of the same name.

 The series' primary setting, Dillon, is a small, close-knit community in rural Texas.

 Team coach Eric Taylor (Kyle Chandler) and his family, Tami, Julie and Grace, are featured.

 The show uses this small-town backdrop to address many issues facing contemporary American culture, including family values, school funding, racism, drugs, abortion and lack of economic opportunities.
