Ford River Rouge Complex

The Ford River Rouge Complex (commonly known as the Rouge Complex or just The Rouge) is a Ford Motor Company automobile factory complex located in Dearborn, Michigan, along the Rouge River, upstream from its confluence with the Detroit River at Zug Island.

 Construction began in 1917, and when it was completed in 1928 it had become the largest integrated factory in the world.

 It served as an inspiration for the Russian GAZ factory in the 1930s, and the Hyundai factory complex in Ulsan, South Korea.

 Designed by Albert Kahn, it was designated a National Historic Landmark District in 1978 for its architecture and historical importance.
