Insidious (film)

Insidious is a 2010 American-Canadian supernatural horror film directed by James Wan, written by Leigh Whannell, and starring Patrick Wilson, Rose Byrne and Barbara Hershey.

 It is the first (chronologically, the third) installment in the "Insidious" franchise.

 The story centers on a couple whose son inexplicably enters a comatose state and becomes a vessel for ghosts in an astral dimension who want to inhabit his body, in order to live once again.

 The film was released in theaters on April 1, 2011, and was FilmDistrict's first theatrical release.
