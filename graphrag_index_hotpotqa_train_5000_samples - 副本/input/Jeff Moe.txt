Jeff Moe

Jeff Moe (born May 19, 1966) is an American former professional basketball player.

 He played in the Continental Basketball Association for the Cedar Rapids Silver Bullets during the first part of the 1988–89 season before being released in late December 1988.

 Moe had a collegiate career at the University of Iowa from 1984 to 1988 before being selected in the 1988 NBA draft.

 He was taken in the second round (42nd overall) by the Utah Jazz, but never appeared in an NBA game.

 After his brief professional basketball career, Moe became a real estate executive in Indiana.
