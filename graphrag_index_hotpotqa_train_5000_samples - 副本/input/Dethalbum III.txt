Dethalbum III

Dethalbum III is the third full-length album by virtual death metal band Dethklok, from the Adult Swim animated series "Metalocalypse".

 The CD and deluxe CD/DVD were released on October 16, 2012.

 It contains music from the second, third and fourth seasons of the show.

 Like "The Dethalbum" and "Dethalbum II" before it, the music is performed by the show's creator Brendon Small and drummer Gene Hoglan.

 Additionally, the band's live bassist, Bryan Beller, performs on the album.
