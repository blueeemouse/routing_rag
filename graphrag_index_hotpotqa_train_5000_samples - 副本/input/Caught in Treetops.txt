Caught in Treetops

Caught in Treetops is a concerto for solo violin and chamber ensemble by the British composer Charlotte Bray.

 The work was commissioned by the Birmingham Contemporary Music Group and Sound and Music.

 It was first performed on 14 November 2010 at the CBSO Centre, Birmingham by the violinist Alexandra Wood and the Birmingham Contemporary Music Group under conductor Oliver Knussen.

 The piece is dedicated to the Birmingham Contemporary Music Group.
