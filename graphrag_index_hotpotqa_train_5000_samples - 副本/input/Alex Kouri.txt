Alex Kouri

Alexander Martin Kouri Bumachar, (born Lima, 7 April 1964), known as Alex Kouri, is a Peruvian lawyer and politician.

 Throughout his fruitful career, he has held multiple titles: president of Beneficiencia del Callao (1990), Congressman (1993-1995), three times elected Mayor of the city of Callao and Governor of Callao region.
