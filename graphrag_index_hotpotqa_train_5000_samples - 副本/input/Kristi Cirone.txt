Kristi Cirone

Kristi Mary Rose Cirone (born May 19, 1987) is an American basketball player who played collegiately for Illinois State University.

 She is a Point Guard for the Redbirds.

 A scholar athlete majoring in Exercise Science.

 Cirone has received many awards including AFNI Athletics Honor Roll: Fall 2005, Spring 2006, Fall 2006, Spring 2007, Fall 2007, Spring 2008, MVC Honor Roll: 2008, MVC Commissioner's Academic Excellence Award: 2008.
