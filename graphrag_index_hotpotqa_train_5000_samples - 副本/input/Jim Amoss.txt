Jim Amoss

Walter James Amoss III (born October 22, 1947) is former editor of "The Times-Picayune".

 Under his leadership and that of the publisher, Ashton Phelps Jr., the paper won two Pulitzer Prizes in 1997 for public service and editorial cartooning, and in 2006 won two more Pulitzer Prizes for its coverage of Hurricane Katrina.
