Laeeq

Laeeq "لئیق" is a masculine name, which means able, worthy, fit, suitable, capable, decent, intelligent.

 The word is commonly pronounced as "Layk".

 It has roots in Urdu, Arabic, Ethiopian and Scottish languages.

 The closest Arabic equivalent of Laeeq/Laiq is "Laeq" "لائق".

 "Laeeq" is also believed to be an African name.

 It is commonly considered as an Urdu name.

 Variants of the name "Laeeq" are also found in Scotland.

 The English variant forms of the name include: Laike, Laiken, Laikin, Laken, Lake, and Lakon.
