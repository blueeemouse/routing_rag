Clerics of Saint Viator

The Viatorians, or Clerics of Saint Viator (C.S.V.), are a Roman Catholic religious institute founded in Lyon, France, in 1831 by Father Louis Querbes.

 Its patron, Saint Viator, was a 4th-century catechist in Lyon.

 The institute soon spread from its origins in France to the United States and now has provinces and missions all over the world.

 They are a teaching order and are thus involved in parish ministries and all levels of education, from grade school through university.
