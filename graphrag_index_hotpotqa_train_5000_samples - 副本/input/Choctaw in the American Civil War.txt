Choctaw in the American Civil War

The Choctaw in the American Civil War participated in two major arenas— the Trans-Mississippi and Western Theaters.

 The Trans-Mississippi had the Choctaw Nation.

 The Western had the Mississippi Choctaw.

 The Choctaw Nation had removed West prior to the War.

 But, the Mississippi Choctaw had elected to remain in the East.

 Both the Choctaw Nation and the Mississippi Choctaw would ultimately side with the Confederacy.
