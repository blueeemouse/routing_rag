Chris Stafford

Chris Stafford (born Christopher Stafford on July 18, 1977, in Collinsville, Illinois) is an American actor.

 He has acted in film and television including "Law & Order" and the coming-out movie "Edge of Seventeen", for which he received the Best Actor award at Outfest, and an Independent Spirit Award nomination.

 After acting Stafford turned his hand to Law.

 He is now a lawyer.
