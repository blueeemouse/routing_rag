Orland Smith (American football)

Orland Francis Smith (November 5, 1905 – August 14, 1977) was an American football player.

 He played at the tackle position in the National Football League (NFL) for the Providence Steam Roller from 1927 to 1929.

 Smith was attending medical school at Boston University during the week while playing in the NFL on Sundays.

 He also played college football at Brown University where he was selected by the Associated Press as a third-team player, and by "Collier's Weekly" as a second-team player, on the 1926 College Football All-America Team.
