Workout (RuPaul song)

"WorkOut" is the second single from RuPaul's album "Red Hot".

 The song is a dance/house song about feeling liberated while dancing.

 The expression "work out" is (not exclusively) gay slang for expressing one's self exuberantly; it is similar to RuPaul's coined phrase "You better work!"

.

 Two versions of the single were made available; one version, with 8 tracks, was released by RuPaul on her own RuCo, Inc. label.

 The other version, a 3 track version released only in Germany, was released on the Dance Street label.
