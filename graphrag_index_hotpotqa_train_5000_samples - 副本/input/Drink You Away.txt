Drink You Away

"Drink You Away" is a song recorded by American singer and songwriter Justin Timberlake for his fourth studio album, "The 20/20 Experience – 2 of 2" (2013).

 It was written and produced by Timberlake, Timothy "Timbaland" Mosley and Jerome "J-Roc" Harmon, with additional writing from James Fauntleroy.

 It was sent to US country radio stations on November 23, 2015 as the fourth and final single from the album.

 Musically, it was defined by critics as a multi-genre song including classic-rock, country-pop, gospel-blues and Memphis soul.

 Lyrically, on the track, Timberlake compares love to alcoholism while making references to several alcoholic brands including Jack Daniel's and Jim Beam.

 As of May 2016, the song has sold over 600,000 copies in the US.
