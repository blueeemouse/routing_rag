List of LSU Tigers head football coaches

The LSU Tigers college football team represents Louisiana State University (LSU) in the West Division of the Southeastern Conference (SEC).

 The Tigers compete as part of the National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) Division I Football Bowl Subdivision.

 The program has had 32 head coaches since it began play during the 1893 season.

 Since September 2016, Ed Orgeron has served as LSU's head coach.
