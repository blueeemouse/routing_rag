Acrobasis pirivorella

Acrobasis pyrivorella, the pear fruit moth, pear moth or pear pyralid, is a moth of the family Pyralidae.

 It is native to the temperate zone of eastern Asia, where it is widely distributed.

 It has been recorded from northern China, the Korean Peninsula, Japan, Taiwan and Russia (Primor'ye and Khabarovskii provinces).
