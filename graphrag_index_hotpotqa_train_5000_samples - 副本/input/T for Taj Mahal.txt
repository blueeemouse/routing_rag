T for Taj Mahal

T for Taj Mahal (2017 film) is an upcoming Bollywood feature film directed by 6-time President's National Award winning filmmaker Kireet Khurana and produced by Mumbai-based businessman and producer, Abis Rizvi.

 Rizvi was one of the victims of the terrorist attack in the Reina nightclub in Istanbul on 1 January 2017 in which 39 people were killed.

 The film is slated for a late 2017 release after making rounds at the international festival circuit.
