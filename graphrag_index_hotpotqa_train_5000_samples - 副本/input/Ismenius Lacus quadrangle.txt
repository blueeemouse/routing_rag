Ismenius Lacus quadrangle

The Ismenius Lacus quadrangle is one of a series of 30 quadrangle maps of Mars used by the United States Geological Survey (USGS) Astrogeology Research Program.

 The quadrangle is located in the northwestern portion of Mars’ eastern hemisphere and covers 0° to 60° east longitude (300° to 360° west longitude) and 30° to 65° north latitude.

 The quadrangle uses a Lambert conformal conic projection at a nominal scale of 1:5,000,000 (1:5M).

 The Ismenius Lacus quadrangle is also referred to as MC-5 (Mars Chart-5).

 The southern and northern borders of the Ismenius Lacus quadrangle are approximately 3065 km and 1500 km wide, respectively.

 The north-to-south distance is about 2050 km (slightly less than the length of Greenland).

 The quadrangle covers an approximate area of 4.9 million square km, or a little over 3% of Mars’ surface area.

 The Ismenius Lacus quadrangle contains parts of Acidalia Planitia, Arabia Terra, Vastitas Borealis, and Terra Sabaea.
