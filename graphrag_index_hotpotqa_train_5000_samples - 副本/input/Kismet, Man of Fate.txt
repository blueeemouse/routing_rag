Kismet, Man of Fate

Kismet, Man of Fate is an ongoing American comic book series currently written by A. David Lewis with art by Noel Tuazon, colors by Rob Croonenborghs, and lettering by Taylor Esposito, published by A Wave Blue World as part of their "Under Current" line.

 The series features the new, modern-day adventures of an Alergian superhero who was thought lost by the Allies at the end of World War II.

 The character originally appeared in "Bomber Comics" #1 (1944), making him the first identified Muslim superhero.
