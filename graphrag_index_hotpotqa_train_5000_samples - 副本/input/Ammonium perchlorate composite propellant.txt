Ammonium perchlorate composite propellant

Ammonium perchlorate composite propellant (APCP) is a modern solid-fuel rocket used in rocket vehicles.

 It differs from many traditional solid rocket propellants such as black powder or zinc-sulfur, not only in chemical composition and overall performance, but also by the nature of how it is processed.

 APCP is cast into shape, as opposed to powder pressing as with black powder.

 This provides manufacturing regularity and repeatability, which are necessary requirements for use in the aerospace industry.
