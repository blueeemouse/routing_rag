South Carolina Highway Patrol

The South Carolina Highway Patrol is the highway patrol agency for South Carolina, which has jurisdiction anywhere in the state except for federal or military installations.

 The Highway Patrol was created in 1930 and is an organization with a rank structure similar to the armed forces.

 The mission of the South Carolina Highway Patrol includes enforcing the rules and regulations in order to ensure road way safety and reducing crime as outlined by South Carolina law.

 The Highway Patrol is the largest division of the South Carolina Department of Public Safety and its headquarters is located in Blythewood.

 This department also includes the South Carolina State Transport Police Division, and the South Carolina Bureau of Protective Services.
