Bill LaBounty

Bill LaBounty is an American musician.

 He was initially a singer-songwriter in the soft rock genre, first as a member of the band Fat Chance, and later as a solo artist.

 As a solo artist, LaBounty recorded six studio albums, including four on Curb Records/Warner Bros.

 Records.

 His first chart single, "This Night Won't Last Forever", was covered in 1979 by Michael Johnson, whose rendition was a Top 20 pop hit that year.
