Little Man, What Now? (novel)

Little Man, What Now?

 (German title: "Kleiner Mann, was nun?")

 is a novel by Hans Fallada, which was first published in 1932, the year before Adolf Hitler's rise to power.

 The book was an immediate success in Germany, where today it is considered to be a modern classic, given its intense descriptions of the last days of the Weimar Republic.

 The book was also the breakthrough for Fallada as a writer of fiction.
