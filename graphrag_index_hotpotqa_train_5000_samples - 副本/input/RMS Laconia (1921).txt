RMS Laconia (1921)

The second RMS "Laconia" was a Cunard ocean liner, built by Swan, Hunter & Wigham Richardson as a successor of the 1911-1917 "Laconia".

 The new ship was launched on 9 April 1921, and made her maiden voyage on 25 May 1922 from Southampton to New York City.

 At the outbreak of World War II she was converted into an Armed Merchant Cruiser, and subsequently a troopship.

 Like her predecessor, sunk during the First World War, this "Laconia" was also destroyed by a German submarine.

 Some estimates of the death toll have suggested that over 1,649 people were killed when the "Laconia" sank.

 The U-boat commander Werner Hartenstein then staged a dramatic effort to rescue the passengers and the crew of "Laconia", which involved additional German U-boats and became known as the Laconia incident.
