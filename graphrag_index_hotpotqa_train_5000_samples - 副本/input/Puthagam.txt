Puthagam

Puthagam (English: Book) is a 2013 Tamil film directed by television personality Vijay Adhiraj and produced by S. Manjula for Ram Pictures Private Limited.

 Sathya, brother of actor Arya plays the lead role.

 Rakul Preet Singh, a former Miss India is the leading lady.

 Sanjay Bharathi, son of veteran actor/director Santhana Barathi along with Vignesh Vijaykumar of "Kadhalil Sodhappuvadhu Yeppadi" fame play pivotal roles.

 Telugu actor Jagapati Babu plays a key role.

 The film has supporting star cast which include Suresh, Thalaivasal Vijay, Santhana Bharathi, Delhi Ganesh, Crazy Mohan, Fathima Babu, Uma Padmanabhan and others.
