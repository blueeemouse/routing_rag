1958 Washington State Cougars football team

The 1958 Washington State Cougars football team was an American football team that represented Washington State University in the Pacific Coast Conference (PCC) during the 1958 college football season.

 In their third season under head coach Jim Sutherland, the Cougars compiled a 7–3 record and outscored their opponents by a combined total of 199 to 117.
