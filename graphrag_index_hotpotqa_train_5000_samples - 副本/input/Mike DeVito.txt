Mike DeVito

Michael Ralph DeVito (born June 10, 1984) is a former American football defensive end.

 He played for the New York Jets of the National Football League (NFL) from 2007 to 2012, and then the Kansas City Chiefs from 2013 to 2015.

 He played college football at The University of Maine.

 DeVito attended high school at Nauset Regional High School in Eastham, Massachusetts.
