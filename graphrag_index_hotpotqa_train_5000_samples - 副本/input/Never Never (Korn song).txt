Never Never (Korn song)

"Never Never" is a song written and recorded by American nu metal band Korn.

 It was released as the lead single from Korn's eleventh studio album, "The Paradigm Shift", on August 12, 2013.

 It marks the first Korn single with original co-founding guitarist Brian "Head" Welch since 2004.

 The single became Korn's first number one on the Mainstream Rock Songs chart.
