The Cutting Edge

The Cutting Edge is a 1992 romantic comedy film directed by Paul Michael Glaser and written by Tony Gilroy.

 The plot is about a very rich, spoiled figure skater (played by Moira Kelly) who is paired with a has-been ice hockey player (played by D. B. Sweeney) for Olympic figure skating.

 Competing at the 1992 Winter Olympics, they have a climactic face off against a Soviet pair.

 The film was primarily shot in Hamilton, Ontario, Canada.
