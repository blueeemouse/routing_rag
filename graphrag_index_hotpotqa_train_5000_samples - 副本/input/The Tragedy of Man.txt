The Tragedy of Man

The Tragedy of Man (Hungarian: "Az ember tragédiája" ) is a play written by the Hungarian author Imre Madách.

 It was first published in 1861.

 The play is considered to be one of the major works of Hungarian literature and is one of the most often staged Hungarian plays today.

 Many lines have become common quotations in Hungary.

 The 1984 film "The Annunciation" ("Angyali üdvözlet") was based on the play, as was the 2011 animated film "The Tragedy of Man".
