Field of Reeds

Field of Reeds is the third studio album by British art rock band These New Puritans, released on 10 June 2013 on Infectious Music.

 Produced by Jack Barnett and Graham Sutton, the album features prominent contributions from over thirty-eight session musicians, including fado singer Elisa Rodrigues, and finds the band "reinventing themselves as a neoclassical ensemble."

 The album is the band's first without keyboardist Sophie Sleigh-Johnson, who departed from the band in 2012.
