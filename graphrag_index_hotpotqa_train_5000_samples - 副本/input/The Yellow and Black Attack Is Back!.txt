The Yellow and Black Attack Is Back!

The Yellow and Black Attack Is Back!

 is a Stryper tribute album by Christian rock band Guardian.

 The album is a re-make of Stryper's debut EP "The Yellow and Black Attack" from 1984.

 This record was released in 1999.

 The cover-artwork is also based on Stryper's version, with the four missiles on it, featuring the initials of each band's member as it is shown on the original cover: "MS" stands for Michael Sweet, "RS" for Robert Sweet, "OF" for Oz Fox, and "TG" for Tim Gaines; here the initials are: "JR" for Jamie Rowe, "TP" for Tony Palacios, "KN" for Karl Ney, and "DB" for David Bach.
