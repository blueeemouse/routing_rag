A Little Less Conversation

"A Little Less Conversation" is a song written by Mac Davis and Billy Strange originally performed by Elvis Presley for the 1968 film "Live a Little, Love a Little".

 The song became a minor hit in the United States when released as a single with "Almost in Love" as the A-side.

 A 2002 remix by Junkie XL of a later re-recording of the song by Presley became a worldwide hit, topping the singles charts in nine countries and was awarded certifications in ten countries by 2003.
