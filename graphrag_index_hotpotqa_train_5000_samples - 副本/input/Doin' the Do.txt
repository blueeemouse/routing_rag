Doin' the Do

"Doin' the Do" is a popular song written by British singer Alison Clarkson, known by the stage name Betty Boo.

 The song was included on her debut album "Boomania" and was released as a single in May 1990.

 It was her second musical release, following her collaboration with The Beatmasters on their song "Hey DJ/I Can't Dance (To That Music You're Playing)" in 1989.

 The song sampled portions of the 1968 single "Captain of Your Ship" by the American girl group Reparata and the Delrons.
