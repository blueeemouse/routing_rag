Luxembourg City Hall

Luxembourg City Hall (French: "Hôtel de ville de Luxembourg" ) is the city hall of Luxembourg City, in southern Luxembourg.

 The city hall is the centre of local government, including being used as the private office of the Mayor of Luxembourg City.

 Due to its position in Luxembourg's capital, it also regularly plays host to foreign dignitaries.

 It is located on the southwestern part of Place Guillaume II (nicknamed "Knuedler"), the main square in the centre of the city.
