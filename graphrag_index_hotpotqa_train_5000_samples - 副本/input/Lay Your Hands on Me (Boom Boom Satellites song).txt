Lay Your Hands on Me (Boom Boom Satellites song)

"Lay Your Hands on Me" (stylized as "LAY YOUR HANDS ON ME") is a song and an EP of the same name by Japanese electronica/rock duo Boom Boom Satellites, scheduled for release on June 22, 2016.

 The track is used as the opening theme for the anime "Kiznaiver".

 Due to vocalist and guitarist Michiyuki Kawashima's failing health, "Lay Your Hands on Me" serves as Boom Boom Satellites' final record.
