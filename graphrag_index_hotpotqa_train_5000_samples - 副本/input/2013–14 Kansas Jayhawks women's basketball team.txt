2013–14 Kansas Jayhawks women's basketball team

The 2013–14 Kansas Jayhawks women's basketball team will represent the University of Kansas in the 2013–14 NCAA Division I women's basketball season.

 This is head coach Bonnie Henrickson's tenth season at Kansas.

 They play their home games at Allen Fieldhouse in Lawrence, Kansas and were members of the Big 12 Conference.

 They finished the season with a record of 13–19 overall, 5–13 for a tie to finish in eighth place.

 They lost in the quarterfinals in the 2014 Big 12 Women's Basketball Tournament to Baylor.
