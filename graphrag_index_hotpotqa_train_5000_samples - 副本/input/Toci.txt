Toci

Toci ( ; Classical Nahuatl: "tocih" , ] , “our grandmother”) is a deity figuring prominently in the religion and mythology of the pre-Columbian Aztec civilization of Mesoamerica.

 In Aztec mythology she is seen as an aspect of the mother goddess Coatlicue, and is thus labeled “mother of the gods” (Classical Nahuatl: "tēteoh īnnān" ).

 She is also called Tlalli Iyollo (Classical Nahuatl: "tlālli īyōlloh" , ] , “heart of the earth”).
