Both (song)

"Both" is the third single from American rapper Gucci Mane's tenth studio album "The Return of East Atlanta Santa".

 The song features Canadian rapper Drake.

 The songwriting was partly handled by Atlanta based Nicholas Cobey between spring/summer 2016, the production of the song was provided by Metro Boomin and Southside.

 This songs marks their second 2016 collaboration following "Back on Road".
