St Peter and St Paul, Bromley

St Peter and St Paul is a church in the town of Bromley, Borough of Bromley, in south east London.

 Known familiarly as Bromley Parish Church, it is not far from Bromley High Street and approximately halfway between Bromley North and Bromley South railway stations.

 The church is part of the Diocese of Rochester within the Church of England.

 Largely destroyed by bombing during the Second World War, St Peter and St Paul was rebuilt in the 1950s.

 It has been Grade II* listed since 1955.
