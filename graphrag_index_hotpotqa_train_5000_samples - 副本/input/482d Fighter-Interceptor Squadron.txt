482d Fighter-Interceptor Squadron

The 482d Fighter-Interceptor Squadron is an inactive United States Air Force unit.

 Its last assignment was with Montgomery Air Defense Sector at Homestead Air Force Base, Florida in 1969.

 During World War II the squadron was a replacement training unit until disbanded in 1944 when the Army Air Forces converted training units to Army Air Force Base Units.

 It was reconstituted in 1955 and served as a fighter interceptor squadron until 1969.
