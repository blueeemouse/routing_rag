Former headquarters of Banca Monte Parma

The former Sede Centrale della Banca del Monte di Parma was the headquarters of Banca Monte Parma, located in the corner of Piazzale Battisti and Strada Cavour in central Parma, Emilia-Romagna region, Italy.

 In 1978 the headquarters was moved to 1 Palazzo Sanvitale.

 The location on 3/A Strada Cavour remained as the main branch in the city.

 Since 2015 the building became a branch of Intesa Sanpaolo.
