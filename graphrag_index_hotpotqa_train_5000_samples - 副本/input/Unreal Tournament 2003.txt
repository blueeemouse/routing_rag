Unreal Tournament 2003

Unreal Tournament 2003 or UT2003 is a first-person shooter video game developed by Epic Games and Digital Extremes, and published by Infogrames.

 It is designed mainly for multiplayer gaming.

 The game is part of the "Unreal series" of games, and is a sequel to "Unreal Tournament" ("UT99").

 The game set a record for the number of downloads (1.2 million) when the demo was released, which is a reflection of the popularity of the original "UT".

 In addition, the game engine has been widely licensed for games such as the "Tom Clancy's Rainbow Six" series, "Splinter Cell", and "America's Army".

 Its sequel/expansion, "Unreal Tournament 2004", was released on 16 March 2004.
