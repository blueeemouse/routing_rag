Vic Morrow

Victor "Vic" Morrow (February 14, 1929 – July 23, 1982) was an American actor and director whose credits include a starring role in the 1960s television series "Combat!

", prominent roles in a handful of other television and film dramas, and numerous guest roles on television.

 Morrow and two child actors were killed in 1982 by a stunt helicopter crash during the filming of "".

 Morrow also gained notice for his roles in movies like "Blackboard Jungle" (1955), "God's Little Acre" (1958), "Dirty Mary, Crazy Larry" (1974), and "The Bad News Bears" (1976).
