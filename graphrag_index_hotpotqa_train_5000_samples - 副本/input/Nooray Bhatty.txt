Nooray Bhatty

Noor Bhatty, better known by her stage name, Nooray Bhatti, is Pakistani supermodel.

 She is regarded as one of the top models of Pakistan.

 Nooray has established herself as a leading model of Pakistani fashion industry and has been nominated four times for Best Model Female.

 She received her first Hum Award nomination at 3rd Hum Awards, as Best Model Female and consecutive nomination at 4th Hum Awards, in same category.
